Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

Classification, preparation, SN1/SN2 mechanisms, elimination, Grignard aur polyhalogens

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Classification aur Nomenclature

  • Haloalkanes: alkane ka H ko halogen (F, Cl, Br, I) se replace kiya — R−X. Haloarenes: benzene ring pe halogen — Ar−X (jaise chlorobenzene).
  • Halogen ki ginti se: mono (CH₃Cl), di (CH₂Cl₂), tri (CHCl₃), tetra/poly (CCl₄).
  • Haloalkanes ka type ⭐: halogen jis C se juda uske hisaab se — primary (1°): C−X wala C sirf 1 dusre C se juda (CH₃CH₂Cl); secondary (2°): 2 C se ((CH₃)₂CHCl); tertiary (3°): 3 C se ((CH₃)₃CCl). Yeh SN1/SN2 me bahut matter karta!
  • Allylic/benzylic vs vinylic/aryl: allylic (X, C=C se adjacent C pe: CH₂=CH−CH₂Cl) aur benzylic (C₆H₅−CH₂Cl) = reactive. vinylic (CH₂=CH−Cl) aur aryl (C₆H₅−Cl) = less reactive (resonance se C−X strong).
  • IUPAC naming: halogen = prefix (fluoro/chloro/bromo/iodo) + parent alkane + number. Example: CH₃CH₂CHClCH₃ = 2-chlorobutane. Haloarene: chlorobenzene, 1-bromo-2-methylbenzene.

💡 1°/2°/3° ka classification = C−X wale carbon ke "neighbours" gino. Allylic/benzylic reactive, vinylic/aryl lazy (resonance). Yeh reactivity order exam me har jagah aata!

2️⃣ Preparation — Haloalkane Kaise Banate

  • From alcohols ⭐: R−OH + HX → R−X. HCl ke saath anhydrous ZnCl₂ chahiye (Lucas reagent) — Lucas test 1°/2°/3° alcohols distinguish karta (3° instant turbid, 2° ~5 min, 1° nahi). PCl₅, PCl₃, SOCl₂ (Darzens, best — side products SO₂+HCl gas banke nikal jate) bhi use hote.
  • From hydrocarbons: alkane ka free radical halogenation (Cl₂/Br₂ + sunlight/heat) — mixture banta, selective nahi (isliye limited use). Alkene + HX (Markovnikov) → haloalkane.
  • Halogen exchange ⭐: Finkelstein reaction: R−Cl/Br + NaI (acetone me) → R−I + NaCl/NaBr↓ (NaCl acetone me insoluble, reaction aage badhti). Swarts reaction: R−Cl/Br + AgF/SbF₃ → R−F (fluoride banana mushkil isliye special method).

💡 SOCl₂ (Darzens) = best alcohol→haloalkane (gas side products, clean). Finkelstein = NaI se iodide, Swarts = AgF se fluoride. Lucas test 1°/2°/3° alcohol alag karta — naam yaad rakho!

3️⃣ Physical Properties

  • Polarity: C−X bond polar (halogen zyada electronegative, δ−; carbon δ+) — lekin alkyl halides phir bhi pani me insoluble (hydrogen bonding nahi bana paate pani ke saath, energy jyada lagti).
  • Boiling point ⭐: same alkyl group ke liye RI > RBr > RCl > RF (size/mass badhne pe van der Waals badhte). Isomers me branching badhne pe BP ghatta (surface area kam). RI ka BP sabse zyada, RF sabse kam.
  • Density: bromo/iodo-alkanes pani se heavy; fluoro/chloro halki. Polyhalogen (CH₂Cl₂, CHCl₃, CCl₄) bhi dense.
  • Melting point: symmetry zyada to MP zyada (para-isomer ka MP ortho/meta se zyada — crystal me achhi packing).

💡 BP order: RI>RBr>RCl>RF (bigger halogen = stronger van der Waals). Pani me nahi ghulte (no H-bond). Symmetry zyada = MP zyada. Ye 3 points ka MCQ pakka!

4️⃣ Nucleophilic Substitution — SN1 vs SN2 ⭐⭐

  • Nucleophilic substitution: halogen ko koi nucleophile (Nu⁻, electron-rich) replace karta. R−X + Nu⁻ → R−Nu + X⁻. Carbon pe δ+ hota isliye nucleophile attack karta. Do mechanisms.
  • SN2 (bimolecular) ⭐: ek step me — nucleophile peeche se attack karta (backside), halogen nikalta, ek hi saath. Rate = k[R−X][Nu⁻] (second order, dono pe depend). 1° haloalkanes fast (kam steric hindrance). Inversion of configuration (Walden inversion) — umbrella palat jaisi. Polar aprotic solvent (acetone, DMSO) favour karta.
  • SN1 (unimolecular) ⭐: do step — pehle R−X slowly ionize hoke carbocation banata (rate-determining), phir nucleophile jaldi attack. Rate = k[R−X] (first order, sirf substrate pe). 3° haloalkanes fast (3° carbocation stable — hyperconjugation/inductive). Racemization (inversion + retention mix, flat carbocation pe dono taraf se attack). Polar protic solvent (pani, alcohol) favour karta (carbocation stabilize).
  • Reactivity order ⭐: SN2 me 1° > 2° > 3°; SN1 me 3° > 2° > 1°. Same alkyl: RI > RBr > RCl > RF (C−I weakest, achha leaving group).
  • Ambident nucleophile: CN⁻ se KCN → mainly nitrile (R−CN, C se bond); AgCN → isocyanide (R−NC, N se bond). KNO₂ → nitrite, AgNO₂ → nitro.

💡 SN2 = "back-door entry, 1° fast, inversion, 2nd order". SN1 = "carbocation pehle, 3° fast, racemization, 1st order". Order ULTA hai (SN2: 1°>3°; SN1: 3°>1°) — yeh sabse zyada puchha jata hai!

5️⃣ Elimination aur Metals ke Saath Reaction

  • Elimination (dehydrohalogenation) ⭐: alcoholic KOH se HX nikal jata, alkene banta. R−CH₂−CHX−R + alc. KOH → alkene. Saytzeff (Zaitsev) rule ⭐: woh alkene major product jisme double bond pe zyada alkyl groups (zyada substituted = zyada stable). β-elimination hoti (β-H nikalta).
  • Substitution vs elimination: aqueous KOH → substitution (alcohol), alcoholic KOH → elimination (alkene). Bulky base ya 3° halide → elimination favour. Solvent aur structure dono matter karte.
  • With metals ⭐: Wurtz reaction: 2R−X + 2Na (dry ether) → R−R + 2NaX (symmetrical alkane, chain double). Grignard reagent: R−X + Mg (dry ether) → R−MgX (organomagnesium, bahut useful — further reactions me alcohols/acids banane me). Grignard moisture-sensitive (dry ether zaroori).
  • Reduction: haloalkane → alkane (Zn/HCl, H₂/Ni, ya LiAlH₄ se).

💡 alc. KOH = elimination (Saytzeff — zyada substituted alkene major). aq. KOH = substitution. Wurtz = Na se R−R (chain double). Grignard = Mg se R−MgX (super useful intermediate). Dry ether Grignard ke liye MUST!

6️⃣ Haloarenes aur Polyhalogen Compounds

  • Haloarenes low reactivity ⭐: chlorobenzene waghera nucleophilic substitution me reactive NAHI (normal conditions pe). Kyun? (1) Resonance — halogen ka lone pair ring ke saath conjugate, C−X ko partial double bond character (strong, chhota). (2) sp² carbon haloarene me zyada electronegative, C−X shorter/stronger vs sp³ haloalkane. (3) electron-rich ring nucleophile ko repel karta.
  • Substitution sirf harsh conditions pe: Dow's process (chlorobenzene + NaOH, 623K/300 atm → phenol). Electron-withdrawing group (−NO₂) ortho/para pe ho to substitution aasaan ho jati.
  • Electrophilic substitution ⭐: halogen deactivating lekin ortho/para-directing (resonance se o/p stabilize). Chlorobenzene pe nitration/halogenation o/p positions pe hoti (slowly).
  • Wurtz-Fittig: haloarene + haloalkane + Na (dry ether) → alkylbenzene. Fittig: 2 haloarene + Na → diaryl (biphenyl).
  • Polyhalogen compounds ⭐ (environment): CHCl₃ (chloroform) — anaesthetic tha, ab limited (liver toxic); CHI₃ (iodoform) — antiseptic, yellow ppt test; CCl₄ (carbon tetrachloride) — solvent, ozone-depleting + carcinogenic; DDT — pehla synthetic insecticide, effective lekin non-biodegradable (bio-magnification, ab banned/restricted); Freons (CFCs) — refrigerants/aerosols, ozone layer destroy karte (Montreal Protocol se phase-out).

💡 Haloarene lazy hai nucleophilic substitution me (resonance + sp² C−X strong). Polyhalogens: DDT aur Freons (CFCs) = environment villains (bio-magnification + ozone hole). Iodoform = yellow antiseptic. Names + harms yaad rakho!

1️⃣ वर्गीकरण और नामकरण

  • 1°/2°/3° — C−X वाले कार्बन के पड़ोसी C की संख्या से। एलिलिक/बेंजिलिक क्रियाशील; विनाइलिक/एरिल कम क्रियाशील (अनुनाद)।

💡 एलिलिक/बेंजिलिक = क्रियाशील।

2️⃣ विधियाँ

  • एल्कोहल + HX/PCl₅/SOCl₂ (Darzens सर्वोत्तम)। Finkelstein: NaI→R−I। Swarts: AgF→R−F। Lucas परीक्षण 1°/2°/3° एल्कोहल पहचानता है।

💡 Finkelstein = आयोडाइड, Swarts = फ्लोराइड।

3️⃣ भौतिक गुण

  • क्वथनांक: RI>RBr>RCl>RF। जल में अघुलनशील। सममिति अधिक = गलनांक अधिक।

💡 बड़ा हैलोजन = उच्च क्वथनांक।

4️⃣ SN1 vs SN2 ⭐⭐

  • SN2: एक चरण, 1° तीव्र, विन्यास उलटा, द्वितीय कोटि। SN1: कार्बधनायन बीच में, 3° तीव्र, रेसीमीकरण, प्रथम कोटि। क्रिया-क्रम: SN2 में 1°>3°, SN1 में 3°>1°।

💡 SN2=1° तीव्र, SN1=3° तीव्र।

5️⃣ विलोपन और धातु

  • alc. KOH = विलोपन (Saytzeff — अधिक प्रतिस्थापित ऐल्कीन मुख्य)। Wurtz: 2R−X+2Na→R−R। Grignard: R−X+Mg→R−MgX (शुष्क ईथर)।

💡 Saytzeff = अधिक प्रतिस्थापित ऐल्कीन।

6️⃣ हैलोएरीन और बहुहैलोजन

  • हैलोएरीन नाभिकस्नेही प्रतिस्थापन में कम क्रियाशील (अनुनाद + sp² C−X)। DDT, फ्रेऑन (CFC) = ओज़ोन क्षय + जैव-आवर्धन। आयोडोफ़ॉर्म = पीला antiseptic।

💡 DDT/CFC = पर्यावरण हानि।

1️⃣ Classification & Naming

  • 1°/2°/3° haloalkanes by how many carbons attach to the C−X carbon. Allylic/benzylic are reactive; vinylic/aryl are less reactive (resonance strengthens C−X). IUPAC: halo prefix + parent alkane.

💡 Allylic/benzylic = reactive.

2️⃣ Preparation

  • From alcohols: R−OH + HX/PCl₅/SOCl₂ (Darzens is cleanest). Finkelstein: R−Cl/Br + NaI → R−I. Swarts: AgF → R−F. The Lucas test (ZnCl₂/HCl) distinguishes 1°/2°/3° alcohols.

💡 Finkelstein = iodide, Swarts = fluoride.

3️⃣ Physical Properties

  • Boiling point: RI > RBr > RCl > RF (larger halogen, stronger van der Waals). Insoluble in water. Greater symmetry → higher melting point.

💡 Bigger halogen → higher BP.

4️⃣ SN1 vs SN2 ⭐⭐

  • SN2: one step, backside attack, 1° fastest, inversion of configuration, second-order kinetics. SN1: carbocation intermediate, 3° fastest, racemization, first-order. Reactivity: SN2 1°>3°, SN1 3°>1°; RI>RBr>RCl>RF.

💡 SN2 = 1° fast; SN1 = 3° fast.

5️⃣ Elimination & Metals

  • alc. KOH = elimination (Saytzeff — the more substituted alkene is major). Wurtz: 2R−X + 2Na → R−R. Grignard: R−X + Mg → R−MgX (dry ether). Reduction gives alkanes.

💡 Saytzeff: most-substituted alkene.

6️⃣ Haloarenes & Polyhalogen

  • Haloarenes resist nucleophilic substitution (resonance + stronger sp² C−X). Electrophilic substitution is o/p-directing but slow. DDT & Freons (CFCs) damage ozone / bio-magnify. Iodoform is a yellow antiseptic.

💡 DDT/CFCs harm the environment.

JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Primary, secondary aur tertiary haloalkane me farak kaise?
Answer dekho

C−X wale carbon ke padosi carbon gino: 1° = 1 C se juda, 2° = 2 C se, 3° = 3 C se. Yeh SN1/SN2 reactivity decide karta.

Q2 Finkelstein aur Swarts reaction batao.
Answer dekho

Finkelstein: R−Cl/Br + NaI (acetone) → R−I. Swarts: R−Cl/Br + AgF/SbF₃ → R−F.

Q3 Same alkyl group ke liye boiling point ka order kyun?
Answer dekho

RI > RBr > RCl > RF — bade halogen ka zyada mass/size → stronger van der Waals forces.

Q4 SN2 mechanism ki do pehchan.
Answer dekho

Ek step (backside attack), second order kinetics, inversion of configuration, 1° haloalkane fastest.

Q5 SN1 mechanism ki do pehchan.
Answer dekho

Carbocation intermediate, first order kinetics, racemization, 3° haloalkane fastest.

Q6 SN1 aur SN2 me reactivity order ka farak.
Answer dekho

SN2: 1° > 2° > 3° (kam steric). SN1: 3° > 2° > 1° (stable carbocation).

Q7 Saytzeff rule kya kehta hai?
Answer dekho

Elimination me woh alkene major product jisme double bond pe zyada alkyl substitution (zyada substituted = zyada stable).

Q8 Grignard reagent kaise banta aur kyun important?
Answer dekho

R−X + Mg (dry ether) → R−MgX. Bahut useful intermediate — alcohols, acids waghera banane me. Moisture-sensitive (dry ether zaroori).

Q9 Haloarenes nucleophilic substitution me kyun slow?
Answer dekho

Resonance se C−X ko partial double-bond character (strong/short) + sp² carbon zyada electronegative + electron-rich ring nucleophile ko repel karta.

Q10 DDT aur Freons (CFCs) ko environment ke liye kyun kharab mana jata hai?
Answer dekho

DDT: non-biodegradable, bio-magnification (food chain me badhta). CFCs: ozone layer ko destroy karte (Montreal Protocol se phase-out).
