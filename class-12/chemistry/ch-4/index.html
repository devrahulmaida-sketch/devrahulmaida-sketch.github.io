The d- and f-Block Elements

d- और f-ब्लॉक तत्व

Transition metals — variable oxidation states, colour, magnetism, KMnO₄, K₂Cr₂O₇, f-block

Notes language:

1️⃣ d-Block — Transition Metals Ka Ghar

  • d-block elements: jinka last electron d-orbital me jata (groups 3-12). Chaar series: 3d (Sc-Zn), 4d (Y-Cd), 5d (La, Hf-Hg), 6d (incomplete).
  • Transition elements ⭐: woh d-block elements jinke atom ya common ion me incompletely filled d-orbital ho (d¹-d⁹). Zn, Cd, Hg transition NAHI hain (d¹⁰ full) — typical transition properties nahi dikhate.
  • Electronic configuration ⭐: general (n−1)d¹⁻¹⁰ ns⁰⁻². Exceptions yaad rakho: Cr = 3d⁵4s¹ (half-filled stability), Cu = 3d¹⁰4s¹ (full d stability) — expected 3d⁴4s² aur 3d⁹4s² nahi!
  • Ionization: pehle ns electrons nikalte, phir (n−1)d. Isliye Fe²⁺ = 3d⁶ (4s pehle gaya), Fe³⁺ = 3d⁵.

💡 Cr aur Cu ke configuration exceptions = CBSE favourite (3d⁵4s¹ aur 3d¹⁰4s¹). Zn/Cd/Hg = "fake transition" (d¹⁰ full) — yeh trick question me har saal aata hai!

2️⃣ Atomic Size aur Ionization Enthalpy

  • Atomic/ionic radii: series me left se right pehle ghatti (nuclear charge badhta), middle me almost constant, end me thodi badhti. 4d aur 5d series ka size almost same — lanthanoid contraction ki wajah se (4f poor shielding).
  • Ionization enthalpy (IE): left se right generally badhti (size kam, nuclear charge zyada) — lekin irregular (d-electron shielding ki wajah se). IE s-block se zyada lekin p-block se kam.
  • Lanthanoid contraction ⭐: lanthanoids (4f) me atomic size dheere-dheere ghat-ta hai kyunki 4f electrons nuclear charge ko achhi tarah shield nahi karte. Result: Zr aur Hf (4d aur 5d) ka size almost barabar — isliye dono ko alag karna mushkil.

💡 Lanthanoid contraction = 4f ka "kamzor chhatri" (poor shielding) — nuclear charge poora asar karta, size sikudta. Zr≈Hf iska direct result!

3️⃣ Oxidation States — Variable Wala Funda ⭐⭐

  • Variable oxidation states ⭐: transition metals ki pehchaan. Kyunki (n−1)d aur ns electrons ki energy almost barabar — dono bonding me participate kar sakte. Example: Mn (+2 se +7 tak), Fe (+2, +3), Cu (+1, +2), Cr (+2 se +6).
  • Maximum oxidation state: middle of series (Mn) pe sabse zyada — saare ns + (n−1)d electrons use ho sakte. Mn +7 (KMnO₄), Cr +6 (K₂Cr₂O₇) yaad rakho.
  • Standard E° aur stability: Mn²⁺ (3d⁵ half-filled) aur Zn²⁺ (3d¹⁰ full) relatively stable. Fe³⁺ (3d⁵) Fe²⁺ se zyada stable in solution. Cu⁺ unstable in water (disproportionate hoke Cu + Cu²⁺ deta) — par solid me stable.
  • Oxidizing/reducing power: higher oxidation state (jaise MnO₄⁻, Cr₂O₇²⁻) = strong oxidizing agents (electron lena chahte). Lower states = reducing.

💡 Variable states ka raaz: ns aur (n−1)d ki energy ~same, to metal "jitna chaahe utne" electrons de sakta. Half-filled (d⁵) aur full (d¹⁰) = extra stable — Mn²⁺, Fe³⁺, Zn²⁺ yaad rakho!

4️⃣ Coloured Ions aur Magnetic Properties ⭐⭐

  • Coloured ions ⭐: transition metal compounds rangiin hote kyunki d-d transition hoti — partially filled d-orbital me electron visible light absorb karke excite hota, baaki colour dikhta. Cu²⁺ (blue), Ni²⁺ (green), Mn²⁺ (pink), Fe³⁺ (yellow).
  • Colourless: d⁰ (Sc³⁺, Ti⁴⁺) ya d¹⁰ (Zn²⁺, Cu⁺) — d-d transition possible nahi, isliye colourless. yeh "kyun Zn²⁺ colourless" question har saal aata hai!
  • Magnetic property ⭐: paramagnetic = unpaired electrons (magnet ki taraf attract); diamagnetic = sab paired (repel). Magnetic moment μ = √n(n+2) BM (n = unpaired electrons, BM = Bohr magneton). n zyada → zyada paramagnetic.
  • Examples: Fe³⁺ (d⁵, n=5) → μ=√35≈5.92 BM. Zn²⁺ (d¹⁰, n=0) → diamagnetic.

💡 Colour = d-d dance (light absorb, colour emit). d⁰/d¹⁰ = no dance = colourless. μ = √n(n+2) formula rat lo — unpaired electrons gino, BM nikalo!

5️⃣ Catalytic, Interstitial aur Alloys

  • Catalytic property: transition metals aur unke compounds ache catalysts — variable oxidation states + surface pe adsorption. Examples: Fe (Haber process), Ni (hydrogenation), V₂O₅ (Contact process, H₂SO₄), Pt (Ostwald), Pd (Lindlar).
  • Interstitial compounds: chhote atoms (H, C, N, B) metal lattice ke khali spaces (interstices) me ghus jate — hard, high melting, metallic conductivity. Example: steel (Fe me C), TiC. Non-stoichiometric (na fixed ratio) hote.
  • Alloy formation ⭐: transition metals aasani se alloys banate kyunki unke atomic sizes ~similar (ek dusre ko lattice me replace kar sakte). Examples: brass (Cu+Zn), bronze (Cu+Sn), stainless steel (Fe+Cr+Ni).

💡 Catalyst = variable states + surface. Interstitial = "chhote mehmaan andar ghus gaye" (steel hard isliye). Alloy = "size same to ghar share" (brass, bronze)!

6️⃣ Important Compounds — KMnO₄ aur K₂Cr₂O₇ ⭐⭐

  • Potassium permanganate (KMnO₄) ⭐: dark purple crystals, strong oxidizing agent. Acidic medium me: MnO₄⁻ (purple, +7) → Mn²⁺ (colourless, +2), 5e⁻ gain. Neutral/alkaline me: → MnO₂ (brown, +4). Strong alkaline me: → manganate MnO₄²⁻ (green, +6).
  • KMnO₄ reactions ⭐: acidic me Fe²⁺ → Fe³⁺, oxalate → CO₂, SO₂ → SO₄²⁻, H₂S → S oxidize karta. Self-indicator (purple colour khud disappear hota) — titration me alag indicator nahi chahiye.
  • Potassium dichromate (K₂Cr₂O₇) ⭐: orange crystals, strong oxidizing agent. Acidic me: Cr₂O₇²⁻ (orange, +6) → Cr³⁺ (green, +3), 6e⁻ gain (3 per Cr). Cr₂O₇²⁻ + 2OH⁻ → 2CrO₄²⁻ (yellow) + H₂O — alkaline me chromate (yellow) ban jata.
  • Structure: dichromate me 2 CrO₄ tetrahedra ek corner share karte. pH change se dichromate ↔ chromate (orange ↔ yellow) interconvert — colour change test me kaam aata.

💡 KMnO₄ = "purple oxidizer" (acidic me colourless Mn²⁺, self-indicator). K₂Cr₂O₇ = "orange oxidizer" (acidic me green Cr³⁺). Dono ke colour changes + medium-wise products = guaranteed numerical/reaction question!

7️⃣ Lanthanoids aur Actinoids — f-Block

  • f-block (inner transition): last electron f-orbital me. Do series: lanthanoids (Ce-Lu, 4f) aur actinoids (Th-Lr, 5f). Alag niche do rows me rakhe (table compact rakhne ke liye).
  • Lanthanoids ⭐: configuration 4f¹⁻¹⁴ 5d⁰⁻¹ 6s². Common oxidation state +3 (most stable); kuch +2 (Eu) aur +4 (Ce) bhi. Lanthanoid contraction (4f poor shielding) — size dheere ghat-ta. Mostly colourless/coloured ions, paramagnetic. Use: misch metal (lighters), Nd lasers.
  • Actinoids: configuration 5f¹⁻¹⁴ 6d⁰⁻¹ 7s². Zyada variable oxidation states (lanthanoids se) kyunki 5f, 6d, 7s energies aur bhi close. Saare radioactive. Actinoid contraction lanthanoid se zyada. Example: U (+3 se +6), Pu.
  • Lanthanoid vs actinoid ⭐: lanthanoids less reactive, +3 dominant, non-radioactive (mostly); actinoids more reactive, variable states, sab radioactive.

💡 Lanthanoids = "+3 wale, lanthanoid contraction ka ghar". Actinoids = "radioactive, zyada oxidation states". Dono f-block, dono contraction dikhate!

1️⃣ d-ब्लॉक और विन्यास

  • संक्रमण तत्व: अपूर्ण d-कक्षक वाले। (n−1)d¹⁻¹⁰ ns⁰⁻²। अपवाद: Cr=3d⁵4s¹, Cu=3d¹⁰4s¹। Zn/Cd/Hg संक्रमण नहीं (d¹⁰)।

💡 Cr, Cu अपवाद याद रखें।

2️⃣ आकार और आयनन एन्थैल्पी

  • त्रिज्या बाएँ→दाएँ घटती; लैन्थनॉयड संकुचन (4f दुर्बल परिरक्षण) से 4d≈5d आकार (Zr≈Hf)। IE सामान्यतः बढ़ती।

💡 लैन्थनॉयड संकुचन से Zr≈Hf।

3️⃣ ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ ⭐⭐

  • परिवर्ती ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ (ns और (n−1)d ऊर्जा ~समान)। Mn +2 से +7। d⁵ (Mn²⁺, Fe³⁺) और d¹⁰ (Zn²⁺) स्थायी।

💡 अर्ध-पूर्ण/पूर्ण d = स्थायी।

4️⃣ रंगीन आयन और चुंबकत्व ⭐⭐

  • d-d संक्रमण से रंग (Cu²⁺ नीला)। d⁰/d¹⁰ रंगहीन (Zn²⁺)। μ=√n(n+2) BM; अयुग्मित e⁻ = अनुचुंबकीय।

💡 d⁰/d¹⁰ = रंगहीन।

5️⃣ उत्प्रेरक, अंतराकाशी, मिश्रधातु

  • उत्प्रेरक: Fe (Haber), V₂O₅ (Contact)। अंतराकाशी (C/H/N रिक्तियों में — इस्पात)। मिश्रधातु: पीतल (Cu+Zn), स्टील।

💡 समान आकार से मिश्रधातु आसान।

6️⃣ KMnO₄ और K₂Cr₂O₇ ⭐⭐

  • KMnO₄: बैंगनी, प्रबल ऑक्सीकारक; अम्लीय में Mn²⁺ (रंगहीन), स्व-सूचक। K₂Cr₂O₇: नारंगी; अम्लीय में Cr³⁺ (हरा)। pH से dichromate↔chromate।

💡 दोनों प्रबल ऑक्सीकारक।

7️⃣ लैन्थनॉयड और ऐक्टिनॉयड

  • लैन्थनॉयड (4f): +3 मुख्य, संकुचन। ऐक्टिनॉयड (5f): परिवर्ती अवस्थाएँ, सभी रेडियोधर्मी।

💡 लैन्थनॉयड +3, ऐक्टिनॉयड रेडियोधर्मी।

1️⃣ d-Block & Configuration

  • Transition elements: incompletely filled d-orbital in atom/common ion. General config (n−1)d¹⁻¹⁰ ns⁰⁻². Exceptions: Cr=3d⁵4s¹, Cu=3d¹⁰4s¹. Zn/Cd/Hg are NOT transition (d¹⁰).

💡 Remember the Cr and Cu exceptions.

2️⃣ Size & Ionization Enthalpy

  • Radius decreases left→right; lanthanoid contraction (poor 4f shielding) makes 4d≈5d sizes (Zr≈Hf). IE generally increases across.

💡 Lanthanoid contraction → Zr≈Hf.

3️⃣ Oxidation States ⭐⭐

  • Variable oxidation states because ns and (n−1)d have similar energies. Mn ranges +2 to +7. d⁵ (Mn²⁺, Fe³⁺) and d¹⁰ (Zn²⁺) are extra stable. High states (MnO₄⁻, Cr₂O₇²⁻) are strong oxidizers.

💡 Half/full d subshells are stable.

4️⃣ Colour & Magnetism ⭐⭐

  • d-d transitions give colour (Cu²⁺ blue, Ni²⁺ green). d⁰/d¹⁰ ions are colourless (Zn²⁺). Magnetic moment μ=√n(n+2) BM; unpaired electrons → paramagnetic.

💡 d⁰/d¹⁰ = colourless.

5️⃣ Catalysis, Interstitial & Alloys

  • Catalysts: Fe (Haber), V₂O₅ (Contact), Ni (hydrogenation). Interstitial compounds (small atoms in voids — steel). Alloys form easily (similar sizes): brass, bronze, stainless steel.

💡 Similar size → easy alloys.

6️⃣ KMnO₄ & K₂Cr₂O₇ ⭐⭐

  • KMnO₄: purple, strong oxidizer; in acid → colourless Mn²⁺ (self-indicator), in neutral/alkaline → MnO₂. K₂Cr₂O₇: orange; in acid → green Cr³⁺. Dichromate↔chromate with pH (orange↔yellow).

💡 Both are strong oxidizers.

7️⃣ Lanthanoids & Actinoids

  • Lanthanoids (4f): +3 dominant, lanthanoid contraction. Actinoids (5f): more variable states, all radioactive, greater contraction.

💡 Lanthanoids +3; actinoids radioactive.

JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Transition elements kaunse hote? Zn kyun nahi?
Answer dekho

Jinke atom ya common ion me incompletely filled d-orbital ho. Zn (d¹⁰) full hai — isliye transition nahi.

Q2 Cr aur Cu ke electronic configuration likho.
Answer dekho

Cr = [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹ (half-filled stability), Cu = [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s¹ (full d stability) — expected se different.

Q3 Transition metals variable oxidation states kyun dikhate?
Answer dekho

Kyunki ns aur (n−1)d electrons ki energy lagbhag same — dono bonding me use ho sakte.

Q4 Zn²⁺ colourless kyun hai jabki Cu²⁺ coloured?
Answer dekho

Zn²⁺ = 3d¹⁰ (d-d transition possible nahi) → colourless. Cu²⁺ = 3d⁹ → d-d transition → blue.

Q5 Magnetic moment ka formula aur paramagnetic ka matlab.
Answer dekho

μ = √n(n+2) BM (n = unpaired electrons). Unpaired e⁻ hone pe paramagnetic (magnet ki taraf attract).

Q6 Lanthanoid contraction kya hai aur ek result?
Answer dekho

4f poor shielding se lanthanoids ka size dheere-dheere ghat-ta. Result: Zr aur Hf ka size almost barabar (4d≈5d).

Q7 Acidic medium me KMnO₄ ka product aur colour change.
Answer dekho

MnO₄⁻ (purple, +7) → Mn²⁺ (colourless, +2). Yeh self-indicator hai — alag indicator nahi chahiye.

Q8 Acidic medium me K₂Cr₂O₇ ka product.
Answer dekho

Cr₂O₇²⁻ (orange, +6) → Cr³⁺ (green, +3).

Q9 Interstitial compound kya hota? Example.
Answer dekho

Chhote atoms (C, H, N) metal lattice ki voids/interstices me ghus jate — hard, high melting. Example: steel (Fe me C).

Q10 Lanthanoids aur actinoids me do differences.
Answer dekho

Lanthanoids: +3 dominant, non-radioactive. Actinoids: zyada variable oxidation states, sab radioactive.
