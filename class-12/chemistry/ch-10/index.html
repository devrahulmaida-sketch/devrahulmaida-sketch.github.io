🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Carbohydrates — Classification

  • Biomolecules: living organisms ke complex organic compounds — carbohydrates, proteins, nucleic acids, lipids, vitamins, hormones. Yeh life ke building blocks aur fuel hain.
  • Carbohydrates: general formula Cₓ(H₂O)ᵧ ("carbon ka hydrate"). Actually polyhydroxy aldehydes/ketones ya unke derivatives. Sweet nahi bhi ho sakte (starch).
  • Classification ⭐: hydrolysis ke basis pe — Monosaccharides (hydrolyze NAHI hote, single unit: glucose, fructose); Oligosaccharides (2-10 units: disaccharides sucrose, maltose, lactose); Polysaccharides (bahut saare units: starch, cellulose, glycogen).
  • Reducing vs non-reducing ⭐: reducing sugars free aldehyde/ketone group rakhte (hemiacetal se free ho sakta) — Tollens'/Fehling's test POSITIVE dete (glucose, fructose, maltose, lactose). Non-reducing (sucrose) — dono anomeric carbons glycosidic bond me busy, free carbonyl nahi → test negative.
  • Aldoses vs ketoses: aldehyde wala monosaccharide = aldose (glucose); ketone wala = ketose (fructose). Carbon count se: triose (3C), tetrose, pentose (5C, ribose), hexose (6C, glucose/fructose).

💡 Mono = 1 unit (glucose), Di = 2 (sucrose), Poly = bahut (starch). Reducing sugar = free carbonyl (Tollens +). Sucrose = NON-reducing (dono anomeric C busy). Glucose = aldose, fructose = ketose!

2️⃣ Glucose — Structure aur Reactions ⭐⭐

  • Glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆): sabse common monosaccharide, aldohexose. Open-chain (Fischer) structure: ek aldehyde (−CHO) + 5 −OH groups (C1 pe CHO, C2-C5 pe OH, C6 pe CH₂OH).
  • Open-chain ke proofs ⭐: (1) HI/red P (heat) → n-hexane (straight 6-C chain). (2) Br₂ water → gluconic acid (—CHO oxidize hota, aldehyde present). (3) HNO₃ → saccharic acid (—CHO aur —CH₂OH dono oxidize). (4) acetic anhydride → pentaacetate (5 −OH). (5) HCN → cyanohydrin (carbonyl present).
  • Cyclic structure ⭐: glucose actually ring me rehta — C1 ka −CHO C5 ke −OH se react karke 6-membered ring (pyranose) banata. Naya chiral centre (anomeric carbon, C1) banta — do forms: α-glucose (C1 OH neeche) aur β-glucose (C1 OH upar). Dono anomers hain. Open chain aur cyclic equilibrium me — isliye glucose Tollens/Fehling positive deta (thoda open-chain form carbonyl dikhata).
  • Fructose (C₆H₁₂O₆): ketohexose — C2 pe ketone. Glucose ka isomer. Cyclic me 5-membered ring (furanose). Sabse sweet natural sugar.

💡 Glucose = aldohexose, open chain me CHO + 5OH. Br₂ water → gluconic acid (aldehyde proof). Cyclic = pyranose ring, α/β anomers (C1 OH neeche/upar). Fructose = ketose, furanose ring. Structure reactions yaad rakho!

3️⃣ Disaccharides aur Polysaccharides ⭐

  • Glycosidic linkage: do monosaccharides anomeric carbon pe −O− bond se jude (condensation, H₂O nikalta). Yeh disaccharides/polysaccharides ka jodne ka tarika.
  • Sucrose ⭐: glucose + fructose, dono ke anomeric carbons (C1 glucose, C2 fructose) linked → non-reducing. Hydrolysis (invertase/H⁺) se glucose + fructose (invert sugar — optical rotation invert hota). Table sugar yahi hai.
  • Maltose: 2 glucose units, α(1→4) linkage. Ek free anomeric carbon → reducing. Starch digestion ka product. Lactose: glucose + galactose (β(1→4)), milk sugar, reducing.
  • Starch ⭐: glucose ka polymer, energy storage (plants). Do parts — amylose (linear, α(1→4), ~15-20%, iodine ke saath blue colour — starch ka test) aur amylopectin (branched, α(1→4) + α(1→6) branches).
  • Cellulose ⭐: glucose ka polymer, β(1→4) linkage, straight chains — structural (cell wall, cotton). Humans digest nahi kar sakte (β-linkage todne ka enzyme nahi); cows/termites kar sakte (gut bacteria). Glycogen: animals ka starch (energy store, liver/muscles), zyada branched.

💡 Sucrose = glucose+fructose, non-reducing. Starch = α-glucose (amylose iodine se BLUE — test). Cellulose = β-glucose (digest nahi hota humans ko). Glycogen = animal starch. α vs β linkage = starch vs cellulose ka farak!

4️⃣ Proteins aur Amino Acids ⭐⭐

  • Amino acids: proteins ke building blocks — ek carbon (α-C) pe amino (−NH₂) + carboxyl (−COOH) + H + side chain (R). ~20 standard amino acids. Essential amino acids ⭐: body nahi bana sakti, diet se lena padta (jaise valine, leucine, lysine). Non-essential: body bana leti.
  • Zwitterion ⭐: amino acid me −NH₂ proton le leta (−NH₃⁺) aur −COOH proton de deta (−COO⁻) — same molecule me + aur − charge = zwitterion (dipolar ion). Isliye amino acids high melting, water-soluble, amphoteric (acid aur base dono se react).
  • Peptide bond ⭐: ek amino acid ka −COOH dusre ke −NH₂ se jude, −CO−NH− bond (amide linkage), H₂O nikalta. Bahut amino acids peptide bonds se jude = polypeptide/protein.
  • Protein structure ⭐ (4 levels): Primary = amino acids ka sequence (peptide bonds). Secondary = folding — α-helix (spring) ya β-pleated sheet, hydrogen bonds se. Tertiary = 3D shape (side chains interact — disulphide bonds, H-bonds). Quaternary = multiple polypeptide chains ek saath (jaise haemoglobin).
  • Denaturation ⭐: heat, acid, agitation se protein ki 2°/3° structure toot jati (1° intact rehti) — protein apni biological activity kho deta. Example: anda ubalna (egg white coagulate), dahi jamna (milk curdling). Usually irreversible.
  • Enzymes: protein nature ke biological catalysts — highly specific (lock-and-key), mild conditions pe kaam karte (body temp, pH). Example: amylase (starch digest), pepsin (protein digest).

💡 Amino acid = α-C pe NH₂+COOH+R. Zwitterion = + aur − charge ek saath. Peptide bond = −CO−NH−. Structure: 1° sequence, 2° helix/sheet, 3° 3D, 4° multi-chain. Denaturation = 2°/3° tootta (anda ubalna). Enzymes = protein catalysts!

5️⃣ Nucleic Acids, Vitamins aur Hormones

  • Nucleic acids: genetic information store/transmit — DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid, heredity/blueprint) aur RNA (ribonucleic acid, protein synthesis).
  • Nucleotide ⭐: nucleic acid ka unit — 3 parts: pentose sugar + phosphate + nitrogenous base. DNA me sugar = deoxyribose, bases = A, T, G, C. RNA me sugar = ribose, bases = A, U, G, C (T ki jagah U — yeh key difference!).
  • DNA double helix ⭐ (Watson-Crick): do strands antiparallel, bases andar pair hote — A=T (2 H-bonds), G≡C (3 H-bonds) (complementary base pairing). Sugar-phosphate bahar (backbone). Strands H-bonds se jude. DNA replication me ye pairing copy banata.
  • Vitamins ⭐: chhoti amounts me zaroori organic compounds (body mostly nahi bana sakti). Water-soluble: B-complex, C (excess urine me nikal jata, store nahi hota). Fat-soluble: A, D, E, K (fat me store, excess toxic ho sakta). Deficiency se diseases: C → scurvy, D → rickets, A → night blindness, B1 → beri-beri, K → bleeding.
  • Hormones: endocrine glands ke chemical messengers (blood se target tak signal). Example: insulin (blood sugar control), adrenaline (fight-or-flight), thyroxine (metabolism).

💡 Nucleotide = sugar+phosphate+base. DNA: deoxyribose + T; RNA: ribose + U. Base pairing: A=T (2 bonds), G≡C (3 bonds). Water-soluble vitamins (B,C) store nahi hote; fat-soluble (A,D,E,K) store. Deficiency diseases yaad rakho!

1️⃣ कार्बोहाइड्रेट वर्गीकरण

  • मोनोसैकराइड (ग्लूकोज़), डाइसैकराइड (सुक्रोज़), पॉलीसैकराइड (स्टार्च)। अपचायी शर्करा = मुक्त कार्बोनिल (Tollens +)। सुक्रोज़ अनपचायी। ग्लूकोज़ = aldose, फ्रक्टोज़ = ketose।

💡 सुक्रोज़ = अनपचायी।

2️⃣ ग्लूकोज़ संरचना ⭐

  • एल्डोहेक्सोज़ — CHO + 5 OH। Br₂ जल → ग्लूकोनिक अम्ल (ऐल्डिहाइड प्रमाण)। चक्रीय pyranose, α/β anomers (C1 OH)। फ्रक्टोज़ = ketose (furanose)।

💡 Br₂ जल = CHO प्रमाण।

3️⃣ डाइ/पॉली सैकराइड ⭐

  • स्टार्च = α-ग्लूकोज़ (amylose आयोडीन से नीला — परीक्षण)। सेल्यूलोज़ = β-ग्लूकोज़ (मनुष्य पाचन नहीं)। ग्लाइकोजेन = प्राणी स्टार्च।

💡 स्टार्च + आयोडीन = नीला।

4️⃣ प्रोटीन और ऐमीनो अम्ल ⭐

  • ऐमीनो अम्ल = α-C पर NH₂+COOH+R; zwitterion। पेप्टाइड बंध −CO−NH−। संरचना: 1° अनुक्रम, 2° हेलिक्स/शीट, 3° त्रिविम, 4° बहु-शृंखला। विकृतीकरण (अंडा उबालना)। एंज़ाइम = प्रोटीन उत्प्रेरक।

💡 पेप्टाइड बंध −CO−NH−।

5️⃣ न्यूक्लिक अम्ल और विटामिन

  • न्यूक्लियोटाइड = शर्करा+फॉस्फेट+क्षार। DNA: डिऑक्सीराइबोज़ + T; RNA: राइबोज़ + U। A=T(2), G≡C(3)। विटामिन B,C जल-घुलनशील; A,D,E,K वसा-घुलनशील।

💡 DNA में T, RNA में U।

1️⃣ Carbohydrate Classification

  • Monosaccharides (glucose), disaccharides (sucrose), polysaccharides (starch). Reducing sugars have a free carbonyl (Tollens/Fehling +). Sucrose is non-reducing. Glucose = aldose, fructose = ketose.

💡 Sucrose is non-reducing.

2️⃣ Glucose Structure ⭐

  • An aldohexose (CHO + 5 OH). Br₂ water → gluconic acid (proves the aldehyde). Forms a cyclic pyranose ring with α/β anomers at C1. Fructose is a ketose (furanose ring).

💡 Br₂ water proves the CHO group.

3️⃣ Di-/Polysaccharides ⭐

  • Starch = α-glucose (amylose turns blue with iodine — the test). Cellulose = β-glucose (humans can't digest). Glycogen = animal starch. α vs β linkage distinguishes starch from cellulose.

💡 Starch + iodine → blue.

4️⃣ Proteins & Amino Acids ⭐

  • Amino acid = α-C with NH₂+COOH+R; exists as a zwitterion. Peptide bond −CO−NH−. Structure: 1° sequence, 2° helix/sheet, 3° 3D fold, 4° multi-chain. Denaturation (boiling an egg). Enzymes are protein catalysts.

💡 Peptide bond = −CO−NH−.

5️⃣ Nucleic Acids & Vitamins

  • Nucleotide = sugar+phosphate+base. DNA: deoxyribose + A,T,G,C; RNA: ribose + A,U,G,C. Base pairing A=T (2), G≡C (3). Vitamins B,C water-soluble; A,D,E,K fat-soluble (deficiency: C→scurvy, D→rickets).

💡 DNA has T, RNA has U.

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Reducing aur non-reducing sugar me farak? Example.
Answer dekho

Reducing: free aldehyde/ketone group → Tollens'/Fehling's POSITIVE (glucose, maltose). Non-reducing: dono anomeric carbons glycosidic bond me busy → negative (sucrose).

Q2. Glucose ke open-chain me aldehyde group ka proof?
Answer dekho

Br₂ water → gluconic acid (—CHO oxidize hoke —COOH banta). Br₂ ka colour disappear hota — yeh aldehyde group ka proof hai.

Q3. α-glucose aur β-glucose me farak?
Answer dekho

Anomers hain — cyclic structure me C1 (anomeric carbon) pe −OH ki position: α me neeche, β me upar. Dono pyranose ring.

Q4. Starch aur cellulose dono glucose polymers hain, to farak kya?
Answer dekho

Starch = α(1→4) glucose (energy storage, digest hota, iodine se BLUE). Cellulose = β(1→4) glucose (structural, humans digest nahi kar sakte — β enzyme nahi).

Q5. Amino acid ka zwitterion kya hota?
Answer dekho

Same molecule me −NH₃⁺ (positive) aur −COO⁻ (negative) dono charge — dipolar ion. Isliye amino acids high melting, water-soluble, amphoteric.

Q6. Peptide bond kaise banta hai?
Answer dekho

Ek amino acid ka −COOH dusre ke −NH₂ se jude, H₂O nikalke −CO−NH− (amide) bond banta. Bahut amino acids = polypeptide/protein.

Q7. Protein denaturation kya hai? Example.
Answer dekho

Heat/acid se protein ki 2°/3° structure toot-ti (1° intact), biological activity kho jata. Example: anda ubalna (egg white coagulate), dahi jamna. Usually irreversible.

Q8. DNA aur RNA me do differences.
Answer dekho

Sugar: DNA me deoxyribose, RNA me ribose. Base: DNA me Thymine (T), RNA me Uracil (U). DNA double helix, RNA usually single-stranded.

Q9. Complementary base pairing ka rule?
Answer dekho

DNA double helix me A = T (2 hydrogen bonds) aur G ≡ C (3 hydrogen bonds). Yeh Watson-Crick pairing hai.

Q10. Water-soluble aur fat-soluble vitamins kaunse?
Answer dekho

Water-soluble: B-complex, C (store nahi hote, excess urine me). Fat-soluble: A, D, E, K (fat me store, excess toxic). Deficiency: C→scurvy, D→rickets, A→night blindness.
