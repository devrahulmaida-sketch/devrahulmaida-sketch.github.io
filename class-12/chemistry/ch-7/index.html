Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

ऐल्कोहल, फ़ीनॉल और ईथर

Classification, preparation, H-bonding, oxidation, phenol acidity, Williamson synthesis

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Classification aur Nomenclature

  • Alcohols: −OH group sp³ carbon pe (R−OH). Phenols: −OH directly benzene ring pe (C₆H₅−OH). Ethers: oxygen do alkyl/aryl groups ke beech (R−O−R′).
  • −OH ki ginti se: monohydric (1 −OH, ethanol), dihydric (2, ethylene glycol), trihydric (3, glycerol).
  • Alcohols ka type ⭐: −OH wale carbon ke hisaab se — 1° (CH₃CH₂OH, −OH wala C sirf 1 C se juda), 2° ((CH₃)₂CHOH), 3° ((CH₃)₃COH). Reactivity aur oxidation products isse decide hote!
  • Ethers: symmetrical (simple, C₂H₅−O−C₂H₅) ya unsymmetrical (mixed, CH₃−O−C₂H₅).
  • IUPAC naming: alcohol = alkane + −ol (ethanol, propan-2-ol). Phenol = hydroxybenzene/phenol. Ether = alkoxy + alkane (methoxyethane) ya common (diethyl ether).

💡 1°/2°/3° = −OH wale C ke neighbours. Phenol me −OH seedha ring pe (isliye zyada acidic). Ether = O "bridge" do groups ke beech. Naming: alkane+ol, alkoxy+alkane!

2️⃣ Preparation — Alcohols aur Phenols Kaise Banate

  • Alcohols from alkenes ⭐: (a) acid-catalysed hydration (alkene + H₂O/H⁺, Markovnikov — OH zyada substituted C pe). (b) Hydroboration-oxidation: alkene + BH₃ phir H₂O₂/OH⁻ → anti-Markovnikov alcohol (OH kam substituted C pe). Dono complementary!
  • From carbonyl (reduction): aldehyde → 1° alcohol, ketone → 2° alcohol (NaBH₄ ya LiAlH₄ se reduce). From Grignard ⭐: RMgX + formaldehyde (HCHO) → 1° alcohol; + any other aldehyde → 2°; + ketone → 3° (phir hydrolysis). Grignard se 1°/2°/3° sab ban sakte!
  • Phenols ⭐: (a) Dow's process — chlorobenzene + NaOH (623 K, 300 atm) → phenol. (b) From cumene (industrial main): benzene + propene → cumene → air oxidation → cumene hydroperoxide → acid → phenol + acetone (dono useful products!). (c) diazonium salt hydrolysis, (d) benzene sulphonic acid + NaOH fusion.

💡 Hydration = Markovnikov, Hydroboration = anti-Markovnikov. Grignard: HCHO→1°, aldehyde→2°, ketone→3°. Phenol industrial = cumene process (phenol + acetone dono milte)!

3️⃣ Physical Properties — H-Bonding Ka Khel

  • Boiling point ⭐: alcohols ka BP comparable alkanes/ethers se bahut zyada — intermolecular hydrogen bonding ki wajah se (−OH...O). Molecules jud ke rehte, zyada energy chahiye alag karne. Isomers me branching badhne pe BP ghatta.
  • Solubility: chhote alcohols (methanol, ethanol, propanol) pani me fully miscible (H-bond banate pani ke saath). Carbon chain badhne pe solubility ghatti (hydrophobic part badhta). Phenols thodi soluble.
  • Phenols: BP ethers/arenes se zyada (H-bonding), pani me partially soluble. Ethers: BP alcohols se kam (khud H-bond donor nahi), lekin comparable alcohols jitna pani me soluble (O acceptor ban sakta).

💡 Alcohol ka BP zyada = H-bonding ("molecules hath pakad ke baithe"). Chhote alcohols pani me ghulte (H-bond with water). Ethers ka BP kam (no O−H). H-bonding = physical properties ka boss!

4️⃣ Alcohols ke Chemical Reactions ⭐⭐

  • Acidity: alcohols weakly acidic (Na se H₂ dete: 2R−OH + 2Na → 2R−ONa + H₂). Lekin phenols se kam acidic — phenoxide ion resonance se stable, alkoxide nahi. Order: phenol > water > alcohol. Electron-withdrawing group acidity badhata, electron-donating ghatata.
  • Esterification ⭐: alcohol + carboxylic acid (conc. H₂SO₄) → ester + H₂O (Fischer esterification). Acyl chloride/acid anhydride se bhi ester banta (alcohols aur phenols dono ka test/derivative).
  • Dehydration ⭐: alcohol + conc. H₂SO₄ (heat) → alkene (Saytzeff — zyada substituted major). Ease: 3° > 2° > 1° (carbocation stability). 443 K pe alkene, 413 K pe excess alcohol → ether.
  • Oxidation ⭐: 1° alcohol → aldehyde → carboxylic acid; 2° alcohol → ketone; 3° alcohol → oxidation nahi (no α-H, sirf drastic conditions pe C−C toot-ta). Mild oxidant (PCC) 1° ko aldehyde pe rokta; strong (KMnO₄/H⁺) acid tak le jata.
  • Lucas test ⭐: ZnCl₂ + HCl se 1°/2°/3° distinguish — 3° instant turbidity (cloudy), 2° ~5 min, 1° room temp pe nahi (heat chahiye).

💡 Phenol zyada acidic (resonance-stabilized phenoxide). Dehydration: 3°>2°>1°, Saytzeff alkene. Oxidation: 1°→acid, 2°→ketone, 3°→NO. Lucas test = turbidity timing. Ye reactions organic conversions me har jagah!

5️⃣ Phenols ke Reactions — Acidic + Ring

  • Phenols acidic ⭐: phenol alcohols se zyada acidic kyunki phenoxide ion resonance-stabilized hai (negative charge ring me delocalize). Phenol NaOH se react karta (salt banta); alcohol nahi karta. Lekin carboxylic acids se kam acidic — NaHCO₃ (carbonate) se react NAHI karta (CO₂ nahi deta), isse carboxylic acid se distinguish hota.
  • Electrophilic substitution ⭐: −OH activating, ortho/para-directing (lone pair ring ko electron-rich banata). Nitration: dilute HNO₃ → o- aur p-nitrophenol (steam distillation se separate, ortho intramolecular H-bond = volatile). Halogenation: Br₂ water → 2,4,6-tribromophenol (white ppt) — phenol ka test!
  • Reimer-Tiemann reaction ⭐: phenol + CHCl₃ + NaOH, phir acid → salicylaldehyde (ortho −CHO). Kolbe's reaction ⭐: sodium phenoxide + CO₂ (heat, pressure), phir acid → salicylic acid (aspirin ka starting). Dono named reactions exam favourites!

💡 Phenol acidic (phenoxide stable), lekin NaHCO₃ se CO₂ nahi (carboxylic se kamzor). −OH = o/p director. Br₂ water → white ppt (test). Reimer-Tiemann = −CHO (salicylaldehyde), Kolbe = −COOH (salicylic acid) — naam rat lo!

6️⃣ Ethers — Preparation aur Cleavage

  • Williamson synthesis ⭐: sodium alkoxide + haloalkane (SN2) → ether. R−ONa + R′−X → R−O−R′ + NaX. Unsymmetrical ether ke liye chhota/1° halide lo (bada halide SN2 slow karta, elimination ho jata). Phenol se bhi (sodium phenoxide + R−X).
  • Dehydration of alcohols: excess alcohol + conc. H₂SO₄ (413 K) → symmetrical ether (ethanol → diethyl ether).
  • Cleavage by HX ⭐: ether + HX (HI/HBr, heat) → C−O bond tootta. Mixed ether me: chhote alkyl group ko halogen leta (SN2 attack chhote pe). Excess HX me dono alkyl halides bante. Example: R−O−R′ + HI → R−OH + R′−I.
  • Electrophilic substitution (aromatic ethers): anisole (C₆H₅−OCH₃) me −OCH₃ activating, o/p-directing — bromination/nitration o/p pe.

💡 Williamson = alkoxide + haloalkane (SN2, chhota halide lo). HX cleavage: chhote group ko I milta. Ethers relatively inert (isliye Grignard ka solvent = dry ether). −OCH₃ o/p director!

1️⃣ वर्गीकरण और नामकरण

  • ऐल्कोहल R−OH, फ़ीनॉल C₆H₅−OH (ring पर OH), ईथर R−O−R। 1°/2°/3° ऐल्कोहल OH वाले C से। नाम: alkane+ol, alkoxy+alkane।

💡 फ़ीनॉल में OH ring पर।

2️⃣ विधियाँ

  • ऐल्कीन से: जलयोजन (Markovnikov) / हाइड्रोबोरेशन (anti)। Grignard: HCHO→1°, ऐल्डिहाइड→2°, कीटोन→3°। फ़ीनॉल: क्यूमीन प्रक्रम (फ़ीनॉल + एसीटोन)।

💡 Grignard से 1°/2°/3° ऐल्कोहल।

3️⃣ भौतिक गुण

  • ऐल्कोहल का क्वथनांक उच्च (हाइड्रोजन बंध)। छोटे ऐल्कोहल जल-मिश्रणीय। ईथर का क्वथनांक कम।

💡 H-बंध = उच्च क्वथनांक।

4️⃣ ऐल्कोहल अभिक्रियाएँ ⭐

  • एस्टरीकरण (अम्ल + H₂SO₄)। निर्जलीकरण → ऐल्कीन (3°>2°>1°, Saytzeff)। ऑक्सीकरण: 1°→अम्ल, 2°→कीटोन, 3°→नहीं। Lucas परीक्षण समय से।

💡 ऑक्सीकरण: 1°→अम्ल, 3°→नहीं।

5️⃣ फ़ीनॉल ⭐

  • फ़ीनॉल अम्लीय (फ़ीनॉक्साइड अनुनाद-स्थायी) लेकिन NaHCO₃ से CO₂ नहीं। Br₂ जल → श्वेत अवक्षेप (परीक्षण)। Reimer-Tiemann (सैलिसिलऐल्डिहाइड), Kolbe (सैलिसिलिक अम्ल)।

💡 Br₂ जल से श्वेत अवक्षेप = परीक्षण।

6️⃣ ईथर

  • Williamson संश्लेषण: R−ONa + R′−X → R−O−R′ (छोटा हैलाइड)। HX विदारण — छोटे समूह को हैलोजन। ईथर अक्रियाशील (Grignard विलायक)।

💡 Williamson = ऐल्कॉक्साइड + हैलोऐल्केन।

1️⃣ Classification & Naming

  • Alcohols R−OH (sp³ C), phenols C₆H₅−OH (OH on the ring), ethers R−O−R′. Alcohols are 1°/2°/3° by the OH-bearing carbon. Naming: alkane+ol; ether = alkoxyalkane.

💡 Phenol has OH directly on the ring.

2️⃣ Preparation

  • From alkenes: acid-catalysed hydration (Markovnikov) or hydroboration-oxidation (anti-Markovnikov). Grignard: HCHO→1°, aldehyde→2°, ketone→3° alcohol. Phenol: cumene process (gives phenol + acetone).

💡 Grignard gives 1°/2°/3° alcohols.

3️⃣ Physical Properties

  • Alcohols have high boiling points due to hydrogen bonding; small alcohols are water-miscible. Ethers have lower BPs (no O−H donor).

💡 H-bonding → high BP.

4️⃣ Alcohol Reactions ⭐

  • Esterification (acid + conc. H₂SO₄). Dehydration → alkene (3°>2°>1°, Saytzeff). Oxidation: 1°→carboxylic acid, 2°→ketone, 3°→resists. The Lucas test distinguishes 1°/2°/3° by turbidity timing.

💡 Oxidation: 1°→acid, 3°→none.

5️⃣ Phenols ⭐

  • Phenols are acidic (resonance-stabilized phenoxide) but weaker than carboxylic acids (no CO₂ with NaHCO₃). Br₂ water → white ppt (test). Reimer-Tiemann → salicylaldehyde; Kolbe → salicylic acid. −OH is activating, o/p-directing.

💡 Br₂ water → white ppt = phenol test.

6️⃣ Ethers

  • Williamson synthesis: R−ONa + R′−X → R−O−R′ (use a small/1° halide). HX cleavage — the smaller alkyl group takes the halogen. Ethers are relatively inert (used as Grignard solvent).

💡 Williamson = alkoxide + haloalkane.

JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Alcohol, phenol aur ether me farak kya hai?
Answer dekho

Alcohol: −OH sp³ carbon pe (R−OH). Phenol: −OH benzene ring pe (C₆H₅−OH). Ether: O do alkyl/aryl groups ke beech (R−O−R′).

Q2 Grignard reagent se 1°, 2°, 3° alcohol kaise bante?
Answer dekho

RMgX + formaldehyde (HCHO) → 1°; + koi aur aldehyde → 2°; + ketone → 3° alcohol (phir hydrolysis).

Q3 Alcohols ka boiling point alkanes se zyada kyun?
Answer dekho

Intermolecular hydrogen bonding (−OH...O) — molecules jud ke rehte, alag karne me zyada energy lagti.

Q4 Alcohol oxidation me 1°, 2°, 3° ka kya product?
Answer dekho

1° → aldehyde → carboxylic acid; 2° → ketone; 3° → oxidation nahi (no α-H).

Q5 Lucas test se 1°/2°/3° alcohol kaise distinguish?
Answer dekho

ZnCl₂ + HCl: 3° instant turbidity, 2° ~5 min, 1° room temp pe nahi (heat chahiye). Turbidity ka timing dekhna.

Q6 Phenol alcohol se zyada acidic kyun hai?
Answer dekho

Phenol ka phenoxide ion resonance-stabilized hai (negative charge ring me delocalize); alkoxide ion stable nahi.

Q7 Phenol ka chemical test (Br₂ water).
Answer dekho

Phenol + Br₂ water → 2,4,6-tribromophenol (white precipitate) — yeh phenol ka test hai.

Q8 Reimer-Tiemann aur Kolbe reaction batao.
Answer dekho

Reimer-Tiemann: phenol + CHCl₃/NaOH → salicylaldehyde (ortho −CHO). Kolbe: sodium phenoxide + CO₂ → salicylic acid.

Q9 Williamson ether synthesis kya hai?
Answer dekho

Sodium alkoxide + haloalkane (SN2) → ether (R−ONa + R′−X → R−O−R′). Unsymmetrical ke liye chhota/1° halide lo.

Q10 Ether ki HI se cleavage me kya hota?
Answer dekho

C−O bond tootta; chhote alkyl group ko iodine (I) milta (SN2 attack chhote pe). Excess HI me dono alkyl iodides bante.
