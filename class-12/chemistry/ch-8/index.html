Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Carbonyl Group aur Nomenclature

  • Carbonyl group (C=O): carbon oxygen ke saath double bond. Aldehyde: C=O wale carbon pe kam se kam ek H (R−CHO, chain end pe). Ketone: C=O wale carbon pe do alkyl/aryl groups (R−CO−R′, beech me). Carboxylic acid: −COOH (carbonyl + hydroxyl ek hi C pe).
  • Carbonyl carbon sp² hybridized: trigonal planar, ~120° angles. C=O polar (oxygen δ−, carbon δ+) — isliye nucleophile carbon pe attack karta (nucleophilic addition = is chapter ka main theme).
  • IUPAC naming: aldehyde = alkane + −al (ethanal, CH₃CHO); ketone = alkane + −one + position (propanone, butan-2-one); carboxylic acid = alkane + −oic acid (ethanoic acid). Aldehyde ka C hamesha C-1.

💡 Aldehyde = end pe C=O (ek H zaroori), ketone = beech me C=O (dono taraf carbon), acid = −COOH. Naming: −al / −one / −oic acid. C=O polar hai — nucleophile wahan jata!

2️⃣ Preparation — Aldehydes aur Ketones

  • From alcohols: 1° alcohol oxidize → aldehyde (PCC mild se rok lo, strong se acid ban jata); 2° alcohol → ketone. Dehydrogenation (Cu, heat) bhi deta.
  • From alkynes ⭐: alkyne + H₂O (H₂SO₄/HgSO₄) → carbonyl. Acetylene → acetaldehyde; baaki alkynes → ketones (Markovnikov hydration).
  • From acyl chloride ⭐: Rosenmund reduction — RCOCl + H₂ (Pd/BaSO₄, poisoned) → aldehyde (BaSO₄ catalyst poison karke over-reduction rokta). Stephen reduction: RCN + SnCl₂/HCl → imine → hydrolysis → aldehyde.
  • From nitriles/esters: nitrile + Grignard/DIBAL-H → ketone/aldehyde. Etard reaction: toluene + CrO₂Cl₂ → benzaldehyde. Gatterman-Koch: benzene + CO + HCl (AlCl₃) → benzaldehyde.
  • Ketones: Friedel-Crafts acylation — benzene + RCOCl (AlCl₃) → aryl ketone. From alkenes (ozonolysis), from alcohols (2° oxidation).

💡 Rosenmund (acyl chloride → aldehyde, Pd/BaSO₄), alkyne hydration (acetylene→acetaldehyde), Friedel-Crafts acylation (→ ketone) — naam + product yaad rakho. PCC mild = aldehyde pe rokta!

3️⃣ Physical Properties

  • Polar but no H-bond (self): aldehydes/ketones polar (C=O) lekin apne beech hydrogen bond nahi bana sakte (O−H nahi hai). Isliye BP alcohols se kam, alkanes se zyada (dipole-dipole force hai).
  • Solubility: chhote aldehydes/ketones (formaldehyde, acetaldehyde, acetone) pani me soluble (water ke H se H-bond banate — O acceptor). Carbon chain badhne pe solubility ghatti.
  • Carboxylic acids ⭐: high BP — do molecules H-bond se dimer banate (vapour me bhi). Chhote acids pani me soluble. Benzoic acid thodi soluble.

💡 Carbonyl = polar par self H-bond nahi → BP alcohol se kam. Carboxylic acid = dimer banata H-bond se → highest BP. Chhote wale pani me ghulte!

4️⃣ Nucleophilic Addition — Carbonyl Ka Main Reaction ⭐⭐

  • Nucleophilic addition: nucleophile (Nu⁻) carbonyl carbon (δ+) pe attack karta, phir H⁺ oxygen pe. Aldehydes ketones se zyada reactive (kam steric hindrance + kam electron-donating alkyl groups — carbonyl C zyada δ+).
  • Common additions ⭐: + HCN → cyanohydrin (C−C bond banta, chain badhti). + NaHSO₃ → bisulphite addition product (crystalline, purification/test ke liye). + Grignard (RMgX) phir hydrolysis → alcohol (HCHO→1°, aldehyde→2°, ketone→3°). + alcohol (dry HCl) → hemiacetal phir acetal (carbonyl protection ke liye). + NH₃ derivatives (NH₂−Z) — hydroxylamine → oxime, hydrazine → hydrazone, 2,4-DNP → orange/yellow ppt (test for carbonyl!).
  • Reactivity order ⭐: HCHO > RCHO > R₂CO (aldehyde > ketone). Aromatic aldehydes/ketones kam reactive (resonance se carbonyl C kam δ+).

💡 Nucleophile carbonyl C pe attack (aldehyde > ketone reactivity). 2,4-DNP = orange ppt (carbonyl test). HCN = cyanohydrin, Grignard = alcohol, alcohol = acetal (protection). Ye conversions ka backbone!

5️⃣ Oxidation, Reduction aur Named Reactions ⭐⭐

  • Oxidation — aldehyde vs ketone ⭐: aldehydes aasani se oxidize hote (acid dete) kyunki C=O pe H hota; ketones mushkil se (strong conditions pe C−C toot-ta). Tollens' test ⭐: ammoniacal AgNO₃ + aldehyde → silver mirror (Ag deposit) — ketone nahi deta. Fehling's test: Cu²⁺ (alkaline) + aldehyde → red-brown Cu₂O ppt. Dono se aldehyde/ketone distinguish.
  • Reduction: aldehyde → 1° alcohol, ketone → 2° alcohol (NaBH₄/LiAlH₄). Clemmensen (Zn-Hg/conc HCl) ya Wolff-Kishner (NH₂NH₂/KOH, glycol) se C=O → CH₂ (alkane banta).
  • Aldol condensation ⭐: α-H wale aldehydes/ketones dilute NaOH me → β-hydroxy aldehyde (aldol), heat pe α,β-unsaturated carbonyl (crotonaldehyde from acetaldehyde). Cross aldol: do alag carbonyl. Ketones bhi dete (slowly).
  • Cannizzaro reaction ⭐: α-H NAHI wale aldehydes (HCHO, C₆H₅CHO) conc. KOH me self redox — ek molecule oxidize (acid salt), dusra reduce (alcohol). Crossed Cannizzaro bhi hota (HCHO hamesha oxidize hota).
  • Haloform reaction ⭐: CH₃−CO− (methyl ketone) ya CH₃CH(OH)− wala compound + I₂/NaOH → iodoform (CHI₃, yellow ppt). Acetaldehyde (eklauta aldehyde) bhi deta — methyl ketones ka test.

💡 Tollens = silver mirror, Fehling = red ppt (dono sirf ALDEHYDE). Clemmensen/Wolff-Kishner = C=O→CH₂. Aldol = α-H wale (dilute NaOH), Cannizzaro = α-H nahi wale (conc KOH). Iodoform = yellow ppt (methyl ketone test). Ye sab named reactions pakke!

6️⃣ Carboxylic Acids — Acidity aur Reactions ⭐⭐

  • Acidity ⭐⭐: carboxylic acids alcohols aur phenols se zyada acidic kyunki carboxylate ion (RCOO⁻) resonance-stabilized hai (dono equivalent resonating structures, charge equally share). Ka order: acid > phenol > water > alcohol. Electron-withdrawing group acidity BADHATA (−I effect, carboxylate stable); electron-donating GHATATA. Example: FCH₂COOH > ClCH₂COOH > BrCH₂COOH > CH₃COOH. Halogens jitna kareeb (α-C) utna zyada effect.
  • Test: carboxylic acids NaHCO₃ se CO₂ dete (brisk effervescence) — phenol nahi deta. Isse acid aur phenol distinguish.
  • Preparation ⭐: (a) oxidation — 1° alcohol/aldehyde (KMnO₄) → acid. (b) Grignard + CO₂ — RMgX + dry ice (CO₂) phir hydrolysis → acid (C chain ek se badhti). (c) nitrile hydrolysis — RCN + H₂O/H⁺ ya OH⁻ → acid.
  • Reactions ⭐: salt (NaOH/NaHCO₃ se). Esterification (alcohol + conc H₂SO₄ → ester). Acyl chloride (SOCl₂/PCl₅ se). HVZ (Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky) ⭐: α-H wala acid + Br₂ (red P) → α-bromo acid (sirf α-position pe halogenation). Decarboxylation: sodium salt + soda lime (NaOH+CaO, heat) → alkane (CO₂ nikalta). Kolbe electrolysis: acid salt electrolyze → alkane.

💡 Carboxylic acid = sabse acidic (carboxylate resonance). NaHCO₃ test = CO₂ bubbles. Electron-withdrawing = zyada acidic. HVZ = α-bromination (red P + Br₂). Grignard + CO₂ = acid (chain +1). Conversions me har jagah!

1️⃣ कार्बोनिल और नामकरण

  • ऐल्डिहाइड R−CHO (end, एक H), कीटोन R−CO−R (मध्य), अम्ल −COOH। C=O ध्रुवीय, sp²; नाभिकस्नेही कार्बन पर आक्रमण। नाम: −al/−one/−oic acid।

💡 ऐल्डिहाइड = end C=O।

2️⃣ विधियाँ

  • Rosenmund: RCOCl + H₂(Pd/BaSO₄)→ऐल्डिहाइड। ऐल्काइन जलयोजन (एसिटिलीन→एसिटैल्डिहाइड)। Friedel-Crafts acylation → कीटोन।

💡 Rosenmund = ऐल्डिहाइड।

3️⃣ भौतिक गुण

  • कार्बोनिल ध्रुवीय पर स्व-H-बंध नहीं → क्वथनांक ऐल्कोहल से कम। कार्बोक्सिलिक अम्ल डाइमर बनाता → उच्चतम क्वथनांक। छोटे जल-घुलनशील।

💡 अम्ल डाइमर = उच्च क्वथनांक।

4️⃣ नाभिकस्नेही योग ⭐

  • नाभिकस्नेही कार्बोनिल C पर; ऐल्डिहाइड > कीटोन क्रियाशील। 2,4-DNP → नारंगी अवक्षेप (परीक्षण)। HCN→cyanohydrin, Grignard→ऐल्कोहल, ऐल्कोहल→acetal।

💡 2,4-DNP = कार्बोनिल परीक्षण।

5️⃣ ऑक्सीकरण, अपचयन, नामांकित ⭐

  • Tollens (Ag दर्पण) व Fehling (लाल अवक्षेप) — केवल ऐल्डिहाइड। Clemmensen/Wolff-Kishner: C=O→CH₂। Aldol (α-H), Cannizzaro (α-H नहीं), iodoform (मेथिल कीटोन, पीला)।

💡 Tollens/Fehling = ऐल्डिहाइड परीक्षण।

6️⃣ कार्बोक्सिलिक अम्ल ⭐

  • सर्वाधिक अम्लीय (कार्बोक्सिलेट अनुनाद-स्थायी)। NaHCO₃ → CO₂ (परीक्षण)। e⁻-withdrawer अम्लता बढ़ाता। HVZ: Br₂/red P → α-ब्रोमो अम्ल। Grignard + CO₂ → अम्ल।

💡 NaHCO₃ से CO₂ = अम्ल परीक्षण।

1️⃣ Carbonyl & Naming

  • Aldehyde R−CHO (terminal, one H), ketone R−CO−R (internal), acid −COOH. C=O is polar, sp²; nucleophiles attack the carbonyl carbon. Naming: −al / −one / −oic acid.

💡 Aldehyde = terminal C=O.

2️⃣ Preparation

  • Rosenmund: RCOCl + H₂ (Pd/BaSO₄) → aldehyde. Alkyne hydration (acetylene → acetaldehyde). Friedel-Crafts acylation → aryl ketone. From alcohols, nitriles, ozonolysis.

💡 Rosenmund gives an aldehyde.

3️⃣ Physical Properties

  • Carbonyls are polar but cannot self-H-bond → BP lower than alcohols. Carboxylic acids form H-bonded dimers → highest BP. Small members are water-soluble.

💡 Acids dimerize → high BP.

4️⃣ Nucleophilic Addition ⭐

  • Nucleophile attacks the carbonyl C; aldehydes > ketones in reactivity. 2,4-DNP → orange ppt (test). HCN → cyanohydrin, Grignard → alcohol, alcohol → acetal (protection).

💡 2,4-DNP = carbonyl test.

5️⃣ Oxidation, Reduction & Named ⭐

  • Tollens' (silver mirror) and Fehling's (red ppt) — aldehydes only. Clemmensen/Wolff-Kishner: C=O → CH₂. Aldol (has α-H, dilute base), Cannizzaro (no α-H, conc. base), iodoform (methyl ketone, yellow ppt).

💡 Tollens/Fehling = aldehyde tests.

6️⃣ Carboxylic Acids ⭐

  • Most acidic (resonance-stabilized carboxylate). NaHCO₃ → CO₂ (test). E-withdrawing groups increase acidity. HVZ: Br₂/red P → α-bromo acid. Grignard + CO₂ → acid. Esterification, HVZ, decarboxylation.

💡 NaHCO₃ → CO₂ = acid test.

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Aldehyde aur ketone me structural farak?
Answer dekho

Aldehyde me carbonyl carbon pe kam se kam ek H (R−CHO, chain end). Ketone me carbonyl carbon pe do alkyl/aryl groups (R−CO−R′, beech me).

Q2. Rosenmund reduction kya deta hai?
Answer dekho

Acyl chloride (RCOCl) + H₂ (Pd/BaSO₄) → aldehyde. BaSO₄ catalyst ko poison karke over-reduction (alcohol tak) rokta.

Q3. Aldehydes ketones se zyada reactive kyun (nucleophilic addition)?
Answer dekho

Kam steric hindrance + kam electron-donating alkyl groups — aldehyde ka carbonyl carbon zyada δ+ hota, nucleophile aasani se attack karta.

Q4. 2,4-DNP test kis liye?
Answer dekho

Carbonyl group (aldehyde/ketone) ka test — 2,4-dinitrophenylhydrazine se orange/yellow precipitate banta.

Q5. Tollens' aur Fehling's test kya distinguish karte?
Answer dekho

Dono aldehyde ko ketone se alag karte — aldehyde deta hai: Tollens' me silver mirror, Fehling's me red Cu₂O ppt. Ketone dono me nahi deta.

Q6. Aldol condensation kis me hoti hai aur product?
Answer dekho

α-H wale aldehydes/ketones dilute NaOH me → β-hydroxy carbonyl (aldol), heat pe α,β-unsaturated carbonyl. Example: 2 CH₃CHO → crotonaldehyde.

Q7. Cannizzaro reaction kis me hoti hai?
Answer dekho

α-H NAHI wale aldehydes (HCHO, C₆H₅CHO) conc. KOH me self redox — ek oxidize (acid salt), ek reduce (alcohol).

Q8. Iodoform test kis ka hai?
Answer dekho

Methyl ketones (CH₃−CO−) ya CH₃CH(OH)− wale + I₂/NaOH → iodoform (CHI₃, yellow ppt). Acetaldehyde bhi positive deta.

Q9. Carboxylic acids phenol se zyada acidic kyun?
Answer dekho

Carboxylate ion (RCOO⁻) zyada resonance-stabilized hai (dono equivalent structures) — phenoxide se zyada. Isliye acid NaHCO₃ se CO₂ deta, phenol nahi.

Q10. Electron-withdrawing group ka acidity pe effect?
Answer dekho

Acidity BADHATA hai (−I effect se carboxylate ion stable). Order: FCH₂COOH > ClCH₂COOH > CH₃COOH. Jitna kareeb (α-C) utna zyada effect.
