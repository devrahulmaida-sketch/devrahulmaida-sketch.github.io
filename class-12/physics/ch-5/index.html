Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Bar Magnet — Magnet Ka ABCD

  • Bar magnet: do poles — North aur South. Kabhi ek pole akela nahi milta (isolated magnetic monopoles ab tak observe nahi hue!) — tod do to har tukda apna N-S wala magnet ban jaata hai.
  • Properties: (1) attractive property — iron ko kheenchta hai; (2) directive property — free magnet N-S direction me align hota hai (compass ka basis!); (3) like poles repel, unlike attract.
  • Magnetic field lines: BAHAR N se S ki taraf, ANDAR S se N ki taraf — closed loops (electric lines se farak: wo + se − pe khatam ho jaati hain!). ⭐
  • Field lines kabhi intersect nahi karti; dense lines = strong field. Magnet ke andar bhi lines jaati hain — closed curves.
  • Magnetic moment (m): magnet ki strength aur direction ka measure — S se N ki taraf, m = q_m × 2l (pole strength × length).

💡 Magnet todne se monopole nahi milega — atom tak jao, har electron khud ek chhota magnet hai!

2️⃣ Bar Magnet as Solenoid aur Dipole Field ⭐

  • Bar magnet = solenoid (qualitative) ⭐: current-carrying solenoid aur bar magnet ke field patterns bilkul same — dono magnetic dipoles hain. (Yehi reason hai ki magnet ko monopole me toda nahi ja sakta!)
  • Axial field ⭐: bar magnet ke axis pe (N-S line ke along, doori r pe) B = (μ₀/4π) · 2m/r³ — dipole moment ki direction me. (Electric dipole jaisa hi 1/r³!)
  • Equatorial field ⭐: B = (μ₀/4π) · m/r³ — axial ka aadha, direction OPPOSITE to m. (E-dipole se pattern yaad karo: 2kp/r³ vs kp/r³!)
  • Dono 1/r³ se girte hain — magnet se doori double → field 1/8.
  • Current loop ka dipole moment: m = NIA — isliye coil, solenoid, magnet sab ek family.

🎯 Axial = 2× equatorial — electric dipole wala hi factor 2. Ek pattern, do jagah!

3️⃣ Uniform Field Me Magnet — Torque aur Energy ⭐

  • Torque on bar magnet ⭐: uniform field B me τ = mB sin θ ya vector form τ = m × B. Field magnet ko apne saath align karna chahta hai (θ = 0 pe torque zero).
  • Potential energy ⭐: U = −mB cos θ — θ = 0 pe minimum (−mB, stable equilibrium), θ = 180° pe maximum (+mB, unstable).
  • Work done in rotating: θ₁ se θ₂ tak ghumane me W_ext = mB(cos θ₁ − cos θ₂). 0 se 180°: W = 2mB.
  • Stable vs unstable: θ = 0: aligned, chhota dhakka wapas kheench laata hai; θ = 180°: anti-aligned, chhoti si disturbance se palat jaayega.
  • Electric dipole se comparison: τ = p×E aur τ = m×B — formula ek, field alag. Physics me patterns repeat hote hain!

💡 Compass needle isi liye kaam karti hai — Earth ka field uspe torque lagata hai aur N-S align kar deta hai!

4️⃣ Magnetization aur Temperature ⭐

  • Magnetization M: material ke volume per magnetic dipole moment; unit A/m.
  • Susceptibility: linear materials me M=χH, aur B=μ₀(H+M); χ sign dia (negative), para (positive), ferro (large positive) batata hai.
  • Temperature: para magnetic response generally temperature badhne pe kam; ferro Curie temperature ke upar paramagnetic ho jaata hai.

💡 Earth magnetism current CBSE theory outline me nahi; magnetization ko focus do.

5️⃣ Magnetic Materials — Diamagnetic, Paramagnetic, Ferromagnetic ⭐⭐

  • Diamagnetic ⭐: field ko WEAKLY repel karte hain — andar field thoda kam. Examples: Bi, Cu, Ag, water, NaCl. Magnetic susceptibility χ chhota NEGATIVE (−10⁻⁵ order).
  • Paramagnetic ⭐: field ko weakly attract — atoms ke permanent dipoles field ke saath align hote hain. Examples: Al, Na, Pt, oxygen. χ chhota POSITIVE (~10⁻⁵ to 10⁻³).
  • Ferromagnetic ⭐⭐: STRONGLY attract — domains (lakhs atoms ke aligned groups) field ke saath align ho jaate hain. Examples: Fe, Co, Ni, gadolinium. χ bahut BADA (~10³!).
  • Domains: ferromagnetic materials me chhote regions jisme saare atomic magnets aligned — random orientation me net zero; field lagane pe domains align → strong magnet!
  • Curie temperature ⭐: is temperature ke upar ferromagnetic → paramagnetic ban jaata hai (thermal energy domains tod deti hai). Iron ka Curie point ≈ 1043 K.
  • Permanent magnets: hard ferromagnetic materials (steel, Alnico) — domains ek baar align to waise hi rehte hain. Electromagnets: soft iron — field hatate hi demagnetize.
  • Field lines ka behavior: ferromagnetic lines ko andar KHEENCH leta hai; diamagnetic lines ko door DHKELTA hai.

🎯 Yaad rakhne ka trick: DIA = डरावना (field se bhaagta), PARA = प्यार (thoda sa attract), FERRO = फ़ुल प्यार (strongly attract, permanent ban sakta hai)!

1️⃣ दंड चुंबक — चुंबकत्व की मूल बातें

  • दंड चुंबक: दो ध्रुव — उत्तरी और दक्षिणी। पृथक चुंबकीय एकध्रुव (monopole) अब तक नहीं पाया गया — तोड़ दो तो प्रत्येक टुकड़ा अपना N-S वाला चुंबक बन जाता है।
  • गुण: (1) आकर्षण — लोहे को खींचता है; (2) दिशात्मकता — मुक्त चुंबक N-S दिशा में संरेखित होता है (कंपास का आधार!)।
  • चुंबकीय क्षेत्र रेखाएं: बाहर N से S, भीतर S से N — बंद वक्र (विद्युत रेखाओं से अंतर!) ⭐
  • क्षेत्र रेखाएं कभी प्रतिच्छेद नहीं करतीं; सघन रेखाएं = प्रबल क्षेत्र।
  • चुंबकीय आघूर्ण (m): चुंबक की प्रबलता व दिशा — S से N की ओर, m = q_m × 2l।

💡 चुंबक तोड़ने से एकध्रुव नहीं मिलेगा — परमाणु तक जाओ, प्रत्येक इलेक्ट्रॉन स्वयं एक छोटा चुंबक है!

2️⃣ परिनालिका के रूप में दंड चुंबक और द्विध्रुव क्षेत्र ⭐

  • दंड चुंबक = परिनालिका (गुणात्मक) ⭐: धारावाही परिनालिका और दंड चुंबक के क्षेत्र पैटर्न समान — दोनों चुंबकीय द्विध्रुव हैं।
  • अक्षीय क्षेत्र ⭐: B = (μ₀/4π) · 2m/r³ — द्विध्रुव आघूर्ण की दिशा में।
  • निरक्षीय क्षेत्र ⭐: B = (μ₀/4π) · m/r³ — अक्षीय का आधा, दिशा m के विपरीत।
  • दोनों 1/r³ से घटते हैं — दूरी दुगुनी → क्षेत्र 1/8।
  • धारा लूप का द्विध्रुव आघूर्ण: m = NIA — कुंडली, परिनालिका, चुंबक सब एक परिवार।

🎯 अक्षीय = 2 × निरक्षीय — विद्युत द्विध्रुव वाला ही गुणक 2!

3️⃣ एकसमान क्षेत्र में चुंबक — बलाघूर्ण और ऊर्जा ⭐

  • दंड चुंबक पर बलाघूर्ण ⭐: τ = mB sin θ या τ = m × B। क्षेत्र चुंबक को संरेखित करना चाहता है।
  • स्थितिज ऊर्जा ⭐: U = −mB cos θ — θ = 0 पर न्यूनतम (स्थायी), θ = 180° पर अधिकतम (अस्थायी)।
  • घुमाने में कार्य: W_ext = mB(cos θ₁ − cos θ₂)। 0 से 180°: W = 2mB।
  • स्थायी बनाम अस्थायी: θ = 0: संरेखित, स्थिर; θ = 180°: प्रतिसंरेखित, हल्की बाधा से पलट जाता है।
  • विद्युत द्विध्रुव से तुलना: τ = p×E और τ = m×B — सूत्र एक, क्षेत्र भिन्न!

💡 कंपास सुई इसीलिए काम करती है — पृथ्वी का क्षेत्र उस पर बलाघूर्ण लगाकर N-S संरेखित करता है!

4️⃣ चुंबकन और ताप ⭐

  • चुंबकन M: पदार्थ का प्रति इकाई आयतन चुंबकीय द्विध्रुव आघूर्ण; मात्रक A/m।
  • चुंबकीय प्रवृत्ति: रैखिक पदार्थ में M=χH और B=μ₀(H+M); प्रति में χ ऋण, अनु में धन, लौह में बड़ा धन।
  • ताप: अनुचुंबकीय प्रतिक्रिया ताप बढ़ने पर सामान्यतः घटती; क्यूरी ताप से ऊपर लौहचुंबकीय पदार्थ अनुचुंबकीय हो जाता है।

💡 पृथ्वी चुंबकत्व वर्तमान CBSE सिद्धांत रूपरेखा में नहीं है।

5️⃣ चुंबकीय पदार्थ — प्रति, अनु, लौह चुंबकीय ⭐⭐

  • प्रतिचुंबकीय ⭐: क्षेत्र को दुर्बलता से प्रतिकर्षित। उदाहरण: Bi, Cu, Ag, जल। प्रवृत्ति χ छोटी ऋणात्मक।
  • अनुचुंबकीय ⭐: क्षेत्र को दुर्बलता से आकर्षित — परमाणुओं के स्थायी द्विध्रुव संरेखित होते हैं। उदाहरण: Al, Na, Pt। χ छोटी धनात्मक।
  • लौहचुंबकीय ⭐⭐: प्रबलता से आकर्षित — डोमेन (संरेखित परमाणुओं के समूह) क्षेत्र के साथ संरेखित। उदाहरण: Fe, Co, Ni। χ अत्यंत बड़ी (~10³!)।
  • डोमेन: छोटे क्षेत्र जिनमें सभी परमाणवीय चुंबक संरेखित — यादृच्छिक अभिविन्यास में कुल शून्य; क्षेत्र लगाने पर संरेखित → प्रबल चुंबक!
  • क्यूरी ताप ⭐: इससे ऊपर लौहचुंबकीय → अनुचुंबकीय (ऊष्मा डोमेन तोड़ देती है)। लोहे का क्यूरी बिंदु ≈ 1043 K।
  • स्थायी चुंबक: कठोर लौहचुंबकीय (steel, Alnico); विद्युतचुंबक: मृदु लोहा।

🎯 याद रखो: प्रति = भागता है, अनु = थोड़ा आकर्षित, लौह = प्रबल आकर्षण!

1️⃣ The Bar Magnet — Magnetism's ABCD

  • Bar magnet: two poles — North and South. A single isolated pole never exists (no isolated monopoles observed!) — break a magnet and every piece becomes its own N-S magnet.
  • Properties: (1) attractive property — pulls iron; (2) directive property — a free magnet aligns N-S (the basis of the compass!); (3) like poles repel, unlike attract.
  • Magnetic field lines: OUTSIDE they run N to S, INSIDE S to N — closed loops (unlike electric lines, which end on charges!). ⭐
  • Field lines never intersect; dense lines = strong field. Lines pass through the magnet itself — closed curves.
  • Magnetic moment (m): the measure of a magnet's strength and direction — points S to N, m = q_m × 2l (pole strength × length).

💡 Breaking a magnet never yields a monopole — go down to the atom and every electron is itself a tiny magnet!

2️⃣ Bar Magnet as a Solenoid and the Dipole Field ⭐

  • Bar magnet = solenoid (qualitative) ⭐: a current-carrying solenoid and a bar magnet have identical field patterns — both are magnetic dipoles. (This is why a magnet cannot be split into monopoles!)
  • Axial field ⭐: on the magnet's axis at distance r, B = (μ₀/4π) · 2m/r³ — in the direction of the dipole moment. (Same 1/r³ as the electric dipole!)
  • Equatorial field ⭐: B = (μ₀/4π) · m/r³ — half the axial value, directed OPPOSITE to m. (Recall the E-dipole pattern: 2kp/r³ vs kp/r³!)
  • Both fall as 1/r³ — double the distance, one-eighth the field.
  • Dipole moment of a current loop: m = NIA — coil, solenoid and magnet are one family.

🎯 Axial = 2× equatorial — the same factor of 2 as the electric dipole. One pattern, two places!

3️⃣ A Magnet in a Uniform Field — Torque and Energy ⭐

  • Torque on a bar magnet ⭐: in a uniform field B, τ = mB sin θ or vector form τ = m × B. The field tries to align the magnet with itself (zero torque at θ = 0).
  • Potential energy ⭐: U = −mB cos θ — minimum at θ = 0 (−mB, stable equilibrium), maximum at θ = 180° (+mB, unstable).
  • Work done rotating: from θ₁ to θ₂, W_ext = mB(cos θ₁ − cos θ₂). From 0 to 180°: W = 2mB.
  • Stable vs unstable: θ = 0: aligned, a small nudge gets pulled back; θ = 180°: anti-aligned, the slightest disturbance flips it.
  • Comparison with the electric dipole: τ = p×E and τ = m×B — same formula, different field. Physics repeats its patterns!

💡 This is exactly why a compass works — Earth's field torques the needle into N-S alignment!

4️⃣ Magnetization and Temperature ⭐

  • Magnetization M: magnetic dipole moment per unit volume, measured in A/m.
  • Susceptibility: for linear materials M=χH and B=μ₀(H+M); χ is negative for dia, positive for para, and strongly positive for ferro.
  • Temperature: paramagnetic response generally falls as temperature rises; ferromagnets become paramagnetic above the Curie temperature.

💡 Earth magnetism is outside the current CBSE theory outline; focus on magnetization.

5️⃣ Magnetic Materials — Dia, Para, Ferro ⭐⭐

  • Diamagnetic ⭐: WEAKLY repelled by a field — the internal field drops slightly. Examples: Bi, Cu, Ag, water, NaCl. Susceptibility χ small and NEGATIVE (~−10⁻⁵).
  • Paramagnetic ⭐: weakly attracted — atoms with permanent dipoles align with the field. Examples: Al, Na, Pt, oxygen. χ small POSITIVE (~10⁻⁵ to 10⁻³).
  • Ferromagnetic ⭐⭐: STRONGLY attracted — domains (aligned groups of lakhs of atoms) line up with the field. Examples: Fe, Co, Ni, gadolinium. χ very LARGE (~10³!).
  • Domains: small regions in ferromagnetic materials where all atomic magnets are aligned — random orientation gives net zero; an applied field aligns the domains → strong magnet!
  • Curie temperature ⭐: above this, a ferromagnet turns paramagnetic (thermal energy breaks the domains). Iron's Curie point ≈ 1043 K.
  • Permanent magnets: hard ferromagnetic materials (steel, Alnico) — domains stay aligned. Electromagnets: soft iron — demagnetizes when the field is removed.
  • Field line behaviour: ferromagnets PULL field lines in; diamagnets PUSH them out.

🎯 Memory trick: DIA runs away from the field, PARA is mildly attracted, FERRO is strongly attracted and can stay magnetized!

JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Magnet ko 2 tukdo me todo to kya milega?
Answer dekho

Do CHHOTE magnets — har ek ke apne N aur S poles. Monopole kabhi nahi milta!

Q2 Bar magnet ke axial point pe field equatorial se kitna zyada?
Answer dekho

Axial = (μ₀/4π)(2m/r³), equatorial = (μ₀/4π)(m/r³) → axial 2 guna.

Q3 m = 0.5 A·m² wala magnet B = 0.2 T me 90° pe hai. Torque?
Answer dekho

τ = mB sinθ = 0.5 × 0.2 × 1 = 0.1 N·m (maximum).

Q4 Wahi magnet θ = 0 pe hai. Potential energy?
Answer dekho

U = −mB cos 0 = −0.5 × 0.2 = −0.1 J (stable equilibrium).

Q5 Magnet ko θ = 0 se 180° tak ghumane me kitna work?
Answer dekho

W = mB(cos 0 − cos 180°) = 2mB = 0.2 J.

Q6 Magnetization ka SI unit?
Answer dekho

Magnetic moment per volume = A/m.

Q7 Diamagnetic, paramagnetic, ferromagnetic ke ek-ek example?
Answer dekho

Dia: Bi/Cu; Para: Al/Pt; Ferro: Fe/Co/Ni.

Q8 Curie temperature kya hai?
Answer dekho

Wo temperature jiske upar ferromagnetic → paramagnetic ho jaata hai (domains toot jaate hain). Iron ka ≈ 1043 K.

Q9 Electromagnet ke core ke liye kaunsa material best?
Answer dekho

Soft iron — high susceptibility, field hatate hi demagnetize. Permanent magnet ke liye hard steel/Alnico.

Q10 Magnetic field lines electric field lines se kaise alag hain?
Answer dekho

Magnetic lines closed loops hain (magnet ke andar bhi jaati hain); electric lines + charge pe shuru hoke − pe khatam hoti hain.
