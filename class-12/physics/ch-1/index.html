Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Electric Charge — Sabse Basic Property

  • Charge: matter ki fundamental property jisse uspe electric force aata hai. Do types: positive (proton) aur negative (electron). Like charges repel, unlike charges attract ⭐
  • Quantization ⭐: charge hamesha q = ±ne hota hai, jahan e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C (elementary charge ka magnitude). Fractional quark charges isolated state me nahi milte.
  • Conservation ⭐: isolated system ka total charge kabhi change nahi hota — charge na banaya ja sakta hai, na destroy; sirf transfer hota hai (rubbing = electrons ka transfer).
  • Additivity: total charge = saare charges ka algebraic sum (sign ke saath).
  • Conductors: free electrons hain, charge flow kar sakta hai (metals). Insulators: charge ek jagah chipka rehta hai (glass, plastic).
  • Earthing/grounding: extra charge ko zameen me bhej dena.

💡 Feel: charge paise jaisa hai — total conserved, ginti ke packet (e) me hi transfer hota hai!

2️⃣ Coulomb's Law — Charges Ke Beech Ka Force ⭐

  • Coulomb's law ⭐: do point charges ke beech force F = k q₁q₂ / r², jahan k = 1/(4πε₀) = 9 × 10⁹ N·m²/C². ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² C²/N·m² (permittivity of free space).
  • Direction: force dono charges ko jodne wali line ke along — same sign = repel (door dhakka), opposite = attract.
  • Vector form ⭐: F₁₂ = k q₁q₂ r̂ / r² — force q₁ pe q₂ ki wajah se, r̂ q₂ se q₁ ki taraf.
  • Inverse square: distance double → force 1/4. Gravity jaisa hi pattern (lekin electric force 10³⁶ guna strong hai protons ke liye!).
  • Dielectric/medium me: F_medium = F_air / K, jahan K = dielectric constant (relative permittivity εᵣ).
  • Comparison with gravity: dono inverse-square, lekin electric force attractive + repulsive dono hota hai; gravity sirf attractive.

🎯 Coulomb's law sirf point charges (ya chhote charged bodies) ke liye valid — bade extended objects ke liye integrate karna padta hai.

3️⃣ Superposition Principle — Multiple Charges

  • Principle of superposition ⭐: bahut saare charges hon to kisi ek charge pe net force = har charge ke force ka vector sum. Do charges ki jodi ka force baaki charges se affect nahi hota.
  • Method: har pair ka Coulomb force nikalo, phir components todke add karo (x aur y alag-alag).
  • Symmetry ka use: symmetric arrangement me kai components cancel ho jaate hain — pehle dekh lo, kaam aadha!
  • Continuous charge distribution: charge spread ho to chhote dq elements me todo aur integrate karo. Linear density λ = q/L, surface σ = q/A, volume ρ = q/V.

💡 Exam tip: force vector hai — sirf magnitude add karne ki galti sabse common hai. Pehle diagram, phir components!

4️⃣ Electric Field aur Field Lines ⭐

  • Electric field (E): charge ke chaaron taraf ka region jahan wo doosre charge pe force lagata hai. E = F/q₀ (test charge q₀ pe force per unit charge). Unit: N/C ya V/m.
  • Point charge ka field ⭐: E = kQ/r², direction radially outward (+Q se) ya inward (−Q ke liye).
  • Field lines ke rules: (1) + se shuru, − pe khatam; (2) kabhi intersect nahi karti (ek point pe do direction ho hi nahi sakti); (3) jahan dense, wahan field strong; (4) conductor ke surface pe perpendicular.
  • Field lines continuous curves hain free space me; unki density field strength batati hai.
  • Uniform field: parallel, equally spaced lines — jaise parallel plate capacitor ke beech.

💡 E ek vector field hai — har point pe ek arrow. Test charge chhota rakhte hain taaki apna khud ka field system ko disturb na kare.

5️⃣ Electric Dipole — Do Charges Ki Jodi ⭐

  • Dipole: −q aur +q chhoti distance 2a pe. Dipole moment p = q × 2a, direction −q se +q ki taraf. Unit: C·m. (Paani ka molecule bhi dipole hai!)
  • Axial line pe field ⭐: E = (1/4πε₀) · 2pr/(r² − a²)². r ≫ a ke liye: E_axial = 2kp/r³.
  • Equatorial line pe field ⭐: Magnitude E = (1/4πε₀) · p/(r² + a²)^(3/2), direction p ke opposite. r ≫ a ke liye: E_equatorial = kp/r³ — axial ka aadha, direction p ke OPPOSITE.
  • Dono 1/r³ se girti hain — point charge (1/r²) se zyada tezi se, kyunki do charges ka effect cancel hone lagta hai.
  • Uniform field me torque ⭐: τ = pE sin θ, vector form τ = p × E. θ = 0 pe zero (stable), θ = 90° pe maximum.
  • Potential energy of dipole: U = −pE cos θ = −p·E. θ = 0 pe minimum (−pE, stable equilibrium), θ = 180° pe maximum.

🎯 Axial = 2kp/r³, equatorial = kp/r³ — factor 2 ka relation exam me seedha puchha jaata hai!

6️⃣ Electric Flux aur Gauss's Law ⭐⭐

  • Electric flux ⭐: kitni field lines surface se guzarti hain — Φ = E·A = EA cos θ. Unit: N·m²/C.
  • Gauss's law ⭐⭐: kisi bhi closed surface (Gaussian surface) se total flux = andar ke charge / ε₀. Φ = ∮ E·dA = q_enclosed/ε₀. Surface ki shape matter nahi karti — sirf andar ka charge!
  • Application 1 — infinite line charge ⭐: cylindrical Gaussian surface se E = λ/(2πε₀r) (1/r se girta hai).
  • Application 2 — infinite plane sheet ⭐: E = σ/(2ε₀) — distance se INDEPENDENT! Dono taraf uniform.
  • Application 3 — spherical shell ⭐: bahar: E = kQ/r² (jaise saara charge center pe ho); andar: E = 0. (Shell theorem — gravity me bhi same!)
  • Gauss's law vs Coulomb: dono equivalent hain, par high symmetry (spherical, cylindrical, planar) me Gauss se derivation seconds me ho jaati hai.

🎯 Yaad rakho: line → 1/r, sheet → constant, sphere bahar → 1/r², sphere andar → 0. Ye 4 results 90% Gauss questions solve kar dete hain!

1️⃣ विद्युत आवेश — मूलभूत गुण

  • आवेश: पदार्थ का मूलभूत गुण जिससे विद्युत बल उत्पन्न होता है। दो प्रकार: धनात्मक (प्रोटॉन) और ऋणात्मक (इलेक्ट्रॉन)। समान आवेश प्रतिकर्षित, असमान आकर्षित करते हैं ⭐
  • क्वांटीकरण ⭐: आवेश सदैव q = ±ne होता है, जहाँ e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C।
  • संरक्षण ⭐: विलगित निकाय का कुल आवेश अपरिवर्तित रहता है — आवेश न उत्पन्न होता है, न नष्ट; केवल स्थानांतरित होता है।
  • योग्यता: कुल आवेश = सभी आवेशों का बीजगणितीय योग।
  • चालक: मुक्त इलेक्ट्रॉन होते हैं (धातु)। कुचालक: आवेश स्थिर रहता है (कांच, प्लास्टिक)।

💡 आवेश = संरक्षित राशि, e के पूर्ण गुणकों में ही स्थानांतरित होता है!

2️⃣ कूलॉम का नियम — आवेशों के बीच बल ⭐

  • कूलॉम का नियम ⭐: दो बिंदु आवेशों के बीच बल F = k q₁q₂ / r², जहाँ k = 1/(4πε₀) = 9 × 10⁹ N·m²/C²।
  • दिशा: बल दोनों आवेशों को जोड़ने वाली रेखा के अनुदिश — समान चिह्न = प्रतिकर्षण, असमान = आकर्षण।
  • सदिश रूप ⭐: F = k q₁q₂ r̂ / r²।
  • व्युत्क्रम वर्ग: दूरी दुगुनी → बल 1/4।
  • माध्यम में: F_माध्यम = F_वायु / K, जहाँ K = परावैद्युतांक।
  • गुरुत्व से तुलना: दोनों व्युत्क्रम-वर्ग, परंतु विद्युत बल आकर्षण + प्रतिकर्षण दोनों; गुरुत्व केवल आकर्षण।

🎯 कूलॉम का नियम केवल बिंदु आवेशों के लिए मान्य है।

3️⃣ अध्यारोपण सिद्धांत — अनेक आवेश

  • अध्यारोपण सिद्धांत ⭐: किसी एक आवेश पर कुल बल = प्रत्येक आवेश के बल का सदिश योग। किसी युग्म का बल अन्य आवेशों से प्रभावित नहीं होता।
  • विधि: प्रत्येक युग्म का बल निकालो, घटकों में विभाजित करके जोड़ो।
  • सममिति का उपयोग: सममित व्यवस्था में अनेक घटक निरस्त हो जाते हैं।
  • सतत आवेश वितरण: रेखीय घनत्व λ = q/L, पृष्ठीय σ = q/A, आयतन ρ = q/V।

💡 बल सदिश है — केवल परिमाण जोड़ना सबसे सामान्य गलती है। पहले आरेख, फिर घटक!

4️⃣ विद्युत क्षेत्र और क्षेत्र रेखाएं ⭐

  • विद्युत क्षेत्र (E): आवेश के चारों ओर का क्षेत्र जहाँ वह दूसरे आवेश पर बल लगाता है। E = F/q₀। मात्रक: N/C या V/m।
  • बिंदु आवेश का क्षेत्र ⭐: E = kQ/r², दिशा त्रिज्यीय बाहर (+Q) या भीतर (−Q)।
  • क्षेत्र रेखाओं के नियम: (1) + से प्रारंभ, − पर समाप्त; (2) कभी प्रतिच्छेद नहीं; (3) जहाँ सघन, वहाँ क्षेत्र प्रबल; (4) चालक पृष्ठ पर लंबवत।
  • एकसमान क्षेत्र: समांतर, समान दूरी की रेखाएं।

💡 E एक सदिश क्षेत्र है — प्रत्येक बिंदु पर एक तीर।

5️⃣ विद्युत द्विध्रुव — दो आवेशों का युग्म ⭐

  • द्विध्रुव: −q और +q, दूरी 2a पर। द्विध्रुव आघूर्ण p = q × 2a, दिशा −q से +q की ओर।
  • अक्षीय रेखा पर क्षेत्र ⭐: r ≫ a के लिए E_अक्षीय = 2kp/r³।
  • निरक्षीय रेखा पर क्षेत्र ⭐: r ≫ a के लिए E_निरक्षीय = kp/r³ — अक्षीय का आधा, दिशा p के विपरीत।
  • दोनों 1/r³ से घटते हैं — बिंदु आवेश (1/r²) से तेज।
  • एकसमान क्षेत्र में बलाघूर्ण ⭐: τ = pE sin θ, τ = p × E।
  • स्थितिज ऊर्जा: U = −pE cos θ = −p·E।

🎯 अक्षीय = 2kp/r³, निरक्षीय = kp/r³ — गुणक 2 का संबंध परीक्षा में सीधे पूछा जाता है!

6️⃣ विद्युत फ्लक्स और गाउस का नियम ⭐⭐

  • विद्युत फ्लक्स ⭐: पृष्ठ से गुजरने वाली क्षेत्र रेखाओं की माप — Φ = EA cos θ।
  • गाउस का नियम ⭐⭐: किसी बंद पृष्ठ से कुल फ्लक्स = भीतर का आवेश / ε₀। Φ = q_परिबद्ध/ε₀।
  • अनंत रेखा आवेश ⭐: E = λ/(2πε₀r)।
  • अनंत समतल चादर ⭐: E = σ/(2ε₀) — दूरी से स्वतंत्र!
  • गोलीय कोश ⭐: बाहर: E = kQ/r²; भीतर: E = 0।

🎯 याद रखो: रेखा → 1/r, चादर → नियत, गोला बाहर → 1/r², गोला भीतर → 0।

1️⃣ Electric Charge — The Basic Property

  • Charge: the fundamental property of matter that produces electric force. Two types: positive (proton) and negative (electron). Like charges repel, unlike attract ⭐
  • Quantization ⭐: charge always comes as q = ±ne, where e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C (magnitude of the electron's charge). Free observable charge comes in units of e (quarks carry fractional charges but are not isolated).
  • Conservation ⭐: the total charge of an isolated system never changes — charge is neither created nor destroyed, only transferred (rubbing = electron transfer).
  • Additivity: total charge = the algebraic sum of all charges (with signs).
  • Conductors: have free electrons, charge can flow (metals). Insulators: charge stays put (glass, plastic).
  • Earthing: sending excess charge into the ground.

💡 Feel: charge is like money — total is conserved, and it moves only in packets of e!

2️⃣ Coulomb's Law — The Force Between Charges ⭐

  • Coulomb's law ⭐: the force between two point charges is F = k q₁q₂ / r², where k = 1/(4πε₀) = 9 × 10⁹ N·m²/C². ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² C²/N·m².
  • Direction: along the line joining the charges — same sign = repel, opposite = attract.
  • Vector form ⭐: F = k q₁q₂ r̂ / r².
  • Inverse square: double the distance → force becomes 1/4. Same pattern as gravity (but 10³⁶ times stronger for protons!).
  • In a medium: F_medium = F_air / K, where K = dielectric constant.
  • Versus gravity: both inverse-square, but electric force is attractive AND repulsive; gravity is only attractive.

🎯 Coulomb's law is valid only for point charges (or small charged bodies) — extended objects need integration.

3️⃣ Superposition Principle — Multiple Charges

  • Principle of superposition ⭐: with many charges, the net force on any one charge = the vector sum of forces from every other charge. Each pair's force is unaffected by the rest.
  • Method: find each pair's Coulomb force, then resolve into components and add (x and y separately).
  • Use symmetry: in symmetric arrangements many components cancel — check first, halve the work!
  • Continuous charge distribution: split into small dq elements and integrate. Linear density λ = q/L, surface σ = q/A, volume ρ = q/V.

💡 Exam tip: force is a vector — adding only magnitudes is the most common mistake. Diagram first, components second!

4️⃣ Electric Field and Field Lines ⭐

  • Electric field (E): the region around a charge where it exerts force on another charge. E = F/q₀. Unit: N/C or V/m.
  • Field of a point charge ⭐: E = kQ/r², radially outward from +Q or inward toward −Q.
  • Field line rules: (1) start on +, end on −; (2) never intersect (a point cannot have two field directions); (3) denser lines = stronger field; (4) perpendicular to a conductor's surface.
  • Field lines are continuous curves in free space; their density shows the field strength.
  • Uniform field: parallel, equally spaced lines — like between the plates of a parallel-plate capacitor.

💡 E is a vector field — an arrow at every point. The test charge is kept tiny so it does not disturb the system.

5️⃣ Electric Dipole — A Pair of Charges ⭐

  • Dipole: −q and +q separated by 2a. Dipole moment p = q × 2a, direction from −q to +q. Unit: C·m. (A water molecule is a dipole too!)
  • Field on the axial line ⭐: for r ≫ a, E_axial = 2kp/r³.
  • Field on the equatorial line ⭐: for r ≫ a, E_equatorial = kp/r³ — half the axial value, directed OPPOSITE to p.
  • Both fall as 1/r³ — faster than a point charge (1/r²), because the two charges start cancelling.
  • Torque in a uniform field ⭐: τ = pE sin θ, vector form τ = p × E. Zero at θ = 0 (stable), maximum at θ = 90°.
  • Potential energy: U = −pE cos θ = −p·E. Minimum at θ = 0 (stable equilibrium), maximum at θ = 180°.

🎯 Axial = 2kp/r³, equatorial = kp/r³ — the factor-of-2 relation is asked directly in exams!

6️⃣ Electric Flux and Gauss's Law ⭐⭐

  • Electric flux ⭐: how many field lines pass through a surface — Φ = E·A = EA cos θ. Unit: N·m²/C.
  • Gauss's law ⭐⭐: the total flux through any closed surface = the enclosed charge / ε₀. Φ = ∮ E·dA = q_enclosed/ε₀. Shape of the surface does not matter — only the charge inside!
  • Application 1 — infinite line charge ⭐: using a cylindrical surface, E = λ/(2πε₀r) (falls as 1/r).
  • Application 2 — infinite plane sheet ⭐: E = σ/(2ε₀) — INDEPENDENT of distance! Uniform on both sides.
  • Application 3 — spherical shell ⭐: outside: E = kQ/r² (as if all charge sits at the centre); inside: E = 0. (Same as the shell theorem in gravity!)
  • Gauss vs Coulomb: equivalent, but with high symmetry (spherical, cylindrical, planar) Gauss finishes the derivation in seconds.

🎯 Remember: line → 1/r, sheet → constant, sphere outside → 1/r², sphere inside → 0. These 4 results solve 90% of Gauss questions!

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Do point charges +2 μC aur −3 μC 30 cm door hain. Force kitna aur kis type ka?
Answer dekho

F = 9×10⁹ × (2×10⁻⁶)(3×10⁻⁶)/(0.3)² = 0.6 N, attractive (opposite signs).

Q2. Distance double kar di to Coulomb force kya hoga?
Answer dekho

Inverse square law → force 1/4 ho jaayega.

Q3. Charge quantization ka matlab?
Answer dekho

Charge hamesha q = ne (e = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C) ke integer multiples me hi hota hai — fractional free charge nahi milta.

Q4. Ek dipole ka p = 4×10⁻⁹ C·m hai, r = 20 cm pe axial point pe field (r ≫ a)?
Answer dekho

E = 2kp/r³ = 2×9×10⁹×4×10⁻⁹/(0.2)³ = 72/0.008 = 9000 N/C.

Q5. Equatorial point pe usi dipole ka field?
Answer dekho

Axial ka aadha = 4500 N/C, direction dipole moment ke opposite.

Q6. Dipole ko uniform field E me 90° pe rakha hai. Torque ka formula aur max value?
Answer dekho

τ = pE sinθ; 90° pe τ_max = pE. Potential energy 0.

Q7. Gauss's law likho.
Answer dekho

Φ = ∮E·dA = q_enclosed/ε₀ — closed surface se total flux andar ke charge pe depend karta hai.

Q8. Infinite line charge (λ) se r distance pe field?
Answer dekho

E = λ/(2πε₀r) — cylindrical symmetry se.
