2. First Law - ΔU = q + w (Energy Hisaab)

Energy na banti hai na mitta hai - sirf transfer hoti hai. Sign convention ⭐: system par heat/kaam AAYA → positive; system NE kaam kiya/heat nikali → negative. q aur w ghumao, ΔU dekho. Yaad rakho: q, w path functions hain, ΔU state function!

3. Enthalpy - Exo vs Endothermic ka Energy Diagram ⭐

Reaction coordinate diagram: ΔH negative → exothermic (products neeche, heat NIKLI - jalna) | ΔH positive → endothermic (products upar, heat LI - pighalna). Upar ki choti = activation energy. Toggle karo, |ΔH| badhao, molecule ko safar karte dekho:

4. Gibbs - Final Referee: ΔG = ΔH − TΔS ⭐⭐

Spontaneous hogi ya nahi, faisla SIRF ΔG karta hai: negative → spontaneous ✅ | positive → nahi ❌ | zero → equilibrium ⚖️. ΔH aur ΔS sirf advisors hain. Teen slider ghumao aur 4 quadrant cases dekho - ya preset try karo:

Basic Terms

Open = matter + energy exchange | Closed = sirf energy | Isolated = kuch nahi (thermos) | State functions: U, H, S, G, P, V, T (raasta matter NAHI) | Path: q, w | Extensive: mass, V, U, H | Intensive: T, P, density

First Law ⭐

ΔU = q + w (w = work ON system) | system par aaya → +, system ne kiya → − | w = −P(ext)ΔV | Isothermal: ΔU=0 | Adiabatic: q=0 → ΔU=w | Isochoric: w=0 → ΔU=q(v) | JEE trap: q(v)=ΔU, q(p)=ΔH!

Enthalpy ⭐

H = U + PV | ΔH = ΔU + Δn(g)RT | ΔH < 0 exothermic (heat nikli), ΔH > 0 endothermic | ΔH°(rxn) = ΣΔH°f(products) − ΣΔH°f(reactants) | element standard state me ΔH°f = 0 (O₂ gas, C graphite, Br₂ LIQUID!) | ΔH = ΣBE(todo) − ΣBE(banao)

Hess + Entropy ⭐

Hess: raasta koi bhi, ΔH same (state function!) | equations jodo/ghatao → ΔH bhi | reverse karo → sign FLIP | S = randomness: Gas > Liquid > Solid | ΔS = q(rev)/T | 2nd law: spontaneous me ΔS(universe) > 0

Gibbs ⭐⭐

ΔG = ΔH − TΔS | ΔG<0 spontaneous, >0 nahi, =0 equilibrium | ΔH−ΔS+: hamesha | ΔH+ΔS−: kabhi nahi | ΔH+ΔS+: HIGH T par | ΔH−ΔS−: LOW T par | ΔG° = −RT ln K = −2.303 RT log K | K>1 → products favoured
