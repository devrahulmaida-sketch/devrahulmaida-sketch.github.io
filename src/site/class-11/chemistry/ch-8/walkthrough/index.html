5 animated scenes - hybridization se electrophile attack tak. Shapes dekho, IUPAC naam banao, isomers compare karo, carbocation ki race dekho, curved arrows udaao.

1. Hybridization - Carbon Ki Shapes

Carbon 4 bonds banata hai, par shape hybridization pe depend karti hai: sp³ = tetrahedral 109.5° (sirf single bonds), sp² = trigonal planar 120° (ek double bond), sp = linear 180° (triple bond). Molecule chuno, angle aur σ/π breakdown dekho!

Catenation + tetravalency = LAKHON compounds! Carbon khud se chains/rings banata hai (catenation) aur 4 bonds (tetravalency) - koi aur element itna versatile nahi. Wedge (▲) = plane ke BAHAR, dash = plane ke ANDAR - 3D structure samajhne ka code!

2. IUPAC Naming Machine

Naam banane ke 4 steps: (1) longest chain dhoondo → root word, (2) functional group ko sabse CHHOTA number do, (3) substituents alphabetical prefix me, (4) suffix: -ane/-ene/-yne/-ol... Example chuno, Agla Step dabao!

Trap: "longest chain" straight nahi honi chahiye - zigzag bhi chalti hai! Aur substituents ko lowest SET of numbers do (2,3- 3,4- ko harata hai). Functional group priority sabse upar: -COOH > -CHO > -CO- > -OH > -NH₂.

3. Isomer Lab - Same Formula, Alag Compounds

Structural isomers: connectivity alag (chain, position, functional, metamerism). Stereoisomers: connectivity SAME, 3D arrangement alag (cis-trans, optical). Type chuno, dono structures compare karo!

Feel: structural isomers = same Lego pieces, different build. Cis-trans = double bond pe rotation ROKA hua hai - same side (cis) ya opposite side (trans). Optical isomers = chiral carbon (4 alag groups) ke mirror images - plane-polarised light ghumate hain!

4. Carbocation Stability - Hyperconjugation Race

3° > 2° > 1° > methyl - JEE ka all-time favourite! Kyun? Hyperconjugation: paas wale α-C-H sigma bonds ka electron density empty p-orbital ko support karta hai. Jitne zyada α-H, utna stable. Har carbocation pe click karo!

Carbanion me order ULTA hai (1° > 2° > 3°) - alkyl groups electron DETE hain, electron-rich carbanion ko unstable karte hain! Free radical carbocation JAISE behave karta hai (3° > 2° > 1°). +I effect bhi carbocation ko help karta hai.

5. Electrophile Attack - Curved Arrow Ka Matlab

Electrophile = electron-lover (bhookha): H⁺, NO₂⁺, BF₃. Nucleophile = nucleus-lover (daani): OH⁻, CN⁻, NH₃. Ethene + H⁺ dekho: curved arrow = electron pair ka movement - π bond ke electrons H⁺ ko pakadte hain, doosra carbon carbocation!

Curved arrow ka tail = electrons jahan se jaate hain, head = jahan jaate hain. Full arrowhead = electron PAIR, fishhook (aadha) = single electron (radicals me). Reaction = electrophile aur nucleophile ka milan! Ab Br₂ addition samjho: alkene + Br₂ → brown color GAYAB - unsaturation ka test!
