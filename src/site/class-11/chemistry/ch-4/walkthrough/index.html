Class 11 Chemistry - Chapter 4 - Chemical Bonding

Chemical Bonding Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - electron transfer se MOT tak. Shapes ghumao, angles naapo, formulas feel karo.

1. Bond Kyun Banta Hai - Octet Ka Khel

Na ke paas 1 extra electron, Cl ko 1 chahiye. Transfer button dabao (ionic bond) ya Share dekho (covalent, H₂). Dono ka maqsad: 8 valence electrons ka settled life!

Octet rule: atom 8 valence e⁻ chahta hai - transfer karke (ionic: metal + non-metal) ya share karke (covalent: 2 non-metals). Exceptions exam favourite: BF₃ (6 e⁻), PCl₅ (10 e⁻), SF₆ (12 e⁻), NO/NO₂ (odd e⁻ radicals).

2. VSEPR Shape Machine - Lone Pairs Dabate Hain

Central atom ke bond pairs aur lone pairs chuno. Electron pairs ek-doosre ko dhakka dete hain → shape banti hai. Dekho lone pairs angles ko kaise DABA dete hain!

Repulsion: lp-lp > lp-bp > bp-bp. Angles: CH₄ 109.5° > NH₃ ~107° > H₂O ~104.5° - lone pairs zyada jagah gherte hain, bonds ko dabate hain. Linear 180°, Trigonal planar 120°.

3. Polarity - Dipole Vectors Ka Hisaab

Bond polar hona alag, MOLECULE polar hona alag! Symmetric shape me dipole vectors CANCEL (CO₂), asymmetric me ADD (H₂O). Dono pe click karke dekho.

Electronegativity difference → bond dipole (arrow non-metal ki taraf). μ = vectors ka sum: CO₂/BF₃/CCl₄ symmetric → cancel → NON-POLAR. H₂O bent, NH₃ pyramidal → survive → POLAR. Isliye paani duniya ka best solvent! Trap: CO₂ ke bonds polar, molecule non-polar.

4. Sigma vs Pi - Overlap Ka Farak

σ bond = head-on overlap (seedha, strong). π bond = side-by-side (weak). Animate karo: orbitals kaise milte hain. Double bond = 1σ + 1π, triple = 1σ + 2π!

Hybridization trick: VSEPR count (bp+lp) hi batati hai - 2=sp (linear), 3=sp² (trigonal), 4=sp³ (tetrahedral), 5=sp³d, 6=sp³d². NH₃ aur H₂O dono sp³ (lone pairs bhi count!). Har single bond σ hota hai.

5. MOT - Bond Order Machine

Molecule banne pe atomic orbitals milke molecular orbitals banate hain. Electrons fill karo (button), bond order nikalo: (bonding − antibonding)/2. O₂ ka kamaal: 2 UNPAIRED electrons = paramagnetic!

Bond order = (Nb − Na)/2 - N₂ = 3 (triple, super strong), O₂ = 2, F₂ = 1, He₂ = 0 (exist hi nahi karta!). Antibonding (σ*, π*) me electron = bond KAMZOR. O₂ ke π* me 2 unpaired e⁻ → paramagnetic (MOT hi iska proof hai, VBT fail!).
