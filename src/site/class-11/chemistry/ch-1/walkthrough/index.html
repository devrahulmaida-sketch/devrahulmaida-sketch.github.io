Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo!

Mole Bridge ⭐

1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ particles (N_A) = molar mass grams = 22.4 L gas (STP) | n = m/M | n = N/N_A | n = V/22.4 (gas, STP) | 46 g Na (M=23) → 2 mol → 1.204 × 10²⁴ atoms

Feel The Mole

18 g paani (~1.5 chammach) me 6.022 × 10²³ molecules | 8 billion log × 1/sec count karein → 2.4 million saal! | Avogadro: same T,P par equal volumes = equal molecules (isliye 22.4 L sab gases ka STP pe)

Atoms vs Molecules TRAP ⚠️

Rule: molecule ke andar jitne atoms → moles ko utne se multiply | 1 mol O₂ = 2 mol O atoms | 1 mol H₂O = 3 mol atoms | 1 mol CO₂ = 3 mol atoms | 1.6 g CH₄ = 0.1 mol → 0.5 mol atoms = 3.011 × 10²³

Empirical → Molecular

① % ko gram maano (100 g) ② moles nikalo (mass/atomic mass) ③ sabse chhote se divide ④ ratio → empirical | n = molecular mass / empirical mass → molecular = n × empirical | C 40% H 6.67% O 53.33% (M=180) → CH₂O (30) → n=6 → C₆H₁₂O₆ glucose!

Limiting Reagent + Yield

Equation = recipe | moles ÷ coefficient compare - jo chhota wo LR | product LR se banta hai | 56 g N₂ + 6 g H₂ → H₂ LR → 2 mol NH₃ = 34 g, N₂ ka 1 mol bacha | % yield = (actual / theoretical) × 100
