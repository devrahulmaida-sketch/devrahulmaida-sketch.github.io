Atom Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - gold foil se orbital clouds tak. Alpha maaro, electron kudao, spectrum dekho, configuration bharo.

1. Rutherford Gold-Foil - Atom Mostly KHAALI Hai

α-particles (positive) ko sonay ki foil pe maaro. Zyada tar seedha nikal gaye (atom khali!), kuch thode mude (beech me positive charge), aur 20,000 me ~1 wapas aa gaya (chhota dense nucleus ~10⁻¹⁵ m!).

Conclusions: atom ~10⁻¹⁰ m, nucleus sirf ~10⁻¹⁵ m me poora positive mass. Par Rutherford model ka drawback: ghoomta electron accelerate hota hai → energy radiate karke nucleus me GIR jaana chahiye tha (par girte nahi!) - yahi problem Bohr ne solve ki.

2. Bohr Ki Energy Ladder - Fixed Seedhi Ke Paidaan

Electron kahin bhi nahi reh sakta - sirf fixed levels (n = 1, 2, 3...). Level pe click karo: neeche girega toh photon emit hoga (ΔE = hν), upar jaane ke liye energy absorb karni padegi. En = −13.6/n² eV.

Bohr ke postulates: fixed orbit me energy radiate NAHI hoti; angular momentum mvr = nh/2π quantized; photon tabhi jab jump ho. n = ∞ matlab electron FREE (ionization, 13.6 eV). Limit: sirf 1-electron species (H, He⁺, Li²⁺) pe kaam karta hai.

3. Hydrogen Spectrum - Continuous Rainbow NAHI, Sirf Lines

Electron upar phir neeche gira → light nikli → prism ne alag-alag LINES dikhayi. Series badlo aur dekho lines kahan padti hain: 1/λ = R(1/n₁² − 1/n₂²), R = 109,677 cm⁻¹. Sirf Balmer (n₁=2) visible hai!

Series yaad rakho: Lyman = 1 (UV), Balmer = 2 (visible - isliye H ka red-cyan-violet glow!), Paschen/Brackett/Pfund = 3, 4, 5 (sab IR). Pehli Balmer line 656 nm (H-alpha, red) - universe ki sabse common spectrum line!

4. Orbit NAHI, Orbital - Probability Ka Cloud

Heisenberg: position aur momentum ek saath exact NAHI naap sakte (Δx·Δp ≥ h/4π) - toh fixed orbit ka concept khatam! Electron probability cloud hai. Dots jama hote dekho - jahan zyada dots, wahan zyada chance. Shape badlo:

Quantum numbers = electron ka address: n (shell/size), l (shape: 0=s, 1=p, 2=d), m (orientation), s (spin ±½). 2s me beech ka khaali ring = radial node - wahan electron MILNA impossible hai! Colors = wave ka phase (+ / −).

5. Aufbau Race - Electrons Kaise Bharte Hain

Rules: Aufbau (pehle low energy, (n+l) chhota wala), Pauli (ek orbital me max 2, opposite spins), Hund (same energy me pehle 1-1 parallel). Z slider ghumao aur filling dekho - Cr/Cu pe EXCEPTION dekho!

Z = 8
Order: 1s → 2s → 2p → 3s → 3p → 4s → 3d → 4p. Exceptions ratta maaro: Cr (24) = [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹, Cu (29) = [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s¹ - half/full d extra stable! Electrons nikaalte waqt 4s PEHLE nikalta hai (Fe²⁺ = 3d⁶, 3d⁶4s⁰ nahi... wohi 4s² wala hisaab!).
