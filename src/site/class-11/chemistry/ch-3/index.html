Notes language:

1️⃣ Classification kyun? + Shuruaati koshishein

100+ elements ko aise hi ratta nahi laga sakte — isliye unko pattern me baanta. Chemistry ka sabse bada "cheat code" hi periodic table hai.

  • Dobereiner ki Triads (1829): 3-3 elements ke groups — beech wale ka atomic mass ≈ baaki dono ka average. Jaise Li (7), Na (23), K (39) → (7+39)/2 = 23. Sirf kuch elements par fit hua.
  • Newlands ka Law of Octaves (1866): mass ke order me rakho toh har 8va element pehle jaisa behave karta hai (sa-re-ga-ma jaisa!). Calcium ke baad pattern toot gaya — log hase uspar. 😢
  • Mendeleev (1869): Atomic mass ke hisaab se table banaya — properties "periodic function of atomic masses" hain.

💡 Mendeleev ne himmat se khaali dabbe chhode aur future elements predict kiye: Eka-aluminium → Gallium, Eka-silicon → Germanium. Baad me dono mile — predictions almost exact! Isi se table famous hua.

Mendeleev ki defects: ① Hydrogen ki jagah fix nahi. ② Isotopes (alag mass, same element) kahin fit nahi. ③ Kuch pairs ulte hain: Ar (39.9) K (39.1) se pehle, Co (58.9) Ni (58.7) se pehle, Te (127.6) I (126.9) se pehle.

2️⃣ Modern Periodic Law (Moseley, 1913)

Moseley ne X-ray experiment se dikhaya: elements ka asli identity card mass nahi, atomic number (Z) hai.

Modern Periodic Law: Properties are a periodic function of ATOMIC NUMBER (Z)

Isse Mendeleev ki saari problems solve — isotopes same Z par ek hi dabbe me, aur Ar-K / Co-Ni / Te-I ka order ab properties ke saath match karta hai (Z ke hisaab se sahi hai!).

3️⃣ Long Form Table — 18 groups, 7 periods

Periods (aadi lines)7Left → right shells bharte hain
Blocks4 (s, p, d, f)Last electron jis subshell me gaya
  • s-block: Group 1 (alkali metals) + Group 2 (alkaline earth). ns¹ ya ns².
  • p-block: Group 13-18. ns² np¹⁻⁶. Group 18 = noble gases (full shell, seedha-sada, react nahi karte 😴).
  • d-block: Group 3-12, beech ka pul = transition metals (Fe, Cu, Zn). (n−1)d¹⁻¹⁰ ns¹⁻².
  • f-block: neeche alag 2 lines = lanthanides + actinides (inner transition).
  • Period lengths: 2, 8, 8, 18, 18, 32, 32.
  • Metals / Non-metals / Metalloids: left side metals (78%+), right-up corner non-metals, beech ki zigzag line par metalloids (Si, Ge…).

💡 Z>100 elements ke IUPAC naam roots: 0=nil, 1=un, 2=bi, 3=tri, 4=quad… Jaise Z=104 → Un-nil-quad + ium = Unnilquadium (ab Rutherfordium).

4️⃣ Configuration se Position kaise nikale ⭐

Period number = sabse bada n (valence shell)  |  Group (s,p-block) = valence electrons (+10 for p-block, new IUPAC 1-18)
  • Na: 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s¹ → max n = 3 → Period 3, 1 valence e⁻ → Group 1.
  • Cl: [Ne] 3s² 3p⁵ → Period 3, 2+5 = 7 → 7+10 = Group 17.
  • d-block: group = (n−1)d electrons + ns electrons. Fe: 3d⁶ 4s² → 6+2 = Group 8.

🎯 Trick: p-block me group = valence e⁻ + 10. Noble gas hamesha Group 18 (He chhodo, woh Group 18 but 2 e⁻).

5️⃣ Trend #1: Atomic Radius (size)

  • Left → Right (period): size ghat-ta hai. Kyun? Electrons same shell me add hote hain, par nucleus ka charge badhta hai → zyada tight kheench (effective nuclear charge ↑).
  • Up → Down (group): size badhta hai — naya shell add hota hai (floor upar floor).
  • Cation vs Anion: Na⁺ < Na (electron gaya, size ghiri), Cl⁻ > Cl (electron aaya, phool gayi). Isoelectronic series (same electrons): Z zyada → size chhota. Jaise N³⁻ > O²⁻ > F⁻ > Na⁺ > Mg²⁺.

💡 Feel: period me nuclear charge = magnet — jitna strong, utna tight. Group me naya floor = building oonchi.

6️⃣ Trend #2: Ionization Enthalpy (IE) ⭐

IE = electron ukhaadne ki energy (gaseous atom se). Jitna mushkil, utna bada IE.
  • Left → Right: IE badhta hai (size chhota → electron tight pakda hua).
  • Up → Down: IE ghat-ta hai (size bada → door ka electron aasaani se nikalta hai).
  • Isliye Cs sabse aasaani se electron deta hai (sabse reactive metal), F sabse mushkil (He/Ne/Ar ko chhod kar).

⚠️ Famous exceptions (ratta):

  • Be > B: Be = 1s² 2s² (full s, stable) — electron todna mushkil.
  • N > O: N = 2p³ (half-filled, extra stable) — isliye N ka IE O se zyada.
  • Same logic: Mg > Al, P > S.

💡 Rule: full-filled (s², p⁶, d¹⁰) aur half-filled (p³, d⁵, f⁷) configs extra stable → unka IE padosi se zyada.

7️⃣ Trend #3: Electron Gain Enthalpy + Electronegativity

  • Electron gain enthalpy: electron pakadne par kitni energy release. Jitni negative, utna shauk. Left → right zyada negative, up → down kam negative.
  • ⚠️ Exception: Cl F se zyada negative! F itna chhota hai ki naya electron andar ghusne par repulsion hota hai. Isliye Cl = sabse negative electron gain (exam trap!).
  • Electronegativity (Pauling scale): bond ke electron ko kheenchne ki power. F = 4.0 = sabse zyada. Left → right badhti, up → down ghat-ti. Noble gases ko nahi milti (bond hi nahi banate).
  • Metallic character: left-down corner zyada (Cs) • Non-metallic: right-up corner (F).
  • Oxides: left ke basic (Na₂O), right ke acidic (SO₃), beech ke amphoteric (Al₂O₃, ZnO).
  • Valence: s/p-block me group se — Group 1: +1, Group 2: +2, Group 13: +3, Group 14: ±4, Group 15: −3, Group 16: −2, Group 17: −1.

🎯 Summary trick: Size aur metallic character → left-down. IE, EN, non-metallic → right-up. Bas ye do line yaad rakho, saare trends set!

1️⃣ वर्गीकरण क्यों? + शुरुआती कोशिशें

100+ तत्वों को ऐसे ही रट्टा नहीं लगाया जा सकता — इसलिए उन्हें pattern में बाँटा गया। Chemistry का सबसे बड़ा "cheat code" ही periodic table है।

  • Dobereiner की Triads (1829): 3-3 तत्वों के groups — बीच वाले का atomic mass ≈ बाकी दोनों का average। जैसे Li (7), Na (23), K (39) → (7+39)/2 = 23। सिर्फ कुछ तत्वों पर fit हुआ।
  • Newlands का Law of Octaves (1866): mass के order में रखो तो हर 8वाँ तत्व पहले जैसा behave करता है (sa-re-ga-ma जैसा!)। Calcium के बाद pattern टूट गया।
  • Mendeleev (1869): Atomic mass के हिसाब से table बनाया — properties "atomic masses का periodic function" हैं।

💡 Mendeleev ने हिम्मत से खाली डिब्बे छोड़े और future तत्व predict किए: Eka-aluminium → Gallium, Eka-silicon → Germanium। बाद में दोनों मिले — predictions almost exact! इसी से table famous हुआ।

Mendeleev की defects: ① Hydrogen की जगह fix नहीं। ② Isotopes (अलग mass, same तत्व) कहीं fit नहीं। ③ कुछ pairs उल्टे हैं: Ar (39.9), K (39.1) से पहले; Co (58.9), Ni (58.7) से पहले; Te (127.6), I (126.9) से पहले।

2️⃣ आधुनिक आवर्त नियम (Moseley, 1913)

Moseley ने X-ray experiment से दिखाया: तत्वों का असली identity card mass नहीं, परमाणु संख्या (Z) है।

आधुनिक आवर्त नियम: गुण परमाणु संख्या (Z) के आवर्ती फलन (periodic function) हैं

इससे Mendeleev की सारी problems solve — isotopes same Z पर एक ही डिब्बे में, और Ar-K / Co-Ni / Te-I का order अब properties के साथ match करता है।

4️⃣ Configuration से Position कैसे निकाले ⭐

Period number = सबसे बड़ा n (valence shell)  |  Group (s,p-block) = valence इलेक्ट्रॉन (+10 for p-block, new IUPAC 1-18)
  • Na: 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s¹ → max n = 3 → Period 3, 1 valence e⁻ → Group 1।
  • Cl: [Ne] 3s² 3p⁵ → Period 3, 2+5 = 7 → 7+10 = Group 17।
  • d-block: group = (n−1)d इलेक्ट्रॉन + ns इलेक्ट्रॉन। Fe: 3d⁶ 4s² → 6+2 = Group 8।

🎯 Trick: p-block में group = valence e⁻ + 10। Noble gas हमेशा Group 18 (He छोड़ो, वह Group 18 पर 2 e⁻)।

5️⃣ Trend #1: परमाणु त्रिज्या (Atomic Radius)

  • Left → Right (period): size घटता है। क्यों? इलेक्ट्रॉन same shell में add होते हैं, पर nucleus का charge बढ़ता है → ज़्यादा tight खेंच (effective nuclear charge ↑)।
  • Up → Down (group): size बढ़ता है — नया shell add होता है (floor ऊपर floor)।
  • Cation vs Anion: Na⁺ < Na (इलेक्ट्रॉन गया, size घिरा), Cl⁻ > Cl (इलेक्ट्रॉन आया, फूल गया)। Isoelectronic series (same इलेक्ट्रॉन): Z ज़्यादा → size छोटा। जैसे N³⁻ > O²⁻ > F⁻ > Na⁺ > Mg²⁺।

💡 Feel: period में nuclear charge = magnet — जितना strong, उतना tight। Group में नया floor = building ऊँची।

6️⃣ Trend #2: आयनन एन्थैल्पी (Ionization Enthalpy) ⭐

IE = इलेक्ट्रॉन उखाड़ने की energy (gaseous परमाणु से)। जितना मुश्किल, उतना बड़ा IE।
  • Left → Right: IE बढ़ता है (size छोटा → इलेक्ट्रॉन tight पकड़ा हुआ)।
  • Up → Down: IE घटता है (size बड़ा → दूर का इलेक्ट्रॉन आसानी से निकलता है)।
  • इसलिए Cs सबसे आसानी से इलेक्ट्रॉन देता है (सबसे reactive metal)।

⚠️ Famous exceptions (रट्टा):

  • Be > B: Be = 1s² 2s² (full s, stable) — इलेक्ट्रॉन तोड़ना मुश्किल।
  • N > O: N = 2p³ (half-filled, extra stable) — इसलिए N का IE, O से ज़्यादा।
  • Same logic: Mg > Al, P > S।

💡 Rule: full-filled (s², p⁶, d¹⁰) और half-filled (p³, d⁵, f⁷) configs extra stable → उनका IE पड़ोसी से ज़्यादा।

7️⃣ Trend #3: इलेक्ट्रॉन लब्धि एन्थैल्पी + ऋणविद्युता (Electronegativity)

  • Electron gain enthalpy: इलेक्ट्रॉन पकड़ने पर कितनी energy release। जितनी negative, उतना शौक। Left → right ज़्यादा negative, up → down कम negative।
  • ⚠️ Exception: Cl, F से ज़्यादा negative! F इतना छोटा है कि नया इलेक्ट्रॉन अंदर घुसने पर repulsion होता है। इसलिए Cl = सबसे negative electron gain (exam trap!)।
  • Electronegativity (Pauling scale): bond के इलेक्ट्रॉन को खींचने की power। F = 4.0 = सबसे ज़्यादा। Left → right बढ़ती, up → down घटती। Noble gases को नहीं मिलती (bond ही नहीं बनाते)।
  • Metallic character: left-down corner ज़्यादा (Cs) • Non-metallic: right-up corner (F)।
  • Oxides: left के basic (Na₂O), right के acidic (SO₃), बीच के amphoteric (Al₂O₃, ZnO)।
  • संयोजकता (Valence): Group 1: +1, Group 2: +2, Group 13: +3, Group 14: ±4, Group 15: −3, Group 16: −2, Group 17: −1।

🎯 Summary trick: Size और metallic character → left-down. IE, EN, non-metallic → right-up. बस ये दो lines याद रखो, सारे trends set!

1️⃣ Why classify? + Early attempts

You cannot memorise 100+ elements one by one — so we sort them into a pattern. The periodic table is chemistry's biggest cheat code.

  • Dobereiner's Triads (1829): groups of 3 — the middle element's atomic mass ≈ the average of the other two. E.g. Li (7), Na (23), K (39) → (7+39)/2 = 23. Fit only a few elements.
  • Newlands' Law of Octaves (1866): in mass order, every 8th element behaves like the first (like sa-re-ga-ma!). The pattern broke after calcium — people laughed at him. 😢
  • Mendeleev (1869): built his table on atomic mass — properties are a "periodic function of atomic masses".

💡 Mendeleev boldly left empty boxes and predicted future elements: Eka-aluminium → Gallium, Eka-silicon → Germanium. Both were found later — predictions almost exact! That made the table famous.

Mendeleev's defects: ① No fixed place for hydrogen. ② Isotopes (different mass, same element) fit nowhere. ③ Some pairs are inverted: Ar (39.9) before K (39.1), Co (58.9) before Ni (58.7), Te (127.6) before I (126.9).

2️⃣ Modern Periodic Law (Moseley, 1913)

Moseley's X-ray experiments showed: an element's true identity card is not its mass but its atomic number (Z).

Modern Periodic Law: properties are a periodic function of ATOMIC NUMBER (Z)

This solves all of Mendeleev's problems — isotopes share one box (same Z), and the Ar-K / Co-Ni / Te-I orders now match the properties (they are correct by Z!).

4️⃣ Finding position from configuration ⭐

Period number = highest n (valence shell)  |  Group (s,p-block) = valence electrons (+10 for p-block, IUPAC 1-18)
  • Na: 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s¹ → max n = 3 → Period 3, 1 valence e⁻ → Group 1.
  • Cl: [Ne] 3s² 3p⁵ → Period 3, 2+5 = 7 → 7+10 = Group 17.
  • d-block: group = (n−1)d electrons + ns electrons. Fe: 3d⁶ 4s² → 6+2 = Group 8.

🎯 Trick: for p-block, group = valence e⁻ + 10. Noble gases are always Group 18 (except He — Group 18 with just 2 e⁻).

5️⃣ Trend #1: Atomic Radius (size)

  • Left → Right (period): size decreases. Why? Electrons enter the same shell, but nuclear charge grows → a tighter pull (effective nuclear charge ↑).
  • Up → Down (group): size increases — a new shell is added (floor upon floor).
  • Cation vs Anion: Na⁺ < Na (lost an electron, shrank), Cl⁻ > Cl (gained one, puffed up). In an isoelectronic series (same electrons): bigger Z → smaller size. E.g. N³⁻ > O²⁻ > F⁻ > Na⁺ > Mg²⁺.

💡 Feel: across a period the nucleus is a magnet — stronger magnet, tighter hold. Down a group you add a floor — taller building.

6️⃣ Trend #2: Ionization Enthalpy (IE) ⭐

IE = energy needed to rip out an electron (from a gaseous atom). The harder it is, the bigger the IE.
  • Left → Right: IE increases (smaller size → electron held tight).
  • Up → Down: IE decreases (bigger size → outer electron slips out easily).
  • That's why Cs lets go of its electron most easily (most reactive metal).

⚠️ Famous exceptions (memorise):

  • Be > B: Be = 1s² 2s² (full s subshell, stable) — hard to break.
  • N > O: N = 2p³ (half-filled, extra stable) — so N's IE beats O's.
  • Same logic: Mg > Al, P > S.

💡 Rule: full-filled (s², p⁶, d¹⁰) and half-filled (p³, d⁵, f⁷) configs are extra stable → their IE is higher than their neighbour's.

7️⃣ Trend #3: Electron Gain Enthalpy + Electronegativity

  • Electron gain enthalpy: energy released when an atom grabs an electron. The more negative, the keener the atom. More negative left → right, less negative up → down.
  • ⚠️ Exception: Cl is more negative than F! F is so small that the incoming electron feels repulsion in the cramped space. So Cl has the most negative electron gain enthalpy (exam trap!).
  • Electronegativity (Pauling scale): power to pull the shared bond electrons towards itself. F = 4.0 = highest. Increases left → right, decreases up → down. Noble gases have none (they don't bond).
  • Metallic character: strongest at the bottom-left (Cs) • Non-metallic: top-right (F).
  • Oxides: left ones are basic (Na₂O), right ones acidic (SO₃), middle ones amphoteric (Al₂O₃, ZnO).
  • Valence: Group 1: +1, Group 2: +2, Group 13: +3, Group 14: ±4, Group 15: −3, Group 16: −2, Group 17: −1.

🎯 Master trick: Size and metallic character grow towards bottom-left. IE, EN and non-metallic character grow towards top-right. Remember these two lines and every trend is sorted!

EGE + EN

  • EGE: इलेक्ट्रॉन पकड़ने का शौक
  • Cl > F (F छोटा, repulsion) — trap!
  • EN: F = 4.0 सबसे ज़्यादा
  • Oxides: left basic, right acidic

2-Line Master Trick

  • Size + metallic → left-down corner (Cs)
  • IE + EN + non-metallic → right-up (F)
  • Noble gases: EN नहीं होती
  • Valence: G15=−3, G16=−2, G17=−1

Early Days

  • Dobereiner: triads, middle mass = average
  • Newlands: every 8th repeats (octaves)
  • Mendeleev: atomic mass + predictions
  • Eka-Al = Gallium, Eka-Si = Germanium

Modern Law

  • Moseley: properties ∝ atomic number (Z)
  • Isotope problem solved
  • Ar-K, Co-Ni, Te-I order fixed

Table Layout

  • 18 groups, 7 periods
  • s=G1-2, p=G13-18, d=G3-12, f=below
  • Period length: 2,8,8,18,18,32
  • Group 18 = noble gases 😴

Find Position

  • Period = max n
  • Group (p-block) = valence e⁻ + 10
  • Na=G1, Cl=G17, Fe=G8

Size

  • L→R shrinks (nuclear pull)
  • Up→Down grows (new shell)
  • Cation small, Anion big
  • Isoelectronic: Z ↑ = size ↓

IE ⭐

  • Energy to remove an electron
  • L→R ↑, Up→Down ↓
  • Exceptions: Be>B, N>O, Mg>Al, P>S
  • Why: full/half-filled = stable

EGE + EN

  • EGE: eagerness to grab an electron
  • Cl > F (F cramped, repulsion) — trap!
  • EN: F = 4.0 highest
  • Oxides: left basic, right acidic

2-Line Master Trick

  • Size + metallic → bottom-left (Cs)
  • IE + EN + non-metallic → top-right (F)
  • Noble gases: no EN
  • Valence: G15=−3, G16=−2, G17=−1

JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Na, Mg, Al, Si me sabse zyada pehli ionization enthalpy kiski?
Answer dekho

Si — period me left→right IE badhti hai (nuclear charge badhta, size ghatta). Order: Na < Mg < Al < Si (Mg > Al ka chhota anomaly: Mg ka full 3s² stable).

Q2 N³⁻, O²⁻, F⁻, Na⁺, Mg²⁺ (sab isoelectronic, 10 e⁻) ko size ke increasing order me likho.
Answer dekho

Isoelectronic me jitna zyada nuclear charge, utna chhota: Mg²⁺ < Na⁺ < F⁻ < O²⁻ < N³⁻

Q3 Sabse zyada negative electron gain enthalpy kis element ki hai — F ya Cl?
Answer dekho

Cl! F ka chhota size → added electron pe repulsion zyada → Cl ki EGE sabse negative (F se bhi). Ye classic exam trap hai.

Q4 Period 3 me sabse metallic aur sabse non-metallic element?
Answer dekho

Metallic: Na (sabse left) • Non-metallic: Cl (sabse right, noble gas Ar chhodkar)

Q5 N (Z=7) ki IE1, O (Z=8) se ZYADA kyun hai?
Answer dekho

N ka config 1s²2s²2p³ = half-filled p-subshell (extra stable). O ka 2p⁴ — ek paired electron nikaalna aasaan. Isliye IE1: N > O (anomaly!).

Q6 Pauling scale pe sabse electronegative element? Aur period me trend?
Answer dekho

Fluorine (4.0). Period me left→right electronegativity BADHTI hai, group me upar→neeche GHATTI hai.

Q7 Group 17 me upar se neeche atomic radius ka trend aur reason?
Answer dekho

Badhta hai (F < Cl < Br < I) — har step me naya shell add hota hai, shielding badhti hai.

Q8 Li aur Mg, Be aur Al — in pairs me similar properties kyun?
Answer dekho

Diagonal relationship — period table me diagonal neighbours ka size aur charge density similar hota hai.

Q9 Effective nuclear charge (Z*) period me kaise badalta hai?
Answer dekho

Left→right badhta hai — same shell me protons badhte hain, shielding lagbhag same → electrons zyada tightly khinche jaate hain (size ghatta hai).

Q10 Na₂O, MgO, Al₂O₃, SiO₂, P₄O₁₀, SO₃, Cl₂O₇ — acidic/basic character ka trend?
Answer dekho

Na₂O strongly basic → MgO basic → Al₂O₃ amphoteric → SiO₂ weakly acidic → P, S, Cl oxides increasingly acidic. Period me L→R basic character ghatta, acidic badhta.
