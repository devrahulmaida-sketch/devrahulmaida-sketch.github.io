1. Char Families - Formula Se Pehchano

Hydrocarbon = sirf C + H. Family bond se decide hoti hai: sab single = alkane, ek double = alkene, ek triple = alkyne. n badlo aur H gino - general formula HAMESHA fit hota hai!

carbons (n)n = 4
Feel: alkane = lazy (saturated, kam reactive - par fuel ka raaja: LPG, CNG, petrol!). Alkene/alkyne = active (pi bond mein extra electrons - reaction ke liye ready!). Aromatic = benzene ring wala VIP.

2. Free Radical Halogenation - Chain Reaction

CH4 + Cl2 ko sunlight do aur chain shuru! 3 steps: initiation (radicals bante hain), propagation (radical naya radical paida karta hai), termination (radicals milke khatam). Animation dekho:

Aage kya hota hai: CH3Cl pe yahi chain phir chalti hai - CH3Cl → CH2Cl2 → CHCl3 → CCl4. Isliye products ka MIXTURE milta hai!

3. Markovnikov Ka Rule - H Kahan Jaayega?

Propene + HBr: H us carbon pe jaata hai jahan pehle se ZYADA H hain ("rich get richer")! Kyun? Kyunki is route se zyada stable carbocation banta hai. Peroxide ON karo - rule ULTA ho jaata hai (sirf HBr ke saath!).

Kharash / peroxide effect: anti-Markovnikov SIRF HBr + peroxide ke saath hota hai - HCl aur HI ke saath NAHI. Exam ka favourite trap!

4. sp, sp2, sp3 - Acidity Ka Order

Terminal alkyne (C≡C-H) WEAK ACID hai - alkene/alkane nahi! Wajah: sp carbon ka s-character 50% - electrons ko zyada kheenchta hai, H ko dheela pakadta hai. Hybridisation badlo, s-character dekho:

Acidity order: alkyne > alkene > alkane (sp > sp2 > sp3). Terminal alkyne ka TEST: ammoniacal AgNO3 → white ppt, ammoniacal Cu2Cl2 → red ppt!

5. Benzene - Delocalised Ring Ka VIP

6 C, har ek sp2, aur 6 pi electrons poore ring mein ghoomte hain - isliye benzene extra stable (aromatic!). Isliye woh addition NAHI, substitution karta hai - aromaticity bachani hai! Reactions aur directive influence try karo:

Huckel nn = 1
Proof of stability: benzene bina catalyst ke Br2 ka colour NAHI udta (addition nahi karta). Huckel rule: cyclic + planar + (4n+2) pi electrons = aromatic. Benzene: n=1, 6 electrons - perfect!
