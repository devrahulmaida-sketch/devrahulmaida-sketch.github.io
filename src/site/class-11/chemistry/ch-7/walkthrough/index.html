Class 11 Chemistry - Chapter 7 - Redox Reactions

Redox Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - electron transfer se electrochemical series tak. Electrons ko udte dekho, oxidation number nikalo, reaction types pehchano, half-reaction se balance karo, metals ka duel karao.

1. Electron Ka Lena-Dena - OIL RIG

Oxidation Is Loss, Reduction Is Gain (of electrons). Zn ko Cu²⁺ wale blue solution me daalo: Zn ke 2 electrons Cu²⁺ ko udke jaate hain - Zn oxidise (ON 0→+2), Cu²⁺ reduce (ON +2→0). Blue color bhi gayab!

Dono HAMESHA saath hote hain - electron ka daan koi deta hai to koi leta hi hai! Jo electron DETA hai (oxidise hota hai) woh reducing agent, jo LETA hai woh oxidising agent. Socho: daan karne wala khud "gareeb" (oxidised) ho gaya!

2. Oxidation Number Machine

ON = electron transfer track karne ka imaginary charge. Rules simple: free element = 0, O usually −2, H usually +1, F hamesha −1, neutral compound me sab ON ka sum = 0 (ion me = charge). Compound chuno, machine solve karegi!

Ye 3 numbers RATT lo: KMnO₄ me Mn = +7, H₂SO₄ me S = +6, Cr₂O₇²⁻ me Cr = +6 - JEE/CBSE ke evergreens! Exceptions alag yaad karo: peroxide me O = −1, superoxide me −½, metal hydride me H = −1, OF₂ me O = +2 (F se zyada electronegative kuch nahi!).

3. Redox Ke 4 Types - ON Arrows Pehchano

Har type me ON ke arrows dekho: upar gaya = oxidation, neeche aaya = reduction. Sabse tricky hai disproportionation - EK HI element ka oxidation AUR reduction dono!

Combination = decomposition ka ulta (2+ → 1 vs 1 → 2+). Displacement me activity series ka UPAR wala metal NEECH wale ko salt solution se dhakka deta hai - isliye Zn, blue CuSO₄ ka color uda deta hai!

4. Half-Reaction Method - Step Machine

MnO₄⁻ + Fe²⁺ → Mn²⁺ + Fe³⁺ (acidic medium) ko balance karte hain - EXAM FAVOURITE method: todo, atoms balance karo, charge balance karo, electrons barabar karo, jodo! Agla Step dabate jao.

Basic medium me? Pehle acidic ki tarah balance karo, phir har H⁺ ke liye dono taraf OH⁻ add kar do - H⁺ + OH⁻ = H₂O ban jaayega. Oxidation number method bhi yehi karta hai, par half-reaction zyada reliable hai tricky cases me!

5. Electrochemical Series - Metal Duel

Series me jitna UPAR (zyada negative E°), utna strong reducing agent (electron daan me sabse generous: Li!). Ek metal doosre ke ion ko TABHI reduce karta hai jab woh usse UPAR ho. Metal aur ion chuno, duel karao!

KMnO₄ titration yaad rakho: purple KMnO₄ khud apna indicator hai - endpoint pe halka PINK reh jaata hai! Mn +7 se +2 tak reduce hota hai. Aur SHE (standard hydrogen electrode) = reference, E° = 0 V.
