Class 11 Chemistry - Chapter 6 - Equilibrium

Equilibrium Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - dynamic balance se Ksp tak. Particles ko convert hote dekho, Q vs K ka compass ghumao, Le Chatelier ka system hilao, pH scale khelo, precipitate girao.

1. Dynamic Equilibrium - Ruka Nahi, Chalta Balance

A ⇌ B reaction chalao. Shuru me sab A (red). Jab forward speed = backward speed ho jaati hai → counts CONSTANT dikhte hain... par particles ab bhi flip ho rahe hain! Yehi hai dynamic equilibrium.

Mall ki escalator yaad karo: upar jaane wale = neeche aane wale → har floor pe log SAME dikhte hain, par sab chal rahe hain. Equilibrium sirf closed system me aata hai - khule me products bhaag jaate hain!

2. Q vs K - Reaction Kis Taraf Jayegi?

A ⇌ B ke liye K = [B]/[A] = 2 (fixed, sirf temperature badlega toh badlega). Slider se starting mix chuno, Q nikalo: Q < K → aage (products banao), Q > K → peeche, Q = K → equilibrium!

A = 80 (B = 20)
Q = abhi kahan ho, K = manzil. Q chhota → aage badho, Q bada → wapas aao. Yaad rakho: K sirf temperature par depend karta hai - concentration, pressure, catalyst se K NAHI badalta!

3. Le Chatelier - System Ka Ego (Haber Process)

N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ + heat - equilibrium pe dabaav daalo, system usko utralne ki koshish karega. Buttons dabao aur shift dekho. Catalyst ka jawab tumhe hara dega!

Haber process ka poora funda isi scene me hai: high pressure (4 gas moles → 2), moderate temp (zyada temp = backward shift, kam temp = slow!), catalyst (jaldi pahuncho, position SAME). Khana isi se aata hai! 🌾

4. pH Scale - Acidity Ka Number

pH = −log[H⁺], pH + pOH = 14 (25°C). Slider se [H⁺] badlo aur scale pe dekho. 1 point = 10× ka difference - pH 3 vs pH 5 matlab 100 guna zyada acidic!

[H⁺] = 10^ = 10⁻⁷ M
Trap alert: strong ≠ concentrated! "Strong" = 100% dissociation, "concentrated" = zyada amount. Patla HCl bhi strong hai. Blood ka buffer (H₂CO₃/HCO₃⁻) pH 7.4 pe rakhta hai - buffer = weak acid + uska salt, pH ko resist karta hai. 🩸

5. Ksp - Precipitate Kab Girega?

AgCl ⇌ Ag⁺ + Cl⁻, Ksp = [Ag⁺][Cl⁻] = 1.8×10⁻¹⁰. Sliders se ions daalo: ionic product > Ksp → precipitate girega, < Ksp → aur gholega. Ksp = paani ki "seating capacity"!

log[Ag⁺] 10⁻⁵
log[Cl⁻] 10⁻⁵
Common ion effect: NaCl daalte hi [Cl⁻] badha → ionic product > Ksp → AgCl gir gaya (solubility GHAT gayi - Le Chatelier wapas!). AgCl (1:1) ke liye Ksp = s², par CaF₂ (1:2) ke liye Ksp = 4s³ - coefficients MATTER karte hain!
