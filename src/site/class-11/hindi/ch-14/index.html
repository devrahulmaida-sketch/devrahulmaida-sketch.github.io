वे आँखें

Ve Aankhen — Sumitranandan Pant

Uss kisan ki aankhen jiska sab kuch loot liya gaya

Board Weightage: HIGH Video available Long + Short notes Hindi • English • Hinglish
Watch on YouTube Notes

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Kavi aur Kavita Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Sumitranandan Pant ⭐⭐: Chhayavad ke chaar stambhon mein se ek (1900-1977); prakriti ke sukumar kavi kehlate hain; Jnanpith puraskar (Chidambara) se sammanit.
  • Kavita ka swaroop ⭐⭐⭐: Pant ke pragativadi charan ki kavita — yahan woh prakriti-prem se samajik yatharth ki or aate hain.
  • Kendriya vishay ⭐⭐⭐: Bharatiya kisan ke shoshan aur dayaniya dasha ka marmik chitran.
  • Kavita ki bhaav-bhumi ⭐⭐: kavi ko us kisan ki aankhen andhkar ki guha jaisi lagti hain, jinse man darta hai.

💡 Pant = Chhayavadi + pragativadi mod | vishay = shoshit kisan | pratik = andhkar ki guha jaisi aankhen.

2️⃣ Kavita-Saransh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Andhkar ki guha ⭐⭐⭐: kisan ki aankhen andhkar ki guha jaisi hain — unme dur tak bhara daarun dainya aur dukh ka neerav rodan hai; kavi ka man unhe dekh kar dar jaata hai.
  • Swadheen kisan ⭐⭐⭐: kisan kabhi swadheen tha, aankhon mein abhimaan tha — par aaj sansaar use majhdhaar mein chhod kar kagar ki tarah bah gaya.
  • Khet chhin gaye ⭐⭐⭐: jin lahlahate khet se woh bedakhal hua, unki harali uske jeevan ki hansi thi — aaj woh sab sirf aankhon mein ghoomte hain.
  • Beta maara gaya ⭐⭐⭐: uska jawaan beta karindon ki lathi se javani mein hi maara gaya — woh aankhon ka tara tha.
  • Patni aur ghar ⭐⭐⭐: bina dawa-darpan ke patni bhi chali gayi; mahajan ka karz aur zamindar ke shoshan ne ghar-dwaar bhi chheen liya.
  • Smriti ka dard ⭐⭐: purane sukh ki yaad aankhon mein kshan bhar chamak laati hai, par phir dard mein badal jaati hai.

🎯 Sequence: andhkar ki guha jaisi aankhen → swadheen kisan ka abhimaan → khet chhine → beta karindon ki lathi se mara → patni bina dawa ke gayi → smriti ka dard.

3️⃣ Kaavya Saundarya ⭐⭐⭐

  • Upama aur roopak ⭐⭐⭐: 'andhkar ki guha sarikhi' — upama; kisan ka kagar ki tarah chhoot jaana bhi sundar bimb.
  • Karun ras ⭐⭐⭐: poori kavita karun ras se bhari hai — padhne wale ki samvedna jagti hai.
  • Bimb ⭐⭐: lahlahate khet, lathi, sookhi aankhen — jeevant bimb.
  • Bhasha ⭐⭐: tatsam-pradhan, sahaj khadi boli — Pant ki komal shabdavali yahan dard ke saath judti hai.

💡 Alankar: upama/roopak | ras: karun | bimb: aankhen, khet, lathi.

4️⃣ Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Samajik asamanta ⭐⭐⭐: kavita zamindari aur sahukari vyavastha ke shoshan par prashn uthaati hai.
  • Kisan ki dasha ⭐⭐⭐: annadata kahe jaane wale kisan ki behaali ka sach.
  • Samvedansheelta ⭐⭐: samaj ko shoshit ke prati karuna aur nyay ki bhavna rakhni chahiye.

🎯 Message: shoshan ke khilaaf chetna + kisan ke prati karuna.

१. कवि और कविता

  • पंत ⭐⭐: छायावाद के प्रमुख कवि।
  • स्वरूप ⭐⭐⭐: प्रगतिवादी कविता।
  • विषय ⭐⭐⭐: शोषित किसान।

२. सारांश

  • आँखें ⭐⭐⭐: अंधकार की गुफा जैसी।
  • शोषण ⭐⭐⭐: ज़मींदार, कारिंदे और महाजन।
  • क्षति ⭐⭐⭐: खेत, बेटा, पत्नी, घर।

३. काव्य-सौंदर्य

  • उपमा ⭐⭐⭐: अंधकार की गुफा।
  • रस ⭐⭐⭐: करुण रस।
  • भाषा ⭐⭐: तत्सम-प्रधान खड़ी बोली।

४. संदेश

  • शोषण ⭐⭐⭐: ज़मींदारी व्यवस्था पर प्रश्न।
  • किसान ⭐⭐⭐: अन्नदाता की बदहाली।
  • संवेदना ⭐⭐: न्याय और करुणा।

1. Poet and Poem

  • Pant ⭐⭐: a leading Chhayavadi poet.
  • Form ⭐⭐⭐: a progressive-phase poem.
  • Theme ⭐⭐⭐: the exploited farmer.

2. Summary

  • Eyes ⭐⭐⭐: like a cave of darkness.
  • Exploitation ⭐⭐⭐: landlord, agents and moneylender.
  • Loss ⭐⭐⭐: fields, son, wife, home.

3. Poetic Beauty

  • Simile ⭐⭐⭐: a cave of darkness.
  • Rasa ⭐⭐⭐: pathos.
  • Language ⭐⭐: Sanskrit-leaning Khari Boli.

4. Message

  • Exploitation ⭐⭐⭐: questions the landlord system.
  • Farmer ⭐⭐⭐: the plight of the provider.
  • Empathy ⭐⭐: justice and compassion.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Ve Aankhen kavita ke kavi kaun hain?
Answer dekho

Sumitranandan Pant — Chhayavad ke pramukh kavi; is kavita mein unka pragativadi swar hai.

Q2 Kavita ka mukhya vishay kya hai?
Answer dekho

Shoshit aur dukhi kisan ki dayaniya dasha aur uski aankhon mein bhara dard.

Q3 Kavi ko kisan ki aankhen kaisi lagti hain?
Answer dekho

Andhkar ki guha jaisi, jinme daarun dainya aur dukh ka neerav rodan hai; kavi ka man unse darta hai.

Q4 'Swadheen kisan' ka kya arth hai?
Answer dekho

Woh kisan jiske paas apne khet aur aankhon mein abhimaan tha — aaj use sab kuch chheen kar majhdhaar mein chhod diya gaya.

Q5 Kisan apne khet se bedakhal kyun hua?
Answer dekho

Zamindar aur mahajan ke shoshan ke karan uske khet, ghar aur jeevan ke sadhan chheen liye gaye.

Q6 Kisan ke jawaan bete ke saath kya hua?
Answer dekho

Woh karindon ki lathi se javani mein hi maara gaya.

Q7 Kisan ki patni ke saath kya hua?
Answer dekho

Woh bina dawa-darpan ke chal basi.

Q8 Kavita mein kaun sa ras hai?
Answer dekho

Karun ras.

Q9 Kavita mein kaun sa alankar dikhta hai?
Answer dekho

Upama (andhkar ki guha sarikhi) aur anya prateekatmak prayog.

Q10 Kavita ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Samajik asamanta aur shoshan ke khilaaf chetna tatha kisan ke prati karuna aur nyay.
