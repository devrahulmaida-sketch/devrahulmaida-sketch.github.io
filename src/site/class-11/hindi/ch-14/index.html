Notes language:

1️⃣ Kavi aur Kavita Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Sumitranandan Pant ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: mool naam Gosain Datt; janm san 1900, Kausani, zila Almora (Uttaranchal); mrityu san 1977; Chhayavad ke mahatvapurn stambh, prakriti ke chitere kavi.
  • Rachnayen aur samman ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: Veena, Granthi, Pallav, Gunjan, Yugvani, Gramya, Chidambara, Uttara, Swarn Kiran, Kala aur Boodha Chand, Lokayatan aadi. Samman: Bharatiya Gyanpeeth, Sahitya Akademi, Soviet Land Nehru puraskar, Padmabhushan (parichay yeh nahin batata ki kaun sa samman kis rachna par mila).
  • Kavita ka swaroop ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: Ve ankhen Pant ji ke pragatisheel daur ki kavita hai; isme vikas ki virodhabhasi avadharnaon par karara prahar kiya gaya hai. Source note: yeh poora path NCERT Aroh 2021-22 edition (archived PDF) se hai; current PDF (Reprint 2026-27) mein is path ka text nahin mila; current syllabus status ka koi claim nahi.
  • Bhaav-bhumi ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: yug-yug se shoshan ke shikar kisan ka jeevan kavi ko aahat karta hai; swadhin Bharat mein bhi kisanon ko kendra mein rakhkar vyavastha ne nirnayak hastakshep nahin kiya; kavita vyaktigat evam parivarik dukhon ki parton ko kholti hai.

💡 Pant (1900-1977) + pragatisheel daur ki kavita + shoshit kisan ka parivar + Edition: archived 2021-22.

2️⃣ Kavita-Saransh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Andhkar ki guha ⭐⭐⭐: Text: andhkar ki guha sarikhi un ankhon se darta hai man; bhara door tak unmein darun dainya dukh ka neerav rodan (shabd-arth: sarikhi = saman; darun = ghor, nirday, kathor). Book Q1 (kha): un ankhon kiski or sanket.
  • Swadheen kisan ⭐⭐⭐: Text: vah swadheen kisan raha, abhiman bhara ankhon mein iska; chhod use manjhdhar aaj sansar kagar sadrish bah khiska.
  • Khet aur bedakhal ⭐⭐⭐: Text: lehrate ve khet drigon mein; hua bedakhal vah ab jinse; hansti thi uske jeevan ki hariyali jinke trin-trin se. Shabd-arth: bedakhal = hissedari se alag karna.
  • Beta ⭐⭐⭐: Text: ankhon hi mein ghuma karta vah uski ankhon ka tara, karkunon ki lathi se jo gaya jawani hi mein mara. Shabd-arth: karkun = zamindaron ke karinde.
  • Ghar aur parivar ⭐⭐⭐: Text: bika diya ghar dwar, mahajan ne na byaj ki kaudi chhodi; kurk hui bardhon ki jodi; gharni bina dava-darpan ke swarg chali; dudhmunhi bitiya do din baad gayi mar; vidhwa patohu ko kotwal ne pakad mangaya, doob kuen mein mari ek din.
  • Smriti ⭐⭐: Text: pichhle sukh ki smriti ankhon mein kshan bhar ek chamak hai laati; turat shunya mein gad vah chitwan teekhi nok sadrish ban jaati (shabd-arth: chitwan = drishti).

🎯 Sequence: andhkar ki guha jaisi ankhen -> swadheen kisan -> khet aur bedakhal -> beta karkunon ki lathi se mara -> ghar-dwar bika, bardhon ki jodi kurk -> gharni, bitiya, patohu -> smriti ki teekhi nok.

3️⃣ Kaavya Saundarya ⭐⭐⭐

  • Chitra bhasha ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: Pant apni rachnaon ki bhasha ko chitra bhasha (bimbatmak bhasha) kehte hain; Brajbhasha aur khadi boli ke vivad mein khadi boli ka paksh liya aur Pallav ki bhumika mein uska samarthan kiya.
  • Sarikhi, sadrish ⭐⭐⭐: Text: andhkar ki guha sarikhi; sansar kagar sadrish bah khiska; teekhi nok sadrish ban jaati. Teaching background: exam mein in tulna-vachak shabdon se upama alankar bataya jaata hai; NCERT parichay alankar ka naam nahin deta.
  • Bimb ⭐⭐: Text mein drishya-bimb: andhkar ki guha, lehrate khet, bardhon ki jodi, dudhmunhi bitiya, kuen, saanp lotte, teekhi nok - yeh supported reading hai ki kavi chitra bhasha se dard dikhata hai.
  • Bhasha ⭐⭐: Text mein shabd-chhavi ke shabd: sarikhi, darun, chitwan, bedakhal, karkun, kurk, bardhon, gharni. Supported reading: sahaj khadi boli aur dehati shabd milkar dard ko saaf dikhate hain.

💡 Tiers: explicit (text/parichay) / supported reading / teaching background.

4️⃣ Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Explicit (NCERT parichay) ⭐⭐⭐: Vikas ki virodhabhasi avadharnaon par karara prahar; swadhin Bharat mein bhi kisanon ko kendra mein rakhkar vyavastha ne nirnayak hastakshep nahin kiya; kavita spasht roop se vibhajit samaj ki vargiya chetna ka khaka prastut karti hai.
  • Supported reading ⭐⭐: Poem mein mahajan (ghar dwar bika, byaj ki kaudi na chhodi), karkunon ki lathi (beta mara) aur kotwal (patohu ko pakad mangaya) ke naam aate hain; karkun = zamindaron ke karinde (shabd-chhavi). Book Q2: kisan ki peeda ke liye kinhein zimmedar bataya gaya.
  • Teaching background ⭐: Book Aas-paas task: kisan apne vyavsay se palayan kar rahe hain - charcha aur karanon ki padtal; Q5: vartman samaj aur stri par lekh. Is path mein koi aankda ya tareekh nahin di gayi.

🎯 Message: kisan ke shoshan ke prati chetna aur karuna | tiers: explicit / supported / background.

१. कवि और कविता

  • पंत ⭐⭐: मूल नाम गोसाईं दत्त; जन्म 1900 कौसानी (अल्मोड़ा); मृत्यु 1977; छायावाद के महत्वपूर्ण स्तंभ।
  • स्वरूप ⭐⭐⭐: पंत के प्रगतिशील दौर की कविता (NCERT परिचय); पाठ 2021-22 संस्करण (संग्रहित) से।
  • विषय ⭐⭐⭐: शोषण का शिकार किसान और उसका परिवार।

२. सारांश

  • आँखें ⭐⭐⭐: अंधकार की गुफा सरीखी; दारुण दैन्य और दुख का नीरव रोदन।
  • हानि ⭐⭐⭐: खेत बेदखल, बेटा करकुनों की लाठी से मरा, घर-द्वार बिका, बरधों की जोड़ी कुर्क।
  • परिवार ⭐⭐⭐: घरनी, दुधमुँही बिटिया और पतोहू की मृत्यु।

३. काव्य-सौंदर्य

  • सरीखी, सदृश ⭐⭐⭐: ‘अंधकार की गुफा सरीखी’; ‘कगार सदृश बह खिसका’।
  • चित्र भाषा ⭐⭐⭐: पंत इसे बिंबात्मक भाषा कहते हैं (NCERT परिचय)।
  • शब्द ⭐⭐: सरीखी, दारुण, चितवन, बेदखल, कारकुन, कुर्क।

४. संदेश

  • स्पष्ट (परिचय) ⭐⭐⭐: स्वाधीन भारत में भी किसानों को केंद्र में रखकर निर्णायक हस्तक्षेप नहीं हुआ।
  • पठन ⭐⭐: महाजन, कारकुन और कोतवाल कविता में दुख के कारण के रूप में आते हैं।
  • पृष्ठभूमि ⭐: अभ्यास में किसानों के पलायन पर चर्चा का कार्य।

1. Poet and Poem

  • Pant ⭐⭐: original name Gosain Datt; born 1900 Kausani (Almora); died 1977; a major pillar of Chhayavad (NCERT introduction).
  • Form ⭐⭐⭐: a poem of Pant's progressive phase (NCERT introduction); text from the archived 2021-22 edition.
  • Theme ⭐⭐⭐: an exploited farmer and his family.

2. Summary

  • Eyes ⭐⭐⭐: like a cave of darkness; full of harsh poverty and silent weeping.
  • Loss ⭐⭐⭐: fields taken, son killed by the karkuns' stick, home sold, pair of oxen auctioned.
  • Family ⭐⭐⭐: the wife, the infant daughter and the widowed daughter-in-law die.

3. Poetic Beauty

  • Comparison words ⭐⭐⭐: sarikhi, sadrish (like).
  • Picture language ⭐⭐⭐: Pant calls it imagistic language (NCERT introduction).
  • Words ⭐⭐: sarikhi, darun, chitwan, bedakhal, karkun, kurk.

4. Message

  • Explicit ⭐⭐⭐: even in free India, no decisive step centred on farmers (NCERT introduction).
  • Reading ⭐⭐: moneylender, karkuns and kotwal appear as causes of suffering in the poem.
  • Background ⭐: the book asks for discussion on farmers leaving farming; no figures are given.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Ve ankhen kavita ke kavi kaun hain?
Answer dekho

Sumitranandan Pant - NCERT parichay: mool naam Gosain Datt, janm san 1900, Kausani (Almora), mrityu san 1977; Chhayavad ke mahatvapurn stambh.

Q2 Kavita ka mukhya vishay kya hai?
Answer dekho

Ek shoshit kisan aur uska parivar: khet bedakhal, ghar dwar bika, beta karkunon ki lathi se mara (NCERT parichay: kisanon ke vyaktigat evam parivarik dukhon ki parten).

Q3 Kavi ko un ankhon se dar kyon lagta hai?
Answer dekho

Text: andhkar ki guha sarikhi un ankhon se darta hai man; bhara door tak unmein darun dainya dukh ka neerav rodan. (Book Q1 ga yahi poochta hai.)

Q4 Swadheen kisan ke baare mein kavita kya kehti hai?
Answer dekho

Text: vah swadheen kisan raha, abhiman bhara ankhon mein iska; chhod use manjhdhar aaj sansar kagar sadrish bah khiska.

Q5 Kisan ke ghar-dwar aur khet ke baare mein kavita kya kehti hai?
Answer dekho

Text: hua bedakhal vah ab jinse (lehrate khet); bika diya ghar dwar, mahajan ne na byaj ki kaudi chhodi; kurk hui bardhon ki jodi (kurk = neelami; bardhon = bailon).

Q6 Kisan ke jawan bete ke saath kya hua?
Answer dekho

Text: karkunon ki lathi se jo gaya jawani hi mein mara. Shabd-arth: karkun = zamindaron ke karinde.

Q7 Kisan ke parivar ki auraton aur bitiya ke saath kya hua?
Answer dekho

Text: bina dava darpan ke gharni swarg chali (gharni = gharwali, patni); dudhmunhi bitiya do din baad gayi mar; vidhwa patohu: pakad mangaya kotwal ne, doob kuen mein mari ek din.

Q8 Kavita ka bhaav aur ras kaisa hai?
Answer dekho

Text mein neerav rodan, darun dainya dukh, fatti chhati jaise shabd aate hain. Teaching background: exam mein ise karun ras kaha jaata hai; NCERT parichay ras ka naam nahin deta.

Q9 Kavita mein sarikhi aur sadrish jaise shabd kya dikhate hain?
Answer dekho

Text: andhkar ki guha sarikhi, kagar sadrish, teekhi nok sadrish. Teaching background: tulna-vachak shabdon se upama alankar bataya jaata hai.

Q10 Kavita ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Explicit (NCERT parichay): swadhin Bharat mein bhi kisanon ko kendra mein rakhkar vyavastha ne nirnayak hastakshep nahin kiya. Supported reading: poem mein mahajan, karkun aur kotwal dukh ke karan ke roop mein aate hain (book Q2). Teaching background: book ke Aas-paas task mein kisanon ke palayan par charcha ka kaam hai; koi aankda nahin diya gaya.
