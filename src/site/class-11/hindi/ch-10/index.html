Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Lekhak aur Path Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Syed Haider Raza ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: janm 1922, Babaria gaon (M.P.); mrityu 2016; samman Grade of Officer of the Order of Arts and Letters. Shiksha Nagpur School of Art aur Sir J.J. School of Art, Mumbai se; 1950 mein French sarkar ki chhatravritti par France gaye. Hussain aur Souza ke saath unka naam; Raza Paris mein bas gaye; bindu unki kala-rachna ke kendra mein.
  • Path ka swaroop ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT: yeh path Raza ki atmakathatmak pustak Aatma ka Taap ka ek adhyay hai; angrezi se Hindi anuvad Madhu B. Joshi ne kiya. Source note: yeh page NCERT Aroh 2021-22 edition ke paath par based hai (archived NCERT PDF); current syllabus status ka koi claim nahi.
  • Kendriya vishay ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT: Raza ne chitrakala mein apne aarambhik sangharsh aur safaltaon ke baare mein bataya hai; kalakar ka jeevan-sangharsh, sarjanatmak bechaini, apni rachna mein sarvasva jhonkne ka junoon.
  • Shirshak ke baare mein ⭐⭐: Text mein Raza likhte hain ki Bombay mein ek urja aur kaam karne ki ichchha thi; aatma ko chadha yeh taap logon ko dikhai deta tha. Aage ve apni aatma, apne chitt ko santapt rakhne ki baat karte hain.

💡 Raza + Aatma ka Taap ka ek adhyay + Madhu B. Joshi ka anuvad + Nagpur-Bombay-Paris + Edition: NCERT 2021-22 (archived).

2️⃣ Path-Saransh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Nagpur se Gondiya ⭐⭐⭐: Nagpur school ki pariksha mein class mein pratham, das mein se nau vishayon mein vishesh yogyata. Pita retire ho chuke the; Raza Gondiya mein drawing adhyapak bane. Mahine-bhar mein J.J. School ke liye Madhya Prant ki sarkari chhatravritti mili (September 1943).
  • Dakhila band ⭐⭐⭐: Amravati ke school se tyagpatra diya; Bombay pahunchne tak J.J. School mein dakhila band; chhatravritti wapas le li gayi. Sarkar ne Akola mein drawing adhyapak ki naukri offer ki, par Raza ne tay kiya ki lautenge nahi, Bombay mein hi adhyayan karenge.
  • Express Block aur Jacob Circle ⭐⭐⭐: Express Block Studio mein design ki naukri; saal-bhar mein Shri Jaleel aur Shri Hussain ne mukhya designer bana diya. Subah 10 se shaam 6 office, phir Mohan Art Club; rehne ke liye Jacob Circle ka chhota kamra, jo bhai ke parichit ek taxi driver ne de rakha tha.
  • Hatya ki vardat ⭐⭐⭐: Ek raat 9:30 baje darwaze par policewala mila: yahan hatya ki vardat hui hai. Raza commissioner se mile; commissioner se baat hui; text mein taxi driver dost Ralph ki taxi mein ek sawari ko chhura maare jaane ka zikr hai. Agle din Jaleel sahab ko batane par unhone art department mein jagah di; Raza farsh par soye.
  • Pehchan ⭐⭐⭐: November 1943 mein Art Society of India ki pradarshani mein pehle do chitra; Rudolf von Leyden ne Times of India mein sameeksha ki; dono jalrang 40-40 rupaye mein bik gaye. Walter Langhammer ne apna studio diya; Emmanuel Schlesinger prashansak bane; 1947 mein Raza J.J. School ke niyamit chhatra bane. 1948 mein Bombay Art Society ka swarn padak (sabse kam aayu ke kalakar).
  • Kathin baras aur Kashmir ⭐⭐⭐: 1947 aur 1948 mere liye kathin baras: mata ka dehant, May 1948 mein pita ka; saath mein vibhajan aur Mahatma Gandhi ki hatya. 1948 mein Srinagar; Sheikh Abdullah ka patra saath tha; policewale ne bus se utara, patra dekhkar salaam kiya aur maafi maangi. Henri Cartier-Bresson: rang hai, bhavna hai, rachna nahi; Cezanne ka kaam dhyan se dekho.
  • Paris ⭐⭐⭐: 1950 mein French embassy ke sanskritik sachiv se vartalap; Raza ke pasandida kalakar Cezanne, Van Gogh, Gauguin, Picasso, Matisse, Chagall, Braque; Picasso genius hai; do baras ki chhatravritti; 2 October 1950 ko Marseilles; Paris mein jeevan shuru.

🎯 Sequence: Nagpur -> Gondiya -> J.J. scholarship (Sept 1943) -> Akola ki offer na lena -> Express Block -> Jacob Circle -> Jaleel sahab -> 1943 ke do chitra -> 1948 swarn padak -> Cartier-Bresson -> Marseilles (2 Oct 1950).

3️⃣ Charitra aur Seekh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Nishchay aur mehnat ⭐⭐⭐: Raza ne Bombay mein hi padhne ka nishchay kiya; Express Block mein aath-das ghanta kaam, phir bhi mahine-bhar mein itne rupaye nahi mil paate the - phir bhi kaam jaari rakha (text).
  • Madad karne wale ⭐⭐: Text mein Jaleel sahab (art department ka kamra), Langhammer (apna studio), Leyden aur Schlesinger (prashansa/kharidari) aur taxi driver (rehne ki jagah) Raza ki madad karte dikhte hain; book ka Aas-paas Q1 (Jaleel sahab ke bina?) isi par apna tark maangta hai.
  • Dhairya aur aatma-vishwas ⭐⭐: Text: Raza ne khud ko yaad dilaya bhagwan ke ghar der hai, andhera nahi.
  • Sikhne ki lagan ⭐⭐: Cartier-Bresson ki tippani ka gehra prabhav: Raza ne Alliance Francaise mein French seekhi aur chitra ki rachna samajhna chaha.

💡 Raza = nishchay + mehnat + madad + aatmavishwas + sikhne ki lagan.

4️⃣ Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Explicit (text) ⭐⭐⭐: Raza yuvaon se kehte hain: chitrakala vyavsay nahi, antaratma ki pukar hai; sarvasva dekar hi thos parinam milte hain; hath par hath dhare na baithe raho - khud kuch karo.
  • Supported reading ⭐⭐: Path ke sangharsh, madad aur mehnat ke prasang dikhate hain ki lagan aur doosron ki madad dono ne Raza ka raasta banaya (book Aas-paas Q1 isi par vichar poochta hai).
  • Teaching background ⭐: Exam mein saadhna se safalta jaisi line likh sakte hain, par text ka apna shabd taap aur antaratma ki pukar hai - unka hawala dein.

🎯 Conclusion: antaratma ki pukar + sangharsh + madad. Tiers: explicit / supported reading / teaching background.

१. लेखक और पाठ

  • रज़ा ⭐⭐: जन्म 1922 बाबरिया गाँव; मृत्यु 2016; चित्रकार।
  • स्वरूप ⭐⭐⭐: आत्मकथात्मक पुस्तक का अध्याय; अनुवाद मधु बी. जोशी; NCERT 2021-22 संस्करण (संग्रहित)।
  • विषय ⭐⭐⭐: आरंभिक संघर्ष और सफलताएँ; सर्जनात्मक बेचैनी।

२. सारांश

  • नागपुर से बंबई ⭐⭐⭐: कक्षा में प्रथम; गोंदिया; जे.जे. छात्रवृत्ति; अकोला का प्रस्ताव नहीं।
  • संघर्ष ⭐⭐⭐: एक्सप्रेस ब्लॉक स्टूडियो; जेकब सर्कल; जलील साहब।
  • पहचान ⭐⭐⭐: 1943 के दो चित्र; 1948 का स्वर्ण पदक; 1950 में फ़्रांस।

३. चरित्र

  • निश्चय ⭐⭐⭐: बंबई में ही अध्ययन।
  • मदद ⭐⭐: जलील साहब, लैंगहैमर, लेडेन, श्लैसिंगर।
  • सीख ⭐⭐: कार्तिए-ब्रेसाँ की टिप्पणी से रचना की समझ।

४. संदेश

  • पाठ ⭐⭐⭐: चित्रकला अंतरात्मा की पुकार है।
  • सहायक पाठ ⭐⭐: लगन और सहायता दोनों।
  • पृष्ठभूमि ⭐: ‘ताप’ और ‘अंतरात्मा की पुकार’ पाठ के अपने शब्द हैं।

1. Writer and Text

  • Raza ⭐⭐: born 1922 Babaria village; died 2016; painter.
  • Form ⭐⭐⭐: a chapter of an autobiographical book; translated by Madhu B. Joshi; NCERT 2021-22 edition (archived).
  • Theme ⭐⭐⭐: early struggles and successes; creative restlessness.

2. Summary

  • Nagpur to Bombay ⭐⭐⭐: first in class; Gondiya; J.J. scholarship; declined the Akola offer.
  • Struggle ⭐⭐⭐: Express Block Studio; Jacob Circle; Jaleel sahab.
  • Recognition ⭐⭐⭐: two paintings in 1943; gold medal in 1948; France in 1950.

3. Character

  • Resolve ⭐⭐⭐: study in Bombay itself.
  • Help ⭐⭐: Jaleel, Langhammer, Leyden, Schlesinger.
  • Learning ⭐⭐: Cartier-Bresson remark gave an understanding of composition.

4. Message

  • Text ⭐⭐⭐: painting is the call of the inner self.
  • Supported reading ⭐⭐: dedication and help, both.
  • Background ⭐: taap and the call of the inner self are the text own words.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Atma ka Taap ke lekhak kaun hain?
Answer dekho

Syed Haider Raza (1922-2016), chitrakar. NCERT parichay: janm Babaria gaon (M.P.); 1950 mein France gaye aur Paris mein bas gaye.

Q2 Yeh path kis vidha ka hai aur anuvaad kisne kiya?
Answer dekho

Atmakathatmak pustak Aatma ka Taap ka ek adhyay; angrezi se Hindi anuvad Madhu B. Joshi ne kiya (NCERT parichay).

Q3 Raza ne Akola mein drawing adhyapak ki naukri ki peshkash kyun nahi li?
Answer dekho

Raza ne tay kiya ki lautenge nahi, Bombay mein hi adhyayan karenge; text mein isi faisle ka zikr hai (book Paath ke saath Q1 ismein karan poochta hai).

Q4 Raza ne Gondiya mein kya kaam kiya?
Answer dekho

Pita retire ho chuke the, isliye Raza ne Gondiya mein drawing adhyapak ki naukri ki; mahine-bhar mein J.J. School ke liye Madhya Prant ki chhatravritti mili (September 1943).

Q5 Bombay mein rehkar kala ke adhyayan ke liye Raza ne kya-kya sangharsh kiye?
Answer dekho

J.J. School mein dakhila band aur chhatravritti wapas; Express Block Studio mein naukri aur subah 10 se shaam 6 office; phir Mohan Art Club; rehne ke liye Jacob Circle ka chhota kamra (taxi driver ke yahan); ek raat hatya ki vardat ke karan darwaze par police; aath-das ghanta kaam ke baad bhi kam rupaye.

Q6 Jalil sahab ne Raza ki kaise madad ki?
Answer dekho

Raza ne agle din Jaleel sahab ko haal bataya; unhone art department mein jagah di (Raza farsh par soye). Text mein months baad 6th floor ke flat mein do bistar wale kamra ka offer aur art department ka head banane ki yojana ka bhi zikr hai.

Q7 Raza ko 1948 mein kya puraskar mila?
Answer dekho

Bombay Art Society ka swarn padak; Raza ke anusar is samman ko paane wale ve sabse kam aayu ke kalakar the.

Q8 Shirshak Atma ka Taap ka kya arth hai?
Answer dekho

Explicit: Raza likhte hain ki Bombay mein ek urja aur kaam karne ki ichchha thi; aatma ko chadha yeh taap logon ko dikhai deta tha; ve apni aatma, apne chitt ko santapt rakhte hain. Supported reading: kaam karne ki bechaini hi shirshak ka arth hai.

Q9 1947 aur 1948 Raza ke liye kathin baras kyon the?
Answer dekho

Text: mata ka dehant, May 1948 mein pita ka, vibhajan ki traasdi aur Mahatma Gandhi ki hatya; text mein zimmedariyan, kiraya, fees, padhai, kaam ka jatil jaal bhi hai (book Paath ke saath Q3).

Q10 Path ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Explicit (text): chitrakala vyavsay nahi, antaratma ki pukar hai; hath par hath dhare na baithe raho - khud kuch karo. Supported reading: lagan aur madad dono ne Raza ka raasta banaya. Teaching background: exam mein apne shabdon ka tark dein, text ke shabdon ka hawala ke saath.
