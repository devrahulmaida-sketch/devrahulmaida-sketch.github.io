Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Lekhak aur Path Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Balmukund Gupt ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: janm 1865, gram Gudiyani, zila Rohtak (Haryana); mrityu 1907. Sampadan: Akhbar-e-Chunar, Hindustan, Hindi Bangvasi, Bharatmitra aadi. Rachnayen: Shivshambhu ke Chitthe, Chitthe aur Khat, Khel Tamasha. Pehle Urdu mein shiksha, baad mein Hindi; Bharatendu-yug aur Dwivedi-yug ke beech ki kadi; khadi boli ke sthapak lekhakon mein se ek.
  • Path kahan se ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT: Vidai-Sambhashan unki sarvadhik charchit vyangya kriti 'Shivshambhu ke Chitthe' ka ek ansh hai. (Yeh text nahi batata ki ansh kis patrika mein chhapa — teaching background.)
  • Swaroop ⭐⭐: vyangyatmak gadya jo Viceroy Lord Curzon ko seedha sambodhit karta hai (“Mai Lord!”). Text mein Shivshambhu naam aata hai (“Shivshambhu ke do gayen”); Gupt ne yeh naam kyon liya — NCERT iska uttar nahi deta, book ka sawaal hai. (Shivshambhu Sharma naam ka pratik — teaching background.)
  • Lord Curzon ⭐⭐: NCERT: 1899-1904 aur 1904-1905 tak do baar Bharat ke Viceroy. Text ke anusaar unhone bang-vichched ki zid ki aur press ki swatantrata par pratibandh lagaya. (Bengal vibhajan ka saal 1905 text mein nahi — teaching background.)

💡 Balmukund Gupt (1865-1907) + Shivshambhu ke Chitthe + Curzon (viceroy 1899-1904, 1904-1905) + vyangya.

2️⃣ Path ka Saransh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Vidai par dukh ka dikhawa ⭐⭐⭐: text kehta hai “bichhad-samay bada karunotpadak hota hai”; par saath hi Curzon ke doosri baar aane se Bharatvasi prasann nahi the aur din-raat manate the ki jaldi yahan se padharen. Phir bhi “jaane par harsh ki jagah vishad” — yeh vyangya hai (NCERT: vyangyatmak dhang se bebasi ko Curzon ki lachari se jodna).
  • Bharatiyon ki bebasi ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT: press ki swatantrata par pratibandh; aath karod praja ke gidgidakar bang-vichched na karne ki prarthna par Curzon ne zara bhi dhyan nahi diya. Tulna: Nadirshah ne Delhi mein katleaam prarthna par usi dam rok diya.
  • Curzon ki apni lachari ⭐⭐⭐: council mein manpasand (faujee) adhikari niyukt karwane ke mudde par Curzon ko desh-videsh dono jagah neecha dekhna pada; kshubdh hokar istifa diya, jo manzoor ho gaya, aur ve England wapas chale gaye (NCERT parichay + text).
  • Teesri shakti ⭐⭐: text: “aapke aur yahan ke niwasiyon ke beech mein koi teesri shakti aur bhi hai, jis par yahan walon ka to kya, aapka bhi kaabu nahi.” Text ise naam nahi deta; aage “parde ke peeche ek aur hi leelamay ki leela” aata hai (supported reading: koi sarvopari/daivi shakti).
  • Do gayen ka drishtant ⭐⭐: Shivshambhu ki do gayen: ek adhik balwali, jo seengon ki takkar se kamzor gaay ko gira deti thi. Balwali gaay purohit ko de di gayi; par kamzor gaay us din bhookhi khadi rahi, chara tak nahi chhua. Text ka nishkarsh: jis desh mein pashuon ki bichhadte samay yeh dasha, wahan manushyon ki dasha ka andaza lagana kathin nahi.

🎯 Sequence: vidai ka vishad (vyangya) → do gayen → Bombay mein kiye iraade ulte → Delhi-darbar ki shaan se jangi lat ke aage patakhni → kaunsil mein faujee afsar ka mudda → istifa → Nadirshah se bhi badi zid (aath karod ki prarthna) → Nar Sultan ka vidai-geet.

3️⃣ Vyangya aur Shaili ⭐⭐⭐

  • Vyangya ki takat ⭐⭐⭐: text mein seedhi aalochna ki jagah vyangya: “jaane par harsh ki jagah vishad”, “kitne uncha hokar aap kitne neeche gire!”, “mai lord ka yeh darja hua ki ... naukar na rakha”. NCERT: yeh us samay ka gadya hai jab press par pabandi ka daur chal raha tha — isliye saahsik gadya.
  • Bhasha ke prayog ⭐⭐: NCERT: vinodpriyata, chulbulapan, sanjeedgi, naveen bhasha-prayog aur ravangi. Bhasha ki baat: kuch vaakya Bharatendu-yugeen Hindi ke hain (jaise “padharen”, “tilanjali”, “aarah”, “patakhni”). Muhavre jaise “diwala nikaal diya”, “neend aur bhookh haraam” text mein aate hain (supported reading: bolchal ka lehja).
  • Sambodhan shaili ⭐⭐: pure path mein “Mai Lord!” baar-baar aata hai; Curzon se sawaal: “kya aankh band karke manmaane hukm chalana ... hi shasan hai?”; “ek kaam to aisa batlaiye...” (text se).
  • Naye prayog aur shabd ⭐: NCERT: naveen bhasha-prayog. Shabd-chhavi ke shabd: chirasthayi, karunotpadak, dukhit, vishad, aavirbhav, dukhant, sutradhar, sukhant, leelamay, saransh, patakhni, tilanjali, paymaal, aarah, adna, vichched, taab. Dukhant = jiska ant dukhad ho; sukhant = jiska ant sukhad ho.

💡 Shaili = vyangya + sambodhan ("Mai Lord!") + ravangi/vinod + naye prayog. Exam: vyangya kahan hai? — "jaane par harsh ki jagah vishad" ya "Aapka rakha ek aadmi naukar na rakha".

4️⃣ Sandesh aur Mahatva ⭐⭐

  • Nirankush shasan ki aalochna ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT: Curzon sarkari nirankushta ke pakshdhar the; shasan ke aatataayi roop se har kisi ko kasht hota hai — sadharan janta ho ya Lord Curzon jaisa viceroy. Text ka sawaal: “kya aankh band karke manmaane hukm chalana aur kisi ki kuchh na sunna hi shasan hai?”
  • Patrakarita ka saahas ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: patrakarita unke liye swadhinta-sangram ka hathiyar thi; unke lekhan mein nirbhikta poori tarah maujood; path press par pabandi ke daur ka saahsik gadya hai. Gupt rashtriya navjagran ke sakriya patrakar the.
  • Praja ki prarthna ka mahatva ⭐⭐: NCERT: Curzon ne aath karod praja ki prarthna par dhyan nahi diya, jabki Kaisar aur Zaar bhi praja ki baat sun lete hain. (Swadeshi aandolan ka sambandh text mein nahi — teaching background.)
  • Zid ka phal ⭐⭐: text (gaur karne ki baat): “yahan ki praja ne aapki zid ka phal yahin dekh liya ... aap swayam uska shikar hue.” Yeh shasak ki zid ke apne hi ulte asar ka sabak hai (supported reading).

🎯 Message (supported reading, saboot upar ke quotes): nirankush shasan ki zid shasak ko bhi neecha dikhati hai + praja ki baat sunna kartavya + press-pabandi mein bhi nirbhik vyangya.

१. लेखक और पाठ

  • बालमुकुंद गुप्त ⭐⭐: जन्म 1865, गुड़ियानी (रोहतक, हरियाणा); मृत्यु 1907; संपादन: अखबार-ए-चुनार, हिंदुस्तान, हिंदी बंगवासी, भारतमित्र आदि।
  • स्रोत ⭐⭐⭐: ‘शिवशंभु के चिट्ठे’ — उनकी सर्वाधिक चर्चित व्यंग्य कृति का अंश।
  • कर्ज़न ⭐⭐: भारत के वायसराय 1899-1904 तथा 1904-1905 तक (दो बार)।

२. सारांश

  • व्यंग्य ⭐⭐⭐: “जाने पर हर्ष की जगह विषाद” — विदाई पर दुख का व्यंग्यात्मक दिखावा।
  • कर्ज़न की लाचारी ⭐⭐⭐: कौंसिल में मनपसंद सदस्य के मुद्दे पर देश-विदेश में नीचा देखना पड़ा; क्षुब्ध होकर इस्तीफ़ा, जो मंज़ूर हुआ।
  • प्रजा की बेबसी ⭐⭐: आठ करोड़ की प्रार्थना अनसुनी; प्रेस पर प्रतिबंध; शासन के आततायी रूप से सब पीड़ित।

३. शैली

  • व्यंग्य ⭐⭐⭐: सीधी आलोचना की जगह चुभता हुआ विनोद।
  • भाषा ⭐⭐: विनोदप्रियता, चुलबुलापन, संजीदगी, नवीन भाषा-प्रयोग, रवानगी (NCERT)।
  • संबोधन ⭐⭐: बार-बार “माइ लॉर्ड!” — सीधे कर्ज़न से।

४. संदेश

  • आलोचना ⭐⭐⭐: सरकारी निरंकुशता और ज़िद की।
  • साहस ⭐⭐: प्रेस पर पाबंदी के दौर का साहसिक गद्य (NCERT)।
  • प्रजा की प्रार्थना ⭐⭐: आठ करोड़ प्रजा की बात न सुनना; स्वदेशी आंदोलन का संदर्भ पाठ में नहीं (शिक्षण-पृष्ठभूमि)।

1. Writer and Text

  • Balmukund Gupt ⭐⭐: born 1865 (Gudiyani, Rohtak, Haryana); died 1907; edited Akhbar-e-Chunar, Hindustan, Hindi Bangvasi, Bharatmitra and others.
  • Source ⭐⭐⭐: Shivshambhu ke Chitthe - his most discussed satirical work (NCERT intro).
  • Curzon ⭐⭐: Viceroy of India 1899-1904 and 1904-1905 (two terms).

2. Summary

  • Irony ⭐⭐⭐: "joy turns to sorrow at your departure" - mock grief at a farewell.
  • Curzon's helplessness ⭐⭐⭐: lost face at home and abroad over the council appointment; resigned in anger and it was accepted.
  • Subjects' plight ⭐⭐: 8 crore people's plea ignored; press curbed; everyone suffers under tyrannical rule.

3. Satire and Style

  • Satire ⭐⭐⭐: a sting in place of direct criticism.
  • Language ⭐⭐: humour, playfulness, seriousness, new usages and flow (NCERT).
  • Address ⭐⭐: repeated "My Lord!" - speaks directly to Curzon.

4. Message and Importance

  • Critique ⭐⭐⭐: of official autocracy and obstinacy.
  • Courage ⭐⭐: bold prose written while the press was under curbs (NCERT).
  • People's plea ⭐⭐: 8 crore people's request ignored; the Swadeshi link is not in this text (teaching background).

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Vidai-Sambhashan ke lekhak kaun hain?
Answer dekho

Balmukund Gupt (1865-1907, Gudiyani, Rohtak); path unki vyangya kriti 'Shivshambhu ke Chitthe' ka ansh hai.

Q2 Yeh path kis rachna ka ansh hai?
Answer dekho

NCERT: 'Shivshambhu ke Chitthe' — unki sarvadhik charchit vyangya kriti. (Kis patrika mein chhapi — text mein nahi, teaching background.)

Q3 Lord Curzon kaun the?
Answer dekho

Bharat ke Viceroy — NCERT: 1899-1904 aur 1904-1905 (do baar). Text ke anusaar unhone press ki swatantrata par pratibandh lagaya aur bang-vichched ki zid ki.

Q4 Lekhak vidai par dukh kyun jatate hain?
Answer dekho

Yeh vyangya hai: Bharatvasi Curzon ke aane se prasann nahi the aur jaldi jaane ki manaut karte the, phir bhi text kehta hai “jaane par harsh ki jagah vishad”. NCERT: bebasi ko Curzon ki lachari se jodna.

Q5 Curzon ne istifa kyun diya?
Answer dekho

Council mein manpasand faujee adhikari niyukt karwane ke mudde par unhein desh-videsh dono jagah neecha dekhna pada; kshubdh hokar istifa diya, jo manzoor ho gaya; ve England chale gaye.

Q6 Path mein Bharatiyon ki bebasi kaise dikhayi gayi hai?
Answer dekho

Aath karod praja ki bang-vichched na karne ki prarthna Curzon ne nahi suni, jabki Nadirshah ne Delhi mein katleaam prarthna par usi dam rok diya; press par pratibandh. Tulna se: Nadir se bhi badhkar zid.

Q7 Path ki bhasha-shaili ki visheshta batayiye.
Answer dekho

NCERT: vinodpriyata, chulbulapan, sanjeedgi, naveen bhasha-prayog, ravangi; “Mai Lord!” se seedha sambodhan; Bharatendu-yugeen prayog (padharen, tilanjali aadi).

Q8 Bengal vibhajan kab hua?
Answer dekho

Text saal nahi deta; sirf kehta hai ki Curzon bang-vichched kiye bina ghar jaana pasand nahi karte aur bang desh ke sir par aarah rakha gaya. (1905 aur Swadeshi aandolan — teaching background.)

Q9 Yeh lekh us samay ke liye saahsik kyun maana jaata hai?
Answer dekho

NCERT: yeh us samay likha gaya gadya hai jab press par pabandi ka daur chal raha tha; ek viceroy ko seedha sambodhit karke vyangya kiya gaya.

Q10 Path ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Text koi seedha moral nahi likhta. Supported reading: shasan ke aatataayi roop se har kisi ko kasht hota hai (NCERT), aur zid ka phal shasak ko bhi bhugatna padta hai — saboot: “aap swayam uska shikar hue”, “praja ki baat sunna kartavya”.
