Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Kavi aur Pad Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Kabir ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: janm san 1398, Varanasi (prachin naam Kashi) ke paas Lahartara (U.P.); mrityu san 1518, Basti ke nikat Maghar; mukhya rachna Bijak (sakhi, sabad, ramaini). Bhaktikaal ki nirgun dhara (gyanashrayi shakha) ke pratinidhi kavi.
  • Pad 1 ⭐⭐⭐: Hum tau ek ek kari jaanan - Ishwar ki ekta aur vyapti (NCERT pad-parichay: kan-kan mein, jyoti roop, advait satta).
  • Pad 2 ⭐⭐⭐: Santo dekhat jag baurana - NCERT parichay: bahyadambaron par prahar; log apne bheetar ki taakat na pehchankar avastavik sansar se rishta bana baithte hain. Source note: pad 2 aur uske sawaal NCERT Aroh 2021-22 edition (archived PDF) se hain; current PDF (Reprint 2026-27) mein sirf pad 1 mila. Syllabus status ka koi claim nahi.
  • Shaili ⭐⭐: NCERT: Kabir saaf aur do took shabdon mein prabhavi dhang se baat kehne ke himayati the; kitabi gyan ki jagah aankhon dekhe satya aur anubhav ko pramukhta di; Hazari Prasad Dwivedi ne unhein vani ka dictator kaha.

💡 Kabir = nirgun, gyanashrayi shakha | Pad 1 = Ishwar ek | Pad 2 = jag baurana (bahyadambar) | Edition: pad 2 archived 2021-22.

2️⃣ Pad 1 - Hum tau ek ek kari jaanan ⭐⭐⭐

  • Ishwar ek ⭐⭐⭐: Kabir: hum to ek-ek karke jaante hain; jo do kehte hain, unke liye dojag (narak) hai, kyunki unhone pehchana nahin.
  • Ekta ke tark ⭐⭐⭐: Ek hi pavan, ek hi paani, ek hi jyoti sab mein samayi; ek hi khaak se sab bhande gade, ek hi konhara (kumhar) ne mila-mila kar (text).
  • Badhai ka drishtant ⭐⭐⭐: Badhai kaasth (lakdi) kaat sakta hai, agni nahin kaat sakta; text: sab ghati antari tunhi vyapak dharai saroopai soi - sabke bheetar vyapak vahi ek, alag-alag swaroop dharan kiye (book ka Pad ke saath Q3 isi panktiyon par poochta hai). Supported reading: lakdi ke roop badal sakte hain, par usmein vyapt tattva (agni) ko badhai nahin kaat sakta.
  • Maya aur garv ⭐⭐: Maya dekhkar jagat lubhaya hua hai; Kabir poochhte hain kahe re nar garbaanan - he manushya, garv kyon karta hai?
  • Nirbhayata ⭐⭐: Text: nirbhai bhaya kachhu nahin byaapai, kahai Kabir divaanan - Kabir deewana hain aur nirbhay; book Q4 poochhta hai unhone apne ko deewana kyon kaha (apna tark likhein).

🎯 Pad 1: Ishwar ek -> pavan-paani-jyoti-khaak -> badhai aur agni -> maya aur garv -> nirbhayata.

3️⃣ Pad 2 - Santo dekhat jag baurana ⭐⭐⭐ (archived 2021-22)

  • Jag baurana ⭐⭐⭐: Text: saanch kahon to maaran dhaave, jhoothe jag patiyana - sach kehne par maarne daudte hain, jhooth par vishwas karte hain. NCERT shabd-arth: baurana = buddhi bhrasht ho jana, pagla jana.
  • Nemi-dharmi ⭐⭐⭐: Text: nemi dekha, dharmi dekha, praat kare asnana (subah snan karne wale); aatam maari pakhanahi poojai, unmein kachhu nahin gyana - apne aap ko maarkar pattharon ki murtiyon ko poojte hain, unmein gyan nahin. Shabd-arth: nemi = niyamon ka palan karne wala; dharmi = dharm ka pakhand karne wala.
  • Peer-auliya aur murid ⭐⭐⭐: Text: bahutak dekha peer auliya, padhain kiteb Kurana; kai murid tadbeer batavain, unmein uhai jo gyana - Quran padhne wale peer-auliya aur murid ko upay batane wale: unmein bhi wahi (gyan ki kami).
  • Aasan aur dimbh ⭐⭐: Text: aasan maari dimbh dhari baithe, man mein bahut gumana - dhyan mudra mein dambh ke saath baithe, man mein bahut ahankar; peepar paathar poojan laage, teerath garv bhulana (peepal aur patthar poojna, teerth ka garv).
  • Bahyadambar ⭐⭐⭐: Text: topi pahire, mala pahire, chhap tilak anumana; saakhi sabdahi gaavat bhoole, aatam khabari na jaana - sakhi aur sabad gaate hue bhi aatam khabari (aatmagyan) nahin jaani.
  • Ram-Rahim ⭐⭐: Text: Hindu kahai mohi Ram piyara, turk kahai Rahimana; aapas mein dou lari lari mooe, marm na kahu jaana - dono lad-lad kar mar gaye, marm kisi ne nahin jaana.

🎯 Pad 2: jag baurana -> nemi-dharmi -> peer-murid -> aasan-dimbh -> topi-mala-tilak -> Ram-Rahim ka jhagda -> guru aur shishya ka pachhtava.

4️⃣ Kavya-Saundarya aur Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Tark aur drishtant ⭐⭐⭐: Explicit: pavan, paani, khaak, konhara, badhai-agni jaise rozmarra ke drishtant se Ishwar ki ekta (pad 1).
  • Guru aur shishya ⭐⭐: Text: ghar ghar mantra det phirat hain, mahima ke abhimana; guru ke sahit sikhya sab boode, ant kaal pachhitana; antim: ee sab bharm bhulana (sab bharm mein bhule hain).
  • Bhasha-shaili ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: saaf, do took baat; sakhi, sabad, ramaini Bijak ke roop. Teaching background: bhasha ko exam mein sadhukkadi kehte hain - yeh NCERT ke is paath mein nahin hai.
  • Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐: Explicit (text): Ishwar ek hai (hum tau ek ek kari jaanan); bahyadambaron ki jagah aatam khabari (pad 2). Supported reading: Hindu-Turk ke jhagde par Kabir ka marm na kahu jaana kehna dikhata hai ki dharm ke naam par ladna dikhave mein atka hona hai. Teaching background: NCERT parichay ke anusar Kabir jati-bhed, varn-bhed, sampraday-bhed ke sthan par prem, sadbhav, samanta ka samarthan karte the.

🎯 Tiers: explicit (text) / supported reading / teaching background; pad 2 ka source = archived 2021-22.

१. कवि और पद

  • कबीर ⭐⭐: जन्म 1398, लहरतारा (वाराणसी के पास); मृत्यु 1518, मगहर; निर्गुण धारा (ज्ञानाश्रयी शाखा)।
  • पद १ ⭐⭐⭐: ‘हम तौ एक एक करि जांनां’ - ईश्वर एक है।
  • पद २ ⭐⭐⭐: ‘संतो देखत जग बौराना’ - बाह्याडंबर पर प्रहार (संग्रहित 2021-22 संस्करण)।

२. पद १

  • ईश्वर ⭐⭐⭐: जो ‘दोइ’ कहें, उनके लिए दोजग; एक पवन, एक पानी, एक ज्योति।
  • तर्क ⭐⭐⭐: एक ही खाक से सब भांडे; कोंहरा; बाढ़ी काष्ठ को काटे, अगिनि को नहीं।
  • निर्भयता ⭐⭐: माया देखि जगत लुभांनां; ‘निरभै भया कछू नहिं ब्यापै कहै कबीर दिवांनां’।

३. पद २ (2021-22 संस्करण)

  • संसार ⭐⭐⭐: साँच कहौं तो मारन धावै, झूठे जग पतियाना।
  • आडंबर ⭐⭐⭐: टोपी, माला, छाप-तिलक; आसन मारि डिंभ धरि बैठे।
  • दोनों ⭐⭐: हिंदू ‘राम’, तुर्क ‘रहिमाना’ - आपस में लरि-लरि मूए, मर्म न काहू जाना।

४. काव्य-सौंदर्य और संदेश

  • शैली ⭐⭐⭐: दृष्टांत - पवन, पानी, खाक, कोंहरा, बाढ़ी-अगिनि।
  • पाठ से बाहर (शिक्षण-पृष्ठभूमि) ⭐: ‘सधुक्कड़ी’ शब्द इस पाठ में नहीं है।
  • संदेश ⭐⭐⭐: ईश्वर एक; बाह्याडंबर से आत्म-ख़बरि की ओर (पाठ-आधारित पठन)।

1. Poet and Padas

  • Kabir ⭐⭐: born 1398 near Varanasi (Lahartara); died 1518 at Maghar; nirgun, gyanashrayi stream.
  • Pad 1 ⭐⭐⭐: Hum tau ek ek kari jaanan - God is one.
  • Pad 2 ⭐⭐⭐: Santo dekhat jag baurana - attack on outward show (archived 2021-22 edition).

2. Pad 1

  • God ⭐⭐⭐: those who say two have dojag (hell); one air, one water, one light.
  • Argument ⭐⭐⭐: one clay for all the pots; the potter; carpenter cuts wood, not fire.
  • Fearless ⭐⭐: the world is lured by maya; Kabir the divana is fearless.

3. Pad 2 (2021-22 edition)

  • World ⭐⭐⭐: says the truth and they rush to kill; they trust the false.
  • Show ⭐⭐⭐: cap, rosary, tilak marks; sitting in a posture with pretence.
  • Both ⭐⭐: Hindu says Ram, Turk says Rahim - they fought and died; neither knew the secret.

4. Poetic Beauty and Message

  • Style ⭐⭐⭐: everyday examples - air, water, clay, potter, carpenter and fire.
  • Outside the text (background) ⭐: the label sadhukkadi is not in this lesson.
  • Message ⭐⭐⭐: God is one; from outward show to self-knowledge (reading from the text).

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Kabir kis shakha ke kavi hain?
Answer dekho

NCERT parichay: Bhaktikaal ki nirgun dhara (gyanashrayi shakha) ke pratinidhi kavi; janm san 1398, Lahartara (Varanasi ke paas), mrityu san 1518, Maghar.

Q2 Pad 1 mein Kabir ne Ishwar ke baare mein kya kaha?
Answer dekho

Hum tau ek ek kari jaanan - Ishwar ek hai; jo do kehte hain unke liye dojag (narak) hai kyunki unhone pehchana nahin; sab ghati antari tunhi vyapak - sabke bheetar vyapak.

Q3 Ishwar ki ekta ke liye Kabir ne kya tark diye?
Answer dekho

Ek hi pavan, ek hi paani, ek hi jyoti sab mein; ek hi khaak se sab bhande gade, ek hi konhara (kumhar) ne mila-mila kar (book Pad ke saath Q1; manav sharir ke panch tatva Q2 - pavan, paani, jyoti, khaak ke saath apna tark).

Q4 Badhai aur aag ke drishtant ka kya arth hai?
Answer dekho

Explicit: badhai kaasth (lakdi) kaat sakta hai, agni nahin; sab ghati antari tunhi vyapak dharai saroopai soi. Supported reading: roop badalne wali cheezon mein vyapt tattva ko kaata nahin ja sakta; Ishwar sabke bheetar vyapak hai (book Pad ke saath Q3).

Q5 Kabir ne apne ko deewana kyon kaha hai?
Answer dekho

Text: nirbhai bhaya kachhu nahin byaapai, kahai Kabir divaanan - Kabir deewana hain aur nirbhay; jagat maya dekhkar lubhaya hai aur nar garv karta hai, par ve nahin. (Book Q4: apne shabdon mein tark dein.)

Q6 Kabir ne sansar ko baurana kyon kaha?
Answer dekho

Pad 2: saanch kahon to maaran dhaave, jhoothe jag patiyana - sach kehne par maarne daudte aur jhooth par vishwas karte hain (baurana = buddhi bhrasht ho jana, pagla jana).

Q7 Niyam aur dharm ka palan karne walon ki kin kamiyon ki or Kabir ne sanket kiya hai?
Answer dekho

Text: nemi dekha, dharmi dekha, praat kare asnana; aatam maari pakhanahi poojai, unmein kachhu nahin gyana (patthar ki murtiyon ko poojte hain, unmein gyan nahin); peepar paathar poojan laage, teerath garv bhulana; aasan maari dimbh dhari baithe, man mein bahut gumana (book Q6).

Q8 Agyani guruon ki sharan mein jaane par shishyon ki kya gati hoti hai?
Answer dekho

Text: guru ke sahit sikhya sab boode, ant kaal pachhitana; ghar ghar mantra det phirat hain, mahima ke abhimana. Peer-auliya ke baare mein: padhain kiteb Kurana; kai murid tadbeer batavain, unmein uhai jo gyana (book Q7).

Q9 Kabir ne kin bahyadambaron ki ninda ki?
Answer dekho

Text: topi pahire, mala pahire, chhap tilak anumana; aasan maari dimbh dhari baithe; peepar paathar poojan; saakhi sabdahi gaavat bhoole, aatam khabari na jaana.

Q10 Kabir ke padon ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Explicit (text): hum tau ek ek kari jaanan; Hindu-Turk ke jhagde mein marm na kahu jaana; aatam khabari ki baat. Supported reading: dikhave ki jagah apne bheetar ko jaano (book Q8 ke saath). Teaching background: exam mein text ki panktiyon ka hawala dein.
