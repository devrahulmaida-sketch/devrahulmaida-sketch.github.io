कबीर के पद

Kabir ke Pad — Sant Kabir

Ek hi Ishwar, ek hi maati — aur duniya ka pagalpan

Board Weightage: HIGH Video available Long + Short notes Hindi • English • Hinglish
Watch on YouTube Notes

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Kavi aur Pad Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Kabir ⭐⭐: Bhaktikaal ki nirguna shakha ke gyanmargi sant kavi; Ishwar ko ek aur sabmein vyapt maante the.
  • Pad 1 ⭐⭐⭐: 'Hum to ek kari janna' — Ishwar ki ekta aur sarvavyapakta.
  • Pad 2 ⭐⭐⭐: 'Santo dekhat jag baurana' — bahyadambar aur pakhand par chot.
  • Shaili ⭐⭐: seedhi, teekhi, lok-bhasha mein tark aur upma ke saath baat.

💡 Kabir = nirguna, gyanmargi | Pad 1 = Ishwar ek | Pad 2 = jag baurana (pakhand).

2️⃣ Pad 1 — Hum to ek kari janna ⭐⭐⭐

  • Ishwar ek ⭐⭐⭐: Kabir kehte hain — maine ek hi Ishwar ko jaana; jo do kehte hain, unke liye dozakh hai, kyunki unhone use pehchana nahi.
  • Ekta ke tark ⭐⭐⭐: ek hi pawan, ek hi paani, ek hi jyoti sab mein; ek hi khaak se sab bartan bane, ek hi kumhaar ne gadhe.
  • Badhai ka drishtant ⭐⭐⭐: badhai lakdi ko kaat sakta hai, aag ko nahi — isi tarah Ishwar ghat-ghat mein vyapt hai, sharir nasht hota hai par aatma amar hai.
  • Maya aur garv ⭐⭐: maya dekhkar jagat lubh gaya; manushya garv karta hai.
  • Nirbhayata ⭐⭐: jo maya se mukt, Ishwar-prem mein diwana ho jaata hai, use koi bhay nahi vyapta.

🎯 Pad 1: Ishwar ek → pawan-paani-jyoti-khaak ek → badhai aur aag → maya aur garv → nirbhayata.

3️⃣ Pad 2 — Santo dekhat jag baurana ⭐⭐⭐

  • Jag baurana ⭐⭐⭐: Kabir kehte hain sansar paagal ho gaya hai — sach bolne wale ko maarne daudta hai, jhooth par vishwas karta hai.
  • Hindu ⭐⭐⭐: niyam-dharm wale, subah snaan karne wale, patthar poojne wale — par aatmchintan nahi, gyaan ka ghamand.
  • Musalman ⭐⭐⭐: peer-auliya jo Quran padhte aur shishyon ko upay batate hain, par khuda ko khud nahi jaante.
  • Dhongi yogi ⭐⭐: aasan lagakar baithe, ahankar se bhare hue.
  • Bahyadambar ⭐⭐⭐: topi, maala, chhaap-tilak — sakhi, shabd gaate huye bhi aatma ki khabar nahi.
  • Ram-Rahim ⭐⭐: Hindu aur Musalman Ram aur Rahim ke naam par ladte hain, jabki dono Ishwar ka marm nahi jaante.

🎯 Pad 2: jag baurana → Hindu ke dambh → Musalman ke dambh → dhongi yogi → topi-maala-tilak → Ram-Rahim ka jhagda.

4️⃣ Kavya-Saundarya aur Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Tark aur upma ⭐⭐⭐: pawan, paani, maati, badhai-aag jaise rozmarra ke drishtant se gehri baat.
  • Bhasha ⭐⭐: sadhukkadi — alag-alag bolion ka mel; seedhi aur teekhi.
  • Vyang ⭐⭐: bahyadambar par seedha prahar.
  • Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐: Ishwar ek hai, use bahar ke dikhave nahi, aatmchintan aur sachche bhaav se pao; dharm ke naam par ladna vyarth hai.

🎯 Shaili = tark + upma + sadhukkadi + vyang.

१. कवि और पद

  • कबीर ⭐⭐: निर्गुण ज्ञानमार्गी संत।
  • पद 1 ⭐⭐⭐: ईश्वर एक है।
  • पद 2 ⭐⭐⭐: पाखंड पर चोट।

२. पद १ — हम तौ एक करि जांनां

  • ईश्वर ⭐⭐⭐: एक और सर्वव्यापी।
  • तर्क ⭐⭐⭐: एक पवन, पानी, ज्योति, खाक।
  • निर्भयता ⭐⭐: माया से मुक्ति।

३. पद २ — संतो देखत जग बौराना

  • संसार ⭐⭐⭐: सच का विरोधी, झूठ का विश्वासी।
  • आडंबर ⭐⭐⭐: टोपी, माला, तिलक।
  • दोनों ⭐⭐: हिंदू-मुसलमान दोनों भटके।

४. काव्य-सौंदर्य और संदेश

  • शैली ⭐⭐⭐: तर्क और उपमा।
  • भाषा ⭐⭐: सधुक्कड़ी।
  • संदेश ⭐⭐⭐: आडंबर छोड़ो, एकता अपनाओ।

1. Poet and Padas

  • Kabir ⭐⭐: a nirguna, knowledge-path saint.
  • Pad 1 ⭐⭐⭐: God is one.
  • Pad 2 ⭐⭐⭐: attack on hypocrisy.

2. Pad 1 — Hum to ek kari janna

  • God ⭐⭐⭐: one and everywhere.
  • Argument ⭐⭐⭐: one air, water, light, clay.
  • Fearless ⭐⭐: freedom from maya.

3. Pad 2 — Santo dekhat jag baurana

  • World ⭐⭐⭐: hates truth, trusts lies.
  • Show ⭐⭐⭐: cap, rosary, tilak.
  • Both ⭐⭐: Hindus and Muslims both astray.

4. Poetic Beauty and Message

  • Style ⭐⭐⭐: logic and similes.
  • Language ⭐⭐: sadhukkadi.
  • Message ⭐⭐⭐: drop the show, embrace unity.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Kabir kis shakha ke kavi hain?
Answer dekho

Bhaktikaal ki nirguna shakha ke gyanmargi sant kavi.

Q2 Pad 1 mein Kabir ne Ishwar ke baare mein kya kaha?
Answer dekho

Ki Ishwar ek hai aur ghat-ghat mein vyapt; do batane wale dozakh ke bhagi.

Q3 Ishwar ki ekta ke liye Kabir ne kya tark diye?
Answer dekho

Ek hi pawan, paani, jyoti aur ek hi maati se bane bartan (ek kumhaar).

Q4 Badhai aur aag ke drishtant ka kya arth hai?
Answer dekho

Badhai lakdi kaat sakta hai, aag nahi — sharir nasht hota hai, aatma/Ishwar amar aur sarvavyapak.

Q5 Pad 1 ke ant mein nirbhay kaun hota hai?
Answer dekho

Jo maya se mukt hokar Ishwar-prem mein diwana ho jaata hai.

Q6 Kabir ne sansar ko baurana kyon kaha?
Answer dekho

Kyunki wo sach kehne wale ko maarne daudta aur jhooth par vishwas karta hai.

Q7 Hinduon ke baare mein Kabir ne kya kaha?
Answer dekho

Wo niyam-dharm, snaan, patthar-pooja karte hain par aatmchintan nahi; gyaan ka ghamand hai.

Q8 Musalmanon ke baare mein Kabir ne kya kaha?
Answer dekho

Peer-auliya Quran padhte aur shishyon ko upay batate hain par khuda ko khud nahi jaante.

Q9 Kabir ne kin bahyadambaron ki ninda ki?
Answer dekho

Topi, maala, chhaap-tilak aur sakhi-shabd gaane ke dikhave ki.

Q10 Kabir ke padon ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Ishwar ek hai; dharm ke naam par ladna aur aadambar vyarth hain.
