1️⃣ Kavayitri aur Kavita Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Nirmala Putul ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: janm 1972, Dumka (Jharkhand); aadivasi parivar mein; pita aur chacha shikshak the; nursing mein diploma aur kaafi baad IGNOU se snatak.
  • Pramukh rachnayein ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: Nagade ki tarah bajte shabd, Apne ghar ki talash mein. Santhali bhasha se Hindi roopantar Ashok Singh ne kiya hai.
  • Kavita ka sthaan ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: kavita mein aadivasi samaj ke dono pakshon ka yatharth chitran hai - sakaratmak (sadgi, bholapan, prakriti se judav, kathor parishram ki kshamta) aur nakaratmak (ashiksha, kuritiyan, sharab ki or badhta jhukav).
  • Kendriya vichar ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: prakriti ke vinash aur visthapan ke karan aaj aadivasi samaj sankat mein hai - yehi kavita ka mool swar hai; kavita swasth samajik-prakritik paryavaran ke liye zaroori cheezon ko bachane ki baat karti hai.

💡 Nirmala Putul (Dumka, 1972) + Nagade ki tarah bajte shabd + Ashok Singh ka roopantar + mool swar = sankat aur bachane ki baat.

2️⃣ Kavita-Saransh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Basti ⭐⭐⭐: Text: apni bastiyon ko nangi hone se; shahar ki aabo-hawa se bachayen use; bachayen doobne se puri ki puri basti ko Hadiya mein. Shabd-chhavi: aabo-hawa = jalvayu. (Hadiya ka arth is NCERT text mein nahin diya gaya.)
  • Pehchaan ⭐⭐⭐: Text: apne chehre par Santhal pargana ki mati ka rang; bhasha mein Jharkhandipan. Shabd-chhavi: mati = mitti. Supported reading: ise pehchan ki or ishara maana ja sakta hai (book Q1-Q2).
  • Bholapan aur jujharupan ⭐⭐⭐: Text: man ka harapan; bholapan dil ka; akkhadpan, jujharupan bhi. Shabd-chhavi: akkhadpan = kisi baat ko lekar rukhai se tan jane ka bhav; jujharupan = joojhne ya sangharsh karne ki pravritti.
  • Jeevan ki garmahat ⭐⭐: Text: thandi hoti dincharya mein jeevan ki garmahat (book Q6 ka); bhitar ki aag.
  • Hathiyar aur prakriti ⭐⭐⭐: Text: dhanush ki dori, teer ka nukilapan, kulhadi ki dhaar; jungle ki taza hawa, nadiyon ki nirmalta, pahadon ka maun.
  • Geet, mitti, jagah ⭐⭐⭐: Text: geeton ki dhun, mitti ka sondhapan, faslon ki lahlahahat; nachne ke liye khula aangan, gaane ke liye geet, hansne ke liye thodi-si khilkhilahat, rone ke liye mutthi bhar ekant; bachchon ke liye maidan, pashuon ke liye hari-hari ghaas, boodhon ke liye pahadon ki shanti. Shabd-chhavi: sondhapan = sugandh.
  • Ant ⭐⭐⭐: Text: is avishwas-bhare daur mein thoda-sa vishwas, thodi-si ummeed, thode-se sapne; aao, milkar bachayen ki is daur mein bhi bachane ko bahut kuchh bacha hai, ab bhi hamare paas! Shabd-chhavi: daur = samay; ummeed = aasha.

🎯 Sequence: basti -> mati ka rang aur Jharkhandipan -> bholapan, akkhadpan, jujharupan -> jeevan ki garmahat -> hathiyar aur prakriti -> geet, aangan, maidan -> thoda vishwas -> antim pankti.

3️⃣ Kaavya Saundarya ⭐⭐⭐

  • Bhasha ⭐⭐: Text mein seedhe, bolchal ke shabd hain (basti, mati, sondhapan, jujharupan). NCERT parichay bhasha ko deshaj nahin kehta; yeh supported reading hai.
  • Bimb ⭐⭐⭐: Text mein dhanush, teer, kulhadi, jungle, nadi, pahad, geet, mitti, faslen ke chitra hain. Supported reading: yeh bimb ke roop mein padhe ja sakte hain; parichay bimb shabd ka naam nahin deta.
  • Dohrav aur suchi ⭐⭐: Text mein bachayen aur ke liye wali panktiyon ki baar-baar aati hain (nachne ke liye..., gaane ke liye...). Supported reading: isse suchi-jaisa roop banta hai.
  • Bhaav ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: kavita aadivasi samaj ke dono pakshon ko samne rakhti hai. Supported reading: chinta aur ummeed dono padhi ja sakti hain (book Q6 kha mein thoda vishwas, thodi ummeed).

💡 Tiers: explicit (text/parichay) / supported reading / teaching background.

4️⃣ Sandesh ⭐⭐

  • Explicit (NCERT parichay) ⭐⭐⭐: Kavita samaj mein un cheezon ko bachane ki baat karti hai jinka hona swasth samajik-prakritik paryavaran ke liye zaroori hai; prakriti ke vinash aur visthapan se samaj sankat mein hai.
  • Supported reading ⭐⭐: Antim pankti bachane ko bahut kuchh bacha hai, ab bhi hamare paas ko ummeed ke roop mein padha ja sakta hai (book Q5 ka aashay).
  • Teaching background ⭐: Book Aas-paas Q1-Q2: apne shehar ya basti ki bachane layak cheezein aur aadivasi samaj ki vartaman sthiti par apni tippani. Is paath mein koi aankda ya tareekh nahin.

🎯 Message: explicit (parichay) / supported reading / teaching background.

१. कवयित्री और कविता

  • निर्मला पुतुल ⭐⭐: जन्म 1972, दुमका (झारखंड); आदिवासी परिवार (NCERT परिचय)।
  • रचनाएँ ⭐⭐⭐: नगाड़े की तरह बजते शब्द, अपने घर की तलाश में; संथाली से हिंदी रूपांतर अशोक सिंह।
  • मूल स्वर ⭐⭐⭐: प्रकृति के विनाश और विस्थापन के कारण आदिवासी समाज संकट में (परिचय)।

२. सारांश

  • बस्ती ⭐⭐⭐: नंगी होने से, शहर की आबो-हवा से, डूबने से (हड़िया में)।
  • पहचान ⭐⭐⭐: संथाल परगना की माटी का रंग; भाषा में झारखंडीपन (पाठ)।
  • आशा ⭐⭐⭐: बचाने को बहुत कुछ बचा है, अब भी हमारे पास (पाठ)।

३. काव्य-सौंदर्य

  • भाषा ⭐⭐: सीधे, बोलचाल के शब्द (पठन)।
  • बिंब ⭐⭐⭐: धनुष, तीर, कुल्हाड़ी, जंगल, नदी, पहाड़ (पाठ)।
  • भाव ⭐⭐: ‘थोड़ा-सा विश्वास, थोड़ी-सी उम्मीद’ (पाठ)।

४. संदेश

  • स्पष्ट (परिचय) ⭐⭐⭐: स्वस्थ सामाजिक-प्राकृतिक परिवेश के लिए ज़रूरी चीज़ें बचाना।
  • पठन ⭐⭐: अंतिम पंक्ति को उम्मीद के रूप में पढ़ा जा सकता है।
  • पृष्ठभूमि ⭐: अभ्यास: अपनी बस्ती की चीज़ें और आदिवासी समाज की स्थिति पर टिप्पणी।

1. Poet and Poem

  • Nirmala Putul ⭐⭐: born 1972, Dumka (Jharkhand); an adivasi family (NCERT introduction).
  • Works ⭐⭐⭐: Nagade ki tarah bajte shabd, Apne ghar ki talash mein; Hindi version from Santhali by Ashok Singh.
  • Main note ⭐⭐⭐: adivasi society is in crisis from the destruction of nature and displacement (introduction).

2. Summary

  • Settlement ⭐⭐⭐: save it from turning bare, from the climate of the city, from drowning (in Hadiya).
  • Identity ⭐⭐⭐: the colour of Santhal Pargana soil; Jharkhandi speech (text).
  • Hope ⭐⭐⭐: much is still left to save, still with us (text).

3. Poetic Beauty

  • Language ⭐⭐: plain, spoken words (reading).
  • Images ⭐⭐⭐: bow, arrow, axe, forest, river, hills (text).
  • Mood ⭐⭐: a little faith, a little hope (text).

4. Message

  • Explicit (introduction) ⭐⭐⭐: save what a healthy social and natural environment needs.
  • Reading ⭐⭐: the last line may be read as hope.
  • Background ⭐: exercise: your own town's things; remark on adivasi society today.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Aao Milkar Bachayen kis kavayitri ki kavita hai?
Answer dekho

NCERT parichay: Nirmala Putul; janm 1972, Dumka (Jharkhand).

Q2 Nirmala Putul ki do pramukh rachnayein kaun si hain?
Answer dekho

NCERT parichay: Nagade ki tarah bajte shabd aur Apne ghar ki talash mein.

Q3 'Maati ka rang' se kis or sanket hai?
Answer dekho

Text: apne chehre par Santhal pargana ki mati ka rang (book Q1). Supported reading: apni jagah aur pehchan ki or ishara; parichay arth seedhe nahin deta.

Q4 'Bhasha mein Jharkhandipan' ka kya arth hai?
Answer dekho

Text: bhasha mein Jharkhandipan (book Q2). Supported reading: bhasha mein apne kshetra ki chhap bani rehna; NCERT parichay isko alag se paribhashit nahin karta.

Q5 Kavita mein dil ke bholepan ke saath akkhadpan aur jujharupan bachane par bal kyon hai?
Answer dekho

Text: bholapan dil ka; akkhadpan, jujharupan bhi (book Q3 poochta hai ki inhe bachane par bal kyon). Shabd-chhavi: akkhadpan = rukhai se tan jane ka bhav; jujharupan = joojhne ya sangharsh karne ki pravritti. Karan ka uttar supported reading hai: aap tark dijiye.

Q6 Kavita aadivasi samaj ki kin buraiyon ki or sanket karti hai?
Answer dekho

NCERT parichay (adivasi samaj ke nakaratmak pehlu): ashiksha, kuritiyan aur sharab ki or badhta jhukav. Text mein Hadiya aur shahar ki aabo-hawa ka zikr hai (Hadiya ka arth text mein nahin diya gaya).

Q7 'Bachane ko bahut kuch bacha hai' ka kya aashay hai?
Answer dekho

Text: is daur mein bhi bachane ko bahut kuchh bacha hai, ab bhi hamare paas. Book Q5 aashay poochta hai; supported reading: sab kuchh nasht nahin hua, bachane ke liye abhi kuchh hai.

Q8 Kavita mein aadivasi jeevan ke kaun se bimb aaye hain?
Answer dekho

Text: dhanush ki dori, teer ka nukilapan, kulhadi ki dhaar, jungle, nadiyan, pahad ke chitra.

Q9 Kavita ki shaili ki kya visheshta hai?
Answer dekho

Text mein bachayen ka dohrav aur ke liye wali panktiyon ki suchi-jaisi rachna hai. Supported reading: isse kavita pukar jaisi lagti hai; NCERT parichay shaili ka naam nahin deta.

Q10 Kavita ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Explicit (parichay): samaj mein un cheezon ko bachana jinka hona swasth samajik-prakritik paryavaran ke liye zaroori hai. Supported reading: ummeed. Teaching background: Aas-paas Q1-Q2 mein apne vichar likhein.
