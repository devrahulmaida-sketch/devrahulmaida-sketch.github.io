आओ, मिलकर बचाएँ

Aao Milkar Bachayen — Nirmala Putul

Jo bachana hai, use abhi bachao — Santhal samaj ki pukar

Board Weightage: HIGH Video available Long + Short notes Hindi • English • Hinglish
Watch on YouTube Notes

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Kavayitri aur Kavita Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Nirmala Putul ⭐⭐: janm 1972, Dumka (Jharkhand); ek aadivasi (Santhal) parivar mein.
  • Pramukh rachnayein ⭐⭐⭐: Nagade ki tarah bajte shabd aur Apne ghar ki talash mein.
  • Kavita ka sthaan ⭐⭐: kavita mein Santhal samaj ke achhe aur bure, dono pakshon ka yatharth chitran hai.
  • Kendriya vichar ⭐⭐⭐: apni basti, bhasha, parampara aur prakriti ko bigadne se bachane ki pukar.

💡 Nirmala Putul (Dumka, 1972, Santhal) + Nagade ki tarah bajte shabd + bachane ki pukar.

2️⃣ Kavita-Saransh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Kya bachana hai ⭐⭐⭐: kavi kehti hain — bastiyon ko nangi hone se, shahar ki aabo-hawa se aur haadiya (sharab) mein poori basti ke doobne se bachao.
  • Pehchaan ⭐⭐⭐: chehre par Santhal Pargana ki maati ka rang aur bhasha mein Jharkhandipan bachana hai.
  • Dil ka bholapan aur jujharupan ⭐⭐: dil ka bholapan, akkhadpan aur jujharupan — teeno bachane hain.
  • Jeevan ki garmahat ⭐⭐: thandi hoti dincharya mein jeevan ki garmahat aur bheetar ki aag.
  • Hathiyar aur prakriti ⭐⭐: dhanush ki dori, teer ka nukilapan, kulhaadi ki dhaar aur jungle ki taazi hawa, nadiyon ki nirmalta, pahadon ka maun.
  • Sanskriti ⭐⭐: geeton ki dhun, maati ka sondhapan, faslon ki lahlahahat; nachne ko khula aangan, gaane ko geet.
  • Ant ⭐⭐⭐: 'is avishwas-bhare daur mein thoda vishwas, thodi ummeed, thode sapne' — 'bachane ko bahut kuch bacha hai, ab bhi hamare paas!'

🎯 Sequence: basti ko bigadne se bachao → pehchaan (maati, bhasha) → swabhav (bholapan, jujharupan) → hathiyar + prakriti → sanskriti → ant mein ummeed.

3️⃣ Kaavya Saundarya ⭐⭐⭐

  • Bhasha ⭐⭐⭐: saral, deshaj shabd — haadiya, jharkhandipan, sondhapan.
  • Bimb aur pratik ⭐⭐⭐: dhanush, teer, kulhaadi, jungle, nadi, pahad — aadivasi jeevan ke jeevant bimb.
  • Shaili ⭐⭐: 'bachayen' ki punaravritti aur suchi-jaisi rachna kavita ko pukar ka roop deti hai.
  • Bhaav ⭐⭐: chinta aur ummeed ka santulan.

💡 Shaili = saral bhasha + aadivasi bimb + punaravritti + chinta-ummeed.

4️⃣ Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Pehchaan bachao ⭐⭐⭐: bhasha, parampara aur prakriti hamari pehchaan hain.
  • Burai se bacho ⭐⭐⭐: nashe aur dikhave jaisi buraiyon se samaj ko bachana hai.
  • Ummeed rakho ⭐⭐: mil-jul kar prayas karne se ab bhi bahut kuch bachaya ja sakta hai.

🎯 Message: pehchaan bachao + buraiyon se bacho + ummeed rakho.

१. कवयित्री और कविता

  • निर्मला पुतुल ⭐⭐: जन्म 1972, दुमका (झारखंड)।
  • रचनाएँ ⭐⭐⭐: नगाड़े की तरह बजते शब्द।
  • विषय ⭐⭐⭐: अपनी बस्ती और संस्कृति को बचाना।

२. सारांश

  • बुराइयाँ ⭐⭐⭐: हड़िया में डूबना, शहरी हवा।
  • पहचान ⭐⭐⭐: माटी का रंग, झारखंडीपन।
  • आशा ⭐⭐⭐: बचाने को बहुत कुछ बचा है।

३. काव्य-सौंदर्य

  • भाषा ⭐⭐⭐: सरल और देशज शब्द।
  • बिंब ⭐⭐⭐: धनुष, तीर, जंगल, नदी।
  • भाव ⭐⭐: चिंता और उम्मीद।

४. संदेश

  • पहचान ⭐⭐⭐: भाषा, परंपरा, प्रकृति।
  • बुराई ⭐⭐⭐: नशे से बचाव।
  • उम्मीद ⭐⭐: मिलकर प्रयास।

1. Poet and Poem

  • Nirmala Putul ⭐⭐: born 1972 in Dumka, Jharkhand.
  • Works ⭐⭐⭐: Nagade ki tarah bajte shabd.
  • Theme ⭐⭐⭐: saving one's settlement and culture.

2. Summary

  • Ills ⭐⭐⭐: drowning in haadiya, urban influence.
  • Identity ⭐⭐⭐: colour of the soil, Jharkhandi speech.
  • Hope ⭐⭐⭐: there is still much left to save.

3. Poetic Beauty

  • Language ⭐⭐⭐: simple, local words.
  • Imagery ⭐⭐⭐: bow, arrow, forest, river.
  • Mood ⭐⭐: worry and hope.

4. Message

  • Identity ⭐⭐⭐: language, tradition, nature.
  • Evil ⭐⭐⭐: guard against addiction.
  • Hope ⭐⭐: act together.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Aao Milkar Bachayen kis kavayitri ki kavita hai?
Answer dekho

Nirmala Putul ki, jinka janm 1972 mein Dumka (Jharkhand) mein hua.

Q2 Nirmala Putul ki do pramukh rachnayein kaun si hain?
Answer dekho

Nagade ki tarah bajte shabd aur Apne ghar ki talash mein.

Q3 'Maati ka rang' se kis or sanket hai?
Answer dekho

Chehre par Santhal Pargana ki maati ka rang — apni mool pehchaan aur mitti se judav.

Q4 'Bhasha mein Jharkhandipan' ka kya arth hai?
Answer dekho

Bhasha mein Jharkhand ke lok-shabdon, lehje aur sanskriti ki mithaas ka bana rehna.

Q5 Kavita mein dil ke bholepan ke saath akkhadpan aur jujharupan bachane par bal kyon hai?
Answer dekho

Kyunki sirf bholapan shoshan ka shikaar bana sakta hai; akkhadpan aur jujharupan sangharsh aur apni raksha ki shakti dete hain.

Q6 Kavita aadivasi samaj ki kin buraiyon ki or sanket karti hai?
Answer dekho

Haadiya (sharab) mein dooba rehna, bastiyon ka nangapan, shahri prabhav aur avishwas ki.

Q7 'Bachane ko bahut kuch bacha hai' ka kya aashay hai?
Answer dekho

Sab kuch nasht nahi hua — prakriti, geet, bhasha, vishwas aur sapne abhi bhi bache hain, unhe bachaya ja sakta hai.

Q8 Kavita mein aadivasi jeevan ke kaun se bimb aaye hain?
Answer dekho

Dhanush ki dori, teer, kulhaadi, jungle, nadiyan, pahad.

Q9 Kavita ki shaili ki kya visheshta hai?
Answer dekho

'Bachayen' ki punaravritti, saral deshaj bhasha aur suchi-roop mein bimb.

Q10 Kavita ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Apni pehchaan, parampara aur prakriti ko milkar bachana aur ummeed banaye rakhna.
