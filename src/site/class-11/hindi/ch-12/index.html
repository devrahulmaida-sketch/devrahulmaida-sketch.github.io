Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Kavayitri aur Pad Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Meera ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: janm san 1498, Kudki gaon (Marwad riyasat); mrityu san 1546; rachnayen Meera Padavali, Narsiji-ro-Mahero. Saguna dhara ki mahatvapurn bhakt kavayitri; Krishna ki upasika, Krishna ko hi aaradhya aur pati maanti rahin; Raidas unke guru mane jate hain.
  • Sankalan ⭐⭐⭐: Pad Narottam Das Swami dwara sankalit-sampadit Meera Muktavali se liye gaye hain. Source note: pad 1 NCERT Aroh ke current PDF (Reprint 2026-27) aur archived 2021-22 dono mein hai; pad 2 aur uske sawaal sirf archived 2021-22 PDF mein mile. Syllabus status ka koi claim nahi.
  • Pad 1 ⭐⭐⭐: Mere to Girdhar Gopal, doosro na koi - NCERT parichay: Krishna se ananyata aur vyarth ke karyon mein vyast logon ke prati dukh.
  • Pad 2 ⭐⭐⭐ (2021-22): Pag ghunghroo baandhi Meeran nachi - NCERT parichay: prem ras mein dubi Meera sabhi reeti-rivajon aur bandhanon se mukt hone aur Giridhar ke sneh ke karan amar hone ki baat kar rahi hain.

💡 Meera = saguna, Krishna aaradhya aur pati | Source = Meera Muktavali (Narottam Das Swami) | Pad 2 = archived 2021-22.

2️⃣ Pad 1 - Mere to Girdhar Gopal ⭐⭐⭐

  • Pati ⭐⭐⭐: Text: mere to Giridhar Gopal, doosro na koi; ja ke sir mor-mukut, mero pati soi - jiske sir par mor-mukut, wahi mera pati.
  • Kul ki kaani ⭐⭐⭐: Text: chhandi dayi kul ki kaani, kaha karihai koi?; santan dhig baithi-baithi, lok-laaj khoyi. NCERT shabd-arth: kaani = maryada; dhig = saath (book Aas-paas Q2: lok-laaj khone ka abhipray).
  • Prem-bel ⭐⭐⭐: Text: ansuvan jal seenchi-seenchi prem-beli boyi; ab ta beli phaili gayi, aanand-phal hoyi. NCERT shabd-arth: beli = prem ki bel.
  • Mathani ⭐⭐: Text: doodh ki mathaniyan bade prem se viloyi; dadhi mathi ghrit kaadhi liyo, daari dayi chhoyi. Shabd-arth: viloyi = mathi; chhoyi = chhachh, saarheen ansh. Supported reading: sar (ghee) rakh liya, chhachh chhod di - book Q2 (kha) bhaav aur shilp saundarya poochhta hai; apna tark likhein.
  • Bhakt aur jagat ⭐⭐: Text: bhagat dekhi raaji huyi, jagat dekhi royi; daasi Meeran Lal Giridhar, taaro ab mohi - bhakt dekhkar raaji, jagat dekhkar roti (book Q3: Meera jagat dekhkar kyon roti hain).

🎯 Pad 1: Giridhar hi pati -> kul ki kaani, lok-laaj -> ansu se prem-bel -> mathani -> bhagat/jagat -> taaro ab mohi.

3️⃣ Pad 2 - Pag ghunghroo baandhi ⭐⭐⭐ (archived 2021-22)

  • Nritya ⭐⭐⭐: Text: pag ghunghroo baandhi Meeran nachi; main to mere Narayan soon, aapahi ho gayi saachi. Shabd-arth: aapahi = apne hi aap, bina prayas; saachi = sachchi.
  • Log aur nyaat ⭐⭐⭐: Text: log kahai Meeran bhayi baavri; nyaat kahai kul-naasi. Shabd-arth: nyaat = kutumb ke log; kul-naasi = kul ka naash karne wali (book Q: log baavri kyon kehte hain).
  • Vis ka pyala ⭐⭐⭐: Text: vis ka pyala Rana bhejya, peevat Meeran haansi - Rana ne vish ka pyala bheja; Meera peete hue hans padi (shabd-arth). Book Q4 poochhta hai isme kya vyangya hai.
  • Sahaj milan ⭐⭐: Text: Meeran ke prabhu Giridhar naagar, sahaj mile avinaasi. Shabd-arth: sahaj = swabhavik roop se, anayas (book Aas-paas Q3: sahaj mile avinaasi kyon kaha).

🎯 Pad 2: ghunghroo baandhi nachi -> baavri aur kul-naasi -> vis ka pyala -> haansi -> sahaj mile avinaasi.

4️⃣ Kavya-Saundarya aur Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Bhav ⭐⭐⭐: Explicit (NCERT parichay): Meera ki kavita mein prem ki gambhir abhivyanjana, virah ki vedna aur milan ka ullas; mool mein dard. Teaching background: bhakti ke roop ko madhurya bhav kehna NCERT ke is paath ka shabd nahin hai.
  • Dristant ⭐⭐: Pad 1 mein prem-beli, mathaniyan, dadhi-ghrit, chhoyi jaise lok ke drishtant (text).
  • Bhasha-geyata ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: kavita ka pradhan gun sadgi aur saralta; muktak gey pad; bhasha mulat Rajasthani, kahin-kahin Brajbhasha ka prabhav; lok aur shastriya sangeet dono mein pad aaj bhi lokpriya.
  • Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐: Explicit: mere to Giridhar Gopal, doosro na koi; chhandi dayi kul ki kaani; bhagat dekhi raaji huyi, jagat dekhi royi. Supported reading: kul-maryada aur logon ki ninda ke bawajood Meera apni bhakti par dridh hain (NCERT parichay: ninda ya bandagi unhein path se vichalit nahin kar payi). Teaching background: parichay mein unhein stri mukti ki awaaz kaha gaya hai.

🎯 Tiers: explicit (text) / supported reading / teaching background; pad 2 ka source = archived 2021-22.

१. कवयित्री और पद

  • मीरा ⭐⭐: जन्म 1498 कुड़की; मृत्यु 1546; सगुण धारा की भक्त कवयित्री; कृष्ण आराध्य और पति।
  • संकलन ⭐⭐⭐: मीराँ मुक्तावली (नरोत्तम दास स्वामी); पद २ संग्रहित 2021-22 संस्करण से।
  • पद १ ⭐⭐⭐: ‘मेरे तो गिरधर गोपाल, दूसरो न कोई’।

२. पद १

  • पति ⭐⭐⭐: जा के सिर मोर-मुकुट, मेरो पति सोई।
  • त्याग ⭐⭐⭐: छाँड़ि दयी कुल की कानि; संतन ढिग बैठि-बैठि लोक-लाज खोयी।
  • प्रेम-बेलि ⭐⭐⭐: अंसुवन जल सींचि-सींचि प्रेम-बेलि बोयी।

३. पद २ (2021-22 संस्करण)

  • नृत्य ⭐⭐⭐: पग घुंघरू बांधि मीरां नाची।
  • विष ⭐⭐⭐: विस का प्याला राणा भेज्या, पीवत मीरां हाँसी।
  • सहज ⭐⭐: सहज मिले अविनासी।

४. काव्य-सौंदर्य और संदेश

  • भाव ⭐⭐⭐: प्रेम की गंभीर अभिव्यंजना; विरह और मिलन (NCERT परिचय)।
  • दृष्टांत ⭐⭐: प्रेम-बेलि, मथनियाँ, दधि-घृत, छोयी।
  • संदेश ⭐⭐⭐: पाठ-आधारित पठन - लोक-निंदा के बाद भी भक्ति में दृढ़ता।

1. Poet and Padas

  • Meera ⭐⭐: born 1498 Kudki; died 1546; saguna devotee poet; Krishna her beloved and husband.
  • Source ⭐⭐⭐: Meera Muktavali (Narottam Das Swami); pad 2 from archived 2021-22 edition.
  • Pad 1 ⭐⭐⭐: Mere to Girdhar Gopal, doosro na koi.

2. Pad 1

  • Husband ⭐⭐⭐: the one with the peacock crown on his head.
  • Sacrifice ⭐⭐⭐: gave up kul ki kaani; lost lok-laaj sitting with saints.
  • Love-vine ⭐⭐⭐: sown and watered with tears.

3. Pad 2 (2021-22 edition)

  • Dance ⭐⭐⭐: tied ghunghroo and danced.
  • Poison ⭐⭐⭐: the Rana sent a cup of poison; Meera laughed as she drank.
  • Sahaj ⭐⭐: sahaj mile avinaasi.

4. Poetic Beauty and Message

  • Feeling ⭐⭐⭐: deep expression of love; pain of separation and joy of union (NCERT note).
  • Imagery ⭐⭐: love-vine, churner, curd-ghee, buttermilk.
  • Message ⭐⭐⭐: reading from the text - steady devotion despite public censure.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Meera ke pad kis sankalan se liye gaye hain?
Answer dekho

Narottam Das Swami dwara sankalit-sampadit Meera Muktavali se (NCERT).

Q2 Meera kise apna pati maanti hain?
Answer dekho

Text: mere to Giridhar Gopal, doosro na koi; ja ke sir mor-mukut, mero pati soi.

Q3 Lok laaj khone ka kya abhipray hai? (chhandi dayi kul ki kaani)
Answer dekho

Text: chhandi dayi kul ki kaani (kaani = maryada) aur santan dhig baithi-baithi lok-laaj khoyi (dhig = saath). Abhipray apne shabdon mein likhein (book Aas-paas Q2).

Q4 Prem-bel ko Meera ne kaise seencha?
Answer dekho

Text: ansuvan jal seenchi-seenchi prem-beli boyi; ab ta beli phaili gayi, aanand-phal hoyi.

Q5 Mathani wali panktiyon mein kya kaha gaya hai?
Answer dekho

Text: doodh ki mathaniyan bade prem se viloyi; dadhi mathi ghrit kaadhi liyo, daari dayi chhoyi (viloyi = mathi; chhoyi = chhachh, saarheen ansh). Bhaav aur shilp saundarya book Q2 (kha) mein poochha gaya hai.

Q6 Meera bhakton aur jagat ko dekhkar kaisa mehsoos karti hain?
Answer dekho

Text: bhagat dekhi raaji huyi, jagat dekhi royi (book Q3: jagat dekhkar kyon roti hain - apna tark likhein).

Q7 Dusre pad (2021-22 edition) mein Meera kya karti hain?
Answer dekho

Text: pag ghunghroo baandhi Meeran nachi; main to mere Narayan soon, aapahi ho gayi saachi.

Q8 Log Meera ko kya kehte the?
Answer dekho

Text: log kahai Meeran bhayi baavri; nyaat kahai kul-naasi (nyaat = kutumb ke log; kul-naasi = kul ka naash karne wali) - 2021-22 edition.

Q9 Rana ji ne Meera ke saath kya kiya?
Answer dekho

Text: vis ka pyala Rana bhejya, peevat Meeran haansi - Rana ne vish ka pyala bheja; Meera peete hue hans padi (book Q4: isme kya vyangya hai).

Q10 Meera ke padon ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Explicit (text): mere to Giridhar Gopal, doosro na koi; bhagat dekhi raaji huyi, jagat dekhi royi. Supported reading: logon ki ninda ke bawajood bhakti mein dridhta. Teaching background: NCERT parichay unhein stri mukti ki awaaz kehta hai.
