Meera ke Pad — Meerabai

Mere to Girdhar Gopal — prem, tyaag aur bhakti

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Kavayitri aur Pad Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Meera ⭐⭐: Krishna ki diwani bhakt kavayitri; sagun bhakti ki kavi, Krishna ko apna pati maanti hain.
  • Sankalan ⭐⭐⭐: pad Narottam Das Swami dwara sankalit 'Meera Muktavali' se liye gaye hain.
  • Pad 1 ⭐⭐⭐: 'Mere to Girdhar Gopal, dusro na koi'.
  • Pad 2 ⭐⭐⭐: 'Pag ghunghru bandh Meera nachi re'.

💡 Meera = sagun, Krishna ko pati maanti | Sankalan = Meera Muktavali (Narottam Das Swami).

2️⃣ Pad 1 — Mere to Girdhar Gopal ⭐⭐⭐

  • Pati ⭐⭐⭐: Meera kehti hain — mere to Girdhar Gopal, dusra koi nahi; jiske sir par mor-mukut hai, wahi mera pati.
  • Tyaag ⭐⭐⭐: kul ki kaani chhod di; lok-laaj kho di; santon ke paas baith-baith kar bhakti mein leen.
  • Prem-bel ⭐⭐⭐: ansuon ke jal se sinch-sinch kar prem-bel boyi; ab bel phail gayi aur anand-phal laga.
  • Mathani ka drishtant ⭐⭐: jaise doodh ko mathkar makkhan nikala aur chhaachh alag kar di, waise Meera ne sansarikta ko alag kiya aur sachchi bhakti paayi.
  • Bhaav ⭐⭐: bhakton ko dekhkar prasann, sansaar mein phanse logon ko dekhkar dukhi; khud ko Krishna ki daasi maanti hain.

🎯 Pad 1: Girdhar hi pati → kul-laaj ka tyaag → ansu se prem-bel → mathani ka drishtant → bhakt dekh khushi, sansari dekh dukh.

3️⃣ Pad 2 — Pag ghunghru bandh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Nritya ⭐⭐⭐: Krishna ke prem mein diwani, pairon mein ghunghru bandh kar nachti hain.
  • Log kehte hain ⭐⭐: log use paagal kehte hain; sage-sambandhi kehte hain ki kul ka naam kharab kar rahi hai.
  • Vish ka pyala ⭐⭐⭐: Rana ji ne use maarne ko vish ka pyala bheja, jise wo hanste-hanste pi gayi.
  • Bhakti ki sahajta ⭐⭐: sachche mann se bhakti kare to prabhu sahaj hi mil jaate hain; Ishwar avinashi hain.

🎯 Pad 2: ghunghru bandh nachi → paagal kaha gaya → Rana ka vish pyala → hanskar piya → sachchi bhakti mein prabhu sahaj.

4️⃣ Kavya-Saundarya aur Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Bhav ⭐⭐⭐: madhurya bhav — Krishna pati ke roop mein; prem, samarpan aur tyaag.
  • Rupak aur drishtant ⭐⭐: prem-bel, mathani aur makkhan — seedhe, lok ke pratik.
  • Geyata ⭐⭐: pad gaane ke liye bane — saral, madhur, lok-gati.
  • Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐: sachcha prem aur samarpan kul-maryada aur duniya ke dabav se bada hai.

🎯 Shaili = madhurya bhav + rupak + geyata.

१. कवयित्री और पद

  • मीरा ⭐⭐: कृष्ण-भक्त कवयित्री।
  • संकलन ⭐⭐⭐: मीरा मुक्तावली।
  • पद ⭐⭐⭐: मेरे तो गिरधर गोपाल।

२. पद १ — मेरे तो गिरधर गोपाल

  • पति ⭐⭐⭐: मोर-मुकुट वाला गिरधर।
  • त्याग ⭐⭐⭐: कुल की कानि, लोक-लाज।
  • प्रेम-बेल ⭐⭐⭐: आँसुओं से सींची।

३. पद २ — पग घुँघरू बाँध

  • नृत्य ⭐⭐⭐: घुँघरू बाँध नाचीं।
  • विष ⭐⭐⭐: राणा का प्याला।
  • भक्ति ⭐⭐: सच्चे मन से।

४. काव्य-सौंदर्य और संदेश

  • भाव ⭐⭐⭐: माधुर्य भाव।
  • प्रतीक ⭐⭐: प्रेम-बेल, मथानी।
  • संदेश ⭐⭐⭐: सच्चा प्रेम सर्वोपरि।

1. Poet and Padas

  • Meera ⭐⭐: a Krishna devotee poet.
  • Source ⭐⭐⭐: Meera Muktavali.
  • Pad ⭐⭐⭐: Mere to Girdhar Gopal.

2. Pad 1 — Mere to Girdhar Gopal

  • Husband ⭐⭐⭐: Girdhar with the peacock crown.
  • Sacrifice ⭐⭐⭐: family honour, public shame.
  • Love-vine ⭐⭐⭐: watered with tears.

3. Pad 2 — Pag ghunghru bandh

  • Dance ⭐⭐⭐: tied anklets and danced.
  • Poison ⭐⭐⭐: the Rana's cup.
  • Devotion ⭐⭐: sincere heart.

4. Poetic Beauty and Message

  • Mood ⭐⭐⭐: madhurya bhakti.
  • Imagery ⭐⭐: love-vine, churner.
  • Message ⭐⭐⭐: true love above all.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Meera ke pad kis sankalan se liye gaye hain?
Answer dekho

Narottam Das Swami dwara sankalit Meera Muktavali se.

Q2 Meera kise apna pati maanti hain?
Answer dekho

Girdhar Gopal (Krishna) ko, jinke sir par mor-mukut hai.

Q3 'Chhaandi dayi kul ki kaani' ka kya arth hai?
Answer dekho

Meera ne kul ki maryada aur lok-laaj ka tyaag kar diya.

Q4 Prem-bel ko Meera ne kaise seencha?
Answer dekho

Ansuon ke jal se; usme anand-phal laga.

Q5 Mathani wale drishtant se Meera kya kehti hain?
Answer dekho

Doodh se makkhan nikaal chhaachh alag karne ki tarah unhone sansarikta chhodkar sachchi bhakti apnayi.

Q6 Meera bhakton aur sansari logon ko dekhkar kaisa mehsoos karti hain?
Answer dekho

Bhakton ko dekh prasann, sansaar mein phanse logon ko dekh dukhi.

Q7 Dusre pad mein Meera kya karti hain?
Answer dekho

Pairon mein ghunghru bandh kar Krishna-prem mein nachti hain.

Q8 Log Meera ko kya kehte the?
Answer dekho

Paagal; sage-sambandhi kehte the ki wo kul ka naam kharab kar rahi hai.

Q9 Rana ji ne Meera ke saath kya kiya?
Answer dekho

Maarne ke liye vish ka pyala bheja, jise Meera hanste-hanste pi gayi.

Q10 Meera ke padon ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Sachcha prem aur samarpan duniya ke dabav aur kul-maryada se badhkar hai.
