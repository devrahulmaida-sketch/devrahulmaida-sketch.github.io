हे भूख! मत मचल

He Bhookh! Mat Machal — Akka Mahadevi

Sharirik icchhaon par vijay — bhakti ki raah

Board Weightage: HIGH Video available Long + Short notes Hindi • English • Hinglish
Watch on YouTube Notes

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Kavayitri aur Vachan Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Akka Mahadevi ⭐⭐: 12vin sadi ki Kannada Virashaiv santkavayitri; Shiv (Chenna Mallikarjun) ki ananya bhakt.
  • Rachna ka swaroop ⭐⭐⭐: yeh vachan hain — Kannada ki mukt-chhand bhakti kavita; Hindi mein anuvaad.
  • Kendriya vichar ⭐⭐⭐: bhakt apni sharirik icchhaon (bhookh, pyas, neend) aur vikaaron (krodh, moh, lobh, mad) se mukti maangta hai.
  • Ishwar se sambandh ⭐⭐: kavi Ishwar ko 'jooh ke phool jaisa' sundar aur komal maanti hain.

💡 Akka Mahadevi + 12vin sadi + Virashaiv + Chenna Mallikarjun + vachan + vikaaron par niyantran.

2️⃣ Vachan ka Saransh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Bhookh-pyas se prarthana ⭐⭐⭐: kavayitri apne sharir ki bhookh, pyas aur neend se kehti hain ki woh na machle, na tadpe, na satayein — kyunki unka lakshya Ishwar-prapti hai.
  • Vikaaron par vijay ⭐⭐⭐: wah krodh, moh, lobh, mad aur matsar se bhi kehti hain ki woh shaant rahein.
  • Sansari maya ka tyaag ⭐⭐⭐: bhakt ke liye ghar, sukh aur shareerik aaram sab gaun hain.
  • Ishwar ko pukar ⭐⭐⭐: woh Ishwar ko 'mere jooh ke phool jaise ishwar' kehkar pukarti hain — apnapan aur komalta.
  • Bhiksha ki baat ⭐⭐: bhakt ko bhiksha ki zaroorat nahi — woh Ishwar ke pyaar se tript hai.

🎯 Sequence: bhookh-pyas-neend se prarthana → vikaaron par vijay → maya ka tyaag → Ishwar ko pukar → Ishwar ke pyaar se triptti.

3️⃣ Kaavya Saundarya ⭐⭐⭐

  • Sambodhan shaili ⭐⭐⭐: bhookh, pyas aur neend ko seedhe sambodhit karna — manvikaran.
  • Saral bhasha ⭐⭐: seedhi, bolchal ki bhasha — vachan ki visheshta.
  • Bhakti ras ⭐⭐⭐: shant aur bhakti ras ka mel.
  • Anuprash aur manvikaran ⭐⭐: 'mat machal' jaisi dhwani aur manvikaran.

💡 Shaili = sambodhan + manvikaran | ras = bhakti/shant | bhasha = saral.

4️⃣ Sandesh ⭐⭐

  • Sanyam ⭐⭐⭐: icchhaon par niyantran hi aadhyatmik unnati ka aadhar hai.
  • Tyaag ⭐⭐: bhakti mein sansari mohon ka tyaag zaroori.
  • Samarpan ⭐⭐: Ishwar ke prati purn samarpan hi sacchi bhakti.

🎯 Message: sanyam + tyaag + samarpan.

१. कवयित्री और वचन

  • अक्क महादेवी ⭐⭐: 12वीं सदी की वीरशैव संत कवयित्री।
  • वचन ⭐⭐⭐: कन्नड़ की भक्ति कविता।
  • विचार ⭐⭐⭐: इच्छाओं पर विजय।

२. सारांश

  • इच्छाएँ ⭐⭐⭐: भूख, प्यास, नींद से प्रार्थना।
  • विकार ⭐⭐⭐: क्रोध, मोह, लोभ, मद।
  • ईश्वर ⭐⭐⭐: जूही के फूल जैसे।

३. काव्य-सौंदर्य

  • संबोधन ⭐⭐⭐: भूख, प्यास से।
  • मानवीकरण ⭐⭐: इच्छाओं का।
  • रस ⭐⭐⭐: भक्ति और शांत।

४. संदेश

  • संयम ⭐⭐⭐: इच्छाओं पर नियंत्रण।
  • त्याग ⭐⭐: सांसारिक मोहों का।
  • समर्पण ⭐⭐: ईश्वर के प्रति।

1. Poet and Vachana

  • Akka Mahadevi ⭐⭐: 12th-century Virashaiva saint-poet.
  • Vachana ⭐⭐⭐: Kannada devotional verse.
  • Idea ⭐⭐⭐: conquering desires.

2. Summary

  • Desires ⭐⭐⭐: pleads with hunger, thirst and sleep.
  • Vices ⭐⭐⭐: anger, attachment, greed, pride.
  • God ⭐⭐⭐: addressed as tender as a jasmine flower.

3. Poetic Beauty

  • Address ⭐⭐⭐: speaks to hunger and thirst.
  • Personification ⭐⭐: of desires.
  • Rasa ⭐⭐⭐: devotion and calm.

4. Message

  • Restraint ⭐⭐⭐: control over desires.
  • Renunciation ⭐⭐: of worldly attachments.
  • Surrender ⭐⭐: to God.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 He Bhookh! Mat Machal ki kavayitri kaun hain?
Answer dekho

Akka Mahadevi — 12vin sadi ki Virashaiv santkavayitri aur Shiv (Chenna Mallikarjun) ki bhakt.

Q2 Yeh rachna kis vidha ki hai?
Answer dekho

Vachan — Kannada ki mukt-chhand bhakti kavita.

Q3 Kavayitri bhookh-pyas-neend se kya kehti hain?
Answer dekho

Ki woh na machalein, na tadpayein, kyunki unka lakshya Ishwar-prapti hai.

Q4 Vachan mein kin vikaaron ka zikr hai?
Answer dekho

Krodh, moh, lobh, mad aur matsar — kavayitri unse shaant rehne ko kehti hain.

Q5 'Jooh ke phool jaise Ishwar' kehne ka kya arth hai?
Answer dekho

Ishwar ko komal, sundar aur apna maanna — bhakti mein apnapan ka bhaav.

Q6 Kavita mein kaun sa alankar hai?
Answer dekho

Manvikaran (bhookh-pyas ko sambodhit karna) aur sambodhan shaili.

Q7 Vachan ki bhasha ki visheshta kya hai?
Answer dekho

Saral aur bolchal ki bhasha; seedhi bhaav-abhivyakti.

Q8 Kavita mein kaun sa ras hai?
Answer dekho

Bhakti aur shant ras.

Q9 Sansari maya ke baare mein kavayitri ka kya drishtikon hai?
Answer dekho

Woh ise gaun maanti hain — ghar, sukh aur sharirik aaram Ishwar ke aage mahatvaheen hain.

Q10 Is vachan ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Sanyam, tyaag aur Ishwar ke prati samarpan hi bhakti ki raah hai.
