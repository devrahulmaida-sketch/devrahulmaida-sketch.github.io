Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Kavi aur Kavita Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Bhawani Prasad Mishra ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: janm san 1913, Tigariya gaon, Hoshangabad (M.P.); mrityu san 1985; rachnayen Satpura ke jungle, Sannata, Geetfarosh, Chakit hai dukh, Buni hui rassi, Khushboo ke shilalekh, Anam tum aate ho, Idam na mam aadi; samman Sahitya Akademi, Madhya Pradesh Shasan ka Shikhar Samman, Delhi Prashasan ka Ghalib Puraskar, Padmashri. Unhen kavita ka Gandhi bhi kaha gaya hai.
  • Kavita ka prasang ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: kavi ko jail-pravas ke dauran ghar se visthapan ki peeda salti hai. Source note: NCERT Aroh current PDF (Reprint 2026-27). Not in this NCERT text: kavita ka rachna-varsh, ya kisi andolan se seedha sambandh (is ansh mein sirf jail-pravas ka zikr hai).
  • Kendriya bhaav ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: ghar ki avadharna ki sarthak aur marmik yaad kavita ki kendriya samvedna hai; kavi ke smriti-sansar mein parijan ek-ek kar shamil hote chale jaate hain.
  • Shaili ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: sahaj lay ki kavita; bol-chal ke gadyatmak-se lagte vakya-vinyas ko kavita mein badalne ki kshamta; jis vishay ko uthate hain use gharelu bana lete hain. Teaching background: NCERT parichay is kavita ka ras ka naam nahin deta; exam mein ise karun ras kaha jaata hai.

💡 Bhawani Prasad Mishra (1913-1985) + jail-pravas + pani ka girna + parivar ki yaad + sawan se sandesh.

2️⃣ Kavita-Saransh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Pani aur ghirna ⭐⭐⭐: Text: aaj pani gir raha hai, bahut pani gir raha hai; raat bhar girta raha hai, pran man ghirta raha hai; ghar nazar mein tir raha hai (shabd-arth: nazar mein tir raha hai = ankhon mein tair raha hai). Book Q1: pani ke raat bhar girne aur pran-man ke ghirne mein sambandh.
  • Ghar aur bhai-behen ⭐⭐⭐: Text: ghar ki mujhse door hai jo, ghar khushi ka poor hai jo (poor hai jo = paripurn, khushiyon se bharapoora); ghar mein char bhai, mayke mein bahin aai; bahin aai baap ke ghar, haay re paritap ke ghar! (paritap = atyadhik dukh; book Q2).
  • Maa ⭐⭐⭐: Text: aur maa bin-padhi meri, dukh mein vah gadhi meri; maa ki jiski god mein sir rakh liya to dukh nahin phir; maa ki jiski sneh-dhara ka yahan tak bhi pasara; use likhna nahin aata jo ki uska patra paata.
  • Pita ⭐⭐⭐: Text: pita ji jinko budhapa ek kshan bhi nahin vyaapa; jo abhi bhi daud jaayen, jo abhi bhi khilkhilayen; maut ke aage na hichken, sher ke aage na bichken; bol mein badal garajta, kaam mein jhanjha larajta (book Q3: pita ke vyaktitva ki visheshtayen).
  • Paanchva main ⭐⭐⭐: Text: paanchva main hun abhaga, jise sone par suhaga; pita ji kehte rahe hain pyaar mein bahte rahe hain; aaj unke swarn bete lage honge unhen hete (hete = gaun, heen), kyonki main un par suhaga bandha betha hun abhaga.
  • Sawan se sandesh ⭐⭐: Text: he sajile hare sawan, he ki mere punya pawan, tum baras lo ve na barsen, paanchven ko ve na tarsen; antim panktiyon mein kavi kehta hai kah na dena jagta hun, kah na dena maun hun main, unhen koi shak na dena (book Q5).

🎯 Sequence: pani ka girna -> ghar nazar mein tirna -> bhai-behen, paritap ka ghar -> maa -> pita -> paanchva main -> sawan se sandesh.

3️⃣ Kaavya Saundarya ⭐⭐⭐

  • Bolchal ki bhasha ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: bol-chal ke gadyatmak-se lagte vakya-vinyas ko kavita mein badalne ki kshamta; kavita sahaj aur lok ke adhik kareeb hai. Text mein kah na dena, yon na kehna, hai ki jo vaisa na kehna jaisi baatcheet ki panktiyan hain.
  • Dohrav ⭐⭐⭐: Text mein pani gir raha hai, ghar nazar mein tir raha hai, char bhai char bahinen, paanchven baar-baar dohraye gaye hain. Supported reading: dohrav se yaad ka ghirna aur laut-laut kar aana dikhta hai.
  • Bimb ⭐⭐: Text mein bimb: pani gir raha hai, sajile hare sawan, maa ki god, sher ke aage na bichken, bol mein badal garajta, kaam mein jhanjha larajta; book ka chitr karavas ki khidki ke saamne parivar dikhata hai.
  • Sambodhan ⭐⭐: Text: he sajile hare sawan, he ki mere punya pawan - kavi sawan ko sambodhit karta hai. Teaching background: Aas-paas Q1: prakriti ke upadan ko sandeshvahak ke roop mein dikhane wali panch rachnayen sankalit karna.

💡 Tiers: explicit (text/parichay) / supported reading / teaching background.

4️⃣ Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Explicit (NCERT parichay) ⭐⭐⭐: Ghar ki avadharna ki sarthak aur marmik yaad kavita ki kendriya samvedna hai; ghar mein parijan ek-ek kar smriti-sansar mein shamil hote chale jaate hain.
  • Supported reading ⭐⭐: Antim panktiyon mein kavi sawan se kehta hai main mast hun, katne mein vyast hun, kah na dena jagta hun, aadmi se bhagta hun, kah na dena maun hun main, unhen koi shak na dena - yeh supported reading hai ki wah apni peeda aur chinta parijanon se chhipana chahta hai (book Q5).
  • Teaching background ⭐: Book Q4: bas shabd ka prayog (main maze mein hun sahi hai, ghar nahin hun bas yahi hai; kintu yah bas bada bas hai, isi bas se sab viras hai); Aas-paas Q2: ghar se alag hokar aap ghar ko kaise yaad karte hain. Is path mein kisi aankde ya aitihasik tareekh ka zikr nahin hai.

🎯 Message: ghar aur parivar ki marmik yaad | tiers: explicit / supported / background.

१. कवि और कविता

  • भवानी प्रसाद मिश्र ⭐⭐: जन्म 1913 टिगरिया (होशंगाबाद); मृत्यु 1985; ‘कविता का गांधी’ (NCERT परिचय)।
  • प्रसंग ⭐⭐⭐: जेल-प्रवास में घर से विस्थापन की पीड़ा (NCERT परिचय)।
  • भाव ⭐⭐⭐: घर की सार्थक और मार्मिक याद (केंद्रीय संवेदना)।

२. सारांश

  • पानी ⭐⭐⭐: पानी रात भर गिरता रहा, प्राण-मन घिरता रहा, घर नज़र में तिर रहा है।
  • परिवार ⭐⭐⭐: चार भाई, चार बहिनें, बिन-पढ़ी माँ, पिता जी जिन्हें बुढ़ापा एक क्षण भी नहीं व्यापा।
  • संदेश ⭐⭐⭐: सावन से: तुम बरस लो वे न बरसें, पाँचवें को वे न तरसें।

३. काव्य-सौंदर्य

  • भाषा ⭐⭐⭐: बोल-चाल का वाक्य-विन्यास, सहज लय (NCERT परिचय)।
  • दोहराव ⭐⭐⭐: पानी गिर रहा है, घर नज़र में तिर रहा है।
  • संबोधन ⭐⭐: ‘हे सजीले हरे सावन’।

४. संदेश

  • स्पष्ट (परिचय) ⭐⭐⭐: घर की अवधारणा की सार्थक और मार्मिक याद।
  • पठन ⭐⭐: अंतिम पंक्तियों में कवि अपनी पीड़ा परिजनों से छिपाना चाहता है (अभ्यास 5)।
  • पृष्ठभूमि ⭐: ‘बस’ शब्द का प्रयोग (अभ्यास 4)।

1. Poet and Poem

  • Bhawani Prasad Mishra ⭐⭐: born 1913 Tigariya (Hoshangabad); died 1985; also called the Gandhi of poetry (NCERT introduction).
  • Context ⭐⭐⭐: the pain of displacement from home during jail (NCERT introduction).
  • Feeling ⭐⭐⭐: a meaningful memory of the idea of home.

2. Summary

  • Rain ⭐⭐⭐: falls all night; the heart is enclosed; home floats before the eyes.
  • Family ⭐⭐⭐: four brothers, four sisters, an unlettered mother, a father untouched by old age.
  • Message ⭐⭐⭐: to the rain: you may fall, but not on them; let the fifth not be left longing.

3. Poetic Beauty

  • Language ⭐⭐⭐: conversational sentence flow, natural rhythm (NCERT introduction).
  • Repetition ⭐⭐⭐: the rain is falling; home floats before the eyes.
  • Address ⭐⭐: to the green monsoon (sawan).

4. Message

  • Explicit ⭐⭐⭐: a meaningful and moving memory of home (NCERT introduction).
  • Reading ⭐⭐: in the last lines the poet wants to hide his pain from his family (Q5).
  • Background ⭐: the use of the word bas (Q4); no dates or figures are given.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Ghar ki yaad ke kavi kaun hain?
Answer dekho

Bhawani Prasad Mishra - NCERT parichay: janm san 1913, Tigariya (Hoshangabad), mrityu san 1985; unhen kavita ka Gandhi bhi kaha gaya hai.

Q2 Is kavita mein kavi ki kaunsi peeda ka zikr NCERT parichay karta hai?
Answer dekho

NCERT parichay: kavi ko jail-pravas ke dauran ghar se visthapan ki peeda salti hai. Is ansh mein rachna ke varsh ya kisi andolan ka zikr nahin hai.

Q3 Pani ke raat bhar girne aur pran-man ke ghirne mein kya sambandh hai?
Answer dekho

Text: aaj pani gir raha hai... raat bhar girta raha hai, pran man ghirta raha hai; ghar nazar mein tir raha hai. Supported reading: barish ke saath kavi ka man ghar ki yaad mein ghir jaata hai (book Q1).

Q4 Kavita mein kin-kin ki yaad aati hai?
Answer dekho

Text: char bhai, char bahinen, bin-padhi maa, pita ji aur paanchva (kavi swayam: paanchva main hun abhaga).

Q5 Kavi sawan se kya kehta hai?
Answer dekho

Text: he sajile hare sawan, he ki mere punya pawan, tum baras lo ve na barsen, paanchven ko ve na tarsen.

Q6 Kavita ki bhasha ki visheshta NCERT parichay ke anusar kya hai?
Answer dekho

NCERT parichay: sahaj lay; bol-chal ke gadyatmak-se lagte vakya-vinyas ko kavita mein badalne ki kshamta.

Q7 Kavita ka bhaav aur ras kaisa hai?
Answer dekho

Text mein haay re paritap ke ghar, nain jal se chhaye honge jaisi panktiyan hain. Teaching background: exam mein ise karun ras kaha jaata hai; NCERT parichay ras ka naam nahin deta.

Q8 Sawan ko sambodhit karne se kavita mein kya hota hai?
Answer dekho

Supported reading: kavi apna sandesh sawan ke madhyam se bhejta hai - tum baras lo ve na barsen - isse kavita seedhi baatcheet jaisi aur marmik lagti hai. Book Aas-paas Q1: prakriti ko sandeshvahak ke roop mein lene wali rachnayen khojna.

Q9 Antim panktiyon mein kavi apni kaunsi sthiti parijanon se chhipana chahta hai?
Answer dekho

Text: kah na dena jagta hun, kah na dena maun hun main, khud na samjhun kaun hun main, unhen koi shak na dena. Supported reading: wah apni peeda aur bechaini chhipana chahta hai (book Q5).

Q10 Kavita ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Explicit (NCERT parichay): ghar ki avadharna ki sarthak aur marmik yaad kavita ki kendriya samvedna hai. Supported reading: kavi parivar ke prati sneh aur chinta apne dukh ke upar rakhta hai (unhen dete dheer rehna). Teaching background: book Q4 bas shabd par aur Aas-paas Q2 apni ghar ki yaad par likhne ko kehta hai; koi aankda nahin diya gaya.
