Ghar ki Yaad — Bhawani Prasad Mishra

Jail ki baarish mein ghar ki yaad — maa, pita aur behnon ka pyaar

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Kavi aur Kavita Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Bhawani Prasad Mishra ⭐⭐: Dusra Saptak ke kavi (1913-1985); Gandhivadi vichardhara ke kavi; saral bhasha aur bolchal ki shaili ke liye prasiddh.
  • Kavita ka prasang ⭐⭐⭐: kavi ne yeh kavita jail mein (Bharat Chhodo Andolan ke daur mein) likhi — sawan ki baarish ne unhe ghar ki yaad dila di.
  • Kendriya bhaav ⭐⭐⭐: ghar, maa-pita aur behnon ke pyaar ki yaad aur unse door hone ki peeda.
  • Ras ⭐⭐: karun ras aur vatsalya/parivarik sneh.

💡 Bhawani Prasad Mishra + jail + sawan ki baarish + ghar ki yaad + parivar ka sneh.

2️⃣ Kavita-Saransh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Baarish aur yaad ⭐⭐⭐: baarish raat bhar gir rahi hai — kavi ke prann-man ghire hue hain, aur ghar ki yaad umad aati hai.
  • Parivar ki tasveer ⭐⭐⭐: kavi ko apne maata-pita, bhai-behnon yaad aate hain; ghar ki sabse chhoti behen aur bhaiyon ke saath bitaya samay man mein taza hota hai.
  • Maa ki yaad ⭐⭐⭐: maa kam padhi-likhi par sneh se bhari hain; woh rote hue bete ko yaad karti hongi — kavi ka man bhar aata hai.
  • Pita ka sneh ⭐⭐⭐: pita kadak dikhte hain par andar se komal — kavi ko unki chinta aur pyaar dikhta hai.
  • Sawan se sandesh ⭐⭐⭐: kavi sawan se kehta hai ki woh ghar jakar maa-pita ko dhadhas de — 'main theek hoon, chinta mat karo'.
  • Kavi ka dard ⭐⭐: jail ki tanhai aur ghar ki yaad milkar kavita ko marmik banate hain.

🎯 Sequence: baarish → ghar ki yaad → maa-pita-behen ki tasveer → sawan se sandesh → kavi ka dard.

3️⃣ Kaavya Saundarya ⭐⭐⭐

  • Bolchal ki bhasha ⭐⭐⭐: saral, baatcheet jaisi bhasha — lagta hai kavi seedhe baat kar rahe hain.
  • Karun ras ⭐⭐⭐: kavita ka poora bhaav karun hai; parivarik sneh ki garmahat bhi.
  • Bimb ⭐⭐: baarish, ghar ka aangan, maa ka chehra — jeevant bimb.
  • Sambodhan ⭐⭐: sawan ko sambodhit karna kavita ko naatkiya aur marmik banata hai.

💡 Shaili = baatcheet + sambodhan | ras = karun | bimb = baarish + parivar.

4️⃣ Sandesh ⭐⭐

  • Parivar ka mahatva ⭐⭐⭐: ghar aur parivar ka sneh insaan ki sabse badi taakat hai.
  • Sangharsh ki keemat ⭐⭐: desh ke liye jail jaane wale swatantrata senaniyon ne apne parivar ka tyaag bhi kiya.
  • Bhavnaon ki saralta ⭐⭐: bade-bade sangharsh ke beech bhi saral, manviya bhavna rehti hai.

🎯 Message: parivar ka sneh + sangharsh ki keemat + manviya bhavna.

१. कवि और कविता

  • भवानी प्रसाद मिश्र ⭐⭐: दूसरा सप्तक के कवि।
  • प्रसंग ⭐⭐⭐: जेल में सावन की बारिश।
  • भाव ⭐⭐⭐: घर-परिवार की याद।

२. सारांश

  • बारिश ⭐⭐⭐: रात भर गिरती बारिश।
  • परिवार ⭐⭐⭐: माँ, पिता, भाई-बहन की याद।
  • संदेश ⭐⭐⭐: सावन से घर संदेश।

३. काव्य-सौंदर्य

  • भाषा ⭐⭐⭐: सरल, बातचीत जैसी।
  • रस ⭐⭐⭐: करुण।
  • संबोधन ⭐⭐: सावन से।

४. संदेश

  • परिवार ⭐⭐⭐: सबसे बड़ी ताक़त।
  • त्याग ⭐⭐: स्वतंत्रता सेनानियों का।
  • भावना ⭐⭐: सरल, मानवीय।

1. Poet and Poem

  • Bhawani Prasad Mishra ⭐⭐: poet of the Dusra Saptak.
  • Context ⭐⭐⭐: rain in jail.
  • Feeling ⭐⭐⭐: longing for family and home.

2. Summary

  • Rain ⭐⭐⭐: falling all night.
  • Family ⭐⭐⭐: memories of parents and siblings.
  • Message ⭐⭐⭐: asks the rain to carry reassurance home.

3. Poetic Beauty

  • Language ⭐⭐⭐: simple, conversational.
  • Rasa ⭐⭐⭐: pathos.
  • Address ⭐⭐: to the rain.

4. Message

  • Family ⭐⭐⭐: our greatest strength.
  • Sacrifice ⭐⭐: by freedom fighters.
  • Emotion ⭐⭐: simple and human.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Ghar ki Yaad ke kavi kaun hain?
Answer dekho

Bhawani Prasad Mishra — Dusra Saptak ke kavi aur Gandhivadi vichardhara se prabhavit.

Q2 Kavi ne yeh kavita kahan likhi?
Answer dekho

Jail mein, Bharat Chhodo Andolan ke daur mein, jab sawan ki baarish ne ghar ki yaad dila di.

Q3 Kavi ko ghar ki yaad kyun aati hai?
Answer dekho

Raat bhar ki baarish aur jail ki tanhai unhe parivar ke sneh ki yaad dilati hai.

Q4 Kavita mein kin-kin ki yaad aati hai?
Answer dekho

Maata-pita, bhai aur behnon ki — khaaskar maa ka sneh aur pita ki chinta.

Q5 Kavi sawan se kya kehta hai?
Answer dekho

Ki woh ghar jaakar maa-pita ko dhadhas de ki kavi theek hai aur chinta na karein.

Q6 Kavita ki bhasha ki visheshta batayiye.
Answer dekho

Saral, baatcheet jaisi bhasha aur sambodhan shaili.

Q7 Kavita mein kaun sa ras hai?
Answer dekho

Karun ras, saath mein parivarik sneh ki komalta.

Q8 Sawan ko sambodhit karne ka kya prabhav hai?
Answer dekho

Kavita naatkiya aur marmik ban jaati hai aur kavi ki bhavnayein seedhe pathak tak pahunchti hain.

Q9 Kavi ki manodasha kaisi hai?
Answer dekho

Bechain, bhavuk aur ghar se door hone ki peeda se bhari.

Q10 Kavita ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Parivar ka sneh insaan ki sabse badi taakat hai; swatantrata sangharsh mein tyaag ki keemat bhi chukani padti hai.
