Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Lekhika aur Path Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Krishna Sobti ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: janm 18 February 1925, Gujrat (pashchimi Punjab, ab Pakistan); mrityu 25 January 2019; Zindaginama, Dilodanish, Ai Ladki, Samay Sargam (upanyas); Sahitya Akademi Samman, Hindi Akademi ka Shalaka Samman, Sahitya Akademi ki Mahattar Sadasyata. Jnanpith ka zikr is text mein nahi (teaching background).
  • Path ka swaroop ⭐⭐: shabdchitra jo ‘Hum-Hashmat’ sangrah se liya gaya; NCERT: Sobti ne apne hi ek doosre vyaktitva ke roop mein Hashmat charitra banaya.
  • Kendra ⭐⭐⭐: khandani nanbai Miyan Nasiruddin ke vyaktitva, ruchiyon aur swabhav ka shabdchitra; unki masihai andaaz se roti pakane ki kala (NCERT parichay). Dukaan Matiya Mahal mein, Jama Masjid ke aade gadhaiya mohalle ki taraf.
  • Shaili ⭐⭐: NCERT: Sobti ki bhasha mein Sanskritnishth tatsamta, Urdu ka baankpan, Punjabi ki zindadili; path mein jeevant bolchal aur sawaal-jawab.

💡 Krishna Sobti + Hum-Hashmat + shabdchitra + Miyan Nasiruddin (nanbai, Matiya Mahal).

2️⃣ Kahani-Saransh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Dukaan ki pehchan ⭐⭐: ek dupahri lekhika Matiya Mahal mein mamuli andheri si dukaan par patapat aate ka dher dekhkar thithakti hain; pata chalta hai khandani nanbai Miyan Nasiruddin ki dukaan hai, jo chhappan kism ki rotiyon ke liye mashhoor hain.
  • Miyan ka roop ⭐⭐: charpai par beedi ka maza; mausamon ki maar se pakka chehra, aankhon mein kaiyan bholapan, peshani par manje hue karigar ke tevar. Lekhika ko akhbarnavis samajhkar kehte hain: “yeh to khojiyon ki khurafat hai.”
  • Hunar kaise aata hai ⭐⭐⭐: Miyan: “nanbai ilm lene kahin aur jayega?”; jo seekha apne walid ustad se, baap-dada ka hunar. “Kaam karne se aata hai, nasihaton se nahi.” Ustad ki ‘alif, be, jim’ wali padhai aur hath sir par.
  • Rotiyon ke naam ⭐⭐: text ki list: bakarkhani, shirmal, taftan, besni, khamiri, rumali, gaon-dida, gazeban, tunki. “Tunki papad se zyada mahin hoti hai.”
  • Taleem aur kahavat ⭐⭐: “taleem ki taleem bhi badi cheez hoti hai”; bartan dhona, bhatti banana, bhatti ko aanch dena; “khandani nanbai kuen mein bhi roti paka sakta hai”; “roti janab pakti hai aanch se.” Badshah salamat ki “na aag se pake, na pani se bane” wali chiz Miyan batate nahi.
  • Ant ⭐⭐: Bahadur Shah Zafar ka naam aate hi Miyan ki dilchaspi ghatti hai; Babban Miyan se bhatti sulgane ko kehte hain; majuri: 2 rupaye man aate ki, 4 rupaye man maide ki. Ant: “utar gaye wo zamane… gaye wo kadrdan… nikali tandoor se, nigli aur hajam!”

    • 3️⃣ Miyan Nasiruddin ka Charitra ⭐⭐⭐

    • Khandani hunarmand ⭐⭐⭐: walid Miyan Barkat Shahi nanbai Gadhaiyawale, dada Aala nanbai Miyan Kallan; Miyan ne walid ke thiye par baithkar kaam sambhala.
    • Pesha aur kala ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: apne pesha ko kala ka darja dete hain aur karke seekhne ko asli hunar maante hain.
    • Rukha, spasht ⭐⭐: kai jagah rukhai se jawab: “yeh hum na batawenge”, “bal ki khal nikalne par tule hain”; khushamad nahi (supported reading).
    • Padhai par rai ⭐⭐: text: “taleem bhi badi cheez”, par nasihat se nahi, kaam se hunar. School ke baare mein text kuch nahi kehta (teaching background).
    • Dilchaspi aur yaadein ⭐⭐: apne karigaron ki shagirdi aur majuri, kadrdan zamane ki yaad — Miyan ko purane din yaad aate hain (supported reading).
  • Quick recap: khandani hunarmand + pesha ko kala maanne wala + rukha-spasht + purane kadrdan zamane ko yaad karne wala (supported reading).

    • 4️⃣ Sandesh aur Shabd-Shilp ⭐⭐

    • Hunar ka samman ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): text koi seedha moral nahi deta; Miyan ka “kaam karne se aata hai” aur parichay ka “pesha ko kala ka darja” yeh arth dete hain. Exam mein hunar ki izzat likho.
    • Karke seekhna ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): walid se seekha, “nasihaton se nahi”, bartan dhona se aanch dena tak — parichay: karke seekhna asli hunar.
    • Shabdchitra ⭐⭐: kisi vyakti ke vyaktitva, ruchiyon aur swabhav ka chitra (NCERT parichay); yahan Miyan Nasiruddin.
    • Purana Delhi aur yaad ⭐⭐ (supported reading): Jama Masjid ke paas ka mohalla, Bahadur Shah Zafar ka naam aur ant ka “utar gaye wo zamane” khoye hue samay ka ehsaas dete hain.
  • Message recap: hunar ki izzat + anubhav se seekhna (supported reading, saboot upar ke quotes).

    • १. लेखिका और पाठ

    • कृष्णा सोबती ⭐⭐: जन्म 18 फ़रवरी 1925, गुजरात (पश्चिमी पंजाब); मृत्यु 25 जनवरी 2019; साहित्य अकादमी सम्मान, हिंदी अकादमी का शलाका सम्मान।
    • स्वरूप ⭐⭐: शब्दचित्र — ‘हम-हशमत’ से।
    • केंद्र ⭐⭐⭐: मटियामहल के खानदानी नानबाई मियाँ नसीरुद्दीन।

    २. सारांश

    • दुकान ⭐⭐: दुपहर में मटियामहल की अँधेरी सी दुकान पर आटे का ढेर।
    • दावा ⭐⭐⭐: छप्पन किस्म की रोटियाँ — खानदानी पेशा।
    • हुनर ⭐⭐⭐: “काम करने से आता है, नसीहतों से नहीं।”
    • स्वभाव ⭐⭐: रुखाई और तेवर; अंत में “उतर गए वे ज़माने”।

    ३. चरित्र

    • खानदानी ⭐⭐⭐: वालिद मियाँ बरकत शाही, दादा आला नानबाई मियाँ कल्लन।
    • स्वाभिमानी ⭐⭐: पेशे को कला का दर्जा (NCERT परिचय)।
    • रुखे-स्पष्ट ⭐⭐ (supported reading): “यह हम न बतावेंगे”; खुशामद नहीं।

    ४. संदेश

    • हुनर का सम्मान ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): “पेशे को कला का दर्जा”।
    • अनुभव ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): “काम करने से आता है, नसीहतों से नहीं”।
    • विधा ⭐⭐: शब्दचित्र।

    1. Writer and Text

    • Krishna Sobti ⭐⭐: born 18 Feb 1925, Gujrat; died 25 Jan 2019; Sahitya Akademi Samman, Hindi Akademi Shalaka Samman.
    • Form ⭐⭐: a word-sketch from Hum-Hashmat.
    • Focus ⭐⭐⭐: Miyan Nasiruddin, the hereditary nanbai (baker) of Matiya Mahal.

    2. Summary

    • The shop ⭐⭐: a midday visit; a dim shop with a heap of flour near Jama Masjid.
    • The claim ⭐⭐⭐: famous for fifty-six kinds of bread; a family trade.
    • Skill ⭐⭐⭐: “comes from doing, not from advice”.
    • Nature ⭐⭐: gruff and proud; ends on “those times are gone”.

    3. Character

    • Hereditary ⭐⭐⭐: father Miyan Barkat Shahi, grandfather Aala nanbai Miyan Kallan.
    • Self-respecting ⭐⭐: treats his trade as an art (NCERT intro).
    • Gruff, plain-spoken ⭐⭐ (supported reading): “I will not tell you that”.

    4. Message and Style

    • Respect for skill ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): “pesha ko kala ka darja” (NCERT intro).
    • Experience ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): “it comes from work, not advice”.
    • Genre ⭐⭐: a word-sketch.

    Board Practice — 10 Questions

    Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

    Q1 Miyan Nasiruddin path ki lekhika kaun hain aur yeh kis pustak se hai?
    Answer dekho

    Krishna Sobti; yeh unke shabdchitra-sangrah ‘Hum-Hashmat’ se liya gaya hai.

    Q2 Miyan Nasiruddin kis pesha ke hain?
    Answer dekho

    Khandani nanbai — chhappan kism ki rotiyon ke liye mashhoor (text).

    Q3 Miyan Nasiruddin ki dukaan kahan hai?
    Answer dekho

    Matiya Mahal mein, Jama Masjid ke aade gadhaiya mohalle ki taraf, ek mamuli andheri si dukaan.

    Q4 Miyan ke anusaar hunar kaise seekha jaata hai?
    Answer dekho

    Unhone jo seekha walid ustad se; “kaam karne se aata hai, nasihaton se nahi”; bartan dhona, bhatti banana, bhatti ko aanch dena.

    Q5 Miyan ko apne pesha par kaisa garv hai?
    Answer dekho

    NCERT parichay: wo pesha ko kala ka darja dete hain; walid Miyan Barkat Shahi, dada Aala nanbai Miyan Kallan bhi nanbai the.

    Q6 Shabdchitra kise kehte hain?
    Answer dekho

    NCERT parichay ke anusaar kisi vyakti ke vyaktitva, ruchiyon aur swabhav ka chitra shabdon mein; yeh path uska udaharan hai.

    Q7 Miyan Nasiruddin kab tunak jaate hain?
    Answer dekho

    Jab lekhika ke sawal unhe nagwaar lagte hain, wo rukhai se bolte hain — jaise “yeh hum na batawenge” aur “bal ki khal nikalne par tule hain”.

    Q8 Path mein kin rotiyon ke naam aaye hain?
    Answer dekho

    Bakarkhani, shirmal, taftan, besni, khamiri, rumali, gaon-dida, gazeban, tunki.

    Q9 Miyan Nasiruddin ka charitra 3 bindu mein likhiye.
    Answer dekho

    1. khandani hunarmand (walid, dada nanbai); 2. pesha ko kala maanne wale; 3. rukhe-spasht aur purane zamane ko yaad karne wale (supported reading).

    Q10 Is path ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
    Answer dekho

    Text koi seedha moral nahi likhta. Supported reading: hunar ki izzat aur karke seekhna — saboot: “kaam karne se aata hai, nasihaton se nahi”, “taleem ki taleem bhi badi cheez”, aur parichay ka pesha ko kala ka darja.
