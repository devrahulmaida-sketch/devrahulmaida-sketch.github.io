मियाँ नसीरुद्दीन

Miyan Nasiruddin — Krishna Sobti

Nanbai ki kala — kaam karke seekhne ka asli hunar

Board Weightage: HIGH Video available Long + Short notes Hindi • English • Hinglish
Watch on YouTube Notes

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Lekhika aur Path Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Krishna Sobti ⭐⭐: Hindi ki prasiddh lekhika — Zindaginama ke liye Sahitya Akademi aur Jnanpith se sammanit.
  • Path ka swaroop ⭐⭐: yeh shabdchitra (rekhachitra) hai — unki pustak 'Hum Hashmat' se liya gaya; Krishna Sobti ne ise Hashmat naam se likha.
  • Kendra ⭐⭐⭐: Delhi ke Matia Mahal (Jama Masjid ke paas) ke khandani nanbai Miyan Nasiruddin ka vyaktitva, swabhav aur kaam.
  • Shaili ⭐: bolchal ki zabaan, chhote-chhote vaakya aur jeevant sanvaad — lagta hai lekhika khud nanbai se baat kar rahi hain.

💡 Krishna Sobti + Hum Hashmat + shabdchitra + Miyan Nasiruddin (nanbai, Matia Mahal). Yeh 4 cheezein yaad rakho.

2️⃣ Kahani-Saransh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Dukaan ki pehchan ⭐⭐: lekhika ek din Matia Mahal ki andheri, chhoti si dukaan mein aate ka dher sanna dekhti hain — pata chalta hai yeh Miyan Nasiruddin ki dukaan hai.
  • Nanbai ka daawa ⭐⭐⭐: Miyan khud ko 'masihai andaaz' se chhappan tarah ki rotiyan banane wala khandani nanbai batate hain — unke baap-dada bhi nanbai; unhone Mughal darbar ke liye bhi roti banayi.
  • Hunar kaise aata hai ⭐⭐⭐: Miyan kehte hain — 'kaam karne se aata hai, baat karne se nahi'; bachcha pehle bartan dhona, bhatti jhonkna seekhta hai, phir haath ki kala aati hai.
  • Rotiyon ke naam ⭐⭐: bakarkhani, shirmal, tunki, gaozaban, kulcha, tandoori — roti mein kalakari bhi hoti hai; Miyan ko apni kaarigari par naaz hai.
  • Lekhika ke sawal ⭐⭐: jab lekhika poochhti hain ki baadshah ke liye kaunsi roti ya ab kya haal hai, Miyan tunak kar jawab dete hain — unhe kaam se pyaar, dikhawe se nahi.

🎯 Sequence: dukaan → Miyan ka daawa → hunar = kaam karke → rotiyon ke naam → tunakmizaji. Mukhya vichar: kaam karke seekhna.

3️⃣ Miyan Nasiruddin ka Charitra ⭐⭐⭐

  • Khandani hunarmand ⭐⭐⭐: baap Miyan Barkat Shahi aur dada Miyan Kalloo bhi nanbai — hunar pidhi-dar-pidhi.
  • Aatm-sammaan ⭐⭐: apne pesha ko kala maante hain — nanbai hona unke liye chhoti baat nahi.
  • Spasht-vaadi ⭐⭐: kisi ki khushamad nahi — jo sach lagta hai wahi kehte hain; isliye lekhika ke sawalon par tunak jaate hain.
  • Padhai par rai ⭐⭐: unka maanna — kitaabi padhai se zyada kaam ka anubhav zaroori; school nahi gaye par hunar mein ustaad.
  • Manavta ⭐: cheezon ko pyaar se banate hain — roti sirf pet bharne ki cheez nahi, kala hai.

💡 Charitra: khandani + swabhimani + spasht-vaadi + hunar ko kala maanne wala.

4️⃣ Sandesh aur Shabd-Shilp ⭐⭐

  • Hunar ka samman ⭐⭐⭐: path kehta hai ki haath ke hunar ki izzat honi chahiye — chahe woh nanbai ka ho ya kisi bhi karigar ka.
  • Kaam se seekhna ⭐⭐⭐: asli shiksha practical anubhav se aati hai — 'kaam karne se aata hai'.
  • Shabdchitra vidha ⭐⭐: sanskaran aur kahani se alag — isme kisi vyakti ka jeevant chitra shabdon se banaya jaata hai.
  • Sanskritik Delhi ⭐: Jama Masjid ki galiyon ka purana sanskritik jeevan aur khan-pan bhi jhalakta hai.

🎯 Message: hunar ka samman + practical anubhav ki shiksha. Exam mein dono zaroor likho.

१. लेखिका और पाठ

  • कृष्णा सोबती ⭐⭐: प्रसिद्ध लेखिका — ज्ञानपीठ से सम्मानित।
  • स्वरूप ⭐⭐: शब्दचित्र — 'हम हशमत' से।
  • केंद्र ⭐⭐⭐: मटिया महल के खानदानी नानबाई मियाँ नसीरुद्दीन।

२. सारांश

  • दुकान ⭐⭐: लेखिका मटिया महल की दुकान पर आटे का ढेर देखती हैं।
  • दावा ⭐⭐⭐: छप्पन तरह की रोटियाँ — खानदानी पेशा।
  • हुनर ⭐⭐⭐: काम करके सीखना, बातों से नहीं।
  • स्वभाव ⭐⭐: तुनकमिजाज पर काम के प्रति समर्पित।

३. चरित्र

  • खानदानी ⭐⭐⭐: पीढ़ी-दर-पीढ़ी नानबाई।
  • स्वाभिमानी ⭐⭐: पेशे को कला मानते हैं।
  • स्पष्टवादी ⭐⭐: खुशामद नहीं, सीधी बात।

४. संदेश

  • हुनर का सम्मान ⭐⭐⭐: हाथ के हुनर की इज्जत।
  • अनुभव ⭐⭐⭐: काम से सीखना असली शिक्षा।
  • विधा ⭐⭐: शब्दचित्र।

1. Writer and Text

  • Krishna Sobti ⭐⭐: noted Hindi novelist, Jnanpith awardee.
  • Form ⭐⭐: a word-sketch from Hum Hashmat.
  • Focus ⭐⭐⭐: Miyan Nasiruddin, the hereditary baker of Matia Mahal, Delhi.

2. Summary

  • The shop ⭐⭐: the writer sees a heap of dough in a dark shop in Matia Mahal.
  • The claim ⭐⭐⭐: fifty-six kinds of bread; a family trade.
  • Skill ⭐⭐⭐: learnt by doing, not by talking.
  • Nature ⭐⭐: short-tempered but devoted to his work.

3. Character

  • Hereditary ⭐⭐⭐: baking runs in the family.
  • Self-respecting ⭐⭐: treats his trade as an art.
  • Outspoken ⭐⭐: speaks plainly, no flattery.

4. Message and Style

  • Respect for skill ⭐⭐⭐: manual skill deserves dignity.
  • Experience ⭐⭐⭐: real learning comes from doing.
  • Genre ⭐⭐: a word-sketch.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Miyan Nasiruddin path ki lekhika kaun hain aur yeh kis pustak se hai?
Answer dekho

Krishna Sobti; yeh unki pustak 'Hum Hashmat' se liya gaya shabdchitra hai.

Q2 Miyan Nasiruddin kis pesha ke hain?
Answer dekho

Woh khandani nanbai (roti banane wale) hain — chhappan tarah ki rotiyan banane mein mahir.

Q3 Miyan Nasiruddin ki dukaan kahan hai?
Answer dekho

Delhi mein Jama Masjid ke paas Matia Mahal ki ek andheri, chhoti si gali mein.

Q4 Miyan ke anusaar hunar kaise seekha jaata hai?
Answer dekho

Kaam karke — pehle bartan dhona, bhatti ka kaam, phir haath ki kala; unke shabdon mein 'kaam karne se aata hai, baat karne se nahi'.

Q5 Miyan ko apne pesha par kaisa garv hai?
Answer dekho

Woh ise kala maante hain; unke baap-dada bhi nanbai the aur unhone bade logon ke liye roti banayi — isliye swabhimaan hai.

Q6 Shabdchitra kise kehte hain?
Answer dekho

Aisi vidha jisme kisi vyakti ya drishya ka jeevant chitra shabdon se khincha jaata hai; yeh path uska udaharan hai.

Q7 Miyan Nasiruddin kab tunak jaate hain?
Answer dekho

Jab lekhika ke sawal unhe dikhawe ya gair-zaroori lagte hain — woh spasht-vaadi hain aur khushamad nahi karte.

Q8 Path mein kin rotiyon ke naam aaye hain?
Answer dekho

Bakarkhani, shirmal, tunki, gaozaban, kulcha aadi — inse unki kaarigari ki range dikhti hai.

Q9 Miyan Nasiruddin ka charitra 3 bindu mein likhiye.
Answer dekho

1. khandani hunarmand; 2. swabhimani aur spasht-vaadi; 3. apne kaam ko kala maanne wale, practical anubhav mein vishwaas.

Q10 Is path ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Hunar ka samman aur kaam karke seekhna — asli shiksha anubhav se aati hai, sirf baaton se nahi.
