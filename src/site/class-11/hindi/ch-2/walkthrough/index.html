Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

DukaanMatiyamahal, andheri dukaan; Miyan chhappan kism ki rotiyon ke liye mashhoor.
RoopCharpai, beedi, pakka chehra, manje hue karigar ke tevar.
HunarWalid Barkat Shahi, dada Miyan Kallan; kaam karne se aata hai.
TaleemTaleem ki taleem: bartan dhona, bhatti, aanch dena.
BadshahNa aag se pake na paani se bane; kahavat; roti aanch se pakti hai.
AntBabban Miyan, majuri, rotiyon ki list; utar gaye wo zamane.
