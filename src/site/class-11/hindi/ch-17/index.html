Notes language:

1️⃣ Kavi aur Ghazal Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Dushyant Kumar ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: janm san 1933, Rajpur Nawada gaon (U.P.); mrityu san 1975; ghazal ki vidha ko Hindi mein pratishthit karne ka shrey akele Dushyant ko jaata hai; sahityik gunvatta se samjhauta na karte hue bhi lokpriyata ke naye pratimaan kayam kiye.
  • Sangrah ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: yeh ghazal unke ghazal sangrah Saye mein dhoop se li gayi hai. Anya rachnayen: Surya ka swagat, Awazon ke ghere, Jalte hue van ka vasant (kavya); Ek kanth vishpayi (geeti-natya).
  • Kendriya sutra ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: rajneeti aur samaj mein jo kuchh chal raha hai, use kharij karne aur vikalp ki talash ko manyata dene ka bhav is ghazal ka kendriya sutra ban gaya hai.
  • Roop ⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: ghazal mein sabhi sher apne-aap mein mukammil aur swatantra hote hain; unhen jodne wali do cheezen hain roop ke star par tuk ka nirvah aur antarvastu ke star par mizaj ka nirvah; ghazalon mein shirshak dene ka koi chalan nahin hai.

💡 Dushyant Kumar (1933-1975) + Saye mein dhoop + shirshak nahin + swatantra sher + tuk aur mizaj.

2️⃣ Sher-wise Saransh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Pehla sher ⭐⭐⭐: Text: kahan to tay tha chiraghan harek ghar ke liye, kahan chirag mayassar nahin shahar ke liye. Shabd-chhavi: mayassar = uplabdh. Book Q2: chirag shabd ek baar bahuvachan aur ek baar ekvachan mein aaya hai.
  • Doosra sher ⭐⭐⭐: Text: yahan darakhton ke saaye mein dhoop lagti hai, chalo yahan se chalen aur umra bhar ke liye. Shabd-chhavi: darakht = ped.
  • Teesra sher ⭐⭐⭐: Text: na ho kameez to paanvon se pet dhank lenge, ye log kitne munasib hain is safar ke liye. Book Q3: is sher mein Dushyant ka ishara kis tarah ke logon ki or hai.
  • Chautha sher ⭐⭐: Text: khuda nahin, na sahi, aadmi ka khwab sahi, koi haseen nazara to hai nazar ke liye. Book Aas-paas Q2: is sher ko Ghalib ke sher se jodkar batana.
  • Paanchva sher ⭐⭐⭐: Text: ve mutmain hain ki patthar pighal nahin sakta, main bekarar hun aavaz mein asar ke liye. Shabd-chhavi: mutmain = itminan se, aashvast; bekarar = bechain, aatur; asar = prabhav.
  • Chhatha sher ⭐⭐⭐: Text: tera nizam hai sil de zubaan shayar ki, ye ehtiyat zaroori hai is bahar ke liye. Shabd-chhavi: nizam = raaj, shasan; ehtiyat = savdhani; bahar = chhand. Book Q4: aashay spasht karna. Not in this NCERT text: censorship ya kisi khas qanoon ka zikr.
  • Aakhiri sher ⭐⭐: Text: jiyen to apne bagiche mein gulmohar ke tale, maren to gair ki galiyon mein gulmohar ke liye. Book Q1: gulmohar ek khas phooldar vriksh hai ya usmein sanketik arth nihit hai - supported reading sanketik arth ki taraf jaati hai; parichay khud arth nahin batata.

🎯 Yaad rakho: chiraghan vs mayassar -> dhoop -> kameez -> khwab -> patthar -> nizam -> gulmohar.

3️⃣ Kaavya Saundarya ⭐⭐⭐

  • Mizaj ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: poori ghazal ek khas manahsthiti mein likhi jaan padti hai; book Aas-paas Q1: is ghazal ka mizaj badlav ke paksh mein hai.
  • Tuk ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT parichay: pehle sher ki dono panktiyon ka tuk milta hai aur baad ke sabhi sheron ki doosri pankti mein us tuk ka nirvah hota hai. Shabd-chhavi se: bahar = chhand.
  • Shabd aur bhasha ⭐⭐: Text mein chirag, darakht, kameez, nazara, nizam jaise shabd hain; NCERT shabd-chhavi unke arth deti hai. Not in this NCERT text: bhasha ko kisi khas naam (jaise Hindustani) se kehna.
  • Prateek ⭐⭐: Supported reading: chirag, dhoop-chhaya, patthar, gulmohar sanketik roop mein padhe ja sakte hain; NCERT parichay sirf kendriya sutra batata hai, har prateek ka arth nahin.

💡 Tiers: explicit (text/parichay) / supported reading / teaching background.

4️⃣ Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Explicit (NCERT parichay) ⭐⭐⭐: Rajneeti aur samaj mein jo kuchh chal raha hai, use kharij karne aur vikalp ki talash ko manyata dene ka bhav kendriya sutra hai; yeh ghazal Hindi ghazal ka sundar namuna prastut karti hai.
  • Supported reading ⭐⭐: Text: main bekarar hun aavaz mein asar ke liye aur tera nizam hai sil de zubaan shayar ki - yeh supported reading hai ki kavi badlav ki talash aur aavaz ke asar ki chinta jata raha hai.
  • Teaching background ⭐: Aas-paas Q2: Ghalib ke sher se tulna; parichay mein Dushyant ke lokpriyata ke naye pratimaan ka zikr hai. Is paath mein koi aankda ya tareekh nahin hai (jhoothe vaade ya kisi satta-pakshiya aarop ka zikr is text mein nahin).

🎯 Message: parichay ke anusar kharij karne aur vikalp ki talash ka bhav | tiers: explicit / supported / background.

१. कवि और ग़ज़ल

  • दुष्यंत कुमार ⭐⭐: जन्म 1933, मृत्यु 1975; हिंदी में ग़ज़ल को प्रतिष्ठित करने का श्रेय (NCERT परिचय)।
  • संग्रह ⭐⭐⭐: ‘साये में धूप’ (NCERT परिचय)।
  • केंद्रीय सूत्र ⭐⭐⭐: ख़ारिज करने और विकल्प की तलाश को मान्यता देने का भाव (NCERT परिचय)।

२. शेर-वार सारांश

  • पहले शेर ⭐⭐⭐: कहाँ तो तय था चिराग़ाँ हरेक घर के लिए, कहाँ चिराग़ मयस्सर नहीं शहर के लिए।
  • तीसरा शेर ⭐⭐⭐: न हो कमीज़ तो पाँवों से पेट ढँक लेंगे।
  • छठा शेर ⭐⭐⭐: तेरा निज़ाम है सिल दे ज़ुबान शायर की (निज़ाम = राज, शासन)।

३. काव्य-सौंदर्य

  • मिज़ाज ⭐⭐⭐: बदलाव के पक्ष में (अभ्यास)।
  • तुक ⭐⭐⭐: पहले शेर की दोनों पंक्तियों में, फिर हर शेर की दूसरी पंक्ति में (NCERT परिचय)।
  • प्रतीक ⭐⭐: पठन: चिराग़, धूप-छाया, पत्थर प्रतीकात्मक हो सकते हैं।

४. संदेश

  • स्पष्ट (परिचय) ⭐⭐⭐: ख़ारिज करने और विकल्प की तलाश का भाव।
  • पठन ⭐⭐: आवाज़ में असर की बेचैनी (पाँचवाँ शेर)।
  • पृष्ठभूमि ⭐: ग़ालिब के शेर से तुलना (अभ्यास)।

1. Poet and Ghazal

  • Dushyant Kumar ⭐⭐: born 1933, died 1975; credited with establishing the ghazal in Hindi (NCERT introduction).
  • Collection ⭐⭐⭐: Saaye mein Dhoop (NCERT introduction).
  • Central thread ⭐⭐⭐: the urge to reject what is going on and to search for an alternative (NCERT introduction).

2. Couplet Summary

  • First couplet ⭐⭐⭐: lamps were promised for every home; now not even for the city.
  • Third couplet ⭐⭐⭐: if there is no shirt, they will cover the belly with their feet.
  • Sixth couplet ⭐⭐⭐: your rule says stitch up the poet's tongue (nizam = rule, governance).

3. Poetic Beauty

  • Temper ⭐⭐⭐: in favour of change (book exercise).
  • Rhyme ⭐⭐⭐: kept in both lines of the first couplet, then in the second line of each couplet (NCERT introduction).
  • Symbols ⭐⭐: reading: lamp, sun-and-shade, stone may be read symbolically.

4. Message

  • Explicit ⭐⭐⭐: rejecting and searching for an alternative (NCERT introduction).
  • Reading ⭐⭐: restlessness for impact in the voice (fifth couplet).
  • Background ⭐: comparison with Ghalib's couplet (book exercise).

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Ghazal ke kavi kaun hain?
Answer dekho

Dushyant Kumar - NCERT parichay: janm 1933, mrityu 1975; ghazal ki vidha ko Hindi mein pratishthit karne ka shrey unhen jaata hai.

Q2 Yeh ghazal kis sangrah se hai?
Answer dekho

NCERT parichay: Saye mein dhoop se.

Q3 'Kahan to tay tha chiraghan har ek ghar ke liye' ka kya arth hai?
Answer dekho

Text: kahan to tay tha chiraghan harek ghar ke liye, kahan chirag mayassar nahin shahar ke liye. Shabd-chhavi: mayassar = uplabdh.

Q4 'Darakhton ke saaye mein dhoop lagti hai' mein kya vyangya hai?
Answer dekho

Text: yahan darakhton ke saaye mein dhoop lagti hai. Supported reading: jahan saaye ki ummeed ho, wahan dhoop lagti hai. Shabd-chhavi: darakht = ped.

Q5 'Na ho kameez to paanvon se pet dhank lenge' se kya pata chalta hai?
Answer dekho

Text: na ho kameez to paanvon se pet dhank lenge, ye log kitne munasib hain is safar ke liye. Book Q3 puchhta hai ki yahan Dushyant ka ishara kis tarah ke logon ki or hai.

Q6 'Khuda nahin na sahi, aadmi ka khwab sahi' ka bhaav batayiye.
Answer dekho

Text: khuda nahin, na sahi, aadmi ka khwab sahi; koi haseen nazara to hai nazar ke liye. Book Aas-paas Q2 is sher ko Ghalib ke sher se jodne ko kehta hai.

Q7 'Woh mutma'in hain ki patthar pighal nahin sakta' mein 'patthar' kya hai?
Answer dekho

Text: ve mutmain hain ki patthar pighal nahin sakta (mutmain = itminan se, aashvast). Supported reading: patthar ka sanketik arth ho sakta hai; NCERT parichay arth nahin batata. Kavi aavaz mein asar ke liye bekarar hai.

Q8 'Tera nizaam hai sil de zubaan shayar ki' mein kaun sa bhaav hai?
Answer dekho

Text: tera nizam hai sil de zubaan shayar ki, ye ehtiyat zaroori hai is bahar ke liye. Shabd-chhavi: nizam = raaj, shasan; ehtiyat = savdhani; bahar = chhand. Book Q4 isi sher ka aashay spasht karne ko kehta hai.

Q9 Antim sher mein kavi ki kaun si ichha vyakt hui hai?
Answer dekho

Text: jiyen to apne bagiche mein gulmohar ke tale, maren to gair ki galiyon mein gulmohar ke liye. Book Q1: gulmohar vriksh hai ya sanketik arth - supported reading sanketik arth ki taraf; parichay khud arth nahin batata.

Q10 Ghazal ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Explicit (NCERT parichay): rajneeti aur samaj mein jo kuchh chal raha hai, use kharij karne aur vikalp ki talash ko manyata dene ka bhav kendriya sutra hai. Teaching background: parichay ek fixed naitik sandesh nahin deta.
