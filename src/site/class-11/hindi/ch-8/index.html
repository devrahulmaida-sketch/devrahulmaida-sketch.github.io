जामुन का पेड़

Jamun ka Ped — Krishan Chander

Sarkari daftar ki lal-feeta-shahi par tikhi vyangya katha

Board Weightage: HIGH Video available Long + Short notes Hindi • English • Hinglish
Watch on YouTube Notes

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Lekhak aur Kahani Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Krishan Chander (Kriśhn Chandar) ⭐⭐: Urdu-Hindi ke prasiddh kahanikar (1914-1977); vyangya aur samajik yatharth ki kahaniyan.
  • Kahani ka swaroop ⭐⭐⭐: haasya-vyangya katha — sarkari daftaron ki akarmanyata, samvedanheenta aur lal-feeta-shahi par chot.
  • Shuruaat ⭐⭐: raat ki tez aandhi mein ek jamun ka ped secretariat ke lawn mein gir jaata hai — uske neeche ek aadmi dab jaata hai.
  • Kendriya vichar ⭐⭐⭐: aam aadmi ki jaan se zyada file aur niyam ko mahatva dene wali vyavastha.

💡 Krishan Chander + vyangya + secretariat + jamun ka ped + daba hua aadmi + file ka chakkar.

2️⃣ Kahani-Saransh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Subah ka drishya ⭐⭐: maali ko ped ke neeche aadmi dabta dikhta hai; khabar maali → chaprasi → clerk → superintendent tak jaati hai.
  • Bheed ki chinta ⭐⭐: log ped ke gir jaane par dukh jata rahe hain, kyunki usse rasile jamun milte the — dabe aadmi ki chinta kisi ko nahi.
  • Superintendent ki rok ⭐⭐⭐: jab log ped hatane badhte hain to superintendent rok dete hain — pehle Under Secretary se poochhna hai.
  • File ka safar ⭐⭐⭐: file Under Secretary, Deputy Secretary, Joint Secretary aur alag-alag vibhagon tak jaati hai; har koi zimmedari doosre par daalta hai.
  • Bahas aur dinon ka gujarna ⭐⭐: kabhi kaha jaata hai yeh ped kisi aur vibhag ka hai, kabhi koi aur — is beech din beet jaate hain.
  • Ant ⭐⭐⭐: jab ant mein ped kaatne ki manzoori milti hai, tab tak woh aadmi mar chuka hota hai — vyavastha ki samvedanheenta apni chaaram seema par.

🎯 Sequence: ped giraa → aadmi dabaa → khabar upar jaati hai → superintendent ki rok → file ka chakkar → manzoori tak aadmi ki maut.

3️⃣ Vyangya aur Patra ⭐⭐⭐

  • Dabaa hua aadmi ⭐⭐⭐: aam aadmi ka prateek — bebas, par apni baat bolta hai; shayar/kavi ke roop mein bhi dikhaya gaya hai.
  • Sarkari karmchari ⭐⭐⭐: chaprasi se secretary tak sab jawabdehi se bachte hain — 'yeh hamara vibhag nahi' ka rawaiya.
  • Bheed ⭐⭐: tamasha dekhne wali samvedanheen janta.
  • Vyangya ki dhaar ⭐⭐⭐: hansi ke beech maut ki gambhirta — isliye kahani haasya bhi, karun bhi hai.

💡 Patra: dabaa aadmi (aam aadmi) + karmchari (jawabdehi se bachne wale) + bheed. Vyangya = hasi ke peeche maut.

4️⃣ Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Insaan pehle ⭐⭐⭐: niyam aur file se zyada insaan ki jaan mahatvapurn hai.
  • Lal-feeta-shahi par prahar ⭐⭐⭐: sarkari tantra ka dheemapan aur behisi aam aadmi par bhaari padti hai.
  • Jawabdehi ⭐⭐: har karmchari ko apni zimmedari leni chahiye.
  • Saamajik chetna ⭐⭐: kahani pathak ko sochne par majboor karti hai.

🎯 Message: insaan > file | lal-feeta-shahi ki aalochna | jawabdehi.

१. लेखक और कहानी

  • कृश्न चंदर ⭐⭐: प्रसिद्ध कहानीकार।
  • स्वरूप ⭐⭐⭐: हास्य-व्यंग्य कथा।
  • विषय ⭐⭐⭐: सरकारी लालफ़ीताशाही।

२. सारांश

  • गिरना ⭐⭐: आँधी में पेड़ गिरा, आदमी दबा।
  • फ़ाइल ⭐⭐⭐: एक विभाग से दूसरे तक।
  • अंत ⭐⭐⭐: मंज़ूरी तक आदमी मर चुका।

३. व्यंग्य और पात्र

  • दबा आदमी ⭐⭐⭐: आम आदमी का प्रतीक।
  • कर्मचारी ⭐⭐⭐: जवाबदेही से बचते।
  • व्यंग्य ⭐⭐⭐: हँसी के पीछे मौत।

४. संदेश

  • इंसान पहले ⭐⭐⭐: फ़ाइल से ज़्यादा जान।
  • आलोचना ⭐⭐⭐: लालफ़ीताशाही की।
  • जवाबदेही ⭐⭐: ज़िम्मेदारी लेना।

1. Writer and Story

  • Krishan Chander ⭐⭐: noted story writer.
  • Form ⭐⭐⭐: humorous satire.
  • Theme ⭐⭐⭐: bureaucratic red tape.

2. Summary

  • The fall ⭐⭐: a storm topples the tree; a man is pinned.
  • The file ⭐⭐⭐: passes from office to office.
  • Ending ⭐⭐⭐: by the time sanction comes, he is dead.

3. Satire and Characters

  • Pinned man ⭐⭐⭐: symbol of the common man.
  • Officials ⭐⭐⭐: dodge responsibility.
  • Satire ⭐⭐⭐: death behind the laughter.

4. Message

  • People first ⭐⭐⭐: a life outweighs a file.
  • Critique ⭐⭐⭐: of red tape.
  • Accountability ⭐⭐: own your responsibility.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Jamun ka Ped ke lekhak kaun hain?
Answer dekho

Krishan Chander (Kriśhn Chandar) — Urdu-Hindi ke prasiddh kahanikar.

Q2 Yeh kahani kis prakar ki hai?
Answer dekho

Haasya-vyangya katha — sarkari daftaron ki lal-feeta-shahi par chot karti hai.

Q3 Jamun ka ped kahan aur kaise gira?
Answer dekho

Raat ki tez aandhi mein secretariat ke lawn mein gira, aur uske neeche ek aadmi dab gaya.

Q4 Ped ke neeche dabe aadmi ko kisne dekha?
Answer dekho

Subah maali ne; khabar chaprasi, clerk aur superintendent tak pahunchi.

Q5 Log ped ke girne par dukhi kyun the?
Answer dekho

Kyunki us ped ke jamun rasile the; dabe aadmi ki chinta kisi ko nahi thi.

Q6 Superintendent ne ped hatane se kyun roka?
Answer dekho

Unhone kaha pehle Under Secretary se poochhna hoga — yeh niyam aur file ko zyada maan dene wali soch hai.

Q7 File ke chakkar se lekhak kya dikhana chahte hain?
Answer dekho

Sarkari tantra mein har koi zimmedari doosre par daalta hai — isse deri aur samvedanheenta badhti hai.

Q8 Kahani ka ant kya hai?
Answer dekho

Jab ped kaatne ki manzoori aati hai, tab tak woh aadmi mar chuka hota hai.

Q9 Kahani mein vyangya kahan hai?
Answer dekho

Hansi ke bahane aam aadmi ki maut aur sarkari behisi dikhayi gayi hai — isliye kahani haasya aur karun dono hai.

Q10 Kahani ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Insaan ki jaan file aur niyam se badi hai; lal-feeta-shahi aam aadmi par bhaari padti hai aur jawabdehi zaroori hai.
