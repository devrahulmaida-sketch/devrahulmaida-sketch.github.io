Apu ke Saath Dhai Saal — Satyajit Ray

Pather Panchali ki shooting ka sangharsh — ek filmkar ki pehli film

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Lekhak aur Path Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Satyajit Ray ⭐⭐: vishwa-prasiddh Bangla filmkar (1921-1992); baad mein Bharat Ratna aur Oscar (Lifetime Achievement) se sammanit.
  • Path ka swaroop ⭐⭐: yeh ek sansmaran hai — Ray ki pehli film 'Pather Panchali' banane ke anubhav; Bangla se Vilas Gite ne anuvaad kiya.
  • Film ka parichay ⭐⭐⭐: Bibhutibhushan Bandopadhyay ke upanyas par; film 1955 mein pradarshit hui — ise Apu Trilogy ka pehla bhaag kaha jaata hai.
  • Shooting ki avadhi ⭐⭐⭐: film banne mein lagbhag dhai saal lage — roz shooting nahi hoti thi kyunki Ray vigyapan company mein naukri karte the aur paise ka intezaam bhi karna padta tha.

💡 Satyajit Ray + Pather Panchali (1955) + Bibhutibhushan ka upanyas + dhai saal + vigyapan company ki naukri.

2️⃣ Shooting ke Mukhya Prasang ⭐⭐⭐

  • Apu ki khoj ⭐⭐: Apu ke role ke liye chhe saal ke ladke ki talash ke liye akhbaar mein vigyapan diya; ant mein Ray ke padosi ka ladka Subir Banerjee chuna gaya.
  • Kaash ke phool ⭐⭐⭐: Durga-Apu ke rail dekhne ka drishya kaash ke phoolon ke khet mein shoot hona tha; shooting ek saal baad (agle mausam) hui kyunki tab tak kaash murjha gaye the.
  • Kutta Bhulo ⭐⭐: kutte Bhulo ke saath drishya ki shooting mein ek saal baad bhi wahi kutta nahi mila — isliye alag kutte ka use karna pada; yeh continuity ki samasya dikhata hai.
  • Mithai-wala ⭐⭐: mithai wale ka drishya aur Indir Thakrun (budhi bua) ka role Chunibala Devi ne nibhaya.
  • Paise ki samasya ⭐⭐⭐: Ray ko apni beemay policy girvi rakhni padi; shooting baar-baar rukti thi; ant mein West Bengal sarkar ne madad ki.

🎯 Sequence: Apu ki khoj → kaash phool (ek saal intezaar) → Bhulo kutta → paise ki tangi → sarkar ki madad.

3️⃣ Film se Jude Log ⭐⭐

  • Bansi Chandragupta ⭐⭐: art director — kam paison mein bhi asli jaisa set banaya.
  • Ravi Shankar ⭐⭐: film ka sangeet unhi ne diya; Subrata Mitra cinematographer the (jinhone pehle kabhi film shoot nahi ki thi).
  • Kalakar ⭐: Harihar = Kanu Bandopadhyay; Sarbojaya = Karuna Bandopadhyay; Durga = Uma Dasgupta; Apu = Subir Banerjee.

💡 Art director = Bansi Chandragupta | Sangeet = Ravi Shankar | Camera = Subrata Mitra | Durga = Uma Dasgupta. Exam mein naam poochhe jaate hain.

4️⃣ Sandesh aur Mahatva ⭐⭐⭐

  • Sadhan kam, drishti bada ⭐⭐⭐: path dikhata hai ki sadhan na hone par bhi kala-drishti ko sakaar karne ka sangharsh hi asli rachna hai.
  • Film ka vyakaran ⭐⭐: path se film banane ki baareekiyan pata chalti hain — set, continuity, lighting, acting.
  • Glamour ke peeche ka sach ⭐⭐: filmi duniya ki chakachaundh ke peeche mehnat aur sangharsh ka sach saamne aata hai.
  • Antarrashtriya pehchan ⭐⭐: Pather Panchali ne Ray ko international pehchan dilayi (Cannes mein 'Best Human Document' puraskar).

🎯 Message: lagan + sangharsh + kala-drishti. Conclusion mein Cannes ka zikr karo.

१. लेखक और पाठ

  • सत्यजित राय ⭐⭐: विश्वप्रसिद्ध बांग्ला फ़िल्मकार।
  • स्वरूप ⭐⭐: संस्मरण — पहली फ़िल्म 'पथेर पांचाली' (1955)।
  • अवधि ⭐⭐⭐: ढाई साल, पैसे की तंगी।

२. मुख्य प्रसंग

  • अपू ⭐⭐: सुबीर बनर्जी को चुना गया।
  • काश के फूल ⭐⭐⭐: रेल का दृश्य एक साल बाद शूट।
  • पैसा ⭐⭐⭐: पैसे की तंगी, अंत में सरकारी मदद।

३. फ़िल्म से जुड़े लोग

  • कला निर्देशन ⭐⭐: बंसी चंद्रगुप्त।
  • संगीत ⭐⭐: पंडित रविशंकर।
  • कलाकार ⭐: दुर्गा — उमा दासगुप्ता; अपू — सुबीर बनर्जी।

४. संदेश

  • संघर्ष ⭐⭐⭐: साधनहीनता में कलादृष्टि।
  • फ़िल्म-व्याकरण ⭐⭐: निर्माण की बारीकियाँ।
  • पहचान ⭐⭐: अंतरराष्ट्रीय ख्याति।

1. Writer and Text

  • Satyajit Ray ⭐⭐: world-famous Bengali filmmaker.
  • Form ⭐⭐: a memoir of his first film, Pather Panchali (1955).
  • Duration ⭐⭐⭐: about two and a half years, always short of money.

2. Key Episodes

  • Apu ⭐⭐: Subir Banerjee was chosen.
  • Kaash flowers ⭐⭐⭐: the train scene was shot a year later.
  • Money ⭐⭐⭐: constant shortage, rescued by government help.

3. People Behind the Film

  • Art director ⭐⭐: Bansi Chandragupta.
  • Music ⭐⭐: Ravi Shankar.
  • Cast ⭐: Durga — Uma Dasgupta; Apu — Subir Banerjee.

4. Message and Importance

  • Struggle ⭐⭐⭐: vision without resources.
  • Craft ⭐⭐: the fine details of filmmaking.
  • Recognition ⭐⭐: international fame for Ray.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Apu ke Saath Dhai Saal ke lekhak kaun hain?
Answer dekho

Satyajit Ray — Bangla ke mahaan filmkar; yeh unki pehli film Pather Panchali ka sansmaran hai.

Q2 Pather Panchali kiske upanyas par bani film hai?
Answer dekho

Bibhutibhushan Bandopadhyay ke upanyas par; film 1955 mein pradarshit hui.

Q3 Film ko banne mein dhai saal kyun lage?
Answer dekho

Ray vigyapan company mein naukri karte the; shooting paise ke intezaam aur office se fursat milne par hi hoti thi.

Q4 Apu ka role kisne kiya?
Answer dekho

Subir Banerjee ne — Ray ke padosi ka ladka, jise chhe saal ke Apu ke liye chuna gaya.

Q5 Durga aur Apu ka kaash-phool wala drishya kab shoot hua?
Answer dekho

Ek saal baad — pehle shooting hui par kaash ke phool murjha gaye the; agle mausam mein naye phoolon mein drishya poora hua.

Q6 Kutte Bhulo wale drishya mein kya samasya aayi?
Answer dekho

Wahi kutta nahi mila jo pehle shoot mein tha — isliye dusre kutte ka upyog karna pada; isse continuity ki samasya samajh aati hai.

Q7 Paise ki kami ka samadhan kaise hua?
Answer dekho

Ray ne apni beemay policy girvi rakhi; ant mein West Bengal sarkar ki madad se film poori hui.

Q8 Film ke kaun se log Ray ke sahyogi the?
Answer dekho

Bansi Chandragupta (art director), Ravi Shankar (sangeet), Subrata Mitra (cinematography) aur kalakar.

Q9 Path se film-nirman ke baare mein kya seekhne ko milta hai?
Answer dekho

Film banana set, continuity, roshni aur acting ki baareek kala hai; isme sadhanheenta mein bhi kala-drishti sakaar hoti hai.

Q10 Is sansmaran ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Lagan aur sangharsh se sadhanhin parishthitiyon mein bhi kala ko sakaar kiya ja sakta hai.
