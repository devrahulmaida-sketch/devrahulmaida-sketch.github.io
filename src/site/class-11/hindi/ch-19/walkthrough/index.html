Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

KaviPash (mool naam Avtar Singh Sandhu); janm 1950, Talwandi Salem, Jalandhar; mrityu 1988.
RachnayenLauh Katha, Udden Baaja Magar, Sadai Samiya Bich, Ladenge Saathi (Punjabi); Beech ka rasta nahin hota (Hindi anuvad).
Nahin khatarnakMehnat ki loot, police ki maar, gaddari-lobh ki mutthi sabse khatarnak nahin.
Bura to haiBaithe-bithaye pakde jana, sahmi chup, kapat ke shor mein dab jana: bura to hai, par sabse khatarnak nahin.
Sabse khatarnakMurda shanti, tadap ka na hona, sapnon ka mar jana; ghadi, aankh, chaand, geet, raat, disha.
Shabd-chhaviGaddari = droh ya dhokha; tadap = bechaini; veeran = ujda hua; marsiya = karun ras ki kavita; rooh = aatma.
