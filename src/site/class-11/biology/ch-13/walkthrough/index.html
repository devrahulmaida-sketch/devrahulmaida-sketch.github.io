Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

GrowthMeristematic, elongation, maturation. Arithmetic (linear) vs geometric (exponential). Sigmoid: lag, log, stationary.
Cell fateDifferentiation, dedifferentiation (cork cambium), redifferentiation. Development = growth + differentiation.
Auxin + GAAuxin: apical dominance, rooting, parthenocarpy, 2,4-D weedicide. GA: bolting, germination, grapes, sugarcane.
Cytokinin, C2H4, ABACytokinin: division, anti-ageing. Ethylene: gas, ripening. ABA: stomata close, dormancy.
PhotoperiodLDP, SDP (long-night), day-neutral by critical duration. Leaves perceive the signal.
VernalisationCold promotes flowering: winter wheat, rye, biennials like cabbage, carrot, sugarbeet.
