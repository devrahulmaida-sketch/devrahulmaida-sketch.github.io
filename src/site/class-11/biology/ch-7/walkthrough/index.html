Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

EpitheliumSquamous, cuboidal, columnar, ciliated. Exocrine (ducts) vs endocrine (ductless). Tight, adhering, gap junctions.
ConnectiveAreolar, adipose, tendon (muscle to bone), ligament (bone to bone), cartilage, bone, blood.
MuscleSkeletal: striated, voluntary. Smooth: non-striated. Cardiac: striated, involuntary, intercalated discs.
NeuralNeuron: dendrites receive, axon transmits. Neuroglia support and protect.
Cockroach bodyHead, thorax, abdomen. Tegmina, anal cerci in both sexes, anal styli in males only.
Cockroach systemsOpen circulation, tracheae with 10 spiracle pairs, Malpighian tubules, ootheca with 14-16 eggs.
