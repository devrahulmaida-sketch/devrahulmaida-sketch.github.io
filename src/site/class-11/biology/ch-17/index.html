Notes language:

1️⃣ Movement Types aur Muscle Basics ⭐

  • Movement vs Locomotion ⭐: movement = body parts ki position change (cells se organs tak — har jagah!); locomotion = POORA body ek jagah se doosri jagah — voluntary ho sakta hai, movement involuntary bhi.
  • Movement ke types ⭐⭐: Amoeboid — pseudopodia se (Amoeba, WBCs, macrophages — cytoskeleton/microfilaments!); Ciliary — cilia se (Paramecium, trachea ki lining — mucus ko upar push!); Flagellar — flagella (sperm!); Muscular — muscles se (humans me limbs, jaws, eyelids!).
  • Muscles ke 3 types ⭐⭐: Skeletal (striated/voluntary) — bones se attached, banding pattern, voluntary control, fast fatigue; Smooth (unstriated/involuntary) — visceral organs (stomach, intestine, blood vessels), spindle-shaped, single nucleus, NO bands; Cardiac ⭐ — sirf heart, striated BUT involuntary, branched, intercalated discs (communication junctions!), never fatigue!
  • Skeletal muscle structure ⭐: muscle → fascicles (muscle fibre bundles!) → muscle fibres (cells!) → myofibrils → actin + myosin filaments. Each fibre multinucleated (syncytium!), sarcolemma = membrane, sarcoplasm = cytoplasm.

💡 Amoeboid = pseudopodia (WBC); ciliary = cilia (trachea); muscular = skeletal (voluntary), smooth (organs), cardiac (heart — special!).

2️⃣ Sarcomere aur Contractile Proteins ⭐⭐

  • Sarcomere ⭐⭐: skeletal muscle ki functional unit — do Z-lines ke beech ka segment! Light band (I band — sirf actin!) + dark band (A band — myosin + overlapping actin!). A band ke centre me H zone (sirf myosin!) + M line (centre!).
  • Actin (thin filament) ⭐⭐: 2 F-actin filaments helically wound — uske saath tropomyosin (myosin-binding sites ko COVER karta hai rest me!) + troponin (3 subunits — Ca2+ binding!).
  • Myosin (thick filament) ⭐⭐: har myosin = head (cross-arm — ATPase activity + actin-binding site!) + tail. Heads actin ki taraf project karte hain.
  • Contraction ke dauran ⭐: I band aur H zone SHORTEN hote hain, A band SAME rehti hai (myosin ki length nahi badalti!) — Z-lines paas aati hain. Yaad rakhna — A band unchanged!
  • Red vs white fibres ⭐: Red — zyada myoglobin + mitochondria (aerobic, slow but sustained — posture muscles!); White — kam myoglobin (anaerobic, fast but fatigue — eyelid/fine movements!).

🎯 Sarcomere = Z to Z. Contraction me I band + H zone chhote, A band SAME. Actin pe troponin+tropomyosin; myosin ka head = motor!

3️⃣ Muscle Contraction — Sliding Filament Theory ⭐⭐

  • Signal ⭐⭐: motor neuron se acetylcholine (ACh) release (neuromuscular junction pe!) → muscle fibre membrane pe action potential → sarcoplasmic reticulum se Ca2+ RELEASE!
  • Ca2+ ka role ⭐⭐: Ca2+ TROPONIN se bind hota hai → tropomyosin HAT jaata hai → actin ke myosin-binding sites EXPOSE!
  • Cross-bridge cycle ⭐⭐: myosin head actin se bind (cross-bridge!) → ATP hydrolysis se energy → head pivot/power stroke → actin ko centre (M line) ki taraf PULL → naya ATP → head detach → repeat! Isse Z-lines paas aati hain = SHORTENING.
  • Relaxation ⭐: Ca2+ wapas sarcoplasmic reticulum me pump → tropomyosin wapas sites COVER → myosin detach → muscle relax.
  • Rigor mortis ⭐: death ke baad ATP nahi banta → myosin heads detach nahi ho paate → muscles STIFF.

🎯 Sequence: ACh → Ca2+ release → troponin bind → tropomyosin shift → myosin-actin bridge → ATP pull → relax (Ca2+ back). ATP = energy + detachment!

4️⃣ Skeletal System — Bones aur Joints ⭐⭐

  • Total bones ⭐⭐: adult me 206 bones! Axial skeleton (80 — skull 22 + hyoid 1 + vertebral column 26 + ribs 24 + sternum 1 + ear ossicles 6!) + Appendicular skeleton (126 — limbs + girdles!).
  • Skull ⭐: 22 bones (8 cranial + 14 facial!) — joints = sutures (immovable/fibrous!). Ear ossicles: malleus, incus, stapes (smallest bones!).
  • Vertebral column ⭐⭐: 26 vertebrae — 7 cervical + 12 thoracic + 5 lumbar + 1 sacrum (5 fused!) + 1 coccyx (4 fused!). First = atlas (skull ke saath!), second = axis. Formula: C7 T12 L5 S5 Co4.
  • Ribs ⭐: 12 pairs — 7 true (sternum se directly!), 3 false (cartilage via!), 2 floating (no sternum attachment — 11th, 12th!). Sternum = breast bone (flat!).
  • Joints ⭐⭐: Fibrous — immovable (skull sutures!); Cartilaginous — limited movement (vertebrae ke beech!); Synovial ⭐ — freely movable, synovial fluid cavity — ball & socket (shoulder/hip — max movement!), hinge (elbow/knee — ek direction!), pivot (atlas-axis — rotation!), gliding (wrist bones!), saddle (thumb!).

🎯 206 bones; C7 T12 L5 S5 Co4; 12 rib pairs (7 true, 3 false, 2 floating). Ball & socket = shoulder/hip; hinge = knee/elbow!

5️⃣ Disorders of Muscles aur Bones ⭐⭐

  • Myasthenia gravis ⭐⭐: AUTOIMMUNE disorder — neuromuscular junction pe ACh receptors destroy → muscle weakness/fatigue (face, swallowing!). Paralysis se alag — nerve-muscle communication toot jaati.
  • Muscular dystrophy ⭐⭐: GENETIC disorder — muscle fibres progressively degenerate (dystrophin protein ki kami — X-linked!).
  • Tetany ⭐: muscles me rapid spasms/cramps — low Ca2+ in body fluid se (hyperexcitability!).
  • Arthritis ⭐⭐: joints ki inflammation — osteoarthritis (cartilage wear — age!); rheumatoid arthritis ⭐ (autoimmune — synovial membrane attack!); gouty arthritis (uric acid crystals joint me jama!).
  • Osteoporosis ⭐⭐: bone mass/DENSITY kam — bones brittle/fracture-prone (ageing, oestrogen kam — post-menopausal women me common!, low calcium/vitamin D!).

🎯 Myasthenia = autoimmune (ACh receptor); dystrophy = genetic (dystrophin); tetany = low Ca2+; osteoporosis = low bone density (oestrogen!).

1️⃣ गति-प्रकार और पेशी-मूल बातें ⭐

  • गति बनाम चलन ⭐: गति = भागों की स्थिति-परिवर्तन; चलन = पूरा शरीर एक स्थान से दूसरे स्थान।
  • गति-प्रकार ⭐⭐: अमीबीय — कटकपाद (अमीबा, WBC!); पक्ष्माभी — पक्ष्माभ (श्वासनली-अस्तर!); कशाभी — कशाभिका (शुक्राणु!); पेशीय — पेशियों से।
  • पेशी-प्रकार ⭐⭐: कंकालीय (रेखित/ऐच्छिक) — अस्थियों से जुड़ी, पट्टी-विन्यास, शीघ्र-थकान; चिकनी (अरेखित/अनैच्छिक) — आंतरिक अंग (आमाशय, आंत, वाहिकाएँ), कोई पट्टी नहीं; हृदय ⭐ — केवल हृदय, रेखित परंतु अनैच्छिक, शाखित, अंतरापट्ट-चक्रिकाएँ (intercalated discs!), कभी थकान नहीं!
  • कंकालीय-पेशी संरचना ⭐: पेशी → पेशी-पुंज → पेशी-तंतु → पेशी-तंतुक → एक्टिन + मायोसिन। प्रत्येक तंतु बहुकेंद्रकी!

💡 अमीबीय = कटकपाद (WBC); कंकालीय = ऐच्छिक; चिकनी = अंग; हृदय = विशेष (रेखित+अनैच्छिक)!

2️⃣ सार्कोमियर और संकुचनशील प्रोटीन ⭐⭐

  • सार्कोमियर ⭐⭐: कंकालीय-पेशी की कार्यात्मक इकाई — दो Z-रेखाओं के बीच! हल्की I-पट्टी (केवल एक्टिन!) + गहरी A-पट्टी (मायोसिन + एक्टिन!)। A-पट्टी के केंद्र में H-क्षेत्र (केवल मायोसिन!) + M-रेखा।
  • एक्टिन (पतला तंतुक) ⭐⭐: 2 F-एक्टिन कुंडलित — ट्रोपोमायोसिन (मायोसिन-बंधन-स्थल ढकता!) + ट्रोपोनिन (Ca2+ बंधन!)।
  • मायोसिन (मोटा तंतुक) ⭐⭐: प्रत्येक में शीर्ष (ATPase + एक्टिन-बंधन!) + पूंछ।
  • संकुचन में ⭐: I-पट्टी और H-क्षेत्र छोटे होते हैं, A-पट्टी समान रहती — Z-रेखाएँ पास आतीं।
  • लाल/श्वेत तंतु ⭐: लाल — अधिक मायोग्लोबिन (वायवीय, दीर्घकालिक!); श्वेत — कम (अवायवीय, तीव्र पर थकान!)।

🎯 सार्कोमियर = Z से Z। I-पट्टी + H-क्षेत्र छोटे, A-पट्टी समान!

3️⃣ पेशी-संकुचन — सर्पी-तंतुक सिद्धांत ⭐⭐

  • संकेत ⭐⭐: गति-तंत्रिका से एसीटिलकोलीन (ACh) (तंत्रिका-पेशी-संधि!) → क्रिया-विभव → द्रव्यजालिका (SR) से Ca2+ मुक्ति!
  • Ca2+ की भूमिका ⭐⭐: Ca2+ ट्रोपोनिन से बंधता → ट्रोपोमायोसिन हटता → एक्टिन के मायोसिन-स्थल खुले!
  • क्रॉस-ब्रिज चक्र ⭐⭐: मायोसिन-शीर्ष एक्टिन से बंधता → ATP-जल-अपघटन से ऊर्जा → शीर्ष झुकता → एक्टिन केंद्र की ओर खिंचता → नया ATP → शीर्ष मुक्त → दोहराव! Z-रेखाएँ पास = संकुचन।
  • शिथिलन ⭐: Ca2+ वापस SR में → ट्रोपोमायोसिन वापस ढके → पेशी शिथिल।
  • राइगर-मोर्टिस ⭐: मृत्यु के बाद ATP नहीं → शीर्ष मुक्त नहीं → पेशी-कठोरता।

🎯 क्रम: ACh → Ca2+ → ट्रोपोनिन → ट्रोपोमायोसिन हटे → सेतु → ATP-खिंचाव → शिथिलन!

4️⃣ कंकाल-तंत्र — अस्थियाँ और संधियाँ ⭐⭐

  • कुल अस्थियाँ ⭐⭐: वयस्क में 206! अक्षीय कंकाल (80 — खोपड़ी 22 + कशेरुक-स्तंभ 26 + पसलियाँ 24 + उरोस्थि 1!) + अनुबंधीय (126 — अंग + ककाठी/स्कंध!)।
  • खोपड़ी ⭐: 22 अस्थियाँ (8 कपालीय + 14 आनन!) — संधियाँ = सीवन (अचल!)। कर्ण-अस्थिकाएँ: मैलियस, इन्कस, स्टेपीज़ (सबसे छोटी!)।
  • कशेरुक-स्तंभ ⭐⭐: 26 कशेरुक — 7 ग्रीवा + 12 वक्षीय + 5 कटि + 1 त्रिक (5 संलयित!) + 1 अनुत्रिक (4 संलयित!)। सूत्र: C7 T12 L5 S5 Co4।
  • पसलियाँ ⭐: 12 जोड़ी — 7 सच्ची (उरोस्थि से सीधे!), 3 झूठी, 2 तैरती (11वीं, 12वीं!)।
  • संधियाँ ⭐⭐: तंतुमयी — अचल (खोपड़ी!); उपास्थिल — सीमित (कशेरुक!); सिनोवियल ⭐ — मुक्त — कंदुक-खल्लिका (स्कंध/नितंब!), हिंज/कब्ज़ा (कोहनी/घुटना!), धुरी (एटलस-एक्सिस!), पल्ली (अंगूठा!)।

🎯 206 अस्थियाँ; C7 T12 L5 S5 Co4; 12 पसली-जोड़े (7 सच्चे, 3 झूठे, 2 तैरते)। कंदुक = स्कंध/नितंब!

5️⃣ पेशी-अस्थि विकार ⭐⭐

  • मायस्थेनिया-ग्रेविस ⭐⭐: स्वप्रतिरक्षी — ACh-ग्राही नष्ट → पेशी-दुर्बलता (मुख, निगलना!)।
  • पेशीय-अपविकास (muscular dystrophy) ⭐⭐: आनुवंशिक — पेशी-तंतु क्रमिक-क्षय (डिस्ट्रोफ़िन की कमी — X-संलग्न!)।
  • दांतपेशी-आक्षेप (tetany) ⭐: तीव्र-ऐंठन — निम्न Ca2+ से!
  • संधिवात (arthritis) ⭐⭐: संधि-शोथ — अस्थि-संधिवात (उपास्थि-क्षय — आयु!); आमवात (rheumatoid) ⭐ (स्वप्रतिरक्षी!); वातरक्त (gout) (यूरिक-अम्ल क्रिस्टल!)।
  • अस्थि-सुषिरता (osteoporosis) ⭐⭐: अस्थि-घनत्व कम — भंगुर (आयु, निम्न ईस्ट्रोजन — रजोनिवृत्ति-पश्चात महिलाओं में!)।

🎯 मायस्थेनिया = स्वप्रतिरक्षी; अपविकास = आनुवंशिक; सुषिरता = अस्थि-घनत्व कम (ईस्ट्रोजन!).

1️⃣ Movement Types and Muscle Basics ⭐

  • Movement vs locomotion ⭐: movement = a change in position of body parts; locomotion = the WHOLE body moving place to place.
  • Movement types ⭐⭐: Amoeboid — pseudopodia (Amoeba, WBCs!); Ciliary — cilia (tracheal lining!); Flagellar — flagella (sperm!); Muscular — muscles (limbs, jaws!).
  • Muscle types ⭐⭐: Skeletal (striated/voluntary) — attached to bones, banded, tire fast; Smooth (unstriated/involuntary) — internal organs, no bands; Cardiac ⭐ — heart only, striated BUT involuntary, branched, intercalated discs, never tire!
  • Skeletal muscle structure ⭐: muscle → fascicles → fibres (multinucleated!) → myofibrils → actin + myosin filaments.

💡 Amoeboid = pseudopodia (WBC); skeletal = voluntary; smooth = organs; cardiac = special (striated + involuntary)!

2️⃣ Sarcomere and Contractile Proteins ⭐⭐

  • Sarcomere ⭐⭐: the functional unit of skeletal muscle — the segment between two Z-lines! Light I band (actin only) + dark A band (myosin + actin). At the A band's centre: the H zone (myosin only!) + M line.
  • Actin (thin filament) ⭐⭐: two helical F-actins — with tropomyosin (covers myosin-binding sites at rest!) + troponin (binds Ca2+!).
  • Myosin (thick filament) ⭐⭐: each has a head (ATPase + actin-binding!) + tail.
  • During contraction ⭐: the I band and H zone SHORTEN; the A band stays the SAME — Z-lines move closer.
  • Red/white fibres ⭐: red — more myoglobin (aerobic, sustained!); white — less (anaerobic, fast but tiring!).

🎯 Sarcomere = Z to Z. I band + H zone shrink; the A band stays the same!

3️⃣ Muscle Contraction — Sliding Filament Theory ⭐⭐

  • Signal ⭐⭐: a motor neuron releases acetylcholine (ACh) (at the neuromuscular junction!) → action potential → Ca2+ released from the sarcoplasmic reticulum!
  • Ca2+ role ⭐⭐: Ca2+ binds TROPONIN → tropomyosin shifts → the actin's myosin-binding sites are exposed!
  • Cross-bridge cycle ⭐⭐: the myosin head binds actin → ATP hydrolysis gives energy → the head pivots → pulls actin towards the centre → new ATP → head detaches → repeat! Z-lines come closer = shortening.
  • Relaxation ⭐: Ca2+ pumped back into the SR → tropomyosin covers the sites again → the muscle relaxes.
  • Rigor mortis ⭐: after death no ATP → heads can't detach → stiffness.

🎯 Sequence: ACh → Ca2+ → troponin → tropomyosin shifts → bridge → ATP pull → relax!

4️⃣ Skeletal System — Bones and Joints ⭐⭐

  • Total bones ⭐⭐: 206 in an adult! Axial skeleton (80 — skull 22 + vertebral column 26 + ribs 24 + sternum 1!) + appendicular (126 — limbs + girdles!).
  • Skull ⭐: 22 bones (8 cranial + 14 facial!) — joints are sutures (immovable!). Ear ossicles: malleus, incus, stapes (smallest bones!).
  • Vertebral column ⭐⭐: 26 vertebrae — 7 cervical + 12 thoracic + 5 lumbar + 1 sacrum (5 fused!) + 1 coccyx (4 fused!). Formula: C7 T12 L5 S5 Co4.
  • Ribs ⭐: 12 pairs — 7 true (direct to sternum!), 3 false, 2 floating (11th, 12th!).
  • Joints ⭐⭐: Fibrous — immovable (skull!); cartilaginous — limited (vertebrae!); Synovial ⭐ — freely movable — ball & socket (shoulder/hip!), hinge (elbow/knee!), pivot (atlas-axis!), saddle (thumb!).

🎯 206 bones; C7 T12 L5 S5 Co4; 12 rib pairs (7 true, 3 false, 2 floating). Ball & socket = shoulder/hip!

5️⃣ Muscle and Bone Disorders ⭐⭐

  • Myasthenia gravis ⭐⭐: an AUTOIMMUNE disorder — ACh receptors destroyed at the neuromuscular junction → muscle weakness.
  • Muscular dystrophy ⭐⭐: a GENETIC disorder — muscle fibres progressively degenerate (lack of dystrophin — X-linked!).
  • Tetany ⭐: rapid muscle spasms — from low Ca2+!
  • Arthritis ⭐⭐: joint inflammation — osteoarthritis (cartilage wear — age!); rheumatoid arthritis ⭐ (autoimmune!); gouty arthritis (uric acid crystals!).
  • Osteoporosis ⭐⭐: low bone density — brittle bones (ageing, low oestrogen — common after menopause!).

🎯 Myasthenia = autoimmune; dystrophy = genetic; osteoporosis = low bone density (oestrogen!).

NEET Practice — 10 Questions

Q1 Amoeboid movement kahan dekhi jaati hai?
Answer dekho

Amoeba me (pseudopodia se!) aur humans me WBCs/macrophages me — infection sites pe cells amoeboid movement se jaate hain (cytoskeleton/microfilaments!).

Q2 Sarcomere kya hai?
Answer dekho

Skeletal muscle ki functional unit — do Z-lines ke beech ka segment. I band (sirf actin!) + A band (myosin + actin!) + centre me H zone (sirf myosin!).

Q3 Contraction ke time kaunsi band shorten hoti hai?
Answer dekho

I band aur H zone SHORTEN hote hain, A band SAME rehti hai — kyunki sirf actin slide karta hai myosin ke upar, myosin ki length nahi badalti!

Q4 Ca2+ muscle contraction me kya karta hai?
Answer dekho

Ca2+ TROPONIN se bind hota hai → tropomyosin shift hoke actin ke myosin-binding sites EXPOSE karta hai → myosin heads actin se bind kar paate hain!

Q5 Sliding filament theory ka basic idea kya hai?
Answer dekho

Myosin heads actin se cross-bridge banake ATP ki energy se actin ko centre ki taraf PULL karte hain — Z-lines paas aati hain = muscle SHORTEN. Filaments slide, khud shorten nahi hote!

Q6 Human body me kitni bones hain?
Answer dekho

206 bones — axial skeleton (80: skull 22 + vertebral 26 + ribs 24 + sternum 1) + appendicular skeleton (126: limbs + girdles).

Q7 Vertebral column ka formula batao.
Answer dekho

C7 T12 L5 S5 Co4 — 7 cervical + 12 thoracic + 5 lumbar + 5 sacral (fused = 1 sacrum!) + 4 coccygeal (fused = 1 coccyx!) = 26 vertebrae.

Q8 Ball and socket joint kahan hota hai?
Answer dekho

Shoulder (humerus + pectoral girdle) aur hip (femur + pelvic girdle) — sabse zyada movement wala synovial joint (har direction me!).

Q9 Myasthenia gravis kya hai?
Answer dekho

AUTOIMMUNE disorder — neuromuscular junction pe ACh receptors destroy ho jaate hain → muscle weakness/fatigue (nerve-muscle communication break!).

Q10 Osteoporosis kis se hota hai aur kis me common?
Answer dekho

Bone mass/density kam hone se — bones brittle/fracture-prone. Ageing + low oestrogen (post-menopausal women me common!) + low calcium/vitamin D.
