Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

TypesAmoeboid, ciliary, flagellar, muscular. Skeletal (voluntary), smooth (visceral), cardiac (striated, involuntary).
SarcomereZ to Z. I band actin only, A band myosin. Contraction: I + H shorten, A same.
ContractionACh, Ca2+ release, troponin, tropomyosin shifts, cross-bridge, ATP pull. Rigor mortis: no ATP.
Skeleton206 bones: axial 80 + appendicular 126. C7 T12 L5 S5 Co4. Ribs 7 true, 3 false, 2 floating.
JointsFibrous (skull), cartilaginous (vertebrae), synovial: ball-socket, hinge, pivot, gliding, saddle.
DisordersMyasthenia (autoimmune), dystrophy (genetic), tetany (low Ca2+), arthritis, osteoporosis (low density).
