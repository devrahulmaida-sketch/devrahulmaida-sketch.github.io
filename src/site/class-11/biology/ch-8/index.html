1️⃣ Cell Theory aur Cell Ki Basics ⭐

  • Discovery: Robert Hooke ne 1665 me cork me 'cells' dekhe (actually dead cell walls!). Antonie von Leeuwenhoek ne pehli LIVING cell dekhi.
  • Cell theory ⭐⭐: Schleiden (1838, botanist) + Schwann (1839, zoologist) ne formulate kiya — (1) sab living organisms cells se bane hain + unke products se, (2) cell = structure aur function ki basic unit. Rudolf Virchow (1855) ⭐ ne add kiya: 'Omnis cellula-e cellula' — har cell existing cell se banti hai!
  • Cell shapes/sizes ⭐: smallest cells = mycoplasma (0.3 µm); largest isolated single cell = ostrich ka egg! Longest cells = nerve cells. Human RBC ~7 µm; bacteria 3-5 µm.
  • Shapes: disc-like (RBC), polygonal, columnar, thread-like (nerve), branched, irregular (Amoeba, WBC — shape change!).
  • Prokaryotic vs eukaryotic ⭐⭐: prokaryotes = no membrane-bound nucleus (nucleoid) + no membrane organelles + 70S ribosomes (bacteria, cyanobacteria, mycoplasma, PPLO); eukaryotes = true nucleus + membrane organelles + 80S ribosomes (protists, fungi, plants, animals).

💡 Feel: cell = lego brick — har organism inhi bricks ka tower hai, aur har brick purani brick se banti hai!

2️⃣ Prokaryotic Cell — Bacteria Ka Blueprint ⭐⭐

  • Cell envelope ⭐⭐: 3 layers — glycocalyx (outermost — slime layer (loose) ya capsule (thick+tough)), cell wall (structure + shape), plasma membrane (selectively permeable). Gram staining cell wall pe depend: Gram+ve (thick wall, stain rakhte hain) vs Gram−ve (thin wall + extra lipid layer, stain nahi rakhte).
  • Mesosome ⭐: plasma membrane ka inward fold — respiration, DNA replication, cell division me help; secretion; wall formation. (Eukaryotic mitochondria jaisa kaam!)
  • Chromatophores: photosynthetic bacteria me pigment-wali membrane folds.
  • Flagella ⭐: movement ke liye — 3 parts: filament (long, outside), hook, basal body (cell wall me embedded motor!). Pili/fimbriae = attachment + conjugation (sex pili se DNA transfer!).
  • Ribosomes ⭐: 70S (50S + 30S subunits) — protein factories; cytoplasm me free + membrane se attached; polysomes = mRNA pe ribosome chains.
  • Inclusion bodies ⭐: reserve material — phosphate granules, cyanophycean granules, glycogen granules, gas vacuoles (buoyancy — aquatic bacteria!). Plasmids = chhote extra circular DNA (antibiotic resistance!).

🎯 Bacteria me ribosome = 70S, eukaryote cytoplasm me = 80S. Par mitochondria/chloroplast KE ANDAR bhi 70S — endosymbiosis ka proof!

3️⃣ Eukaryotic Cell — Membrane, Wall, Endomembrane System ⭐⭐

  • Plasma membrane ⭐⭐: fluid mosaic model (Singer & Nicolson, 1972) — phospholipid bilayer (quasi-fluid) me proteins embedded (integral + peripheral) + cholesterol. Selectively permeable: neutral molecules passive transport (concentration gradient), polar molecules carriers se (facilitated), active transport ⭐ = energy (ATP) + against gradient (Na-K pump!).
  • Cell wall ⭐: plants me cellulose + hemicellulose + pectin (middle lamella = calcium pectate — cells ko jodti hai!); fungi me chitin. Plasmodesmata ⭐ = cell walls ke beech connections (cytoplasmic bridges — cells ki 'whatsapp groups'!).
  • Endoplasmic reticulum (ER) ⭐⭐: membrane network nucleus se plasma membrane tak. Rough ER (RER) — ribosomes attached (dotted dikhti hai!) → proteins banati hai (secretory); Smooth ER (SER) — ribosomes nahi → lipid synthesis, steroid hormones, detoxification!
  • Golgi apparatus ⭐⭐: (Camillo Golgi, 1898) — flattened disc-shaped cisternae ke stacks (2-7); cis face (forming, ER ki taraf) aur trans face (maturing). Kaam: proteins ka packaging + modification + dispatch (post office!), glycoproteins + glycolipids banana, plant me cell plate formation (cell division ke time!).
  • Lysosomes ⭐: single membrane vesicles — hydrolytic enzymes (optimum acidic pH) — sab kuch digest: worn-out organelles, food, microbes, even apni hi cell (autolysis — 'suicidal bags' ⭐!).
  • Vacuoles ⭐: membrane-bound sacs; plant me ek BADI central vacuole (90% volume! — sap, tonoplast membrane), animal me chhoti; Amoeba me food vacuole + contractile vacuole (osmoregulation!).

💡 RER = protein factory (dots = ribosomes), SER = lipid lab (smooth), Golgi = post office (packaging+addressing), lysosome = demolition crew!

4️⃣ Mitochondria, Plastids, Ribosomes, Cytoskeleton ⭐⭐

  • Mitochondria ⭐⭐ — 'powerhouse of the cell': double membrane — outer smooth + inner cristae (folds — surface area badhati hain!). Matrix me enzymes (aerobic respiration), own DNA (circular!) + 70S ribosomes + RNA — semi-autonomous (khud divide karte hain — fission!). ATP yahi banta hai. Count varies — muscle cells me zyada!
  • Plastids ⭐: (sirf plants + euglenoids!) 3 types — chloroplasts (green, photosynthesis: double membrane, thylakoids stacks = grana (light reactions), stroma (dark reactions — enzymes, own DNA + 70S ribosomes!)), chromoplasts (coloured — carotene etc., flowers/fruits ka colour!), leucoplasts (colourless, storage: amyloplasts = starch, elaioplasts = oils, aleuroplasts = proteins).
  • Ribosomes ⭐: non-membrane granules — rRNA + proteins; eukaryotic cytoplasm me 80S (60S+40S), organelles/prokaryotes me 70S. Site of protein synthesis!
  • Cytoskeleton ⭐: protein filaments ka network — microtubules, microfilaments, intermediate filaments; shape + support + motility + division (spindle!).
  • Cilia/flagella ⭐: hair-like outgrowths — 9+2 arrangement of microtubules (axoneme — 9 peripheral doublets + 2 central singlets!); basal body (9+0) se arise. Cilia = short + many; flagella = long + few.

🎯 9+2 = cilia/flagella ka signature! Mito + chloroplast = double membrane + own DNA + 70S ribosomes (semi-autonomous) — ye dono similarities NEET favourite!

5️⃣ Nucleus, Chromosomes aur Cell Membrane Recap ⭐⭐

  • Nucleus ⭐⭐: (Robert Brown, 1831 ne describe kiya) — double membrane nuclear envelope with nuclear pores (transport control!). Andar: nucleoplasm + chromatin (DNA + histone proteins) + nucleolus (rRNA synthesis + ribosome assembly — membrane-less!). Nucleus = cell ka 'control room'.
  • Chromatin: loosely coiled = euchromatin (active, transcriptionally ON); dense/condensed = heterochromatin (inactive). Division me chromatin condense hoke chromosomes banti hain.
  • Chromosome structure ⭐: duplicated chromosome = 2 sister chromatids, jude hue centromere (primary constriction) pe; kuch me secondary constriction bhi (satellite — NOR = nucleolar organizer!).
  • Centromere position se 4 types ⭐⭐: Metacentric (middle — V-shaped, arms equal), Sub-metacentric (thoda off — L-shaped), Acrocentric (almost end — J-shaped), Telocentric (terminal — I-shaped, rod).
  • Karyotype ⭐: chromosomes ka number + morphology (size, shape) — species-specific (human = 46 = 23 pairs!).
  • Nucleolus ⭐: spherical, nucleus ke andar — ribosomal RNA (rRNA) ka site; active protein-synthesizing cells me BADA hota hai!

🎯 Chromosome shapes = V-L-J-I (meta-submeta-acro-telo). Nucleolus = rRNA factory. Karyotype = chromosome ID card!

1️⃣ कोशिका सिद्धांत और मूल बातें ⭐

  • खोज: रॉबर्ट हुक ने 1665 में कॉर्क में 'कोशिकाएँ' देखीं; ल्यूवेनहुक ने प्रथम जीवित कोशिका देखी।
  • कोशिका सिद्धांत ⭐⭐: श्लाइडन (1838) + श्वान (1839) — (1) सभी जीव कोशिकाओं से बने हैं, (2) कोशिका = संरचना व क्रिया की मूल इकाई। रुडोल्फ़ विर्को (1855) ⭐: 'ओम्निस सेल्युला-ए सेल्युला' — प्रत्येक कोशिका पूर्ववर्ती कोशिका से!
  • आकार ⭐: सबसे छोटी = माइकोप्लाज़्मा (0.3 µm); सबसे बड़ी एकल कोशिका = शुतुरमुर्ग का अंडा! सबसे लंबी = तंत्रिका कोशिकाएँ। मानव RBC ~7 µm।
  • प्रोकैरियोटिक बनाम यूकैरियोटिक ⭐⭐: प्रोकैरियोट = झिल्लीबद्ध केंद्रक नहीं + झिल्लीबद्ध अंगक नहीं + 70S राइबोसोम; यूकैरियोट = सच्चा केंद्रक + झिल्लीबद्ध अंगक + 80S राइबोसोम।

💡 कोशिका = लेगो ईंट — प्रत्येक जीव इन्हीं ईंटों का मीनार!

2️⃣ प्रोकैरियोटिक कोशिका — जीवाणु का नक्शा ⭐⭐

  • कोशिका आवरण ⭐⭐: 3 परतें — ग्लाइकोकैलिक्स (सबसे बाहर — श्लेष्मा परत/कैप्सूल), कोशिका-भित्ति, प्लाज़्मा-झिल्ली। ग्राम-अंजन भित्ति पर आधारित: ग्राम-धनात्मक (मोटी भित्ति) बनाम ग्राम-ऋणात्मक (पतली + लिपिड परत)।
  • मीज़ोसोम ⭐: प्लाज़्मा-झिल्ली का अंतर्वलन — श्वसन, DNA-प्रतिकृति, विभाजन में सहायता।
  • ध्वजिकाएँ ⭐: गमन हेतु — तंतु + हुक + आधारी पिंड (भित्ति में धँसा मोटर!)। पिलाई/फिम्ब्री = आसंजन + संयुग्मन।
  • राइबोसोम ⭐: 70S (50S + 30S) — प्रोटीन कारख़ाने; पॉलीसोम = mRNA पर शृंखलाएँ।
  • अंतर्वेशी पिंड ⭐: संचित पदार्थ — फॉस्फ़ेट-कण, ग्लाइकोजन-कण, गैस-रिक्तिकाएँ (प्लवनशीलता!)। प्लाज़्मिड = छोटा अतिरिक्त DNA (प्रतिजैविक-प्रतिरोध!)।

🎯 जीवाणु में 70S, यूकैरियोट-द्रव्य में 80S; परंतु माइटोकॉन्ड्रिया/हरितलवक में भी 70S — अंतःसहजीविता का प्रमाण!

3️⃣ यूकैरियोटिक कोशिका — झिल्ली, भित्ति, अंतःझिल्ली तंत्र ⭐⭐

  • प्लाज़्मा-झिल्ली ⭐⭐: तरल-मोज़ेक मॉडल (सिंगर और निकोलसन, 1972) — फ़ॉस्फ़ोलिपिड द्विपरत में धँसे प्रोटीन + कोलेस्ट्रॉल। चयनात्मक पारगम्य: उदासीन अणु निष्क्रिय, ध्रुवीय वाहकों से, सक्रिय परिवहन ⭐ = ऊर्जा (ATP) + प्रवणता के विरुद्ध।
  • कोशिका-भित्ति ⭐: पादपों में सेलुलोज़ + पेक्टिन (मध्यपट्टिका = कैल्शियम पेक्टेट); कवक में काइटिन। प्लाज़्मोडेस्माटा ⭐ = भित्तियों के बीच सेतु!
  • अंतर्द्रव्यी जालिका (ER) ⭐⭐: खुरदुरी ER — राइबोसोम युक्त → प्रोटीन; चिकनी ER — राइबोसोम-रहित → लिपिड, स्टेरॉयड हॉर्मोन, विषहरण!
  • गॉल्जिकाय ⭐⭐: चपटी थैलियों (सिस्टर्नी) के ढेर; सिस फलक (निर्माण) व ट्रांस फलक (परिपक्वता)। कार्य: प्रोटीन पैकेजिंग + प्रेषण (डाकघर!), ग्लाइकोप्रोटीन, पादपों में कोशिका-पट्टिका निर्माण!
  • लाइसोसोम ⭐: एकल-झिल्ली पुटक — अपघटनकारी एंज़ाइम; जीर्ण अंगक, जीवाणु, स्वयं की कोशिका भी (स्व-अपघटन — 'आत्मघाती थैलियाँ' ⭐)!
  • रिक्तिकाएँ ⭐: पादप में एक बड़ी केंद्रीय (90% आयतन — टोनोप्लास्ट झिल्ली); अमीबा में आहार-रिक्तिका + संकुचनशील रिक्तिका (परासरण-नियमन)।

💡 खुरदुरी ER = प्रोटीन कारख़ाना, चिकनी = लिपिड प्रयोगशाला, गॉल्जिकाय = डाकघर, लाइसोसोम = विध्वंस-दल!

4️⃣ माइटोकॉन्ड्रिया, लवक, राइबोसोम, कंकाल-तंत्र ⭐⭐

  • माइटोकॉन्ड्रिया ⭐⭐ — 'कोशिका का ऊर्जा-गृह': द्वि-झिल्ली — बाह्य चिकनी + अंतर क्रिस्टी (वलन — सतह-क्षेत्र वृद्धि!)। मैट्रिक्स में एंज़ाइम, स्वयं का DNA (वृत्तीय!) + 70S राइबोसोम — अर्ध-स्वायत्त! ATP यहीं बनता है।
  • लवक ⭐: (केवल पादप + यूग्लीना!) 3 प्रकार — हरितलवक (प्रकाश-संश्लेषण: थाइलैकॉइड ढेर = ग्रैना (प्रकाश-अभिक्रियाएँ), स्ट्रोमा (अंध-अभिक्रियाएँ — स्वयं का DNA!)), वर्णीलवक (रंगीन — पुष्प/फल का रंग!), अवर्णीलवक (रंगहीन — संचय: स्टार्च/तेल/प्रोटीन)।
  • राइबोसोम ⭐: झिल्ली-रहित कण — rRNA + प्रोटीन; यूकैरियोट-द्रव्य में 80S। प्रोटीन-संश्लेषण का स्थल!
  • कोशिका-कंकाल ⭐: प्रोटीन तंतुओं का जाल — आकार + आधार + गतिशीलता + विभाजन (तरकू!)।
  • पक्ष्माभ/कशाभिकाएँ ⭐: माइक्रोट्यूब्यूल का 9+2 विन्यास (एक्सोनीम — 9 परिधीय युग्म + 2 केंद्रीय!)। पक्ष्माभ = छोटे + अनेक; कशाभिका = लंबी + कुछ।

🎯 9+2 = पक्ष्माभ-हस्ताक्षर! माइटो + हरितलवक = द्वि-झिल्ली + स्वयं DNA + 70S (अर्ध-स्वायत्त)!

5️⃣ केंद्रक और गुणसूत्र ⭐⭐

  • केंद्रक ⭐⭐: (रॉबर्ट ब्राउन, 1831) — द्वि-झिल्ली केंद्रकीय आवरण + केंद्रकीय छिद्र (परिवहन-नियंत्रण)। अंदर: केंद्रकद्रव्य + क्रोमैटिन (DNA + हिस्टोन) + केंद्रिका (rRNA संश्लेषण — झिल्ली-रहित!)।
  • क्रोमैटिन: ढीला = यूक्रोमैटिन (सक्रिय); सघन = हेटेरोक्रोमैटिन (निष्क्रिय)। विभाजन में संघनित होकर गुणसूत्र बनते हैं।
  • गुणसूत्र संरचना ⭐: द्विगुणित गुणसूत्र = 2 सहोदर क्रोमैटिड, सेंट्रोमियर (प्राथमिक संकीर्णन) पर जुड़े।
  • सेंट्रोमियर स्थिति से 4 प्रकार ⭐⭐: मध्य (V-आकार, भुजाएँ समान), उप-मध्य (L-आकार), उपांत्य (J-आकार), अंत्य (I-आकार/छड़)।
  • कैरियोटाइप ⭐: गुणसूत्रों की संख्या + आकारिकी — जाति-विशिष्ट (मानव = 46 = 23 जोड़े!)।

🎯 गुणसूत्र आकार = V-L-J-I; केंद्रिका = rRNA कारख़ाना; कैरियोटाइप = गुणसूत्र पहचान-पत्र!

1️⃣ Cell Theory and Cell Basics ⭐

  • Discovery: Robert Hooke saw 'cells' in cork (1665); Leeuwenhoek saw the first LIVING cell.
  • Cell theory ⭐⭐: Schleiden (1838) + Schwann (1839) — (1) all living organisms are made of cells and their products, (2) the cell is the basic unit of structure and function. Rudolf Virchow (1855) ⭐ added: 'Omnis cellula-e cellula' — every cell comes from a pre-existing cell!
  • Sizes ⭐: smallest cells = mycoplasma (0.3 µm); largest single cell = ostrich egg! Longest = nerve cells. Human RBC ~7 µm.
  • Prokaryotic vs eukaryotic ⭐⭐: prokaryotes = no membrane-bound nucleus, no membrane organelles, 70S ribosomes; eukaryotes = true nucleus + membrane organelles + 80S ribosomes.

💡 Feel: a cell is a lego brick — every organism is a tower of these bricks, and every brick comes from an older brick!

2️⃣ Prokaryotic Cell — The Bacterial Blueprint ⭐⭐

  • Cell envelope ⭐⭐: 3 layers — glycocalyx (outermost — slime layer or capsule), cell wall, plasma membrane. Gram staining depends on the wall: Gram+ve (thick wall, holds stain) vs Gram−ve (thin wall + extra lipid layer).
  • Mesosome ⭐: inward fold of the plasma membrane — helps respiration, DNA replication, and cell division.
  • Flagella ⭐: for movement — filament + hook + basal body (a motor embedded in the wall!). Pili/fimbriae = attachment and conjugation.
  • Ribosomes ⭐: 70S (50S + 30S) — protein factories; polysomes = ribosome chains on mRNA.
  • Inclusion bodies ⭐: stored material — phosphate granules, glycogen granules, gas vacuoles (buoyancy!). Plasmids = small extra DNA rings (antibiotic resistance!).

🎯 Bacteria have 70S ribosomes, eukaryotic cytoplasm has 80S — but mitochondria/chloroplasts also have 70S. Proof of endosymbiosis!

3️⃣ Eukaryotic Cell — Membranes, Walls, Endomembrane System ⭐⭐

  • Plasma membrane ⭐⭐: fluid mosaic model (Singer & Nicolson, 1972) — phospholipid bilayer with embedded proteins + cholesterol. Selectively permeable: neutral molecules move passively, polar ones via carriers, active transport ⭐ uses ATP against the gradient (Na-K pump!).
  • Cell wall ⭐: plants — cellulose + pectin (middle lamella = calcium pectate glues cells!); fungi — chitin. Plasmodesmata ⭐ = cytoplasmic bridges through walls!
  • Endoplasmic reticulum (ER) ⭐⭐: Rough ER — studded with ribosomes → proteins; Smooth ER — no ribosomes → lipids, steroid hormones, detoxification!
  • Golgi apparatus ⭐⭐: stacks of flattened cisternae with a cis face (receiving, towards ER) and trans face (shipping). Job: packaging + modifying + dispatching proteins (the post office!), making glycoproteins, and forming the cell plate in dividing plant cells!
  • Lysosomes ⭐: single-membrane vesicles full of hydrolytic enzymes — digest worn-out organelles, food, microbes, even their own cell (autolysis — 'suicidal bags' ⭐!).
  • Vacuoles ⭐: one HUGE central vacuole in plants (90% of volume, tonoplast membrane); Amoeba has food + contractile vacuoles (osmoregulation!).

💡 RER = protein factory, SER = lipid lab, Golgi = post office, lysosome = demolition crew!

4️⃣ Mitochondria, Plastids, Ribosomes, Cytoskeleton ⭐⭐

  • Mitochondria ⭐⭐ — 'powerhouse of the cell': double membrane — smooth outer + folded inner (cristae — more surface area!). Matrix holds enzymes, its own circular DNA + 70S ribosomes — semi-autonomous! ATP is made here.
  • Plastids ⭐: (plants + euglenoids only!) — chloroplasts (photosynthesis: grana = thylakoid stacks for light reactions, stroma for dark reactions — own DNA too!), chromoplasts (coloured — flower/fruit colours!), leucoplasts (colourless storage — starch/oils/proteins).
  • Ribosomes ⭐: non-membrane granules of rRNA + protein; eukaryotic cytoplasm has 80S. Sites of protein synthesis!
  • Cytoskeleton ⭐: protein filament network — shape, support, motility, division (the spindle!).
  • Cilia/flagella ⭐: microtubules in a 9+2 arrangement (axoneme — 9 outer doublets + 2 central singlets!). Cilia = short and many; flagella = long and few.

🎯 9+2 = the cilia signature! Mitochondria + chloroplasts = double membrane + own DNA + 70S (semi-autonomous)!

5️⃣ Nucleus and Chromosomes ⭐⭐

  • Nucleus ⭐⭐: (described by Robert Brown, 1831) — double-membrane nuclear envelope with nuclear pores (controlled transport). Inside: nucleoplasm + chromatin (DNA + histones) + nucleolus (rRNA synthesis — membrane-less!).
  • Chromatin: loose = euchromatin (active); dense = heterochromatin (inactive). During division it condenses into chromosomes.
  • Chromosome structure ⭐: a duplicated chromosome = 2 sister chromatids joined at the centromere (primary constriction).
  • 4 types by centromere position ⭐⭐: Metacentric (middle — V-shaped), Sub-metacentric (L-shaped), Acrocentric (J-shaped), Telocentric (I-shaped/rod).
  • Karyotype ⭐: the number + morphology of chromosomes — species-specific (humans = 46 = 23 pairs!).

🎯 Chromosome shapes = V-L-J-I; nucleolus = rRNA factory; karyotype = chromosome ID card!

NEET Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Cell theory ke 2 main points + Virchow ka addition batao.
Answer dekho

Schleiden+Schwann: (1) sab organisms cells + unke products se bane, (2) cell = structure/function ki basic unit. Virchow (1855): 'Omnis cellula-e cellula' — har cell purani cell se banti hai!

Q2 Prokaryotic aur eukaryotic cell me 3 main differences batao.
Answer dekho

Prokaryote: no membrane nucleus (nucleoid), no membrane organelles, 70S ribosomes; Eukaryote: true nucleus, membrane organelles, 80S ribosomes.

Q3 Rough ER aur Smooth ER ka kaam kya hai?
Answer dekho

RER (ribosomes attached) = protein synthesis (secretory proteins); SER (no ribosomes) = lipid synthesis, steroid hormones, detoxification.

Q4 Mitochondria ko 'semi-autonomous' kyun kehte hain?
Answer dekho

Kyunki inme khud ka circular DNA + 70S ribosomes + RNA hain — ye khud divide (fission) kar sakte hain, apne kuch proteins khud banate hain.

Q5 Lysosomes ko 'suicidal bags' kyun kehte hain?
Answer dekho

Kyunki inke hydrolytic enzymes zaroorat pade to poori cell ko hi digest kar sakte hain (autolysis) — worn-out organelles aur microbes bhi kha jaate hain.

Q6 Golgi apparatus ke cis aur trans faces me kya farak hai?
Answer dekho

Cis face = forming face (ER ki taraf — materials receive karta hai); trans face = maturing face (packaged materials dispatch hoti hain — plasma membrane ki taraf).

Q7 Grana aur stroma me kya difference hai?
Answer dekho

Grana = thylakoids ke stacks — pigments + light reactions; Stroma = matrix — enzymes + dark reactions (+ own DNA/ribosomes).

Q8 9+2 arrangement kahan milta hai?
Answer dekho

Cilia aur flagella ke axoneme me — 9 peripheral microtubule doublets + 2 central singlets. (Basal body me 9+0.)

Q9 Centromere position se chromosomes ke 4 types batao.
Answer dekho

Metacentric (V-shaped, middle), Sub-metacentric (L-shaped), Acrocentric (J-shaped), Telocentric (I-shaped, terminal).

Q10 Nucleolus ka kaam kya hai?
Answer dekho

rRNA synthesis + ribosome assembly ka site — nucleus ke andar membrane-less spherical body. Active protein-synthesizing cells me BADI hoti hai!
