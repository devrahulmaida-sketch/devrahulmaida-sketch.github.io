Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

Cell theorySchleiden + Schwann; Virchow 1855: every cell from a pre-existing cell. Mycoplasma smallest (0.3 µm), ostrich egg largest single cell.
ProkaryoteNo true nucleus, 70S ribosomes. Envelope: glycocalyx, wall, membrane. Gram +ve thick wall. Mesosome (NCERT term; likely EM artifact), plasmid, flagellum.
Membrane + wallFluid mosaic (Singer-Nicolson 1972). Active transport needs ATP. Plant wall cellulose, fungi chitin. Plasmodesmata link cells.
EndomembraneRER proteins, SER lipids. Golgi: cis to trans, packaging. Lysosome hydrolytic, acidic. Plant vacuole ~90%, tonoplast.
Mito + plastidsCristae, matrix, own DNA + 70S. Chloroplast, chromoplast, leucoplast. Mito + chloroplast semi-autonomous.
Nucleus + chromosome80S eukaryote ribosome. Cilia/flagella 9+2. Euchromatin active. V, L, J, I = meta, submeta, acro, telo. Human karyotype 46.
