Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

HistoryAristotle, Linnaeus (2 kingdoms), Whittaker 1969 (5 kingdoms). Viruses, viroids, lichens are outside.
MoneraProkaryotes, peptidoglycan wall. Archaebacteria, cyanobacteria (N2 fixing), Mycoplasma (no wall, smallest).
ProtistaUnicellular eukaryotes. Diatoms, dinoflagellates, Euglena (pellicle), slime moulds, protozoans.
FungiChitin wall, mycelium. Phyco-, asco-, basidio-, deuteromycetes.
Plantae, AnimaliaPlants: chlorophyll, cellulose, alternation of generations. Animals: holozoic, no wall.
Virus, viroid, lichenVirus: DNA or RNA + capsid. Viroid: free RNA. Lichen: alga + fungus. Mycorrhiza: fungus + root.
