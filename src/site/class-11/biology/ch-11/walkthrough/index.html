Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

ExperimentsPriestley: plants restore air. Ingenhousz: light needed. Sachs: starch. Engelmann: blue + red. van Niel: O₂ from water.
MachineryThylakoid/grana: light rxn. Stroma: dark rxn. Chl a main; chl b, xanthophylls, carotenoids accessory. Green reflected.
Light reactionPS II P680, PS I P700. Z-scheme: water splits, NADPH. Cyclic: PS I only, ATP only. Chemiosmosis: ATP synthase. Products ATP + NADPH.
Calvin cycleCO₂ + RuBP (5C) → 2 3-PGA (RuBisCO). Reduction: ATP + NADPH. Regeneration. 6 CO₂ → 18 ATP + 12 NADPH.
C4OAA first product, PEPcase in mesophyll, CO₂ released in bundle sheath, Kranz anatomy. No photorespiration. 30 ATP per glucose.
FactorsBlackman: limiting factor. Light, CO₂ (0.03-0.04%), temperature (25-35°C), water. Photorespiration: RuBisCO + O₂ = waste.
