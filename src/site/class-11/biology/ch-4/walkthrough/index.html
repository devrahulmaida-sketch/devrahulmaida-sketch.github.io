Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

BasisSymmetry: asymmetrical, radial, bilateral. Diploblastic vs triploblastic. Coelom: acoelomate, pseudocoelomate, coelomate.
Porifera + CnidariaSponges: ostia, canal system, choanocytes. Cnidaria: cnidoblasts, polyp and medusa. Ctenophora: 8 comb plates.
WormsFlatworms: flame cells. Roundworms: pseudocoelom, complete gut. Annelids: segmented, setae, closed circulation.
Arthropoda + MolluscaArthropods: jointed legs, chitin, open circulation. Molluscs: shell, mantle, radula, foot.
EchinodermsMarine, spiny skin, water vascular system, radial adults, bilateral larvae. Hemichordata: proboscis, collar, trunk.
ChordataNotochord, dorsal nerve cord, gill slits. Heart: fish 2, amphibian 3, reptile 3 (croc 4), bird and mammal 4. Platypus lays eggs.
