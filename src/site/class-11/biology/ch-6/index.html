Notes language:

1️⃣ Meristematic Tissue — Growth Ka Engine ⭐

  • Meristems: actively dividing cells — plant growth inhi se hoti hai. Cells chhoti, dense cytoplasm, prominent nucleus, thin cellulose walls, vacuoles chhote ya absent.
  • Position ke basis pe ⭐⭐: Apical meristem — root aur shoot ke TIP pe (length badhata hai — primary growth!); Intercalary meristem — mature regions ke beech me (grasses me nodes ke paas — katne ke baad bhi ugte hain isliye!); Lateral meristem ⭐ — sides me (vascular cambium + cork cambium) — THICKNESS badhata hai (secondary growth, dicots me!).
  • Primary meristems (apical + intercalary — early life me appear) vs secondary meristems (lateral — baad me mature tissues se bante hain, jaise cork cambium).
  • Apical meristem se bante hain: dermal tissue, ground tissue, vascular tissue — permanent tissues.

💡 Feel: apical = height ka boss, lateral = motai ka boss. Ghas kat-te hi wapas ug jaati hai — intercalary meristem ka kamaal!

2️⃣ Permanent Tissues — Simple aur Complex ⭐⭐

  • Simple tissues ⭐ (ek hi type ki cells): Parenchyma — living, thin-walled, isodiametric (gol-mol); photosynthesis, storage, secretion; Collenchyma ⭐ — living, corners pe CELLULOSE thickening (uneven), flexibility + support (leaf stalks/petioles me); Sclerenchyma ⭐ — DEAD cells, LIGNIN thickening (uniform), hard + rigid support; 2 types: fibres (lambe, pointed — jute!) aur sclereids (gol — nut shells, seed coats ki hardness!).
  • Complex tissues ⭐⭐ (kai type ki cells, ek kaam): Xylem = water conduction — 4 elements: tracheids (dead, lignified, tapering ends), vessels (dead, lambe tubes — angiosperms me!), xylem parenchyma (LIVING — storage), xylem fibres (dead — support). Phloem = food conduction — sieve tube elements (living, nucleus NAHI!) + companion cells ⭐ (nucleus wale, sieve tubes ko control karte hain) + phloem parenchyma (living — gymnosperms me absent!) + phloem fibres (dead — bast fibres, jute!).
  • Gymnosperm fact ⭐: gymnosperms me vessels absent (sirf tracheids) aur companion cells absent (albuminous cells hote hain) — ye differences puchhe jaate hain!
  • Xylem ke types: protoxylem (pehle bana, chhota) + metaxylem (baad me, bada). Arrangement: exarch (protoxylem bahar — ROOTS ⭐) vs endarch (protoxylem andar — STEMS ⭐).

🎯 Living/dead trick: parenchyma+collenchyma+companion cells+sieve tubes = living; sclerenchyma+tracheids+vessels+fibres = dead. Sieve tube = living WITHOUT nucleus — paradox question!

3️⃣ Tissue Systems aur Dicot vs Monocot Root ⭐

  • 3 tissue systems ⭐: Epidermal (outer covering — epidermis, cuticle, stomata, trichomes/hairs), Ground/Fundamental (cortex + pith + mesophyll — sab kuch jo dermal aur vascular nahi), Vascular (xylem + phloem — vascular bundles).
  • Stomata ⭐: epidermis ke pores — guard cells regulate karte hain; transpiration + gas exchange. Dicot leaf me zyada LOWER surface pe; monocot me dono surfaces pe barabar.
  • Vascular bundles ⭐⭐: Conjoint (xylem+phloem saath — stems/leaves) vs Radial (xylem-phloem ALTERNATE radii pe — ROOTS!). Open (cambium present — dicots, secondary growth possible) vs Closed (cambium absent — monocots, no secondary growth).
  • Dicot root ⭐: xylem bundles 2-4 (diarch-tetrarch), conjunctive tissue chhota, cambium baad me develop hota hai (secondary growth), pith chhota ya absent.
  • Monocot root ⭐: xylem bundles BAHUT zyada (polyarch — 6+), conjunctive tissue bada, sclerenchymatous, pith BADA + well-developed, secondary growth NAHI. Dono me radial bundles + exarch xylem!

💡 Root me radial arrangement matlab xylem-phloem alternate — jaise wheel ki spokes alag-alag colour ki!

4️⃣ Dicot vs Monocot Stem aur Leaf ⭐⭐

  • Dicot stem ⭐⭐: epidermis (cuticle + hairs), cortex (3 zones: hypodermis COLLENCHYMA + cortical parenchyma + endodermis = 'starch sheath'), vascular bundles RING me arranged, conjoint, collateral, OPEN (cambium present), medullary rays bade, pith bada + well-developed. Example: sunflower.
  • Monocot stem ⭐⭐: hypodermis SCLERENCHYMATOUS, vascular bundles SCATTERED (bikharke) + CLOSED (cambium nahi), ground tissue undifferentiated (cortex/pith alag nahi!), peripheral bundles chhote + central bade. Example: maize. Secondary growth NAHI.
  • Dicot leaf (dorsiventral) ⭐⭐: upper aur lower surfaces alag; mesophyll DIFFERENTIATED — palisade parenchyma (upper side, elongated, photosynthesis machine) + spongy parenchyma (lower side, loose, air spaces). Stomata zyada lower pe. Vascular bundles midrib me — xylem upar, phloem neeche.
  • Monocot leaf (isobilateral) ⭐: dono surfaces SAME; mesophyll UNDIFFERENTIATED (palisade nahi!); stomata dono sides barabar; bulliform cells ⭐ — bade empty colourless cells upper epidermis me (leaf rolling in water stress!). Parallel venation.
  • Bundle sheath: monocot leaf me vascular bundles ke around sclerenchymatous sheath.

🎯 Dicot stem = bundles ring me (disciplined queue); monocot = scattered (free-for-all crowd). Dicot leaf = 2-storey (palisade+spongy); monocot = 1 uniform floor!

5️⃣ Secondary Growth — Motai Ka Raaz ⭐⭐

  • Secondary growth: dicot stems/roots ki THICKNESS badhna — lateral meristems (vascular cambium + cork cambium) se. Monocots me absent (closed bundles!).
  • Vascular cambium ⭐⭐: fascicular (bundles ke andar) + interfascicular (bundles ke beech ke cells se) milke complete RING banate hain. Andar ki taraf secondary xylem (zyada!), bahar ki taraf secondary phloem (kam) cut karta hai.
  • Annual rings ⭐⭐: spring wood (early — light, bade vessels, zyada banta hai) + autumn wood (late — dark, chhote elements) = ek annual ring. Rings gino = tree ki AGE! (Dendrochronology)
  • Heartwood vs sapwood ⭐⭐: heartwood (duramen) — central, DEAD, dark (tannins/resins/gums deposited), hard, durable (furniture!), conduction nahi karta; sapwood (alburnum) — peripheral, LIVING, light coloured, water conduction karta hai.
  • Cork cambium (phellogen) ⭐: outer cortex ke cells se banta hai; bahar ki taraf cork/phellem (dead, suberin walls — impervious to water!) + andar secondary cortex (phelloderm). Phellem + phellogen + phelloderm = periderm. Bark = sab kuch phloem se bahar! Lenticels ⭐ — bark ke chhote pores, gas exchange ke liye.
  • Secondary growth in dicot ROOT ⭐: vascular cambium xylem bundles ke neeche ke conjunctive tissue se banta hai (completely secondary origin!); cork cambium pericycle se.

🎯 Heartwood = furniture wood (dark+dead+strong); sapwood = working pipes (light+living). Annual ring count = age. Exam me teeno pakke!

1️⃣ विभज्योतक ऊतक — वृद्धि का इंजन ⭐

  • विभज्योतक (meristems): सक्रिय विभाजनशील कोशिकाएँ — छोटी, सघन कोशिकाद्रव्य, प्रमुख केंद्रक, पतली भित्ति।
  • स्थिति के आधार पर ⭐⭐: शीर्षस्थ विभज्योतक — मूल/प्ररोह के शीर्ष पर (लंबाई!); अन्तर्वेशी — परिपक्व क्षेत्रों के बीच (घास के नोड्स); पार्श्वीय ⭐ — किनारों पर (संवहन-कैम्बियम + कॉर्क-कैम्बियम) — मोटाई (द्वितीयक वृद्धि)।
  • प्राथमिक (शीर्षस्थ + अन्तर्वेशी) बनाम द्वितीयक (पार्श्वीय — परिपक्व ऊतकों से बाद में)।

💡 शीर्षस्थ = ऊँचाई का बॉस, पार्श्वीय = मोटाई का बॉस।

2️⃣ स्थायी ऊतक — सरल और जटिल ⭐⭐

  • सरल ऊतक ⭐: मृदूतक (parenchyma) — जीवित, पतली भित्ति; प्रकाश-संश्लेषण, संचय; स्थूलकोणोतक (collenchyma) ⭐ — जीवित, कोनों पर मोटाई, लचीलापन (पर्णवृंत); दृढ़ोतक (sclerenchyma) ⭐ — मृत, लिग्निन-मोटाई, कठोर आधार; 2 प्रकार: रेशे (जूट!) + पाषाणकोशिकाएँ (सकलराइड्स — गिरियों की कठोरता)।
  • जटिल ऊतक ⭐⭐: जाइलम = जल-संचालन — वाहिकाएँ (मृत), नलिकाएँ/vessels (मृत, आवृतबीजियों में!), जाइलम-मृदूतक (जीवित), जाइलम-रेशे (मृत)। फ्लोएम = भोजन-संचालन — चालिनी-नलिकाएँ (जीवित, केंद्रक नहीं!) + सहचर कोशिकाएँ ⭐ (केंद्रकयुक्त — नियंत्रण!) + फ्लोएम-मृदूतक + फ्लोएम-रेशे (मृत — जूट!)।
  • जिम्नोस्पर्म तथ्य ⭐: जिम्नोस्पर्म में vessels नहीं (केवल वाहिकाएँ) और सहचर कोशिकाएँ नहीं।
  • जाइलम विन्यास: बहिर्भागी (exarch) — मूल में ⭐ बनाम अन्तर्भागी (endarch) — तने में ⭐।

🎯 चालिनी-नलिका = जीवित पर केंद्रक-रहित — विरोधाभासी प्रश्न!

3️⃣ ऊतक-तंत्र और द्विबीजपत्री बनाम एकबीजपत्री मूल ⭐

  • 3 ऊतक-तंत्र ⭐: अधित्वचीय (बाह्य आवरण — अधित्वचा, क्यूटिकल, रंध्र, रोम), भूमितल/मूलभूत (वल्कुट + मज्जा + मध्योतक), संवहनी (जाइलम + फ्लोएम)।
  • रंध्र ⭐: अधित्वचा के छिद्र — द्वार-कोशिकाएँ नियंत्रित करती हैं; वाष्पोत्सर्जन + गैस-विनिमय।
  • संवहन-पुंज ⭐⭐: संयुक्त (जाइलम+फ्लोएम साथ — तना/पत्ती) बनाम अरीय (एकांतर त्रिज्याओं पर — मूल!)। विवृत (कैम्बियम — द्विबीजपत्री) बनाम संवृत (कैम्बियम नहीं — एकबीजपत्री)।
  • द्विबीजपत्री मूल ⭐: जाइलम 2-4 (द्वि-चतुररीय), कैम्बियम बाद में (द्वितीयक वृद्धि), मज्जा छोटी/अनुपस्थित।
  • एकबीजपत्री मूल ⭐: जाइलम अनेक (बहुरीय/polyarch), मज्जा बड़ी + विकसित, द्वितीयक वृद्धि नहीं। दोनों में अरीय पुंज + बहिर्भागी जाइलम!

💡 मूल में अरीय विन्यास = जाइलम-फ्लोएम पहिये की तीलियों जैसे एकांतर!

4️⃣ द्विबीजपत्री बनाम एकबीजपत्री तना और पत्ती ⭐⭐

  • द्विबीजपत्री तना ⭐⭐: अधित्वचा (क्यूटिकल), वल्कुट (हाइपोडर्मिस स्थूलकोणोतक + अन्तस्त्वचा = 'स्टार्च आवरण'), संवहन-पुंज वलय में, संयुक्त, पार्श्वसम्मुख, विवृत (कैम्बियम युक्त), मज्जा बड़ी। उदा: सूरजमुखी।
  • एकबीजपत्री तना ⭐⭐: हाइपोडर्मिस दृढ़ोतकीय, संवहन-पुंज बिखरे + संवृत (कैम्बियम नहीं), भूमितल ऊतक अविभेदित (वल्कुट/मज्जा पृथक नहीं!)। उदा: मक्का। द्वितीयक वृद्धि नहीं।
  • द्विबीजपत्री पत्ती (पृष्ठ-अधरीय) ⭐⭐: मध्योतक विभेदित — स्तंभाकार मृदूतक (ऊपर, लंबी कोशिकाएँ — प्रकाश-संश्लेषण फ़ैक्ट्री) + स्पंजी मृदूतक (नीचे, ढीली, वायु-रिक्तियाँ)। रंध्र नीचे अधिक।
  • एकबीजपत्री पत्ती (समद्विपार्श्वीय) ⭐: दोनों सतहें समान; मध्योतक अविभेदित (स्तंभाकार नहीं!); बुलिफ़ॉर्म कोशिकाएँ ⭐ — ऊपरी अधित्वचा की बड़ी रिक्त कोशिकाएँ (जल-तनाव में पत्ती मुड़ती है!)।

🎯 द्विबीजपत्री तना = पुंज वलय में; एकबीजपत्री = बिखरे। द्विबीजपत्री पत्ती = दो मंज़िल; एकबीजपत्री = एक समान!

5️⃣ द्वितीयक वृद्धि — मोटाई का रहस्य ⭐⭐

  • द्वितीयक वृद्धि: द्विबीजपत्री तने/मूलों की मोटाई — पार्श्वीय विभज्योतकों से। एकबीजपत्रियों में अनुपस्थित।
  • संवहन-कैम्बियम ⭐⭐: पूलान्तरालीय + अन्तःपूलीय मिलकर वलय बनाते हैं; अंदर द्वितीयक जाइलम (अधिक!), बाहर द्वितीयक फ्लोएम (कम)।
  • वार्षिक वलय ⭐⭐: वसंत-काष्ठ (हल्का, बड़ी नलिकाएँ) + शरद-काष्ठ (गहरा) = एक वलय = एक वर्ष! वृक्ष की आयु गिनने की विधि।
  • हृदय-काष्ठ बनाम बहिःकाष्ठ ⭐⭐: हृदय-काष्ठ — केंद्रीय, मृत, गहरा (टैनिन/राल), कठोर, टिकाऊ (फ़र्नीचर!), संचालन नहीं; बहिःकाष्ठ — परिधीय, जीवित, हल्का, जल-संचालन करता है।
  • कॉर्क-कैम्बियम (फ़ेलोजन) ⭐: बाह्य वल्कुट से; बाहर कॉर्क (फ़ेलम) — मृत, स्यूबेरिन-भित्ति (जल-अभेद्य!) + अंदर द्वितीयक वल्कुट (फ़ेलोडर्म)। फ़ेलम+फ़ेलोजन+फ़ेलोडर्म = परित्रचा (periderm)। वर्विद्र (lenticels) ⭐ — छाल के छिद्र, गैस-विनिमय।

🎯 हृदय-काष्ठ = फ़र्नीचर लकड़ी; बहिःकाष्ठ = काम करती नलियाँ; वार्षिक वलय = आयु!

1️⃣ Meristematic Tissue — The Growth Engine ⭐

  • Meristems: actively dividing cells — all plant growth starts here. Small cells, dense cytoplasm, prominent nucleus, thin walls.
  • By position ⭐⭐: Apical meristem — tips of roots and shoots (adds LENGTH — primary growth); Intercalary meristem — between mature regions (grass nodes — why grass regrows after cutting!); Lateral meristem ⭐ — on the sides (vascular cambium + cork cambium) — adds THICKNESS (secondary growth, in dicots!).
  • Primary meristems (apical + intercalary) vs secondary meristems (lateral — arise later from mature tissues).

💡 Feel: apical = the height boss, lateral = the thickness boss. Grass regrows after mowing — intercalary magic!

2️⃣ Permanent Tissues — Simple and Complex ⭐⭐

  • Simple tissues ⭐ (one cell type): Parenchyma — living, thin-walled; photosynthesis and storage; Collenchyma ⭐ — living, uneven cellulose thickening at corners, flexible support (petioles); Sclerenchyma ⭐ — DEAD, uniform LIGNIN thickening, rigid support; fibres (long — jute!) + sclereids (round — nutshell hardness!).
  • Complex tissues ⭐⭐: Xylem = water conduction — tracheids (dead), vessels (dead — in angiosperms!), xylem parenchyma (LIVING), xylem fibres (dead). Phloem = food conduction — sieve tube elements (living, NO nucleus!) + companion cells ⭐ (have nucleus, control the sieve tubes) + phloem parenchyma + phloem fibres (dead — jute!).
  • Gymnosperm fact ⭐: gymnosperms lack vessels (tracheids only) and companion cells — a favourite exam difference!
  • Xylem arrangement: exarch (protoxylem outward — ROOTS ⭐) vs endarch (protoxylem inward — STEMS ⭐).

🎯 Sieve tube = living WITHOUT a nucleus — the classic paradox question!

3️⃣ Tissue Systems and Dicot vs Monocot Root ⭐

  • 3 tissue systems ⭐: Epidermal (outer covering — epidermis, cuticle, stomata, hairs), Ground (cortex + pith + mesophyll), Vascular (xylem + phloem bundles).
  • Stomata ⭐: epidermal pores regulated by guard cells — transpiration + gas exchange. Mostly on the lower surface in dicots; equal on both in monocots.
  • Vascular bundles ⭐⭐: Conjoint (xylem+phloem together — stems/leaves) vs Radial (alternate radii — ROOTS!). Open (with cambium — dicots) vs Closed (no cambium — monocots).
  • Dicot root ⭐: 2-4 xylem bundles (diarch-tetrarch), cambium develops later (secondary growth), pith small or absent.
  • Monocot root ⭐: many xylem bundles (polyarch — 6+), large well-developed pith, NO secondary growth. Both have radial bundles + exarch xylem!

💡 Radial arrangement in roots = xylem and phloem on alternate radii — like spokes of different colours!

4️⃣ Dicot vs Monocot Stem and Leaf ⭐⭐

  • Dicot stem ⭐⭐: epidermis (cuticle + hairs), cortex (hypodermis of COLLENCHYMA + endodermis = 'starch sheath'), vascular bundles in a RING, conjoint, collateral, OPEN (with cambium), large pith. E.g. sunflower.
  • Monocot stem ⭐⭐: SCLERENCHYMATOUS hypodermis, vascular bundles SCATTERED + CLOSED, undifferentiated ground tissue (no separate cortex/pith!). E.g. maize. No secondary growth.
  • Dicot leaf (dorsiventral) ⭐⭐: mesophyll DIFFERENTIATED — palisade parenchyma (upper, elongated — photosynthesis factory) + spongy parenchyma (lower, loose, air spaces). More stomata below. Xylem on top in the bundle, phloem below.
  • Monocot leaf (isobilateral) ⭐: both surfaces similar; mesophyll UNDIFFERENTIATED (no palisade!); stomata equal on both sides; bulliform cells ⭐ — large empty cells on the upper epidermis (leaf rolling under water stress!).

🎯 Dicot stem = bundles in a ring (a disciplined queue); monocot = scattered (a free-for-all). Dicot leaf = two storeys; monocot = one uniform floor!

5️⃣ Secondary Growth — The Secret of Thickness ⭐⭐

  • Secondary growth: thickening of dicot stems/roots by lateral meristems (vascular cambium + cork cambium). Absent in monocots.
  • Vascular cambium ⭐⭐: fascicular + interfascicular cambium join to form a complete RING. It cuts secondary xylem inward (more!) and secondary phloem outward (less).
  • Annual rings ⭐⭐: spring wood (light, large vessels) + autumn wood (dark) = one ring = one year of the tree's age!
  • Heartwood vs sapwood ⭐⭐: heartwood (duramen) — central, DEAD, dark (tannins/resins), hard, durable (furniture!), no conduction; sapwood (alburnum) — peripheral, LIVING, light-coloured, conducts water.
  • Cork cambium (phellogen) ⭐: arises from the outer cortex; cuts cork/phellem outward (dead, suberin walls — waterproof!) and phelloderm inward. Phellem + phellogen + phelloderm = periderm. Lenticels ⭐ — small pores in the bark for gas exchange.

🎯 Heartwood = furniture wood; sapwood = working pipes; annual rings = age. All three are exam staples!

  • Monocot: polyarch, big pith, no cambium

Stem/Leaf ⭐⭐

  • Dicot stem: ring + open bundles
  • Monocot stem: scattered + closed
  • Dicot leaf: palisade+spongy; monocot: bulliform

2° Growth ⭐⭐

  • Annual ring = 1 year (spring+autumn)
  • Heartwood: dead, dark, durable
  • Lenticels: bark pores, gas exchange

NEET Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Apical, intercalary aur lateral meristems me kya farak hai?
Answer dekho

Apical = tips pe, length badhata hai; intercalary = mature regions ke beech (grass nodes); lateral = sides me, thickness (dicots me secondary growth).

Q2 Sclerenchyma parenchyma se kaise alag hai?
Answer dekho

Sclerenchyma = DEAD cells, uniform lignin thickening, sirf support; parenchyma = living, thin-walled, photosynthesis + storage.

Q3 Sieve tube elements ka sabse ajeeb fact kya hai?
Answer dekho

Ye LIVING hain par inme NUCLEUS nahi hota — unka kaam companion cells (nucleus wale) control karte hain.

Q4 Gymnosperms ki xylem/phloem me kya missing hai?
Answer dekho

Vessels (xylem me — sirf tracheids) aur companion cells (phloem me) — dono absent.

Q5 Dicot aur monocot stem ke vascular bundles me kya difference hai?
Answer dekho

Dicot: bundles RING me + OPEN (cambium) — secondary growth hoti hai; Monocot: SCATTERED + CLOSED (no cambium) — secondary growth nahi.

Q6 Dicot leaf ko 'dorsiventral' kyun kehte hain?
Answer dekho

Kyunki uski upper aur lower surfaces alag hain — mesophyll differentiated: palisade (upar) + spongy (neeche). Monocot leaf isobilateral (dono same).

Q7 Bulliform cells kya karte hain?
Answer dekho

Monocot leaf ki upper epidermis ki bade, empty, colourless cells — water stress me leaf ko roll karke water loss kam karte hain.

Q8 Annual rings se tree ki age kaise pata chalti hai?
Answer dekho

Har saal spring wood (light) + autumn wood (dark) = ek ring banta hai. Rings count karo = years.

Q9 Heartwood aur sapwood me farak batao.
Answer dekho

Heartwood: central, DEAD, dark, hard — conduction nahi karta (furniture wood!); Sapwood: peripheral, LIVING, light — water conduction karta hai.

Q10 Lenticels kya hain?
Answer dekho

Bark ke chhote pores jo secondary growth ke baad gas exchange karwate hain (cork to impervious hai, isliye ye zaroori).
