Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

MeristemsApical = length. Intercalary = regrowth (grasses). Lateral (cambium) = thickness.
Simple tissuesParenchyma (living), collenchyma (living, cellulose corners), sclerenchyma (dead, lignin).
Xylem + phloemXylem: water, mostly dead. Phloem: food, sieve tubes living without nucleus + companion cells.
RootRadial bundles, exarch xylem. Dicot 2-4 xylem, monocot polyarch with large pith.
Stem + leafDicot stem: ring, open. Monocot: scattered, closed. Dicot leaf: palisade + spongy. Monocot: bulliform cells.
Secondary growthCambium, annual rings, heartwood (dead) vs sapwood (living), periderm, lenticels.
