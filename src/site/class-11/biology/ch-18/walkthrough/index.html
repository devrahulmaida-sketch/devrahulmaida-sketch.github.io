Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

NeuronCNS = brain + cord; PNS somatic + autonomic. Dendrite IN, axon OUT. Multipolar most common. Myelinated = fast (Nodes of Ranvier).
ImpulseResting −70 mV (3 Na⁺ out, 2 K⁺ in). Na⁺ in = depolarisation (+30). K⁺ out = repolarisation. Saltatory in myelinated.
SynapseElectrical: fast, rare. Chemical: Ca²⁺ in → vesicles → neurotransmitter → receptor. One-way.
BrainForebrain: cerebrum, thalamus, hypothalamus. Midbrain: corpora quadrigemina. Hindbrain: pons, cerebellum (balance), medulla (vital).
Reflex + eyeReceptor → sensory → CNS → motor → effector. Rods dim, cones colour. Blind spot: no receptors. Fovea: sharpest.
EarOuter, middle (malleus, incus, stapes), inner. Cochlea hears. Semicircular canals + utricle/saccule balance.
