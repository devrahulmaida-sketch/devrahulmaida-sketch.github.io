Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

Cell cycleInterphase ~95% (G1, S, G2) + M phase. S: DNA 2C → 4C, chromosome number same. G0: nerve, heart. Checkpoints G1/S, G2/M.
MitosisEquational, 2n → 2n. Prophase condense, metaphase equator (best to study), anaphase centromere split, telophase 2 nuclei.
CytokinesisAnimal: cleavage furrow. Plant: cell plate. No cytokinesis = syncytium (coconut).
Prophase ILeptotene, zygotene (synapsis), pachytene (crossing over), diplotene (chiasmata), diakinesis.
MeiosisReductional I: homologues separate at anaphase I. Interkinesis: no S. Meiosis II equational. 4 haploid cells, different.
CompareMitosis: 1 division, 2 cells, 2n → 2n, identical, somatic. Meiosis: 2 divisions, 4 cells, 2n → n, variable, germ cells.
