Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Excretion Basics aur Excretory Organs ⭐

  • Excretion: metabolic nitrogenous waste + excess salts/water ko body se nikaalna. Main wastes: ammonia, urea, uric acid.
  • Nitrogenous waste types ⭐⭐: Ammonotelic — ammonia release (very toxic, LOTS of water needed — aquatic animals: bony fish, aquatic amphibians, insects!); Ureotelic ⭐ — urea release (less toxic, less water — mammals, terrestrial amphibians, marine fish!); Uricotelic ⭐ — uric acid release (least toxic, MINIMUM water — reptiles, birds, land snails, insects — water conservation ke liye!).
  • Human excretory organs ⭐: pair of kidneys (main!), ureters, urinary bladder, urethra. Skin (sweat!), lungs (CO2 + water!), liver (detox!), sebaceous glands bhi help.
  • Kidney position: abdominal cavity me, last thoracic to third lumbar vertebra level. Right kidney liver ki wajah se thoda neeche!
  • Kidney structure ⭐: outer cortex + inner medulla (medullary pyramids!) → pelvis → ureter. Functional unit = NEPHRON (~1 million per kidney!).

💡 Ammonia (aquatic, zyada paani) → urea (mammals, balanced) → uric acid (reptiles/birds, kam paani) — water availability decide karti hai!

2️⃣ Nephron Structure ⭐⭐

  • Nephron ⭐⭐: kidney ki structural + functional unit — 2 parts: Malpighian body (glomerulus + Bowman's capsule) + renal tubule.
  • Glomerulus ⭐: capillaries ka tuft — afferent arteriole (IN, wider!) se blood aati, efferent arteriole (OUT, narrower!) se jaati — high pressure se ultrafiltration!
  • Bowman's capsule ⭐: glomerulus ko wrap karti double-walled cup — filtrate yahan collect hota hai.
  • Renal tubule ⭐⭐: Bowman's capsule → PCT (Proximal Convoluted Tubule) → Henle's loop (descending + ascending limb — U-shape!) → DCT (Distal Convoluted Tubule) → collecting duct → pelvis.
  • Cortical nephrons (80-85%): chhoti Henle's loop (cortex me hi!); Juxtamedullary nephrons (15-20%): lambe loop medulla me DEEP jaata hai — concentrated urine ke liye!
  • Blood supply ⭐: afferent → glomerulus → efferent → peritubular capillaries/vasa recta (Henle's loop ke around U-shape!).

🎯 Path: glomerulus → Bowman's → PCT → Henle's loop → DCT → collecting duct. Nephron = kidney ka filter factory!

3️⃣ Urine Formation — 3 Processes ⭐⭐

  • 1. Glomerular filtration ⭐⭐: blood glomerulus me high pressure pe — water + small solutes (glucose, amino acids, salts, urea, uric acid) Bowman's capsule me filter; blood cells + proteins FILTER NAHI hote! ~125 mL/min = 180 L/day GFR (glomerular filtration rate!). Filtration membrane = 3 layers (endothelium + basement membrane + podocytes of Bowman's!).
  • 2. Reabsorption ⭐⭐: filtrate me se useful cheezin wapas blood me — PCT me ~70-80% water + electrolytes, SAARA glucose + amino acids reabsorb! Henle's loop me water (descending — water out, NaCl concentrated!) + NaCl (ascending — NaCl out, water stay!). Active + passive dono.
  • 3. Secretion ⭐: tubule cells se kuch wastes/ions (H+, K+, ammonia, drugs!) filtrate me add hote hain — blood pH + ionic balance maintain.
  • Final urine: 180 L filtrate se sirf ~1.5 L/day urine! Urea, uric acid, excess salts, water. pH acidic.
  • Summary ⭐: Filter (glomerulus) → Reabsorb (PCT + Henle) → Secrete (DCT) → Concentrate (collecting duct) → Urine!

🎯 180 L filter → 1.5 L urine! PCT = glucose+amino acids+70-80% water reabsorb. 3 processes: filter-reabsorb-secrete!

4️⃣ Concentration Mechanism + Regulation ⭐⭐

  • Counter-current mechanism ⭐⭐: Henle's loop (descending ↓ + ascending ↑ opposite flow!) + vasa recta (opposite!) — osmolarity gradient banta hai cortex (300 mOsmol) se medulla (1200 mOsmol!) tak. Ascending limb se NaCl bahar (water bahar nahi — impermeable!), descending se water bahar. Collecting duct se water reabsorb → concentrated urine (zyada solute per litre!).
  • ADH (Vasopressin) ⭐⭐: posterior pituitary se — water reabsorption BADHATA hai (DCT + collecting duct ko water-permeable!). Dehydration me ADH ↑ → concentrated kam urine. Diabetes insipidus ⭐: ADH ki kami → bahut zyada dilute urine (polyuria!).
  • JGA (Juxta Glomerular Apparatus) ⭐⭐: jab glomerular blood flow/GFR GIR jaaye → JGA cells RENIN release → angiotensinogen → angiotensin I → angiotensin II (powerful vasoconstrictor!) → BP ↑ + aldosterone (adrenal cortex se!) → Na+ + water reabsorption ↑ → GFR normal!
  • ANF (Atrial Natriuretic Factor) ⭐: jab BP ZYADA ho → heart walls se ANF → blood vessels dilate → NaCl reabsorption ↓ → BP ↓ (RAAS ka opponent!).
  • Feedback loop: Low GFR → renin → angiotensin → aldosterone → reabsorb → GFR back. High BP → ANF → opposite!

🎯 ADH = water bachao (kam urine); Renin/aldosterone = BP bachao (Na+ bachao); ANF = BP ghatao. Counter-current = concentrated urine ka raaz!

5️⃣ Micturition aur Disorders ⭐⭐

  • Micturition (urination) ⭐: bladder bharta hai → stretch receptors signal → CNS → micturition reflex — detrusor muscle (bladder wall) contract + sphincters relax → urine out. Adult me voluntary control (cerebrum!).
  • Normal urine: pale yellow, slightly acidic (pH ~6), ~1-1.5 L/day — 25-30 g urea/day. Glycosuria (urine me glucose — diabetes!) aur ketonuria (ketone bodies — diabetes mellitus!) abnormal hain.
  • Disorders ⭐⭐: Uremia — blood me urea accumulate (kidney failure — toxic!); Renal calculi (kidney stones) — calcium oxalate/uric acid crystals kidney me; Glomerulonephritis — glomerulus inflammation (Bright's disease!); Renal failure — kidneys filter nahi kar paati.
  • Dialysis ⭐⭐: artificial kidney — blood machine me jaake filter hoti hai (nitrogenous waste bahar!), wapas body me. Haemodialysis: blood externally; peritoneal: abdominal cavity me fluid exchange. Kidney transplant = permanent solution (matching donor!).
  • Other waste routes: lungs (CO2 + H2O), sweat glands (NaCl, urea, lactic acid!), sebaceous glands (sebum — sterols, waxes!), liver (bile pigments — bilirubin, cholesterol!).

🎯 Uremia = urea in blood; stones = crystals; glomerulonephritis = inflammation. Dialysis = artificial filter; transplant = permanent!

1️⃣ उत्सर्जन की मूल बातें और अंग ⭐

  • उत्सर्जन: उपापचयी नाइट्रोजनी अपशिष्ट + अतिरिक्त लवण/जल को शरीर से बाहर निकालना।
  • नाइट्रोजनी अपशिष्ट ⭐⭐: अमोनोटेलिक — अमोनिया (विषैली, बहुत जल चाहिए — जलीय जीव!); यूरिओटेलिक ⭐ — यूरिया (कम विषैली — स्तनधारी, स्थलीय उभयचर!); यूरिकोटेलिक ⭐ — यूरिक अम्ल (न्यूनतम जल — सरीसृप, पक्षी, कीट!)।
  • मानव उत्सर्जी अंग ⭐: जोड़ी वृक्क, मूत्रवाहिनी, मूत्राशय, मूत्रमार्ग। त्वचा (पसीना!), फेफड़े (CO2!), यकृत सहायक।
  • वृक्क-संरचना ⭐: बाहरी कॉर्टेक्स + आंतरिक मेडुला (पिरामिड!) → पेल्विस → मूत्रवाहिनी। कार्यात्मक इकाई = नेफ्रॉन (~10 लाख प्रति वृक्क!)।

💡 अमोनिया (जलीय) → यूरिया (स्तनधारी) → यूरिक अम्ल (सरीसृप/पक्षी) — जल-उपलब्धता निर्णायक!

2️⃣ नेफ्रॉन-संरचना ⭐⭐

  • नेफ्रॉन ⭐⭐: वृक्क की संरचनात्मक + कार्यात्मक इकाई — 2 भाग: माल्पीघी-पिंड (ग्लोमेरुलस + बोमैन-संपुट) + वृक्क-नलिका।
  • ग्लोमेरुलस ⭐: केशिकाओं का गुच्छा — आवर्त धमनिका (अंदर, चौड़ी!) से रक्त, अपवर्त धमनिका (बाहर, संकरी!) — उच्च दाब से अत्यल्पीकरण (ultrafiltration)!
  • बोमैन-संपुट ⭐: ग्लोमेरुलस को लपेटने वाला द्वि-भित्तिक कप — निस्यंद यहाँ एकत्रित।
  • वृक्क-नलिका ⭐⭐: बोमैन → PCT → हेनले-पाश (अवरोही + आरोही — U-आकार!) → DCT → संग्राहक-नलिका → पेल्विस।
  • कोर्टिकल (80-85%): छोटा पाश; जक्स्टामेडुलरी (15-20%): लंबा पाश मेडुला में गहरा — सांद्र मूत्र!

🎯 मार्ग: ग्लोमेरुलस → बोमैन → PCT → हेनले-पाश → DCT → संग्राहक-नलिका।

3️⃣ मूत्र-निर्माण — 3 प्रक्रियाएँ ⭐⭐

  • 1. ग्लोमेरुलर-निस्यंदन ⭐⭐: उच्च दाब पर जल + छोटे घुलक (ग्लूकोज़, अमीनो-अम्ल, लवण, यूरिया) बोमैन में छनते; रक्तकण + प्रोटीन नहीं! ~125 mL/मिनट = 180 L/दिन GFR।
  • 2. पुनःअवशोषण ⭐⭐: उपयोगी पदार्थ वापस रक्त में — PCT में ~70-80% जल + विद्युत-अपघट्य, सारा ग्लूकोज़ + अमीनो-अम्ल! हेनले-पाश में जल (अवरोही!) + NaCl (आरोही!)।
  • 3. स्रावण ⭐: नलिका-कोशिकाओं से अपशिष्ट/आयन (H+, K+, अमोनिया!) निस्यंद में — pH + आयनिक संतुलन।
  • अंतिम मूत्र: 180 L से केवल ~1.5 L/दिन! यूरिया, अतिरिक्त लवण, जल। अम्लीय।

🎯 180 L निस्यंद → 1.5 L मूत्र! PCT = ग्लूकोज़+अमीनो+70-80% जल पुनःअवशोषण!

4️⃣ सांद्रण-क्रिया + नियमन ⭐⭐

  • प्रतिप्रवाह-क्रियाविधि ⭐⭐: हेनले-पाश (विपरीत प्रवाह!) + वासा-रेक्टा — परासरणता-प्रवणता कॉर्टेक्स (300) से मेडुला (1200!)। आरोही से NaCl बाहर, अवरोही से जल बाहर → सांद्र मूत्र।
  • ADH (वैसोप्रेसिन) ⭐⭐: पश्च-पीयूषिका से — जल-पुनःअवशोषण बढ़ाता (DCT + संग्राहक-नलिका जल-पारगम्य!)। निर्जलीकरण में ADH ↑। मधुमेह-अस्फुटता (diabetes insipidus) ⭐: ADH की कमी → अत्यधिक तनु मूत्र!
  • JGA ⭐⭐: GFR गिरे → JGA रेनिन स्रावित → एंजियोटेंसिन II (शक्तिशाली वाहिका-संकुचक!) → एल्डोस्टेरॉन → Na+ + जल पुनःअवशोषण ↑ → GFR सामान्य!
  • ANF ⭐: BP अधिक → हृदय-भित्ति से ANF → वाहिकाएँ फैलें → BP ↓।

🎯 ADH = जल बचाओ; रेनिन/एल्डोस्टेरॉन = BP+Na+ बचाओ; ANF = BP घटाओ!

5️⃣ मूत्र-त्याग और विकार ⭐⭐

  • मूत्र-त्याग (micturition) ⭐: मूत्राशय भरे → खिंचाव-ग्राही संकेत → CNS → मूत्र-त्याग-प्रतिवर्त — देह-पेशी संकुचित + वलय-पेशी शिथिल → मूत्र बाहर।
  • सामान्य मूत्र: हल्का पीला, अम्लीय (pH ~6), ~1-1.5 L/दिन — 25-30 g यूरिया। ग्लाइकोसूरिया (मूत्र में ग्लूकोज़ — मधुमेह!) असामान्य।
  • विकार ⭐⭐: यूरीमिया — रक्त में यूरिया (वृक्क-विफलता!); वृक्क-पथरी (renal calculi) — कैल्शियम-ऑक्सैलेट क्रिस्टल; ग्लोमेरुलोनेफ्राइटिस — ग्लोमेरुलस-शोथ (ब्राइट-रोग!); वृक्क-विफलता।
  • डायलिसिस ⭐⭐: कृत्रिम वृक्क — रक्त मशीन में छनता (अपशिष्ट बाहर!), वापस शरीर में। वृक्क-प्रत्यारोपण = स्थायी समाधान।

🎯 यूरीमिया = रक्त में यूरिया; पथरी = क्रिस्टल; डायलिसिस = कृत्रिम वृक्क!

1️⃣ Excretion Basics and Organs ⭐

  • Excretion: removing metabolic nitrogenous waste + excess salts/water. Main wastes: ammonia, urea, uric acid.
  • Nitrogenous waste ⭐⭐: Ammonotelic — ammonia (very toxic, lots of water — aquatic animals!); Ureotelic ⭐ — urea (mammals, terrestrial amphibians!); Uricotelic ⭐ — uric acid (minimum water — reptiles, birds, insects!).
  • Human excretory organs ⭐: a pair of kidneys, ureters, urinary bladder, urethra. Skin, lungs, liver help.
  • Kidney structure ⭐: outer cortex + inner medulla (pyramids!) → pelvis → ureter. Functional unit = the NEPHRON (~1 million per kidney!).

💡 Ammonia (aquatic) → urea (mammals) → uric acid (reptiles/birds) — water availability decides!

2️⃣ Nephron Structure ⭐⭐

  • Nephron ⭐⭐: the kidney's structural + functional unit — two parts: Malpighian body (glomerulus + Bowman's capsule) + renal tubule.
  • Glomerulus ⭐: a capillary tuft — afferent arteriole (in, wider!) and efferent arteriole (out, narrower!) create high pressure for ultrafiltration.
  • Bowman's capsule ⭐: a double-walled cup wrapping the glomerulus — filtrate collects here.
  • Renal tubule ⭐⭐: Bowman's → PCT → Henle's loop (descending + ascending, U-shaped!) → DCT → collecting duct → pelvis.
  • Cortical (80-85%): short loop; Juxtamedullary (15-20%): long loop deep into medulla — for concentrated urine!

🎯 Path: glomerulus → Bowman's → PCT → Henle's loop → DCT → collecting duct.

3️⃣ Urine Formation — 3 Processes ⭐⭐

  • 1. Glomerular filtration ⭐⭐: high pressure pushes water + small solutes (glucose, amino acids, salts, urea) into Bowman's; blood cells and proteins DON'T filter. ~125 mL/min = 180 L/day GFR.
  • 2. Reabsorption ⭐⭐: useful substances return to blood — PCT: ~70-80% water + electrolytes, ALL glucose + amino acids! Henle's loop: water (descending) + NaCl (ascending).
  • 3. Secretion ⭐: tubule cells add wastes/ions (H+, K+, ammonia) to the filtrate — maintaining pH + ionic balance.
  • Final urine: 180 L filtrate → just ~1.5 L/day! Urea, excess salts, water. Acidic pH.

🎯 180 L filtered → 1.5 L urine! PCT = glucose + amino acids + 70-80% water reabsorbed!

4️⃣ Concentration Mechanism + Regulation ⭐⭐

  • Counter-current mechanism ⭐⭐: Henle's loop (opposite flows!) + vasa recta create an osmolarity gradient from cortex (300) to medulla (1200!). The ascending limb pumps NaCl out (water stays), descending releases water — so the collecting duct reabsorbs water → concentrated urine.
  • ADH (Vasopressin) ⭐⭐: from the posterior pituitary — increases water reabsorption (makes DCT + collecting duct water-permeable). Dehydration → more ADH. Diabetes insipidus ⭐: ADH deficiency → huge volumes of dilute urine!
  • JGA ⭐⭐: when GFR falls, JGA releases RENIN → angiotensin II (vasoconstrictor!) → aldosterone → more Na+ + water reabsorption → GFR normal.
  • ANF ⭐: when BP is too high, the heart releases ANF → vessels dilate → BP falls.

🎯 ADH = save water; renin/aldosterone = save BP + Na+; ANF = lower BP!

5️⃣ Micturition and Disorders ⭐⭐

  • Micturition ⭐: the bladder fills → stretch receptors signal the CNS → the micturition reflex — bladder wall contracts + sphincters relax → urine out.
  • Normal urine: pale yellow, acidic (pH ~6), ~1-1.5 L/day. Glycosuria (glucose in urine — diabetes!) is abnormal.
  • Disorders ⭐⭐: Uremia — urea accumulates in blood (kidney failure!); Renal calculi (kidney stones) — calcium oxalate crystals; Glomerulonephritis — glomerulus inflammation (Bright's disease!); renal failure.
  • Dialysis ⭐⭐: an artificial kidney — blood is filtered by machine and returned. Kidney transplant = the permanent solution.

🎯 Uremia = urea in blood; stones = crystals; dialysis = artificial kidney!

NEET Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Ammonotelic, ureotelic aur uricotelic animals me farak batao.
Answer dekho

Ammonotelic = ammonia release (aquatic — zyada water!); ureotelic = urea (mammals — kam toxic!); uricotelic = uric acid (reptiles/birds — minimum water, paste/pellets!).

Q2 Nephron ke 2 main parts batao.
Answer dekho

Malpighian body (glomerulus + Bowman's capsule — filtration!) aur renal tubule (PCT → Henle's loop → DCT → collecting duct — reabsorption+secretion!).

Q3 Glomerular filtration me kya filter hota hai aur kya nahi?
Answer dekho

Water + small solutes (glucose, amino acids, salts, urea) filter; blood cells aur proteins FILTER NAHI hote. ~180 L/day filtrate (GFR 125 mL/min).

Q4 PCT me kya reabsorb hota hai?
Answer dekho

SAARA glucose + amino acids aur ~70-80% water + electrolytes — PCT sabse zyada reabsorption karta hai!

Q5 Counter-current mechanism kaise concentrated urine banata hai?
Answer dekho

Henle's loop ke opposite flows + vasa recta se osmolarity gradient (cortex 300 → medulla 1200!) — ascending limb se NaCl bahar, descending se water bahar → collecting duct se water reabsorb → concentrated urine!

Q6 ADH ka role kya hai?
Answer dekho

Water reabsorption BADHATA hai (DCT + collecting duct ko water-permeable!) — dehydration me ADH ↑ → concentrated kam urine. ADH ki kami = diabetes insipidus (bahut dilute urine!).

Q7 JGA kab renin release karta hai?
Answer dekho

Jab glomerular blood flow/GFR GIR jaaye → JGA se renin → angiotensin II (vasoconstrictor!) → aldosterone → Na+ + water reabsorption ↑ → GFR wapas normal!

Q8 Glycosuria kya hai?
Answer dekho

Urine me glucose ka aana — normally saara glucose PCT me reabsorb hota hai; yeh diabetes mellitus ka sign hai (blood sugar bahut high!).

Q9 Uremia aur renal calculi me farak batao.
Answer dekho

Uremia = blood me urea accumulate (kidney failure — toxic!); renal calculi = kidney me calcium oxalate/uric acid crystals (stones) — bahut painful!

Q10 Dialysis kya hai aur kab chahiye?
Answer dekho

Artificial kidney — jab kidneys filter nahi kar paati (renal failure!), blood machine me filter hoti hai (waste bahar!) aur wapas body me. Kidney transplant = permanent solution.
