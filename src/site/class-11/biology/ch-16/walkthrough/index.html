Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

WastesAmmonotelic (bony fish), ureotelic (mammals), uricotelic (reptiles, birds). Ammonia needs most water, uric acid least.
NephronMalpighian body + tubule. Afferent wider, efferent narrower. Bowman's, PCT, Henle's loop, DCT, collecting duct. Cortical 80-85%.
UrineFiltration (GFR ~125 mL/min, 180 L/day), reabsorption (PCT all glucose), secretion. Only ~1.5 L/day urine.
ConcentrationCounter-current in loop + vasa recta. Gradient 300 to 1200 mOsmol. Ascending limb: NaCl out, no water.
RegulationADH: water reabsorption up. Low GFR: renin, angiotensin II, aldosterone. ANF lowers BP.
DisordersMicturition reflex. Glycosuria, ketonuria. Uremia, renal calculi, glomerulonephritis. Dialysis or transplant.
