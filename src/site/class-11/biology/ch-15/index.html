Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Blood — Composition aur Functions ⭐

  • Blood: special connective tissue — plasma (~55%) + formed elements (~45%). Plasma me water (~90-92%), proteins (fibrinogen, globulins, albumins — clotting, defence, osmotic balance!), ions, nutrients, gases, hormones.
  • RBC (erythrocytes) ⭐⭐: ~5-5.5 million/mm³ — biconcave, no nucleus (mammals me!), haemoglobin se O2 carry. Life span ~120 days; bone marrow me bante, spleen me destroy (graveyard of RBCs!).
  • WBC (leucocytes) ⭐⭐: ~6000-8000/mm³ — granulocytes (neutrophils 60-65% — phagocytosis; eosinophils 2-3% — allergies/parasites; basophils 0.5-1% — histamine/serotonin — inflammation!) + agranulocytes (lymphocytes 20-25% — immunity B/T cells!; monocytes 6-8% — phagocytosis — largest WBC!).
  • Platelets (thrombocytes): 1.5-3.5 lakh/mm³ — bone marrow ke megakaryocytes ke fragments — clotting me.
  • Serum ⭐: plasma MINUS clotting factors (fibrinogen) = serum!

💡 RBC = O2 truck (5M, no nucleus); WBC = army (neutrophils max!); platelets = repair crew. Serum = plasma - fibrinogen!

2️⃣ Blood Groups aur Coagulation ⭐⭐

  • ABO grouping ⭐⭐: A group — A antigens, anti-B antibodies; B group — B antigens, anti-A; AB group — A+B antigens, NO antibodies (universal recipient!); O group — NO antigens, anti-A+anti-B antibodies (universal donor!).
  • Rh factor ⭐⭐: Rh antigen ho to Rh+ (~80% humans!), na ho to Rh-. Erythroblastosis foetalis ⭐⭐: Rh- maa + Rh+ foetus — pehli pregnancy safe, but delivery pe maa ke body me Rh antibodies ban jaati hain → doosri Rh+ pregnancy me ye antibodies foetal RBCs destroy karti hain (severe anaemia/jaundice — fatal!).
  • Clotting mechanism ⭐⭐: injury → platelets + injured tissue se thromboplastin → (Ca2+ ke saath) prothrombin → thrombin (thrombokinase enzyme!) → fibrinogen → fibrin (mesh!) → blood cells trap = CLOT.
  • Anticoagulants: heparin (natural, basophils se!) — clotting rokta hai.
  • Compatibility: O- = universal donor; AB+ = universal recipient (practically cross-match zaroori!).

🎯 AB+ = recipient (no antibodies), O- = donor (no antigens). Erythroblastosis = Rh- maa + doosra Rh+ baby — NEET ka classic!

3️⃣ Heart Structure aur Cardiac Cycle ⭐⭐

  • Heart ⭐⭐: 4 chambers — 2 atria (upper, receive!) + 2 ventricles (lower, pump out!). Atria-ventricles ke beech AV valves (tricuspid right, bicuspid/mitral left!) — backflow rokte hain. Ventricles-arteries pe semilunar valves.
  • Conducting system ⭐⭐: SAN (sino-atrial node) — right atrium me, 'pacemaker' (60-70/min impulses khud banata hai!) → AVN (AV node) → AV bundle (Bundle of His) → Purkinje fibres (ventricular walls me spread!).
  • Cardiac cycle ⭐⭐: 0.8 sec = joint diastole (0.4s — sab relax, blood in!) → atrial systole (0.1s — atria contract, ventricles fill!) → ventricular systole (0.3s — ventricles pump, semilunar open, blood to arteries!) → diastole. Heart sounds ⭐: 'lubb' (S1 — AV valves CLOSE, ventricular systole start!) + 'dub' (S2 — semilunar valves CLOSE, ventricular diastole!).
  • Stroke volume × heart rate = cardiac output (~70 mL × 72 = ~5000 mL/min!).
  • ECG ⭐: P wave = atrial depolarisation (systole!); QRS complex = ventricular depolarisation (systole start!); T wave = ventricular repolarisation (relaxation!).

🎯 SAN = pacemaker. Lubb = AV valves band; dub = semilunar band. P=atrial, QRS=ventricular, T=relax. Cardiac cycle 0.8 sec!

4️⃣ Double Circulation aur Circulatory Pathways ⭐⭐

  • Double circulation ⭐⭐: mammals/birds me — pulmonary circuit (heart → lungs → heart: RIGHT ventricle → pulmonary artery → lungs → oxygenated → pulmonary vein → LEFT atrium!) + systemic circuit (heart → body → heart: LEFT ventricle → aorta → body → deoxygenated → vena cava → RIGHT atrium!). Oxygenated/deoxygenated blood MIX nahi hoti!
  • Fish me single circulation ⭐: sirf 2 chambers (1 auricle + 1 ventricle) — heart se gills, gills se body (deoxygenated hi heart me!).
  • Amphibians/reptiles (except crocodiles): 3 chambers (2 auricles + 1 ventricle) — mixing hoti hai!
  • Portal systems ⭐⭐: Hepatic portal vein — digestive tract ka blood liver me pehle (detox + nutrient processing!) phir heart. Renal portal — amphibians/fish me.
  • Lymph ⭐: colourless fluid (blood se filter) — tissue fluid → lymph capillaries → lymph nodes (filter + lymphocytes!) → back to blood. Functions: fat absorption (lacteals!), immunity, fluid balance.

🎯 Pulmonary = heart-lung; systemic = heart-body. Fish = 2ch single; amphibians = 3ch mixing; mammals = 4ch double clean!

5️⃣ Circulatory Disorders ⭐⭐

  • Hypertension (high BP) ⭐⭐: normal 120/80 mm Hg (systolic/diastolic!); persistently 140/90+ = hypertension — heart, brain, kidneys damage (silent killer!).
  • Coronary artery disease (CAD) ⭐⭐: arteries me atherosclerosis — cholesterol/Ca deposits (plaque!) → lumen narrow → less blood to heart muscle.
  • Angina pectoris ⭐: chest pain — heart muscle ko O2 kam (CAD symptom!).
  • Myocardial infarction (heart attack) ⭐⭐: heart muscle ka part suddenly blood/O2 se vanchit — tissue dies (emergency!).
  • Heart failure ⭐: heart itna pump nahi kar paata jitna body ko chahiye — congestion (lungs/body me blood jama!). 'Cardiac arrest' (heart stops!) se ALAG.

💡 HTN = 140/90+; CAD = plaque; angina = chest pain; MI = heart attack (tissue death); failure = weak pump. Confuse mat karo!

1️⃣ रक्त — संरचना और कार्य ⭐

  • रक्त: विशेष संयोजी ऊतक — प्लाज़्मा (~55%) + रक्तकणिकाएँ (~45%)। प्लाज़्मा में जल, प्रोटीन (फ़ाइब्रिनोजन, ग्लोबुलिन, एल्ब्यूमिन!), आयन, पोषक।
  • लाल रक्तकण (RBC) ⭐⭐: ~5-5.5 मिलियन/मिमी³ — द्वि-अवतल, केंद्रक रहित (स्तनधारियों में!), हीमोग्लोबिन से O2। जीवन-काल 120 दिन; अस्थि-मज्जा में निर्माण, प्लीहा में विनाश!
  • श्वेत रक्तकण (WBC) ⭐⭐: ~6000-8000/मिमी³ — कणिकामय (न्यूट्रोफ़िल 60-65% — भक्षण; इओसिनोफ़िल — एलर्जी; बेसोफ़िल — हिस्टैमिन!) + अकणिकामय (लसीकाणु — प्रतिरक्षा; मोनोसाइट — सबसे बड़े!)।
  • प्लेटलेट्स: 1.5-3.5 लाख/मिमी³ — स्कंदन में।
  • सीरम ⭐: प्लाज़्मा − स्कंदन-कारक (फ़ाइब्रिनोजन)!

💡 RBC = O2 वाहक; WBC = सेना (न्यूट्रोफ़िल अधिकतम!); सीरम = प्लाज़्मा − फ़ाइब्रिनोजन!

2️⃣ रक्त-वर्ग और स्कंदन ⭐⭐

  • ABO वर्गीकरण ⭐⭐: A — A प्रतिजन, anti-B; B — B प्रतिजन, anti-A; AB — A+B प्रतिजन, कोई प्रतिरक्षी नहीं (सर्वग्राही!); O — कोई प्रतिजन नहीं, दोनों प्रतिरक्षी (सर्वदाता!)।
  • Rh कारक ⭐⭐: Rh प्रतिजन हो तो Rh+, नहीं तो Rh-। एरिथ्रोब्लास्टोसिस फ़ीटेलिस ⭐⭐: Rh- माता + Rh+ भ्रूण — दूसरी Rh+ गर्भावस्था में माता की प्रतिरक्षियाँ भ्रूण की RBC नष्ट करती हैं (घातक!)।
  • स्कंदन-क्रिया ⭐⭐: चोट → थ्रोम्बोप्लास्टिन → (Ca2+ के साथ) प्रोथ्रोम्बिन → थ्रोम्बिन → फ़ाइब्रिनोजन → फ़ाइब्रिन (जाल!) → थक्का।
  • प्रतिस्कंदक: हेपरिन (प्राकृतिक!)।

🎯 AB+ = ग्राही, O- = दाता। एरिथ्रोब्लास्टोसिस = Rh- माता + दूसरा Rh+ शिशु!

3️⃣ हृदय-संरचना और हृदय-चक्र ⭐⭐

  • हृदय ⭐⭐: 4 कक्ष — 2 अलिंद (ग्रहण!) + 2 निलय (पंप!)। AV कपाट (त्रिवलयी दायाँ, द्विवलयी बायाँ!) — प्रतिप्रवाह-रोक। धमनी-मुख पर अर्धचंद्राकार कपाट।
  • चालन-तंत्र ⭐⭐: SAN (साइनो-एट्रियल नोड) — दायाँ अलिंद, 'पेसमेकर' (स्वयं आवेग!) → AVN → हिस-बंडल → पर्किंजे-तंतु।
  • हृदय-चक्र ⭐⭐: 0.8 सेकंड = संयुक्त अनुशिथिलन (0.4s) → अलिंद-संकुचन (0.1s) → निलय-संकुचन (0.3s)। हृदय-ध्वनियाँ ⭐: 'लब' (AV कपाट बंद!) + 'डब' (अर्धचंद्राकार कपाट बंद!)।
  • हृदय-निर्गमन: स्ट्रोक-आयतन × हृदय-दर (~70 × 72 = ~5 ली/मिनट!)।
  • ECG ⭐: P = अलिंद-विभवहरण; QRS = निलय-विभवहरण; T = निलय-पुनर्ध्रुवण।

🎯 SAN = पेसमेकर। लब = AV बंद; डब = अर्धचंद्राकार बंद। चक्र 0.8 सेकंड!

4️⃣ द्वि-परिसंचरण और पथ ⭐⭐

  • द्वि-परिसंचरण ⭐⭐: स्तनधारियों/पक्षियों में — फुफ्फुसीय (हृदय → फेफड़े → हृदय: दायाँ निलय → फुफ्फुस-धमनी → फेफड़े → फुफ्फुस-शिरा → बायाँ अलिंद!) + तंत्रिका (systemic) (हृदय → शरीर → हृदय: बायाँ निलय → महाधमनी → शरीर → महाशिरा → दायाँ अलिंद!)। मिश्रण नहीं!
  • मछली में एकल परिसंचरण ⭐: 2 कक्ष (1 अलिंद + 1 निलय) — केवल अनॉक्सीजनीकृत रक्त हृदय में!
  • उभयचर/सरीसृप (मगरमच्छ छोड़): 3 कक्ष (2 अलिंद + 1 निलय) — मिश्रण!
  • पोर्टल-तंत्र ⭐⭐: यकृत-पोर्टल शिरा — आहार-नाल का रक्त पहले यकृत में (विषहरण + पोषक-प्रसंस्करण!)।
  • लसीका ⭐: रंगहीन तरल — वसा-अवशोषण + प्रतिरक्षा + तरल-संतुलन।

🎯 मछली 2कक्ष, उभयचर 3कक्ष, स्तनधारी 4कक्ष (मिश्रण-रहित)। यकृत-पोर्टल = आंत→यकृत→हृदय!

5️⃣ परिसंचरण-विकार ⭐⭐

  • उच्च रक्तचाप ⭐⭐: सामान्य 120/80; सतत 140/90+ = उच्च रक्तचाप — हृदय, मस्तिष्क, वृक्क क्षति (मूक-घातक!)।
  • हृदयी धमनी-रोग (CAD) ⭐⭐: एथेरोस्क्लेरोसिस — कोलेस्ट्रॉल/Ca जमाव (प्लाक!) → धमनी संकरी → हृदय-पेशी को रक्त कम।
  • हृद्शूल (angina) ⭐: सीने में दर्द — हृदय-पेशी को O2 कम!
  • हृद्याघात (heart attack) ⭐⭐: हृदय-पेशी का भाग अचानक रक्त से वंचित — ऊतक-मृत्यु (आपातकाल!)।
  • हृदय-विफलता ⭐: हृदय पर्याप्त पंप नहीं कर पाता — रक्त-जमाव। 'कार्डिएक अरेस्ट' (हृदय-स्थगन!) से भिन्न!

💡 उच्चचाप = 140/90+; CAD = प्लाक; हृद्शूल = दर्द; हृद्याघात = ऊतक-मृत्यु!

1️⃣ Blood — Composition and Functions ⭐

  • Blood: a special connective tissue — plasma (~55%) + formed elements (~45%). Plasma holds water, proteins (fibrinogen, globulins, albumins!), ions, nutrients.
  • RBC (erythrocytes) ⭐⭐: ~5-5.5 million/mm³ — biconcave, no nucleus (in mammals!), carry O2 via haemoglobin. Live 120 days; made in bone marrow, destroyed in the spleen!
  • WBC (leucocytes) ⭐⭐: ~6000-8000/mm³ — granulocytes (neutrophils 60-65% — phagocytosis; eosinophils — allergies; basophils — histamine!) + agranulocytes (lymphocytes — immunity; monocytes — the largest!).
  • Platelets: 1.5-3.5 lakh/mm³ — clotting.
  • Serum ⭐: plasma MINUS clotting factors (fibrinogen)!

💡 RBC = O2 carrier; WBC = the army (neutrophils most!); serum = plasma - fibrinogen!

2️⃣ Blood Groups and Coagulation ⭐⭐

  • ABO grouping ⭐⭐: A — A antigens, anti-B antibodies; B — B antigens, anti-A; AB — both antigens, no antibodies (universal recipient!); O — no antigens, both antibodies (universal donor!).
  • Rh factor ⭐⭐: Rh antigen present = Rh+, absent = Rh-. Erythroblastosis foetalis ⭐⭐: Rh- mother + Rh+ foetus — in the SECOND Rh+ pregnancy her antibodies destroy the foetal RBCs (fatal!).
  • Clotting ⭐⭐: injury → thromboplastin → (with Ca2+) prothrombin → thrombin → fibrinogen → fibrin mesh → clot.
  • Anticoagulant: heparin (natural!).

🎯 AB+ = recipient, O- = donor. Erythroblastosis = Rh- mother + second Rh+ baby — a NEET classic!

3️⃣ Heart Structure and Cardiac Cycle ⭐⭐

  • Heart ⭐⭐: 4 chambers — 2 atria (receive!) + 2 ventricles (pump!). AV valves (tricuspid right, bicuspid left!) stop backflow; semilunar valves at the artery exits.
  • Conducting system ⭐⭐: SAN (sino-atrial node) — the 'pacemaker' (makes its own impulses!) → AVN → Bundle of His → Purkinje fibres.
  • Cardiac cycle ⭐⭐: 0.8 sec = joint diastole (0.4s) → atrial systole (0.1s) → ventricular systole (0.3s). Heart sounds ⭐: 'lubb' (AV valves close!) + 'dub' (semilunar valves close!).
  • Cardiac output: stroke volume × heart rate (~70 × 72 = ~5 L/min!).
  • ECG ⭐: P = atrial depolarisation; QRS = ventricular depolarisation; T = ventricular repolarisation.

🎯 SAN = pacemaker. Lubb = AV closes; dub = semilunar closes. Cycle = 0.8 sec!

4️⃣ Double Circulation and Pathways ⭐⭐

  • Double circulation ⭐⭐: in mammals/birds — pulmonary (heart → lungs → heart: right ventricle → pulmonary artery → lungs → pulmonary vein → left atrium!) + systemic (heart → body → heart: left ventricle → aorta → body → vena cava → right atrium!). No mixing!
  • Fish — single circulation ⭐: 2 chambers (1 auricle + 1 ventricle) — only deoxygenated blood in the heart!
  • Amphibians/reptiles (except crocodiles): 3 chambers (2 auricles + 1 ventricle) — mixing occurs!
  • Portal systems ⭐⭐: the hepatic portal vein sends gut blood to the liver first (detox + processing!).
  • Lymph ⭐: colourless fluid — fat absorption + immunity + fluid balance.

🎯 Fish 2ch, amphibians 3ch, mammals 4ch (clean, no mixing). Hepatic portal = gut → liver → heart!

5️⃣ Circulatory Disorders ⭐⭐

  • Hypertension ⭐⭐: normal 120/80; persistent 140/90+ = hypertension — damages heart, brain, kidneys (the silent killer!).
  • Coronary artery disease (CAD) ⭐⭐: atherosclerosis — cholesterol/Ca plaque narrows arteries → less blood to heart muscle.
  • Angina pectoris ⭐: chest pain — heart muscle starved of O2!
  • Myocardial infarction (heart attack) ⭐⭐: a part of heart muscle suddenly loses blood supply — tissue dies (emergency!).
  • Heart failure ⭐: the heart can't pump enough — congestion. Different from cardiac arrest (heart stops!).

💡 HTN = 140/90+; CAD = plaque; angina = chest pain; MI = heart attack (tissue death)!

NEET Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Serum aur plasma me kya farak hai?
Answer dekho

Serum = plasma MINUS clotting factors (fibrinogen!) — jab blood clot ho jaata hai, jo bachta hai woh serum hai.

Q2 AB+ ko universal recipient kyun kehte hain?
Answer dekho

Kyunki AB group me donon antigens (A+B) hain aur KOI antibodies nahi — to A, B, AB, O sabki blood le sakte hain (antibody attack nahi!).

Q3 Erythroblastosis foetalis kya hai?
Answer dekho

Rh- maa + Rh+ foetus — pehli delivery pe maa me Rh antibodies ban jaati hain; doosri Rh+ pregnancy me ye antibodies foetal RBCs destroy karti hain — severe anaemia, fatal ho sakta hai!

Q4 SAN ko pacemaker kyun kehte hain?
Answer dekho

Kyunki SAN (right atrium me) khud 60-70/min impulses generate karta hai — heart ka natural rhythm yahin set hota hai! Isliye 'pacemaker'.

Q5 Heart ke 'lubb' aur 'dub' sounds kis se bante hain?
Answer dekho

Lubb (S1) = AV valves ka CLOSE hona (ventricular systole shuru!); dub (S2) = semilunar valves ka CLOSE hona (ventricular diastole!).

Q6 Double circulation kya hai?
Answer dekho

Pulmonary circuit (heart → lungs → heart — oxygenation!) + systemic circuit (heart → body → heart) — dono ALAG, oxygenated/deoxygenated blood mix nahi hoti!

Q7 Fish aur mammals ke hearts me farak batao.
Answer dekho

Fish: 2 chambers (single circulation — sirf deoxygenated blood heart me!); mammals: 4 chambers (double circulation — mixing nahi, efficient O2 supply!).

Q8 Hepatic portal system kya karta hai?
Answer dekho

Digestive tract ka blood pehle LIVER me le jaata hai — nutrients process + toxins detox — phir heart. 'Ek organ se doosre organ' wala portal!

Q9 Hypertension ki BP limit kya hai?
Answer dekho

Normal 120/80 mm Hg; persistently 140/90 ya usse zyada = hypertension — heart, brain, kidneys ko damage (silent killer!).

Q10 Heart attack aur heart failure me farak batao.
Answer dekho

Heart attack (MI) = heart muscle ka part suddenly blood supply kho deta hai — tissue dies (emergency!); heart failure = heart par adequate pump nahi kar paata — gradual congestion.
