Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

BloodPlasma 55% + cells 45%. RBC 5M, no nucleus, 120 days. Neutrophils most, monocyte largest. Serum = plasma − fibrinogen.
GroupsAB = universal recipient, O = universal donor. Rh− mother + 2nd Rh+ baby = erythroblastosis foetalis.
ClottingThromboplastin + Ca²⁺ → prothrombin → thrombin → fibrinogen → fibrin clot. Heparin stops it.
Heart4 chambers. Tricuspid right, bicuspid left. SAN → AVN → Bundle of His → Purkinje. Lubb = AV close, dub = semilunar close.
Cycle + ECG0.8 s cycle. P atrial, QRS ventricular depol., T repol. CO = SV × HR = ~5000 mL/min.
Circulation + disordersPulmonary + systemic, no mixing. Fish 2, amphibian 3, mammal 4 chambers. BP 120/80; HTN 140/90+; CAD plaque; MI tissue death.
