Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

LivingDefining: metabolism, cellular organisation, consciousness. Growth and reproduction are not absolute (mule).
NamingBinomial: Genus + species, by Linnaeus. Latin, italics, Genus capital, species small. ICN plants, ICZN animals.
TermsIdentification, nomenclature, classification. Taxonomy covers all three. Systematics adds phylogeny. Taxon = any level.
HierarchyKingdom, Phylum (Division in plants), Class, Order, Family, Genus, Species. King Philip Came Over For Good Soup.
CategoriesSpecies = interbreed, fertile. Family -aceae plants, -idae animals. Order -ales plants.
AidsHerbarium, botanical garden, museum, zoo, key (couplet, lead, analytical), flora and manuals.
