Respiration in Plants

Glycolysis se ETC tak — energy ka ATP bank

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Respiration Basics aur Glycolysis ⭐⭐

  • Respiration: food (glucose) ko todkar energy (ATP) nikalna — breathing (gas exchange) se alag! Cellular respiration mitochondria + cytoplasm me hoti hai. Equation: C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + energy (ATP).
  • Types: Aerobic (oxygen ke saath — complete breakdown, zyada energy) aur Anaerobic (oxygen ke bina — partial breakdown, kam energy).
  • Glycolysis ⭐⭐ (EMP pathway — Embden-Meyerhof-Parnas): glucose (6C) ka 2 pyruvate (3C) me breakdown. Site = CYTOPLASM ⭐ (mitochondria nahi!) — saare living organisms me same! Oxygen ki zaroorat NAHI is step me.
  • Glycolysis steps ⭐: glucose → glucose-6-phosphate (1 ATP use) → fructose-6-phosphate → fructose-1,6-bisphosphate (1 aur ATP use) → 2 × 3-carbon sugars (PGAL) → ... → 2 pyruvate. Total: 4 ATP bane, 2 use hue = NET 2 ATP ⭐⭐ + 2 NADH bane (electron carriers!).
  • Key enzymes ⭐: hexokinase (glucose → G6P), PFK (phosphofructokinase — rate-limiting!), pyruvate kinase.
  • Glycolysis universal kyun?: bacteria se humans tak sab me same — evolution ka sabse purana energy pathway!

🎯 Glycolysis = cytoplasm me, NET 2 ATP + 2 NADH, oxygen-free. 4 banaye 2 kharch = net 2!

2️⃣ Fermentation — Anaerobic Rasta ⭐⭐

  • Fermentation ⭐: jab oxygen na mile — pyruvate ka incomplete breakdown (anaerobic). Sirf glycolysis wale NET 2 ATP milte hain — bahut kam!
  • Alcoholic fermentation ⭐⭐: yeast (aur kuch bacteria) me — pyruvate → ethanol + CO2. Enzymes: pyruvic acid decarboxylase + alcohol dehydrogenase. Uses: bread (CO2 se dough phulti hai ⭐), beer/wine (ethanol!).
  • Lactic acid fermentation ⭐⭐: muscles me (jab oxygen supply kam pade — intense exercise!) + Lactobacillus bacteria (curd!) — pyruvate → lactic acid. Muscle cramps/dard ka culprit = lactic acid jama hona!
  • Fermentation ki problems ⭐: (1) bahut kam energy (sirf 2 ATP), (2) products toxic hain (alcohol/acid — 13% alcohol pe yeast khud mar jaata hai — isliye natural wine 13% se strong nahi!), (3) NADH wapas NAD+ banana padta hai taaki glycolysis chalti rahe.
  • Anaerobic respiration vs fermentation: dono oxygen-free, par anaerobic respiration (kuch bacteria) me ETC chalta hai (final acceptor = sulphate/nitrate — O2 nahi) — NCERT level pe dono ~same treat karte hain.

💡 Yeast = alcohol factory (bread+beer), muscles = lactic acid (cramps!). Dono sirf 2 ATP — jugaad mode, permanent solution nahi!

3️⃣ Krebs Cycle — Mitochondria Ka Chakra ⭐⭐

  • Link reaction (gateway step) ⭐⭐: pyruvate cytoplasm se mitochondrial matrix me jaake acetyl CoA (2C) banta hai — CO2 + NADH release! Enzyme complex = pyruvate dehydrogenase. Reaction: pyruvate + CoA + NAD+ → acetyl CoA + CO2 + NADH.
  • Krebs cycle (TCA/citric acid cycle) ⭐⭐: site = mitochondrial matrix ⭐. Acetyl CoA (2C) + OAA (oxaloacetate, 4C) → citrate (6C) — cycle start! Phir series of steps me citrate tootta jaata hai: 2 CO2 nikalte hain, 3 NADH + 1 FADH2 + 1 ATP (GTP) bante hain per cycle (1 acetyl CoA), OAA regenerate hota hai!
  • Yield per glucose ⭐⭐: 1 glucose = 2 pyruvate = 2 acetyl CoA = 2 Krebs turns → total 6 NADH + 2 FADH2 + 2 ATP (Krebs se) + link reaction se 2 NADH.
  • Important intermediates ⭐: citrate (6C), α-ketoglutarate (5C), succinyl CoA (4C), succinate, fumarate, malate, OAA (4C). Decarboxylation 2 jagah (isocitrate → α-KG, α-KG → succinyl CoA) — CO2 wahi se!
  • Amphibolic pathway ⭐: Krebs cycle respiratory (catabolic) bhi hai aur biosynthesis (anabolic) ke liye intermediates deta hai — isliye 'amphibolic'!

🎯 Krebs = matrix me; per glucose: 6 NADH + 2 FADH2 + 2 ATP (cycle) + 2 NADH (link). OAA = cycle ka driver jo kabhi khatam nahi hota!

4️⃣ ETC aur Oxidative Phosphorylation — Asli ATP Factory ⭐⭐

  • Electron Transport Chain (ETC) ⭐⭐: site = inner mitochondrial membrane (cristae) ⭐. NADH aur FADH2 ke high-energy electrons yahan protein complexes (I, II, III, IV) se pass hote hain → oxygen final electron acceptor ⭐⭐ (isliye 'aerobic'!) → water banta hai.
  • Path: NADH → Complex I → ubiquinone (Q) → Complex III → cytochrome c → Complex IV → O2. FADH2 Complex II se enter karta hai (Complex I skip! — isliye kam ATP ⭐).
  • Proton pumping ⭐: electrons flow karte hue H+ matrix se intermembrane space me pump hote hain → gradient banta hai → H+ wapas ATP synthase (Complex V) se flow → ATP banta hai = oxidative phosphorylation / chemiosmosis ⭐⭐ (Peter Mitchell ka theory — Nobel!).
  • ATP values ⭐⭐: 1 NADH = 3 ATP (new books me 2.5, par NCERT 3!), 1 FADH2 = 2 ATP.
  • Oxygen ka role ⭐: final electron acceptor — O2 na ho to poora ETC jam! (Cyanide jaise poisons Complex IV block karte hain — deadly!)

💡 ETC = water slide: electrons upar se neeche, unki energy se protons pump, proton waterfall se ATP turbine ghoomta hai!

5️⃣ Total ATP Count aur RQ ⭐⭐

  • Total ATP per glucose (aerobic) ⭐⭐: Glycolysis: 2 ATP (net) + 2 NADH; Link reaction: 2 NADH; Krebs: 6 NADH + 2 FADH2 + 2 ATP. Count karo: 2 (glycolysis net) + 2 (Krebs GTP) + (2+2+6) NADH × 3 + 2 FADH2 × 2 = 4 + 30 + 4 = 38 ATP ⭐⭐ (NCERT value — old scheme me! New scheme me 30-32, par exam me 38 hi puchte hain).
  • Efficiency ⭐: ~40% energy capture — baaki heat (warm-blooded animals ke liye useful!).
  • Respiratory Quotient (RQ) ⭐⭐: RQ = CO2 released / O2 consumed. Carbohydrates: RQ = 1 (glucose: 6CO2/6O2); Fats: RQ < 1 (~0.7) — fats me oxygen kam hota hai, to zyada O2 chahiye (tripalmitin: 102/145 ≈ 0.7); Proteins: ~0.9.
  • RQ batata hai ⭐: kaunsa fuel burn ho raha hai! Mixed diet me RQ ~0.85.
  • Plants me respiration: photosynthesis reverse nahi hai! Respiration 24×7 chalti hai (photosynthesis sirf light me); plants ko bhi ATP chahiye — roots, seeds, fruits sab respire karte hain.

🎯 38 ATP = NCERT answer (carbohydrate, complete oxidation). RQ: carb = 1, fat = 0.7, protein = 0.9 — classic MCQ!

1️⃣ श्वसन की मूल बातें और ग्लाइकोलाइसिस ⭐⭐

  • श्वसन: भोजन (ग्लूकोज़) तोड़कर ऊर्जा (ATP) — समीकरण: C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + ऊर्जा।
  • प्रकार: वायवीय (O2 सहित — पूर्ण, अधिक ऊर्जा) और अवायवीय (O2 बिना — अपूर्ण, कम ऊर्जा)।
  • ग्लाइकोलाइसिस ⭐⭐ (EMP मार्ग): ग्लूकोज़ (6C) → 2 पाइरुवेट (3C)। स्थल = कोशिकाद्रव्य ⭐ — सभी जीवों में समान! O2 आवश्यक नहीं।
  • चरण ⭐: ग्लूकोज़ → G6P (1 ATP) → F6P → F1,6BP (1 ATP) → 2 × 3C → 2 पाइरुवेट। कुल: 4 ATP बने, 2 खर्च = शुद्ध 2 ATP ⭐⭐ + 2 NADH।
  • प्रमुख एंज़ाइम ⭐: हेक्सोकाइनेज़, PFK (दर-सीमित!), पाइरुवेट काइनेज़।

🎯 ग्लाइकोलाइसिस = कोशिकाद्रव्य, शुद्ध 2 ATP + 2 NADH, O2-मुक्त!

2️⃣ किण्वन — अवायवीय मार्ग ⭐⭐

  • किण्वन ⭐: O2 न मिले तो पाइरुवेट का अपूर्ण अपघटन — केवल शुद्ध 2 ATP!
  • ऐल्कोहॉलिक किण्वन ⭐⭐: यीस्ट में — पाइरुवेट → एथेनॉल + CO2। उपयोग: ब्रेड (CO2 से सूजन ⭐), बीयर/वाइन।
  • लैक्टिक अम्ल किण्वन ⭐⭐: पेशियों में (तीव्र व्यायाम!) + लैक्टोबैसिलस (दही!) — पाइरुवेट → लैक्टिक अम्ल। माँसपेशी-ऐंठन का कारण!
  • समस्याएँ ⭐: (1) बहुत कम ऊर्जा (2 ATP), (2) उत्पाद विषाक्त (13% ऐल्कोहॉल पर यीस्ट स्वयं मर जाता है!), (3) NADH → NAD+ आवश्यक।

💡 यीस्ट = ऐल्कोहॉल फ़ैक्ट्री; पेशियाँ = लैक्टिक अम्ल। दोनों केवल 2 ATP — जुगाड़ मोड!

3️⃣ क्रेब्स चक्र — माइटोकॉन्ड्रिया का चक्र ⭐⭐

  • संयोजक अभिक्रिया ⭐⭐: पाइरुवेट माइटोकॉन्ड्रियल मैट्रिक्स में एसिटिल CoA (2C) — CO2 + NADH मुक्त!
  • क्रेब्स चक्र (TCA) ⭐⭐: स्थल = मैट्रिक्स ⭐। एसिटिल CoA (2C) + OAA (4C) → सिट्रेट (6C)! फिर: 2 CO2 निकलते हैं, 3 NADH + 1 FADH2 + 1 ATP प्रति चक्र, OAA पुनर्जनित!
  • प्रति ग्लूकोज़ ⭐⭐: 2 पाइरुवेट = 2 चक्र → कुल 6 NADH + 2 FADH2 + 2 ATP + संयोजक से 2 NADH।
  • मध्यवर्ती ⭐: सिट्रेट (6C), α-कीटोग्लूटैरेट (5C), सक्सिनेट, मैलेट, OAA (4C)।
  • उभयमार्गी (amphibolic) ⭐: क्रेब्स अपघटन भी, जैव-संश्लेषण हेतु मध्यवर्ती भी!

🎯 क्रेब्स = मैट्रिक्स में; प्रति ग्लूकोज़: 6 NADH + 2 FADH2 + 2 ATP!

4️⃣ ETC और ऑक्सीडेटिव फ़ॉस्फोरीकरण ⭐⭐

  • इलेक्ट्रॉन-परिवहन शृंखला ⭐⭐: स्थल = अंतर-झिल्ली (क्रिस्टी) ⭐। NADH/FADH2 के इलेक्ट्रॉन संकुल I-IV से → ऑक्सीजन अंतिम ग्राही ⭐⭐ → जल।
  • मार्ग: NADH → संकुल I → Q → III → साइटोक्रोम c → IV → O2। FADH2 संकुल II से (I छोड़ — कम ATP ⭐)।
  • प्रोटॉन पंपिंग ⭐: H+ मैट्रिक्स → अंतराझिल्ली-स्थान → प्रवणता → H+ ATP संश्लेषक से वापस → ATP = रासायनिक-परासरण ⭐⭐ (पीटर मिशेल)।
  • ATP मान ⭐⭐: 1 NADH = 3 ATP, 1 FADH2 = 2 ATP।
  • ऑक्सीजन की भूमिका ⭐: अंतिम इलेक्ट्रॉन-ग्राही — O2 नहीं तो ETC जाम!

💡 ETC = पानी की फिसलपट्टी: इलेक्ट्रॉन नीचे, प्रोटॉन पंप, प्रोटॉन-झरने से ATP टरबाइन!

5️⃣ कुल ATP और श्वसन-गुणांक ⭐⭐

  • कुल ATP प्रति ग्लूकोज़ ⭐⭐: ग्लाइकोलाइसिस: 2 ATP + 2 NADH; संयोजक: 2 NADH; क्रेब्स: 6 NADH + 2 FADH2 + 2 ATP। योग: 2 + 2 + (10 NADH × 3) + (2 × 2) = 38 ATP ⭐⭐ (NCERT मान!)।
  • दक्षता ⭐: ~40% — शेष ऊष्मा (उष्णरक्तियों हेतु उपयोगी!)।
  • श्वसन-गुणांक (RQ) ⭐⭐: RQ = CO2 मुक्त / O2 उपभुक्त। कार्बोहाइड्रेट: 1; वसा: ~0.7 (अधिक O2 चाहिए!); प्रोटीन: ~0.9।
  • RQ बताता है ⭐: कौन-सा ईंधन जल रहा है! मिश्रित आहार में ~0.85।
  • पादपों में श्वसन: प्रकाश-संश्लेषण का उल्टा नहीं! श्वसन 24×7 चलता है।

🎯 38 ATP = NCERT उत्तर; RQ: शर्करा 1, वसा 0.7, प्रोटीन 0.9!

1️⃣ Respiration Basics and Glycolysis ⭐⭐

  • Respiration: breaking food (glucose) to release energy (ATP). Equation: C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + energy.
  • Types: Aerobic (with O2 — complete, more energy) and anaerobic (without O2 — partial, less energy).
  • Glycolysis ⭐⭐ (EMP pathway): glucose (6C) → 2 pyruvate (3C). Site = CYTOPLASM ⭐ — identical in ALL living organisms! No O2 needed.
  • Steps ⭐: glucose → G6P (1 ATP used) → F6P → F1,6BP (1 more ATP) → 2 × 3C sugars → 2 pyruvate. Total: 4 ATP made, 2 used = NET 2 ATP ⭐⭐ + 2 NADH.
  • Key enzymes ⭐: hexokinase, PFK (rate-limiting!), pyruvate kinase.

🎯 Glycolysis = cytoplasm, NET 2 ATP + 2 NADH, oxygen-free. Made 4, spent 2!

2️⃣ Fermentation — The Anaerobic Route ⭐⭐

  • Fermentation ⭐: when O2 is unavailable — partial pyruvate breakdown. Only NET 2 ATP (from glycolysis) — very little!
  • Alcoholic fermentation ⭐⭐: yeast — pyruvate → ethanol + CO2. Uses: bread (CO2 makes dough rise ⭐), beer/wine (ethanol!).
  • Lactic acid fermentation ⭐⭐: muscles (during intense exercise, low O2!) + Lactobacillus (curd!) — pyruvate → lactic acid. The cause of muscle cramps!
  • Problems ⭐: (1) very little energy (2 ATP), (2) products are toxic (yeast dies at 13% alcohol — that's why natural wines cap at 13%!), (3) NADH must be recycled to NAD+.

💡 Yeast = alcohol factory (bread + beer); muscles = lactic acid (cramps!). Both give just 2 ATP — emergency mode only!

3️⃣ Krebs Cycle — The Mitochondrial Wheel ⭐⭐

  • Link reaction ⭐⭐: pyruvate enters the mitochondrial matrix and becomes acetyl CoA (2C) — releasing CO2 + NADH!
  • Krebs cycle (TCA cycle) ⭐⭐: site = matrix ⭐. Acetyl CoA (2C) + OAA (4C) → citrate (6C)! Then: 2 CO2 released, 3 NADH + 1 FADH2 + 1 ATP per cycle, and OAA is regenerated!
  • Per glucose ⭐⭐: 2 pyruvate = 2 turns → 6 NADH + 2 FADH2 + 2 ATP total + 2 NADH from the link reaction.
  • Intermediates ⭐: citrate (6C), α-ketoglutarate (5C), succinate, malate, OAA (4C).
  • Amphibolic pathway ⭐: Krebs both breaks down (respiration) and supplies building blocks (biosynthesis)!

🎯 Krebs = matrix; per glucose: 6 NADH + 2 FADH2 + 2 ATP. OAA = the driver that never runs out!

4️⃣ ETC and Oxidative Phosphorylation ⭐⭐

  • Electron Transport Chain ⭐⭐: site = inner mitochondrial membrane (cristae) ⭐. High-energy electrons from NADH/FADH2 pass through complexes I-IV → oxygen is the final electron acceptor ⭐⭐ → water forms.
  • Path: NADH → Complex I → ubiquinone → Complex III → cytochrome c → Complex IV → O2. FADH2 enters at Complex II (skips I — less ATP ⭐).
  • Proton pumping ⭐: H+ is pumped from the matrix to the intermembrane space → gradient → H+ flows back through ATP synthase → ATP = chemiosmosis ⭐⭐ (Peter Mitchell — Nobel!).
  • ATP values ⭐⭐: 1 NADH = 3 ATP, 1 FADH2 = 2 ATP.
  • Oxygen's role ⭐: the final acceptor — without O2, the whole chain jams!

💡 ETC = a water slide: electrons drop down, pumping protons; the proton waterfall spins the ATP turbine!

5️⃣ Total ATP Count and RQ ⭐⭐

  • Total ATP per glucose (aerobic) ⭐⭐: Glycolysis: 2 ATP + 2 NADH; link: 2 NADH; Krebs: 6 NADH + 2 FADH2 + 2 ATP. Sum: 2 + 2 + (10 NADH × 3) + (2 FADH2 × 2) = 38 ATP ⭐⭐ (the NCERT value!).
  • Efficiency ⭐: ~40% captured — the rest is heat (useful for warm-blooded animals!).
  • Respiratory Quotient (RQ) ⭐⭐: RQ = CO2 released / O2 consumed. Carbohydrates: 1; fats: ~0.7 (fats need MORE O2!); proteins: ~0.9.
  • RQ tells you ⭐: which fuel is burning! Mixed diet ≈ 0.85.
  • Plant respiration: NOT the reverse of photosynthesis! Respiration runs 24×7 — even plants need ATP.

🎯 38 ATP = the NCERT answer; RQ: carbs 1, fats 0.7, proteins 0.9 — classic MCQ!

NEET Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Glycolysis kahan hota hai aur net gain kitna hai?
Answer dekho

Cytoplasm me — glucose (6C) → 2 pyruvate (3C). Net gain = 2 ATP + 2 NADH (4 ATP bane, 2 use hue). Oxygen zaroori nahi!

Q2 Alcoholic aur lactic acid fermentation me difference batao.
Answer dekho

Alcoholic (yeast): pyruvate → ethanol + CO2; Lactic acid (muscles, Lactobacillus): pyruvate → lactic acid. Dono sirf 2 ATP dete hain.

Q3 Krebs cycle kahan hota hai aur per glucose kitna yield hai?
Answer dekho

Mitochondrial matrix me. Per glucose (2 turns): 6 NADH + 2 FADH2 + 2 ATP + link reaction se 2 NADH.

Q4 Acetyl CoA kaise banta hai?
Answer dekho

Link reaction me — pyruvate mitochondrial matrix me jaake CO2 + NADH release karke acetyl CoA (2C) banta hai (pyruvate dehydrogenase complex se).

Q5 ETC me oxygen ka kya role hai?
Answer dekho

Final electron acceptor — electrons Complex IV se O2 ko jaate hain aur water banta hai. O2 na ho to chain jam + ATP production band!

Q6 1 NADH aur 1 FADH2 se kitne ATP bante hain?
Answer dekho

1 NADH = 3 ATP, 1 FADH2 = 2 ATP (NCERT values). FADH2 Complex II se enter karta hai (Complex I skip) — isliye kam.

Q7 Ek glucose se aerobic respiration me total kitne ATP bante hain (NCERT)?
Answer dekho

38 ATP — glycolysis (2 net + 2 NADH), link (2 NADH), Krebs (6 NADH + 2 FADH2 + 2 ATP): 2+2+30+4 = 38.

Q8 RQ kya hai? Carbohydrates aur fats ke liye values batao.
Answer dekho

RQ = CO2 released / O2 consumed. Carbohydrates = 1; fats = ~0.7 (fats me oxygen content kam — zyada O2 lagti hai!); proteins ~0.9.

Q9 Fermentation me sirf 2 ATP hi kyun milte hain?
Answer dekho

Kyunki sirf glycolysis chalta hai — pyruvate mitochondria me nahi jaata (Krebs + ETC band). NADH ko NAD+ me recycle karne ke liye hi pyruvate ethanol/lactate banta hai.

Q10 Muscle cramps ka chemical reason kya hai?
Answer dekho
