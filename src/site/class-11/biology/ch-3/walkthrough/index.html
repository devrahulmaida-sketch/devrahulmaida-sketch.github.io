Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

AlgaeGreen: a + b, starch. Brown: a + c, fucoxanthin, laminarin. Red: a + d, phycoerythrin, floridean starch.
BryophytesGametophyte dominant, rhizoids, water needed. Sporophyte dependent. Moss: protonema, leafy stage.
PteridophytesFirst vascular plants. Sporophyte dominant, prothallus free-living. Selaginella and Salvinia are heterosporous.
GymnospermsNaked seeds, wind pollination, male and female cones. Cycas coralloid roots, Pinus mycorrhiza.
AngiospermsSeeds in fruits. Double fertilisation: zygote (2n) + endosperm (3n). Dicot vs monocot.
Life cyclesHaplontic (Volvox), diplontic (Fucus, seed plants), haplo-diplontic (bryophytes, pteridophytes, kelps).
