1️⃣ Algae — Paani Ke Photosynthesizers ⭐

  • Algae: chlorophyll-bearing, simple, thalloid (root/stem/leaf nahi), autotrophic organisms. Mostly aquatic (fresh + marine), kuch moist stones/wood/soil pe.
  • Reproduction ⭐: vegetative (fragmentation), asexual (zoospores — motile), sexual (fusion of gametes).
  • Sexual reproduction ke types ⭐⭐: Isogamous (same size gametes — Spirogyra, kuch Chlamydomonas), Anisogamous (different size — kuch Chlamydomonas, Eudorina), Oogamous (bada non-motile egg + chhota motile male — Volvox, Fucus).
  • Chlorophyceae (green algae) ⭐: chlorophyll a + b; pigments chloroplast me (pyrenoids me starch stored); cell wall cellulose; food = starch. Examples: Chlamydomonas, Volvox, Spirogyra, Chara.
  • Phaeophyceae (brown algae) ⭐: chlorophyll a + c + fucoxanthin (brown colour!); marine; food = laminarin/mannitol; body holdfast + stipe + frond. Examples: Ectocarpus, Laminaria, Sargassum, Fucus.
  • Rhodophyceae (red algae) ⭐: chlorophyll a + d + phycoerythrin (red colour!); mostly marine; food = floridean starch; cell wall cellulose + agar/carrageen. Examples: Polysiphonia, Porphyra, Gelidium (agar!), Gracilaria.
  • Uses: oxygen + food (Porphyra, Laminaria, Sargassum khaye jaate hain); hydrocolloids — agar (Gelidium, Gracilaria — microbiology medium), carrageen, algin.

🎯 Pigment + food storage = algae classes ka master key: green = a,b + starch; brown = a,c,fucoxanthin + laminarin; red = a,d,phycoerythrin + floridean starch!

2️⃣ Bryophytes — Amphibians of Plant Kingdom ⭐

  • Bryophytes ko 'amphibians of plant kingdom' kehte hain — land pe rehte hain par sexual reproduction ke liye PAANI zaroori! Moist, shaded areas me.
  • Body: thallus-like ya leafy; true roots nahi — rhizoids se attach; conducting tissue (xylem/phloem) nahi.
  • Main plant body = gametophyte (haploid) ⭐⭐ — dominant phase! Sex organs multicellular: male antheridium, female archegonium (flask-shaped).
  • Zygote se embryo nahi banke seedha sporophyte banta hai jo gametophyte pe dependent/parasitic rehta hai — spores banata hai. Ye sporophyte FREE-LIVING nahi hota!
  • Liverworts ⭐: moist, shady jagah; body thalloid (Marchantia) ya leafy. Asexual reproduction: fragmentation + gemmae (gemma cups me — green multicellular buds!).
  • Mosses ⭐: gametophyte 2 stages — protonema (creeping, green, branched, filamentous — spore germination se banta hai) + leafy stage (protonema se develop). Rhizoids multicellular + branched. Examples: Funaria, Polytrichum, Sphagnum.
  • Economic uses: Sphagnum moss — peat (fuel), packing material, soil conditioner.

💡 Feel: bryophyte = woh dost jo land pe rehta hai par swimming pool (paani) ke bina romance (reproduction) nahi kar sakta!

3️⃣ Pteridophytes — Pehle Vascular Plants ⭐

  • Pehle terrestrial plants jinme VASCULAR TISSUE (xylem + phloem) hai ⭐ — ferns aur unke relatives. Moist, shady places me.
  • Main plant body = sporophyte (diploid) ⭐⭐ — ab dominant phase shift ho gaya! True roots, stem, leaves hain. Leaves: small (microphylls — Selaginella) ya large (megaphylls — ferns).
  • Reproduction: spores sporangia me bante hain; sporangia leaves (sporophylls) pe sori (groups) me. Zygote se embryo banta hai.
  • Gametophyte: spore se banta hai — small, free-living, photosynthetic thalloid body jise prothallus kehte hain. Ispe antheridia + archegonia. Fertilization ke liye paani chahiye (bryophytes ki tarah!).
  • Classes ⭐: Psilopsida (Psilotum), Lycopsida (Selaginella, Lycopodium), Sphenopsida (Equisetum), Pteropsida (ferns — Pteris, Dryopteris).
  • Homosporous ⭐: ek hi type ke spores (zyadatar). Heterosporous ⭐⭐: 2 types — macro (large, female) + micro (small, male) spores — Selaginella aur Salvinia. Heterospory = seed habit ki taraf pehla kadam!
  • Uses: ornamental; medicinal; soil binders; Sphagnum... (ferns decorative).

🎯 Selaginella + Salvinia = heterosporous — 100% pakka exam question. In dono ko 'seed ki aadat ka trailer' samjho!

4️⃣ Gymnosperms — Naked Seeds ⭐

  • Gymnosperms = 'naked seeds' ⭐⭐ (gymnos = naked, sperma = seed) — ovules/seeds kisi ovary/fruit ke andar ENCLOSED nahi hote!
  • Size: medium/tall trees aur shrubs; sabse tall tree species Sequoia (redwood), sabse old = bristlecone pine.
  • Roots: tap roots; kuch me fungal association (mycorrhiza — Pinus) ya N2-fixing cyanobacteria (coralloid roots — Cycas ⭐).
  • Leaves: needle-like (Pinus — pinnate nahi!) ya pinnate (Cycas); adaptations against harsh conditions: thick cuticle, sunken stomata, needle leaves (surface area kam).
  • Reproduction ⭐: heterosporous — microspores (pollen grains, male) + megaspores (female) alag cones me. Male cone + female cone. Pollination = WIND (anemophily) — pollen grains air currents se aate hain, female cone ke ovule tak directly (naked!) pahunchte hain.
  • Female gametophyte ⭐: archegonia banata hai — ovule ke andar hi rehta hai (independent nahi, bryo/pterido se different!). Seeds wings ke saath dispersal ke liye.
  • Examples ⭐: Cycas, Pinus, Cedrus (deodar), Ginkgo, Sequoia.

💡 Feel: gymnosperm = seed jo BINA suitcase (fruit) ke travel karta hai — sab kuch exposed!

5️⃣ Angiosperms + Plant Life Cycles ⭐

  • Angiosperms = covered seeds ⭐⭐ (angeion = vessel) — seeds FRUIT ke andar enclosed! Flowering plants — sabse successful group.
  • Size range: microscopic Wolfia se 100m Eucalyptus tak.
  • Reproduction: flower me male (stamen → pollen) + female (pistil → ovary me ovules). Double fertilization ⭐⭐ — unique to angiosperms! Ek male gamete + egg = zygote; dusra + 2 polar nuclei = triploid endosperm (food tissue). Ovary → fruit, ovule → seed.
  • 2 classes: Dicotyledons (2 cotyledons, reticulate venation, tap root) aur Monocotyledons (1 cotyledon, parallel venation, fibrous roots).
  • Life cycles ⭐⭐: Haplontic — dominant haploid gametophyte, sporophyte sirf zygote (algae jaise Volvox, Spirogyra). Diplontic — dominant diploid sporophyte, gametophyte sirf gametes tak limited (gymnosperms, angiosperms, Fucus!). Haplo-diplontic — dono phases multicellular free-living (bryophytes, pteridophytes, Ectocarpus, Polysiphonia, kelps!).
  • Evolution ka flow ⭐: algae (haplontic) → bryo/pterido (haplo-diplontic) → gymno/angio (diplontic) — sporophyte dominant hota gaya, gametophyte reduce hota gaya!

🎯 Life-cycle table yaad karo: haplontic = Volvox/Spirogyra/Chlamydomonas; haplo-diplontic = bryo/pterido/Ectocarpus/kelps; diplontic = Fucus + seed plants!

1️⃣ शैवाल — जल के प्रकाश-संश्लेषक ⭐

  • शैवाल: हरितलवकयुक्त, सरल, थैलस-सदृश (मूल/तना/पत्ती नहीं), स्वपोषी जीव। अधिकतर जलीय, कुछ नम पत्थरों/मिट्टी पर।
  • जनन ⭐: कायिक (खंडन), अलैंगिक (चल बीजाणु), लैंगिक (युग्मकों का संलयन)।
  • लैंगिक जनन के प्रकार ⭐⭐: समयुग्मकी (समान आकार — स्पाइरोगायरा), विषमयुग्मकी (असमान आकार — यूडोरिना), अंडयुग्मकी (बड़ा स्थिर अंड + छोटा चल नर — वॉल्वॉक्स, फ्यूकस)।
  • हरित शैवाल (Chlorophyceae) ⭐: हरितलवक a + b; भोजन = स्टार्च (पायरिनॉइड में); भित्ति सेलुलोज़। उदा: क्लैमाइडोमोनास, वॉल्वॉक्स, स्पाइरोगायरा, कारा।
  • भूरे शैवाल (Phaeophyceae) ⭐: हरितलवक a + c + फ्यूकोज़ेंथिन; समुद्री; भोजन = लैमिनेरिन/मैनिटॉल; शरीर = होल्डफास्ट + स्टाइप + फ्रोंड। उदा: एक्टोकार्पस, लैमिनेरिया, सारगैसम।
  • लाल शैवाल (Rhodophyceae) ⭐: हरितलवक a + d + फाइकोएरिथ्रिन; समुद्री; भोजन = फ्लोरिडियन स्टार्च। उदा: पॉलीसाइफोनिया, पोरफाइरा, जेलिडियम (अगर!), ग्रेसिलेरिया।
  • उपयोग: ऑक्सीजन + खाद्य; अगार (जेलिडियम, ग्रेसिलेरिया), कैरेजीन, ऐल्जिन।

🎯 वर्णक + संचित भोजन = शैवाल वर्गों की कुंजी!

2️⃣ ब्रायोफाइट्स — पादप जगत के उभयचर ⭐

  • ब्रायोफाइट्स को 'पादप जगत के उभयचर' कहते हैं — स्थल पर रहते हैं परंतु लैंगिक जनन हेतु जल आवश्यक! नम, छायादार स्थान।
  • शरीर: थैलस-सदृश या पर्णीय; सच्ची जड़ें नहीं — मूलाभास (rhizoids); संवहन ऊतक नहीं।
  • मुख्य पादप शरीर = युग्मकद (अगुणित) ⭐⭐ — प्रभावी अवस्था! लैंगिक अंग बहुकोशिकीय: नर पुंकेसरकोश (antheridium), मादा अग्रबीजाणुधानी (archegonium)।
  • युग्मनज से सीधे बीजाणुक बनता है जो युग्मकद पर परजीवी-सदृश निर्भर रहता है।
  • लिवरवर्ट ⭐: थैलस-सदृश (मार्केंशिया); अलैंगिक जनन: खंडन + जेमा (जेमा कप में)।
  • मॉस ⭐: युग्मकद 2 अवस्थाओं में — प्रोटोनीमा (रेशेदार, हरा) + पर्णीय अवस्था। उदा: फ्यूनेरिया, पॉलीट्राइकम, स्फैग्नम।

💡 ब्रायोफाइट = स्थल का रहने वाला, पर जनन के लिए जल-प्रेमी!

3️⃣ टेरिडोफाइट्स — प्रथम संवहनी पादप ⭐

  • प्रथम स्थलीय पादप जिनमें संवहन ऊतक (जाइलम + फ्लोएम) है ⭐ — फर्न एवं सम्बन्धी।
  • मुख्य शरीर = बीजाणुक (द्विगुणित) ⭐⭐ — प्रभावी अवस्था अब ये! सच्ची जड़, तना, पत्तियाँ। पत्तियाँ: छोटी (माइक्रोफिल — सेलाजिनेला) या बड़ी (मेगाफिल — फर्न)।
  • जनन: बीजाणुधानियों में बीजाणु; पर्णों पर सोरी (समूह) में।
  • युग्मकद: बीजाणु से बनता है — छोटा, स्वतंत्र, प्रकाश-संश्लेषी प्रोथैलस। निषेचन हेतु जल आवश्यक।
  • वर्ग ⭐: साइलॉप्सिडा (साइलोटम), लाइकॉप्सिडा (सेलाजिनेला), स्फीनॉप्सिडा (इक्वीसीटम), टेरॉप्सिडा (फर्न)।
  • समबीजाणुकी ⭐: एक ही प्रकार के बीजाणु। विषमबीजाणुकी ⭐⭐: 2 प्रकार — गुरु (मादा) + लघु (नर) बीजाणु — सेलाजिनेला और साल्विनिया। बीज-प्रवृत्ति की ओर प्रथम कदम!

🎯 सेलाजिनेला + साल्विनिया = विषमबीजाणुकी — पक्का प्रश्न!

4️⃣ जिम्नोस्पर्म — अनावृत बीज ⭐

  • जिम्नोस्पर्म = 'नग्न बीज' ⭐⭐ — अंडाशय/फल के अंदर बीज आवृत नहीं होते!
  • आकार: मध्यम/ऊँचे वृक्ष; सबसे ऊँचा वृक्ष सीकोया।
  • जड़ें: मूसला जड़; कुछ में कवक-सहजीविता (माइकोराइज़ा — पाइनस) या N2-स्थिरीकरण (प्रवालाकार जड़ें — साइकस ⭐)।
  • पत्तियाँ: सुई-नुमा (पाइनस) या पंख-नुमा (साइकस); मोटी क्यूटिकल, धँसे रंध्र — कठोर दशाओं के अनुकूलन।
  • जनन ⭐: विषमबीजाणुकी — नर शंकु + मादा शंकु। परागण = वायु द्वारा — परागकण सीधे नग्न बीजाण्ड तक पहुँचते हैं।
  • मादा युग्मकद ⭐: अग्रबीजाणुधानी बनाता है — बीजाण्ड के अंदर ही रहता है।
  • उदाहरण ⭐: साइकस, पाइनस, सीड्रस (देवदार), जिन्कगो।

💡 जिम्नोस्पर्म = बिना सूटकेस (फल) के यात्रा करने वाला बीज!

5️⃣ आवृतबीजी + पादप जीवनचक्र ⭐

  • आवृतबीजी = आवृत बीज ⭐⭐ — बीज फल के अंदर! पुष्पी पादप — सबसे सफल समूह।
  • आकार: सूक्ष्म वोल्फिया से 100 मीटर यूकेलिप्टस तक।
  • जनन: पुष्प में नर (पुंकेसर → पराग) + मादा (स्त्रीकेसर → अंडाशय में बीजाण्ड)। द्वि-निषेचन ⭐⭐ — केवल आवृतबीजियों में! एक नर युग्मक + अंड = युग्मनज; दूसरा + 2 ध्रुवीय केंद्रक = त्रिगुणित भ्रूणपोष। अंडाशय → फल, बीजाण्ड → बीज।
  • 2 वर्ग: द्विबीजपत्री (2 बीजपत्र, जालिका-सदृश शिरा-विन्यास, मूसला जड़) और एकबीजपत्री (1 बीजपत्र, समांतर शिरा-विन्यास, रेशेदार जड़)।
  • जीवनचक्र ⭐⭐: अगुणित-प्रधान (haplontic) — वॉल्वॉक्स, स्पाइरोगायरा। द्विगुणित-प्रधान (diplontic) — जिम्नो/आवृतबीजी, फ्यूकस। अगुणित-द्विगुणित (haplo-diplontic) — ब्रायो/टेरिडोफाइट, एक्टोकार्पस, केल्प।
  • विकास का क्रम ⭐: शैवाल → ब्रायो/टेरिडो → जिम्नो/आवृतबीजी — बीजाणुक प्रभावी होता गया!

🎯 जीवनचक्र तालिका याद रखें — हर साल प्रश्न!

1️⃣ Algae — The Photosynthesizers of Water ⭐

  • Algae: chlorophyll-bearing, simple, thalloid (no root/stem/leaf), autotrophic organisms. Mostly aquatic, some on moist stones, wood, soil.
  • Reproduction ⭐: vegetative (fragmentation), asexual (zoospores), sexual (gamete fusion).
  • Types of sexual reproduction ⭐⭐: Isogamous (same-size gametes — Spirogyra), Anisogamous (different sizes — Eudorina), Oogamous (large non-motile egg + small motile male — Volvox, Fucus).
  • Chlorophyceae (green algae) ⭐: chlorophyll a + b; food = starch (stored in pyrenoids); cellulose wall. E.g. Chlamydomonas, Volvox, Spirogyra, Chara.
  • Phaeophyceae (brown algae) ⭐: chlorophyll a + c + fucoxanthin; marine; food = laminarin/mannitol; body = holdfast + stipe + frond. E.g. Ectocarpus, Laminaria, Sargassum, Fucus.
  • Rhodophyceae (red algae) ⭐: chlorophyll a + d + phycoerythrin; mostly marine; food = floridean starch. E.g. Polysiphonia, Porphyra, Gelidium (agar!), Gracilaria.
  • Uses: oxygen + food; agar (Gelidium, Gracilaria), carrageen, algin.

🎯 Pigment + stored food = the master key for algae classes!

2️⃣ Bryophytes — Amphibians of the Plant Kingdom ⭐

  • Bryophytes are called the 'amphibians of the plant kingdom' — they live on land but NEED water for sexual reproduction! Found in moist, shaded areas.
  • Body: thallus-like or leafy; no true roots — attached by rhizoids; no conducting tissue.
  • Main plant body = gametophyte (haploid) ⭐⭐ — the dominant phase! Sex organs are multicellular: male antheridium, female archegonium.
  • The zygote directly forms a sporophyte that stays dependent (parasitic) on the gametophyte — not free-living!
  • Liverworts ⭐: thalloid (Marchantia) or leafy; asexual reproduction by fragmentation + gemmae (in gemma cups).
  • Mosses ⭐: gametophyte has 2 stages — protonema (filamentous, green) + leafy stage. E.g. Funaria, Polytrichum, Sphagnum.

💡 Feel: a bryophyte lives on land but can't reproduce without water — the plant kingdom's amphibian!

3️⃣ Pteridophytes — The First Vascular Plants ⭐

  • First terrestrial plants with VASCULAR TISSUE (xylem + phloem) ⭐ — ferns and their relatives. Found in moist, shady places.
  • Main plant body = sporophyte (diploid) ⭐⭐ — the dominant phase shifts! True roots, stems, leaves. Leaves: small (microphylls — Selaginella) or large (megaphylls — ferns).
  • Reproduction: spores form in sporangia, grouped as sori on sporophylls.
  • Gametophyte: grows from the spore — small, free-living, photosynthetic prothallus with antheridia + archegonia. Fertilization still needs water.
  • Classes ⭐: Psilopsida (Psilotum), Lycopsida (Selaginella, Lycopodium), Sphenopsida (Equisetum), Pteropsida (ferns).
  • Homosporous ⭐: one spore type (most). Heterosporous ⭐⭐: two types — macrospores (female) + microspores (male) — Selaginella and Salvinia. The first step toward seed habit!

🎯 Selaginella + Salvinia = heterosporous — a guaranteed exam question!

4️⃣ Gymnosperms — Naked Seeds ⭐

  • Gymnosperms = 'naked seeds' ⭐⭐ (gymnos = naked, sperma = seed) — ovules/seeds are NOT enclosed in any ovary or fruit!
  • Size: medium/tall trees and shrubs; tallest tree species = Sequoia (redwood).
  • Roots: tap roots; some have fungal association (mycorrhiza — Pinus) or N2-fixing coralloid roots (Cycas ⭐).
  • Leaves: needle-like (Pinus) or pinnate (Cycas); adaptations to harsh conditions: thick cuticle, sunken stomata.
  • Reproduction ⭐: heterosporous — male and female cones. Pollination by WIND — pollen lands directly on the naked ovule.
  • Female gametophyte ⭐: bears archegonia, stays inside the ovule (not independent — unlike bryo/pteridophytes!).
  • Examples ⭐: Cycas, Pinus, Cedrus (deodar), Ginkgo, Sequoia.

💡 Feel: a gymnosperm seed travels without a suitcase (fruit) — fully exposed!

5️⃣ Angiosperms + Plant Life Cycles ⭐

  • Angiosperms = covered seeds ⭐⭐ (angeion = vessel) — seeds enclosed inside a FRUIT! The most successful plant group.
  • Size: from microscopic Wolfia to 100 m Eucalyptus.
  • Reproduction: flower has male (stamen → pollen) + female (pistil → ovules in ovary). Double fertilization ⭐⭐ — unique to angiosperms! One male gamete + egg = zygote; the other + 2 polar nuclei = triploid endosperm. Ovary → fruit, ovule → seed.
  • 2 classes: Dicots (2 cotyledons, reticulate venation, tap roots) and Monocots (1 cotyledon, parallel venation, fibrous roots).
  • Life cycles ⭐⭐: Haplontic — dominant haploid phase (Volvox, Spirogyra). Diplontic — dominant diploid sporophyte (gymnosperms, angiosperms, Fucus!). Haplo-diplontic — both phases multicellular and free-living (bryophytes, pteridophytes, Ectocarpus, kelps!).
  • Evolutionary flow ⭐: algae (haplontic) → bryo/pterido (haplo-diplontic) → gymno/angio (diplontic) — sporophyte keeps gaining dominance!

NEET Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Bryophytes ko 'amphibians of plant kingdom' kyun kehte hain?
Answer dekho

Kyunki ye land pe to rehte hain par sexual reproduction (fertilization) ke liye PAANI zaroori hai — bilkul amphibians ki tarah jo dono dunia ke hain.

Q2 Green, brown aur red algae ko alag karne wale 2 characters batao.
Answer dekho

Pigments aur stored food: Green = chl a,b + starch; Brown = chl a,c + fucoxanthin + laminarin/mannitol; Red = chl a,d + phycoerythrin + floridean starch.

Q3 Heterosporous pteridophytes ke 2 examples do aur ye important kyun hai.
Answer dekho

Selaginella aur Salvinia. Important kyunki macro + micro spores ka hona seed habit ki evolution ki shuruaat hai.

Q4 Gymnosperm aur angiosperm seeds me kya farak hai?
Answer dekho

Gymnosperm ke seeds naked hote hain (ovary/fruit ke andar enclosed nahi); angiosperm ke seeds fruit ke andar hote hain.

Q5 Moss gametophyte ki 2 stages batao.
Answer dekho

Protonema (creeping, green, filamentous — spore germination se) aur leafy stage (protonema se develop hoti hai).

Q6 Double fertilization kya hai?
Answer dekho

Angiosperms ka unique feature: ek male gamete egg se fuse karke zygote banata hai, dusra 2 polar nuclei se fuse karke triploid endosperm banata hai.

Q7 Pteridophytes ka gametophyte kya kehlata hai aur uski khasiyat kya hai?
Answer dekho

Prothallus — small, free-living, photosynthetic thalloid body jispe antheridia aur archegonia hote hain.

Q8 Haplontic aur diplontic life cycles me difference batao (examples ke saath).
Answer dekho

Haplontic: haploid gametophyte dominant (Volvox, Spirogyra); Diplontic: diploid sporophyte dominant, gametophyte reduce (gymnosperms, angiosperms, Fucus).

Q9 Cycas ki roots me kya special hai?
Answer dekho

Coralloid roots — inme N2-fixing cyanobacteria (Anabaena, Nostoc) rehte hain (symbiosis).

Q10 Oogamous sexual reproduction kya hai? Examples do.
Answer dekho

Bada non-motile female gamete (egg) + chhota motile male gamete ka fusion. Examples: Volvox, Fucus.
