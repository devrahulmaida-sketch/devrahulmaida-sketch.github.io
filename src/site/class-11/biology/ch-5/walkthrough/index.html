Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

RootTap (dicots), fibrous (monocots), adventitious. Regions: cap, division, elongation, maturation. Pneumatophores in mangroves.
StemPotato tuber, ginger rhizome, Colocasia corm. Runner, stolon (mint, jasmine), offset, sucker (banana, pineapple). Tendril, thorn, Opuntia.
LeafBase, petiole, lamina. Reticulate (dicot), parallel (monocot). Pinnate (neem), palmate (silk cotton).
FlowerCalyx, corolla, androecium, gynoecium. Hypogynous superior, perigynous half inferior, epigynous inferior ovary.
OvulesMarginal (pea), axile (China rose), parietal (mustard), free central (Dianthus), basal (sunflower).
Seed + familiesMaize: scutellum, coleoptile, coleorhiza. Fabaceae, Solanaceae, Liliaceae.
