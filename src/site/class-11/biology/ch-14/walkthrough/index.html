Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

Organs + pathEarthworm skin, insects trachea, aquatic gills, mammals lungs. Nostrils, pharynx, larynx, trachea, bronchi, bronchioles, alveoli.
MechanismInspiration active: diaphragm + external intercostals contract, volume up, pressure down. Normal expiration passive.
VolumesTV ~500, IRV 2500-3000, ERV 1000-1100, RV 1100-1200 mL. VC = TV + IRV + ERV. TLC = VC + RV. FRC = ERV + RV.
Gas exchangePartial pressure gradient, passive diffusion. Alveoli pO2 104, pCO2 40. Blood 40 and 45. Tissue 40 and 45.
TransportO2: 97% Hb, 3% plasma. CO2: ~70% bicarbonate (carbonic anhydrase), 20-25% carbamino-Hb, ~7% dissolved. Bohr effect.
Control + disordersRhythm centre medulla, pneumotaxic pons, chemosensitive area senses CO2/H+. Asthma, emphysema (alveoli damage), silicosis, pneumonia.
