Oscillations Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - SHM ke har concept ko chalta-dikhata hua. Sliders ghumao, jhoola jhulao, formulas feel karo.

1. SHM Kya Hai - Circle Ki Shadow Ka Khel

Particle circle pe uniform chal raha hai. Uski shadow diameter pe aage-peeche = SHM. Saath me sine wave trace hoti hai - x = A sin(ωt + φ).

Periodic = fixed time baad repeat (period T). Oscillatory = mean ke dono taraf. Har oscillatory periodic hai, har periodic oscillatory NAHI. SHM tab jab restoring force ∝ displacement aur OPPOSITE: F = −kx ω = 2π/T

2. x, v, a - Teeno Ka Phase Khel

Spring-block oscillate karta hai, neeche teeno graphs LIVE. Dhyan se dekho: velocity displacement se 90° aage, acceleration hamesha opposite (180°).

Mean position pe speed MAX = Aω, acceleration ZERO. Extremes pe speed zero, acceleration MAX = ω²A aur mean ki taraf. v = ±ω√(A² − x²) a = −ω²x - ye a = −ω²x dikhe to samajh jao SHM hai!

3. Energy Ka Ping-Pong - KE ↔ PE

Oscillator chalta hai, neeche energy graph: PE parabola (extremes pe max), KE ulta parabola (mean pe max), TOTAL flat - energy kabhi banati-nasht nahi hoti, bas convert!

KE = ½mω²(A² − x²) PE = ½mω²x² E = ½kA² = constant E ∝ A² - amplitude DOUBLE karo, energy 4 GUNA! Ek poore cycle me average KE = average PE = E/2.

4. Pendulum Lab - T Kis Pe Depend Karta Hai?

Length, mass, g badlo aur period dekho. Surprise: mass ka koi asar NAHI! Moon pe le jao - same pendulum slow ho jaata hai.

T = 2π√(L/g) - sirf L aur g. Seconds pendulum: T=2s wala, L ≈ 1 m (99.4 cm). Spring ke liye T = 2π√(m/k) - wahan mass matter karta hai! Spring aadhi kaato → k double → T ÷ √2.

5. Damping aur Resonance - Jhoola Kab Rukta Hai, Kab Uchhalta Hai

Upar: friction se amplitude exponential ghat-ta hai. Neeche: driving frequency badlo - jab wo natural frequency ke barabar aaye, amplitude MAXIMUM = RESONANCE.

Damped: A(t) = A₀e^(−bt/2m) - har real jhoola rukta hai. Forced oscillation: system DRIVER ki frequency pe chalta hai. Resonance: νdrive = νnatural → amplitude max. Isliye troops bridge pe march break karte hain (1940 Tacoma bridge collapse!), swing ko perfect-timing dhakka, radio tuning. Kam damping = sharp resonance.
