Class 11 Physics - Chapter 7 - Gravitation

Gravitation Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - inverse square se escape velocity tak. Force ghatao, g map karo, orbit me phenko, Kepler ke areas dekho.

1. Inverse Square - Distance Double, Force 1/4

F = G·m₁m₂/r² - har cheez har cheez ko kheenchti hai! Distance ka slider ghumao: r double → force 1/4, triple → 1/9. Force hamesha attractive, dono pe barabar (third law pair!).

distance r = 1.0×
G = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg² (Cavendish ne measure kiya). Earth tumhe jitne force se kheenchti hai, tum bhi Earth ko utne hi force se kheench rahe ho - bas tumhara acceleration dikhta hai, Earth ka nahi! g = GM/R² - mass cancel, isliye vacuum me feather = hammer.

2. g Ka Variation - Upar Bhi Kam, Andar Bhi Kam

Ball ko slider se ghumao: center (r=0) se surface (R) tak, phir 3R tak door. Andar: g ∝ r (sirf andar wala mass khinchta hai). Bahar: g ∝ 1/r². Center pe g = ZERO - sab taraf se pull cancel!

position r = 1.00×R
Height h pe: g' = g(1 − 2h/R)  |  Depth d pe: g' = g(1 − d/R) - height pe g FASTER ghatta hai (factor 2!). Exam favourite: g sabse zyada poles pe (rotation + shape), center pe zero.

3. Newton Ka Cannonball - Kitni Tez Phenko?

Newton ka thought experiment: itni oonchi tower (~190 km, atmosphere se upar!) se topu horizontal chalaao. Slow = gir jaayegi. ~7.8 km/s = orbit shuru! (satellite lagatar Earth KI TARAF gir raha hai, bas aage itna tez ki Earth "chhoot" jaati hai). ~11 km/s = escape - kabhi wapas nahi! (Surface se: vₓ = 7.9, vₑ = 11.2 km/s.)

v < v_e → bound (ellipse), v = v_e → parabola, v > v_e → hyperbola. Near-Earth orbit: vo = √(GM/r) ≈ 7.9 km/s = ve/√2. Weightlessness: satellite me sab same g se free fall me - gravity wahan ~90% hai, zero NAHI!

4. Kepler Ka Area Law - Paas Fast, Door Slow

Planet ellipse me ghoomta hai, Sun ek focus pe (center pe nahi!). Equal time ke shaded wedges dekho - sab equal area! Perihelion (paas) = fast, aphelion (door) = slow. Angular momentum conserved!

Law 3: T² ∝ a³ - door ke planets ke BAHUT lambe years: Mercury 0.24 yr, Earth 1 yr, Neptune 165 years! Ye laws Moon aur artificial satellites pe bhi lagte hain. T² = 4π²r³/GM.

5. Escape Velocity - Kuen Se Bahar Ka Kick

Gravity ek kuen (well) hai: U = −GMm/r batata hai tum kitne andar ho. ve = √(2GM/R) woh kick hai jo bahar phenk de. Planet chuno, rocket launch karo - v_e se kam pe wapas gir jaayega!

Earth: ve = 11.2 km/s, vo = 7.9  |  Moon: 2.4  |  Jupiter: 60 km/s! Escape velocity angle ya rocket ke mass pe depend NAHI karti - sirf planet ke M aur R pe. Satellite energy: E = −GMm/2r (negative = bound).
