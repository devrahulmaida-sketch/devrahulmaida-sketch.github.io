Notes language:

1️⃣ Newton Ka Universal Law — Har Cheez Har Cheez Ko Kheenchti Hai

Universal law of gravitation ⭐: F = G·m₁m₂/r² — do masses ke beech attraction, distance ke square ke inversely proportional.

  • G = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg² — universal constant, sab jagah same. (Cavendish ne measure kiya.)
  • Inverse square ⭐: distance double → force 1/4; triple → 1/9. Isliye door planets pe gravity kamzor!
  • Force hamesha attractive (repulsive gravity nahi hoti), dono masses pe equal-opposite (third law pair).
  • g nikalna: Earth pe mg = GMm/R² → g = GM/R² — mass cancel! Isliye sab objects same rate se girte hain (Galileo ka tower experiment, vacuum me feather = hammer!).

💡 Feel: tum bhi Earth ko utne hi force se kheench rahe ho jitna Earth tumhe! Bas tumhara mass chhota hai, isliye tumhara acceleration dikhta hai, Earth ka nahi.

2️⃣ g Ka Variation — Upar, Neeche, Idhar-Udhar

g har jagah same nahi — teen classic variations:

  • Rotation effect: equator pe centrifugal effect g ko kam karta hai (g' = g − ω²R cos²λ). Earth ruk jaaye toh equator pe weight badh jayega, poles pe same! ⭐
  • Height pe 2h/R factor, depth pe d/R — height pe g FASTER ghatta hai (factor 2!).

🎯 Exam favourite: "kahan g sabse zyada?" — Poles pe! "Center pe?" — ZERO!

3️⃣ Gravitational PE aur Escape Velocity ⭐

Gravitational potential energy: U = −GMm/r — MINUS sign zaroori! (Reference infinity pe U = 0; paas aate energy negative, yani bound system.)

  • Potential: V = −GM/r (per unit mass) • field: E = GM/r² (per unit mass ka force).
  • Escape velocity ⭐: ve = √(2GM/R) = √(2gR) — Earth ke liye 11.2 km/s. Itni speed se phenko toh object kabhi wapas nahi aata (energy zero ya positive ho jaati hai, bound nahi rehta).
  • Escape velocity angle pe depend NAHI karti, aur na hi object ke mass pe — sirf planet ke M aur R pe! Moon pe 2.4 km/s, Jupiter pe 60 km/s.
  • Orbit condition: v < ve → bound (ellipse), v = ve → parabola (bhaag gaya), v > ve → hyperbola.

💡 Feel: gravity ek kuan (well) hai — escape velocity woh kick hai jo kuen se bahar phenk de. U = −GMm/r batata hai tum kuen me kitne andar ho!

4️⃣ Satellites — Girta Hua, Kabhi Nahi Girta ⭐

Orbital velocity: vo = √(GM/r) — circular orbit ke liye gravity hi centripetal force: GMm/r² = mv²/r.

  • Near-Earth orbit: vo ≈ 7.9 km/s = ve/√2 ⭐ (escape se √2 guna kam!).
  • Time period: T² = 4π²r³/GM — T² ∝ r³ (Kepler's third law!). Door ka satellite = slow + lambi orbit.
  • Geostationary satellite: T = 24 hr, equator ke upar, height ≈ 36,000 km — Earth ke saath same rate ghoomta hai, isliye dish ek jagah fix rehti hai (TV!).
  • Weightlessness ⭐: satellite me sab (astronaut + ship) same g se FREE FALL me hain — isliye float karte hain. Gravity ZERO nahi hai wahan (~90% hai), bas sab saath-saath gir raha hai!
  • Energy of satellite: E = −GMm/2r (negative = bound). KE = +GMm/2r, PE = −GMm/r.

🎯 Satellite ka secret: woh lagatar Earth KI TARAF gir raha hai, bas itni tezi se aage badh raha hai ki Earth usse "chhoot" jaati hai — orbit = permanent free fall!

5️⃣ Kepler Ke 3 Laws

Planetary motion ke observational laws (Newton ne baad me gravity se prove kiye):

  • Law 1 (Orbits): planets ellipse me chalte hain, sun ek FOCUS pe (center pe nahi!).
  • Law 2 (Areas) ⭐: planet-sun line equal areas in equal times sweep karti hai — paas (perihelion) = fast, door (aphelion) = slow. (Angular momentum conservation ka result!)
  • Law 3 (Periods) ⭐: T² ∝ a³ (a = semi-major axis). Door ke planets = BAHUT lambe years: Neptune ka year = 165 Earth years!
  • Ye laws satellites (moon, artificial) pe bhi lagte hain — bas center planet ho jaata hai.

💡 Feel: Law 2 ek skater jaisa hai — paas aao, spin fast; door jao, slow. Orbit ka angular momentum fix hai!

1️⃣ न्यूटन का सार्वत्रिक नियम — हर वस्तु हर वस्तु को खींचती है

गुरुत्वाकर्षण का सार्वत्रिक नियम ⭐: F = G·m₁m₂/r² — दो द्रव्यमानों के बीच आकर्षण, दूरी के वर्ग के व्युत्क्रमानुपाती।

  • G = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg² — सार्वत्रिक नियतांक (कैवेंडिश ने मापा)।
  • व्युत्क्रम वर्ग ⭐: दूरी दुगुनी → बल 1/4; तिगुनी → 1/9।
  • बल सदैव आकर्षण प्रकृति का, दोनों द्रव्यमानों पर समान-विपरीत।
  • g का निगमन: mg = GMm/R² → g = GM/R² — द्रव्यमान निरस्त! इसलिए सभी वस्तुएँ समान दर से गिरती हैं (निर्वात में पंख = हथौड़ा!)।

💡 आप भी पृथ्वी को उतने ही बल से खींचते हैं — बस आपका द्रव्यमान छोटा है!

🎯 परीक्षा प्रश्न: "g अधिकतम कहाँ?" — ध्रुवों पर! "केंद्र पर?" — शून्य!

3️⃣ गुरुत्वीय PE और पलायन वेग ⭐

गुरुत्वीय स्थितिज ऊर्जा: U = −GMm/r — ऋण चिह्न आवश्यक! (अनंत पर U = 0; पास आने पर ऋणात्मक = बंधा निकाय।)

  • विभव: V = −GM/r • क्षेत्र: E = GM/r²।
  • पलायन वेग ⭐: ve = √(2GM/R) = √(2gR) — पृथ्वी के लिए 11.2 km/s। इस चाल से फेंको तो वस्तु कभी वापस नहीं आती।
  • पलायन वेग कोण या वस्तु के द्रव्यमान पर निर्भर नहीं — केवल ग्रह के M और R पर! चंद्रमा पर 2.4 km/s।
  • कक्षा शर्त: v < ve → बंधा (दीर्घवृत्त), v = ve → परवलय (पलायन), v > ve → अतिपरवलय।

💡 गुरुत्व एक कुआँ है — पलायन वेग वह लात जो कुएँ से बाहर फेंक दे!

4️⃣ उपग्रह — गिरता हुआ, कभी नहीं गिरता ⭐

कक्षीय वेग: vo = √(GM/r) — वृत्ताकार कक्षा में गुरुत्व ही अभिकेंद्रीय बल: GMm/r² = mv²/r।

  • निकट पृथ्वी कक्षा: vo ≈ 7.9 km/s = ve/√2 ⭐।
  • आवर्तकाल: T² = 4π²r³/GM — T² ∝ r³ (केप्लर का तृतीय नियम!)।
  • भू-स्थैतिक उपग्रह: T = 24 घंटे, विषुवत रेखा के ऊपर, ऊँचाई ≈ 36,000 km — डिश एक जगह स्थिर (TV!)।
  • भारहीनता ⭐: उपग्रह में सब (अंतरिक्ष यात्री + यान) समान g से मुक्त पतन में — इसलिए तैरते हैं। वहाँ गुरुत्व शून्य नहीं (~90%), बस सब साथ-साथ गिर रहे हैं!
  • उपग्रह की ऊर्जा: E = −GMm/2r (ऋणात्मक = बंधा)। KE = +GMm/2r, PE = −GMm/r।

🎯 उपग्रह का रहस्य: वह लगातार पृथ्वी की ओर गिर रहा है, पर इतनी तेज़ी से आगे बढ़ रहा है कि पृथ्वी "छूट" जाती है!

5️⃣ केप्लर के 3 नियम

ग्रहीय गति के प्रेक्षणीय नियम (न्यूटन ने बाद में गुरुत्व से सिद्ध किए):

  • नियम 1 (कक्षाएँ): ग्रह दीर्घवृत्त में चलते हैं, सूर्य एक फोकस पर (केंद्र पर नहीं!)।
  • नियम 2 (क्षेत्रफल) ⭐: ग्रह-सूर्य रेखा समान समय में समान क्षेत्रफल काटती है — पास = तीव्र, दूर = मंद (कोणीय संवेग संरक्षण!)।
  • नियम 3 (आवर्तकाल) ⭐: T² ∝ a³ (a = अर्ध-दीर्घ अक्ष)। दूर के ग्रह = बहुत लंबे वर्ष: नेपच्यून का वर्ष = 165 पृथ्वी वर्ष!
  • ये नियम उपग्रहों पर भी लागू — केंद्र में ग्रह आ जाता है।

💡 नियम 2 स्केटर जैसा — पास आओ, तीव्र; दूर जाओ, मंद।

1️⃣ Newton's Universal Law — Everything Pulls Everything

Universal law of gravitation ⭐: F = G·m₁m₂/r² — attraction between two masses, inversely proportional to the square of the distance.

  • G = 6.674 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg² — a universal constant, same everywhere (measured by Cavendish).
  • Inverse square ⭐: double the distance → quarter the force; triple → one-ninth. That is why distant planets feel weak gravity!
  • The force is always attractive (gravity never repels), equal and opposite on both masses (third-law pair).
  • Deriving g: mg = GMm/R² → g = GM/R² — mass cancels! That is why everything falls at the same rate (in vacuum, feather = hammer!).

💡 Feel: you pull the Earth with the same force it pulls you! Your mass is just tiny, so only YOUR acceleration shows.

2️⃣ Variation of g — Up, Down, Sideways

g is not the same everywhere — three classic variations:

  • Height carries a 2h/R factor, depth d/R — g falls FASTER with height (factor of 2!).

🎯 Exam favourite: "where is g maximum?" — the poles! "At the center?" — ZERO!

3️⃣ Gravitational PE and Escape Velocity ⭐

Gravitational potential energy: U = −GMm/r — the MINUS sign matters! (Reference: U = 0 at infinity; closer means negative energy, i.e. a bound system.)

  • Potential: V = −GM/r (per unit mass) • field: E = GM/r² (force per unit mass).
  • Escape velocity ⭐: ve = √(2GM/R) = √(2gR) — for Earth 11.2 km/s. Launch at this speed and the object never returns (its energy reaches zero, no longer bound).
  • Escape velocity depends on NEITHER the angle NOR the object's mass — only the planet's M and R! Moon: 2.4 km/s, Jupiter: 60 km/s.
  • Orbit condition: v < ve → bound (ellipse), v = ve → parabola (gone), v > ve → hyperbola.

💡 Feel: gravity is a well — escape velocity is the kick that throws you out of it. U = −GMm/r tells you how deep in the well you are!

4️⃣ Satellites — Falling, Yet Never Falling ⭐

Orbital velocity: vo = √(GM/r) — in a circular orbit gravity IS the centripetal force: GMm/r² = mv²/r.

  • Near-Earth orbit: vo ≈ 7.9 km/s = ve/√2 ⭐ (escape speed divided by √2!).
  • Time period: T² = 4π²r³/GM — T² ∝ r³ (Kepler's third law!). Farther satellite = slower, longer orbit.
  • Geostationary satellite: T = 24 hr, above the equator, height ≈ 36,000 km — rotates with the Earth, so your dish stays fixed (TV!).
  • Weightlessness ⭐: inside a satellite everything (astronaut + ship) is in free fall at the same g — that is why they float. Gravity there is NOT zero (~90% of surface) — everything is just falling together!
  • Satellite energy: E = −GMm/2r (negative = bound). KE = +GMm/2r, PE = −GMm/r.

🎯 The satellite's secret: it keeps falling TOWARD Earth, but moves forward so fast that Earth curves away — an orbit is a permanent free fall!

5️⃣ Kepler's 3 Laws

Observational laws of planetary motion (Newton later proved them from gravity):

  • Law 1 (Orbits): planets move in ellipses with the sun at one FOCUS (not the center!).
  • Law 2 (Areas) ⭐: the planet-sun line sweeps equal areas in equal times — near (perihelion) = fast, far (aphelion) = slow. (A result of angular momentum conservation!)
  • Law 3 (Periods) ⭐: T² ∝ a³ (a = semi-major axis). Distant planets have VERY long years: Neptune's year = 165 Earth years!
  • These laws apply to satellites too (moons, artificial) — the planet just takes the center.

💡 Feel: Law 2 is the skater again — come close, spin fast; go far, slow down. Orbital angular momentum stays fixed!

JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Do planets ke beech distance double kar do. Gravitational force ka kya hoga?
Answer dekho

Inverse square: F → F/4 (distance double → force one-fourth).

Q2 Earth ke surface pe g = 9.8. Height h = R/2 pe g kitna hoga (approx)?
Answer dekho

g' = g(1 − 2h/R) = g(1 − 1) = 0... exact formula: g' = g/(1+h/R)² = g/2.25 ≈ 4.36 m/s² (approx formula sirf h ≪ R ke liye valid!).

Q3 Mine me depth d = R/2 pe g?
Answer dekho

g' = g(1 − d/R) = g/2 = 4.9 m/s². (Depth pe linear ghatna, height pe faster!)

Q4 Earth ki escape velocity 11.2 km/s hai. 4 guna massive, same radius planet ki?
Answer dekho

v_e ∝ √M → √(4M) = 2× → 22.4 km/s.

Q5 Near-Earth satellite ki orbital velocity?
Answer dekho

v_o = √(gR) ≈ 7.9 km/s (= v_e/√2 = 11.2/1.414).

Q6 Satellite ka orbit radius 4 guna kar do. Time period kitna hoga?
Answer dekho

T² ∝ r³ → T ∝ r^1.5 → 4^1.5 = 8 guna.

Q7 Satellite me astronaut float kyun karta hai? Kya wahan gravity zero hai?
Answer dekho

Gravity zero nahi (~90% hai) — astronaut aur satellite dono same acceleration se free fall me hain, isliye relative motion nahi = floating feel.

Q8 Kepler ka second law kis conservation principle pe based hai?
Answer dekho

Angular momentum conservation — equal areas in equal times ka matlab L = constant (gravity central force hai, torque zero).

Q9 Geostationary satellite ke liye time period aur direction?
Answer dekho

T = 24 hours, equator ke upar west to east (Earth ki rotation ke saath), height ≈ 36,000 km.

Q10 Planet ka sun se average distance Earth ka 8 guna hai. Uska year kitne Earth-years?
Answer dekho

T² ∝ a³ → T = 8^1.5 = 22.6 Earth years.
