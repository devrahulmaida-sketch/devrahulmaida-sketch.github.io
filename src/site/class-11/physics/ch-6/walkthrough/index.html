Rotational Motion Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - COM se rolling race tak. Sliders ghumao, skater ke haath sameto, formulas feel karo.

1. Centre of Mass - System Ka Average Point

Do masses ek dande pe. Masses aur positions badlo - COM (yellow diamond) kaise shift hota hai dekho. Zyada mass apni taraf COM kheench leta hai!

xCOM = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂)/(m₁ + m₂) - mass-weighted average. Symmetric bodies ka COM geometric center (ring ke center pe - wahan material hai bhi nahi!). External force sirf COM ko accelerate karti hai: firecracker explode ho, tukde idhar-udhar, COM wahi parabola follow karta hai.

2. Torque - Ghumane Wala Force

Darwaze pe force lagao. Force kitna door hinge se (r), kitna bara (F), aur kis ANGLE pe (θ)? Nut-khulna meter neeche live hai.

τ = rF sin θ - hinge ke PAAS dhakka = kam torque, handle pe (door r) = zyada. Force rod ke ALONG lage (θ=0) to torque ZERO - isliye darwaza kheenchne se nahi khulta, PUSH karke khulta hai!

3. Skater Ka Trick - L = Iω Conservation

Ghoomti skater ke haath sameto ya failao (slider). Haath sametne se I ghatta hai - L = Iω ko SAME rakhne ke liye ω BADH jaata hai. Yehi diver ka tuck aur planet ka Kepler law hai!

L = Iω = constant (jab external torque zero). I = Σmr² - mass axis se JITNA DOOR, utna zyada I. I values: ring MR² | disc ½MR² | solid sphere (2/5)MR² | rod (center) ML²/12 | rod (end) ML²/3. τ = Iα = F = ma ka rotational version.

4. The Great Rolling Race - Sphere vs Disc vs Ring

Teen objects incline pe RACE. Same mass, same radius - phir bhi winner alag! Kaun jitna compact (mass axis ke paas), utna tez. Rolling = rotation + translation, bottom point instant REST pe.

v = √(2gh/(1 + I/MR²)) - I/MR² jitna CHHOTA utna FAST: solid sphere (2/5) > disc (1/2) > ring (1). Friction STATIC hai (bottom rest) - energy waste nahi, lekin friction ke BINA sirf slide hoga, rolling nahi!

5. Equilibrium - Book Kab Giregi?

Book ko table ke edge pe slide karo (slider). Jab tak COM table ke ANDAR hai - stable. COM edge CROSS kiya - torque palat gaya, book GIR jaati hai!

Static equilibrium ki 2 conditions: ΣF = 0 (na hilo) aur Στ = 0 (na ghoomo). Stable: COM neeche, thoda tilt karo to wapas. Unstable: COM upar - gir jaata hai. Couple = do equal-opposite forces: sirf torque, zero net force (steering wheel!).
