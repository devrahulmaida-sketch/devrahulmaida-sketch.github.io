1. Inertia Ki Race - Mass Hi Sab Kuch Hai

Newton ka first law: object apni state APNI marzi se nahi badalta - isi aalas ko inertia kehte hain. Mass = inertia ka measure! Dono blocks pe SAME force lagao aur dekho kaun zyada "na-nu-nu-nu" karta hai:

Force F (dono pe same)F = 10 N
Block A ka massmA = 6 kg
Feel: inertia = aalas! Bus achanak chale toh body PEECHE jhukti hai (rest mein rehna chahti hai), bus ruke toh AAGE girte hain (motion mein rehna chahti hai), turn pe seedha jaana chahti hai. Mass zyada = state badalna aur mushkil.

2. Catch Lagao - Impulse Ka Game

Second law ka asli roop: F = dp/dt (constant mass pe F = ma). Impulse J = F x Δt = Δp. Cricket ball ka momentum FIXED hai - par Δt badhao (haath peeche kheencho) toh F ghat jaata hai. Slider se catch time badlo:

catch time ΔtΔt = 0.15 s
Real life: cricket catch mein haath peeche, airbags, gym mats, helmet ka foam - sab Δt badha ke F ghatate hain. Exam trap: "object chal raha hai toh force zaroori hai" - GALAT! Uniform velocity pe net force ZERO. Force velocity ka CHANGE banata hai.

3. Lift Ka Weight - Normal Force Ka Jadoo

FBD ka rule: sirf OBJECT PE lage forces dikhao! Lift mein 2 forces: neeche mg (fixed), upar Normal (scale ka reading). Lift accelerate karo aur N badalta dekho - free fall mein N = 0 (weightlessness)!

lift acceleration a (upar +)a = 0.0 m/s2
Formula: upar accelerate → N = m(g+a) - BHAARI feel; neeche → N = m(g-a) - HALKA feel; free fall (a = -g) → N = 0 - weightless! Third law yaad rakho: action-reaction DO ALAG OBJECTS pe hote hain, isliye cancel NAHI hote.

4. Friction - Self-Adjusting Dost Ki Limit

Static friction self-adjusting hai - utna hi lagta hai jitna zaroorat, max μsN tak! Force dheere dheere badhao: block tikka rehta hai... phir SLIP! Uske baad kinetic friction (μkN, HAMESHA chhota) le leta hai:

applied force FF = 0 N
mu-s (roughness)mu-s = 0.50
Yaad rakho: fs ≤ μsN (self-adjusting), fk = μkN (constant), μk < μs hamesha, rolling sabse kam (isliye wheels!). Incline pe: mg sinθ neeche kheenchta, N = mg cosθ, slip condition tanθ > μs (angle of repose!).

5. Banked Road - Bina Friction Ka Turn

Circular turn ke liye centripetal force kisi REAL force se aana chahiye. Banked road pe Normal ka horizontal component kaam aata hai - friction ke bina bhi! Angle ghumao, ideal speed dekho. Velodrome aur railway tracks aise hi design hote hain:

banking angle θθ = 20°
turn radius rr = 100 m
Level road pe sirf friction centripetal deta hai: vmax = √(μrg) - isse tez toh skid! Banked: tanθ = v²/rg. Friction ke SAATH banked: vmax = √(rg(μ+tanθ)/(1-μtanθ)). Centrifugal force sirf ghoomte frame mein FEEL hota hai - inertial frame mein woh real NAHI hai!
