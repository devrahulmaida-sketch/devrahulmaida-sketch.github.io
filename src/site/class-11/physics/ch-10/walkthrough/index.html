Class 11 Physics - Chapter 10 - Thermal Properties of Matter

Garmi Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - absolute zero se Stefan-Boltzmann tak. Molecules ko freeze karo, bimetallic strip mudao, ice ko steam banao, conduction race lagao, filament ko chamkao.

1. Temperature vs Heat - Absolute Zero Tak

Temperature = molecules ki average kinetic energy ka indicator. Slider se °C badlo: molecules tez/mande hote dekho, teeno scales (°C, °F, K) saath dekho. 0 K pe motion MINIMUM - isse neeche kuch nahi!

T = 25 °C
Temperature alag, heat alag! Temperature = paani ka "level", heat = behta hua paani. Chhota cup aur bada bucket dono 25°C ho sakte hain, par bucket me zyada heat ENERGY hai. Kelvin absolute kyun: 0 K pe molecular motion minimum - negative Kelvin possible nahi!

2. Thermal Expansion - Garmi Me Phailna

ΔL = αLΔT - garm karo, lambai badhti hai. Upar: steel rail (expansion chhota par REAL - isliye railway tracks me gaps!). Neeche: bimetallic strip - do metals, alag α → garm karne pe MUD jaati hai. Thermostat ka heart!

ΔT = 0 °C
β ≈ 2α, γ ≈ 3α (area aur volume expansion) - exam favourite! Pendulum clock garmi me SLOW chalti hai (lambai badhi → time period badha). Aur paani 0-4°C me ULTA karta hai - garm karne pe SIKUDTA hai (4°C pe density max, isliye barf tairti hai!).

3. Latent Heat - Temperature RUK Jaata Hai

1 g ice (-20°C) ko heat do. Slider se heat daalo: T badhta hai... phir 0°C pe RUK jaata hai (melting!), phir 100°C pe RUK jaata hai (boiling!). Plateau pe heat kahan ja rahi hai? State todne me!

Heat daali = 0 J
Steam burns khatarnak kyun: 100°C steam pehle L_v = 2260 J/g release karti hai, PHIR paani ki tarah cool hoti hai - double damage! Melting plateau (334 J/g) boiling plateau (2260 J/g) se bahut CHHOTA hai - graph pe dekho!

4. Conduction Race - Copper vs Wood

H = kA(T₁−T₂)/d - conduction rate thermal conductivity k par depend. Dono rods ka left end garam (100°C): heat front KITNI TEZI se aage badhta hai? k_copper = 400, k_wood = 0.4 - 1000 guna ka fark!

Metals me free electrons heat bhi carry karte hain - isliye achhe electrical conductors achhe thermal bhi! Cooking utensils metal ke, handles lakdi/plastic ke. Convection alag hai: garam fluid KHUD upar uthta hai (sea breeze, room heater) - courier service, relay race nahi!

5. Radiation - T⁴ Ka Kamaal

Stefan-Boltzmann: P = eσAT⁴ - har body radiate karti hai, medium ki zaroorat NAHI (suraj ki dhoop isi se!). Slider se filament ka T badlo: T double → radiation 16 guna! Color bhi badalta hai (red → orange → white).

T = 800 K
Good absorbers are good emitters! Dark clothes garmi me jaldi garam, thandi jagah jaldi thande bhi. Bulb ka filament 2500 K pe hai - isliye itna chamakta hai. Newton's cooling: chai TAB sabse tez thandi hoti hai jab sabse garam ho (rate ∝ T difference)!
