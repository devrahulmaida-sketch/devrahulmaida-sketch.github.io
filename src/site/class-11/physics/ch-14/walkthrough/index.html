Waves Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - notes wale har concept ko chalta-dikhata hua. Sliders ghumao, patterns dekho, formulas feel karo.

1. Wave Kya Hai - Energy Chalti Hai, Matter Nahi

Neeche dots medium ke particles hain. Dekho: wave aage badhti hai, particles bas apni jagah oscillate karte hain - stadium ki Mexican wave jaisa.
Transverse: particles medium ke PERPENDICULAR hilti hain (crest-trough). Sirf solids + liquid surface pe. Longitudinal: particles PARALLEL hilti hain (compression-rarefaction) - solids, liquids, gases teeno me!

2. Wave Equation aur Speed - v = νλ

Sliders se wave ko morph karo. Speed v = νλ medium set karta hai, frequency source.
Travelling wave: y(x,t) = A sin(kx - ωt)   String pe speed: v = √(T/μ) - tight ya patli string → tez wave! Air me sound ≈ 340 m/s; v ∝ √T (Kelvin) - garmi me tez.

3. Superposition → Standing Wave - Pattern FREEZE

Do identical waves opposite directions me (red + blue). Neeche unka SUM. “Lock karo” - pattern ruk jaata hai: yahan Nodes aur Antinodes bante hain.
y = 2A sin(kx)cos(ωt)   Nodes (N): hamesha zero - khamosh points. Antinodes (A): maximum hilne wale. N-to-N = λ/2, N-to-A = λ/4. Standing wave me energy travel NAHI hoti - sab stored! Fixed end reflection: wave PALT ke aati hai (phase change π).

4. Organ Pipes - Open vs Closed Ka Khel

Pipe chuno, harmonic badhao. Air column ka standing pattern dekho - closed end = NODE, open end = ANTINODE (yaad rakhne ka rule!).
Open pipe: νₙ = nν₁ SAARE harmonics (string jaisa). Closed pipe: ν₁ = v/4L sirf ODD harmonics (1,3,5...) - 2ν₁ allowed NAHI! Same length ka open pipe DOUBLE fundamental deta hai. End correction e ≈ 0.3r per open end.

5. Beats - Do Frequencies Ka WAH-WAH

Do tuning forks thode alag frequencies pe. Envelope ka slow wah-wah = beats. Beat frequency = |ν₁ - ν₂|
Wax lagane se mass badhta hai → frequency GHAT-ti hai. Filing se frequency BADH-ti hai. Beat count sunke pata chalta hai kaunsa fork kitna off hai - piano tuners yahi karte hain! fbeat = |ν₁ - ν₂|

Formula Pack - Pocket Mein Daal Lo

Jo abhi animate hokar dikha, wahi yahan ek jagah.
v = νλ (medium decides v, source decides ν) String: v = √(T/μ) Standing wave: y = 2A sin(kx)cos(ωt) - N-N = λ/2 Open pipe: νₙ = nν₁ (saare harmonics) Closed pipe: ν₁ = v/4L (sirf ODD harmonics) Beats: fbeat = |ν₁ - ν₂| - wax = slow, filing = fast
