5 animated scenes - Bernoulli se viscosity tak. Pipe dabao, hydraulic lift chalao, cheezein dubao, tank se paani phenko, honey me ball girao.

1. Bernoulli - Pipe Patli, Pressure Kam

Paani pipe me behta hai: continuity A₁v₁ = A₂v₂ (patli jagah speed TEZ), aur Bernoulli: P + ½ρv² = constant (speed tez → pressure KAM!). Constriction slider ghumao, gauges dekho.

constriction = 2.0×
Yehi hai lift ka raaz: airplane wing ke upar hawa tez → pressure kam → lift. Toofan me roofs udd jaati hain, sprayer liquid kheenchta hai, spinning ball mudti hai (Magnus effect) - sab "speed zyada, pressure kam"!

2. Pascal's Law - Hydraulic Lift Ka Magic

Enclosed fluid pe pressure sab taraf equally transmit hota hai. Chhote piston pe chhota force, bade piston pe F₂ = F₁ × A₂/A₁ = 5×! Car 300 N ki hai - F₁ slider se kitna force chahiye?

F₁ = 40 N
Free lunch nahi hai: chhota piston 5× zyada NEECHE jaata hai, car 5× KAM upar - work dono taraf same (F₁·d₁ = F₂·d₂). Hydraulic brakes aur lifts isi pe chalte hain. Pressure scalar hai - har direction me same!

3. Buoyancy - Kaun Tairega, Kaun Doobega?

Archimedes: buoyant force = displaced paani ka weight (F_b = ρVg). Rule: density < paani → tairti hai, zyada → doobti hai, barabar → kahin bhi rukti hai. Cheez chuno aur tank me phenko!

Ice 0.9 density → 90% paani ke ANDAR (iceberg ka raaz - Titanic!). Steel ki ship tairti hai kyunki hollow design se average density paani se kam. Apparent weight = actual weight − buoyant force.

4. Torricelli - Tank Ke Chhed Ka Fountain

Chhed se nikalne ki speed v = √(2gh) - utni hi jitni h height se GIRNE pe hoti! Hole ki height slider se badlo: stream kitni door jaati hai? Range MAX at h = H/2 (range = 2√(y(H−y))).

hole height y = 0.50×H
Torricelli ka theorem Bernoulli ka special case hai. Tank ke top aur chhed - dono pe atmospheric pressure, to sirf ρgh bachta hai → ½ρv² = ρgh. y = H/2 pe range maximum = H (poore tank ke barabar!).

5. Viscosity Race - Paani vs Honey

Viscosity = fluid ka androoni friction (η). Stokes' law: drag F = 6πηrv. Jab weight = buoyancy + drag → terminal velocity v_t = 2r²(ρ−σ)g/9η - honey me ball DHIRE settle hoti hai. Race chalao!

Baarish ki boondein chot nahi karti - terminal velocity pe pahunch jaati hain! Temperature badhane pe liquids ka η GHATTA hai, gases ka BADHTA hai (classic MCQ!). Surface tension: boondein isliye GOL hain (min area), detergent S ghata deta hai.
