🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Physics Ki Language — Physical Quantities aur Units

Physics mein jo bhi measure kar sakte hain woh physical quantity hai — length, time, mass, velocity, force... Har quantity = number × unit. "5 metre" me 5 number, metre unit.

  • Fundamental (base) quantities (7): length (metre), mass (kg), time (second), current (ampere), temperature (kelvin), amount (mole), luminous intensity (candela). Ye sab ke building blocks hain.
  • Derived quantities: baaki sab base se bante — speed (m/s), force (kg·m/s² = newton), energy (joule), pressure (pascal).
  • SI system duniya bhar ka standard hai — isliye science me sab same units bolte hain.
  • Supplementary: plane angle (radian), solid angle (steradian).

💡 Feel: fundamental units Lego ke basic blocks hain — derived units unse bane castles. Force banana hai? Mass × acceleration ka block jodo!

2️⃣ Dimensions — Har Quantity ka DNA ⭐

Har derived quantity ko base quantities ke powers me likhna = dimensional formula. Base: [M] mass, [L] length, [T] time, [A] current, [K] temperature.

QuantityFormulaDimensions
Speeddistance/time[LT⁻¹]
Accelerationspeed/time[LT⁻²]
Forcemass × acceleration[MLT⁻²]
Work/Energyforce × distance[ML²T⁻²]
Pressureforce/area[ML⁻¹T⁻²]
Powerwork/time[ML²T⁻³]

Dimensional analysis ke 3 superpowers ⭐:

  1. Formula check karna: equation ke dono sides ke dimensions SAME hone chahiye — warna formula galat! (LHS = RHS dimensionally)
  2. Formula nikaalna: jaise pendulum ka time period T ∝ lᵃgᵇ maan ke dimensions match karo → T = 2π√(l/g) milta hai.
  3. Units convert karna: 1 newton = 10⁵ dyne (dimensional conversion se).

🎯 Limitation: dimensionless constants (jaise 2π) nahi milte analysis se. Aur trigonometric/exponential functions pe kaam nahi karta.

3️⃣ Significant Figures + Errors — Measurement Ka Sach ⭐

Koi bhi measurement 100% exact nahi hota — uski "sachai" significant figures aur errors batate hain.

Significant figures (sig figs) rules:

  • Non-zero digits sab significant: 234 → 3 sig figs.
  • Beach ke zeroes significant: 2.03 → 3. Leading zeroes NAHI: 0.0023 → 2 sig figs. Trailing zeroes decimal ke baad significant: 2.300 → 4.
  • Multiply/divide me answer utne sig figs jitne KAM wale number me. Add/subtract me decimal places dekho.

Errors (exam favourite):

  • Absolute error = |measured − true| • Relative error = Δa/a • Percentage error = (Δa/a) × 100.
  • Combination rules: Z = A + B ya A − B → ΔZ = ΔA + ΔB (errors JUDTE hain!). Z = AᵖBᑫ → ΔZ/Z = p(ΔA/A) + q(ΔB/B) — powers multiply ho jaate hain!

💡 Feel: errors dosti nahi, bill hai — divide ya multiply karo, percentage shares add ho jaate hain!

4️⃣ Measurement Techniques — Chhota se Chhota, Bada se Bada

  • Vernier callipers: 0.01 cm tak measure. Least count = 1 MSD − 1 VSD.
  • Screw gauge (micrometer): 0.001 cm (0.01 mm) tak. LC = pitch / circular scale divisions.
  • Parallax method (bade distances): door star/planet ka distance — Earth ki orbit ke dono ends se angle (parallax) measure karo: D = b/θ. 1 parsec = 3.26 light years — wo distance jahan se 1 AU ka angle 1 arc-second lage.
  • Size estimation: oleic acid film se molecule ka size (~10⁻¹⁰ m); electron microscope se atoms.

Range yaad rakho (exam me aata hai):

  • Smallest: nucleus ~10⁻¹⁴ m • atom ~10⁻¹⁰ m • red blood cell ~10⁻⁵ m.
  • Biggest: observable universe ~10²⁶ m. Mass: electron 10⁻³⁰ kg, universe 10⁵⁵ kg. Time: light travel across nucleus 10⁻²² s, age of universe 10¹⁷ s.

🎯 Prefix table zaroori: kilo (10³), mega (10⁶), giga (10⁹), milli (10⁻³), micro (10⁻⁶), nano (10⁻⁹), pico (10⁻¹²).

1️⃣ भौतिकी की भाषा — भौतिक राशियाँ और मात्रक

भौतिकी में जो कुछ भी मापा जा सकता है वह भौतिक राशि है — लंबाई, समय, द्रव्यमान, वेग, बल... प्रत्येक राशि = संख्या × मात्रक।

  • मूल (आधारभूत) राशियाँ (7): लंबाई (मीटर), द्रव्यमान (किग्रा), समय (सेकंड), धारा (ऐम्पियर), ताप (केल्विन), पदार्थ की मात्रा (मोल), ज्योति तीव्रता (कैंडेला)।
  • व्युत्पन्न राशियाँ: शेष सभी मूल राशियों से बनती हैं — चाल (m/s), बल (kg·m/s² = न्यूटन), ऊर्जा (जूल), दाब (पास्कल)।
  • SI पद्धति विश्व-मानक है। पूरक: समतल कोण (रेडियन), ठोस कोण (स्टेरेडियन)।

2️⃣ विमाएँ — हर राशि का DNA ⭐

प्रत्येक व्युत्पन्न राशि को मूल राशियों की घातों में लिखना = विमीय सूत्र। आधार: [M] द्रव्यमान, [L] लंबाई, [T] समय, [A] धारा, [K] ताप।

राशिसूत्रविमाएँ
चालदूरी/समय[LT⁻¹]
त्वरणचाल/समय[LT⁻²]
बलद्रव्यमान × त्वरण[MLT⁻²]
कार्य/ऊर्जाबल × दूरी[ML²T⁻²]
दाबबल/क्षेत्रफल[ML⁻¹T⁻²]
शक्तिकार्य/समय[ML²T⁻³]

विमीय विश्लेषण के 3 उपयोग ⭐:

  1. सूत्र जाँचना: समीकरण के दोनों पक्षों की विमाएँ समान होनी चाहिए।
  2. सूत्र निकालना: जैसे लोलक का आवर्तकाल T ∝ lᵃgᵇ से → T = 2π√(l/g)।
  3. मात्रक परिवर्तन: 1 न्यूटन = 10⁵ डाइन।

🎯 सीमा: विमाहीन स्थिरांक (2π) नहीं मिलते; त्रिकोणमितीय/घातांकीय फलनों पर लागू नहीं।

3️⃣ सार्थक अंक + त्रुटियाँ — मापन का सच ⭐

सार्थक अंकों के नियम:

  • सभी अशून्य अंक सार्थक: 234 → 3। बीच के शून्य सार्थक: 2.03 → 3। आरंभिक शून्य नहीं: 0.0023 → 2। दशमलव के बाद अंतिम शून्य सार्थक: 2.300 → 4।
  • गुणा/भाग में उत्तर में उतने ही सार्थक अंक जितने न्यूनतम वाली संख्या में।

त्रुटियाँ (परीक्षा-पसंदीदा):

  • निरपेक्ष त्रुटि = |मापित − वास्तविक| • सापेक्ष त्रुटि = Δa/a • प्रतिशत त्रुटि = (Δa/a) × 100।
  • संयोजन: Z = A + B या A − B → ΔZ = ΔA + ΔB (त्रुटियाँ जुड़ती हैं!)। Z = AᵖBᑫ → ΔZ/Z = p(ΔA/A) + q(ΔB/B)।

4️⃣ मापन विधियाँ — सूक्ष्म से विशाल तक

  • वर्नियर कैलिपर्स: 0.01 cm तक। अल्पतमांक = 1 MSD − 1 VSD।
  • स्क्रू गेज (माइक्रोमीटर): 0.001 cm तक। LC = pitch / वृत्तीय स्केल विभाजन।
  • लंबन विधि (दूरियाँ): पृथ्वी की कक्षा के दोनों सिरों से कोण मापें: D = b/θ। 1 पारसेक = 3.26 प्रकाश-वर्ष।
  • आकार आकलन: ओलिक अम्ल फिल्म से अणु का आकार (~10⁻¹⁰ m)।

परिसर याद रखें: नाभिक ~10⁻¹⁴ m • परमाणु ~10⁻¹⁰ m • ब्रह्मांड ~10²⁶ m। इलेक्ट्रॉन 10⁻³⁰ kg, ब्रह्मांड 10⁵⁵ kg।

🎯 उपसर्ग: किलो (10³), मेगा (10⁶), गीगा (10⁹), मिली (10⁻³), माइक्रो (10⁻⁶), नैनो (10⁻⁹), पिको (10⁻¹²)।

1️⃣ The Language of Physics — Physical Quantities and Units

Anything physics can measure is a physical quantity — length, time, mass, velocity, force... Every quantity = number × unit. In "5 metres", 5 is the number and metre is the unit.

  • Fundamental (base) quantities (7): length (metre), mass (kg), time (second), current (ampere), temperature (kelvin), amount of substance (mole), luminous intensity (candela). These are the building blocks.
  • Derived quantities: everything else is built from the base ones — speed (m/s), force (kg·m/s² = newton), energy (joule), pressure (pascal).
  • The SI system is the world standard. Supplementary: plane angle (radian), solid angle (steradian).

💡 Feel: fundamental units are basic Lego blocks — derived units are castles built from them. Want force? Join the mass × acceleration blocks!

2️⃣ Dimensions — every quantity's DNA ⭐

Writing any derived quantity as powers of the base ones gives its dimensional formula. Base: [M] mass, [L] length, [T] time, [A] current, [K] temperature.

QuantityFormulaDimensions
Speeddistance/time[LT⁻¹]
Accelerationspeed/time[LT⁻²]
Forcemass × acceleration[MLT⁻²]
Work/Energyforce × distance[ML²T⁻²]
Pressureforce/area[ML⁻¹T⁻²]
Powerwork/time[ML²T⁻³]

Three superpowers of dimensional analysis ⭐:

  1. Checking a formula: both sides of an equation must have the SAME dimensions — otherwise it's wrong!
  2. Deriving a formula: assume a pendulum's period T ∝ lᵃgᵇ, match dimensions → T = 2π√(l/g).
  3. Converting units: 1 newton = 10⁵ dyne.

🎯 Limitations: dimensionless constants (like 2π) can't be found this way, and it doesn't work for trigonometric/exponential functions.

3️⃣ Significant Figures + Errors — the truth of measurement ⭐

No measurement is 100% exact — significant figures and errors tell you how truthful it is.

Significant figures rules:

  • All non-zero digits count: 234 → 3 sig figs. Zeros BETWEEN digits count: 2.03 → 3. Leading zeros DON'T: 0.0023 → 2. Trailing zeros after a decimal DO: 2.300 → 4.
  • In multiply/divide, the answer keeps as many sig figs as the LEAST precise number. In add/subtract, look at decimal places.

Errors (exam favourite):

  • Absolute error = |measured − true| • Relative error = Δa/a • Percentage error = (Δa/a) × 100.
  • Combination rules: Z = A + B or A − B → ΔZ = ΔA + ΔB (errors ADD!). Z = AᵖBᑫ → ΔZ/Z = p(ΔA/A) + q(ΔB/B) — the powers multiply in!

4️⃣ Measurement Techniques — from the tiny to the huge

  • Vernier callipers: measures to 0.01 cm. Least count = 1 MSD − 1 VSD.
  • Screw gauge (micrometer): to 0.001 cm (0.01 mm). LC = pitch / circular scale divisions.
  • Parallax method (large distances): measure a star's angle from two ends of Earth's orbit: D = b/θ. 1 parsec = 3.26 light years.
  • Size estimation: the oleic acid film gives a molecule's size (~10⁻¹⁰ m).

Ranges to remember: nucleus ~10⁻¹⁴ m • atom ~10⁻¹⁰ m • red blood cell ~10⁻⁵ m • observable universe ~10²⁶ m. Electron 10⁻³⁰ kg, universe 10⁵⁵ kg.

🎯 Prefix table: kilo (10³), mega (10⁶), giga (10⁹), milli (10⁻³), micro (10⁻⁶), nano (10⁻⁹), pico (10⁻¹²).

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Force ke dimensions [MLT⁻²] hain. Energy (force × distance) ke dimensions nikalo.
Answer dekho

[MLT⁻²] × [L] = [ML²T⁻²] — joule ka dimensional formula!

Q2. Check karo: kya s = ut + ½at² dimensionally sahi hai?
Answer dekho

ut: [LT⁻¹][T] = [L] ✓ • ½at²: [LT⁻²][T²] = [L] ✓ • s: [L] ✓. Sahi hai — teeno terms [L]!

Q3. Pendulum ka time period T mass m, length l, aur g pe depend karta hai (T ∝ mᵃlᵇgᶜ). a, b, c nikalo.
Answer dekho

[T] = [M]ᵃ[L]ᵇ[LT⁻²]ᶜ → M: a=0; T: 1=−2c → c=−½; L: 0=b+c → b=½. T ∝ √(l/g) — mass pe depend nahi karta!

Q4. 1 newton kitne dyne? (CGS: g·cm/s²)
Answer dekho

N = kg·m/s² = 10³g × 10²cm /s² = 10⁵ g·cm/s² = 10⁵ dyne

Q5. Pressure [ML⁻¹T⁻²] ko force aur area ke dimensions se verify karo.
Answer dekho

P = F/A = [MLT⁻²]/[L²] = [ML⁻¹T⁻²] ✓

Q6. 0.00450 × 10³ me kitne significant figures?
Answer dekho

Leading zeroes count nahi hote, 4-5-0 (decimal ke baad trailing zero counts) → 3 significant figures

Q7. Ek length 5.2 ± 0.1 cm aur doosri 3.8 ± 0.1 cm. Dono ka sum aur usme error?
Answer dekho

Sum = 9.0 cm, ΔZ = ΔA + ΔB = 0.2 cm → 9.0 ± 0.2 cm (errors judte hain, subtract bhi karo to!)

Q8. Resistance R = V/I, jahan V = 100 ± 2 V, I = 5.0 ± 0.1 A. Percentage error in R?
Answer dekho

ΔR/R = ΔV/V + ΔI/I = 2/100 + 0.1/5 = 2% + 2% = 4%. R = 20 ± 0.8 Ω

Q9. Screw gauge jiski pitch 0.5 mm aur circular scale pe 50 divisions — least count?
Answer dekho

LC = pitch/divisions = 0.5/50 = 0.01 mm

Q10. 1 parsec kitna hota hai — light years me?
Answer dekho

3.26 light years (≈ 3.08 × 10¹⁶ m) — wo distance jahan se 1 AU ka parallax angle exactly 1 arc-second lage.
