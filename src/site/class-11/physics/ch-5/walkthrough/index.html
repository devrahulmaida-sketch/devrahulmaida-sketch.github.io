1. Work Ka Sign - Angle Hi Boss Hai

Work W = F·s·cosθ - scalar hai, par + / - / 0 sab ho sakta hai! Block right ja raha hai, force ka angle ghumao aur dekho W kab positive, kab ZERO, kab negative:

Force ka angle θ (displacement se)θ = 30°
Zero work ke 2 star cases: θ = 90° (force ⊥ displacement - isliye circular motion me centripetal force KAAM NAHI karta!) aur displacement = 0 (deewar ko dhakka lagate raho, deewar nahi hili - work ZERO). Bag utha ke khade rehna = physics mein ZERO work, chahe thakan ho!

2. Braking Distance - Speed Double, Rasta 4x!

Work-energy theorem: W_net = ΔK. Brake ka friction gaadi ki POORI kinetic energy kha jaata hai: f·d = ½mv² → d = v²/(2μg). Speed badhao aur stopping distance ka explosion dekho:

speed v0v0 = 10 m/s
mu (tyre-road friction)mu = 0.50
Square ka kamaal: KE = ½mv² - speed DOUBLE toh KE 4x, braking distance bhi 4x! 20 se 40 m/s jaoge toh rukne mein 4 GUNA lambi road chahiye. Isliye "thoda zyada speed" bhi bahut zyada khatarnak hai.

3. Energy Bank Account - KE + PE Pakka Constant

Sirf gravity kaam kare (no friction) toh mechanical energy conserve: girte hue ball ki PE ghatti, KE badhti - TOTAL hamesha same! Height badlo, landing speed v = √(2gh) verify karo - mass toh cancel hi ho jaata hai:

drop height hh = 10 m
Bank account socho: PE aur KE mein transfer hota rehta hai, balance same (jab tak friction "fee" na kaate). Pendulum: ends pe sab PE, bottom pe sab KE. Roller coaster bhi yahi - height se seedha speed: v = √(2gh).

4. Collision Lab - Momentum Pakka, KE Shart Se

HAR collision mein momentum conserve (external force ~0), par KE sirf ELASTIC mein bachti hai! Equal masses ka elastic collision = velocities EXCHANGE (Newton's cradle ka raaz). Mode badlo aur dekho:

cart 1 mass (cart 2 = 4 kg static)m1 = 4 kg
cart 1 speed u1u1 = 10 m/s
1D elastic formulas: v1 = ((m1-m2)/(m1+m2))u1, v2 = (2m1/(m1+m2))u1. m1 = m2 → poora EXCHANGE! Heavy hits light → light ~2x se udta. Perfectly inelastic: v = (m1u1)/(m1+m2), dono chipak ke saath. e = separation/approach: elastic e = 1, chipakne pe e = 0.

5. Power Ka Tradeoff - P = F x v

Power = kaam kitni JALDI hua: P = W/t = F·v. Cyclist ka power fixed hai - chadhai pe gravity ke against zyada FORCE chahiye, toh speed GIR jaati hai. Isliye uphill pe gear DOWN karte hain! Slope badhao:

cyclist ka power PP = 400 W
slope angleslope = 4°
Yaad rakho: 1 HP = 746 W (ghode ki power se compare!). Bijli ka bill: 1 UNIT = 1 kWh = 3.6 x 10^6 J - yeh ENERGY hai, power NAHI! Constant P pe: speed badhao toh force ghatti hai - truck chadhai pe slow kyun? Kyunki F = P/v!
