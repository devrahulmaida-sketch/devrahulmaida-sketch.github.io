Straight Line Motion Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - distance vs displacement, x-t aur v-t graphs ka slope-area khel, free fall ka apex trap, aur trains ka relative velocity.

1. Distance vs Displacement - Odometer vs Seedha Arrow

Car ko road pe ghumao: distance odometer hai (kabhi kam nahi hota, hamesha positive), displacement final minus initial position (vector - sign hi direction hai). Round trip pe displacement ZERO, distance 560 km!

trip progress 0%

2. x-t Graph - Slope HI Velocity Hai

Constant velocity se car chalao aur x-t graph LIVE banta dekho: slope = dx/dt = v. Positive v → upar jaati line, negative v → neeche, v = 0 → horizontal (rest). Steeper = faster!

velocity v = 10 m/s

3. v-t Graph - Slope = a, Area = Displacement

u = 20 m/s se shuru, constant acceleration slider se badlo: v-t line ka slope = a, neeche ka SIGNED area = displacement. Negative a ka matlab direction change bhi ho sakta hai - jab v-t line time axis CROSS kare, wahan v = 0 aur gaadi PALAT jaati hai!

acceleration a = 0 m/s²

4. Free Fall - Upar Phenko, Apex Ka Trap

Ball ko upar phenko: H = u²/2g, T = 2u/g. EXAM TRAP: highest point pe v = 0 par a = −g abhi bhi lag raha hai! Velocity shrink hoti hai, zero, phir neeche badhti hai - wapas launch point pe speed = u (direction neeche).

throw speed u u = 20 m/s

5. Relative Velocity - Trains Ka Khel

vAB = vA − vB - "uski nazar se main kitna tez hoon". OPPOSITE direction: relative speed = dono ka SUM (trains jaldi cross hoti hain). SAME direction: DIFFERENCE (overtake slow-mo lagta hai). Dono trains 100 m lambi:

train A speed 25 m/s
train B speed 15 m/s

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo!

Distance vs Displacement

distance = total path (scalar, kabhi negative nahi) | displacement = x_final − x_initial (vector, round trip = 0) | avg speed = total distance / time | avg velocity = displacement / time (round trip = 0!)

Velocity aur Acceleration

v = dx/dt (x-t graph ka slope) | a = dv/dt | speed up: a aur v SAME sign | retardation: OPPOSITE signs | TRAP: v = 0 ≠ a = 0 (ball at top) | a = 0 ≠ rest (uniform velocity)

Equations of Motion (constant a) ⭐

v = u + at | s = ut + ½at² | v² = u² + 2as | n-th second: s_n = u + (a/2)(2n−1) | rest se: 1:3:5:7 ratio (odd numbers!)

Graphs ⭐

x-t: slope = velocity | v-t: slope = acceleration, SIGNED area = displacement (unsigned = distance) | a-t: area = velocity change | v-t line time axis CROSS = direction change (v = 0 wahan) | shortcut: "slope next, area previous"

Free Fall + Relative Velocity

upar phenko: H = u²/2g, T = 2u/g, wapas same point pe speed = u | drop: h = ½gt², t = √(2h/g) | apex pe v = 0, a = −g (trap!) | v_AB = v_A − v_B | opposite: sum, same direction: difference
