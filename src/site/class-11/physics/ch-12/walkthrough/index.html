Class 11 Physics - Chapter 12 - Kinetic Theory

Kinetic Theory Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - molecules ki bheed se Van der Waals tak. Temperature badhao, speeds dekho, energy pockets bharo.

1. Pressure Ka Asli Raaz - Wall Pe Bombardment

Gas = crores of tiny balls, lagatar random motion me. Pressure kuch aur nahi - molecules ke wall pe DHAKKE hain! Temperature badhao aur dekho: hits per second badh jaate hain (P = ⅓ρv²rms).

Kinetic theory postulates: molecules point-size, elastic collisions, beech me koi force nahi (ideal gas). P = (1/3)ρv²rms - speed double, pressure 4×! Isliye sealed dabba garam karne pe FATT sakta hai.

2. Temperature = Average Kinetic Energy

Temperature koi "garmi ka meter" nahi - yeh molecules ki average KE ka direct measure hai: KE = (3/2)kBT per molecule. T badhao, speedometer dekho. T = 0 pe sab RUK jaayenge (isliye absolute zero pahunchna impossible!).

Per molecule: KE = (3/2)kBT  |  per mole: KE = (3/2)RT. Ideal gas ki internal energy SIRF kinetic - sirf T pe depend karti hai, P ya V pe nahi! Speed ∝ √T (T chaar guna → speed double).

3. Maxwell Distribution - Kaun Kitna Fast?

Saare molecules same speed pe NAHI hote - ek distribution hai: zyadaatar middle me, kuch slow, kuch rocket-fast. Teen speeds yaad rakho: v_p < v_avg < v_rms. Gas badlo: halki gas (H₂) sabse tez!

Ratio pakka: v_p : v_avg : v_rms = 1 : 1.128 : 1.224 (√2 : √(8/π) : √3). vrms = √(3RT/M) - mass upar, speed neeche. Isliye H₂ atmosphere se escape kar jaati hai, O₂ nahi!

4. Degrees of Freedom - Energy Ke Pockets

Molecule energy kitne independent tareeko me store kar sakta hai, wahi degrees of freedom (f). Equipartition: har pocket ko (1/2)kBT milta hai - fair distribution! Monatomic vs Diatomic dekho.

U = (f/2)RT per mole  |  Cv = (f/2)R, Cp = (f/2 + 1)R  |  γ = 1 + 2/f: monatomic 5/3, diatomic 7/5 = 1.4. Room temp pe vibration soya hota hai (warna f = 7!).

5. Mean Free Path - Kitni Door Tak Free?

Ek molecule do collisions ke beech average kitna door jaata hai - wohi mean free path λ. Bheed badhao (pressure) aur λ GHAT jaata hai: λ = 1/(√2 πd²n). Air me sirf ~10⁻⁷ m!

Real gases: high P, low T pe ideal law FAIL - molecules ka apna volume + attraction. Van der Waals: (P + a/V²)(V − b) = RT (a = attraction, b = volume correction). Critical temperature ke upar gas liquefy nahi hoti!
