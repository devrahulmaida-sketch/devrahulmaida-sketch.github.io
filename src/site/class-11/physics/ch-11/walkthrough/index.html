Thermodynamics Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - P-V diagram se Carnot engine tak. Processes kheencho, heat ka batwara dekho, engine chalao, cycle ghumo.

1. P-V Diagram Ka Map - 4 Processes

Gas state A (V=1, P=4, T=400K) se shuru. Process chuno aur curve khichte dekho: shaded area = WORK (∫P dV). Adiabatic curve isothermal se zyada STEEP hoti hai (γ = 1.4)!

State function vs path function: ΔU sirf end states pe depend karta hai; Q aur W PATH pe. U = bank balance, Q/W = deposit-withdrawal ke tareeke! Cyclic process me ΔU = 0 → Q_net = W_net = loop ka area.

2. First Law - Heat Ka Batwara (ΔU = Q − W)

Piston free hai (isobaric). Heat daalo aur dekho energy ka split: diatomic gas me har 7 units heat me se 5 internal energy (gas garam) + 2 work (piston upar)! Isi liye C_p > C_v - Mayer: C_p − C_v = R.

Sign convention: Q positive jab heat SYSTEM me aaye, W positive jab SYSTEM work kare (gas expand ho). Isothermal: ΔU=0 → Q=W. Adiabatic: Q=0 → ΔU=−W (gas apni jeb se work karti hai, THANDI hoti hai). Isochoric: W=0 → ΔU=Q.

3. Race: Isothermal vs Adiabatic Expansion

Dono cylinders same state (V=1, P=4, T=400K) se double volume tak expand honge. Left: heat reservoir se juda (T constant). Right: insulated (Q=0) - gas apni energy kharch karke thandi ho jaati hai! Kaun zyada work karega?

Adiabatic relations: PVγ = constant, TVγ−1 = constant. Work: W = (P₁V₁ − P₂V₂)/(γ−1) = μR(T₁−T₂)/(γ−1). Isothermal: W = μRT ln(V₂/V₁). Adiabatic me KAM work milta hai - pressure jaldi girta hai!

4. Heat Engine - 100% Kyun IMPOSSIBLE?

Engine hot source (T₁) se Q₁ heat leta hai, kuch W work banata hai, baaki Q₂ cold sink ko DENA hi padta hai (Kelvin-Planck!). Arrows ki motai = energy. Sliders se T₁, T₂ badlo: Carnot limit η = 1 − T₂/T₁ se zyada koi engine nahi!

T₁ (hot) = 600 K
T₂ (cold) = 300 K
Carnot = speed limit of engines. η = W/Q₁ = 1 − Q₂/Q₁ ≤ 1 − T₂/T₁ (temperatures hamesha Kelvin me!). Refrigerator ulta hai: work daalo, heat cold se hot pump karo - COP = T₂/(T₁−T₂), 1 se ZYADA ho sakta hai!

5. Carnot Cycle - 4 Steps Ka Perfect Loop

Ideal reversible engine ka cycle: 2 isothermal + 2 adiabatic steps. Dot ke saath ghumo: T₁=400K, T₂=300K. Clockwise loop = positive net work = andar ka shaded area (W = 0.69 units, η = 25%).

Steps: A→B isothermal expansion (reservoir se Q₁ = 2.77 units), B→C adiabatic expansion (thandi 400→300K), C→D isothermal compression (sink ko Q₂), D→A adiabatic compression (wapas 400K). Anticlockwise chalao to refrigerator!
