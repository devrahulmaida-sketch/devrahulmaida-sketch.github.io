5. Rapid Quiz - Lines Pakki?

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: m = (y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁), perpendicular ⇒ m₁m₂ = −1, d = |Ax₁+By₁+C|/√(A²+B²)!

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo!

Slope ⭐

m = tan θ = (y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁) = rise/run | horizontal: m = 0 | vertical: m UNDEFINED | θ = x-axis se anti-clockwise angle

Parallel & Perpendicular ⭐

parallel: m₁ = m₂ | perpendicular: m₁ × m₂ = −1 | angle: tan θ = |(m₁−m₂)/(1+m₁m₂)| | collinear: slope(AB) = slope(BC)

5 Forms ⭐

point-slope: y−y₁ = m(x−x₁) | two-point: y−y₁ = m(x−x₁) with m = (y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁) | slope-intercept: y = mx + c | intercept: x/a + y/b = 1 | normal: x cos α + y sin α = p | general: Ax+By+C = 0, slope = −A/B

Distance ⭐

point (x₁,y₁) se Ax+By+C=0: d = |Ax₁+By₁+C|/√(A²+B²) | parallel lines: |C₁−C₂|/√(A²+B²) | origin se: |C|/√(A²+B²)

Tricks ⭐

family of lines through (x₁,y₁): y−y₁ = m(x−x₁) | foot of perpendicular = image ka midpoint | shifting origin: x = X+h, y = Y+k | general form se slope seedha: m = −A/B
