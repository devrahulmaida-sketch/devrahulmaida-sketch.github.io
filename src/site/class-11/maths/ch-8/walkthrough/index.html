Sequences & Series Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - AP ka live term builder with Gauss pairing, GP exponential visualiser, infinite GP jo 0.333... = 1/3 banata hai, Σn/Σn²/Σn³ ka visual proof, aur quiz.

1. AP Builder - Gauss Ki Pairing Trick ⭐

AP: har term pichle se d zyada. Gauss bachpan me Sₙ nikaal chuka tha: pehli + aakhri term jodo - har pair ka sum SAME! Sliders se a, d, n badlo aur pairs dekho:

a (pehli term) 3
d (common diff) 4
n (terms) 8

2. GP Machine - Exponential Dhamaka ⭐⭐

GP: har term pichli ka r guna. r > 1 to bars FAT-te hain (viral!), r < 1 to sikud-te hain. r chuno, a aur n ghumao - heights √-scale pe hain taaki sab dikhe:

a 2
n (terms) 6

3. Infinite GP - 0.333... Ka Raaz ⭐⭐

jab |r| < 1, terms marte jaate hain aur sum ek line pe ruk jaata hai: S∞ = a/(1−r). Partial sums ko asymptote ki taraf badhte dekho - aur 0.333... = 1/3 ka demo zaroor dabao:

a 4
r 0.50

4. Σn, Σn², Σn³ - Formula Ka Visual Proof ⭐⭐

Σn = 1+2+...+n ek seedha (staircase) hai - use ulta karke jodo to n × (n+1) ka rectangle banta hai, aadha = Σn! Mode chuno aur n ghumao:

n 6

5. Rapid Quiz - Sequence Pakki?

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: aₙ = a+(n−1)d, Sₙ(GP) = a(rⁿ−1)/(r−1), S∞ = a/(1−r), Σn² = n(n+1)(2n+1)/6!

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo!

AP ⭐

aₙ = a + (n−1)d | Sₙ = n/2 [2a + (n−1)d] = n/2 (a + aₙ) | AM = (a+b)/2 | Gauss pairing: (pehli+aakhri) × n/2

GP ⭐

aₙ = arⁿ⁻¹ | Sₙ = a(rⁿ−1)/(r−1), r≠1 | infinite sum S∞ = a/(1−r) sirf jab |r|<1 | GM = √(ab)

Special Sums ⭐

Σn = n(n+1)/2 | Σn² = n(n+1)(2n+1)/6 | Σn³ = [n(n+1)/2]² = (Σn)² | staircase + ulta staircase = rectangle!

Relations ⭐

a, b, c AP me → 2b = a+c | a, b, c GP me → b² = ac | AM ≥ GM hamesha (barabar sirf jab a=b)

Tricks ⭐

Gauss pairing se Sₙ seedha | differences se pattern pehchano (same diff = AP, same ratio = GP) | 0.333... = 3/10 + 3/100 + ... = infinite GP = 1/3
