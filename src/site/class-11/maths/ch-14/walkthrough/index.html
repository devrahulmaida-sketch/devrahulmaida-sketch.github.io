5. Rapid Quiz - Probability Pakki?

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo!

Basics ⭐

P(E) = n(E) / n(S) | S = saare outcomes, E = subset | 0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1 | P(φ) = 0 impossible, P(S) = 1 sure

Events ⭐

Complement: P(A′) = 1 − P(A) | Mutually exclusive: A∩B = φ (saath kabhi nahi) | A−B: P(A) − P(A∩B)

Addition Rule ⭐

P(A∪B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A∩B) | Exclusive: P(A∪B) = P(A) + P(B) | −P(A∩B) mat bhoolo - double count!

'At Least One' Trick ⭐

P(at least one) = 1 − P(none) = 1 − (1−p)ⁿ | 2 dice → 36 outcomes TABLE banao | n × p seedha karna GALAT

Cards (52) ⭐

4 suits × 13 | 12 face cards, 4 aces, 4 kings | 26 red + 26 black | P(face) = 12/52 = 3/13
