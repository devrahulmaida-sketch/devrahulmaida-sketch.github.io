Statistics Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - data ko DRAG karke dispersion feel karo, mean deviation aur variance ki machines, CV se consistency battle, aur quiz.

1. Dispersion Playground - Dots Ko DRAG Karo ⭐

Team A (cyan) aur Team B (gold) - dono ka mean SAME hai. Gold dots ko drag karo - spread badalte hi range aur σ LIVE badalte hain. Shaded band = mean ± σ. Kaun zyada consistent?

2. Mean Deviation - Doori Ka Average ⭐

5 values (sliders) aur unka mean (gold line). Har point ka mean se distance magenta segment hai. SIGNED vs ABSOLUTE toggle dabao - signed deviations ka sum HAMESHA 0 hota hai (isliye absolute lena padta hai!).

3. Variance & SD Machine - Square Karke Dekho ⭐

Mean deviation ka |x| clumsy hai - isliye square karte hain. Har squared deviation ek block (area = (xᵢ−x̄)²). Variance = blocks ka average, σ = uska root. DIRECT vs SHORTCUT toggle: σ² = mean of squares − square of mean - dono ka answer MATCH!

4. CV Battle - Kaun Zyada Consistent? ⭐

Do teams alag means ke saath - raw σ se compare nahi hota, isliye CV = (σ/mean) × 100. Sliders se dono teams ke mean aur σ ghumao - CV ka LIVE battle dekho. Kam CV = zyada consistent!

5. Rapid Quiz - Statistics Pakki?

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: σ² = (1/n)Σ(xᵢ−x̄)² = (1/n)Σxᵢ² − x̄², CV = σ/x̄ × 100, same number add ⇒ σ unchanged!

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo!

Dispersion ⭐

data mean se kitna BIKHRA hai | kam dispersion = zyada consistent | measures: Range, Mean Deviation, Variance, SD

Range & Mean Deviation

Range = max − min (sirf 2 extremes!) | M.D. about mean = (1/n)Σ|xᵢ − x̄| | grouped: Σfᵢ|xᵢ−x̄|/Σfᵢ | absolute KYUN? signed sum hamesha 0!

Variance & SD ⭐

σ² = (1/n)Σ(xᵢ−x̄)² | SHORTCUT: σ² = (1/n)Σxᵢ² − x̄² | grouped: (1/N)Σfᵢxᵢ² − x̄² | σ = √variance (original units me!)

CV ⭐

CV = (σ/x̄) × 100 | kam CV = zyada CONSISTENT | kab? jab means alag hon ya units alag hon | same mean to seedha σ compare karo

Properties & Tricks ⭐

har value + c ⇒ mean +c, σ UNCHANGED | har value × a ⇒ mean ×a, σ ×|a| | assumed mean: u = xᵢ−A | TABLE banao: xᵢ, fᵢ, fᵢxᵢ, xᵢ², fᵢxᵢ² - calculation mistakes hi asli villain!
