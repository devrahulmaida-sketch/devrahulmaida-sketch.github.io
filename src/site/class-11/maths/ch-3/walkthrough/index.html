Class 11 Mathematics - Chapter 3 - Trigonometric Functions

Trig Ka Visual Walkthrough

5 animated scenes - radians se compound angles tak. Radius ko circle pe lapeto, unit circle pe sin/cos dekho, wave ko circle se nikalo, bade angles ko todo, sin(x+y) khud banao.

1. Radian - Arc Ko Radius Se Naapo

1 radian = jab arc ki length radius ke BARABAR ho. Slider ghumao: kitne radius lambi arc bani? Utne radians! Pura circle = exactly 2π radian = 360°. π rad = 180°, 1 rad ≈ 57.3°.

θ = 57°
Radian kyun, degree kyun nahi? Degree me 360 koi natural number nahi - purani convention hai. Radian me arc formula l = rθ DIRECT chalta hai (degree me galat answer!). Isliye JEE/CBSE me radian default hai. Yaad rakho: 30° = π/6, 45° = π/4, 60° = π/3, 90° = π/2.

2. Unit Circle - sin θ = y, cos θ = x

Radius 1 wale circle pe angle θ ka point P(x, y): cos θ = x, sin θ = y - yehi DEFINITION hai, 90°+ ke liye bhi! Slider 720° tak ghumao (angles kitne bhi bade ho sakte hain). Quadrant badalte hi signs dekho - ASTC!

θ = 30°
ASTC = "All Students Take Chemistry": Q1 me SAB positive, Q2 me sirf Sin, Q3 me sirf Tan, Q4 me sirf Cos. Aur hamesha sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 - Pythagoras hi hai (x² + y² = r², r = 1)! tan undefined jahan cos = 0 (90°, 270°).

3. Wave Kahan Se Aati Hai - Circle Se!

Point circle pe ghoomta hai, uski height ko right pe plot karo - ban gayi sine wave! Play dabao aur dekho circle aur wave EXACTLY sync me. Period = 2π (ek pura chakra = ek pura wave). Cos = sin ko π/2 aage shift!

θ = 0°
Range yaad rakho: −1 ≤ sin x, cos x ≤ 1 hamesha (circle ki height 1 se upar kabhi nahi!). Par tan x KUCH BHI ho sakta hai (−∞, ∞). sin aur cos ka period 2π, tan ka sirf π. sin wave 0 se start, cos 1 se!

4. Bade Angles Ka Machine - Reference Angle

Koi bhi bada angle (720° tak!) do steps me solve: (1) reference angle nikalo (nearest x-axis se chhota angle), (2) quadrant se sign lagao (ASTC). Machine khud todti hai - slider ghumao!

θ = 210°
Trick: angle ko π/2 ke multiples me todo - EVEN multiple pe function SAME, ODD multiple pe cofunction (sin↔cos). Example: sin(7π/6) = sin(π + π/6) = −sin(π/6) = −1/2 (Q3 me sin negative!). sin(π − x) = sin x, sin(π + x) = −sin x.

5. Compound Angle Lab - sin(x+y) Khud Banao

sin(x+y) = sin x cos y + cos x sin y - yaad karne ki jagah VERIFY karo! x aur y sliders ghumao: direct sin(x+y) aur expansion ke do bars HAMESHA barabar. 75° button dabao: sin 75° ka exact value!

x = 45°
y = 30°
Memory trick: cos me sign FLIP hota hai (cos(x+y) = cos x cos y − sin x sin y - plus andar, MINUS bahar), sin me SAME rehta hai. sin 75° = (√6 + √2)/4 ≈ 0.966 - compound angle se EXACT values nikalte hain, calculator nahi chahiye!
