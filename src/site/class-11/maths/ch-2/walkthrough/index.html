Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo!

Cartesian Product ⭐

(a,b) ordered pair - ORDER matter karta hai | A × B = {(a,b) : a∈A, b∈B} | n(A×B) = n(A)·n(B) | A × B ≠ B × A (jab A≠B) | A × φ = φ | R × R = R² = coordinate plane

Relations ⭐

relation R = A × B ka koi bhi SUBSET | domain = involved first components | range = involved second components | codomain = poora B (range ⊆ codomain!) | total relations = 2^(n(A)·n(B)) subsets

Function + Vertical Line Test ⭐⭐

function: A ka HAR element ka EXACTLY EK image | koi chhoota nahi, kisi ke do nahi | many-to-one CHALTA HAI | vertical line test: ek se zyada cut = function NAHI | x²+y²=25 se y function nahi (ek x ke ±y)

Famous Graphs ⭐

|x|: V-shape, range [0,∞) | [x] floor: steps, [−2.3]=−3 (LEFT!) | sgn(x): {−1,0,1}, zero pe 0 | 1/x: hyperbola, x=0 pe undefined, do branches | identity f(x)=x | constant f(x)=c, range {c}

Algebra of Functions ⭐

(f+g)(x)=f(x)+g(x) | (f−g)=f−g | (fg)=f·g | (f/g)(x)=f(x)/g(x) jahan g(x)≠0 | domain = INTERSECTION (divide me denominator ke zeros hatao) | √x + 1/x ka domain = (0,∞)
