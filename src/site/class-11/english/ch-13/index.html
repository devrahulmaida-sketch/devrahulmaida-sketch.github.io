Father to Son (Poem)

Elizabeth Jennings — Hornbill Poem 5

Baap-beta ke beech ki khamoshi — generation gap ka dard

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Poet aur Poem ⭐⭐

  • Elizabeth Jennings ⭐⭐: English poet (1926-2001).
  • Vishay ⭐⭐⭐: ek pita ka apne bete se doori ka dard — generation gap.
  • Swar ⭐⭐: pita ka aatm-sweekar (confession) — woh samajhna aur rishta jodna chahta hai.
  • Structure ⭐: do stanza aur ek antim stanza — pita ki soch, phir dono ki aawaz.

💡 Father to Son = pita ka confession + generation gap + sulah ki chaah.

2️⃣ Stanza-wise Summary ⭐⭐⭐

  • Stanza 1 ⭐⭐⭐: 'I do not understand this child, though we have lived together now in the same house for years' — pita kuch nahi jaanta bete ke baare mein; wo rishta bachpan ke roop se dobara banana chahta hai. Sawal: kya maine beej maar diya, ya aisi zameen mein bo diya jo uski hai, meri nahi?
  • Stanza 2 ⭐⭐⭐: 'We speak like strangers, there's no sign of understanding in the air' — bacha pita ke design par bana hai, par jo use pasand hai pita share nahi kar sakta. 'Silence surrounds us.' Pita chahta hai beta prodigal ki tarah lautkar ghar aaye, apni alag duniya na banaye; wo use maaf bhi karega aur dukh se naya pyaar gadhega.
  • Stanza 3 ⭐⭐⭐: 'Father and son, we both must live on the same globe and the same land' — bete ki aawaz: 'I cannot understand myself, why anger grows from grief.'
  • Ant ⭐⭐⭐: 'We each put out an empty hand, longing for something to forgive.' — dono ek-doosre tak haath badhate hain par khaali haath; maafi ki chaah dono mein hai.

🎯 Sequence: pita samajh nahi paata → strangers jaisi baat, silence → prodigal ki chaah → dono ek hi duniya mein → khaali haath, maafi ki chaah.

3️⃣ Key Lines aur Devices ⭐⭐⭐

  • 'Yet have I killed the seed I spent or sown it where the land is his and none of mine?' ⭐⭐⭐: metaphor — beej = pita ka prabhav; zameen = bete ka alag vyaktitva.
  • 'This child is built to my design yet what he loves I cannot share' ⭐⭐⭐: shareer se pita jaisa, par ruchiyan alag.
  • 'Prodigal' ⭐⭐⭐: Bible ke prodigal son ka sanket — beta bhatak kar lautkar ghar aata hai.
  • 'Empty hand' ⭐⭐: dono ki khaali koshish, par maafi ki chaah.
  • Bhasha ⭐⭐: saral, seedhi aur rhyming stanza.

💡 Devices: metaphor (beej/zameen) + allusion (prodigal son) + symbol (empty hand).

4️⃣ Themes ⭐⭐⭐

  • Generation gap ⭐⭐⭐: saath rehna = samajhna nahi.
  • Samvaad ki kami ⭐⭐: dono ke beech silence aur anger.
  • Maafi aur prem ⭐⭐⭐: dono kuch maaf karna/maangna chahte hain.
  • Sandesh ⭐⭐⭐: rishta baat aur samajh se bachta hai; pehla kadam uthana zaroori.

🎯 Theme: generation gap + samvaad ki kami + maafi ki chaah.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Who wrote Father to Son?
Answer dekho

Elizabeth Jennings (1926-2001).

Q2 What is the father's complaint?
Answer dekho

He does not understand his son although they have lived in the same house for years.

Q3 What does he want to do now?
Answer dekho

To build the relationship again from how the son was when small.

Q4 What does 'killed the seed' mean?
Answer dekho

He fears he has harmed the bond, or sown it where the land is the son's and not his.

Q5 How do they speak to each other?
Answer dekho

Like strangers — there is no sign of understanding in the air.

Q6 What is meant by 'This child is built to my design'?
Answer dekho

The son physically resembles the father, but what he loves the father cannot share.

Q7 What does the father want the son to be?
Answer dekho

Prodigal, returning to his father's house, the home he knew.

Q8 What does the son say?
Answer dekho

'I cannot understand myself, why anger grows from grief.'

Q9 What does the 'empty hand' suggest?
Answer dekho

Both reach out but with nothing to offer yet, longing for something to forgive.

Q10 What is the poem's theme?
Answer dekho

The generation gap and the longing for forgiveness between father and son.
