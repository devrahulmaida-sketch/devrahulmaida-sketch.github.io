Father to Son (Poem)

Elizabeth Jennings — Hornbill Poem 5

Baap-beta ke beech ki khamoshi — generation gap ka dard

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Kavi aur Kavita Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Elizabeth Jennings ⭐⭐: famous English poet (1926-2001) — 'The Movement' kaviyon mein ginnti; unki kavita sadbhav, parivar aur manav-sambandhon par likhi gayi.
  • Kavita ka vishay ⭐⭐⭐: ek pita apne bete se badhti doori par dukhi hai — dono ek hi ghar mein rehte hain par ajnbi (strangers) ki tarah.
  • Kavita ka swar ⭐⭐: pita ka ekantvaas (monologue) — woh bete se sulah chahta hai par rasta nahi milta.
  • Kendriya samsya ⭐⭐⭐: generation gap — pita ne bete ko apne hisaab se banana chaha, beta apna vyaktitva dhoondh gaya; ab dono ke beech khamoshi aur khudgarzi (ego) deewar bane hai.
  • Vishay ki prasangikta ⭐⭐: har ghar ki kahani — jab bachche bade hote hain, sambandh badal jaate hain; samajh aur samvaad ki kami = doori.

💡 Poem = pita ka monologue. Core: ek ghar, do ajnabi. Problem = generation gap + dono ki ego.

2️⃣ Kavita-Saransh (Stanza-wise) ⭐⭐⭐

  • Stanza 1 — ajnabiyat ⭐⭐⭐: pita kehta hai — main apne bete ko samajh nahi pata; usne mujhe kuch bhi bataya nahi; hum saalon se ek saath rehte hain phir bhi main use jaanta nahi — 'I do not understand this child'.
  • Stanza 2 — nirmit vs mila beta ⭐⭐⭐: pita ne bete ko apni ichha se gadhna chaha tha (the child I wanted), par usne use doosra banaya; beta ab apni duniya ka hai — jo pita ki duniya se bilkul alag; dono ke beech koi sambandh-chinha (no sign of understanding) nahi.
  • Stanza 3 — doori ka dard ⭐⭐: pita chahta hai beta wapas aaye — usi bachpan wale rishte mein; woh us bhool-bhulaiya (land where I see nothing) se bhaagna chahta hai jahan beta ab rehta hai; par beta ka jawab sirf 'krodh (anger)' hai — dono ek hi ghar mein ajnabi.
  • Stanza 4 — sulah ki chahat, ego ki deewar ⭐⭐⭐: dono milna chahte hain par koi pehla kadam nahi uthata — 'each speaks a different language'; pita kehta hai khali haath (empty hand) se kaam nahi chalega — kisi ko haath badhana hoga; 'I would have him prodigal, returning to his father's house' — pita use wapas chahta hai.
  • Ant — asafalta ⭐⭐: dono ek jaisa dard mehsoos karte hain par ek doosre ko samajh nahi paate — 'silence surrounds us'; kavita be-raunak umeed ke saath khatam hoti hai.

🎯 Structure: samajh nahi → nirmit beta kho gaya → wapas chahiye par ego → dono milna chahte par haath nahi badhate → khamoshi.

3️⃣ Pramukh Panktiyan (Vyakhya) ⭐⭐

  • 'I do not understand this child, though we have lived together now for many years' ⭐⭐⭐: poem ki shuruaat hi ajnbiyat se — saath rehna ≠ samajhna; pita ki bechaini ka swar.
  • 'I know nothing of him' ⭐⭐: pita ka seedha sweekar — itni doori ki woh apne hi bete ke baare mein kuch nahi jaanta.
  • 'We speak like strangers... each speaks a different language' ⭐⭐⭐: generation gap ka siddhant — do bhashayein, do sanskar; dono ka ek ghar par alag-alag duniya.
  • 'I would have him prodigal, returning to his father's house' ⭐⭐⭐: Bible ki prodigal son ki kahani ka sanket — pita chahta hai beta 'kho kar wapas' aaye jaise woh baible ka beta; par beta wapas nahi aata.
  • 'Silence surrounds us' ⭐⭐: antim dhwani — khamoshi hi ab dono ke beech ki bhasha hai; doori poori ho chuki.

💡 Must-quotes: "I do not understand this child", "each speaks a different language", "prodigal son", "silence surrounds us".

4️⃣ Kavya-Saundarya aur Vishay ⭐⭐

  • Mukhya vishay ⭐⭐⭐: generation gap aur sambandhon ka tootna — pyaar ke bawajood samajh ki kami rishton ko ajnabi bana deti hai.
  • Pita ka drishtikon ⭐⭐: kavita ek-tarfa (one-sided) hai — sirf pita bolta hai; isi se reader samajhta hai ki samvaad ki kami hi asli samsya hai.
  • Poetic devices ⭐⭐: monologue (pita ki ekantvani), allusion (prodigal son — Bible), metaphor (different languages, silence surrounds), contrast (ghar ek, duniya do), free verse.
  • Ego aur sulah ⭐⭐⭐: dono milna chahte hain par koi haath nahi badhata — 'empty hand' se kuch nahi banta; sulah ke liye kisi ek ko jhukna hoga.
  • Sandesh ⭐⭐: rishton mein samajh aur samvaad zaroori hai — pyaar akela kaafi nahi; samay rehte pehla kadam uthao, warna khamoshi deewar ban jati hai.

🎯 Theme = generation gap + one-sided monologue + ego vs sulah. Prodigal son allusion exam favourite.

1️⃣ कवि और कविता परिचय ⭐⭐

  • एलिज़ाबेथ जेनिंग्स ⭐⭐: प्रसिद्ध इंग्लिश कवयित्री (1926-2001) — 'The Movement' कवियों में।
  • विषय ⭐⭐⭐: पिता-पुत्र की बढ़ती दूरी — एक घर, दो अजनबी।
  • रूप ⭐⭐: पिता का एकांतवाणी (monologue)।

2️⃣ कविता-सारांश ⭐⭐⭐

  • अजनबियत ⭐⭐⭐: 'I do not understand this child' — सालों साथ, फिर भी अनजान।
  • निर्मित बनाम मिला बेटा ⭐⭐⭐: पिता ने अपनी इच्छा से गढ़ना चाहा; बेटा अपनी दुनिया का बन गया।
  • दूरी का दर्द ⭐⭐: पिता चाहता है बेटा बचपन वाले रिश्ते में लौटे; बेटे का उत्तर सिर्फ क्रोध।
  • सुलह की चाह ⭐⭐⭐: दोनों मिलना चाहते हैं, पर कोई हाथ नहीं बढ़ाता — 'silence surrounds us'।

3️⃣ प्रमुख पंक्तियाँ ⭐⭐

  • 'I do not understand this child' ⭐⭐⭐: साथ रहना ≠ समझना।
  • 'Each speaks a different language' ⭐⭐⭐: generation gap।
  • 'Prodigal, returning to his father's house' ⭐⭐⭐: बाइबिल की prodigal son कथा का संकेत।
  • 'Silence surrounds us' ⭐⭐: खामोशी ही अब बीच की भाषा।

4️⃣ काव्य-सौंदर्य ⭐⭐

  • विषय ⭐⭐⭐: generation gap + रिश्तों का टूटना।
  • एक-तरफा स्वर ⭐⭐: सिर्फ पिता बोलता है — संवाद की कमी ही समस्या।
  • अलंकार ⭐⭐: monologue, allusion (prodigal son), metaphor।
  • संदेश ⭐⭐: समझ और संवाद ज़रूरी — समय रहते पहला कदम उठाओ।

1️⃣ Poet and Poem ⭐⭐

  • Elizabeth Jennings ⭐⭐: a noted English poet (1926-2001), grouped with 'The Movement' poets.
  • Subject ⭐⭐⭐: a father mourns the growing distance from his son — one house, two strangers.
  • Form ⭐⭐: the father's monologue.

2️⃣ Stanza Summary ⭐⭐⭐

  • Strangeness ⭐⭐⭐: 'I do not understand this child' — years together, yet unknown.
  • Made vs found ⭐⭐⭐: he wanted to shape the boy; the son built his own world instead.
  • Pain of distance ⭐⭐: the father longs for the old bond; the son answers with anger.
  • Wish for reunion ⭐⭐⭐: both want to meet, but neither extends a hand — 'silence surrounds us'.

3️⃣ Key Lines ⭐⭐

  • 'I do not understand this child' ⭐⭐⭐: living together is not understanding.
  • 'Each speaks a different language' ⭐⭐⭐: the generation gap.
  • 'Prodigal, returning to his father's house' ⭐⭐⭐: the biblical prodigal son allusion.
  • 'Silence surrounds us' ⭐⭐: silence is now their only language.

4️⃣ Poetic Beauty ⭐⭐

  • Theme ⭐⭐⭐: generation gap and broken bonds.
  • One-sided voice ⭐⭐: only the father speaks — the missing dialogue is the problem.
  • Devices ⭐⭐: monologue, allusion, metaphor, contrast; free verse.
  • Message ⭐⭐: love needs understanding and dialogue — take the first step in time.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Father to Son ke kavi kaun hain?
Answer dekho

Elizabeth Jennings — English kavayitri (1926-2001), 'The Movement' kaviyon mein ginnti; poem ek pita ki monologue hai jo bete se badhti doori par dukhi hai.

Q2 Pita apne bete ke baare mein kya shikayat karta hai?
Answer dekho

Ki woh apne bete ko samajh nahi pata — 'I do not understand this child' — saalon se ek ghar mein rehne ke bawajood woh use jaanta nahi.

Q3 'Nirmit beta vs mila beta' se kya matlab hai?
Answer dekho

Pita ne bete ko apni ichha aur sapnon ke anusaar gadhna chaha tha, par bete ne apna vyaktitva aur apni duniya bana li — jo pita ki duniya se bilkul alag hai.

Q4 'Each speaks a different language' ka bhav kya hai?
Answer dekho

Dono ke vichar, priyataayein aur sanskar alag ho gaye hain — baat hoti hai par samvad nahi; yeh generation gap ka seedha chitra hai.

Q5 Prodigal son ka sanket (allusion) kyun diya gaya hai?
Answer dekho

Bible ki kahani mein beta ghar chhodkar kho jata hai phir pita ke ghar laut aata hai — pita chahta hai uska beta bhi 'prodigal' banke wapas aaye; par woh nahi aata.

Q6 Dono milna chahte hain phir mil kyun nahi paate?
Answer dekho

Kyunki koi pehla kadam nahi uthata — dono ki ego beech mein hai; pita samajhta hai khali haath (empty hand) se kuch nahi banta — kisi ko haath badhana hoga.

Q7 'Silence surrounds us' ka kya arth hai?
Answer dekho

Ab dono ke beech sirf khamoshi bachi hai — baat-cheet ka pul toot chuka; yeh pankti rishtey ke poora tootne ki dhwani hai.

Q8 Kavita sirf pita ke drishtikon se kyun hai?
Answer dekho

Ek-tarfa monologue isliye rakha gaya taaki reader samjhe ki samvaad ki kami hi asli samsya hai — beta bolta hi nahi, sirf krodh dikhata hai.

Q9 Kavita mein prayukt poetic devices kaun se hain?
Answer dekho

Monologue (pita ki ekantvani), allusion (prodigal son), metaphor (different languages, empty hand), contrast (ek ghar, do duniya) aur free verse.

Q10 Kavita ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Rishton mein pyaar ke saath samajh aur samvaad zaroori hai — sulah ke liye samay rehte pehla kadam uthao, warna khamoshi deewar ban jati hai.
