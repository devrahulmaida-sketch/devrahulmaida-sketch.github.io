Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

ChildI do not understand this child, though we have lived together now in the same house for years.
SeedYet have I killed the seed I spent or sown it where the land is his and none of mine?
DesignWe speak like strangers; this child is built to my design, yet what he loves I cannot share.
SilenceSilence surrounds us. I would have him prodigal, returning to his father’s house.
GlobeFather and son, we both must live on the same globe and the same land.
HandsWe each put out an empty hand, longing for something to forgive.
