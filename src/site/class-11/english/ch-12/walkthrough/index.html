Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

QuestionWhen did my childhood go? Stanzas 1, 2 and 3 offer a possible day; stanza 4 asks where.
ElevenWas it the day I ceased to be eleven; Hell and Heaven could not be found in Geography, and therefore could not be.
AdultsAdults were not all they seemed to be; they talked of love and preached of love, but did not act so lovingly.
MindWas it when I found my mind was really mine, to use whichever way I choose; thoughts my own, and mine alone.
PlaceIt went to some forgotten place, that’s hidden in an infant’s face, that’s all I know.
ShapeFour stanzas; Was that the day! ends stanzas 1 to 3; rhymes like eleven/Heaven and face/place.
