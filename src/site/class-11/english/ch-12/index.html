Childhood (Poem)

Markus Natten — Hornbill Poem 4

Bachpan kahan kho gaya? — Innocence se maturity tak ka safar

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Kavi aur Kavita Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Markus Natten ⭐⭐: Norwegian poet — unki yeh choti si poem bachpan ke khone par hai; zyada jaankari simit hai, par poem NCERT ki favourite hai.
  • Kendriya prashna ⭐⭐⭐: poem ka dil ek hi sawaal hai — 'When did my childhood go?' — mera bachpan kab aur kahan chala gaya?
  • Kavita ka swaroop ⭐⭐: 4 stanzas — pehle teen mein kavi teen jawab ki possibilities deta hai, aakhri mein bachpan kahan gaya iska jawab.
  • Vishay ⭐⭐: innocence ka khatma aur rational soch ka aagman — bachcha bada hokar duniya ko sawaalon ki nazar se dekhne lagta hai.
  • Swar ⭐: yaadgar, thodi udasi bhara (nostalgic) — kavi apne khoye bachpan ko dhundhta hai.

💡 Poem = ek sawaal (When did my childhood go?) + teen jawab + aakhri jawab (in some forgotten land). Structure yaad rakho.

2️⃣ Stanza-wise Saransh ⭐⭐⭐

  • Stanza 1 — 11 saal ki umar ⭐⭐⭐: pehla jawab — shayad mera bachpan gyarah saal ki umar mein chala gaya, jab maine samjha ki Hell aur Heaven sach mein nahi hain (kyunki geography mein woh kahin nahi milte) — tark (reason) jag gaya.
  • Stanza 2 — adults ka saccha chehra ⭐⭐⭐: doosra jawab — shayad us din gaya jab maine dekha ki bade log waisa nahi karte jaisa kehte hain — woh pyaar ki baatein karte hain par khud pyaar se jeete nahi; unki dikhawati zindagi (hypocrisy) dikh gayi.
  • Stanza 3 — apna mann ⭐⭐⭐: teesra jawab — shayad us din gaya jab mujhe ehsaas hua ki mere mann ka apna wajood hai — main apne vichar chun sakta hoon, doosron ke nahi chalne wale; individuality janm le gayi bachpan.
  • Stanza 4 — kahan gaya bachpan? ⭐⭐⭐: aakhri jawab — mera bachpan kisi bhoole-bisare desh (some forgotten land) mein chala gaya — jahan se woh wapas nahi aayega; ab woh sirf ek bachche ke chehre mein jhalakta hai.
  • Refrain ⭐⭐: har stanza ke ant mein — 'Was that the day!' — sawaal ka dohrana poem ko musical aur yaadgar banata hai.

🎯 3 jawab: (1) 11 saal — Hell/Heaven nahi, tark aaya; (2) adults hypocrite nikle; (3) apni soch ka janm (individuality). 4th: forgotten land mein kho gaya.

3️⃣ Pramukh Panktiyan (Vyakhya) ⭐⭐

  • 'When did my childhood go? Was that the day I ceased to be eleven' ⭐⭐⭐: kavi gyarah saal ki umar ko turning point maanta hai — jab bachche ki andhi aastha tootti aur tark jaaga.
  • 'Hell and Heaven could not be found in Geography' ⭐⭐: bachpan ka anth — jab bachcha kalpana ko bhi padhai ke aine mein tolne laga; jo map mein nahi, woh sach nahi — rational thinking.
  • 'Adults... were not all they seemed to be... they talked of love and preached of love, but did not act so lovingly' ⭐⭐⭐: badon ka dwand (hypocrisy) — kehna aur karna alag; bachpan ki masoom nazar ab sach dekh leti hai.
  • 'My mind was really mine... to use whichever way I choose' ⭐⭐: swatantra vichar ka janm — ab kavi doosron ke kathputli nahi; yeh individuality bachpan ka ant bhi hai.
  • 'It went to some forgotten place, that's hidden in an infant's face' ⭐⭐⭐: sabse sundar pankti — bachpan mit nahi, bas chhup gaya hai — kisi masoom bachche ke chehre mein dikh jata hai.

💡 Must-quotes: "ceased to be eleven", "talked of love... did not act so lovingly", "hidden in an infant's face".

4️⃣ Kavya-Saundarya aur Vishay ⭐⭐

  • Mukhya vishay ⭐⭐⭐: masoomiyat (innocence) ka khatma aur pragalbhyata (maturity) ka aagman — bachpan tark, hypocrisy ki pehchan aur apni soch ke janm se khatam hota hai.
  • Nostalgia ⭐⭐: kavi apne bachpan ko khoya hua khazana maanta hai — use wapas nahi paa sakta, bas yaad kar sakta hai.
  • Poetic devices ⭐⭐: refrain ('Was that the day!'), rhetorical question (When did my childhood go?), imagery (infant's face), free verse, antithesis (preach vs act).
  • Badon ki hypocrisy ⭐⭐: poem ka teekha point — bade prem ka updesh dete hain par jite nahi; bachche ki nazar yeh dikhawa pakad leti hai.
  • Sandesh ⭐⭐: bachpan masoomiyat ka yug hai — jise hum tark aur duniya-dari mein kho dete hain; par woh har bachche ke chehre mein zinda rehta hai.

🎯 Theme = loss of innocence + nostalgia + hypocrisy of adults. Devices: refrain + rhetorical question.

1️⃣ कवि और कविता परिचय ⭐⭐

  • मार्कस नैटन ⭐⭐: नॉर्वेजियन कवि — कविता बचपन के खोने पर।
  • केंद्रीय प्रश्न ⭐⭐⭐: 'When did my childhood go?' — बचपन कब और कहां गया?
  • रूप ⭐⭐: 4 स्तंभ (stanzas) — तीन संभावित उत्तर + अंतिम उत्तर।

2️⃣ कविता-सारांश ⭐⭐⭐

  • पहला उत्तर ⭐⭐⭐: 11 वर्ष की आयु — जब समझा कि नर्क-स्वर्ग वास्तविक नहीं; तर्क जागा।
  • दूसरा उत्तर ⭐⭐⭐: जब देखा कि बड़े कहते कुछ, करते कुछ हैं — प्रेम का उपदेश, जीवन में नहीं (hypocrisy)।
  • तीसरा उत्तर ⭐⭐⭐: जब एहसास हुआ कि मन मेरा अपना है — अपने विचार चुन सकता हूँ (individuality)।
  • चौथा उत्तर ⭐⭐⭐: बचपन किसी भूली-बिसरी भूमि में खो गया — अब शिशु के चेहरे में झलकता है।

3️⃣ प्रमुख पंक्तियाँ ⭐⭐

  • 'Ceased to be eleven' ⭐⭐⭐: 11 साल — अंधी आस्था का अंत, तर्क की शुरुआत।
  • 'Talked of love... did not act so lovingly' ⭐⭐⭐: बड़ों का पाखंड।
  • 'My mind was really mine' ⭐⭐: स्वतंत्र विचार का जन्म।
  • 'Hidden in an infant's face' ⭐⭐⭐: बचपन मिटा नहीं — मासूम चेहरे में जिंदा।

4️⃣ काव्य-सौंदर्य ⭐⭐

  • विषय ⭐⭐⭐: मासूमियत का अंत + परिपक्वता का आगमन।
  • नोस्टैल्जिया ⭐⭐: खोया बचपन — अब सिर्फ याद।
  • अलंकार ⭐⭐: refrain, rhetorical question, imagery; free verse।
  • संदेश ⭐⭐: बचपन हर बच्चे के चेहरे में जिंदा रहता है।

1️⃣ Poet and Poem ⭐⭐

  • Markus Natten ⭐⭐: a Norwegian poet; this poem reflects on the loss of childhood.
  • Central question ⭐⭐⭐: 'When did my childhood go?' — when and where did it vanish?
  • Structure ⭐⭐: 4 stanzas — three possible answers, then the final one.

2️⃣ Stanza Summary ⭐⭐⭐

  • First answer ⭐⭐⭐: at age eleven — when he realised Hell and Heaven are not real places; reason awoke.
  • Second answer ⭐⭐⭐: when he saw adults preach love but not practise it — hypocrisy exposed.
  • Third answer ⭐⭐⭐: when he found his mind was his own — individuality was born.
  • Final answer ⭐⭐⭐: childhood went to some forgotten land — now seen only in an infant's face.

3️⃣ Key Lines ⭐⭐

  • 'Ceased to be eleven' ⭐⭐⭐: blind faith ends, reason begins.
  • 'Talked of love... did not act so lovingly' ⭐⭐⭐: adult hypocrisy.
  • 'My mind was really mine' ⭐⭐: birth of independent thought.
  • 'Hidden in an infant's face' ⭐⭐⭐: childhood survives in every child's face.

4️⃣ Poetic Beauty ⭐⭐

  • Theme ⭐⭐⭐: loss of innocence and arrival of maturity.
  • Nostalgia ⭐⭐: the lost childhood lives on only in memory.
  • Devices ⭐⭐: refrain, rhetorical question, imagery; free verse.
  • Message ⭐⭐: childhood lives on in every infant's face.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Childhood poem ke kavi kaun hain?
Answer dekho

Markus Natten — Norwegian kavi; poem bachpan ke khone (loss of innocence) par hai aur free verse mein likhi gayi hai.

Q2 Poem ka kendriya prashna kya hai?
Answer dekho

'When did my childhood go?' — kavi baar-baar poochta hai ki uska bachpan kab aur kahan chala gaya; har stanza 'Was that the day!' ke saath khatam hota hai.

Q3 Pehla jawab (11 saal ki umar) kya hai?
Answer dekho

Kavi maanta hai bachpan shayad gyarah saal ki umar mein khatam hua — jab use samajh aaya ki Hell aur Heaven vastav mein nahi (geography mein kahin nahi milte); tark jaag gaya.

Q4 Doosra jawab — adults ke baare mein kavi ko kya pata chala?
Answer dekho

Ki bade log jaisa kehte hain waisa karte nahi — woh prem ki baat aur updesh dete hain par khud prem se jite nahi; unki hypocrisy (dikhawa) zahir ho gayi.

Q5 Teesra jawab — 'my mind was really mine' ka matlab?
Answer dekho

Kavi ko ehsaas hua ki uska mann uska apna hai — woh apne vichar khud chun sakta hai, doosron ke banaye raaste par chalna zaroori nahi; individuality ka janm bachpan ka ant hai.

Q6 Bachpan aakhir kahan chala gaya?
Answer dekho

'Some forgotten land' — kisi bhoole-bisare desh mein; ab woh kavi ke paas nahi, bas ek masoom bachche ke chehre mein jhalakta hai.

Q7 'Was that the day!' ka kya mahatva hai?
Answer dekho

Yeh refrain hai — har jawab ke baad kavi swayam se poochta hai 'kya wahi din tha!' — isse poem mein musical quality aur yaadgar pan (nostalgia) dono aate hain.

Q8 Poem mein nostalgia ka swar kahan dikhta hai?
Answer dekho

Poori poem mein — kavi apne khoye bachpan ko dhundhta hai, use wapas nahi paa sakta, sirf yaad kar sakta hai; 'forgotten land' ka shabd-prayog isi udaasi ko dikhata hai.

Q9 Poem mein prayukt poetic devices kaun se hain?
Answer dekho

Refrain (Was that the day!), rhetorical question (When did my childhood go?), imagery (infant's face), antithesis (preach vs act) aur free verse.

Q10 Poem ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Bachpan masoomiyat ka yug hai jo hum tark, duniya-dari aur badon ki hypocrisy ki pehchan mein kho dete hain — par woh kabhi marta nahi, har bachche ke chehre mein zinda rehta hai.
