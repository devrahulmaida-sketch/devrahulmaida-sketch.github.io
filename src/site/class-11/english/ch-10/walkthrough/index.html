Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

TreeSilent, quite still in yellow September sunlight; leaves yellowing, all its seeds fallen.
BirdTill the goldfinch comes, with a twitching chirrup: a suddeness, a startlement.
ThickSleek as a lizard (simile); she enters the thickness and a machine starts up.
ThrillThe whole tree trembles and thrills: chitterings, tremor of wings, trillings.
EngineIt is the engine of her family (metaphor); she stokes it full.
AwayShe launches away, towards the infinite; the laburnum subsides to empty.
