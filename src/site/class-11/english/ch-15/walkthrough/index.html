ClosedI regret I cannot do anything for you. The name-plate says Dorling; the number is 46.
MotherMrs Dorling took the table silver, antique plates, large vases and crockery. Remember Number 46.
LiberationAfter the Liberation she was afraid of the stored things, then gradually grew curious.
