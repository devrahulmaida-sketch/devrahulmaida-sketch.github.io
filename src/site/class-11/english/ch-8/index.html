Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Lekhak aur Path Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Nick Middleton ⭐⭐: lekhak (narrator). Excerpt unki Mount Kailash ki kora ki taraf yatra ka hissa hai; NCERT text mein unke baare mein aur kuch nahi diya.
  • Path ka swaroop ⭐: travelogue — NCERT notes: Mt Kailash ka panoramic view. Title Silk Road kyun: text mein mastiffs ko Silk Road par tribute, aur Hor ko Lhasa-Kashmir old trade route ke highway par bataya gaya hai.
  • Lakshya ⭐⭐: lekhak Mount Kailash jaa raha hai kora poori karne (“to complete the kora”). Lhamo ne use garam kapde lene ko kaha tha.
  • Saathi ⭐: Tsetan (car chalane wala), Daniel (Lhamo se baat karne mein madad, Hor tak saath), baad mein Norbu.
  • Shuruaat ⭐: Ravu se vidai ki subah; Lhamo farewell present mein sheepskin coat deti hai; Tsetan: “drokba, sir”. Phir Changtang se shortcut.

💡 Route: Ravu → shortcut over passes (5,515 m) → salt-lake workcamp → Hor → Darchen → (Norbu) kora ki tayyari.

2️⃣ Katha-Saransh ⭐⭐

  • Ravu se ⭐: wide plains, gazelles; kyang (wild ass) ka jhund; drokbas (nomads) bhed-bakriyon ke saath; Tibetan mastiff kaale kutte tent ke paas pehra dete hain.
  • Pass aur barf ⭐⭐: 5,210 m par barf; Tsetan mitti daalta hai; doosre block ke paas gaadi ghumata hai. 5,400 m par lekhak ka sir dard; pass 5,515 m (cairn, scarves, prayer flags, clockwise parikrama).
  • Salt lake aur Hor ⭐⭐: 2 baje salt-lake workcamp mein noodles; Tethys Ocean ke vestiges. Hor mein Daniel truck se Lhasa lauta; do punctures; Hor grim, miserable, kachra; Manasarovar ke kinare.
  • Darchen ⭐⭐: raat 10:30 ke baad pahunche (4,760 m); lekhak so nahi paya; agle din Tibetan doctor: “a cold and the effects of altitude”; paanch din ki dawa; Tsetan Lhasa laut gaya.
  • Norbu ⭐⭐: cafe mein milta hai: Tibetan, Beijing ke Institute of Ethnic Literature mein kaam karta hai; kora par papers likhe par kabhi ki nahi. “Two academics who have escaped from the library.”

🎯 Sequence: Ravu → kyang/mastiffs → snow aur pass → salt lake → Hor → Darchen (doctor) → Norbu.

3️⃣ Pramukh Patra aur Anubhav ⭐⭐

  • Lekhak ⭐⭐: apni cold, neend ki dikkat aur dar ke baare mein khul kar likhta hai; self-help programme on positive thinking par halka mazaak.
  • Tsetan ⭐⭐: barf par mitti daal kar raasta banata hai, tyres check karta hai; mazaak: “As a Buddhist… it would be bad for business.”
  • Norbu ⭐: windcheater, metal-rimmed spectacles; khud ko fat kehta hai; yaks par saman ka suggestion; prostrate nahi karega (“Not possible”).
  • Tibet ki jhalak ⭐: kyang, mastiffs, drokbas, cairn, prayer flags, salt flats; Manasarovar par Hindu aur Buddhist cosmology.
  • Altitude ⭐: sir dard, kaan ka pressure, nocturnal disturbances — lekhak ko sone se dar lagta hai.

🎯 3 patra: narrator, Tsetan, Norbu. Hor ka grim town vs Kawaguchi/Hedin ke emotional accounts — contrast yaad rakho.

4️⃣ Mukhya Vishay ⭐⭐

  • Kitabon vs reality ⭐⭐: Kawaguchi (1900) Manasarovar dekh kar ro pade; Hedin par bhi asar; lekhak ko Hor mein kachra aur dust.
  • Pahadi log ⭐: NCERT ‘Talking about the text’: sensitive behaviour of hill-folk; nomads wave karte hain, kutte gaadi ke saamne aate hain.
  • Darchen ki nirasha ⭐⭐: koi pilgrim nahi — lekhak season ki shuruaat mein aaya; sirf wait kar sakta tha. Norbu ke milne se relief.
  • Geography ⭐: plateau mein salt flats aur brackish lakes — Tethys Ocean ke bache hue nishan.
  • Sandesh ⭐⭐: NCERT ‘Talking’: log kathin yatrayen kyun jhelte hain, aur legend aur reality ka fark.

🎯 Theme: yatra ka asli roop + saathi ka milna. Hor aur Darchen ke examples.

1️⃣ लेखक और पाठ परिचय ⭐⭐

  • निक मिडलटन ⭐⭐: लेखक (वाचक)। अंश माउंट कैलाश की कोरा की ओर यात्रा का है।
  • पाठ का स्वरूप ⭐: यात्रा-वृत्तांत — NCERT: माउंट कैलाश का व्यापक चित्रण।
  • साथी ⭐: त्सेतान (चालक), डैनियल (होर तक), बाद में नोरबू।

2️⃣ कथा-सारांश ⭐⭐

  • रावु ⭐: लामो विदाई में भेड़ की खाल का कोट देती है; चांगथांग से छोटा रास्ता।
  • दर्रा और बर्फ़ ⭐⭐: 5,210 मी. पर बर्फ़; दर्रा 5,515 मी. (केर्न, रंग-बिरंगे झंडे)।
  • होर ⭐⭐: मानसरोवर के किनारे का उदास कस्बा, कचरा; डैनियल ल्हासा लौटता है।
  • डार्चेन ⭐⭐: 4,760 मी.; नींद नहीं; तिब्बती डॉक्टर: “सर्दी और ऊँचाई का असर”।
  • नोरबू ⭐⭐: तिब्बती; बीजिंग में जातीय साहित्य संस्थान; कोरा पर लिखा पर कभी की नहीं।

3️⃣ प्रमुख पात्र ⭐⭐

  • लेखक ⭐⭐: अपनी बीमारी और डर के बारे में ईमानदार; हल्का हास्य।
  • त्सेतान ⭐⭐: बर्फ़ पर मिट्टी डालता है; “व्यापार के लिए बुरा होगा” वाला मज़ाक।
  • नोरबू ⭐: खुद को मोटा कहता है; सामान के लिए याक सुझाता है; दंडवत नहीं करेगा।

4️⃣ मुख्य विषय ⭐⭐

  • किताब बनाम वास्तविकता ⭐⭐: कावागुची (1900) मानसरोवर देख रो पड़े; लेखक को होर में कचरा।
  • डार्चेन ⭐⭐: कोई तीर्थयात्री नहीं (मौसम की शुरुआत); नोरबू से मुलाक़ात।
  • भूगोल ⭐: नमक के मैदान — टेथिस सागर के अवशेष।
  • संदेश ⭐⭐: NCERT चर्चा: कठिन यात्राएँ लोग क्यों झेलते हैं; किंवदंती और वास्तविकता।

1️⃣ Author and Lesson Introduction ⭐⭐

  • Nick Middleton ⭐⭐: the narrator; the excerpt covers his journey towards the Mount Kailash kora.
  • Form ⭐: a travelogue; NCERT notes call it a panoramic view of Mt Kailash.
  • Companions ⭐: Tsetan (car), Daniel (until Hor), later Norbu.

2️⃣ Summary ⭐⭐

  • From Ravu ⭐: Lhamo gives a sheepskin coat; shortcut off the Changtang; gazelles, kyang, drokbas, Tibetan mastiffs.
  • Snow and the pass ⭐⭐: snow at 5,210 m; Tsetan spreads dirt; the pass at 5,515 m with a cairn, scarves and prayer flags.
  • Hor ⭐⭐: a grim, miserable town on Lake Manasarovar; Daniel leaves for Lhasa; two punctures.
  • Darchen ⭐⭐: 4,760 m; he cannot sleep; a Tibetan doctor says: a cold and the effects of altitude; a five-day course.
  • Norbu ⭐⭐: a Tibetan at Beijing’s Institute of Ethnic Literature; has written on the kora, never done it.

3️⃣ Main Characters ⭐⭐

  • The writer ⭐⭐: frank about his cold, sleeplessness and fear; light humour.
  • Tsetan ⭐⭐: spreads dirt on snow, checks tyres; jokes “it would be bad for business”.
  • Norbu ⭐: calls himself fat; suggests yaks for luggage; will not prostrate.

4️⃣ Main Themes ⭐⭐

  • Books vs reality ⭐⭐: Kawaguchi (1900) wept at Manasarovar; the writer finds rubbish and dust at Hor.
  • Darchen ⭐⭐: no pilgrims (he was early in the season); meeting Norbu is a relief.
  • Geography ⭐: salt flats and brackish lakes, vestiges of the Tethys Ocean.
  • Discussion ⭐⭐: why people undergo difficult journeys; legends of exotic places vs reality.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Silk Road ke lekhak kaun hain aur path kis vidha ka hai?
Answer dekho

Nick Middleton (narrator); path ek travelogue hai — NCERT notes ke mutabik Mt Kailash ka panoramic view.

Q2 Lekhak is yatra par kyun nikla hai?
Answer dekho

Woh Mount Kailash ki taraf jaa raha hai kora poori karne ke liye.

Q3 Tsetan ne barf par kya kiya?
Answer dekho

5,210 m par barf ki sataha par mitti daali taaki gaadi fisle nahi; doosre block par gaadi barf ke aas-paas ghumayi.

Q4 Tibetan mastiffs china ke imperial courts mein kyun popular the?
Answer dekho

Text ke mutabik wo hunting dogs the, jo Silk Road ke raste Tibet se tribute ke roop mein laaye gaye.

Q5 Hor kaisa kasba tha aur wahan kya hua?
Answer dekho

Grim, miserable: kachra aur dust; Manasarovar ke kinare. Daniel Lhasa laut gaya; do punctures hue.

Q6 Hor ka anubhav pehle ke yatriyon se kaise alag tha?
Answer dekho

Kawaguchi (1900) Manasarovar ki pavitrata se ro pade; Sven Hedin par bhi asar; lekhak ko Hor mein kachra mila.

Q7 Darchen mein lekhak ko kya taklif hui?
Answer dekho

4,760 m par naak band/seena bhaari; uska so jaane se dar laga. Tibetan doctor: cold aur altitude ka asar; paanch din ki dawa.

Q8 Norbu kaun hai?
Answer dekho

Tibetan; Beijing ke Chinese Academy of Social Sciences ke Institute of Ethnic Literature mein; kora par papers likhe par kabhi ki nahi.

Q9 Norbu ke milne par lekhak ko kya laga?
Answer dekho

“We could be a team… two academics who have escaped from the library”; pehle relief, par woh bhi kam tayyar nikla; yaks par saman ka plan.

Q10 “As a Buddhist… bad for business” kaun, kab bola?
Answer dekho

Tsetan, jab usne dekha ki lekhak zinda rahega aur use chhod kar Lhasa laut gaya.
