The Browning Version

Terence Rattigan — Hornbill Ch 6

Ek teacher ki kahani — Taplow ka gift jo dil chhu gaya

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Lekhak aur Natak Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Terence Rattigan ⭐⭐: famous British playwright (1911-1977) — 'The Browning Version' unka prasiddh one-act play hai (1948).
  • Title ka matlab ⭐⭐: Robert Browning ne Aeschylus ke Greek play 'Agamemnon' ka English translation kiya tha — use 'Browning version' kehte hain; natak mein yahi book ek gift ke roop mein centre par hai.
  • Setting ⭐: ek English public school — term ka aakhri din; ek teacher ka retirement ka din.
  • Pramukh patra ⭐⭐: Andrew Crocker-Harris (retiring classics master), Taplow (student), Frank (young science teacher), Millie (Crocker-Harris ki patni).
  • Kendriya bhav ⭐: ek unpopular teacher ke jeevan ki khamosh tragedy — aur ek chhote se gift mein chhupa gehra samman.

💡 Title yaad rakhne ka formula: Browning = poet → Agamemnon translation → gift book. Rattigan, 1948, one-act play.

2️⃣ Katha-Saransh (Plot) ⭐⭐

  • Taplow ka intezaar ⭐: student Taplow apne teacher Crocker-Harris (jise students 'Crock' kehte hain) ke ghar par extra work ke liye aata hai — Crock ghar par nahi hai, wahan Frank (science master) se uski baat hoti hai.
  • Taplow-Frank baatcheet ⭐⭐: Taplow Crock ka mazaak udta hai par saath hi kehta hai ki woh use pasand karta hai — uske hisaab se Crock ruthless nahi, bas sad aadmi hai. Ye line natak ka dil hai.
  • Millie ki entry ⭐: Crock ki patni Millie aati hai — woh apne husband ke prati bekar aur be-akhlaq hai; uska Frank ke saath ghinavna rishta bhi jhalakta hai.
  • Gift ka drishya ⭐⭐⭐: Taplow Crock ko 'Agamemnon' ki Browning translation gift karta hai — jis par usne Greek mein kuch likha hai ('God looks after the kind'). Strict aur emotionless dikhaane wala Crock bhaavuk ho kar ro padta hai.
  • Ant ⭐⭐: natak dikhata hai ki ek fail aur unpopular maanne jane wale teacher ne bhi apne students ke dil mein samman kamaya tha — bus kisi ne use kabhi dikhaya nahi.

🎯 Key scenes: Taplow-Frank baat → "sad, not ruthless" → Millie → gift (Agamemnon) → Crock ro pade. Ye sequence poora natak cover karta hai.

3️⃣ Charitra-Chitran ⭐⭐

  • Crocker-Harris ⭐⭐: strict, disciplinarian, niyamon wala classics teacher — students use 'Crock' bula kar darte hain. Par bheetar se woh akela, thaka aur samvedansheel hai; biwi uski izzat nahi karti, school use retirement par farewell bhi theek se nahi deta.
  • Taplow ⭐⭐: imandar, halka aur sach bolne wala student — mazaak karta hai par galti se nahi, pyaar se; Crock ke prati uski sachchi feelings gift mein jhalakti hain.
  • Frank ⭐: young science master — Taplow se dosti wala vyavhar; beech ka patra jo reader ko Crock ke jeevan ki sachchai tak pahunchata hai.
  • Millie ⭐: Crock ki patni — swarthi aur be-muruvvat; apne husband ke dukh ki wajah mein se ek.
  • Samajik drishya ⭐: natak dikhata hai ki samaj unpopular logon ke sath kaise karta hai — unki kabiliyat ko unke mannerism se tol leta hai.

🎯 Crock = strict bahar, toota hua andar. Taplow = mazaak + sachcha samman. Ye contrast answer ka core hai.

4️⃣ Mukhya Vishay ⭐⭐

  • Teacher-student sambandh ⭐⭐: sachcha sambandh dar se nahi, samman se banta hai — Crock ke students use darte the, par Taplow jaise kuch use dil se maante the.
  • Appearances are deceptive ⭐⭐: jo dikhta hai woh hota nahi — 'ruthless' teacher asal mein 'sad' hai; gift ka drishya iski kasauti hai.
  • Akelapan aur uppeksha ⭐: ek zindagi bhar padhane wale aadmi ka bina samman ke nikal jana — samaj ki ungratefulness.
  • Chhote gesture ka mahatva ⭐⭐: Taplow ka chhota sa gift Crock ki poori zindagi ki sabse badi kamai sabit hota hai.
  • Marriage aur zimmedari ⭐: Millie-Crock ka rishta dikhata hai ki ghar mein samman na milna insaan ko andar se tod deta hai.

🎯 Theme ke 4 shabd: samman (respect), deceptive appearances, akelapan, chhota gesture. In par theme answer banao.

1️⃣ लेखक और नाटक परिचय ⭐⭐

  • टेरेंस रैटिगन ⭐⭐: प्रसिद्ध ब्रिटिश नाटककार (1911-1977) — एकांकी नाटक (1948)।
  • शीर्षक ⭐⭐: रॉबर्ट ब्राउनिंग द्वारा ग्रीक नाटक 'एगामेम्नॉन' का अंग्रेज़ी अनुवाद — नाटक में यही पुस्तक उपहार है।
  • पात्र ⭐⭐: क्रॉकर-हैरिस (क्लासिक्स अध्यापक), टैपलो (छात्र), फ्रैंक (विज्ञान अध्यापक), मिली (पत्नी)।

2️⃣ कथा-सारांश ⭐⭐

  • टैपलो-फ्रैंक बातचीत ⭐⭐: टैपलो क्रॉक का मज़ाक उड़ाता है पर कहता है कि वह उसे पसंद करता है — वह 'निर्दयी नहीं, बस उदास' है।
  • मिली ⭐: पत्नी — पति के प्रति असम्मानजनक व्यवहार।
  • उपहार ⭐⭐⭐: टैपलो क्रॉक को ब्राउनिंग का 'एगामेम्नॉन' अनुवाद भेंट करता है — कठोर अध्यापक भावुक होकर रो पड़ता है।
  • अंत ⭐⭐: अलोकप्रिय समझा जाने वाला शिक्षक भी छात्रों के दिल में सम्मान कमा चुका था।

3️⃣ चरित्र-चित्रण ⭐⭐

  • क्रॉकर-हैरिस ⭐⭐: बाहर से कठोर अनुशासक, भीतर से अकेला और संवेदनशील।
  • टैपलो ⭐⭐: ईमानदार और सच्चा छात्र — मज़ाक के पीछे गहरा सम्मान।
  • फ्रैंक ⭐: युवा अध्यापक — क्रॉक की सच्चाई तक पहुँचाने वाला माध्यम।
  • मिली ⭐: स्वार्थी पत्नी — क्रॉक के दुःख का कारण।

4️⃣ मुख्य विषय ⭐⭐

  • शिक्षक-छात्र संबंध ⭐⭐: डर से नहीं, सम्मान से बनता है।
  • दिखावा भ्रामक ⭐⭐: 'निर्दयी' शिक्षक वास्तव में 'उदास' है।
  • अकेलापन ⭐: जीवनभर की सेवा पर भी असम्मान।
  • छोटा इशारा ⭐⭐: एक उपहार जीवन की सबसे बड़ी कमाई।

1️⃣ Author and Play Introduction ⭐⭐

  • Terence Rattigan ⭐⭐: a famous British playwright (1911-1977) — this is his one-act play (1948).
  • The title ⭐⭐: Robert Browning's English translation of the Greek play 'Agamemnon' — the book that appears as a gift.
  • Characters ⭐⭐: Crocker-Harris (classics master), Taplow (student), Frank (science master), Millie (his wife).

2️⃣ Plot Summary ⭐⭐

  • The Taplow-Frank talk ⭐⭐: Taplow jokes about 'the Crock' but admits he likes him — he is 'not ruthless, just sad'.
  • Millie ⭐: the disrespectful wife — one cause of Crock's sorrow.
  • The gift ⭐⭐⭐: Taplow presents Browning's 'Agamemnon' — the stern teacher breaks into tears.
  • The end ⭐⭐: the supposedly unpopular teacher had earned true respect after all.

3️⃣ Character Sketches ⭐⭐

  • Crocker-Harris ⭐⭐: outwardly a strict disciplinarian, inwardly lonely and sensitive.
  • Taplow ⭐⭐: an honest and genuine boy — deep respect behind the jokes.
  • Frank ⭐: the young master through whom we learn Crock's truth.
  • Millie ⭐: the selfish wife — a source of Crock's misery.

4️⃣ Main Themes ⭐⭐

  • Teacher-student bond ⭐⭐: built on respect, not fear.
  • Deceptive appearances ⭐⭐: the 'ruthless' teacher is really 'sad'.
  • Loneliness ⭐: a lifetime of service ends without honour.
  • The small gesture ⭐⭐: one gift becomes a life's greatest earning.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 The Browning Version ke lekhak kaun hain aur ye kaisa natak hai?
Answer dekho

Terence Rattigan — British playwright; ye ek one-act play (1948) hai.

Q2 Natak ke title ka kya mahatva hai?
Answer dekho

Robert Browning ne Greek play 'Agamemnon' ka English translation kiya tha — natak mein Taplow yahi book Crock ko gift karta hai, isliye title 'The Browning Version' hai.

Q3 Taplow Crocker-Harris ke baare mein kya kehta hai?
Answer dekho

Woh mazaak zaroor udta hai par kehta hai ki Crock 'ruthless nahi, bas sad' hai — aur use sach mein pasand karta hai.

Q4 Taplow ka gift kya tha aur uska Crock par kya asar hua?
Answer dekho

Browning ka Agamemnon translation (jis par Greek mein kuch likha tha) — dekh kar strict Crock bhaavuk ho kar ro padta hai.

Q5 Crocker-Harris ka charitra kaisa hai?
Answer dekho

Bahar se strict, niyamon wala disciplinarian; andar se akela, thaka aur samvedansheel — na school ne na biwi ne use samman diya.

Q6 Millie Crock ka kirdaar kya hai?
Answer dekho

Crock ki patni — apne husband ke prati swarthi aur be-izzati bhari vyavhar; uske dukh ki pramukh wajah.

Q7 Natak mein 'appearances are deceptive' kaise dikhaya gaya hai?
Answer dekho

Jo teacher 'ruthless' dikhta tha, asal mein 'sad' tha — uski sakhti ke peeche ek toota hua, samman ke layak insaan tha.

Q8 Taplow ke gift ka prateekatmak mahatva kya hai?
Answer dekho

Chhota sa gift Crock ki zindagi bhar ki sabse badi kamai hai — pramaan ki usne students ke dilon mein samman kamaya tha.

Q9 Teacher-student sambandh par natak kya sikhata hai?
Answer dekho

Sachcha sambandh dar se nahi, samman se banta hai — achha teacher shayad popular na ho, par uska asar gehra hota hai.

Q10 Natak ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Logon ko unke bahari vyavhar se mat tolo — har sakht chehre ke peeche ek kahani hai; chhote samman-bhare gesture zindagi bhar ka sahara ban sakte hain.
