Notes language:

1️⃣ Lekhak aur Natak Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Terence Rattigan ⭐⭐: British playwright; ye The Browning Version ka ek excerpt hai (NCERT text mein lekhak ke saal ya natak ki taareekh nahi di gayi).
  • Title ka matlab ⭐⭐: NCERT footnote: natak ke andar Robert Browning ka Greek tragedy ‘Agamemnon’ ka translation aata hai. Excerpt mein Taplow ki book Aeschylus ka Agamemnon hai.
  • Setting ⭐: ek school; term ka aakhri din. Taplow extra work ke liye Mr Crocker-Harris ka intezaar kar raha hai, jo kal hamesha ke liye ja rahe hain.
  • Pramukh patra ⭐⭐: Frank (young master), Crocker-Harris (middle-aged master), Taplow (16 saal ka ladka), Millie Crocker-Harris (unki patni).
  • Kendriya bhav ⭐: students ek strict teacher ke baare mein peeth peeche kaise baat karte hain — dar ke saath ek halka lagav bhi.

💡 Yaad rakho: Excerpt mein Crocker-Harris stage par aate hi nahi — sirf Frank, Taplow aur Millie.

2️⃣ Katha-Saransh (Plot) ⭐⭐

  • Taplow ka intezaar ⭐: Frank Taplow se milta hai (“Do I know you?”). Taplow lower fifth mein hai; science tabhi le sakta hai jab use remove mil jaye. Mr Crocker-Harris results nahi batate.
  • Extra work ⭐⭐: aakhri din extra work (pichhle hafte ek din bimar tha); 6:30 ka time tha, Crocker-Harris 10 minute late. Taplow Agamemnon ko ‘muck’ kehta hai, phir kehta hai ki plot achha hai — bura hai padhane ka tareeka.
  • “Exactly what you deserve” ⭐⭐: Crocker-Harris ne Taplow se kaha: “I have given you exactly what you deserve. No less; and certainly no more.” Taplow ko lagta hai ki extra work ke karan marks neeche bhi ho sakte hain; phir ‘hardly human’ kehta hai. Frank: “Much too far.”
  • Sadist ⭐⭐: Taplow: sadist = jo dard dene mein khushi paaye. Crock sadist nahi — tab kam se kam feelings to hoti. Taplow ke mutabik woh “shrivelled up inside like a nut” hai; phir bhi Taplow use rather like karta hai.
  • Joke aur Millie ⭐⭐⭐: Taplow Crock ke Latin joke aur uske jawab ki nakal karta hai; tabhi Millie aa jaati hai. Taplow: “there goes my remove”. Millie: “I’ll take the blame”, aur use prescription chemist ke paas le jaane ko kehti hai.

🎯 Excerpt ka sequence: Taplow-Frank baat → “exactly what you deserve” → sadist → joke ki nakal → Millie ki entry → prescription.

3️⃣ Charitra-Chitran ⭐⭐

  • Crocker-Harris ⭐⭐: Taplow ke mutabik results nahi batate, students scared to death, ‘hardly human’, ‘shrivelled up like a nut’. Excerpt mein woh khud aate nahi.
  • Taplow ⭐⭐: 16 saal ka ladka; khulkar bolta hai par jaldi sorry kehta hai (“I got carried away”); Crock ko cut karne ki himmat nahi; phir bhi use rather like karta hai.
  • Frank ⭐: young master. Taplow ko rokta bhi hai, baat karne bhi deta hai (“Much too far”, phir use line repeat karne ko kehta hai); Millie ko dekh kar “Oh Lord!” kehta hai.
  • Millie ⭐: Mrs Crocker-Harris; late thirties, patli, smartly dressed. Pehle bina expression (“Hullo”), phir Taplow se smiling kehti hai: “I’ll take the blame.”
  • Vishay ⭐: students aur masters ka peeth peeche baat karna; NCERT notes mein Science aur Literature ka dichotomy bhi.

🎯 Crock = strict, dar ka karan (Taplow ke mutabik). Taplow = mazaak + halka lagav. Frank = beech ka master.

4️⃣ Mukhya Vishay ⭐⭐

  • Teacher aur dar ⭐⭐: Taplow: students Crocker-Harris se scared to death; use cut karna kabhi nahi hua.
  • Bahari vs andar ⭐⭐: Taplow ‘hardly human’ kehta hai par saath hi use rather like karta hai — bahari bartav aur asli feeling alag ho sakti hain.
  • Peeth peeche baat ⭐: Frank Taplow ko ‘much too far’ kehta hai; NCERT ‘Talking about the text’ teachers ke baare mein baat karne ke tareeke par discuss karne ko kehta hai.
  • Science vs Literature ⭐⭐: Taplow ko science zyada exciting lagta hai; Frank ko jo science use padhana padta hai woh pasand nahi.
  • Dar aur hasi ⭐: Taplow Crock ke joke par politeness mein hansa tha; us par Crock ka jawab uski nakal mein aata hai.

🎯 Theme ke 4 shabd: dar, bahari vs andar, peeth peeche baat, science vs literature.

1️⃣ लेखक और नाटक परिचय ⭐⭐

  • टेरेंस रैटिगन ⭐⭐: ब्रिटिश नाटककार; यह द ब्राउनिंग वर्ज़न का एक अंश है (NCERT पाठ में लेखक के वर्ष या नाटक की तारीख़ नहीं दी गई)।
  • शीर्षक ⭐⭐: NCERT की पाद-टिप्पणी: नाटक में रॉबर्ट ब्राउनिंग द्वारा ग्रीक त्रासदी ‘एगामेम्नॉन’ का अनुवाद आता है। अंश में टैपलो की किताब एस्किलस का एगामेम्नॉन है।
  • पात्र ⭐⭐: फ्रैंक (युवा अध्यापक), क्रॉकर-हैरिस (अधेड़ अध्यापक), टैपलो (16 वर्ष का लड़का), मिली (क्रॉकर-हैरिस की पत्नी)।

2️⃣ कथा-सारांश ⭐⭐

  • टैपलो-फ्रैंक बातचीत ⭐⭐: टैपलो लोअर फ़िफ़्थ में है; क्रॉकर-हैरिस नतीजे नहीं बताते। वह उनके जवाब “जो तुम्हें मिलना चाहिए, न कम, न ज़्यादा” की नक़ल करता है।
  • सैडिस्ट ⭐⭐: टैपलो: सैडिस्ट वह जो दर्द देकर आनंद पाए; क्रॉक वैसा नहीं, पर टैपलो के अनुसार भीतर से “सिकुड़ा हुआ” है। फिर भी टैपलो उन्हें पसंद करता है।
  • मिली ⭐⭐⭐: मिली भाव-रहित “हलो” कहकर आती है; टैपलो को मुस्कुराकर कहती है: “दोष मैं अपने ऊपर ले लूँगी”, और दवा का पर्चा केमिस्ट के पास ले जाने को कहती है।
  • अंश का अंत ⭐⭐: टैपलो पर्चा लेकर दरवाज़े की ओर चल देता है; क्रॉकर-हैरिस इस अंश में मंच पर नहीं आते।

3️⃣ चरित्र-चित्रण ⭐⭐

  • क्रॉकर-हैरिस ⭐⭐: टैपलो के अनुसार नतीजे न बताने वाले, विद्यार्थी उनसे बहुत डरते हैं।
  • टैपलो ⭐⭐: 16 वर्ष का, खुलकर बोलने वाला पर जल्दी माफ़ी माँगने वाला; क्रॉक को पसंद भी करता है।
  • फ्रैंक ⭐: युवा अध्यापक — टैपलो को टोकता भी है, सुनता भी है।
  • मिली ⭐: पैंतीस-चालीस की (“late thirties”), सलीक़े से सजी; “मैं दोष ले लूँगी” कहने वाली।

4️⃣ मुख्य विषय ⭐⭐

  • शिक्षक और डर ⭐⭐: टैपलो के अनुसार विद्यार्थी क्रॉकर-हैरिस से बहुत डरते हैं।
  • बाहर और भीतर ⭐⭐: टैपलो उन्हें ‘मुश्किल से इंसान’ कहता है, फिर भी पसंद करता है।
  • पीठ पीछे बात ⭐: फ्रैंक टैपलो को ‘बहुत आगे बढ़ गए’ कहता है।
  • विज्ञान बनाम साहित्य ⭐⭐: टैपलो को विज्ञान अधिक रोचक लगता है।

1️⃣ Author and Play Introduction ⭐⭐

  • Terence Rattigan ⭐⭐: British playwright; this is an excerpt from The Browning Version (the NCERT text gives no dates).
  • The title ⭐⭐: the NCERT footnote: the play refers to Robert Browning’s translation of the Greek tragedy Agamemnon. In the excerpt, Taplow’s book is Aeschylus’ Agamemnon.
  • Characters ⭐⭐: Frank (young master), Crocker-Harris (middle-aged master), Taplow (a boy of sixteen), Millie (Crocker-Harris’s wife).

2️⃣ Plot Summary ⭐⭐

  • Taplow and Frank ⭐⭐: Taplow is in the lower fifth; Crocker-Harris does not tell results. He imitates the reply “exactly what you deserve. No less; and certainly no more.”
  • Sadist ⭐⭐: Taplow: a sadist gets pleasure out of giving pain; Crock is not one, but is “shrivelled up inside like a nut” in Taplow’s view. Taplow still rather likes him.
  • Millie ⭐⭐⭐: she enters without expression (“Hullo”), then tells Taplow smiling: “I’ll take the blame”, and sends him to the chemist with a prescription.
  • The end of the excerpt ⭐⭐: Taplow heads for the door with the prescription; Crocker-Harris does not appear in this excerpt.

3️⃣ Character Sketches ⭐⭐

  • Crocker-Harris ⭐⭐: in Taplow’s account he does not tell results and the boys are scared to death of him.
  • Taplow ⭐⭐: sixteen, outspoken but quick to say sorry; he rather likes Crock all the same.
  • Frank ⭐: the young master who both checks Taplow and lets him talk.
  • Millie ⭐: late thirties, smartly dressed; the one who says “I’ll take the blame”.

4️⃣ Main Themes ⭐⭐

  • Teacher and fear ⭐⭐: Taplow says the boys are scared to death of Crocker-Harris.
  • Outside and inside ⭐⭐: Taplow calls him ‘hardly human’ yet rather likes him.
  • Talking behind backs ⭐: Frank tells Taplow he has gone ‘much too far’.
  • Science vs literature ⭐⭐: Taplow finds science more exciting.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 The Browning Version ke lekhak kaun hain aur ye kya hai?
Answer dekho

Terence Rattigan — British playwright; ye The Browning Version ka ek excerpt hai (NCERT text mein saal nahi diye gaye).

Q2 Natak ke title ka kya sandarbh hai?
Answer dekho

NCERT footnote: natak mein Robert Browning ka Greek tragedy ‘Agamemnon’ ka translation aata hai. Excerpt mein Taplow ki book Aeschylus ka Agamemnon hai.

Q3 Taplow Crocker-Harris ke baare mein kya kehta hai?
Answer dekho

Woh results nahi batate, students unse scared to death hain, aur “hardly human”; par woh use rather like bhi karta hai.

Q4 Crocker-Harris ne Taplow ko kya jawab diya tha?
Answer dekho

“My dear Taplow, I have given you exactly what you deserve. No less; and certainly no more.”

Q5 Taplow ke mutabik sadist kaun hota hai?
Answer dekho

Jo dard dene mein khushi paaye. Taplow: Crock sadist nahi, par “shrivelled up inside like a nut”.

Q6 Taplow ko extra work kyun karna pad raha hai?
Answer dekho

Pichhle hafte ek din bimar tha, isliye aakhri din extra work; 6:30 ka time tha.

Q7 Frank Taplow ke comments par kaisa react karta hai?
Answer dekho

Use sunta bhi hai aur “Yes. Much too far” kehkar rokta bhi hai; baad mein use line repeat karne ko kehta hai.

Q8 Millie ka kirdaar excerpt mein kya hai?
Answer dekho

Crocker-Harris ki patni, late thirties; “I’ll take the blame” kehti hai aur Taplow ko prescription chemist ke paas bhejti hai.

Q9 Taplow ko science aur Aeschylus mein kya pasand hai?
Answer dekho

Use science zyada exciting lagta hai; Agamemnon ko ‘muck’ kehta hai par plot ko achha maanta hai — shikayat padhane ke tareeke se hai.

Q10 Excerpt ke baare mein ek zaroori baat kya hai?
Answer dekho

Crocker-Harris stage par nahi aate; unke baare mein sab kuch Taplow ke bayan se pata chalta hai.
