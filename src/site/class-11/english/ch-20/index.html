Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Writer aur Background ⭐⭐

  • A.J. Cronin ⭐⭐: NCERT excerpt mein writer ka naam nahi aata; teaching background: kahani A.J. Cronin ki hai, jo Scottish author aur trained doctor the.
  • Source ⭐⭐: teaching background: yeh novel The Citadel ka extract mana jata hai; excerpt mein novel ya saal ka zikr nahi hai.
  • Setting ⭐⭐⭐: Blaenelly (miners ka kasba), raat ke lagbhag midnight mein Andrew Bryngower pahunchta hai; ghar Number 12, Blaina Terrace. Text mein Andrew ka Dr Edward Page ka assistant hona nahi likha.
  • Theme-line ⭐⭐ (supported reading): ek doctor ke liye textbook ke baad asli kaam; story ka ant: “I’ve done something real at last.”

💡 Cronin (Scottish doctor-writer) + The Citadel (1937) + Blaenelly (Wales) + Andrew Manson.

2️⃣ Story Summary ⭐⭐⭐

  • Shuruaat ⭐⭐⭐: raat ko Andrew Bryngower pahunchta hai; Joe Morgan, burly driller, uska intezaar kar raha hai — “The missus wants ye — before time, too.” Andrew Christine aur Cardiff station ki ghatna mein uljha hai.
  • Joe aur ghar ⭐⭐⭐: Joe kehta hai “I’ll not come in” aur bahar rukta hai; text mein “20 saal” ka koi zikr nahi hai.
  • Kamre mein ⭐⭐: Mrs Morgan ki maa (lambi, grey-haired, nearly seventy) aur stout, elderly midwife; budhiya tea banane ko kehti hai; Andrew: “I’ll not run away.”
  • Intezaar aur soch ⭐⭐: Andrew Bramwell, Edward Page, Denny ke dukhi vivaah yaad karta hai; Christine ki yaad se uska dil aur dimag conflict mein hai. Budhiya: “She’s awful set upon this child.”
  • Mushkil delivery ⭐⭐⭐: subah ki pehli kiran ke saath baccha lifeless paida; Susan collapsed, almost pulseless. Andrew pehle Susan ko injection deta hai, phir poochta hai “Where’s the child?”
  • Baccha bachana ⭐⭐⭐: midwife ne bachche ko bed ke neeche rakha tha; Andrew use nikalta hai (asphyxia pallida), garam aur thande paani mein baari-baari dalta hai aur chest dabata-chodta hai.
  • Jeet ⭐⭐⭐: pehle fifteen minutes, phir kul half an hour ki koshish; chest mein convulsive heave, phir cry; nurse: “it’s come alive.”
  • Ant ⭐⭐: Andrew Joe se kehta hai “Both all right”; subah miners ke saath chalte hue sochta hai “I’ve done something real at last.”

🎯 Sequence: buri sham → Joe ka bulava → lamba intezaar → lifeless baccha → pehle maa → garam-thanda paani → pehli saans → santushti.

3️⃣ Characters ⭐⭐⭐

  • Andrew Manson ⭐⭐⭐: thaka aur pareshan; baccha jab lifeless nikalta hai to shiver of horror, phir pehle maa, phir aadhe ghante tak koshish. Text mein “Manson” naam nahi aata; naam Andrew hai.
  • Joe Morgan ⭐⭐: burly driller; strain mein, bahar paace karta hai; “I know ye’ll do well for us.”
  • Susan Morgan ⭐⭐: maa; delivery mein jaan ka khatra.
  • Susan ki maa ⭐⭐: wise in experience; praying jab baccha zinda hota hai.
  • Midwife ⭐⭐: stout, elderly; frightened; kehti hai “It’s stillborn.”

💡 Andrew = himmati doctor | Joe = chintit pati | Susan ki maa = anubhavi.

4️⃣ Themes ⭐⭐⭐

  • Pesha aur farz ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): Andrew mother ki jaan ko pehle rakhta hai, phir bachche ko bachane ki koshish — book Q2 ka textbook vs practising physician sawal.
  • Himmat aur dhairya ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): “beaten, despairing” hone par bhi “one last effort”.
  • Jeevan ⭐⭐ (supported reading): “as by a miracle”; bachche ka rang pink; book Q3: medical help se jeevan bachane ke udaharan.
  • Nijee uljhan aur kartavya ⭐⭐ (supported reading): Christine aur vivaah ki soch ke baad turant kartavya (“after all that he had promised” ka dar).

🎯 Theme: pesha + himmat + jeevan ka chamatkar + kartavya.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Birth kis novel ka hissa hai?
Answer dekho

Text mein novel ka naam nahi; teaching background: yeh The Citadel (A.J. Cronin) ka extract mana jata hai.

Q2 Story kahan set hai?
Answer dekho

Blaenelly — miners ka kasba; ghar 12 Blaina Terrace.

Q3 Andrew Manson kaun hai?
Answer dekho

Ek doctor jo raat ko Susan Morgan ki delivery ke liye bulaya jaata hai (text mein sirf “Andrew”).

Q4 Joe Morgan Andrew ko kyon bulata hai?
Answer dekho

Kyunki uski patni Susan ko delivery honi hai aur use doctor ki zaroorat hai.

Q5 Susan ke bacche ka is baar kya khaas tha?
Answer dekho

Susan ki maa kehti hai: “She’s awful set upon this child” — sab ko is bacche se bahut lagaav hai. (20 saal ka zikr text mein nahi.)

Q6 Andrew ne pehle kisko bachaya aur kyon?
Answer dekho

Susan (maa) ko, kyunki uski halat bahut nazuk thi aur uske bina bachche ko bachane ka faayda nahi.

Q7 Andrew ne bachche ko kaise zinda kiya?
Answer dekho

Garam aur thande paani mein baari-baari dalkar aur artificial respiration se.

Q8 Bachcha kitni der baad saans leta hai?
Answer dekho

Pehle fifteen minutes, phir kul lagbhag half an hour ki koshish ke baad.

Q9 Story ke ant mein Andrew kaisa mehsoos karta hai?
Answer dekho

Gehri santushti — use lagta hai usne kuch sacha aur arthpurn kiya hai.

Q10 Story ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Text koi moral nahi deta. Supported reading: Andrew ka “I’ve done something real at last” dikhata hai ki asli kaam (textbook se alag) himmat aur lagan se jeevan bachata hai; book Q1-Q2 isi par hain.
