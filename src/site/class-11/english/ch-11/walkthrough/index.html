Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

AskAnd who art thou? said I to the soft-falling shower; the answer comes as here translated.
PoemI am the Poem of Earth, said the voice of the rain.
RiseEternal I rise impalpable out of the land and the bottomless sea, upward to heaven.
ChangeVaguely form’d, altogether changed, and yet the same.
DescendI descend to lave the drouths, atomies, dust-layers; seeds only, latent, unborn.
OriginI give back life to my own origin; the bracketed song returns with love.
