The Ailing Planet: the Green Movement's Role

Nani Palkhivala — Hornbill Ch 5

Bimar dharti — environment bachana hi insaan ka sabse bada dharma

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Lekhak aur Path Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Nani Palkhivala ⭐⭐: Bharat ke mahan vidhigya (jurist) aur vicharak — ye lekh unhone 1994 mein likha tha, aur aaj bhi utna hi relevant hai.
  • Path ka swaroop ⭐: ek vicharatmak lekh (essay) — environment ki bigadti halat aur Green Movement ki bhumika par.
  • 'Ailing Planet' ⭐⭐: 'ailing' ka matlab bimari se grast — dharti ko Palkhivala ek mareez ki tarah dikhate hain jiska ilaaj ab zaroori hai.
  • Green Movement ⭐⭐: 1972 mein New Zealand mein shuru hua world's first Green Party — uske baad duniya bhar mein environment bachane ka andolan phaila.
  • Kendriya bhav ⭐: dharti ko humne virasat mein nahi, udhaar liya hai apne bacchon se — use sambhal kar lautana humara farz hai.

💡 Pakka: Nani Palkhivala, 1994, Green Movement 1972 New Zealand. 'Ailing' = bimar. Title ka matlab samajhna sabse pehla question hai.

2️⃣ Dharti ki Haalat — Kya Bigda? ⭐⭐

  • 1972 ka Stockholme sammelan ⭐: pehli baar duniya ke desh ek saath mile — environment ko bachane ke liye UN Conference hua.
  • Jangal ka vinash ⭐⭐: duniya ke pramukh van (Amazon, etc.) kat rahe hain — lekhak kehte hain 'duniya ke jangal duniya ke aankhon ke saamne gayab ho rahe hain'.
  • Jaiv-vividhata ka khatra ⭐⭐: fish stocks khatam, species vilupt ho rahi hain — insaan ne prakriti ke balance ko tod diya.
  • Overpopulation ⭐⭐: duniya ki jan sankhya sabse bada dabaav hai — har 3-4 din mein duniya mein 10 lakh (one million) log badh jaate hain.
  • Soil aur land ⭐: desert badh raha hai, upjao zameen barbaad ho rahi hai — ek samay tha jab dharti khud sambhalti thi, ab nahi.

💡 Facts yaad rakho: Stockholm 1972, forest loss, fisheries collapse, population +1 million every 3-4 days, desertification.

3️⃣ Samadhan — Kya Karna Hoga? ⭐⭐

  • Sustainable development ⭐⭐: lekhak ka mool mantra — vikas aisa ho jo aaj ki zaroorat poori kare, bina kal ko nuksan pahunchaye. Iska zikr 1987 ki Brundtland Report ne kiya.
  • Population control ⭐⭐: Palkhivala kehte hain — 'development hi sabse accha contraceptive hai' — garibi kam hogi to jan sankhya apne aap sambhlegi.
  • Udyog ki zimmedari ⭐: industry ko environment-friendly hona hoga — pollute karne wale model se nikalna hoga.
  • Har vyakti ka yogdan ⭐⭐: ped lagana, paani bachana, plastic kam karna — chhote kadam milkar bada badlav laate hain.
  • Kanoon aur neeti ⭐: governments ko sakht environmental kanoon banane honge aur unhe lagu karna hoga.

🎯 Sustainable development definition + Brundtland Report 1987 + "development is the best contraceptive" — ye 3 exam must-haves hain.

4️⃣ Mukhya Vishay aur Sandesh ⭐⭐

  • Environment ki chintaa ⭐⭐: path ka dil — dharti bimari mein hai, aur insaan hi uska kaaran aur ilaaj dono hai.
  • Intergenerational responsibility ⭐⭐: hum dharti ke maalik nahi, trustee hain — use apni aane wali peedhiyon ke liye surakshit rakhna hai.
  • Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam ⭐: Palkhivala Bharat ki prachin soch ka zikr karte hain — poori duniya ek parivaar; environment kisi ek desh ka nahi, sabka mudda hai.
  • Hope ⭐: lekh nirasha ka nahi — Green Movement ne dikhaya ki sahi disha mein prayas ho to paristhiti sudhar sakti hai.
  • Prasangikta ⭐⭐: climate change ke aaj ke yug mein ye path aur bhi zyada relevant hai — har chhota prayas mayne rakhta hai.

🎯 Conclusion line: dharti udhaar li hai bacchon se, virasat mein nahi — is quote ko har theme answer mein lao.

1️⃣ लेखक और पाठ परिचय ⭐⭐

  • नानी पalkhivala ⭐⭐: भारत के प्रसिद्ध विधिज्ञ और विचारक — लेख 1994 में लिखा गया।
  • 'Ailing Planet' ⭐⭐: बीमार पृथ्वी — धरती एक रोगी की तरह है जिसका उपचार आवश्यक।
  • ग्रीन मूवमेंट ⭐⭐: 1972, न्यूज़ीलैंड में आरंभ — विश्व भर में पर्यावरण-रक्षा का आंदोलन।
  • केंद्रीय भाव ⭐: धरती हमें विरासत में नहीं, उधार मिली है अपने बच्चों से।

2️⃣ धरती की दशा ⭐⭐

  • स्टॉकहोम सम्मेलन ⭐: 1972 — पर्यावरण पर पहला वैश्विक मंच।
  • वन-विनाश ⭐⭐: प्रमुख जंगल तेज़ी से कट रहे हैं।
  • जैव-विविधता ⭐⭐: मछली भंडार समाप्त, प्रजातियाँ विलुप्त हो रही हैं।
  • जनसंख्या ⭐⭐: हर 3-4 दिन में दस लाख की वृद्धि — सबसे बड़ा दबाव।
  • भूमि ⭐: रेगिस्तान का विस्तार, उपजाऊ भूमि का क्षरण।

3️⃣ समाधान ⭐⭐

  • सतत विकास ⭐⭐: ऐसा विकास जो वर्तमान की आवश्यकता पूरी करे, भविष्य को नुकसान न पहुँचाए (ब्रुंडटलैंड रिपोर्ट, 1987)।
  • जनसंख्या नियंत्रण ⭐⭐: 'विकास ही सर्वोत्तम गर्भनिरोधक है' — ग़रीबी घटेगी तो जनसंख्या संभलेगी।
  • उद्योग और कानून ⭐: पर्यावरण-हितैशी उद्योग और सशक्त कानून आवश्यक।
  • व्यक्तिगत योगदान ⭐⭐: वृक्षारोपण, जल-बचत — छोटे कदम, बड़ा परिवर्तन।

4️⃣ मुख्य विषय ⭐⭐

  • पर्यावरण की चिंता ⭐⭐: धरती बीमार है; इंसान ही कारण और इलाज दोनों।
  • अंतर-पीढ़ीगत दायित्व ⭐⭐: हम धरती के स्वामी नहीं, संरक्षक (trustee) हैं।
  • वसुधैव कुटुम्बकम् ⭐: पर्यावरण सबका साझा दायित्व।
  • आशा ⭐: ग्रीन मूवमेंट सकारात्मक बदलाव का प्रमाण है।

1️⃣ Author and Lesson Introduction ⭐⭐

  • Nani Palkhivala ⭐⭐: a great Indian jurist and thinker — the article was written in 1994.
  • 'Ailing Planet' ⭐⭐: the Earth presented as a sick patient needing urgent treatment.
  • The Green Movement ⭐⭐: began in 1972 in New Zealand — a worldwide movement to protect the environment.
  • Core feeling ⭐: we have borrowed the Earth from our children, not inherited it from our ancestors.

2️⃣ The State of the Earth ⭐⭐

  • Stockholm Conference ⭐: 1972 — the first global platform on the environment.
  • Deforestation ⭐⭐: the world's great forests are disappearing before our eyes.
  • Loss of species ⭐⭐: fisheries collapsing, species going extinct.
  • Overpopulation ⭐⭐: a million people added every 3-4 days — the greatest pressure.
  • Land ⭐: deserts spreading, fertile soil eroding.

3️⃣ The Solutions ⭐⭐

  • Sustainable development ⭐⭐: development that meets present needs without harming the future (Brundtland Report, 1987).
  • Population control ⭐⭐: 'development is the best contraceptive' — prosperity stabilises population.
  • Industry and law ⭐: environment-friendly industry and strong laws are essential.
  • Individual effort ⭐⭐: planting trees, saving water — small steps, big change.

4️⃣ Main Themes ⭐⭐

  • Concern for the environment ⭐⭐: the Earth is ailing; man is both the cause and the cure.
  • Intergenerational duty ⭐⭐: we are trustees of the planet, not its owners.
  • Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam ⭐: the environment is everyone's shared responsibility.
  • Hope ⭐: the Green Movement proves change is possible.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 The Ailing Planet ke lekhak kaun hain?
Answer dekho

Nani Palkhivala — Bharat ke prasiddh jurist aur thinker; lekh 1994 mein likha gaya.

Q2 'Ailing Planet' title ka kya matlab hai?
Answer dekho

'Ailing' ka arth bimari se grast — dharti ek mareez ki tarah hai jiska ilaaj turant zaroori hai; isliye ye title uchit hai.

Q3 Green Movement kab aur kahan shuru hua?
Answer dekho

1972 mein New Zealand mein — duniya ki pehli Green Party ke saath; phir ye andolan vishwa-vyapi ho gaya.

Q4 Environment ke saamne pramukh samasyayein kya hain?
Answer dekho

Deforestation, species ka vilupt hona, fisheries ka collapse, desertification aur overpopulation — har 3-4 din mein duniya mein 10 lakh log badhte hain.

Q5 Sustainable development ka kya arth hai?
Answer dekho

Aisa vikas jo aaj ki zarooratein poori kare bina bhavishya ki peedhi ko nuksan pahunchaye — 1987 ki Brundtland Report ne is concept ko diya.

Q6 'Development is the best contraceptive' ka kya matlab hai?
Answer dekho

Palkhivala kehte hain vikas hi jan sankhya niyantran ka sabse accha tarika hai — garibi kam hogi aur log shikshit honge to population apne aap sambhal jayegi.

Q7 Dharti humein virasat mein nahi, udhaar mili hai — iska kya ashay hai?
Answer dekho

Hum dharti ke maalik nahi, trustee hain — use waise hi sambhal kar aane wali peedhiyon ko dena hai jaise humein mila tha.

Q8 Vyakti star par environment bachane ke liye kya kar sakte hain?
Answer dekho

Ped lagana, paani bachana, plastic ka upyog kam karna — chhote kadam milkar bada parivartan laate hain.

Q9 Stockholm Conference (1972) ka mahatva kya hai?
Answer dekho

Ye environment par duniya ka pehla vishwa manch tha — jahan deshon ne pehli baar milkar dharti bachane ki baat ki.

Q10 Path ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Dharti bachana har insaan ka dharma hai — hum hi is bimari ke kaaran hain aur hum hi ilaaj; Green Movement dikhata hai ki badlav sambhav hai.
