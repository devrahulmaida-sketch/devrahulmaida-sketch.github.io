Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Lekhak aur Path Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Nani Palkhivala ⭐⭐: Bharat ke mahan vidhigya (jurist) aur vicharak — ye lekh The Indian Express mein 24 November 1994 ko chhapa tha.
  • Path ka swaroop ⭐: ek vicharatmak lekh (essay) — environment ki bigadti halat aur Green Movement ki bhumika par.
  • 'Ailing Planet' ⭐⭐: 'ailing' ka matlab bimari se grast — dharti ko Palkhivala ek mareez ki tarah dikhate hain jiska ilaaj ab zaroori hai.
  • Green Movement ⭐⭐: 1972 mein New Zealand mein shuru hua world's first Green Party — uske baad duniya bhar mein environment bachane ka andolan phaila.
  • Kendriya bhav ⭐: dharti ko humne virasat mein nahi, udhaar liya hai apne bacchon se — use sambhal kar lautana humara farz hai.

💡 Pakka: Nani Palkhivala, 1994, Green Movement 1972 New Zealand. 'Ailing' = bimar. Title ka matlab samajhna sabse pehla question hai.

2️⃣ Dharti ki Haalat — Kya Bigda? ⭐⭐

  • Brandt Commission ⭐: shuruati international commissions mein se ek; member L.K. Jha (Bharatiya). First Brandt Report ka sawaal: kya hum apne uttaradhikariyon ko jhulsi hui dharti (advancing deserts, impoverished landscapes, ailing environment) dene wale hain?
  • Jangal ka vinash ⭐⭐: lekh ke mutabik tropical forests 40-50 million acres prati saal ki dar se kat rahe the; lekh (1994) ke mutabik Bharat mein har saal 3.7 million acres jangal ghat rahe the (Parliament ki Estimates Committee), aur asli nuksan sarkari aankdon se lagbhag 8 guna andaza tha.
  • Jaiv-vividhata ka khatra ⭐⭐: 1.4 million species ginti mein hain; lagbhag 3 se 100 million aur anaam hain. Dr Myers: tropical forest “powerhouse of evolution” hai, uske vinash se kai species khatre mein.
  • Overpopulation ⭐⭐: duniya ki jan sankhya sabse bada dabaav hai — text ke mutabik har chaar din mein 1 million log badhte hain (lekh ke 1994 ke andaze: duniya ~5.7 billion; Bharat ~920 million).
  • Soil aur land ⭐: desert badh raha hai, fisheries, forests, grasslands, croplands (Lester Brown ke chaar principal biological systems) par maang (lekh ke mutabik) unsustainable star par pahunch rahi thi: fisheries collapse, forests ghatein, grasslands banjar, croplands bigadti hain.

💡 Facts yaad rakho: Brandt Commission, 4 biological systems, forests 40-50 million acres/yr, India 3.7 million acres/yr, population +1 million every four days.

3️⃣ Samadhan — Kya Karna Hoga? ⭐⭐

  • Sustainable development ⭐⭐: lekhak ka mool mantra — vikas aisa ho jo aaj ki zaroorat poori kare, bina kal ko nuksan pahunchaye. Ise 1987 mein World Commission on Environment and Development ne popular kiya.
  • Population control ⭐⭐: Palkhivala kehte hain — 'development hi sabse accha contraceptive hai' — fertility tab girti hai jab income, education aur health badhte hain; par text voluntary family planning chahta hai, zabardasti nahi. Choice: population control ya garibi ka jaari rehna.
  • Udyog ki zimmedari ⭐: industry ka crucial role hai: Du Pont ke Chairman Edgar S. Woolard ne khud ko “Chief Environmental Officer” kaha — environmental performance mein excel karna zaroori.
  • Zoo ka aaina ⭐⭐: Lusaka (Zambia) ke zoo mein cage par likha tha “The world’s most dangerous animal” — andar sirf ek aaina. Lekh ke mutabik insaan domination se partnership ki taraf badalne laga.
  • Kanoon ⭐: Article 48A kehta hai ki State paryavaran aur van-jeevan ki raksha karega; par lekhak (1994) ke mutabik Bharat mein kanoon ka palan aur enforcement nahi hota tha.

🎯 Sustainable development definition (WCED, 1987) + "development is the best contraceptive" + Lusaka zoo ka aaina — text ke 3 pakke points.

4️⃣ Mukhya Vishay aur Sandesh ⭐⭐

  • Environment ki chintaa ⭐⭐: path ka dil — dharti bimari mein hai, aur insaan hi uska kaaran aur ilaaj dono hai.
  • Intergenerational responsibility ⭐⭐: hum dharti ke maalik nahi, trustee hain — use apni aane wali peedhiyon ke liye surakshit rakhna hai.
  • Thatcher aur Brown ⭐: Margaret Thatcher: “No generation has a freehold on this earth. All we have is a life tenancy”. Lester Brown: dharti forefathers se inherit nahi, bacchon se borrow ki hai.
  • Era of Responsibility ⭐: environmental problem hamara “passport for the future” hai; nayi soch ne Era of Responsibility shuru kiya — duniya ko ek integrated whole ki tarah dekhna.
  • Holistic view ⭐⭐: mechanistic se holistic aur ecological view mein badlav, Copernicus jaisa kraantikari; dharti ek living organism hai jiske hum hisse hain.

🎯 Conclusion line: Lester Brown ka quote — dharti hamein forefathers se nahi mili, bacchon se udhaar li hai.

1️⃣ लेखक और पाठ परिचय ⭐⭐

  • नानी पालखीवाला ⭐⭐: भारत के प्रसिद्ध विधिज्ञ और विचारक — लेख द इंडियन एक्सप्रेस में 24 नवंबर 1994 को छपा।
  • 'Ailing Planet' ⭐⭐: बीमार पृथ्वी — धरती एक रोगी की तरह है जिसका उपचार आवश्यक।
  • ग्रीन मूवमेंट ⭐⭐: 1972, न्यूज़ीलैंड में आरंभ — विश्व भर में पर्यावरण-रक्षा का आंदोलन।
  • केंद्रीय भाव ⭐: धरती हमें विरासत में नहीं, उधार मिली है अपने बच्चों से।

2️⃣ धरती की दशा ⭐⭐

  • ब्रांट आयोग ⭐: प्रारंभिक अंतर्राष्ट्रीय आयोगों में से एक; भारतीय सदस्य एल.के. झा। प्रश्न: क्या हम उत्तराधिकारियों को झुलसी हुई धरती देंगे?
  • वन-विनाश ⭐⭐: लेख (1994) के अनुसार उष्णकटिबंधीय वन हर साल 4-5 करोड़ एकड़ घट रहे थे; भारत में हर साल 37 लाख एकड़।
  • जैव-विविधता ⭐⭐: मछली भंडार समाप्त, प्रजातियाँ विलुप्त हो रही हैं।
  • जनसंख्या ⭐⭐: लेख के अनुसार हर चार दिन में दस लाख की वृद्धि; 1994 का अनुमान लगभग 5.7 अरब।
  • भूमि ⭐: रेगिस्तान का विस्तार, उपजाऊ भूमि का क्षरण।

3️⃣ समाधान ⭐⭐

  • सतत विकास ⭐⭐: ऐसा विकास जो वर्तमान की आवश्यकता पूरी करे, भविष्य को नुकसान न पहुँचाए (विश्व पर्यावरण एवं विकास आयोग, 1987)।
  • जनसंख्या नियंत्रण ⭐⭐: 'विकास ही सर्वोत्तम गर्भनिरोधक है' — ग़रीबी घटेगी तो जनसंख्या संभलेगी।
  • उद्योग और कानून ⭐: उद्योग की महत्वपूर्ण भूमिका (डुपॉन्ट के एडगर वूलार्ड: “मुख्य पर्यावरण अधिकारी”); अनुच्छेद 48A है, पर लेखक (1994) के अनुसार भारत में कानून का पालन नहीं होता था।
  • लुसाका का आईना ⭐⭐: पिंजरे पर “दुनिया का सबसे ख़तरनाक जानवर” — भीतर सिर्फ़ आईना; अब वर्चस्व से साझेदारी की ओर।

4️⃣ मुख्य विषय ⭐⭐

  • पर्यावरण की चिंता ⭐⭐: धरती बीमार है; इंसान ही कारण और इलाज दोनों।
  • अंतर-पीढ़ीगत दायित्व ⭐⭐: हम धरती के स्वामी नहीं, संरक्षक (trustee) हैं।
  • थैचर और ब्राउन ⭐: “किसी पीढ़ी का इस धरती पर स्वामित्व नहीं”; लेस्टर ब्राउन: धरती पूर्वजों से नहीं, बच्चों से उधार ली है।
  • आशा ⭐: ग्रीन मूवमेंट सकारात्मक बदलाव का प्रमाण है।

1️⃣ Author and Lesson Introduction ⭐⭐

  • Nani Palkhivala ⭐⭐: a great Indian jurist and thinker — the article was published in The Indian Express on 24 November 1994.
  • 'Ailing Planet' ⭐⭐: the Earth presented as a sick patient needing urgent treatment.
  • The Green Movement ⭐⭐: began in 1972 in New Zealand — a worldwide movement to protect the environment.
  • Core feeling ⭐: we have borrowed the Earth from our children, not inherited it from our ancestors.

2️⃣ The State of the Earth ⭐⭐

  • Brandt Commission ⭐: an early international commission on ecology; Indian member L.K. Jha. Its first report asked: are we to leave a scorched planet?
  • Deforestation ⭐⭐: per the 1994 article, tropical forests were eroding by 40-50 million acres a year; India was losing 3.7 million acres a year.
  • Loss of species ⭐⭐: fisheries collapsing, species going extinct.
  • Overpopulation ⭐⭐: a million people added every four days; the article estimates 5.7 billion (1994).
  • Land ⭐: deserts spreading, fertile soil eroding.

3️⃣ The Solutions ⭐⭐

  • Sustainable development ⭐⭐: development that meets present needs without harming the future (popularised in 1987 by the World Commission on Environment and Development).
  • Population control ⭐⭐: 'development is the best contraceptive' — prosperity stabilises population.
  • Industry and law ⭐: industry has a crucial role (Du Pont's Edgar Woolard, "Chief Environmental Officer"); Article 48A exists, but the author (1994) says laws were not enforced in India.
  • The Lusaka mirror ⭐⭐: a zoo cage reads "The world's most dangerous animal" - inside is a mirror; humans shift from domination to partnership.

4️⃣ Main Themes ⭐⭐

  • Concern for the environment ⭐⭐: the Earth is ailing; man is both the cause and the cure.
  • Intergenerational duty ⭐⭐: we are trustees of the planet, not its owners.
  • Thatcher and Brown ⭐: "No generation has a freehold on this earth"; Lester Brown: we have borrowed the earth from our children.
  • Era of Responsibility ⭐: the problem is our "passport for the future"; a holistic view sees the world as an integrated whole.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 The Ailing Planet ke lekhak kaun hain?
Answer dekho

Nani Palkhivala — Bharat ke prasiddh jurist aur thinker; lekh The Indian Express mein 24 Nov 1994 ko chhapa.

Q2 'Ailing Planet' title ka kya matlab hai?
Answer dekho

'Ailing' ka arth bimari se grast — dharti ek mareez ki tarah hai jiska ilaaj turant zaroori hai; isliye ye title uchit hai.

Q3 Green Movement kab aur kahan shuru hua?
Answer dekho

1972 mein New Zealand mein — duniya ki pehli Green Party ke saath; phir ye andolan vishwa-vyapi ho gaya.

Q4 Environment ke saamne pramukh samasyayein kya hain?
Answer dekho

Chaar principal biological systems (fisheries, forests, grasslands, croplands) par unsustainable dabaav, tropical forests ka kata jana (species khatre mein) aur population — lekh ke mutabik har chaar din mein 10 lakh log badhte the/badhte hain (1994 ka andaza).

Q5 Sustainable development ka kya arth hai?
Answer dekho

Aisa vikas jo aaj ki zarooratein poori kare bina bhavishya ki peedhi ko nuksan pahunchaye — 1987 mein World Commission on Environment and Development ne ise popular kiya.

Q6 'Development is the best contraceptive' ka kya matlab hai?
Answer dekho

Palkhivala kehte hain vikas hi jan sankhya niyantran ka sabse accha tarika hai — income, education aur health badhne se fertility girti hai; par text voluntary family planning chahta hai (zabardasti nahi). Choice: population control ya garibi.

Q7 Dharti humein virasat mein nahi, udhaar mili hai — iska kya ashay hai?
Answer dekho

Hum dharti ke maalik nahi, trustee hain — use waise hi sambhal kar aane wali peedhiyon ko dena hai jaise humein mila tha.

Q8 Lusaka (Zambia) ke zoo ke cage par kya likha tha aur iska kya matlab hai?
Answer dekho

Notice: “The world’s most dangerous animal”, par cage mein sirf aaina — insaan khud sabse khatarnak jaanwar hai; ab wo domination se partnership ki taraf badal raha hai.

Q9 Lekhak Bharat mein kanoon ke baare mein kya kehte hain?
Answer dekho

Article 48A State se paryavaran aur van-jeevan ki raksha ko kehta hai, par lekhak (1994) ke mutabik Bharat mein kanoon kabhi respect ya enforce nahi hote the; Estimates Committee ne jangalon ke near catastrophic depletion ko highlight kiya.

Q10 Path ka mukhya sandesh kya hai?
Answer dekho

Dharti bachana har insaan ka dharma hai — hum hi is bimari ke kaaran hain aur hum hi ilaaj; Green Movement dikhata hai ki badlav sambhav hai.
