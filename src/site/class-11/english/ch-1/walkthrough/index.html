Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

PortraitOld, wrinkled, short, fat, slightly bent; never pretty but always beautiful; spotless white, rosary.
VillageWakes him, prayer, slate bundle, stale chapatti; school at the temple; stale chapattis for dogs.
CityTurning-point. English school by bus. She feeds sparrows. Music = lewd associations; silence.
UniversityOwn room, link snapped. Spinning wheel sunrise to sunset; sparrow half-hour is her happiest.
ReturnKiss on the forehead at the station. Five years. Drum and home-coming songs; first time she did not pray.
DeathMild fever, end near, prays. Red shroud. Thousands of sparrows silent, ignore the bread, fly away.
