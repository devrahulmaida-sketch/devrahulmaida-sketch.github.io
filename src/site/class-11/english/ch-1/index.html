The Portrait of a Lady

Khushwant Singh — Hornbill Ch 1

Dadi ki kahani — pyaar, badlav aur khamosh judai

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ About the Author — Khushwant Singh ⭐

  • Khushwant Singh ⭐: (1915-2014) — famous Indian writer, journalist aur historian. Known for his wit, honest style aur simple prose.
  • Ye chapter ⭐: unki grandmother ke saath bachpan ki autobiographical sketch — real memories se likha gaya, isliye emotions bilkul genuine lagte hain.
  • Style: seedhi saadi narrative, chhoti-chhoti details se poora character khada kar deta hai — show, don't tell ka best example.
  • Title ⭐⭐: "The Portrait of a Lady" — portrait = dadi ka chitra, jo sirf shakal ka nahi, character aur sanskaaron ka hai. Kabhi physical beauty ka portrait nahi — spiritual beauty ka.

💡 Author + title ke meaning dono 3-mark questions mein aate hain. Autobiographical + simple prose + symbolic title — yahi teen points.

2️⃣ Story Summary — Teen Phases ⭐⭐

  • Phase 1 — Village ⭐⭐: narrator (Khushwant) chhota tha, parents city mein the, woh dadi ke saath gaon mein raha. Dadi use school chhodne jaati, temple mein beth kar scripture padhti, ghar aakar dono saath waqt bitate. Dadi roz subah usse uthati, choolhe par roti sekti, school ke raaste kutte ko roti khilati.
  • Phase 2 — City ⭐⭐: parents ne dono ko city bula liya. Ab woh English school mein bus se jaane laga — dadi school nahi ja sakti thi. Dheere-dheere common link tootne laga: naye school mein scripture nahi padhaya jaata tha, English words woh samajh nahi paati thi, music lessons use bilkul pasand nahi aaye (music = society mein "low" samajhti thi).
  • Phase 3 — University + Sparrows ⭐⭐: jab narrator university gaya, usko apna alag kamra mila — ab dadi aur bhi akeli. Usne apna spinning wheel chalana shuru kiya aur chugti hui sparrows ko daana khilana apna sabse bada sukh bana liya.
  • Abroad aur wapsi ⭐: narrator 5 saal ke liye abroad gaya — dadi ne airport par koi rona-dhona nahi kiya, bas maatha choom liya aur mala japne lagi. Wapsi par bhi woh waisi hi thi.
  • Death ⭐⭐: ek din dadi bimar padi. Doctors ne kaha mild fever hai, par use pata tha samay aa gaya hai. Usne kaha — kaam chhodkar bhajan aur prayer mein waqt bitayegi. Shaam ko mala japan karti hui chir-nidra mein chali gayi.

💡 3 phases yaad rakho: Village (close bond) → City (distance badha) → University (almost khatam). Har phase mein ek symbol: temple/dog → bus/school → spinning wheel/sparrows.

3️⃣ Grandmother ka Character Sketch ⭐⭐

  • Appearance ⭐: "could never have been pretty, but was always beautiful" — mature, wrinkled, short, thoda jhuki hui, spotless white clothes, haath mein mala, honthon par hamesha prayer. Beauty = shanti aur pavitrata se.
  • Deeply religious ⭐⭐: temple mein scripture, school mein prayer, har waqt mala — uski poori zindagi Ishwar-bhakti mein tuli hui.
  • Kind aur selfless ⭐: kutte ko roti, sparrows ko daana, potе ki poori dekh-bhaal — sabke liye prem, koi shikayat nahi.
  • Emotionally strong ⭐⭐: bidai par na royi, na roki — sirf maatha chooma aur prayer. Apne dard ko kabhi doosron par nahi daala.
  • Traditional values ⭐: English education aur music ko pasand nahi karti thi (scripture ke bina padhai adhoori, music "not for gentlefolk") — par apni choice zabardasti nahi thopти thi.

🎯 Character sketch 5-6 marks ka pakka question hai: appearance + religious + kind + strong + traditional — 5 points = full marks.

4️⃣ Themes aur Literary Devices ⭐⭐

  • Changing relationships ⭐⭐: samay aur modern life ke saath bachpan ka bond dheere-dheere kamzor hota hai — par pyaar kam nahi hota, sirf doori badhti hai.
  • Tradition vs Modernity ⭐: dadi = puraani values (scripture, prayer, simplicity); narrator = nayi generation (English school, science, abroad). Dono sahi — bas alag duniya.
  • Symbolism of sparrows ⭐⭐: dadi ke jaate hi hazaaron sparrows chupchaap aa baithin — daana rakha to khaya nahi, koi cheekh-taheen nahi. Unka khamosh shok insaano se zyada gehra tha. Death ke baad sab udd gaye, daana waise ka waisa.
  • Silence as language ⭐: dadi ki emotions kabhi shabdon mein nahi — khamoshi, mala, choomna — sab uski bhaasha the.
  • Narrative style ⭐: first-person memoir — chhoti observations se poori personality ubharti hai (white clothes, mala, wrinkles, sparrows).

🎯 Sparrows episode = sabse important passage. "Why didn't the sparrows eat?" — kyunki unka shok bhojan se upar tha; unhe dadi ke pyaar ki aadat thi.

1️⃣ लेखक परिचय — खुशवंत सिंह ⭐

  • खुशवंत सिंह ⭐: (1915-2014) — प्रसिद्ध भारतीय लेखक, पत्रकार व इतिहासकार; अपनी सरल, विनोदी और निष्पक्ष शैली के लिए जाने जाते हैं।
  • पाठ परिचय ⭐: यह पाठ उनकी दादी माँ के साथ बिताए बचपन की आत्मकथात्मक झलक है — जीवंत अनुभवों पर आधारित।
  • शीर्षक-विशेषण ⭐⭐: "The Portrait of a Lady" अर्थात एक स्त्री का चित्र — यह चित्र बाहरी सौंदर्य का नहीं, चरित्र, संस्कार और आध्यात्मिक सुंदरता का है।

2️⃣ कथा-सार — तीन चरण ⭐⭐

  • गाँव का जीवन ⭐⭐: लेखक अपनी दादी के साथ गाँव में रहता। दादी उसे स्कूल छोड़ने जातीं, मंदिर में बैठकर धार्मिक ग्रंथ पढ़तीं, रास्ते में कुत्ते को रोटी खिलातीं।
  • शहर का जीवन ⭐⭐: शहर आने पर लेखक अंग्रेज़ी स्कूल में बस से जाने लगा; दादी स्कूल नहीं जा सकतीं। नई पढ़ाई में धार्मिक ग्रंथ नहीं थे और संगीत-शिक्षा दादी को अनुचित लगी — दोनों का साझा बंधन टूटने लगा।
  • विश्वविद्यालय और गौरैयाँ ⭐⭐: विश्वविद्यालय जाने पर लेखक को अलग कमरा मिला; दादी चरखा चलाने लगीं और गौरैयों को दाना खिलाना उनका एकमात्र सुख बन गया।
  • विदेश-यात्रा व मृत्यु ⭐⭐: विदेश जाते समय दादी न रोईं, न रोकीं — माथा चूमा और माला जपने लगीं। लौटने के कुछ समय बाद उन्होंने काम छोड़कर प्रार्थना-भजन में समय बिताया और शांतिपूर्वक देह त्याग दी।

3️⃣ दादी का चरित्र-चित्रण ⭐⭐

  • रूप-रंग ⭐: "सुंदर कभी नहीं रही होंगी, पर सदैव मनोहर रहीं" — झुर्रियों भरा शांत मुख, सफेद वस्त्र, हाथ में माला, होंठों पर प्रार्थना।
  • परम धार्मिक ⭐⭐: मंदिर, प्रार्थना, माला-जप — जीवन का प्रत्येक क्षण ईश्वर-स्मरण में।
  • दयालु व निःस्वार्थ ⭐: कुत्ते, गौरैयाँ, पौत्र — सबके प्रति स्नेह; अपना कष्ट कभी व्यक्त न किया।
  • भावनात्मक दृढ़ता ⭐⭐: विदाई के समय भी संयम — रोया नहीं, केवल आशीर्वाद और प्रार्थना।
  • परंपरागत मूल्य ⭐: आधुनिक शिक्षा व संगीत से असहमति, पर कभी अपनी बात किसी पर नहीं थोपी।

4️⃣ विषय व साहित्यिक विशेषताएँ ⭐⭐

  • बदलते संबंध ⭐⭐: समय के साथ निकटता घटती है, किंतु प्रेम नहीं — दूरी बढ़ती है, बंधन नहीं टूटता।
  • परंपरा बनाम आधुनिकता ⭐: दादी पुरानी परंपरा की प्रतीक, लेखक नई पीढ़ी का — दोनों अपनी-अपनी दुनिया के प्रतीक।
  • गौरैयों का प्रतीकात्मक महत्व ⭐⭐: दादी के देहांत पर हज़ारों गौरैयाँ मौन बैठ गईं; दाना न खाया, चीखीं नहीं — उनका मौन शोक मनुष्यों के रोने से अधिक गहरा था।
  • मौन की भाषा ⭐: दादी ने अपनी भावनाएँ शब्दों में नहीं, कर्मों और मौन में व्यक्त कीं।

1️⃣ About the Author ⭐

  • Khushwant Singh ⭐: (1915-2014) — celebrated Indian novelist, journalist and historian, known for his wit, candour and lucid prose.
  • The text ⭐: an autobiographical sketch of his grandmother — drawn from real childhood memories, which gives it genuine warmth.
  • The title ⭐⭐: "The Portrait of a Lady" is a portrait not of physical prettiness but of character, values and spiritual beauty.

2️⃣ Summary — Three Phases ⭐⭐

  • Village life ⭐⭐: the young narrator lived with his grandmother. She woke him, walked him to school, read scriptures in the temple, and fed the village dogs on the way back.
  • City life ⭐⭐: in the city he went to an English school by bus; she could no longer accompany him. The new education taught no scriptures, and music lessons distressed her — the common link snapped.
  • University and the sparrows ⭐⭐: with a room of his own, she grew lonelier, took to her spinning wheel, and found her happiest hours feeding the sparrows.
  • Departure and death ⭐⭐: before his five years abroad she showed no tears — only a kiss on the forehead and her prayer beads. On his return she was unchanged; soon after, she stopped work, devoted herself to prayer, and died peacefully.

3️⃣ Character Sketch of the Grandmother ⭐⭐

  • Appearance ⭐: "could never have been pretty, but she was always beautiful" — wrinkled, bent, in spotless white, prayer beads in hand — beauty of serenity, not features.
  • Deeply religious ⭐⭐: scriptures, prayer and the rosary filled every moment.
  • Kind and selfless ⭐: dogs, sparrows, grandson — she cared for all and complained about nothing.
  • Emotionally strong ⭐⭐: at the farewell she neither wept nor protested — only kissed his forehead and prayed.
  • Traditional ⭐: she disapproved of English schooling and music, yet never imposed her views.

4️⃣ Themes and Literary Devices ⭐⭐

  • Changing relationships ⭐⭐: time and modern life loosen childhood bonds — distance grows, love does not die.
  • Tradition vs modernity ⭐: the grandmother embodies the old world; the narrator, the new.
  • Symbolism of the sparrows ⭐⭐: thousands of sparrows gathered silently at her death, refused the crumbs and mourned without a sound — a grief deeper than any human wailing.
  • Silence as language ⭐: her love spoke through deeds — the kiss, the beads, the feeding — never through words.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Khushwant Singh ne apni grandmother ko "pretty nahi par beautiful" kyun kaha?
Answer dekho

Kyunki unka nikhar shakal se nahi, shanti, pavitrata aur sanskaaron se aata tha — white clothes, mala aur prayer unki asli beauty thi.

Q2 Village mein dadi aur narrator ka routine kya tha?
Answer dekho

Dadi use uthati, taiyar karti, school chhodne jaati, temple mein scripture padhti, aur wapas aate waqt kutte ko roti khilati.

Q3 City aane par dono ke beech ka "common link" kyun toota?
Answer dekho

Naya English school — na scripture tha, na dadi usse padhane mein madad kar sakti thi; music lessons to use bilkul bure lage. Saath bitane ka waqt khatam ho gaya.

Q4 Dadi ko music lessons kyun pasand nahi aaye?
Answer dekho

Woh music ko achhe gharon ke layak nahi samajhti thi — uske hisaab se woh "gentlefolk" ka kaam nahi tha. Ye unki traditional soch dikhata hai.

Q5 University jaane par dadi ne apna waqt kaise bitaya?
Answer dekho

Din bhar spinning wheel chalaya aur shaam ko sparrows ko daana khilaya — yehi unka sabse bada sukh ban gaya.

Q6 Airport par dadi ne bidai kaise di?
Answer dekho

Na royi, na roki — 5 saal ki judai par bhi sirf maatha chooma aur mala japne lagi. Ye unki emotional strength aur asanskrit bhavna dikhata hai.

Q7 Maut se pehle dadi ne kaunsa naya faisla liya?
Answer dekho

Usne kaam chhodne ka faisla kiya — kaha ki ab sirf bhajan aur prayer mein waqt bitayegi. Use apne ant ka ehsaas tha.

Q8 Sparrows ne dadi ki maut par kaisa vyavhar dikhaya?
Answer dekho

Hazaaron sparrows chupchaap aa baithin; daana nahi khaya, cheekhin nahi. Chittha uthne ke baad sab khamoshi se udd gayin.

Q9 Sparrows ka episode kya symbolise karta hai?
Answer dekho

Shuddh, nishkamaan pyaar aur khamosh shok — insaano ke rone se zyada gehra. Dadi ne jinhe pyaar diya, woh usko antim shraddhanjali dene aaye.

Q10 Chapter ka mukhya theme kya hai?
Answer dekho

Samay ke saath badalte rishte aur tradition vs modernity — doori badhti hai par pyaar nahi marta; dadi ki maryada aur khamoshi uski sabse badi shakti hai.
