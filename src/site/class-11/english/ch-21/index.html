Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Poet aur Poem ⭐⭐

  • Vikram Seth ⭐⭐: intro: poem Mappings (1981) se, Collected Poems mein shamil, aur “(After Idries Shah)”. Seth ki doosri kitabon ka zikr is text mein nahi (teaching background).
  • Swaroop ⭐⭐ (supported reading): narrative poem — rhyming couplets mein ek kahani; humour aur irony (book Q4) aur satire ka asar.
  • Shuruaat ⭐⭐: 'In the city of which I sing / There was a just and placid King'.
  • Kendriya vichar ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): book intro: “Does he carry his notion of justice a bit too far?” — andha nyay, bheed ki maang aur raja ki murkhta par vyang.

💡 Vikram Seth + narrative satire + just and placid king + arch + melon king.

2️⃣ Poem Summary ⭐⭐⭐

  • Arch ka hukm ⭐⭐⭐: raja ne major thoroughfare par triumphal arch banwane ka aadesh diya “to edify spectators there”; workmen ne use banaya “since he was the King”.
  • Taj giri ⭐⭐⭐: raja arch ke neeche se guzra aur mukut kho baitha; “The arch was built too low.” Raja: “This is a disgrace. The chief of builders will be hanged.”
  • Blame game ⭐⭐⭐: chief builder → workmen → wrong-sized bricks → masons → architect; architect: “You made certain amendments to the plans” — doshi khud raja nikalta hai, tab raja “saw red”.
  • Wise man ka fasla ⭐⭐⭐: wisest man ko laya (carried) gaya — could not walk and could not see, so old (and therefore wise). Uska faisla: “the arch… banged the crown off, and it must be hanged.”
  • Councillor ki aapatti ⭐⭐: “How can we hang so shamefully what touched your head, Your Majesty?” — raja: “True.”
  • Bheed ki maang ⭐⭐⭐: crowd restless, muttering; raja trembled: “Let us postpone consideration of finer points like guilt. The nation wants a hanging.”
  • Raja hi phaansi par ⭐⭐⭐: noose somewhat high; har aadmi ko napa gaya, sirf ek aadmi itna lamba nikla — the King; “hanged by Royal Decree”.
  • Melon raja ⭐⭐⭐: ministers practical-minded; heralds ne elaan kiya ki City Gate se agla guzarne wala raja chunega. Ek idiot guzra; “A melon” uska standard answer tha (he liked melons) — melon ko takht par bitha diya gaya.

🎯 Sequence: neecha arch → mukut giri → blame game → wise man → bheed ki maang → raja phaansi par → idiot ka jawab → melon king.

3️⃣ Characters aur Devices ⭐⭐⭐

  • The King ⭐⭐⭐: text: “just and placid”; par anger (frown, saw red), phir trembled bheed se (supported reading: kamzor aur apni jaan bachane wala).
  • Chief builder, workmen, masons, architect ⭐⭐: har koi dosh agle par daalta hai; architect raja ke amendments yaad dilata hai.
  • Wise man ⭐⭐ (supported reading): “old (and therefore wise)” — yeh irony hai: salah ek arch ko phaansi dene ki.
  • Ministers ⭐⭐: “practical-minded men”; kehte hain “Thank Goodness we found someone” aur “Long live the King! The King is dead.”
  • Satire aur irony ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): book Q4 ke udaharan: “The King was therefore hanged”, “old (and therefore wise)”, “Long live the King! The King is dead.”

💡 King = kamzor | Wise man = absurd salah | Ministers = chaalaak | Style = satire + irony.

4️⃣ Themes ⭐⭐⭐

  • Andha nyay ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): “finer points like guilt” ko taal diya gaya; “someone, and that immediately” phaansi par.
  • Sattadhaari ki murkhta ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): raja apni hi amendments bhool gaya aur kisi aur ko phaansi dena chahta tha.
  • Zimmedari se bachna ⭐⭐: builder se architect tak har koi dosh doosre par daalta hai.
  • Laissez faire aur choice ⭐⭐ (supported reading): ant mein log kehte hain “customary choice” aur “Peace and Liberty”; poem “laissez faire” kehti hai. Ise democracy par vyang padhna ek supported reading hai; book Q3 poochta hai ki peace aur liberty kaise banaye rakhen.

🎯 Theme: andha nyay + murkh satta + zimmedari se bachna + loktantra par vyang.

१. कवि और कविता

  • विक्रम सेठ ⭐⭐: परिचय के अनुसार कविता Mappings (1981) से; “After Idries Shah”।
  • स्वरूप ⭐⭐: तुकबंद कथा-कविता; हास्य और विडंबना (supported reading)।
  • लक्ष्य ⭐⭐: अंधा न्याय और सत्ता।

२. सारांश

  • कारण ⭐⭐⭐: नीचा मेहराब, गिरा मुकुट।
  • दोष-खेल ⭐⭐⭐: बिल्डर, मज़दूर, ईंटें, राजमिस्त्री, वास्तुकार (राजा के बदलाव)।
  • अंत ⭐⭐⭐: राजा को फाँसी, खरबूजा राजा।

३. पात्र और शैली

  • राजा ⭐⭐⭐: “just and placid”; गुस्सा और डर (supported reading: कमज़ोर)।
  • मंत्री ⭐⭐: “practical-minded men”।
  • शैली ⭐⭐⭐: व्यंग्य और विडंबना।

४. विषय

  • सत्ता ⭐⭐⭐: अहंकार और मूर्खता।
  • व्यंग्य ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): “customary choice” से चुना गया खरबूजा राजा।

1. Poet and Poem

  • Vikram Seth ⭐⭐: intro: from Mappings (1981), “After Idries Shah”.
  • Form ⭐⭐ (supported reading): a rhymed narrative poem with humour and irony.
  • Target ⭐⭐: blind justice and power.

2. Poem Summary

  • Cause ⭐⭐⭐: a low arch, a fallen crown.
  • Blame game ⭐⭐⭐: builder, workmen, bricks, masons, architect (the king’s amendments).
  • End ⭐⭐⭐: the king is hanged, a melon is crowned.

3. Characters and Devices

  • King ⭐⭐⭐: “just and placid”; angry, then frightened (supported reading: weak).
  • Ministers ⭐⭐: “practical-minded men”.
  • Style ⭐⭐⭐: satire and irony.

4. Themes

  • Justice ⭐⭐⭐: blind and for show.
  • Power ⭐⭐⭐: arrogance and folly.
  • Satire ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): a melon chosen by “customary choice”.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Who wrote The Tale of Melon City?
Answer dekho

Vikram Seth; the intro says the poem is from Mappings (1981) and is “After Idries Shah”.

Q2 What kind of poem is it?
Answer dekho

A rhymed narrative poem; the humour and irony (book Q4) give it a satirical edge.

Q3 Why did the king order an arch?
Answer dekho

To span the major thoroughfare triumphally and “edify spectators there”.

Q4 What went wrong with the arch?
Answer dekho

It was too low; the king's crown knocked against it and fell off.

Q5 Who did the king blame first?
Answer dekho

The chief of builders, who blamed the workmen; they blamed the bricks, then the masons, then the architect, who said the king had made amendments.

Q6 What did the wise man say?
Answer dekho

That the arch itself was guilty because it had struck the king's crown.

Q7 Why was the arch not hanged?
Answer dekho

A councillor said it would be shameful to hang an arch that had touched the king's crown.

Q8 Why was the king finally hanged?
Answer dekho

The crowd wanted a hanging, and he was the only man tall enough for the noose.

Q9 How was the new king chosen?
Answer dekho

The next person through the City Gate would choose; an idiot said 'a melon', so a melon was crowned.

Q10 What is the poem's main message?
Answer dekho

The text states no moral. Supported reading: the king’s “finer points like guilt” line and the melon crowned by “customary choice” show rule without thought and justice for show; book Q2-Q3 ask what “just and placid” and “peace and liberty” mean.
