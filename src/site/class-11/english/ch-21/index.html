The Tale of Melon City

Vikram Seth — Snapshots Poem

Ek raja, ek arch aur ek kharbooza — loktantra par vyang

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Poet aur Poem ⭐⭐

  • Vikram Seth ⭐⭐: Indian poet aur novelist (A Suitable Boy ke lekhak).
  • Swaroop ⭐⭐⭐: ek narrative (kahani-jaisi) poem, jo hasya-vyang (satire) se bhari hai.
  • Shuruaat ⭐⭐: 'In the city of which I sing / There was a just and placid King'.
  • Kendriya vichar ⭐⭐⭐: andhe nyay, bheed ki maang aur sattadhaariyon ki murkhta par vyang.

💡 Vikram Seth + narrative satire + just and placid king + arch + melon king.

2️⃣ Poem Summary ⭐⭐⭐

  • Arch ka hukm ⭐⭐⭐: just and placid king ne mukhya sadak par ek arch banwane ka aadesh diya, jise dekhkar logon ka mann aur charitra sudhre.
  • Taj giri ⭐⭐⭐: arch bahut neecha tha; raja ka mukut arch se takraya aur gir gaya. Raja ne ise apmaan maana.
  • Blame game ⭐⭐⭐: raja ne pehle chief builder ko phaansi ka hukm diya; builder ne mazdooron par, mazdooron ne eenton ke galat size par, phir masons par dosh daala — aur sab par baari-baari phaansi ka aadesh hota gaya.
  • Wise man ka fasla ⭐⭐⭐: raja ne sabse budhe aur samajhdar aadmi se salah li; usne kaha arch hi doshi hai, kyunki usne mukut ko maara. Arch ko phaansi ke liye laya gaya.
  • Parshad ki aapatti ⭐⭐: ek councillor ne kaha ki jis arch ne raja ke mukut ko chhua, use phaansi dena sharm ki baat hogi.
  • Bheed ki maang ⭐⭐⭐: tamasha dekhne aayi bheed bechain ho gayi; raja ne kaha kisi ko to phaansi honi hi chahiye, kyunki desh ko phaansi chahiye.
  • Raja hi phaansi par ⭐⭐⭐: phande ka naap thoda ooncha tha; sabko baari-baari naapa gaya aur sirf raja hi itna lamba nikla — isliye raja ko phaansi di gayi.
  • Melon raja ⭐⭐⭐: naya raja chunne ke liye tay hua ki jo agla vyakti City Gate se guzre, wahi raja chunega. Ek idiot guzra, jiska har sawal ka pasandida jawab tha 'melon' — isliye ek melon ko raja bana diya gaya.

🎯 Sequence: neecha arch → mukut giri → blame game → wise man → bheed ki maang → raja phaansi par → idiot ka jawab → melon king.

3️⃣ Characters aur Devices ⭐⭐⭐

  • The King ⭐⭐⭐: 'just and placid' dikhta hai, par kamzor, bina soch ka aur apmaan se chidne wala.
  • Chief builder aur workmen ⭐⭐: apni zimmedari doosron par daalne wale.
  • Wise man ⭐⭐: budha par sankeerna — aisi salah jo tark ko hasya bana deti hai.
  • Ministers ⭐⭐: chaalaak, bheed ke dar se kisi ko bhi phaansi dene ko taiyaar.
  • Satire aur irony ⭐⭐⭐: seedhi, saral bhasha mein gambhir baat — isse hasya aur vyang paida hota hai.

💡 King = kamzor | Wise man = absurd salah | Ministers = chaalaak | Style = satire + irony.

4️⃣ Themes ⭐⭐⭐

  • Andha nyay ⭐⭐⭐: nyay ki jagah dikhava aur bheed ki khushi — asli doshi ki jagah koi bhi phaansi par.
  • Sattadhaari ki murkhta ⭐⭐⭐: bina soche-samjhe faisle aur ahankaar.
  • Zimmedari se bachna ⭐⭐: har koi dosh doosre par daalta hai.
  • Loktantra par vyang ⭐⭐⭐: bina soche chuna gaya shasak — ek melon bhi raja ban sakta hai.

🎯 Theme: andha nyay + murkh satta + zimmedari se bachna + loktantra par vyang.

१. कवि और कविता

  • विक्रम सेठ ⭐⭐: कवि और उपन्यासकार।
  • स्वरूप ⭐⭐⭐: व्यंग्य-कथा कविता।
  • लक्ष्य ⭐⭐: अंधा न्याय और सत्ता।

२. सारांश

  • कारण ⭐⭐⭐: नीचा मेहराब, गिरा मुकुट।
  • दोष-खेल ⭐⭐⭐: बिल्डर, मज़दूर, ईंट, राजमिस्त्री।
  • अंत ⭐⭐⭐: राजा को फाँसी, खरबूजा राजा।

३. पात्र और शैली

  • राजा ⭐⭐⭐: कमज़ोर और मूर्ख।
  • मंत्री ⭐⭐: चालाक।
  • शैली ⭐⭐⭐: व्यंग्य और विडंबना।

४. विषय

  • न्याय ⭐⭐⭐: अंधा और दिखावटी।
  • सत्ता ⭐⭐⭐: अहंकार और मूर्खता।
  • व्यंग्य ⭐⭐⭐: बिना सोचे चुना शासक।

1. Poet and Poem

  • Vikram Seth ⭐⭐: poet and novelist.
  • Form ⭐⭐⭐: a satirical narrative poem.
  • Target ⭐⭐: blind justice and power.

2. Poem Summary

  • Cause ⭐⭐⭐: a low arch, a fallen crown.
  • Blame game ⭐⭐⭐: builder, workmen, bricks, masons.
  • End ⭐⭐⭐: the king is hanged, a melon is crowned.

3. Characters and Devices

  • King ⭐⭐⭐: weak and foolish.
  • Ministers ⭐⭐: cunning.
  • Style ⭐⭐⭐: satire and irony.

4. Themes

  • Justice ⭐⭐⭐: blind and for show.
  • Power ⭐⭐⭐: arrogance and folly.
  • Satire ⭐⭐⭐: a ruler chosen without thought.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Who wrote The Tale of Melon City?
Answer dekho

Vikram Seth.

Q2 What kind of poem is it?
Answer dekho

A narrative poem full of satire.

Q3 Why did the king order an arch?
Answer dekho

To improve the minds and character of the people who passed under it.

Q4 What went wrong with the arch?
Answer dekho

It was too low; the king's crown knocked against it and fell off.

Q5 Who did the king blame first?
Answer dekho

The chief builder, who blamed the workmen; they blamed the bricks and then the masons.

Q6 What did the wise man say?
Answer dekho

That the arch itself was guilty because it had struck the king's crown.

Q7 Why was the arch not hanged?
Answer dekho

A councillor said it would be shameful to hang an arch that had touched the king's crown.

Q8 Why was the king finally hanged?
Answer dekho

The crowd wanted a hanging, and he was the only man tall enough for the noose.

Q9 How was the new king chosen?
Answer dekho

The next person through the City Gate would choose; an idiot said 'a melon', so a melon was crowned.

Q10 What is the poem's main message?
Answer dekho

A satire on foolish rulers, blind justice and unthinking choices in government.
