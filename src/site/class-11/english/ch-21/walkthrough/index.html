Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

ArchA just and placid King has an arch built to edify spectators; workmen build it since he is King.
CrownThe arch is too low; he loses his crown; the chief of builders is to hang.
BlameBuilder, workmen, bricks, masons, architect - and the King’s own amendments.
WiseThe old wise man says hang the arch; a Councillor objects; the crowd mutters.
Noose“The nation wants a hanging”; only the King is tall enough for the high noose.
MelonThe City Gate custom; an idiot says “A melon”; people accept it: “Peace and Liberty”.
