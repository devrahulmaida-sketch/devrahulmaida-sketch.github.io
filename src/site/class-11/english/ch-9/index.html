Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Kavi aur Kavita Parichay ⭐⭐

  • Shirley Toulson ⭐⭐: kavita ka naam A Photograph; speaker apni maa ki purani photo (“the cardboard”) dekh rahi hai. NCERT text mein kavi ke baare mein isse zyada nahi diya.
  • Photo ⭐⭐: photo mein two girl cousins samundar mein paddle kar rahi hain, har ek speaker ki maa ka ek haath pakde; maa “the big girl — some twelve years or so”. Sab uncle with the camera ke liye muskurate hain. Maa ke dwara baad mein naam aate hain: Betty aur Dolly.
  • Kavita ka dhancha ⭐: NCERT ka sawaal: three stanzas = three phases — (1) photo ka din, (2) twenty-thirty years later maa ka snapshot par hasna, (3) Now — maa ki maut ke baad.
  • Kendriya bhav ⭐⭐: text se: sea changed less, par photo ke log aur ‘sea holiday’ past ban gaye; ant mein loss aur silence.
  • Form ⭐: short poem, teen stanza; bhasha saral par bhaav gehra. NCERT poems ko rhythm aur sound patterns ke saath padhne ko kehta hai (poem aloud).

💡 Photo (cardboard) → maa ka laughter (twenty-thirty years later) → ‘Now’ (dead nearly as many years as the girl lived) → silence.

2️⃣ Kavita-Saransh (Stanza-wise) ⭐⭐

  • Stanza 1 ⭐⭐: cardboard dikhata hai “how it was”: do cousins paddling, maa ke haath pakde; sab muskurate hain “through their hair”; a sweet face, my mother’s, that was before I was born. Sea changed less; usne “terribly transient feet” dhoye.
  • Stanza 2 ⭐⭐: “Some twenty — thirty years later” maa snapshot par laugh karti: “See Betty and Dolly… look how they dressed us for the beach.” The sea holiday was her past, mine is her laughter. Dono “wry with the laboured ease of loss”.
  • Past aur laughter ⭐⭐: maa ke liye sea holiday past hai; speaker ke liye maa ki laughter past hai. Text yehi dono past saath rakhta hai.
  • Stanza 3 ⭐⭐: “Now she’s been dead nearly as many years / As that girl lived.” Photo ki ladki lagbhag 12 saal ki thi, to maut ko lagbhag utne hi saal hue; “nothing to say at all.”
  • Antim pankti ⭐⭐⭐: “Its silence silences.” — is circumstance par kehne ko kuch nahi; NCERT Q6 poochta hai ki “this circumstance” kis ke liye hai (maa ki maut ke saal ginne wali baat).

🎯 3 stanza pakka: photo (smiling) → maa ka laugh (wry) → ‘Now’ (silence). NCERT Q7 yehi poochta hai.

3️⃣ Pramukh Panktiyan (Vyakhya) ⭐⭐

  • ‘The cardboard shows me how it was…’ ⭐: NCERT Q1: cardboard kya dikhata hai aur ye shabd kyun? Text photo ko cardboard kehta hai; ye kyun, ye apne shabdon mein likho (text khud nahi batata).
  • ‘And the sea, which appears to have changed less…’ ⭐⭐: NCERT Q3: kya nahi badla? The sea; terribly transient feet (paddling wali ladkiyan) ke saath contrast. Transient = temporary.
  • ‘A sweet face, my mother’s…’ ⭐: speaker ne apni maa ko tab dekha jab “before I was born”; photo se ek aisi maa dikhti hai jise speaker ne bachpan mein nahi dekha.
  • ‘Both wry with the laboured ease of loss’ ⭐⭐⭐: NCERT Q5. Wry = rueful, aadha hansi aadha dukh; laboured ease = ease jo mehnat se aati hai. Dono (maa aur speaker) loss ke saath jeete hain.
  • ‘Its silence silences’ ⭐⭐⭐: “There is nothing to say at all. Its silence silences.” — kehne ko kuch nahi bacha; text yehi bolta hai.

💡 3 must-quote lines: sea changed less / laboured ease of loss / its silence silences.

4️⃣ Kavya-Saundarya aur Vishay ⭐⭐

  • Mukhya vishay ⭐⭐: time aur loss — photo ek din ko dikhati hai; baad mein laughter aur phir maut. ‘Transient’ aur ‘changed less’ ka contrast.
  • Shabd aur sound ⭐⭐: text mein ‘laboured ease’ (do alag bhaav ek saath), ‘silence silences’ (shabd ka dohrana), ‘terribly transient feet’ (t-sounds). Inhe exam mein apne shabdon mein samjhao; NCERT yahan device ke naam nahi deta.
  • Tone ⭐: wry aur shaant, ant mein silence; speaker zyada kuch nahi kehti.
  • Yaad aur photo ⭐: photo how it was dikhati hai; maa ki laughter ek aur yaad hai. NCERT Q2: camera ne kya capture kiya? ek pal — girls, sea, smiles.
  • Sandesh ⭐⭐: Text mein koi moral label ke roop mein nahi likha. Supported reading: photo aur laughter ke zariye loss ko yaad kiya ja raha hai, aur ant mein kehne ko kuch nahi bachta. Exam mein pehle text ki line quote karo, phir ye reading apne shabdon mein likho.

🎯 Exam: text ki line quote karo phir apni vyakhya likho (loss, transient, silence).

1️⃣ कवि और कविता परिचय ⭐⭐

  • शर्ली टूल्सन ⭐⭐: कविता A Photograph; वक्ता अपनी माँ की पुरानी फ़ोटो (“the cardboard”) देख रही है। NCERT पाठ में कवि के बारे में इससे ज़्यादा नहीं दिया।
  • फ़ोटो ⭐⭐: दो चचेरी बहनें समुद्र में paddle कर रही हैं, हर एक ने माँ का एक हाथ पकड़ा है; माँ “the big girl — some twelve years or so”। बाद में माँ Betty और Dolly के नाम लेती हैं।
  • ढाँचा ⭐: NCERT का प्रश्न: तीन stanza = तीन चरण — फ़ोटो का दिन; twenty-thirty years later माँ का हँसना; Now — माँ की मृत्यु के बाद।

2️⃣ कविता-सारांश ⭐⭐

  • चरण 1 ⭐⭐: cardboard दिखाता है कि वह दिन कैसा था: लड़कियाँ बालों के बीच से मुस्कुराती हैं; sea changed less; terribly transient feet।
  • चरण 2 ⭐⭐: twenty-thirty years later माँ snapshot पर हँसतीं: “See Betty and Dolly…”। The sea holiday was her past, mine is her laughter.
  • चरण 3 ⭐⭐: “Now she’s been dead nearly as many years / As that girl lived.” यानी लगभग उतने ही वर्ष जितने फ़ोटो की लड़की की उम्र थी (लगभग 12)।
  • अंतिम पंक्ति ⭐⭐⭐: “Its silence silences.” — इस स्थिति पर कहने को कुछ नहीं।

3️⃣ प्रमुख पंक्तियाँ ⭐⭐

  • ‘The sea… changed less’ ⭐⭐: समुद्र कम बदला; फ़ोटो की लड़कियों के पैर transient (अस्थायी)।
  • ‘Laboured ease of loss’ ⭐⭐⭐: wry और मेहनत से आई सहजता — माँ और वक्ता दोनों loss के साथ।
  • ‘Silence silences’ ⭐⭐⭐: कहने को कुछ नहीं बचा।

4️⃣ काव्य-सौंदर्य ⭐⭐

  • विषय ⭐⭐: समय और loss — फ़ोटो एक दिन दिखाती है, फिर हँसी और फिर मृत्यु।
  • शब्द-ध्वनि ⭐⭐: ‘laboured ease’, ‘silence silences’ — पाठ में ये शब्द-प्रयोग हैं; नाम परीक्षा में अपने शब्दों में समझाओ।
  • भाव ⭐: wry और शांत; अंत में silence।
  • परीक्षा ⭐⭐: पाठ की पंक्ति उद्धृत करो, फिर अपनी व्याख्या लिखो।

1️⃣ Poet and Poem Introduction ⭐⭐

  • Shirley Toulson ⭐⭐: the poem is A Photograph; the speaker looks at an old photo of her mother (“the cardboard”). The NCERT text gives no more about the poet.
  • The photograph ⭐⭐: two girl cousins paddle in the sea, each holding one of the mother’s hands; the mother is “the big girl — some twelve years or so”. Later she names Betty and Dolly.
  • Structure ⭐: NCERT asks about three stanzas, three phases: the photo’s day; twenty-thirty years later, the mother laughing; Now, after her death.

2️⃣ Stanza Summary ⭐⭐

  • Stanza 1 ⭐⭐: the cardboard shows how it was: all smile through their hair at the uncle with the camera; the sea has changed less; terribly transient feet.
  • Stanza 2 ⭐⭐: twenty-thirty years later she laughs at the snapshot: “See Betty and Dolly…”. The sea holiday was her past, mine is her laughter.
  • Stanza 3 ⭐⭐: “Now she’s been dead nearly as many years / As that girl lived” - so nearly twelve years, going by the girl’s age in the photo.
  • Final line ⭐⭐⭐: “Its silence silences.” - there is nothing to say about this circumstance.

3️⃣ Key Lines Explained ⭐⭐

  • ‘The sea… changed less’ ⭐⭐: the sea changed less; the girls’ feet are transient (temporary).
  • ‘Laboured ease of loss’ ⭐⭐⭐: wry and an ease that takes effort - mother and speaker both live with loss.
  • ‘Silence silences’ ⭐⭐⭐: nothing is left to say.

4️⃣ Poetic Beauty ⭐⭐

  • Theme ⭐⭐: time and loss - a photo holds a day, then laughter, then death.
  • Wording ⭐⭐: ‘laboured ease’, ‘silence silences’ are the poem’s own word choices. NCERT does not label them, but teachers commonly read ‘laboured ease’ as an oxymoron-style pairing and ‘silence silences’ as word play with repetition (teaching reading, not NCERT text). Quote the phrase first, then explain it in your own words.
  • Tone ⭐: wry, quiet; ends in silence.
  • Exam ⭐⭐: quote the line from the text, then give your own explanation.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 A Photograph kiski kavita hai aur speaker kiski photo dekhti hai?
Answer dekho

Shirley Toulson; speaker apni maa ki purani photo (“the cardboard”) dekhti hai.

Q2 Photo mein kaun hain aur maa kitni badi thi?
Answer dekho

Do girl cousins paddling karti hain, har ek ne maa ka ek haath pakda hai; maa “the big girl — some twelve years or so”. Photo uncle with the camera ke liye hai.

Q3 Teen stanza kaunse teen phases dikhate hain?
Answer dekho

(1) photo ka din; (2) twenty-thirty years later maa ka snapshot par laugh; (3) Now — maa ki maut ke baad.

Q4 ‘The sea… changed less’ — text mein kya contrast hai?
Answer dekho

Sea changed less, jabki girls ke terribly transient feet hain (transient = temporary).

Q5 Maa snapshot par kya kehti thi?
Answer dekho

“See Betty and Dolly… and look how they dressed us for the beach.”

Q6 ‘Both wry with the laboured ease of loss’ ka matlab?
Answer dekho

NCERT Q5. Wry = rueful; laboured ease = mehnat se aayi sahajta. Maa aur speaker dono loss ke saath jeete hain — apne shabdon mein likho.

Q7 ‘Its silence silences’ ka kya matlab hai?
Answer dekho

“There is nothing to say at all. Its silence silences.” — is circumstance par kehne ko kuch nahi bacha.

Q8 ‘This circumstance’ kis ke liye hai?
Answer dekho

NCERT Q6. Pichhli lines: “Now she’s been dead nearly as many years / As that girl lived.”

Q9 Maut ko kitne saal hue (text ke mutabik)?
Answer dekho

Nearly as many years as that girl lived — photo ki ladki lagbhag 12 thi, to lagbhag utne saal.

Q10 ‘Paddling’ aur ‘transient’ ka NCERT kya karne ko kehta hai?
Answer dekho

Context se meaning infer karo, phir dictionary se check karo (paddling = pani mein halke se chalna; transient = temporary).
