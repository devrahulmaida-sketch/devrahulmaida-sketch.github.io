Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

PhotoThe cardboard shows how it was: two cousins paddling, the big girl about twelve, the uncle with the camera.
SeaThe sea appears to have changed less; the girls’ feet are terribly transient.
LaughTwenty-thirty years later she laughs: “See Betty and Dolly”, “look how they dressed us”.
WryHer past is the sea holiday, mine is her laughter; both wry with the laboured ease of loss.
NowDead nearly as many years as that girl lived; nothing to say about this circumstance.
SilenceThe last line: Its silence silences. Quote, then explain in your own words.
