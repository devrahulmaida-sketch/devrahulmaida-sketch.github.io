RangaBack from Bangalore after six months; the old lady says the janewara is still there.
MarriageRanga is not marrying now; he wants a mature girl he admires and criticises arranged marriages.
RatnaRama Rao’s niece, eleven, sings in Shyama’s room and runs inside shyly.
LieShyama says she was married a year ago; Ranga’s face shrivels like a roasted brinjal.
ShastriTutored by Shyama: pearl or ratna, definitely. Ending: Shyama is three; he puts a ring on the boy’s finger.
