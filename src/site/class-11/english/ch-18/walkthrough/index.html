Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

RoomLondon suburb, early autumn; Mrs Pearson is pleasant but worried; Mrs Fitzgerald says put your foot down.
SwapMrs Fitzgerald proposes to change places (bodies); after the spell Mrs Pearson turns bold.
DorisSpoilt; wants her yellow silk ironed; cries when Charlie is insulted.
CyrilWants tea and his things out; refused: “I don’t like mending”; the Union and a forty-hour week.
GeorgePompous; hears he is Pompy-ompy Pearson at the club; “feelings hurt” line.
BackThey change back; “keep firm”; rummy and supper; the family gathers round Mother.
