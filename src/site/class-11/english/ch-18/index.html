Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Writer aur Play Parichay ⭐⭐

  • J.B. Priestley ⭐⭐: NCERT text mein sirf naam hai; intro ise “a humorous portrayal of the status of the mother in a family” kehta hai aur book ka Q1 kehta hai ki play 1950s mein likha gaya. Dates aur dusre naatak text mein nahi hain (teaching background).
  • Swaroop ⭐⭐: humorous aur satirical play (book Q1); ek hi living-room mein ek ghatna — parde ek baar uthta aur girta hai (supported reading).
  • Characters ⭐⭐⭐: Mrs Annie Pearson, George Pearson (pati), Doris, Cyril aur padosan Mrs Fitzgerald. Setting: London ke suburb mein Pearsons ka living-room.
  • Kendriya vishay ⭐⭐⭐: kya maa ko ghar mein izzat milti hai? Apne liye khade hone ka mahatva.

💡 Priestley + Mrs Pearson (maa) + Mrs Fitzgerald (padosan) + Doris + Cyril + George + personality swap.

2️⃣ Play Summary ⭐⭐⭐

  • Mrs Pearson ki halat ⭐⭐⭐: woh seedhi aur dabbu hai; bachche aur pati uska hukm nahi maante, uspar hukm chalate hain.
  • Mrs Fitzgerald ki salah ⭐⭐⭐: woh kehti hain “Put your foot down… be the mistress of your own house”; Mrs Pearson kehti hai ki use unpleasantness se nafrat hai aur start karna mushkil hai.
  • Ajeeb prayog ⭐⭐⭐: Mrs Fitzgerald cards se fortune batati hain aur kehti hain “We change places. Or — really — bodies.” Text: “You look like me. I look like you.” Spell (“Arshtatta dum…”) ke baad Mrs Pearson Fitzgerald jaisi bold behave karti hai aur Fitzgerald nervous.
  • Badla hua vyavhar ⭐⭐⭐: jab bachche aur George aate hain, 'Mrs Pearson' tej aur dridh hoti hai — woh bahar jaane ka elaan karti hai aur khana bhi nahi banati; sab hairan aur pareshan.
  • Bachchon ki pratikriya ⭐⭐⭐: Doris gusse mein aur phir rone lagti hai, Cyril naraaz hota hai; baad mein dono aapas mein hanste hain (“If she’s going to be like this when Dad comes home…”). Phir bhi ‘nayi maa’ jhukti nahi.
  • Ant ⭐⭐⭐: Mrs Fitzgerald kehti hain ki sab miserable hain aur change back karna hai; jaate waqt “keep firm” ki salah dekar jaati hain. Mrs Pearson rummy aur supper ka plan batati hai aur ghar wale maan jaate hain.

🎯 Sequence: Mrs Pearson ka dabna → Fitzgerald ki salah → personality swap → badla hua vyavhar → bachchon ki hairani → Pearson ka aatm-vishwaas.

3️⃣ Characters ⭐⭐⭐

  • Mrs Pearson ⭐⭐⭐: text: “pleasant but worried-looking”; shuru mein apologetic; swap ke baad bold aur dominating.
  • Mrs Fitzgerald ⭐⭐⭐: tez, dridh, practical; dost aur margdarshak.
  • Doris aur Cyril ⭐⭐: Doris “spoilt” (stage direction) aur Cyril uska masculine counterpart; Mrs Fitzgerald ke shabdon mein ghar wale maa ko “servant” ki tarah treat karte hain.
  • George ⭐⭐: “solemn, self-important, pompous” (stage direction); club mein use Pompy-ompy Pearson kehte hain.

💡 Pearson = narm + dabbu → dridh | Fitzgerald = practical guide | bachche/pati = swarthi.

4️⃣ Themes ⭐⭐⭐

  • Maa ki izzat ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): book Q1: play ka muddha ghar mein maa ka darja hai; Mrs Fitzgerald ke shabd: “It’s the ruin of them as well as you.”
  • Apne liye khade hona ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): Mrs Fitzgerald ki salah aur swap ke baad ghar ka badalna; book Q1(ii) poochta hai ki aap resolution se sehmat hain ya nahi.
  • Haasya aur vyangya ⭐⭐: comedy ke zariye gambhir baat.
  • Samajik prashn ⭐⭐ (supported reading): book Q2: aaj ke incidents aur examples sochkar compare karo; Q3: kya drama social message ka achha madhyam hai?

🎯 Theme: maa ka sammaan + aatm-vishwas + comedy ke zariye vyangya.

१. लेखक और नाटक

  • प्रीस्टले ⭐⭐: NCERT text में केवल नाम; परिचय के अनुसार यह माँ की स्थिति का हास्यपूर्ण चित्रण है।
  • स्वरूप ⭐⭐: हास्य-नाटक।
  • विषय ⭐⭐⭐: माँ का सम्मान।

२. सारांश

  • श्रीमती पियर्सन ⭐⭐⭐: दबी हुई माँ।
  • अदला-बदली ⭐⭐⭐: व्यक्तित्व की अदला-बदली।
  • अंत ⭐⭐⭐: वापस अदला-बदली; फ़िट्ज़गेराल्ड की “keep firm” सलाह; परिवार रम्मी खेलने को राज़ी।

३. पात्र

  • पियर्सन ⭐⭐⭐: नरम, दबी हुई।
  • फ़िट्ज़गेराल्ड ⭐⭐⭐: तेज़, व्यावहारिक।
  • बच्चे ⭐⭐: डोरिस “spoilt”, साइरिल उसका पुरुष-समकक्ष।

४. विषय

  • सम्मान ⭐⭐⭐: माँ को सम्मान।
  • आत्मविश्वास ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): अपने लिए खड़े होना — फ़िट्ज़गेराल्ड की सलाह से।
  • हास्य ⭐⭐: मज़ाक के ज़रिए गंभीर बात।

1. Writer and Play

  • Priestley ⭐⭐: the text gives only the name; the intro calls the play a humorous portrayal of the mother’s status (written in the 1950s, book Q1).
  • Form ⭐⭐: a humorous, satirical play set in one living-room.
  • Theme ⭐⭐⭐: respect for the mother.

2. Play Summary

  • Mrs Pearson ⭐⭐⭐: the subdued mother.
  • Swap ⭐⭐⭐: personalities exchanged.
  • End ⭐⭐⭐: they change back; Mrs Fitzgerald says “keep firm”; the family agrees to a game of rummy.

3. Characters

  • Pearson ⭐⭐⭐: gentle, subdued.
  • Fitzgerald ⭐⭐⭐: sharp, practical.
  • Children ⭐⭐: Doris is “spoilt”; Cyril is her masculine counterpart.

4. Themes

  • Respect ⭐⭐⭐: mothers deserve respect.
  • Self-confidence ⭐⭐⭐ (supported reading): standing up for yourself, from Mrs Fitzgerald’s advice.
  • Comedy ⭐⭐: a serious point through humour.

Board Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Who is the writer of Mother's Day?
Answer dekho

J.B. Priestley — the NCERT text gives the name; the intro calls the play a humorous portrayal of the mother’s status, and the book says it was written in the 1950s.

Q2 What kind of person is Mrs Pearson at the start?
Answer dekho

She is “pleasant but worried-looking” and apologetic; her family expects her to do everything (Doris wants her yellow silk ironed; Cyril his things out).

Q3 Who is Mrs Fitzgerald?
Answer dekho

Mrs Pearson's forceful neighbour who advises her to assert herself.

Q4 What does Mrs Fitzgerald suggest?
Answer dekho

Mrs Fitzgerald proposes “We change places. Or — really — bodies.”; “You look like me. I look like you.” After the spell Mrs Pearson acts bold and Mrs Fitzgerald nervous.

Q5 How does 'Mrs Pearson' behave after the swap?
Answer dekho

She becomes bold and dominating, smokes, does not get tea ready and says she might get a meal at the Clarendon.

Q6 How do Doris and Cyril react?
Answer dekho

Doris is angry and then in tears; Cyril is annoyed. Later they giggle together, but the ‘new’ mother does not give in.

Q7 What does the play say about mothers?
Answer dekho

Supported reading: the family takes her for granted; Mrs Fitzgerald says “It’s the ruin of them as well as you.”

Q8 What makes the play humorous?
Answer dekho

The personality swap and the family's surprised reactions.

Q9 What is the role of Mrs Fitzgerald in the play?
Answer dekho

Supported reading: she urges Mrs Pearson to put her foot down, performs the swap, then restores it and says “keep firm”.

Q10 What is the message of Mother's Day?
Answer dekho

The text states no moral. Supported reading: Mrs Fitzgerald’s “Put your foot down” and the family’s agreeing to rummy show the issue and its resolution; the book asks whether you agree with that resolution (Q1).
