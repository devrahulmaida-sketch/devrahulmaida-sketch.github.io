Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

OccurrenceVeins and lodes, layers, surface decomposition (bauxite), placer deposits, ocean.
FerrousMagnetite up to 70 per cent iron, hematite 50 to 60; 97 per cent from four states in 2018-19.
Non-ferrousCopper deficient; bauxite Odisha 65 per cent; mica Koderma; limestone for cement.
ConservationOne per cent workable; finite; recycle, substitute, low grade ore technology.
Conventional energyCoal (Gondwana, Tertiary), petroleum, natural gas, hydro and thermal electricity.
RenewableNuclear, solar, wind, biogas, tidal, geothermal; conserve and use renewables.
