Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

BiodiversityWildlife and cultivated species in a network of interdependencies; forests are primary producers.
ConservationPreserves ecological and genetic diversity and life support systems: water, air, soil.
Wildlife Act 1972Hunting ban, habitat protection, trade restriction; Project Tiger 1973.
Forest categoriesReserved: more than half. Protected: almost one-third. Unclassed: other forests and wastelands.
CommunitySariska, Sonchuri (1,200 ha), Chipko, Beej Bachao, Navdanya.
JFM and grovesJFM since 1988 (Odisha); sacred groves; Bishnoi villages.
