Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

ImportanceBackbone of development: jobs, less poverty, exports and foreign exchange.
ClassificationBy source, role, ownership and bulk; small scale limit one crore as given in the chapter.
Textiles and sugarCotton mill Mumbai 1854; jute mill Rishra 1855; sugar is seasonal and cooperative.
Iron and steelOre, coal, limestone about 4 : 2 : 1; Chhotanagpur; aluminium needs power and bauxite.
Chemicals and cementPotash fully imported; cement from limestone, silica, gypsum; first plant Chennai 1904.
PollutionAir, water, land, noise; three phases of effluent treatment; NTPC ISO 14001.
