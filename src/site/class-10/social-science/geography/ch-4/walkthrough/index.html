Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

Farming typesPrimitive subsistence, intensive subsistence, commercial, plantation.
SeasonsRabi (sow Oct-Dec), kharif (monsoon), zaid (short summer).
CerealsRice above 25 C and 100 cm; wheat 50 to 75 cm; millets; maize 21 to 27 C.
Other food cropsPulses fix nitrogen; sugarcane; oilseeds; tea; coffee; horticulture.
Non-food cropsRubber above 200 cm; cotton with 210 frost-free days; jute, the golden fibre.
ReformsLand reform, Green and White Revolution, KCC, PAIS, MSP, Bhoodan-Gramdan.
