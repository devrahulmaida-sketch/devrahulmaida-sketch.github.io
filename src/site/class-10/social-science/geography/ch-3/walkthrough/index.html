Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

ScarcityRenewable via the hydrological cycle; scarcity from over-exploitation, unequal access and pollution.
CausesPopulation, irrigation (largest consumer), industry, urbanisation, falling groundwater.
Multi-purpose projectsIrrigation, power, flood control, navigation; Bhakra-Nangal, Hirakud.
DisadvantagesSedimentation, fragmented rivers, floods, salinisation, inter-state disputes.
Traditional harvestingGuls and kuls, khadins, johads, tankas with palar pani.
Modern adaptationsGendathur, Tamil Nadu compulsory, Shillong, bamboo drip.
