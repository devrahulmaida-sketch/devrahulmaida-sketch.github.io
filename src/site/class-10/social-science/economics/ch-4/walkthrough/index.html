Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

MNCOwns or controls production in more than one country; invests where cost is low.
RoutesJoint production, buying local companies, orders to small producers.
Foreign tradeMore choice, prices move closer, producers compete: integration of markets.
GlobalisationRapid integration of countries through foreign trade and foreign investment.
FactorsTechnology (containers, IT), liberalisation, WTO pressure.
ImpactNot uniform; fair globalisation means opportunities and benefits for all.
