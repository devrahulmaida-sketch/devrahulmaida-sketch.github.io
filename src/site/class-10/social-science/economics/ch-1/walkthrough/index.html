Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

GoalsDifferent people, different and sometimes conflicting goals; income plus freedom, security, respect.
AveragesPer capita income = total income / population; Countries A and B both average 10000 but differ in spread.
StatesIncome Haryana 2,36,147, Kerala 2,04,105, Bihar 40,982; IMR 30, 7, 32 (as stated).
HDIUNDP compares education, health and income; India rank 132, Sri Lanka 73 (HDR 2021-22).
GroundwaterAbout 300 districts with over 4 metre decline; renewable but can be overused.
Crude oilNon-renewable; world reserves 50.2 years at the 2017 rate per Table 1.7.
