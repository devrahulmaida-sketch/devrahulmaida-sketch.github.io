Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

SectorsPrimary: natural goods; secondary: manufacturing; tertiary: services that help the others.
GDPOnly final goods and services count; GDP is their total value in a year.
IndiaAs stated in the chapter: tertiary largest in GVA in 2017-18, primary still largest employer.
UnemploymentDisguised unemployment: people work below capacity and are not visibly jobless.
OrganisedRegular and protected work; the unorganised sector has low pay and no security.
Public, privatePublic: government owns; private: profit motive.
