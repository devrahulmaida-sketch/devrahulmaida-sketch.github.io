1. Right triangle mein sides ke naam

Angle A chuno. Jo side A ke saamne hai woh opposite, jo A ke paas (hypotenuse ke alawa) hai woh adjacent, aur sabse lambi side hypotenuse hai. Angle C chuno to opposite aur adjacent badal jaate hain. Hamara example: A ke liye BC opposite, AB adjacent, AC hypotenuse.

Hamara apna diagram; book ke Fig. 8.4/8.5 copy nahin kiye. Schematic, scale nahin.

Source basis: NCERT jemh108 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution.

2. Chhe trigonometric ratios

sin A = opposite/hypotenuse = BC/AC. cos A = adjacent/hypotenuse = AB/AC. tan A = opposite/adjacent = BC/AB. cosec A = 1/sin A = AC/BC. sec A = 1/cos A = AC/AB. cot A = 1/tan A = AB/BC. Aur tan A = sin A / cos A, cot A = cos A / sin A. Hamara example: 3-4-5 triangle mein A ke saamne 3, paas 4, hypotenuse 5 ho to sin A = 3/5, cos A = 4/5, tan A = 3/4.

Source basis: NCERT jemh108 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution.

3. Triangle bada ya chhota, ratio wahi

Agar angle A wahi rahe to sin A, cos A waghera ki value triangle ki size se nahin badalti. Wajah: bade aur chhote right triangles AA se similar hote hain, isliye sides ke ratio barabar hote hain (book ka Fig. 8.6 ka argument). Hamara example: 3-4-5 ko double karke 6-8-10 karo, sin A phir bhi 3/5.

Source basis: NCERT jemh108 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution.

4. Notation pe dhyan

sin A ka matlab 'angle A ka sine' hai; yeh 'sin' aur A ka product nahin hai. sin²A ka matlab (sin A)² hai. cosec A = (sin A)⁻¹ hai, lekin sin⁻¹A (sine inverse) alag cheez hai jo upar ki classes mein padhoge (book ka note). Greek letter θ (theta) bhi angle ke liye use hota hai.

Source basis: NCERT jemh108 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution.

5. Ek ratio se baaki sab

Maan lo tan A = 4/3. Opposite = 4k, adjacent = 3k lo (k positive). Pythagoras: hypotenuse² = 16k² + 9k² = 25k², hypotenuse = 5k. Ab sin A = 4/5, cos A = 3/5, cot A = 3/4, cosec A = 5/4, sec A = 5/3. Yeh Example 1 ka tareeka hai; k cut ho jaata hai.

Source basis: NCERT jemh108 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution.

6. 45° ke ratios

45° wale right triangle mein dono acute angle 45° hote hain, isliye BC = AB = a (barabar angle ke saamne ki sides barabar). Pythagoras: AC² = 2a², AC = a√2. sin 45° = a/(a√2) = 1/√2, cos 45° = 1/√2, tan 45° = 1. cosec 45° = √2, sec 45° = √2, cot 45° = 1.

7. 30° aur 60° ke ratios

Equilateral triangle ABC lo (har angle 60°). A se BC par perpendicular AD kheencho. △ABD ≅ △ACD, isliye BD = DC aur ∠BAD = 30°. Ab △ABD mein ∠D = 90°, ∠A = 30°, ∠B = 60°. AB = 2a lo to BD = a aur AD = √(4a² − a²) = a√3. sin 30° = a/(2a) = 1/2, cos 30° = √3/2, tan 30° = 1/√3. sin 60° = √3/2, cos 60° = 1/2, tan 60° = √3. cosec 30° = 2, sec 30° = 2/√3, cot 30° = √3; cosec 60° = 2/√3, sec 60° = 2, cot 60° = 1/√3.

8. 0° aur 90° ke ratios (definition)

1. समकोण त्रिभुज की भुजाओं के नाम

कोण A चुनो। जो भुजा A के सामने है वह सम्मुख (opposite), जो A के पास है (कर्ण को छोड़कर) वह संलग्न (adjacent), और सबसे लंबी भुजा कर्ण (hypotenuse) है। कोण C चुनो तो सम्मुख और संलग्न बदल जाते हैं। हमारा उदाहरण: A के लिए BC सम्मुख, AB संलग्न, AC कर्ण।

हमारा अपना चित्र; पुस्तक के चित्र 8.4/8.5 दोबारा नहीं छापे। योजनात्मक, पैमाना नहीं।

2. छह त्रिकोणमितीय अनुपात

sin A = सम्मुख/कर्ण = BC/AC। cos A = संलग्न/कर्ण = AB/AC। tan A = सम्मुख/संलग्न = BC/AB। cosec A = 1/sin A = AC/BC। sec A = 1/cos A = AC/AB। cot A = 1/tan A = AB/BC। साथ ही tan A = sin A / cos A, cot A = cos A / sin A। हमारा उदाहरण: A के सामने 3, पास 4, कर्ण 5 हो तो sin A = 3/5, cos A = 4/5, tan A = 3/4।

3. त्रिभुज बड़ा या छोटा, अनुपात वही

यदि कोण A वही रहे तो sin A, cos A आदि का मान त्रिभुज के आकार से नहीं बदलता। कारण: बड़े और छोटे समकोण त्रिभुज AA से समरूप होते हैं, इसलिए भुजाओं के अनुपात बराबर होते हैं (पुस्तक के चित्र 8.6 का तर्क)। हमारा उदाहरण: 3-4-5 को दुगुना करके 6-8-10 करो, sin A फिर भी 3/5।

स्रोत आधार: NCERT jemh108 Reprint 2026-27; हमारा हल-सहायक, आधिकारिक हल नहीं।

4. संकेत-चिह्न पर ध्यान

sin A का अर्थ 'कोण A की ज्या (sine)' है; यह 'sin' और A का गुणनफल नहीं है। sin²A का अर्थ (sin A)² है। cosec A = (sin A)⁻¹ है, पर sin⁻¹A (sine inverse) अलग चीज़ है जो ऊपर की कक्षाओं में पढ़ोगे (पुस्तक का नोट)। यूनानी अक्षर θ (थीटा) भी कोण के लिए प्रयोग होता है।

6. 45° के अनुपात

45° वाले समकोण त्रिभुज में दोनों न्यून कोण 45° होते हैं, इसलिए BC = AB = a (बराबर कोणों के सामने की भुजाएँ बराबर)। पाइथागोरस: AC² = 2a², AC = a√2। sin 45° = a/(a√2) = 1/√2, cos 45° = 1/√2, tan 45° = 1। cosec 45° = √2, sec 45° = √2, cot 45° = 1।

7. 30° और 60° के अनुपात

समबाहु त्रिभुज ABC लो (हर कोण 60°)। A से BC पर लंब AD खींचो। △ABD ≅ △ACD, इसलिए BD = DC और ∠BAD = 30°। अब △ABD में ∠D = 90°, ∠A = 30°, ∠B = 60°। AB = 2a लो तो BD = a और AD = √(4a² − a²) = a√3। sin 30° = a/(2a) = 1/2, cos 30° = √3/2, tan 30° = 1/√3। sin 60° = √3/2, cos 60° = 1/2, tan 60° = √3। cosec 30° = 2, sec 30° = 2/√3, cot 30° = √3; cosec 60° = 2/√3, sec 60° = 2, cot 60° = 1/√3।

8. 0° और 90° के अनुपात (परिभाषा)

A को छोटा करते जाओ: BC घटता है, AC लगभग AB के बराबर हो जाता है। इसलिए sin A ≈ 0 और cos A ≈ 1। पुस्तक परिभाषित करती है: sin 0° = 0, cos 0° = 1। फिर tan 0° = 0, sec 0° = 1; cot 0° और cosec 0° परिभाषित नहीं (1/0 होता)। उल्टे, A को 90° के पास ले जाओ तो sin A ≈ 1, cos A ≈ 0। पुस्तक परिभाषित करती है: sin 90° = 1, cos 90° = 0। तालिका 8.1 में 'Not defined' वहाँ है जहाँ 0 से भाग होता है।

1. Naming the sides of a right triangle

Pick angle A. The side facing A is opposite, the side touching A (other than the hypotenuse) is adjacent, and the longest side is the hypotenuse. Pick angle C instead and opposite and adjacent swap. Our example: for A, BC is opposite, AB is adjacent, AC is the hypotenuse.

Our own diagram; the book's Figs 8.4/8.5 are not reproduced. Schematic, not to scale.

Source basis: NCERT jemh108 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution.

2. The six trigonometric ratios

sin A = opposite/hypotenuse = BC/AC. cos A = adjacent/hypotenuse = AB/AC. tan A = opposite/adjacent = BC/AB. cosec A = 1/sin A = AC/BC. sec A = 1/cos A = AC/AB. cot A = 1/tan A = AB/BC. Also tan A = sin A / cos A and cot A = cos A / sin A. Our example: if the side facing A is 3, the side touching it is 4 and the hypotenuse is 5, then sin A = 3/5, cos A = 4/5, tan A = 3/4.

Source basis: NCERT jemh108 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution.

3. Bigger or smaller triangle, same ratio

If angle A stays the same, the values of sin A, cos A and the rest do not change with the size of the triangle. Reason: bigger and smaller right triangles with the same angle are similar by AA, so side ratios are equal (the argument around the book's Fig. 8.6). Our example: double 3-4-5 to 6-8-10 and sin A is still 3/5.

Source basis: NCERT jemh108 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution.

4. Notation to watch

sin A means 'the sine of angle A'; it is not 'sin' times A. sin²A means (sin A)². cosec A = (sin A)⁻¹, but sin⁻¹A (sine inverse) is something different that you meet in higher classes (the book's note). The Greek letter θ (theta) is also used for an angle.

Source basis: NCERT jemh108 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution.

5. From one ratio to all the others

Say tan A = 4/3. Take opposite = 4k, adjacent = 3k (k positive). Pythagoras: hypotenuse² = 16k² + 9k² = 25k², hypotenuse = 5k. Now sin A = 4/5, cos A = 3/5, cot A = 3/4, cosec A = 5/4, sec A = 5/3. This is the method of Example 1; k cancels.

Source basis: NCERT jemh108 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution.

6. Ratios of 45°

In a right triangle with a 45° angle, both acute angles are 45°, so BC = AB = a (sides opposite equal angles are equal). Pythagoras: AC² = 2a², AC = a√2. sin 45° = a/(a√2) = 1/√2, cos 45° = 1/√2, tan 45° = 1. cosec 45° = √2, sec 45° = √2, cot 45° = 1.

Our own diagram; the book's Fig. 8.14 is not reproduced. Schematic.

7. Ratios of 30° and 60°

Take an equilateral triangle ABC (every angle 60°). Draw the perpendicular AD from A to BC. △ABD ≅ △ACD, so BD = DC and ∠BAD = 30°. Now △ABD has ∠D = 90°, ∠A = 30°, ∠B = 60°. Take AB = 2a, so BD = a and AD = √(4a² − a²) = a√3. sin 30° = a/(2a) = 1/2, cos 30° = √3/2, tan 30° = 1/√3. sin 60° = √3/2, cos 60° = 1/2, tan 60° = √3. cosec 30° = 2, sec 30° = 2/√3, cot 30° = √3; cosec 60° = 2/√3, sec 60° = 2, cot 60° = 1/√3.

Source basis: NCERT jemh108 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution.

8. Ratios of 0° and 90° (by definition)

Make A smaller and smaller: BC shrinks and AC gets almost equal to AB. So sin A is close to 0 and cos A is close to 1. The book defines sin 0° = 0 and cos 0° = 1. Then tan 0° = 0 and sec 0° = 1; cot 0° and cosec 0° are not defined (they would be 1/0). In the other direction, as A nears 90°, sin A is close to 1 and cos A is close to 0, and the book defines sin 90° = 1, cos 90° = 0. In Table 8.1, 'Not defined' marks the places where we would divide by 0.

Source basis: NCERT jemh108 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution.

Example 2: sin B = sin Q ho to ∠B = ∠Q

Ratio barabar rakho, similar triangles ya identity se result nikalo.

Steps:

Right triangles ABC (right angle at C) and PQR (right angle at R): sin B = AC/AB, sin Q = PR/PQ AC/AB = PR/PQ = k (say), so AC/PR = AB/PQ = k BC = √(AB² − AC²) = √(k²PQ² − k²PR²) = k√(PQ² − PR²) = k · QR, so BC/QR = k So AC/PR = AB/PQ = BC/QR By the SSS similarity criterion (Theorem 6.4 in the book), △ACB ~ △PRQ, so ∠B = ∠Q

Answer: ∠B = ∠Q

Check (ours): this is the idea behind Exercise 8.1 Q6 for cosine.
