4. Finding the height of the minar (the book's Fig. 9.1)

The book says that to find the height CD without measuring it you need three things: (i) the distance DE of the student from the foot of the minar, (ii) the angle of elevation ∠BAC of the top, (iii) the height AE of the student. Then CD = CB + BD, and BD = AE. In right △ABC, BC is the side opposite the known ∠A, so tan A = BC/AB (or cot A = AB/BC) gives BC. Add AE to BC to get the height of the minar.

Source basis: NCERT jemh109 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution. Printed pages: p134-135.

5. Our 4-step method (ours, not the book's)

(1) Draw a small diagram and put in the given numbers. (2) Find the right triangle in it; if there is more than one, take them one at a time. (3) Pick the ratio: if the known and wanted sides are height and base, use tan; height and hypotenuse, use sin; base and hypotenuse, use cos. (4) Take the values for 30°, 45° and 60° from Table 8.1: tan 30° = 1/√3, tan 45° = 1, tan 60° = √3, sin 30° = 1/2, sin 60° = √3/2. Then solve the equation.

Source basis: NCERT jemh109 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution. Printed pages: p135-141.

Example 1: 15 m door se 60° elevation, tower ki height

Hamara apna diagram; book ka Fig. 9.4 copy nahin kiya. Schematic.

Problem: Ek tower zameen par seedha khada hai. Tower ke foot se 15 m door zameen ke ek point se tower ki top ka angle of elevation 60° hai. Tower ki height nikalo.

Pehle chhota diagram banao, usme right triangle dhundho, phir wo ratio chuno jo di hui aur chahiye wali side ko jodta ho.

Steps:

Right △ABC, right angle at B: AB = tower, CB = 15 m, ∠ACB = 60° tan 60° = AB/BC √3 = AB/15 AB = 15√3 m

Answer: 15√3 m

Hamari jaanch: 15 × 1.732 ≈ 25.98 m, and 25.98/15 ≈ 1.732 = tan 60°.

Source basis: NCERT jemh109 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution. Chhapa prishth: p135.

Example 2: pole par 1.3 m niche tak ladder

Hamara apna diagram; book ka Fig. 9.5 copy nahin kiya. Schematic.

Problem: Electrician ko 5 m ooncha pole par top se 1.3 m niche ek point tak pahunchna hai. Ladder zameen se 60° par jhuki hai. Ladder kitni lambi ho, aur uska foot pole se kitni door rakhe? (√3 = 1.73 lo.)

Pehle chhota diagram banao, usme right triangle dhundho, phir wo ratio chuno jo di hui aur chahiye wali side ko jodta ho.

Steps:

BD = AD − AB = 5 − 1.3 = 3.7 m Ladder BC = hypotenuse of right △BDC, ∠C = 60° sin 60° = BD/BC, so √3/2 = 3.7/BC BC = 7.4/√3 ≈ 4.28 m (taking √3 = 1.73) cot 60° = DC/BD, so 1/√3 = DC/3.7 DC = 3.7/√3 ≈ 2.14 m

Answer: Ladder about 4.28 m; its foot about 2.14 m from the pole

Hamari jaanch: 2.14² + 3.7² ≈ 18.27 and 4.28² ≈ 18.32, equal up to rounding.

Source basis: NCERT jemh109 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution. Chhapa prishth: p136.

Example 3: chimney ki height, 45° elevation

Problem: 1.5 m lambi observer chimney se 28.5 m door hai. Uski aankhon se chimney ki top ka angle of elevation 45° hai. Chimney ki height kya hai?

Pehle chhota diagram banao, usme right triangle dhundho, phir wo ratio chuno jo di hui aur chahiye wali side ko jodta ho.

Steps:

AB = chimney, CD = observer, ∠ADE = 45°; right angle of △ADE at E DE = CB = 28.5 m and BE = CD = 1.5 m tan 45° = AE/DE, so 1 = AE/28.5, AE = 28.5 m AB = AE + BE = 28.5 + 1.5 = 30 m

Answer: 30 m

Hamari jaanch: the chimney is 28.5 m above eye level, the same as the distance, as tan 45° = 1 needs.

Source basis: NCERT jemh109 Reprint 2026-27; our worked aid, not an official solution. Chhapa prishth: p137.

Example 4: building par jhanda, 30° aur 45°

Hamara apna diagram; book ka Fig. 9.7 copy nahin kiya. Schematic.

Problem: Zameen ke ek point P se 10 m ooncha building ki top ka angle of elevation 30° hai. Building ki top par jhanda lagaya gaya, aur P se jhande ke danda ki top ka angle of elevation 45° hai. Danda ki length aur building ki P se doori nikalo. (√3 = 1.732 lo.)

Pehle chhota diagram banao, usme right triangle dhundho, phir wo ratio chuno jo di hui aur chahiye wali side ko jodta ho.

Steps:

AB = building (10 m), BD = flagstaff, PA = distance; right triangles PAB and PAD Right △PAB: tan 30° = AB/AP, so 1/√3 = 10/AP AP = 10√3 = 17.32 m (taking √3 = 1.732) Flagstaff BD = x m, so AD = (10 + x) m Right △PAD: tan 45° = AD/AP, so 1 = (10 + x)/(10√3) x = 10√3 − 10 = 10(√3 − 1) = 7.32 m

Answer: Flagstaff 7.32 m; the building is 17.32 m from P

Hamari jaanch: AD = 10 + 7.32 = 17.32 m = AP, as tan 45° = 1 needs.

Example 5: shadow 40 m lamba, Sun 30° aur 60°

Hamara apna diagram; book ka Fig. 9.8 copy nahin kiya. Schematic.

Problem: Level zameen par khade tower ki chhaya tab 40 m lambi hoti hai jab Sun ki altitude 30° ho, us samay se jab 60° ho. Tower ki height nikalo.

Pehle chhota diagram banao, usme right triangle dhundho, phir wo ratio chuno jo di hui aur chahiye wali side ko jodta ho.

Steps:

AB = tower = h m; BC = x m (shadow at 60°); DB = (40 + x) m (shadow at 30°) △ABC: tan 60° = h/x, so h = √3 x ...(1) △ABD: tan 30° = h/(40 + x), so h = (40 + x)/√3 ...(2) From (1) and (2): √3 x = (40 + x)/√3, so 3x = 40 + x x = 20, so h = 20√3 m from (1)

Example 6: do buildings, depression 30° aur 45°

Hamara apna diagram; book ka Fig. 9.9 copy nahin kiya. Schematic.

Problem: Ek bahumanzila building ki top se 8 m ooncha building ki top aur bottom ke angle of depression 30° aur 45° hain. Bahumanzila building ki height aur dono buildings ke beech ki doori nikalo.

Pehle chhota diagram banao, usme right triangle dhundho, phir wo ratio chuno jo di hui aur chahiye wali side ko jodta ho.

Steps:

PC = multi-storeyed building, AB = 8 m building; PQ and BD are horizontal Alternate angles: ∠PBD = 30° and ∠PAC = 45° Right △PBD: tan 30° = PD/BD, so BD = √3 PD Right △PAC: tan 45° = PC/AC = 1, so PC = AC AC = BD and DC = AB = 8 m, so PC = PD + 8 and AC = BD = √3 PD PD + 8 = √3 PD, so PD = 8/(√3 − 1) = 8(√3 + 1)/2 = 4(√3 + 1) m PC = PD + 8 = 4(√3 + 1) + 8 = 4(3 + √3) m, and AC = PC

Answer: Height 4(3 + √3) m; distance between the buildings also 4(3 + √3) m

Hamari jaanch: 4(3 + 1.732) ≈ 18.93 m; PD ≈ 10.93, BD = 1.732 × 10.93 ≈ 18.93 m and PC = 10.93 + 8 = 18.93 m.
