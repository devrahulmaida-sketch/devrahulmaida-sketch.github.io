Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

IndicatorsLitmus: acid blue ko red, base red ko blue; phenolphthalein NaOH mein pink.
ReactionsAcid + metal gives salt + H2; carbonate + acid gives salt + CO2 + H2O; CO2 lime water ko milky karti hai.
NeutraliseAcid + base gives salt + water; H+ + OH- gives H2O; metal oxides basic, non-metal oxides acidic.
pH7 neutral, 7 se kam acidic, 7 se zyada basic; body 7.0-7.8; acid rain 5.6 se kam.
SaltsStrong acid + strong base neutral; NaCl se NaOH, Cl2, H2, baking soda, washing soda.
CrystalCuSO4.5H2O blue; gypsum CaSO4.2H2O; Plaster of Paris CaSO4.half H2O, nami se bachao.
