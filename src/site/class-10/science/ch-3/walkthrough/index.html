Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

PhysicalMetals: lustre, malleable, ductile, conductors; exceptions: Hg liquid, graphite conducts, I2 lustrous.
ReactionsMetal + O2 gives basic oxide; Al2O3 aur ZnO amphoteric; Na, K kerosene mein; Fe steam se Fe3O4 + H2.
SeriesK Na Ca Mg Al Zn Fe Pb [H] Cu Hg Ag Au; zyada reactive metal displace karta hai.
IonicMetal se non-metal electron transfer; high MP/BP; molten aur aqueous mein conduct.
ExtractionRoasting, calcination, carbon reduction, thermit; top metals electrolysis; electrolytic refining.
RustAir + water dono; galvanisation zinc coating; alloy homogeneous mixture; 22 carat = 22 parts gold.
