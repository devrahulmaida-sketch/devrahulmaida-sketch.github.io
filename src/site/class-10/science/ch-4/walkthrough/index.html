Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

CovalentElectron sharing; H2 single, O2 double, N2 triple; low MP and BP, poor conductors.
CarbonCatenation + tetravalency; diamond, graphite, C-60; saturated aur unsaturated.
SeriesCnH2n+2, CnH2n, CnH2n-2; members differ by -CH2-; -ol, -al, -one, -oic acid.
ReactionsCombustion, oxidation (alcohol to acid), addition (H2, Ni), substitution (Cl2, sunlight).
Ethanol, AcidEthanol + Na gives H2; acid + carbonate gives CO2; acid + alcohol gives ester.
SoapMicelle cleans oily dirt; scum in hard water; detergent works in hard water.
