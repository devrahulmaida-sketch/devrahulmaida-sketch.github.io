Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

Life processesNutrition, respiration, transport, excretion: maintenance ke liye.
PhotosynthesisCO2 + water, sunlight, chlorophyll gives carbohydrates; stomata; starch test.
DigestionAmylase, HCl + pepsin, bile, trypsin, lipase; villi absorb.
RespirationGlucose to pyruvate; anaerobic: ethanol or lactic acid; aerobic in mitochondria; ATP.
TransportDouble circulation; artery, vein, capillary; xylem water, phloem food.
ExcretionNephron, selective reabsorption; plants: vacuoles, leaves, gums.
