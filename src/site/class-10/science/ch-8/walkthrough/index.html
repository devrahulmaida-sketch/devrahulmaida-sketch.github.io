Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

VariationAccumulates; sexual reproduction gives more diversity; environment selects.
MendelF1 all tall; F2 one quarter short; TT, Tt, tt in 1:2:1.
Dominant, recessiveOne T is enough for tall; both must be t for short.
Two traitsF2 ratio 9:3:3:1; new combinations; independent inheritance.
Genes, chromosomesGene makes a protein; chromosomes in pairs; germ-cell takes one from each pair.
SexWomen XX, men XY; mother gives X; father decides.
