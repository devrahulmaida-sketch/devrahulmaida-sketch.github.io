Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

DNA copyingCopy of DNA plus cellular apparatus; variation is the basis of evolution.
AsexualFission, fragmentation, regeneration, budding, vegetative propagation, spores.
SexualTwo individuals; meiosis halves chromosomes; fertilisation gives a zygote.
Flowering plantsPollination, pollen tube, fertilisation; ovule becomes seed, ovary becomes fruit.
Human systemsTestes, vas deferens, urethra; ovaries, oviduct, uterus.
HealthPlacenta, menstruation, contraception, sex ratio.
