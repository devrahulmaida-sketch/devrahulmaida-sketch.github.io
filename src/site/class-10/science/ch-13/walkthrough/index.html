Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

EcosystemBiotic plus abiotic; natural (forest, pond, lake) and human-made (garden, crop-field, aquarium).
OrganismsProducers make food; consumers eat; decomposers break down dead remains.
Food chainFour trophic levels; food web is branching; producers are most numerous.
EnergyAbout 1 percent captured; average 10 percent passes on; one-way flow; 3 or 4 steps.
MagnificationNon-degradable chemicals build up at each level; maximum in humans.
Ozone and wasteOzone shields from UV; CFCs, UNEP 1987; biodegradable or not.
