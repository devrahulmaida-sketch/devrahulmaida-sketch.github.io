Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

BalanceSkeletal equation ko balance karo: 2Mg + O2 gives 2MgO, atoms dono taraf barabar.
States(s), (l), (g), (aq) aur conditions equation ko informative banate hain.
CombineCaO + H2O gives Ca(OH)2 with heat; coal ka jalna aur respiration exothermic hain.
DecomposeHeat, electricity ya light se ek reactant tootkar do ya zyada products deta hai.
DisplaceFe + CuSO4 gives FeSO4 + Cu; Na2SO4 + BaCl2 gives white BaSO4 precipitate.
RedoxOxygen gain ya hydrogen loss = oxidation; reverse = reduction. Rust aur rancidity bhi oxidation hain.
