Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

Field linesOut of N, into S; closed curves; never cross; crowded means stronger.
Straight wireConcentric circles; right-hand thumb rule; weaker with distance.
SolenoidField like a bar magnet; uniform inside; soft iron core gives an electromagnet.
ForceLargest when current is perpendicular to field; Fleming left-hand rule.
DomesticLive red, neutral black, earth green; 220 V; appliances in parallel.
SafetyShort circuit and overloading; fuse melts; earth wire prevents shock.
