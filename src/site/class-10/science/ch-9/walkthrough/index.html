Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

Reflectioni = r; plane mirror image is virtual, erect, same size, laterally inverted.
MirrorsR = 2f; concave gives virtual erect enlarged image only between P and F.
Convex mirrorAlways virtual, erect, diminished; wider field of view.
Mirror formula1/v + 1/u = 1/f; m = -v/u.
RefractionRarer to denser: towards normal; n = c/v; slab ray emerges parallel.
Lens1/v - 1/u = 1/f; m = v/u; P = 1/f in dioptres.
