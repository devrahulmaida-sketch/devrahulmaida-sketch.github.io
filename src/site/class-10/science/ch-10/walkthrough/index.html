Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

EyeMost refraction at the cornea; inverted real image on the retina.
AccommodationRelaxed muscles: thin lens; contracted: thick lens; near point about 25 cm.
DefectsMyopia: concave lens; hypermetropia: convex lens; presbyopia: ageing.
DispersionWhite light splits into VIBGYOR; red bends least, violet most.
AtmosphereStars twinkle; planets do not; Sun seen about 2 minutes early.
ScatteringTyndall effect; blue scatters more, so the sky is blue; red signals.
