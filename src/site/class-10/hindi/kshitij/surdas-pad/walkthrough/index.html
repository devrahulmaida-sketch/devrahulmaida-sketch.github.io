Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

PoetSurdas, believed born 1478, died 1583; Sursagar is his most popular work (author page).
SourceFour pads from the Bhramargeet of Sursagar (NCERT box).
The padsThe irony, the examples, the haril and the rajdharm line are the gopis words in the poem; meanings beyond the page glossary are my reading.
GlossaryPurain paat is a lotus leaf; haril is a bird that holds its stick; aniti is injustice (page glossary).
Open tasksQ4, Q5, Q6, Q8, Q10 to Q15 and Patheytar are interpretation or opinion tasks and are not keyed.
