Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

AuthorSarveshwar Dayal Saxena, 1927 to 1983; Basti; Dinman, Parag; Sahitya Akademi (author page).
SubjectA memoir of Father Kamil Bulke, born in Belgium, India his field of work (NCERT box).
LifeEngineering left, the India condition, thesis on the Ramkatha in 1950, Ranchi, dictionary, Bible; all as the author reports.
FarewellFuneral at Kashmiri Gate on 18 August 1982, in the author's account; Hindi prayer.
