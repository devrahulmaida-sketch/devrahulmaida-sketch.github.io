Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

AuthorYashpal, 1903 to 1976; Firozpur Cantonment; Jhootha Sach.
The pieceA satire, as the NCERT box says, aimed at a declining feudal class.
The sceneA second class compartment, a nawab and two cucumbers; two offers refused (narrator's account).
The routineWashed, cut, peeled and salted, then smelled and thrown out the window; the nawab belched.
