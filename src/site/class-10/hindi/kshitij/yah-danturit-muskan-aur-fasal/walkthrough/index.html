Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

PoetNagarjun, 1911 to 1998; Satlakha, Darbhanga; real name Vaidyanath Mishra.
WorkYugdhara; Nagarjun Rachnavali in seven volumes; called the modern Kabir.
SmileYah danturit muskan: a child's smile, the mother as medium, the poet as outsider.
FasalA crop is water, hands, soil, sun rays and air together.
WordsDanturit new teeth; jaljaat lotus; animesh without blinking; kankhi sideways look.
