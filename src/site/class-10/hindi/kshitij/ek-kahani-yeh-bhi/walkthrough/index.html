Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

AuthorMannu Bhandari, born 1931, died 2021; Mahabhoj and Aapka Bunty are novels (author page).
SourceAn excerpt of her autobiographical writing; the box says it is not a chronological autobiography.
Her accountWhat she says of her father, mother, sister and teacher is her own account and analysis, not independent fact.
GlossaryNishiddh is that which is banned; ahamvadi is proud; bhagnavshesh is ruins (page glossary).
