Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Lekhak aur NCERT box ⭐⭐⭐

  • NCERT author page ke anusar Yatindra Mishra ka janm 1977 mein Ayodhya mein hua; unhone Lucknow vishwavidyalaya se M.A. kiya. Page ke anusar ve ardhvarshik patrika Sahit ka sampadan karte hain aur Vimla Devi Foundation (1999) chalate hain.
  • Page par unki pustakein: Yada-Kada, Ayodhya tatha anya kavitayein, Draudhi par Alap, Girija. Ye lekhak-parichay ki baatein hain.
  • NCERT box ke anusar ye ek vyakti-chitra hai Ustad Bismillah Khan ka. Box kehta hai ki sangeet aradhana hai, jiska apna shastra hai aur guru-shishya parampara hai; unki sadhana 80 varsh ki umr mein bhi jaari rahi.

💡 Lekhak 1977 Ayodhya; vyakti-chitra; sangeet = aradhana (box).

2️⃣ Dumraon, Kashi aur bachpan ⭐⭐⭐

  • Lekhak ke anusar Bismillah Khan ka janm Dumraon (Bihar) mein hua; wahan Son nadi ke kinaron par milne wali narkat ghaas se shehnai ki reed banti hai (Prashn 11 ka vakya bhi yahi kehta hai). Unke pardada Ustad Salar Hussain Khan the; pita Paigambar Baksh Khan, mata Mitthan (text).
  • Text ke anusar 1916 se 1922 ke beech ka samay Kashi ka hai: Balaji mandir ki draudhi aur Panchganga ghat. Bachpan ka naam Amiruddin (umra 6) aur bhai Shamsuddin (umra 9); mamu Sadiq Hussain aur Alibaksh (text).
  • Text ke anusar 14 varsh ke Amiruddin Rasulanbai aur Batulanbai ke ghar ke raste se Balaji mandir jate the; Khan sahab maante hain ki sangeet ka shauk inhi gayika behenon se laga.

💡 Dumraon se reed; Kashi ka Balaji mandir; Rasulanbai-Batulanbai (text).

3️⃣ Sur ki khoj aur Muharram ⭐⭐⭐

  • Text ke anusar shehnai sushir vadya hai (phoonk kar bajne wala). Khan sahab 80 varsh tak sachche sur ki talaash mein rahe aur namaz mein dua karte the: 'mere malik, ek sur bakhsh de'.
  • Muharram par ve Shia parampara se shok manate hain aur 10 din shehnai nahin bajate; 8vin tarikh ko ve paidal lagbhag 8 km chalkar nauha bajate hain (text).
  • Text mein Kulsum Halwain ki kachaudi ki dukaan, Geetabali aur Sulochana (unki pasandida heroine) ki yaadein bhi hain; bachpan mein 4 saal ki umr mein chhupkar nana ka riyaz sunna aur Sankatmochan mandir mein Hanuman Jayanti ke 5 din ke sangeet ka zikr hai.

💡 Sachcha sur, Muharram ke 10 din, nauha (text).

4️⃣ Kashi ka badalna aur ant ⭐⭐⭐

  • Text mein lekhak kehta hai ki Pakka Mahal se malai barf bechne wale chale gaye; Khan sahab ko iski kami khalti hai. Ab sangatiyon ke liye gayakon mein aadar nahin aur ghanton ke riyaz ko koi nahin poochta; kajli, chaiti aur adab ka zamana nahin raha.
  • Lekhak ke anusar Kashi mein Baba Vishwanath aur Bismillah Khan ek-dusre ke poorak rahe, waise hi Muharram-Taziya aur Holi-Abeer ki ganga-jamuni sanskriti. Khan sahab ka kehna ki Bharat Ratna shehnai pe mila hai, lungiya pe nahin (text).
  • Lekhak ke anusar Khan sahab ka 21 August 2006 ko nabbe varsh ki umra mein dehant hua. Unki sabse badi den ye ki assi baras unhone sangeet ko sampoornata se seekhne ki jijivisha zinda rakhi (text, Q12 ke vakya mein bhi).

💡 Kashi ki parampara; 21 Aug 2006; jijivisha (text).

1️⃣ लेखक और NCERT बॉक्स ⭐⭐⭐

  • NCERT के लेखक-परिचय के अनुसार यतींद्र मिश्र का जन्म 1977 में अयोध्या में हुआ; उन्होंने लखनऊ विश्वविद्यालय से एम.ए. किया। पृष्ठ के अनुसार वे अर्धवार्षिक पत्रिका सहित का संपादन करते हैं और विमला देवी फ़ाउंडेशन (1999) चलाते हैं।
  • NCERT बॉक्स के अनुसार यह उस्ताद बिस्मिल्ला ख़ाँ का व्यक्ति-चित्र है। संगीत आराधना है, जिसका अपना शास्त्र और गुरु-शिष्य परंपरा है; उनकी साधना 80 वर्ष की उम्र में भी जारी रही।

💡 व्यक्ति-चित्र; संगीत = आराधना (बॉक्स)।

2️⃣ डुमराँव, काशी और बचपन ⭐⭐⭐

  • लेखक के अनुसार बिस्मिल्ला ख़ाँ का जन्म डुमराँव (बिहार) में हुआ; वहाँ सोन नदी के किनारों पर पाई जाने वाली नरकट से शहनाई की रीड बनती है (प्रश्न 11 का वाक्य भी यही कहता है)।
  • पाठ के अनुसार काशी में बालाजी मंदिर की ड्योढ़ी पर रियाज़ का सिलसिला रहा। 14 वर्ष के अमीरुद्दीन रसूलनबाई और बतूलनबाई के रास्ते से बालाजी मंदिर जाते थे; ख़ाँ साहब मानते हैं कि संगीत का शौक इन्हीं गायिका बहनों से लगा।

💡 डुमराँव से रीड; काशी का बालाजी मंदिर (पाठ)।

3️⃣ सुर की खोज और मुहर्रम ⭐⭐⭐

  • पाठ के अनुसार शहनाई सुषिर वाद्य है। ख़ाँ साहब सच्चे सुर की तलाश में रहे और दुआ करते थे: 'मेरे मालिक, एक सुर बख़्श दे'।
  • मुहर्रम पर वे शोक मनाते हैं और 10 दिन शहनाई नहीं बजाते; 8वीं तारीख़ को लगभग 8 किलोमीटर पैदल चलकर नौहा बजाते हैं (पाठ)।

💡 सच्चा सुर, मुहर्रम के 10 दिन (पाठ)।

4️⃣ काशी का बदलना और अंत ⭐⭐⭐

  • पाठ में लेखक कहता है कि पक्का महाल से मलाई बरफ़ बेचने वाले चले गए; ख़ाँ साहब को इसकी कमी खलती है। अब घंटों के रियाज़ को कोई नहीं पूछता; कजली, चैती और अदब का ज़माना नहीं रहा।
  • लेखक के अनुसार ख़ाँ साहब का 21 अगस्त 2006 को नब्बे वर्ष की आयु में निधन हुआ। उनकी सबसे बड़ी देन यही कि अस्सी बरस उन्होंने संगीत को सीखने की जिजीविषा अपने भीतर ज़िंदा रखी (पाठ)।

💡 काशी की परंपरा; 21 अगस्त 2006 (पाठ)।

1️⃣ The writer and the NCERT box ⭐⭐⭐

  • According to the NCERT author page Yatindra Mishra was born in 1977 in Ayodhya and did an M.A. at Lucknow University. The page says he edits the half-yearly Sahit and runs the Vimla Devi Foundation (1999). His books named there: Yada-Kada, Ayodhya tatha anya kavitayein, Draudhi par Alap, Girija.
  • The NCERT box calls this a profile (vyakti-chitra) of Ustad Bismillah Khan. It says music is worship (aradhana) with its own shastra and a guru-shishya tradition, and that his practice went on at the age of 80.

💡 A profile; music as worship (box).

2️⃣ Dumraon, Kashi and childhood ⭐⭐⭐

  • The writer says Bismillah Khan was born at Dumraon (Bihar), where the reed (narkat) for the shehnai grows on the banks of the Son river (the Q11 sentence says the same). His great-grandfather was Ustad Salar Hussain Khan, his father Paigambar Baksh Khan and his mother Mitthan (text).
  • The text places his early years in Kashi, at the Balaji temple doorway by Panchganga ghat; as a boy he was Amiruddin, with brother Shamsuddin and uncles Sadiq Hussain and Alibaksh. At 14 he went past the homes of the singers Rasulanbai and Batulanbai, and Khan sahab admits his taste for music came from them (text).

💡 Reed from the Son; Kashi's Balaji temple (text).

3️⃣ The search for the true note, and Muharram ⭐⭐⭐

  • The text treats the shehnai as a sushir vadya (wind instrument). For 80 years Khan sahab looked for the true note and prayed: 'my Lord, grant me one note'.
  • At Muharram he mourns in the Shia tradition and does not play the shehnai for 10 days; on the 8th he walks about 8 km playing the nauha (text). The text also records memories of a kachaudi shop, of the film heroines Geetabali and Sulochana, and of five days of music at the Sankatmochan temple on Hanuman Jayanti.

💡 The true note; Muharram (text).

4️⃣ Kashi changes, and the end ⭐⭐⭐

  • The writer says the malai barf sellers have left Pakka Mahal and Khan sahab misses them; singers no longer respect their accompanists, hours of riyaz are not valued, and the age of kajli, chaiti and adab is gone.
  • The text says he died on 21 August 2006, in his ninetieth year, and that his greatest gift is that for eighty years he kept alive the hunger to learn music fully. He is quoted as saying the Bharat Ratna came for the shehnai, not for the lungi.

💡 Kashi's mixed culture; 21 Aug 2006 (text).

Practice — 5 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Nauabatkhane mein Ibadat Prashn-abhyas 1: shehnai ki duniya mein Dumraon ko kyon yaad kiya jata hai
Answer dekho

Text mein stated (Prashn 11 ka vakya): shehnai ki reed narkat se banti hai, jo Dumraon mein mukhyatah Son nadi ke kinaron par paayi jaati hai; aur Bismillah Khan ka janm Dumraon mein hua (text).

Q2 Nauabatkhane mein Ibadat Prashn-abhyas 3: sushir vadya se kya abhipray hai
Answer dekho

Page ke 'Yeh bhi janen' ke anusar sushir vadya wo hain jo phoonk kar bajaye jate hain: bansuri, shehnai, nagaswaram, been (glossary-style box). Shehnai ko 'sushir vadyon mein Shah' kyon kaha gaya hoga, ye meri vyakhya hai: kyonki mangal avsaron par uska bajna aur Khan sahab ki sadhana use sabse upar rakhte hain; text ye karan seedha nahin deta.

Q3 Nauabatkhane mein Ibadat Prashn-abhyas 4(ka): 'Fata sur na bakhshen. Lungiya ka kya hai...'
Answer dekho

Text mein stated: Khan sahab malik se dua karte hain ki fata sur na den, kyonki lungiya aaj fati hai to kal si jayegi. Meri vyakhya: unke liye sur ki poornata kapde ya dikhawe se bahut upar thi; ye vyakhya text ka seedha vakya nahin hai.

Q4 Nauabatkhane mein Ibadat Prashn-abhyas 5: Kashi mein ho rahe kaun-se parivartan Bismillah Khan ko vyathit karte the
Answer dekho

Text mein stated: Pakka Mahal se malai barf bechne wale chale gaye; desi ghee ki kachaudi-jalebi ki wo baat nahin rahi; sangatiyon ke liye gayakon mein aadar nahin raha; ghanton ke riyaz ko koi nahin poochta; kajli, chaiti aur adab ka zamana nahin raha; sangeet, sahitya aur adab ki kai parampara lupt ho gayi.

Q5 Open and practice tasks, not answered or keyed: Q2, Q4(kha), Q6 to Q12, Rachna aur abhivyakti, Bhasha-adhyayan, Patheytar
Answer dekho

Q2 (mangaldhwani ka nayak) aur Q7 (jeevan ki ghatnayein) text par aadharit lekin apne shabdon mein likhne wale prashn hain; upar ke notes mein tathya diye gaye hain par poora uttar nahin likha gaya. Q4(kha) aur Q6, Q8, Q9, Q10 vichar ya tark wale kaam hain. Q11 (upvakya chhantna) aur Q12 (mishrit vakya) vyakaran ke abhyas hain, keyed nahin. Patheytar mein notice, anuchhed aur Kashi ke yogdan par charcha jaise kaam hain. Shabd-sampada page par di gayi hai (jaise dyodhi = dahleez, nauabatkhana = pravesh dwar ke upar mangal dhwani bajane ka sthan, riyaz = abhyas, marfat = dwara, sajda = matha tekna, ibadat = upasana, taseer = gun, prabhav, asar, jijivisha = jeene ki ichchha). Yeh bhi janen mein sam, shruti aur vadya yantron ke char varg (tat-vitat, sushir, ghanvadya, avanaddh) aur chaiti, thumri, tappa, dadra ke namoone diye gaye hain.
