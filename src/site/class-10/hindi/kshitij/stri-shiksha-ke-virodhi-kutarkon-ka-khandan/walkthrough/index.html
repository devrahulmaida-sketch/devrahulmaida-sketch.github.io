Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

AuthorMahavir Prasad Dwivedi, 1864 to 1938; editor of Saraswati 1903 to 1920 (author page).
EssayFirst printed September 1914 as Padhe-likhon ka pandity (NCERT box).
RepliesPrakrit as the common language, the named learned women, Shakuntala and Sita; all as the author argues.
