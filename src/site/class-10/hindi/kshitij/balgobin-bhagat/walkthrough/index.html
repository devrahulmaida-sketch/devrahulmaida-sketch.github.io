Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

AuthorRamvriksh Benipuri, 1899 to 1968; Benipur; the magician of the pen.
SketchA householder who was a sadhu by humane concern, as the NCERT box says.
BhagatKabir was his Saheb; never lied; offered the harvest at the math (narrator's account).
The endSang at his son's death; had the daughter-in-law sent to marry again; found gone one morning.
