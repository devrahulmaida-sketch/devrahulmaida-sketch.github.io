Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

PoetManglesh Dabral, 1948 to 2020; Kafalpani; four collections.
ThemeThe accompanist who supports the lead singer; not coming forward is humaneness.
LinesHolds the sthayi; comforts the failing voice; shows the singer he is not alone.
WordsNausikhiya beginner; dhaandhas bandhana to console; saptak group of seven.
MusicSa re ga ma pa dha ni; madhya, taar and mandra saptak.
