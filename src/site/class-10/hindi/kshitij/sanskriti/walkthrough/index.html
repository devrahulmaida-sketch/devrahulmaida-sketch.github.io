Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

AuthorBhadant Anand Kausalyayan, 1905 to 1988; Sohana, Ambala; Buddhist monk; Wardha.
Core ideaSabhyata is the result of sanskriti; human culture is indivisible (author and NCERT box).
ExamplesFire, needle and thread, Newton, Lenin, Marx, Siddhartha are the author's examples, reported as his.
WelfareThe author says culture cut off from welfare becomes non-culture; the welfare part lasts.
Open tasksQ7 to Q9 and Patheytar are open, writing or grammar tasks and are not keyed.
