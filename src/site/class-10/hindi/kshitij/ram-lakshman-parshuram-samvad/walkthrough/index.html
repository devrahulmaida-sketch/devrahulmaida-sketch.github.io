Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

SourceBalkand of Ramcharitmanas by Tulsidas, as the NCERT box says; the poet lived 1532 to 1623 per the author page.
ParshuramHis anger and his claims about himself are his own words in the poem.
LakshmanHis arguments and satire are his words in the poem; meanings beyond the page glossary are my reading.
GlossaryParasu is a farsa; tarjani is the finger beside the thumb; kumhadbatiya is a weak person (page glossary).
Open tasksQ2, Q3, Q6 to Q15 and Patheytar are opinion, writing or figure-of-speech tasks and are not keyed.
