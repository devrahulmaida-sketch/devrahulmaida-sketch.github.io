Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

PoetNirala, 1899 to 1961; Mahishadal; Gadhakola.
StyleFree verse; sympathy for the exploited; resistance to power.
UtsahCall-song to the cloud; destruction and new growth.
FagunAt nahi rahi hai; eyes will not turn; beauty cannot be contained.
WordsDharadhar cloud; nidagh heat; unmana restless; at to fit in.
