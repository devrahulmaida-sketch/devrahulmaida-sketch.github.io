The Book That Saved the Earth

वह किताब जिसने धरती बचाई

Claire Boiko's play about a Martian invasion stopped by Mother Goose

Long + Short notes Hindi • English • Hinglish NCERT Class X English, Footprints without Feet (supplementary reader), chapter file jefp109 (print stamp seen on its pages: Reprint 2025-26). The whole play was retrieved, with the Read and Find Out questions, glossary, Think about it, Talk about it and Suggested reading. These notes are summaries and analysis, not a reproduction of the play. Dates, distances and events (the invasion of 2040, the twenty-fifth century, a hundred million miles) are the play's own fiction and are not facts about the real world. The glossary is not reproduced. The opinion and discussion questions (Think about it 1 to 4 and Talk about it 1 and 2) are open and are not keyed

Long Notes - Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye - aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Historian aur natak ka dhaancha ⭐⭐⭐

  • The Book That Saved the Earth Claire Boiko ka natak hai. Chhaapne wali bhumika poochti hai ki kya Mother Goose jaisi nursery rhymes ki kitaab dharti ko Martian invasion se bacha sakti hai. Natak chaar sadiyan aage ka hai.
  • Natak ke anusar pachhiswi sadi mein Museum of Ancient History ki ek Historian darshakon ko historiscope (projector) se purani ghatna dikhati hai. Wo kehti hai ki bisvi sadi ko Era of the Book kaha jaata tha, kyunki har cheez par kitaabein thi.
  • Historian ke anusar 2040 ka Martian invasion kabhi hua hi nahi, kyunki ek kitaab ne use roka. Ye Read and Find Out ke jawab hain (natak ke apne kalpanik saal).

💡 Claire Boiko, Historian, Era of the Book, 2040.

2️⃣ Mars par Think-Tank ⭐⭐⭐

  • Mars Space Control mein Great and Mighty Think-Tank commander-in-chief hai. Uska anda jaisa bada sir aur taaron wala chola hai. Uska Apprentice Noodle switchboard sambhalta hai.
  • Think-Tank ko poori salutation chahiye, aur wo sheeshe se poochta hai ki sabse zyada buddhimaan kaun hai. Wo dharti ko chhota, aadim gola maanta hai aur lunch se pehle hamla karna chahta hai.
  • Natak yahan dikhata hai ki Think-Tank ghamandi hai aur uski galtiyan koi nahi kehta: sab kehte hain It shall be done, sir.

💡 Think-Tank, Noodle, sheesha, ghamand.

3️⃣ Library mein probe ⭐⭐⭐

  • Captain Omega, Lieutenant Iota aur Sergeant Oop dharti par Centerville Public Library mein utarte hain. Unhe nahi pata ye kaun si jagah hai.
  • Iota do hazaar kitaabein ginti hai aur kehti hai ki ye storage barn hai. Oop kehta hai shayad ye topiyan hain aur kitaab sir par rakhta hai (haberdashery). Think-Tank kehta hai ki ye crude refreshment stand hai.
  • Read and Find Out ka jawab: unke andaze galat hain, kyunki wo kitaab pehchaan hi nahi pate.

💡 Omega, Iota, Oop, barn, topi, refreshment stand.

4️⃣ Sandwich ka bhram ⭐⭐⭐

  • Think-Tank kitaab ko sandwich kehta hai aur Oop ko use khane ka hukm deta hai. Oop kehta hai ki wo sukha tha, Martian dust jaisa.
  • Noodle dheere se kehta hai ki Earthlings sandwiches khaate nahi, balki use communication device ki tarah dekhte hain. Think-Tank turant kehta hai ki ye communication sandwiches hain, jaise ye uska hi vichaar ho.
  • Phir hukm hota hai sunne ka. Kuch nahi sunai deta, to Noodle yaad dilata hai ki Earthling unhe kholte aur dekhte hain. Think-Tank ab kehta hai ki ye eye communication hai.

💡 Sandwich, khana, sunna, dekhna. Noodle chaaplusi nahi, chatur sudhaar.

5️⃣ Nursery rhymes ka code ⭐⭐⭐

  • Crew ek bada rangeen Mother Goose kholta hai. Usme Earthlings ki tasveerein aur lakeeren, squiggles, dots hain, jise Think-Tank code maanta hai. Wo vitamins khane ka hukm deta hai taaki crew decipher kar sake.
  • Omega Mistress Mary padhti hai. Think-Tank sochta hai ki Earthlings kheti aur khanan jodkar chaandi aur explosives ugaa sakte hain. Iota Hey diddle diddle padhti hai aur wo sochta hai ki unhone jaanwaron ko sangeet aur space techniques sikha di hain.
  • Ye Think-Tank ka galat matlab nikaalna hai: masoom rhymes ko khatre samajhna (Talk about it ka pehla sawaal khula hai).

💡 Mistress Mary, Hey diddle diddle, galat matlab.

6️⃣ Humpty Dumpty aur ant ⭐⭐⭐

  • Oop Humpty Dumpty padhta hai aur uski tasveer Think-Tank ki taraf karta hai. Think-Tank chillata hai: It is me! Wo had a great fall ka matlab nikaalta hai ki Earthling Mars Central Control par kabza karenge.
  • Wo invasion fleet ko Mars khaali karne ka hukm deta hai aur Alpha Centauri ki taraf bhaagta hai, natak ke anusar sau million meel door.
  • Ant mein Historian kehti hai ki pachhiswi sadi mein dharti ne Mars se sampark phir shuru kiya aur dosti hui. Tab tak Think-Tank ki jagah Noodle aa chuka tha. Martians ko padhna sikhaya gaya, par Mother Goose ab bhi nahi padh paate.

💡 Humpty Dumpty, Alpha Centauri, Noodle ab leader, Mother Goose.

1️⃣ इतिहासकार और नाटक का ढाँचा ⭐⭐⭐

  • The Book That Saved the Earth क्लेयर बॉयको का नाटक है। शुरुआती भूमिका पूछती है कि क्या मदर गूज़ जैसी बालगीतों की किताब धरती को मंगल-आक्रमण से बचा सकती है। नाटक चार सदियाँ आगे का है।
  • नाटक के अनुसार पच्चीसवीं सदी में प्राचीन इतिहास संग्रहालय की एक इतिहासकार दर्शकों को हिस्टोरिस्कोप (प्रोजेक्टर) से पुरानी घटना दिखाती है। वह कहती है कि बीसवीं सदी को किताबों का युग कहा जाता था, क्योंकि हर चीज़ पर किताबें थीं।
  • इतिहासकार के अनुसार 2040 का मंगल-आक्रमण कभी हुआ ही नहीं, क्योंकि एक किताब ने उसे रोका। ये Read and Find Out के जवाब हैं (नाटक के अपने काल्पनिक साल)।

💡 क्लेयर बॉयको, इतिहासकार, किताबों का युग, 2040।

2️⃣ मंगल पर थिंक-टैंक ⭐⭐⭐

  • मार्स स्पेस कंट्रोल में ग्रेट एंड माइटी थिंक-टैंक प्रधान सेनापति है। उसका अंडे जैसा बड़ा सिर और सितारों वाला चोगा है। उसका शिष्य नूडल स्विचबोर्ड सँभालता है।
  • थिंक-टैंक को पूरा अभिवादन चाहिए, और वह आईने से पूछता है कि सबसे ज़्यादा बुद्धिमान कौन है। वह धरती को छोटा, आदिम गोला मानता है और दोपहर के खाने से पहले हमला करना चाहता है।
  • नाटक यहाँ दिखाता है कि थिंक-टैंक घमंडी है और उसकी ग़लतियाँ कोई नहीं कहता: सब कहते हैं It shall be done, sir।

💡 थिंक-टैंक, नूडल, आईना, घमंड।

3️⃣ पुस्तकालय में यान ⭐⭐⭐

  • कैप्टन ओमेगा, लेफ़्टिनेंट आयोटा और सार्जेंट ऊप धरती पर सेंटरविल पब्लिक लाइब्रेरी में उतरते हैं। उन्हें नहीं पता यह कौन सी जगह है।
  • आयोटा दो हज़ार किताबें गिनती है और कहती है कि यह भंडार-घर है। ऊप कहता है शायद ये टोपियाँ हैं और किताब सिर पर रखता है (haberdashery)। थिंक-टैंक कहता है कि यह कच्चा जलपान-स्टॉल है।
  • Read and Find Out का जवाब: उनके अंदाज़े ग़लत हैं, क्योंकि वे किताब पहचान ही नहीं पाते।

💡 ओमेगा, आयोटा, ऊप, भंडार, टोपी, जलपान-स्टॉल।

4️⃣ सैंडविच का भ्रम ⭐⭐⭐

  • थिंक-टैंक किताब को सैंडविच कहता है और ऊप को उसे खाने का हुक्म देता है। ऊप कहता है कि वह सूखा था, मंगल की धूल जैसा।
  • नूडल धीरे से कहता है कि धरतीवासी सैंडविच खाते नहीं, बल्कि उसे संचार-साधन की तरह देखते हैं। थिंक-टैंक तुरंत कहता है कि ये संचार-सैंडविच हैं, जैसे यह उसी का विचार हो।
  • फिर सुनने का हुक्म होता है। कुछ नहीं सुनाई देता, तो नूडल याद दिलाता है कि धरतीवासी उन्हें खोलते और देखते हैं। थिंक-टैंक अब कहता है कि यह आँख से संचार है।

💡 सैंडविच, खाना, सुनना, देखना। नूडल चापलूस नहीं, चतुर सुधारक।

5️⃣ बालगीतों का कोड ⭐⭐⭐

  • दल एक बड़ी रंगीन मदर गूज़ खोलता है। उसमें धरतीवासियों की तस्वीरें और लकीरें, घुमावदार निशान, बिंदु हैं, जिसे थिंक-टैंक कोड मानता है। वह विटामिन खाने का हुक्म देता है ताकि दल डिकोड कर सके।
  • ओमेगा मिस्ट्रेस मैरी पढ़ती है। थिंक-टैंक सोचता है कि धरतीवासी खेती और खनन जोड़कर चाँदी और विस्फोटक उगा सकते हैं। आयोटा Hey diddle diddle पढ़ती है और वह सोचता है कि उन्होंने जानवरों को संगीत और अंतरिक्ष-तकनीक सिखा दी है।
  • यह थिंक-टैंक का ग़लत अर्थ निकालना है: मासूम बालगीतों को ख़तरा समझना (Talk about it का पहला सवाल खुला है)।

💡 मिस्ट्रेस मैरी, Hey diddle diddle, ग़लत अर्थ।

6️⃣ हम्प्टी डम्प्टी और अंत ⭐⭐⭐

  • ऊप हम्प्टी डम्प्टी पढ़ता है और उसकी तस्वीर थिंक-टैंक की ओर करता है। थिंक-टैंक चिल्लाता है: It is me! वह had a great fall का अर्थ निकालता है कि धरतीवासी मार्स सेंट्रल कंट्रोल पर क़ब्ज़ा करेंगे।
  • वह आक्रमण-बेड़े को मंगल ख़ाली करने का हुक्म देता है और अल्फ़ा सेंटॉरी की ओर भागता है, नाटक के अनुसार सौ मिलियन मील दूर।
  • अंत में इतिहासकार कहती है कि पच्चीसवीं सदी में धरती ने मंगल से संपर्क फिर शुरू किया और दोस्ती हुई। तब तक थिंक-टैंक की जगह नूडल आ चुका था। मंगलवासियों को पढ़ना सिखाया गया, पर मदर गूज़ वे अब भी नहीं पढ़ पाते।

💡 हम्प्टी डम्प्टी, अल्फ़ा सेंटॉरी, नूडल अब नेता, मदर गूज़।

1️⃣ The Historian and the frame ⭐⭐⭐

  • The Book That Saved the Earth is a play by Claire Boiko. The opening note asks whether a book of nursery rhymes like Mother Goose can save Earth from a Martian invasion. The play is set four centuries in the future.
  • In the play's twenty-fifth century, a Historian at the Museum of Ancient History uses a historiscope (projector) to show an old event. She says the twentieth century was called the Era of the Book, because there were books about everything.
  • She says the Martian invasion of 2040 never happened because a single book stopped it. These answer the Read and Find Out questions (the play's own fictional dates).

💡 Claire Boiko, Historian, Era of the Book, 2040.

2️⃣ Think-Tank on Mars ⭐⭐⭐

  • At Mars Space Control, the Great and Mighty Think-Tank is commander-in-chief, with a huge egg-shaped head and a starry robe. His Apprentice Noodle works the switchboard.
  • Think-Tank demands the whole salutation and asks his mirror who is the most gifted being. He calls Earth a primitive ball of mud and wants to invade it before lunch.
  • The play shows Think-Tank as vain, and nobody says his mistakes aloud: everyone answers It shall be done, sir.

💡 Think-Tank, Noodle, mirror, vanity.

3️⃣ The probe in the library ⭐⭐⭐

  • Captain Omega, Lieutenant Iota and Sergeant Oop land on Earth in the Centerville Public Library. They do not know what the place is.
  • Iota counts two thousand books and calls it a storage barn. Oop says maybe they are hats and puts a book on his head (a haberdashery). Think-Tank says it is a crude refreshment stand.
  • Read and Find Out answer: their guesses are wrong because they cannot recognise a book.

💡 Omega, Iota, Oop, barn, hats, refreshment stand.

4️⃣ The sandwich mistake ⭐⭐⭐

  • Think-Tank calls a book a sandwich and orders Oop to eat it. Oop says it was dry as Martian dust.
  • Noodle gently says the Earthlings did not eat the sandwiches but used them as a communication device. Think-Tank at once says they are communication sandwiches, as if it were his own idea.
  • Next he orders them to listen. They hear nothing, so Noodle recalls Earthlings opened and watched them. Think-Tank then says it is eye communication.

💡 Sandwich, eating, listening, watching. Noodle corrects without offending.

5️⃣ The nursery rhyme code ⭐⭐⭐

  • The crew opens a large colourful Mother Goose. It has pictures of Earthlings and lines, squiggles and dots, which Think-Tank calls a code. He orders them to take vitamins so they can decipher it.
  • Omega reads Mistress Mary. Think-Tank thinks Earthlings can combine agriculture and mining and grow silver and explosives. Iota reads Hey diddle diddle and he thinks they have taught animals music and space techniques.
  • This is Think-Tank misreading innocent rhymes as threats (Talk about it 1 is an open question).

💡 Mistress Mary, Hey diddle diddle, misreading.

6️⃣ Humpty Dumpty and the end ⭐⭐⭐

  • Oop reads Humpty Dumpty and turns the picture to Think-Tank. Think-Tank screams It is me! and reads had a great fall as a plan to capture Mars Central Control.
  • He orders the invasion fleet to evacuate Mars and flees toward Alpha Centauri, which the play says is a hundred million miles away.
  • At the end the Historian says that in the twenty-fifth century Earth resumed contact and became friendly with Mars, and Noodle had replaced Think-Tank. Martians were taught to read, but cannot bring themselves to read Mother Goose.

💡 Humpty Dumpty, Alpha Centauri, Noodle leads, Mother Goose.

Practice - 6 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Read and Find Out 1: why was the twentieth century called the Era of the Book
Answer dekho

The Historian says there were books about everything, from ant eaters to Zulus. They taught people how, when, where and why to do things, and they illustrated, educated, punctuated and even decorated.

Q2 Read and Find Out 1 (part 2): who tried to invade the earth in the twenty-first century
Answer dekho

The Martians, led by Think-Tank. The play says the invasion of 2040 never happened because a single book stopped it.

Q3 Read and Find Out 2: what guesses does Think-Tank make about the books found on earth
Answer dekho

He says the library is a crude refreshment stand and the books are sandwiches. Then he says they are communication sandwiches, first for ear communication, then for eye communication. Finally he calls the pictures and print a code. Iota guessed a storage barn and Oop guessed hats.

Q4 Facts: how does Think-Tank react to Humpty Dumpty, and how does the play end
Answer dekho

He thinks the picture of Humpty Dumpty is himself and that Had a great fall means Earthlings plan to capture Mars Central Control. He orders Mars to be evacuated. The Historian says that five hundred years later Noodle had replaced Think-Tank and Earth became friendly with Mars.

Q5 Facts: how does Noodle correct Think-Tank
Answer dekho

By offering small bits of data in a humble way, for example that Earthlings did not eat the sandwiches, so Think-Tank can present the correction as his own point. This reading is from the play's lines.

Q6 Pending, not keyed
Answer dekho

Think about it 1 to 4 (how Noodle manages Think-Tank, how you would handle him, whether books are being replaced by electronic media, books as a best companion and your favourite book) and Talk about it 1 and 2 (misinterpreting rhymes, the language of the aliens): all are open or opinion questions.
