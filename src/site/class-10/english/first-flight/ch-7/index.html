Long Notes - Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye - aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Valli aur Bus ki Chahat ⭐⭐⭐

  • Chapter ke anusar Valli (Valliammai) aath saal ki bahut curious ladki hai. Uska favourite pastime ghar ke front doorway mein khade hokar gali dekhna tha, kyunki gali mein uski umar ka koi saathi nahi tha.
  • Sabse fascinating cheez gaon aur nazdiki town ke beech chalne wali bus thi, jo har ghante ek baar town ki taraf aur ek baar wapas aati. Bus dekh dekhkar uske man mein bus mein ek baar baithne ki chahat badhti gayi.
  • Dost bus ki sair batate to wo jalan se English mein Proud! proud! chillati (chapter kehta hai ki wo uska matlab nahi samajhte the, disapproval ka slang).
  • Usne logon ki baatein sunkar details jodi: town chhe mile door, kiraya ek taraf tees paise, safar paintalis minute. Wapsi us hi bus se ho sakti hai, aur tees paise aur dekar.

💡 Valli 8 saal, bus ki chahat, 30 paise, 6 mile, 45 minute.

2️⃣ Bus mein Chadhna ⭐⭐⭐

  • Ek spring ki dopahar Stop the bus! ki chhoti awaaz aayi. Conductor mazaakiya tha; usne Valli ko madam kehkar seat di. Valli ne kaha ki wo khud chadh sakti hai.
  • Bus naya aur chamakdar tha; Valli ne khidki se canal, palm trees, hare khet dekhe. Elderly aadmi ne use child kaha to usne gusse mein kaha ki wo child nahi, aath saal ki hai, aur tees paise de chuki hai.
  • Pas baithi elderly aurat (betel nut, bade earrings) se Valli ko ghin aayi; usne seedha jawab diya ki wo akeli hai aur uske paas ticket hai.
  • Oral Check ka jawab: conductor mazaak mein madam kehta hai, kyunki wo bade logon ki tarah khud kiraya dekar akeli travel kar rahi hai.

💡 Conductor ka madam, canal aur khet, elderly log, tees paise.

3️⃣ Valli ka Plan aur Safar ⭐⭐⭐

  • Valli ne sixty paise bachaye: peppermints, toys, balloons aur village fair mein merry-go-round ka mann daba diya. Ghar se nikalne ke liye uski maa dopahar ek se chaar baje ke beech sati thi.
  • Safar mein bus ne kheton, gaon aur dukanon ko paar kiya; ek young cow bus ke aage bhaagi aur Valli itna hansi ki aankhon mein aansu aa gaye. Phir railway crossing par train gujri.
  • Town mein bade chamakdar shops aur bheed dekhkar wo dang rah gayi. Sab utre, par Valli ne dusre tees paise dekar wapsi ka ticket liya.
  • Conductor ne cold drink ya sair ko kaha; Valli ne akele darr aur paise kam hone ka bahana diya. Isse uski savdhani aur khuddaari dikhti hai (ye hamara analysis hai, chapter ka likha jawab nahi).

💡 60 paise bachat, maa ka sona, cow, train, dusra ticket.

4️⃣ Wapsi aur Maut ka Ehsaas ⭐⭐⭐

  • Wapsi mein Valli ne wahi cow sadak ke kinare mari hui dekhi, kisi tez gaadi ki chot se. Jo kuch der pehle pyari thi wo ab bhayanak lag rahi thi.
  • Cow ki yaad ne uska utsaah dabaa diya; wo khidki se bahar nahi dekhna chahti thi aur seat par chipki baithi rahi. Bus teen chalis par gaon pahunchi.
  • Ghar mein maa aur chatterbox aunt baatein kar rahi thi. Maa ne kaha ki duniya ki bahut cheezein hum jaan nahi paate, aur jaan bhi lein to poori samajh nahi paate. Valli ne dheere se kaha ki wo isse agree karti hai.
  • Chapter ke Before You Read ke anusar ye kahani ek bachchi ke jeevan aur maut ke rahasya mein pehle kadam ki hai. Aunt ne use chit of a girl kaha aur Valli andar hi andar muskuraayi.

💡 Mari hui cow, udasi, 3:40, maa ka vaakya.

5️⃣ Poem: The Tale of Custard the Dragon ⭐⭐⭐

  • Kavi Ogden Nash. Chapter ke anusar ye ek humorous ballad hai, jo parody ke kareeb hai. Chapter ye bhi kehta hai ki Nash ne chaar sau se zyada comic verse likhe (14 volumes, 1931 se 1972).
  • Patra: Belinda, kitten Ink, mouse Blink, dog Mustard aur dragon Custard. Sab bahadur hain, par Custard nice safe cage maangta hai, isliye coward kehlata hai.
  • Ek pirate khidki se ghus aata hai. Mustard bhaag jata hai, Ink aur Blink chhup jaate hain, par Custard use gobble kar jata hai.
  • Baad mein sab kehte hain ki wo aur bahadur hote; Custard kehta hai ki everybody is braver than me. Ant mein wo phir cage ke liye rota hai.

💡 Ogden Nash, ballad, Custard ne pirate khaya, cage.

1️⃣ वल्ली और बस की चाह ⭐⭐⭐

  • अध्याय के अनुसार वल्ली (वल्लियम्मई) आठ साल की बहुत जिज्ञासु लड़की है। उसका पसंदीदा शौक़ घर के सामने के दरवाज़े पर खड़े होकर गली देखना है, क्योंकि गली में उसकी उम्र का कोई साथी नहीं।
  • सबसे रोचक चीज़ गाँव और पास के क़स्बे के बीच चलने वाली बस है, जो हर घंटे एक बार जाती और एक बार लौटती है। उसे देखकर वल्ली के मन में एक बार बस में बैठने की चाह बढ़ती गई।
  • दोस्त क़स्बे का हाल बताते तो वह जलन से अंग्रेज़ी में Proud! proud! चिल्लाती (अध्याय के अनुसार वे इसका अर्थ नहीं समझते थे, इसे नापसंदगी की बोलचाल के रूप में बोलते थे)।
  • उसने बातचीत सुनकर ब्योरा जोड़ा: क़स्बा छह मील दूर, किराया एक तरफ़ तीस पैसे, सफ़र पैंतालीस मिनट। उसी बस से लौटना संभव है, तीस पैसे और देकर।

💡 वल्ली 8 साल, बस की चाह, 30 पैसे, 6 मील, 45 मिनट।

2️⃣ बस में चढ़ना ⭐⭐⭐

  • एक बसंत की दोपहर Stop the bus! की छोटी आवाज़ आई। हँसमुख कंडक्टर ने उसे मैडम कहकर आगे की सीट दी। वल्ली ने कहा कि वह ख़ुद चढ़ सकती है।
  • बस नई और चमकदार थी; सीट पर खड़े होकर उसने नहर, ताड़ के पेड़ और हरे खेत देखे। बुज़ुर्ग ने बच्ची कहा तो उसने कहा कि वह बच्ची नहीं, आठ साल की है और तीस पैसे दे चुकी है।
  • पास बैठी बुज़ुर्ग महिला (पान की सुपारी, बड़ी बालियाँ) उसे घिनौनी लगी; उसने रूखेपन से कहा कि वह अकेली है और उसके पास टिकट है।
  • Oral Check उत्तर: कंडक्टर मज़ाक में मैडम कहता है क्योंकि वह अपना किराया ख़ुद देकर बड़ों की तरह अकेली सफ़र कर रही है, और यात्री उसके साथ हँसते हैं।

💡 कंडक्टर का मैडम, नहर और खेत, बुज़ुर्ग लोग, तीस पैसे।

3️⃣ वल्ली की योजना और सफ़र ⭐⭐⭐

  • वल्ली ने साठ पैसे बचाए: पेपरमिंट, खिलौने, ग़ुब्बारे और गाँव के मेले की चरखी का मन दबाया। उसकी माँ दोपहर में लगभग एक से चार बजे सोती थी, यही उसका मौक़ा था।
  • सफ़र में बस खेतों, छोटी बस्तियों और दुकानों से गुज़री; एक जवान गाय बस के आगे दौड़ी और वल्ली इतना हँसी कि आँखों में आँसू आ गए। फिर रेल फाटक पर ट्रेन गुज़री।
  • क़स्बे की चमकदार दुकानें और भीड़ देखकर वह दंग रह गई। सब उतरे, पर वल्ली ने वापसी के लिए तीस पैसे और दिए।
  • कंडक्टर ने घूमने और ठंडा पीने को कहा; वल्ली ने अकेले होने के डर और पैसे न होने की बात कही। इससे उसके स्वभाव (सावधान, आत्मनिर्भर) का अनुमान हमारा विश्लेषण है।

💡 60 पैसे की बचत, माँ की नींद, गाय, ट्रेन, दूसरा टिकट।

4️⃣ वापसी और मृत्यु का एहसास ⭐⭐⭐

  • वापसी में वल्ली ने वही गाय सड़क किनारे मरी देखी, किसी तेज़ वाहन की टक्कर से। जो कुछ देर पहले प्यारी थी वह अब भयानक लग रही थी।
  • उस याद ने उसका उत्साह दबा दिया; उसने खिड़की से देखना छोड़ दिया और सीट से चिपकी बैठी रही। बस गाँव तीन बजकर चालीस मिनट पर पहुँची।
  • घर में माँ और बातूनी चाची बात कर रही थीं। माँ ने कहा कि दुनिया की कई चीज़ें हम जान नहीं पाते, और जान भी लें तो पूरी तरह समझ नहीं पाते। वल्ली ने धीरे से सहमति जताई।
  • Before You Read के अनुसार कहानी जीवन और मृत्यु के रहस्य में उसका पहला क़दम है। चाची ने उसे chit of a girl कहा और वल्ली मन ही मन मुस्कुराई।

💡 मरी गाय, उदासी, 3:40, माँ का वाक्य।

5️⃣ कविता: The Tale of Custard the Dragon ⭐⭐⭐

  • कवि ओग्डन नैश। अध्याय इसे हास्य-बैलेड (पैरोडी के क़रीब) कहता है और बताता है कि नैश ने चार सौ से ज़्यादा हास्य कविताएँ लिखीं (14 खंड, 1931 से 1972)।
  • पात्र: बेलिंडा, बिल्ली का बच्चा इंक, चुहिया ब्लिंक, कुत्ता मस्टर्ड और ड्रैगन कस्टर्ड। सब बहादुर हैं, पर कस्टर्ड एक सुरक्षित पिंजरा माँगकर रोता है, इसलिए डरपोक कहलाता है।
  • एक समुद्री डाकू खिड़की से घुसता है। मस्टर्ड भाग जाता है, इंक और ब्लिंक छिप जाते हैं, पर कस्टर्ड डाकू को निगल जाता है।
  • बाद में सब कहते हैं कि वे और बहादुर होते; कस्टर्ड कहता है everybody is braver than me। अंत में वह फिर पिंजरे के लिए रोता है।

💡 ओग्डन नैश, बैलेड, कस्टर्ड ने डाकू खाया, पिंजरा।

1️⃣ Valli and her wish for the bus ⭐⭐⭐

  • The chapter says Valli (Valliammai) is an eight-year-old girl, very curious. Her favourite pastime is standing in the front doorway watching the street, since no playmates her age live there.
  • The most fascinating thing is the bus between her village and the nearest town, passing once each hour in each direction. Watching it, a wish to ride it once grows in her.
  • When friends describe the town she is too jealous to listen and shouts Proud! proud! (the chapter says neither she nor her friends understood the word; they used it as slang for disapproval).
  • She collects details from conversations: the town is six miles away, the fare is thirty paise one way, the trip takes forty-five minutes, and she can return on the same bus by paying again.

💡 Valli, 8; the wish; 30 paise, 6 miles, 45 minutes.

2️⃣ Getting on the bus ⭐⭐⭐

  • One spring afternoon a small voice shouts Stop the bus! The jolly conductor jokes, calls her madam and offers a front seat. Valli says she can get on by herself.
  • The bus is new and shining. Standing on the seat, she sees the canal, palm trees and green fields. When an elderly man calls her a child she says she is not, she is eight years old and has paid her thirty paise.
  • She finds the elderly woman beside her repulsive (betel nut, big earrings) and answers curtly that she is travelling alone and has a ticket.
  • Oral Check answer: the conductor says madam in jest because she pays her own fare and travels alone like a grown-up, and the passengers laugh with him.

💡 The conductor's madam, canal and fields, the elders, thirty paise.

3️⃣ Her plan and the journey ⭐⭐⭐

  • Valli saved sixty paise, resisting peppermints, toys, balloons and the merry-go-round at the village fair. Her mother naps from about one to four, which gave her time to slip out.
  • On the ride the bus passes fields, hamlets and shops. A young cow runs in front of the bus and Valli laughs until tears come. Then a train rushes past at a railway crossing.
  • In town she is struck dumb by the bright shops and crowds. Everyone gets off except Valli, who pays another thirty paise for the return.
  • The conductor offers sights and a cold drink; she refuses, saying she would be afraid alone and she has no spare money. That this shows her as careful and independent is our analysis.

💡 60 paise saved, mother's nap, the cow, the train, the second ticket.

4️⃣ The return and an idea of death ⭐⭐⭐

  • On the way back Valli sees the same cow lying dead by the roadside, struck by a fast vehicle. What was lovable a short while ago now looks horrible.
  • The memory dampens her enthusiasm; she stops looking out and sits glued to her seat until the bus reaches the village at three forty.
  • At home her mother and a chatterbox aunt are talking. The mother says there are many things we cannot know, and even when we know them we cannot fully understand. Valli softly agrees.
  • The Before You Read note says the story is her induction into the mystery of life and death. The aunt calls her a chit of a girl and Valli smiles to herself so they do not understand.

💡 The dead cow, sadness, 3:40, the mother's remark.

5️⃣ Poem: The Tale of Custard the Dragon ⭐⭐⭐

  • Poet Ogden Nash. The chapter calls it a humorous ballad, close to a parody, and says Nash wrote over four hundred pieces of comic verse (14 volumes, 1931 to 1972).
  • Characters: Belinda, kitten Ink, mouse Blink, dog Mustard and dragon Custard. All are brave except Custard, who cries for a nice safe cage, so he is called a coward.
  • A pirate climbs in through the window. Mustard flees, Ink and Blink hide, but Custard gobbles the pirate.
  • Afterwards the others say they would have been braver; Custard says everybody is braver than me. At the end he is still crying for a cage.

💡 Ogden Nash, ballad, Custard eats the pirate, the cage.

Practice - 11 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Oral Check 1 (part 1): Valli's favourite pastime
Answer dekho

Standing in the front doorway of her house, watching what was happening in the street outside.

Q2 Oral Check 2: source of unending joy, strongest desire
Answer dekho

The bus between her village and the nearest town was a source of unending joy. Her strongest desire was to ride on that bus, even just once.

Q3 Oral Check 3: what she found out and how
Answer dekho

The town was six miles away, the fare thirty paise one way, the trip forty-five minutes, and she could return on the same bus by paying again. She listened to conversations between neighbours and regular bus users and asked a few discreet questions.

Q4 Oral Check 4: what was she planning
Answer dekho

To take the one-o'clock bus, reach town at one forty-five and be back about two forty-five (the chapter's own calculation); she was planning her first bus ride.

Q5 Oral Check (part 2) 1 and 2: madam, standing on the seat
Answer dekho

The conductor, fond of joking, calls her madam in jest. She stands on the seat because the canvas blind cuts off her view; she then sees the canal, palm trees, grassland, distant mountains and green fields.

Q6 Oral Check (part 2) 3 and 4: what she tells the man, the elderly woman
Answer dekho

She says there is nobody there who is a child, she has paid her thirty paise like everyone else, and later that she is eight years old. She finds the elderly woman repulsive (big holes in the ear lobes, ugly earrings, betel nut and juice) and answers her curtly.

Q7 Oral Check (part 3) 1 to 3: saving, what made her laugh, why she stayed
Answer dekho

She saved stray coins, resisting peppermints, toys, balloons and even the merry-go-round, and reached sixty paise. It was not easy, as the village fair shows. She laughed at the young cow running in front of the bus. She did not get off at the bus station because she was going back on the same bus and said she would be afraid to see the sights alone.

Q8 Oral Check (part 3) 4: why not the drink
Answer dekho

She said she did not have enough money and refused even a free treat. What it shows about her is our analysis.

Q9 Exercise 1, 2, 6, 7: deepest desire, plan, return, the agreeing remark
Answer dekho

Her deepest desire was to ride the bus. She planned a one-o'clock bus, saved sixty paise and used her mother's nap time. On the way back she refused to look out because the dead cow haunted her. By agreeing about things happening without our knowledge, she silently refers to what she learnt that day, as the chapter's closing scene shows.

Q10 Poem questions 1 to 3
Answer dekho

Characters: Belinda, with Ink the black kitten, Blink the grey mouse, Mustard the yellow dog and Custard the dragon. Custard cried for a nice safe cage, so he is called the cowardly dragon. Belinda tickled him unmercifully, and the others laughed at him.

Q11 Pending, not keyed
Answer dekho

Fill-in clue task (Q3), Q5 and Q8 (finding lines, evidence of an eight-year-old's view) and the role-play, the three-paragraph writing topics, ticket collage and discussions; poem Q4 (poetic devices), Q5 (appearance), Q6 (rhyme scheme), Q7 (images), Q8 (serious or light-hearted), Q9 (ballad project) and the ballad-writing guidelines.
