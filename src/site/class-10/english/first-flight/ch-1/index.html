Long Notes - Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye - aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Setting aur Author ⭐⭐

  • Story ka author contents page par G.L. Fuentes likha hai. Chapter ke text mein unki biography nahi hai, isliye yahan nahi di gayi.
  • Setting: ek akela ghar, poori valley mein sirf wahi ek, ek low hill ki crest par. Wahan se river aur ripe corn ke khet dikhte hain, jisme flowers hain jo achhi harvest ka vaada karte the.
  • Before You Read ka sawaal: faith kis par rakhein? Kya Lencho ka letter God tak pahunchega? Kya God paisa bhejega?

💡 Lonely house on a hill + ripe corn + Lencho ki umeed + faith ka central question.

2️⃣ Barish se Hailstorm tak ⭐⭐⭐

  • Lencho farmer hai jo apne khet ko achhe se jaanta hai. Subah usne north-east ke aasman ko dekha, kyunki khet ko bas ek downpour ya shower chahiye tha.
  • Barish shuru hui to usne kaha ki ye raindrops nahi, new coins hain: bade drops ten-cent ke aur chhote five ke.
  • Phir tez hawa chali aur bade hailstones girne lage, jo silver coins jaise lagte the. Ek ghante tak hail giri, khet safed ho gaya (jaise namak se dhaka ho), pedon par ek patta nahi bacha aur corn poori tarah destroy ho gayi.
  • Lencho ne kaha ki locusts ki plague bhi isse zyada nahi chhodti. Raat sorrowful thi: "We'll all go hungry this year."

💡 Rain = new coins (hope), hail = silver coins jo fasal tabah kar deti hai; locusts se bhi bura nuksan.

3️⃣ Letter to God aur Postmaster ⭐⭐⭐

  • Ghar waalon ki ek hi umeed thi: help from God. Lencho ne socha ki God ki aankhein sab kuch dekhti hain, conscience ke andar ki cheez bhi.
  • Agle Sunday Lencho ne letter likha: sau pesos chahiye khet dobara bone aur fasal aane tak jeene ke liye. Envelope par likha To God aur khud town jaakar mailbox mein daal diya.
  • Postman hanste hue postmaster ke paas gaya. Postmaster pehle hansa, phir serious ho gaya: "What faith!" Usne Lencho ka faith na tootne dene ke liye jawab dene ka idea nikala.
  • Postmaster ne employees se paisa maanga, apni salary ka hissa diya, dosti-yaaron se bhi charity ke naam par liya, par sau pesos poore na ho paye. Usne aadhe se thoda zyada bheja aur letter par sirf ek word signature kiya: God.

💡 Faith, charity, postmaster ka kind idea; paisa sau se kam; signature 'God'.

4️⃣ Lencho ka Dusra Letter aur Irony ⭐⭐⭐

  • Agle Sunday Lencho jaldi aaya. Postman ne letter diya, postmaster door se santosh se dekh raha tha. Lencho ko paise dekhkar zara bhi surprise nahi hua, itna confidence tha.
  • Par paise ginte hi wo naraaz ho gaya: God galti nahi kar sakta aur na hi maangi cheez se mana kar sakta tha.
  • Usne dusra letter likha: sirf seventy pesos mile, baaki bhej do, par mail se nahi, kyunki post office ke employees crooks hain.
  • Irony (chapter ki definition: situation ka unexpected pehlu, jo expected ka ulta hota hai): jinhone madad ki, unhi ko Lencho crooks keh raha hai.

💡 Lencho ko sirf 70 mile, usne postal employees ko crooks kaha: yahi irony hai.

5️⃣ Characters aur Themes ⭐⭐⭐

  • Lencho: mehnati, faith mein unquestioning aur seedha. Wo bahut mehnat karta tha, par likhna jaanta tha. Chapter ke anusar uska faith aisa hai ki God galti hi nahi kar sakta.
  • Postmaster: amiable, hasne wala par jaldi serious; faith ki qadar karta hai aur act of charity karta hai.
  • Conflict ke do roop (chapter ke anusar): insaan aur nature (hailstorm) ke beech, aur insaan aur insaan ke beech (Lencho ka post office staff par shak).
  • Theme: faith ki taqat, human kindness, aur ek aadmi ki seedhi soch ki irony.

💡 Lencho = faith + sadgi; postmaster = kindness; conflict = nature vs man, man vs man.

6️⃣ Poem: Dust of Snow ⭐⭐⭐

  • Poet Robert Frost. Ek crow hemlock tree se poet par snow ki dhool jhaad deta hai. Isse poet ka mood badal jata hai aur din ka kuch hissa, jis par use afsos tha, bach jata hai.
  • Chapter ke anusar poem ek simple moment dikhati hai jiska larger significance hai; Frost ka quote: "A little thing touches a larger thing."
  • Crow aur hemlock aam taur par poetic nahi maane jaate; chapter inhe joy ya sorrow ke representation ke sawaal ke roop mein poochta hai.

💡 Crow + hemlock + snow ki dhool = chhoti cheez se mood change.

7️⃣ Poem: Fire and Ice ⭐⭐⭐

  • Poet Robert Frost. Kuch kehte hain duniya fire se khatam hogi, kuch kehte hain ice se. Poet ne desire ka swaad liya hai, to wo fire ke saath hai.
  • Par agar duniya do baar nasht hoti, to poet kehta hai ki usne hate itna jaana hai ki destruction ke liye ice bhi great hai aur kaafi hoga.
  • Chapter ke questions mein fire aur ice ke liye greed, cruelty, hatred, indifference jaise ideas diye gaye hain; rhyme scheme poem ke text se: abaabcbcb.

💡 Fire = desire; ice = hate (poet ke words); rhyme scheme abaabcbcb.

1️⃣ परिवेश और लेखक ⭐⭐

  • विषय-सूची में लेखक का नाम G.L. Fuentes दिया है। अध्याय के पाठ में उनकी जीवनी नहीं है, इसलिए यहाँ नहीं दी गई।
  • परिवेश: पूरी घाटी में अकेला घर, एक नीची पहाड़ी की चोटी पर, जहाँ से नदी और पके मक्के का खेत दिखता था, जिसमें फूल थे जो अच्छी फ़सल का वादा करते थे।
  • पढ़ने से पहले का प्रश्न: हमें किस पर विश्वास रखना चाहिए? क्या लेंचो का पत्र ईश्वर तक पहुँचेगा? क्या ईश्वर पैसे भेजेगा?

💡 पहाड़ी पर अकेला घर, पका मक्का, लेंचो की उम्मीद और विश्वास का केंद्रीय प्रश्न।

2️⃣ बारिश से ओलावृष्टि तक ⭐⭐⭐

  • लेंचो किसान है जो अपने खेतों को अच्छी तरह जानता है। उसने उत्तर-पूर्व का आसमान देखा क्योंकि खेत को बस एक झड़ी या कम से कम बौछार चाहिए थी।
  • बारिश आई तो उसने कहा कि ये बूँदें नहीं, नए सिक्के हैं: बड़ी बूँदें दस सेंट के सिक्के और छोटी पाँच के।
  • फिर तेज़ हवा चली और बड़े ओले गिरे, जो चाँदी के सिक्कों जैसे दिखते थे। एक घंटे तक ओले गिरे, खेत नमक से ढका जैसा सफ़ेद हो गया, पेड़ों पर एक पत्ता न बचा और मक्का पूरी तरह नष्ट हो गई।
  • लेंचो ने कहा कि टिड्डियों का हमला भी इससे ज़्यादा नहीं छोड़ता। रात दुखभरी थी: "इस साल हम सब भूखे रहेंगे।"

💡 बारिश = नए सिक्के (आशा), ओले = चाँदी के सिक्के जो फ़सल बर्बाद करते हैं।

3️⃣ ईश्वर को पत्र और पोस्टमास्टर ⭐⭐⭐

  • घर वालों की एक ही उम्मीद थी: ईश्वर की मदद। लेंचो ने सोचा कि ईश्वर की आँखें सब कुछ देखती हैं, अंतरात्मा के भीतर की बात भी।
  • अगले रविवार लेंचो ने पत्र लिखा: खेत फिर बोने और फ़सल आने तक जीने के लिए सौ पेसो चाहिए। लिफ़ाफ़े पर लिखा To God और ख़ुद शहर जाकर डाक-पेटी में डाल दिया।
  • डाकिया हँसते हुए पोस्टमास्टर के पास गया। पोस्टमास्टर पहले हँसा, फिर गंभीर हो गया: "कितना विश्वास!" लेंचो का विश्वास न टूटे, इसलिए उसने पत्र का जवाब देने का विचार किया।
  • उसने कर्मचारियों से पैसे माँगे, अपनी तनख़्वाह का हिस्सा दिया और दोस्तों से दान लिया, पर सौ पेसो पूरे न हुए। उसने आधे से कुछ ज़्यादा भेजे और पत्र पर एक ही शब्द से दस्तख़त किए: God।

💡 विश्वास, दान, पोस्टमास्टर की भली योजना; सौ से कम पेसो; हस्ताक्षर 'God'।

4️⃣ लेंचो का दूसरा पत्र और विडंबना ⭐⭐⭐

  • अगले रविवार लेंचो जल्दी आया। डाकिये ने पत्र दिया और पोस्टमास्टर संतोष से देख रहा था। लेंचो को पैसे देखकर ज़रा भी हैरानी नहीं हुई।
  • पर पैसे गिनते ही वह नाराज़ हो गया: ईश्वर ग़लती नहीं कर सकता था और न माँगी चीज़ से इनकार कर सकता था।
  • उसने दूसरा पत्र लिखा: सिर्फ़ सत्तर पेसो मिले, बाक़ी भेज दो, पर डाक से नहीं, क्योंकि डाकघर के कर्मचारी बदमाश हैं।
  • विडंबना (अध्याय की परिभाषा: स्थिति का अप्रत्याशित पहलू, जो अपेक्षा के उलट हो): जिन्होंने मदद की, लेंचो उन्हीं को बदमाश कह रहा है।

💡 लेंचो को सिर्फ़ 70 मिले और उसने डाक कर्मचारियों को बदमाश कहा: यही विडंबना है।

5️⃣ पात्र और विषय ⭐⭐⭐

  • लेंचो: मेहनती, सीधा और विश्वास में बिना सवाल करने वाला। वह जानवर की तरह खेत में काम करता था पर लिखना जानता था। कहानी में उसका विश्वास ऐसा है कि ईश्वर ग़लती कर ही नहीं सकता।
  • पोस्टमास्टर: मोटा, मिलनसार आदमी जो हँसता है फिर गंभीर हो जाता है, विश्वास की क़द्र करता है और दान का काम करता है।
  • संघर्ष के दो रूप (अध्याय के अनुसार): मनुष्य और प्रकृति (ओलावृष्टि) के बीच, और मनुष्य और मनुष्य के बीच (लेंचो का डाक कर्मचारियों पर शक)।
  • विषय: विश्वास की ताक़त, मानवीय दया और एक सीधे आदमी की सोच की विडंबना।

💡 लेंचो: विश्वास और सादगी; पोस्टमास्टर: दया; संघर्ष: मनुष्य बनाम प्रकृति, मनुष्य बनाम मनुष्य।

6️⃣ कविता: Dust of Snow ⭐⭐⭐

  • कवि: रॉबर्ट फ़्रॉस्ट। एक कौआ हेमलॉक पेड़ से बर्फ़ की धूल कवि पर झाड़ देता है। इससे कवि का मन बदल जाता है और दिन का वह हिस्सा बच जाता है जिस पर उसे पछतावा था।
  • अध्याय के अनुसार कविता एक साधारण क्षण दिखाती है जिसका बड़ा महत्व है; फ़्रॉस्ट का कथन: "A little thing touches a larger thing."
  • कौआ और हेमलॉक आम तौर पर काव्यात्मक नहीं माने जाते; अध्याय पूछता है कि वे खुशी के प्रतीक हैं या दुख के।

💡 कौआ, हेमलॉक और बर्फ़ की धूल: छोटी चीज़ से मन बदलता है।

7️⃣ कविता: Fire and Ice ⭐⭐⭐

  • कवि: रॉबर्ट फ़्रॉस्ट। कुछ कहते हैं दुनिया आग से ख़त्म होगी, कुछ कहते हैं बर्फ़ से। कवि ने इच्छा का स्वाद चखा है, इसलिए वह आग के पक्ष में है।
  • पर अगर दुनिया को दो बार नष्ट होना पड़े, तो कवि कहता है कि उसने नफ़रत इतनी जानी है कि विनाश के लिए बर्फ़ भी महान है और काफ़ी होगी।
  • अध्याय के प्रश्नों में आग और बर्फ़ के लिए लालच, क्रूरता, घृणा, उदासीनता जैसे विचार दिए हैं; पाठ से तुक-योजना: abaabcbcb।

💡 आग: इच्छा; बर्फ़: नफ़रत (कवि के शब्दों में); तुक-योजना abaabcbcb।

1️⃣ Setting and author ⭐⭐

  • The contents page names the author as G.L. Fuentes. The chapter text gives no biography, so none is given here.
  • Setting: the only house in the entire valley, on the crest of a low hill, with a view of the river and a field of ripe corn dotted with flowers that always promised a good harvest.
  • The Before You Read question: what should we put our faith in? Does Lencho's letter reach God, and does God send the money?

💡 Lonely house on a hill, ripe corn, Lencho's hope, and faith as the central question.

2️⃣ From rain to hailstorm ⭐⭐⭐

  • Lencho is a farmer who knows his fields intimately. He watched the north-east sky because the field needed only a downpour or at least a shower.
  • When the rain came he said the drops were not raindrops but new coins: the big drops ten-cent pieces and the little ones fives.
  • Then a strong wind blew and very large hailstones fell, which also resembled silver coins. For an hour the hail fell; the field turned white as if covered with salt, no leaf remained on the trees and the corn was totally destroyed.
  • Lencho said that a plague of locusts would have left more than the hail. The night was sorrowful: "We'll all go hungry this year."

💡 Rain as new coins (hope), hail as silver coins that destroy the crop, worse than locusts.

3️⃣ The letter to God and the postmaster ⭐⭐⭐

  • The family had a single hope: help from God. Lencho thought of God, whose eyes see everything, even what is deep in one's conscience.
  • The next Sunday Lencho wrote a letter asking for a hundred pesos to sow the field again and live until the crop came. He wrote To God on the envelope and mailed it himself in town.
  • The postman laughed and showed it to the postmaster. The postmaster laughed, then turned serious: "What faith!" To avoid shaking Lencho's faith he decided to answer the letter.
  • He asked his employees for money, gave part of his own salary, and got friends to give for charity, but could not reach a hundred pesos. He sent a little more than half, with a letter signed with one word: God.

💡 Faith, charity, the postmaster's kind plan; less than a hundred pesos; signed 'God'.

4️⃣ Lencho's second letter and the irony ⭐⭐⭐

  • The next Sunday Lencho came early. The postman handed him the letter while the postmaster watched with the contentment of a man who has done a good deed. Lencho showed not the slightest surprise at the money.
  • But he became angry when he counted it: God could not have made a mistake, nor denied him what he asked.
  • He wrote again: only seventy pesos reached him, send the rest, but not through the mail, because the post office employees are crooks.
  • Irony (the chapter's definition: an unexpected aspect of a situation, the opposite of what is expected): the people who helped him are the ones he calls crooks.

💡 Lencho got only 70 and called the post office staff crooks: that is the irony.

5️⃣ Characters and themes ⭐⭐⭐

  • Lencho: hard-working, plain and unquestioning in faith. He worked like an animal in the fields yet knew how to write. In the story his faith is so complete that God could not make a mistake.
  • Postmaster: a fat, amiable man who laughs, then turns serious, respects the faith and acts with charity.
  • Two kinds of conflict (as the chapter puts it): humans against nature (the hailstorm) and humans against humans (Lencho's suspicion of the post office staff).
  • Themes: the power of faith, human kindness, and the irony of a simple man's reasoning.

💡 Lencho: faith and simplicity; postmaster: kindness; conflict: man versus nature, man versus man.

6️⃣ Poem: Dust of Snow ⭐⭐⭐

  • Poet: Robert Frost. A crow shakes the dust of snow from a hemlock tree onto the speaker. This changes his mood and saves part of a day he had rued.
  • The chapter says the poem presents a simple moment with a larger significance, and quotes Frost: "A little thing touches a larger thing."
  • A crow and a hemlock are not the usual poetic choices; the chapter asks whether they stand for joy or sorrow.

💡 Crow, hemlock and the dust of snow: a small thing changes a mood.

7️⃣ Poem: Fire and Ice ⭐⭐⭐

  • Poet: Robert Frost. Some say the world will end in fire, some in ice. From what he has tasted of desire, the speaker holds with those who favour fire.
  • But if it had to perish twice, he knows enough of hate to say that for destruction ice is also great and would suffice.
  • The chapter offers ideas such as greed, cruelty, hatred and indifference for fire and ice; the rhyme scheme, read from the text, is abaabcbcb.

💡 Fire: desire; ice: hate (the speaker's words); rhyme scheme abaabcbcb.

Story

  • Lencho, a farmer, in a lone house
  • Rain like new coins, then hail
  • Corn totally destroyed
  • Single hope: help from God

Letter

  • Asks God for 100 pesos
  • Postmaster moved by faith
  • Collects money, sends a little more than half
  • Signed: God

Irony

  • Calls the post office staff crooks

Practice - 9 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Thinking about the Text 1: who does Lencho have complete faith in, and which sentences show it
Answer dekho

God. The story says that all through the night Lencho thought only of his one hope, the help of God, whose eyes see everything. He writes To God on the envelope, and when the money comes he shows no surprise because God could not have made a mistake.

Q2 Thinking about the Text 2: why does the postmaster send money, and why does he sign God
Answer dekho

He is struck by Lencho's faith and does not want to shake it, so he decides to answer the letter and gathers money from his employees, his own salary and friends. He signs God so that the reply appears to come from God.

Q3 Thinking about the Text 3: did Lencho try to find out who sent the money
Answer dekho

No. The story says he showed not the slightest surprise, such was his confidence that the money came from God.

Q4 Thinking about the Text 4: who does Lencho think took the rest, and what is the irony
Answer dekho

He thinks the post office employees are crooks who took the rest, since only seventy pesos reached him. The irony, by the chapter's definition, is that the people who helped him by collecting the money are the ones he accuses.

Q5 Thinking about the Text 5: are there people like Lencho, and what kind of person is he
Answer dekho

This is an open opinion question and no key is given. The story shows him as unquestioning in his faith, plain and hard-working,. He is also angry when he counts the money, because he expected the full amount.

Q6 Thinking about the Text 6: two kinds of conflict
Answer dekho

Humans against nature: the hailstorm destroys the corn. Humans against humans: Lencho distrusts the post office employees and accuses them of being crooks.

Q7 Dust of Snow: what changes the poet's mood
Answer dekho

A crow shakes the dust of snow from a hemlock tree onto the poet. This changes his mood and saves part of a day he had rued.

Q8 Fire and Ice: rhyme scheme
Answer dekho

Read from the poem lines: fire, ice, desire, fire, twice, hate, ice, great, suffice. The scheme is abaabcbcb.
