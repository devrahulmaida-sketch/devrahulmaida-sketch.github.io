Long Notes - Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye - aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Anne Frank aur Diary ka Background ⭐⭐⭐

  • Chapter ke Before You Read ke anusar Anne Frank ek German-born Jewish ladki thi jisne Amsterdam mein German occupation ke dauran chhupte hue likha. Ye bhi chapter ka kathan hai ki parivar ne July 1942 mein Otto Frank ki office building ke hidden rooms mein panah li.
  • Chapter ke anusar do saal chhupne ke baad group ko dhokha diya gaya, Anne ki Bergen-Belsen mein typhus se maut hui aur sirf pita Otto Frank bache. Diary Anne ko thirteenth birthday par mili thi aur English mein The Diary of a Young Girl naam se chhapi.
  • Chapter ke anusar diary 12 June 1942 se 1 August 1944 tak ki zindagi darj karti hai. Ye extract us diary se hai (slight adaptation ke saath).

💡 Source: chapter ka Before You Read; ye sab chapter ke statements hain.

2️⃣ Kyun Diary aur Kitty ⭐⭐⭐

  • Anne ko diary likhna strange experience lagta hai: usne pehle kuch nahi likha tha aur use lagta hai ki ek terah saal ki schoolgirl ke vichaar mein kisi ko dilchaspi nahi hogi. Par use dil ki baatein off her chest karni hain.
  • Kahawat: "Paper has more patience than people." Uske paas pyaar karne wale parents, sixteen saal ki behen aur lagbhag tees log jinhe wo dost kehti hai, par koi one true friend nahi jise wo confide kar sake.
  • Isliye usne diary ko apna dost bana liya aur use 'Kitty' naam diya. Wo sirf facts nahi likhna chahti.

💡 Paper ka patience, ek true friend ki kami, diary = Kitty.

3️⃣ Anne ke Parivar ka Sketch ⭐⭐⭐

  • Anne brief sketch deti hai taaki Kitty uski kahaniyan samajh sake. Pita ne chhattis saal ki umar mein shaadi ki aur mother Edith Hollander Frank paiyees saal ki thi. Behen Margot 1926 mein Frankfurt mein paida hui; Anne ka janm 12 June 1929 ko hua.
  • Pita 1933 mein Holland gaye, mother September mein; Margot December mein aur Anne February mein. Anne ne Montessori nursery school mein shuru kiya aur sixth form mein uski teacher headmistress Mrs Kuperus thi.
  • Grandma 1941 mein beemar padi aur January 1942 mein guzar gayi; Anne use ab bhi yaad karti aur pyaar karti hai. Isse Anne ka grandmother ke prati pyaar dikhta hai.

💡 Parivar ka sketch, Holland 1933, grandmother ka pyaar, diary ki dedication 20 June 1942.

4️⃣ Class, Exams aur Mr Keesing ⭐⭐⭐

  • Saturday, 20 June 1942: class result ke meeting se quaking in its boots thi, kyunki teachers decide karte hain ki kaun agle form mein jayega. Anne ko sirf maths ke baare mein pakka nahi tha.
  • Maths ke teacher Mr Keesing uski bahut baat karne ki aadat se naraaz the aur unhone extra homework diya: essay 'A Chatterbox'.
  • Anne ne tark diya ki baat karna student ka trait hai aur wo kabu mein rakhne ki koshish karegi, par inherited trait (uski mother bhi utna hi bolti thi) ko theek nahi kar sakti.

💡 Maths ka darr, teachers unpredictable, chatterbox essay.

5️⃣ Teen Essays aur Ant ⭐⭐⭐

  • Doosra essay 'An Incorrigible Chatterbox'; teesra 'Quack, Quack, Quack, Said Mistress Chatterbox'. Class hans padi.
  • Anne ki dost Sanne, jo poetry mein achhi hai, ne essay verse mein likhne mein madad ki: ek mother duck aur father swan ke teen ducklings jo zyada quack karne par father ne kaat liye.
  • Mr Keesing ne mazaak sahi tarah liya, poem class ko aur kai doosri classes ko padhi, aur Anne ko baat karne ki ijazat mil gayi. Ant Yours, Anne.

💡 Teen essays, verse mein poem, Mr Keesing ka hasna.

6️⃣ Poem: Amanda! ⭐⭐⭐

  • Poet Robin Klein. Odd stanzas (1, 3, 5, 7) mein koi Amanda ko roktok karta hai: nails mat kaato, kandhe mat jhukao, homework, kamra, joote, chocolate, acne, aur sulking.
  • Bracket wale stanzas (2, 4, 6) mein Amanda ki apni imagination hai: mermaid jo shant sea mein khushi se bahti hai, orphan jo gali mein nange pair ghoomti hai aur Rapunzel jo apne bright hair kabhi neeche nahi girayegi.
  • Poem dikhati hai ki Amanda azaadi ki khwahish rakhti hai jabki use har waqt tokha jaata hai (Amanda ki umar chapter mein nahi di gayi).

💡 Roktok vs imagination; mermaid, orphan, Rapunzel.

1️⃣ ऐनी फ़्रैंक और पृष्ठभूमि ⭐⭐⭐

  • अध्याय के Before You Read के अनुसार ऐनी फ़्रैंक जर्मनी में जन्मी यहूदी लड़की थी जिसने एम्स्टर्डम में जर्मन क़ब्ज़े के दौरान छिपकर लिखा। अध्याय यह भी कहता है कि परिवार जुलाई 1942 में पिता ऑटो फ़्रैंक की दफ़्तर-इमारत के गुप्त कमरों में छिप गया।
  • अध्याय के अनुसार दो साल बाद समूह के साथ विश्वासघात हुआ, ऐनी की बर्गन-बेल्सन में टाइफ़स से मृत्यु हुई और केवल पिता ऑटो फ़्रैंक बचे। डायरी उसे तेरहवें जन्मदिन पर मिली और अंग्रेज़ी में The Diary of a Young Girl नाम से छपी।
  • अध्याय के अनुसार डायरी 12 जून 1942 से 1 अगस्त 1944 तक का जीवन दर्ज करती है। यह अंश उसी से है (थोड़े रूपांतर के साथ)।

💡 स्रोत: अध्याय का Before You Read; ये अध्याय के कथन हैं।

2️⃣ डायरी क्यों, और किटी ⭐⭐⭐

  • ऐनी को डायरी लिखना अजीब अनुभव लगता है: उसने पहले कभी नहीं लिखा और उसे लगता है कि एक तेरह साल की स्कूली लड़की के विचारों में किसी की रुचि नहीं होगी। पर उसे मन की बातें बाहर निकालनी हैं।
  • कहावत: "Paper has more patience than people." उसके पास प्यार करने वाले माता-पिता, सोलह साल की बहन और लगभग तीस लोग जिन्हें वह दोस्त कहती है, पर कोई सच्चा दोस्त नहीं जिससे सब कह सके।
  • इसलिए उसने डायरी को अपना दोस्त बनाया और उसे 'किटी' नाम दिया। वह केवल तथ्य नहीं लिखना चाहती।

💡 काग़ज़ का धैर्य, सच्चे दोस्त की कमी, डायरी = किटी।

3️⃣ ऐनी के परिवार का ख़ाका ⭐⭐⭐

  • ऐनी संक्षिप्त ख़ाका देती है ताकि किटी उसकी कहानियाँ समझ सके। पिता ने छत्तीस की उम्र में शादी की और माँ एडिथ हॉलैंडर फ़्रैंक पच्चीस की थीं। बहन मार्गोट 1926 में फ़्रैंकफ़र्ट में जन्मी; ऐनी का जन्म 12 जून 1929 को हुआ।
  • पिता 1933 में हॉलैंड गए, माँ सितंबर में, मार्गोट दिसंबर में और ऐनी फ़रवरी में। उसने मॉन्टेसरी नर्सरी स्कूल से शुरुआत की और छठी कक्षा में उसकी शिक्षिका प्रधानाध्यापिका श्रीमती कुपेरस थीं।
  • दादी 1941 में बीमार पड़ीं और जनवरी 1942 में चल बसीं; ऐनी कहती है कि वह अब भी उन्हें याद करती और प्यार करती है। इससे दादी के प्रति उसका प्यार दिखता है।

💡 परिवार का ख़ाका, 1933 में हॉलैंड, दादी से प्यार, 20 जून 1942 को डायरी का समर्पण।

4️⃣ कक्षा, परीक्षा और श्री कीसिंग ⭐⭐⭐

  • शनिवार, 20 जून 1942: शिक्षकों की बैठक से पहले, जो तय करती है कि कौन अगली कक्षा में जाएगा, पूरी कक्षा डर से काँप रही है। ऐनी को केवल गणित को लेकर पक्का नहीं है।
  • गणित के शिक्षक श्री कीसिंग उसके ज़्यादा बोलने से नाराज़ थे और अतिरिक्त गृहकार्य दिया: निबंध 'A Chatterbox'।
  • ऐनी ने तर्क दिया कि बोलना विद्यार्थी का गुण है, वह उसे क़ाबू में रखने की कोशिश करेगी, पर यह विरासत में मिला गुण है (उसकी माँ भी उतना ही बोलती थीं) और ठीक नहीं हो सकता।

💡 गणित का डर, अप्रत्याशित शिक्षक, बातूनी निबंध।

5️⃣ तीन निबंध और अंत ⭐⭐⭐

  • दूसरा निबंध 'An Incorrigible Chatterbox' था; तीसरा 'Quack, Quack, Quack, Said Mistress Chatterbox'। कक्षा हँस पड़ी।
  • उसकी सहेली सान्ने, जो कविता में अच्छी है, ने निबंध कविता में लिखने में मदद की: एक माँ बत्तख़ और पिता हंस के तीन बत्तख़ के बच्चे जिन्हें पिता ने ज़्यादा क्वैक करने पर काट डाला।
  • श्री कीसिंग ने मज़ाक सही ढंग से लिया, कविता उसकी और कई और कक्षाओं को पढ़ी और ऐनी को बोलने की अनुमति मिल गई। अंत में Yours, Anne।

💡 तीन निबंध, कविता में निबंध, श्री कीसिंग की हँसी।

6️⃣ कविता: Amanda! ⭐⭐⭐

  • कवि रॉबिन क्लाइन। विषम छंदों (1, 3, 5, 7) में कोई अमांडा को टोकता रहता है: नाख़ून, कंधे, गृहकार्य, कमरा, जूते, चॉकलेट, मुँहासे, मुँह फुलाना।
  • कोष्ठक वाले छंद (2, 4, 6) अमांडा की अपनी कल्पना हैं: शांत समुद्र में तैरती जलपरी, नंगे पाँव गली में घूमती अनाथ, और रैपंज़ल जो अपने चमकीले बाल कभी नीचे नहीं गिराएगी।
  • कविता एक ऐसी लड़की दिखाती है जो आज़ादी चाहती है जबकि उसे हर समय बताया जाता है कि क्या करना है (अध्याय अमांडा की उम्र नहीं बताता)।

💡 टोका-टाकी बनाम कल्पना; जलपरी, अनाथ, रैपंज़ल।

1️⃣ Anne Frank and the background ⭐⭐⭐

  • The chapter's Before You Read says Anne Frank was a German-born Jewish girl who wrote while in hiding in Amsterdam during the German occupation. It also states that the family went into hiding in July 1942 in hidden rooms of her father Otto Frank's office building.
  • The chapter says that after two years the group was betrayed, Anne died of typhus in Bergen-Belsen and only her father Otto Frank survived. The diary was a gift for her thirteenth birthday and was published in English as The Diary of a Young Girl.
  • The chapter says the diary covers 12 June 1942 to 1 August 1944. This extract is from it, with slight adaptation.

💡 Source: the chapter's Before You Read; these are the chapter's statements.

2️⃣ Why a diary, and Kitty ⭐⭐⭐

  • Anne finds writing a diary a strange experience: she has never written before and thinks nobody will be interested in the musings of a thirteen-year-old schoolgirl. But she needs to get things off her chest.
  • The saying she recalls: "Paper has more patience than people." She has loving parents, a sixteen-year-old sister and about thirty people she calls friends, yet no one true friend to confide in.
  • So she makes the diary her friend and calls it 'Kitty'. She does not want to jot down only facts.

💡 Paper's patience, no true friend, diary = Kitty.

3️⃣ A sketch of Anne's family ⭐⭐⭐

  • Anne gives a brief sketch so that Kitty can understand her stories. Her father married at thirty-six and her mother, Edith Hollander Frank, was twenty-five. Her sister Margot was born in Frankfurt in 1926; Anne was born on 12 June 1929.
  • Her father emigrated to Holland in 1933, her mother followed in September, Margot in December and Anne in February. She began at a Montessori nursery school and in the sixth form her teacher was the headmistress Mrs Kuperus.
  • Her grandmother fell ill in 1941 and died in January 1942; Anne says she still thinks of her and loves her. This shows her love for her grandmother.

💡 Family sketch, Holland in 1933, love for her grandmother, the diary's dedication on 20 June 1942.

4️⃣ Class, exams and Mr Keesing ⭐⭐⭐

  • Saturday, 20 June 1942: the class is quaking in its boots before the teachers' meeting that decides who moves up. Anne is unsure only about maths.
  • The maths teacher Mr Keesing was annoyed with her for talking so much and gave extra homework: an essay titled 'A Chatterbox'.
  • Anne argued that talking is a student's trait, that she would try to control it, but that it is an inherited trait (her mother talked as much as she did) and could not be cured.

💡 Fear of maths, unpredictable teachers, the chatterbox essay.

5️⃣ Three essays and the end ⭐⭐⭐

  • The second essay was 'An Incorrigible Chatterbox'; the third 'Quack, Quack, Quack, Said Mistress Chatterbox'. The class roared.
  • Her friend Sanne, who is good at poetry, helped her write the essay in verse: a mother duck and a father swan with three ducklings bitten to death by the father because they quacked too much.
  • Mr Keesing took the joke the right way, read the poem to her class and several others, and Anne was allowed to talk. She signs off Yours, Anne.

💡 Three essays, the poem in verse, Mr Keesing laughs.

6️⃣ Poem: Amanda! ⭐⭐⭐

  • Poet Robin Klein. In the odd stanzas (1, 3, 5, 7) someone keeps correcting Amanda: nails, shoulders, homework, her room, shoes, chocolate, acne, sulking.
  • The bracketed stanzas (2, 4, 6) are Amanda's own imagination: a mermaid drifting in a calm sea, an orphan roaming the street with bare feet, and Rapunzel who will never let down her bright hair.
  • The poem shows a girl who longs for freedom while being told what to do all the time (the chapter does not state Amanda's age).

💡 Nagging versus imagination; mermaid, orphan, Rapunzel.

Practice - 13 Questions

Q1 Oral Comprehension Check: what makes writing in a diary a strange experience
Answer dekho

Anne has never written before and thinks nobody, neither she nor anyone else, will later be interested in the musings of a thirteen-year-old schoolgirl.

Q2 Oral Comprehension Check: why does Anne want to keep a diary
Answer dekho

She feels she has no one true friend and has a great need to get things off her chest; she wants the diary to be her friend, Kitty.

Q3 Oral Comprehension Check: why could she confide more in her diary than in people
Answer dekho

The chapter gives her saying that paper has more patience than people; she does not plan to let anyone read the notebook unless she finds a real friend, and with her friends she can talk only about ordinary everyday things.

Q4 Oral Comprehension Check: why does Anne give a brief sketch of her life
Answer dekho

Because no one would understand her stories to Kitty if she plunged right in.

Q5 Oral Comprehension Check: what tells you Anne loved her grandmother
Answer dekho

She says no one knows how often she thinks of her and still loves her, and Grandma's candle was lit at the birthday celebration.

Q6 Oral Comprehension Check: why was Mr Keesing annoyed, and what did he ask her to do
Answer dekho

He was annoyed because she talked so much; after several warnings he assigned extra homework, an essay on A Chatterbox.

Q7 Oral Comprehension Check: how did Anne justify being a chatterbox
Answer dekho

She argued that talking is a student's trait, that she would try to control it but could not cure it since her mother talked as much as she did, and that inherited traits cannot be helped.

Q8 Oral Comprehension Check: what made Mr Keesing allow Anne to talk
Answer dekho

Her verse essay about the ducks and the swan: he took the joke the right way, read it to the class and other classes, and after that allowed her to talk and gave no extra homework.

Q9 Thinking about the Text: what does Anne write in her first essay
Answer dekho

See above: the argument that talking is a student's trait, which she would try to control but never cure, as it is inherited from her mother.

Q10 Amanda!: Q3 why are stanzas 2, 4 and 6 in brackets
Answer dekho

They are Amanda's own thoughts and imagination, set apart from the spoken scolding of the other stanzas. This is read from the poem's layout.

Q11 Amanda!: Q5 and Q7 what could Amanda do as a mermaid, why Rapunzel
Answer dekho

As a mermaid she would drift blissfully in a calm emerald sea. As Rapunzel she imagines a tranquil life in a tower, never letting down her hair.
