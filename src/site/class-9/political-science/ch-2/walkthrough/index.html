Constitutional Design

संविधान निर्माण
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation ya video nahin.

1. Rules se trust

Source pp 18,22: Constitution ke basic roles.

Authority

Kaun decision lega aur government kaise banegi? Constitution basic framework deta hai.

Limits + aspirations

Rights aur power limits ke saath society ki aspirations bhi constitutional design ka part hain.

2. Historical South Africa

Source pp 19-22. Past case, not current-country advice.

Apartheid

Racial discrimination aur segregation ne non-white people ke rights deny kiye. Struggle aur negotiations new agreement ki background the.

Shared rules

Majority rule ke saath minority protection/basic rights par agreement. Durable trust ke liye supreme rules, sirf verbal promise nahin.

3. Indian making process

Source pp 23-25: broad agreement, real debate.

Earlier groundwork

Freedom movement ne democracy/rights/equality par broad consensus develop kiya; har detail already fixed nahin thi.

Assembly

Indirect election, varied opinions, Drafting Committee aur systematic debates. Adopted 26 November 1949; effective 26 January 1950.

4. Preamble vocabulary

Source pp 28-29: introductory values, not full legal definitions.

People + republic

Authority people aur representatives se; republic mein head of state elected hota hai, hereditary nahin.

Sovereign + secular

Sovereign: external dictation se freedom. Secular: no official religion, beliefs ke prati equal respect. Textbook-level explanation.

5. Values ko saath padho

Source p 29. Liberty unlimited permission nahin.

Justice + equality

Discrimination aur inequalities ko reduce karna; law ke saamne equality aur equal opportunity.

Liberty + fraternity

Unreasonable restrictions se freedom; fellow citizens ki dignity/belonging. Rights aur respect ko saath samjho.

6. Institutions + change

Source p 30: values ko practical form.

Institutions

Government formation, decisions ki authority, power ki limits aur citizen rights. Constitution values bhi hai, structure bhi.

Amendment

Constitution change ho sakta hai. Meri vyakhya: iska matlab ruler ki arbitrary rewriting nahin. Detailed current amendment procedure yahan cover nahin.
