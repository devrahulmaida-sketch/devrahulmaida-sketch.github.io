6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation ya video nahin.

1. Decision ka chain

Source pp 57-60: historical example, current reservation instructions nahin.

Policy

Cabinet policy direction decide karta hai; officer ka signature akela policy authorship nahin batata.

Implementation

Ministerial approval aur officials ki implementation ke baad order issue hota hai. Challenge par Court review kar sakti hai.

2. Rules ki value

Source pp 60-61: delay aur deliberation dono.

Continuity

Institutions ke roles aur procedures officeholders badalne par bhi government work ko continue rakhte hain.

Consultation

Meetings decision slow kar sakti hain, par more views aur hasty bad decisions ke against check bhi deti hain. Meri vyakhya: fastest decision always best nahin.

3. Parliament aur Houses

Source pp 61-63: naam se power mat guess karo.

Functions

Law-making, public money, government accountability aur debate Parliament ke important roles hain.

Confidence

Council of Ministers ko Lok Sabha majority support chahiye. Rajya Sabha ka legislative/state role phir bhi meaningful hai; Upper House name every matter mein supremacy nahin.

4. Executive ke do roles

Source pp 64-66: expertise aur accountability.

Political

Ministers policy objectives aur major decisions ke liye public ko answer dete hain. PM aur Cabinet coordination karte hain.

Permanent

Civil servants advice aur daily administration karte hain; political party badalne par sab officers automatically replace nahin hote. Expert advice aur political responsibility different hain.

5. PM aur President

Source pp 65,67-68: parliamentary support central.

Support

PM post par separate direct election nahin. President appointment karta hai, lekin Lok Sabha majority support matters; coalition bhi support de sakti hai.

Formal and real

President head of State, PM head of government. Advice, reconsideration aur majority constraints matter karte hain; this scene all bill/legal procedures ka guide nahin.

6. Judicial review

Source pp 69-70: independent scrutiny.

Review

Challenged law/action ki constitutional validity examine karna judicial review hai. Har law ki prior Supreme Court approval required nahin.

Independence

Court government ke direction par verdict nahin deti. Government se agree karna dependence ka automatic proof nahin; disagree ya modification ki freedom important hai.
