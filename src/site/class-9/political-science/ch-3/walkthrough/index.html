Electoral Politics

चुनावी राजनीति
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation ya video nahin.

1. Representative choice

Source pp 34-36: large democracy mein choice aur change.

Choose

Voters apne representatives, government direction aur policies ke beech choice karte hain.

Change

Regular elections mein performance par reward/punishment possible. Haryana 1987 historical example, har incumbent ki defeat ki guarantee nahin.

2. Poll se aage

Source pp 36-37: democratic election ki conditions.

Equal + real

One person, one vote, equal value. Contest freedom aur real alternatives chahiye.

Regular + free

Regular intervals; preferred choice elected; free/fair expression. Poll hona alone democracy prove nahin karta.

3. Competition ka tradeoff

Source pp 37-38: costs aur incentives.

Costs

Divisions, allegations, dirty tricks aur short-term pressure. Chapter inko ignore nahin karta.

Incentive

Voters reward/punish kar sakte hain. Meri vyakhya: incentive guarantee nahin; fair rules aur genuine choice matter.

4. Area aur list

Source pp 38-44: representation aur preparation.

Constituency

Area ke voters representative elect karte hain. Reserved candidacy ≠ sirf us community ka vote. Current boundaries/seat numbers yahan nahin.

Roll + nomination

Roll eligible voters ki list, nomination candidate entry. Education disclosure ≠ compulsory graduate degree. Current ID/forms/deadlines ka guide nahin.

5. Campaign to count

Source pp 44-46: discuss, vote, count.

Fair campaign

Policies aur candidates par debate. Bribery, threats, government-resource misuse equal competition damage karte hain. Law aur Model Code identical lists nahin.

Counting

Described constituency contest mein highest votes wala wins; >50% always necessary nahin. Turnout aur candidate vote share alag denominators hain.

6. Fairness + challenges

Source pp 47-52: multiple evidence, not one magic test.

Assess

Independent EC, participation aur result acceptance examine karo. High turnout ya incumbent defeat alone perfect fairness prove nahin karta.

Reform

Money, criminal connections, family dominance, limited alternatives aur small-party disadvantages. Textbook account, not current-election/legal verdict.
