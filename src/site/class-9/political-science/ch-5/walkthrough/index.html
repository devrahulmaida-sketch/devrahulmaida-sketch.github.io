Democratic Rights

लोकतांत्रिक अधिकार
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation ya video nahin.

1. Majority ki limit

Source pp 74-78: historical cases, present country profiles nahin.

Election

Election important hai, lekin elected government bhi rights ko ignore nahin kar sakti.

Protection

Rights dignity aur minorities ko protect karte hain. Meri vyakhya: majority support unlimited power nahin.

2. Claim se right

Source pp 77-78: wish aur right same nahin.

Reasonable

Claim others ke equal claims ke compatible ho. Mera benefit kisi aur ki equal dignity cancel nahin karta.

Recognition

Source rights ko society recognised aur law sanctioned claims batata hai. Moral claims existing legal rights se wider ho sakte hain.

3. Equal opportunity

Source pp 79-80: identical aur fair hamesha same nahin.

Rule of law

Official ya ruler law se above nahin. Discrimination aur untouchability equal dignity deny karte hain.

Support

Barriers kam karne ke liye special support equal opportunity ko help kar sakta hai. Current quota/eligibility advice nahin.

4. Freedom with respect

Source pp 81-84: unlimited licence nahin.

Peaceful

Criticism, peaceful assembly, association, movement aur occupation democratic freedoms hain. Violence ko peaceful dissent se confuse mat karo.

Dignity

Exploitation se protection aur religious freedom others ke rights respect karne se linked hain. Religious freedom coercion ka licence nahin.

5. Identity aur culture

Source pp 84-85: minority context matters.

Conserve

Distinct language/culture conserve karna aur minority educational institutions ka protection identity ko space deta hai.

Context

Language group ek state mein majority aur doosri mein minority ho sakta hai. Detailed admission law is scene mein nahin.

6. Rights ko force

Source pp 85-88: remedies aur expansion.

Remedies

Constitutional Remedies rights enforce karne se linked hain. Independent judiciary important hai; NHRC inquiry court punishment nahin.

Expand

Law, interpretation aur struggles se rights expand ho sakte hain. All rights Fundamental Rights nahin; dated list ko current privacylaw key mat banao.
