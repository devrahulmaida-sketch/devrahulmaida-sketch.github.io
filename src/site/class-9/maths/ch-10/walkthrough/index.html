Circles

वृत्त
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation ya video nahin.

1. Terms alag rakho

Boundary aur region same nahin.

Circle

Centre se fixed distance par planar points: boundary (p 169).

Regions

Circle+interior=circular region. Segment arc+chord; sector arc+two radii (pp 169-170).

2. Chord aur centre angle

Same/congruent circles ki condition.

Forward

Equal chords⇒equal central angles. Radius triangles SSS (pp 172-173).

Converse

Equal central angles⇒equal chords; equal radii ke saath SAS. Different radii par shortcut nahin.

3. Perpendicular aur midpoint

Zero segment ko line mat banao.

Bisects

Centre ka chord par perpendicular chord ko do equal parts mein baantta hai (p 173).

Converse caution

Non-diameter chord ka midpoint centre se join karo: perpendicular. Diameter midpoint centre hi hai; OM direction define nahin karta (p 174; meri vyakhya).

4. Circle aur distance

Non-collinear aur perpendicular.

Three points

Teen distinct non-collinear points: exactly one circle. Teen distinct collinear: none (pp 174-175).

Equal distance

Same/congruent circles mein equal chords⇔equal perpendicular centre-distances (pp 176-178).

5. Arc aur angles

Chosen arc ko identify karo.

Double angle

Chosen arc centre par double angle deta hai, vertex remaining circle par. Major arc mein reflex central angle ho sakta hai (pp 180-181).

Same segment

Same segment mein angles equal; semicircle 90°. Opposite segments ko mix mat karo (pp 181-182).

6. Cyclic quadrilateral

Opposite, adjacent nahin.

Opposite angles

Four vertices same circle par: opposite angle pair sum 180°; converse bhi true (pp 182-183).

Rectangle proof

Cyclic parallelogram: opposite angles equal+supplementary. Har angle 90°, isliye rectangle (Ex 10.5 Q 12,p 186).
