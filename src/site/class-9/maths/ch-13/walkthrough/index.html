Surface Areas and Volumes

पृष्ठीय क्षेत्रफल और आयतन
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation ya video nahin.

1. Measure kya hai?

Surface aur space ko alag rakho.

Area

Covering uses square units. Cuboid total 2(lb+bh+hl), cube 6 a² (pp 210-211).

Volume

Occupied space uses cubic units. Capacity is interior fill volume (pp 226-227).1 m³=1000 litres (p 228).

2. Faces count karo

Question decides covered boundary.

Cuboid

LSA 2(l+b)h excludes top/bottom (p 211). Open-top box:lb+2 h(l+b), our clarification with p 213 Q 1.

Cylinder

Curved 2πrh; closed total 2πr(h+r), because two ends add 2πr² (p 215). Diameter d gives r=d/2.

3. Cone mein two heights

Right circular cone only.

Surface

Slant l satisfies l²=r²+h². CSA = πrl; TSA = πr(l+r) (pp 219-220).

Volume

V=(1/3)πr²h uses perpendicular h. Same r AND h as cylinder implies volume one-third (pp 231-232).

4. Hemisphere ka base

Half volume does not mean half total area.

Areas

Sphere 4πr²; hemisphere curved 2πr², total 3πr² (pp 223-224). Flat base matters.

Volumes

Solid sphere(4/3)πr³; hemisphere(2/3)πr³ (pp 234-235). Both use cubic units.

5. Volume ka setup

Common units first.

Prisms

Cuboid lbh, cube a³, cylinder πr²h (pp 226-229). Base area × perpendicular height.

Tank

Ex 13.5 Q 2:6×5×4.5=135 m³=135000 litres (p 228). Real capacity uses inner dimensions; our clarification.

6. Calculation audit

Approximation ko exact π mat bolo.

π and units

π irrational; 22/7 or 3.14 are approximations (p 216). Square units for area, cubic for volume; question convention follow karo.

Independent check

Hemisphere r 10 cm, π3.14:curved 628 cm²+base 314 cm²=total 942 cm² (Ex 13.4 Q 3, p 225). Not 628 cm² total.
