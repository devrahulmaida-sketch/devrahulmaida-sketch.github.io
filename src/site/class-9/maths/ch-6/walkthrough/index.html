1. Pair ko pehchano

Sum se pehle arrangement check karo. pp 91,93-94.

Supplementary

Do angles ka sum 180° ho sakta hai bina adjacency ke. Isse apne aap straight line prove nahin hoti.

Linear pair

Adjacent angles aur non-common arms opposite rays: sum 180°. Converse mein adjacency aur 180° dono chahiye.

2. Opposite aur adjacent

Intersecting lines ke do alag rules. pp 94-95.

Opposite

Vertically opposite angles equal: shared adjacent angle ko dono 180° equations se subtract karo.

Adjacent

Linear pair ratio 5:7: 12 k=180°, k=15°. Angles 75°,105°. Opposite partners respectively 75°,105°.

3. Parallel se angle

Parallel given hona zaroori hai. pp 99-100.

Equal pairs

Corresponding aur alternate interior angles equal hote hain. Pair positions source figures mein check karo.

Supplementary pair

Same-side interior angles ka sum 180°. Yeh dono equal sirf special case 90°+90° mein honge.

4. Angle se parallel

Converse ka direction palto. pp 99-101.

Criteria

Ek equal corresponding pair, ek equal alternate interior pair, ya ek same-side interior supplementary pair se lines parallel prove hoti hain.

Common parallel

m parallel l aur n parallel l hon to distinct m,n parallel hain. Transversal ke corresponding angles ko equal karke converse lagao.

5. Triangle se missing angle

Source Ex 6.3 Q 1, p 107.

Interior banao

Exterior 135° ⇒ interior 45°. Exterior 110° ⇒ interior 70°. Dono conversions linear pair se.

Triangle sum

Third interior=180°−45°−70°=65°. Exterior ko interior ki jagah use karna galat hoga.

6. Bisector ka half

Source Ex 6.3 Q 2, p 107.

Pehle whole angle

X=62°, Y=54° ⇒ Z=64°. YO se Y ka half 27°, ZO se Z ka half 32°.

Chhota triangle

Triangle YOZ mein angles 27°,32°,∠YOZ. Isliye ∠YOZ=121°; requested ∠OZY=32°. Figures yahan draw nahin kiye gaye.
