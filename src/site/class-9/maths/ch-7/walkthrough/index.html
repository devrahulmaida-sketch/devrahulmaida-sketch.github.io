1. Vertex order pehle

Same shape-size aur correct correspondence ko alag checks samjho.

Order padho

ΔABC ≅ ΔPQR means A↔P, B↔Q, C↔R. AB↔PQ; BC↔QR; AC↔PR (pp 111-112).

CPCT kab?

Pehle congruence prove karo. Uske baad corresponding sides ya angles ko equal bolo. CPCT khud initial congruence rule nahin.

2. Included condition

Har do sides aur ek angle SAS nahin hote.

SAS

AB=PQ, AC=PR aur ∠BAC=∠QPR: equal angle equal sides ke beech hai. SAS applies (pp 113-114).

SSA caution

Non-included angle ki equality general congruence nahin deti. AAA se size fix nahin hota. Source counterexamples pp 114,117-118,127.

3. Angle-based rules

Side ka role aur angle sum ko sahi rakho.

ASA

Do corresponding angles aur unke beech ki side equal: ASA. Vertex matching ko side matching ke saath check karo (pp 115-117).

AAS

Do angles equal ho to teesra bhi equal, kyunki sum 180°. Corresponding non-included side ke saath ASA tak pahunch sakte ho (p 117).

4. Sides aur right triangles

SSS universal triangle rule; RHS right-triangle rule.

SSS

Teen corresponding side pairs equal ⇒ congruence. Diagram ka rotation ya reflection matter nahin (p 125).

RHS

Dono triangles right-angled hon. Hypotenuses aur ek corresponding leg equal ho. Right angle ke opposite side ko hypotenuse bolo (p 126).

5. Isosceles reasoning

Equal sides se angles, aur equal angles se sides.

Equal sides

AB=AC ⇒ ∠B=∠C. Equilateral ke teen angles 60° hain (pp 121-124).

Altitude example

AB=AC aur AD altitude ho to ΔADB≅ΔADC by RHS. Phir BD=CD aur ∠BAD=∠CAD by CPCT. Ex 7.3 Q 2, p 128.

6. Inequality ka strict sign

Opposite relation aur strict > ko mat bhoolo.

Side-angle link

AB>AC ⇒ ∠ACB>∠ABC. Right triangle mein hypotenuse largest angle 90° ke opposite hai, isliye longest hai (pp 130-132).

Two-side sum

AB+BC>AC, BC+CA>AB, CA+AB>BC. Meri vyakhya: 2+3=5, isliye 2,3,5 genuine triangle nahin banate (p 131 rule).
