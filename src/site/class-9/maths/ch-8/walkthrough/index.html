1. Chaar angles

Angle sum ko triangle sum se jodo.

Diagonal idea

Ek diagonal ordinary quadrilateral ko do triangles mein divide karta hai. Interior angle sum 180°+180°=360° (pp 136-137).

Ratio workflow

Angles 3 x,5 x,9 x,13 x hon to 30 x=360°. Har angle alag recover karo; sirf x final answer nahin.

2. Family aur properties

Har property har quadrilateral par nahin lagti.

Parallelogram

Dono opposite-side pairs parallel. Opposite sides/angles equal, adjacent angles supplementary, diagonals mutually bisecting (pp 137-141).

Special family

Square rectangle aur rhombus dono hai. General rectangle ki sides sab equal aur general rhombus ke diagonals equal hona zaroori nahin (pp 138,151).

3. Sufficient test

Condition ka poora pair padho.

Alternative tests

Both opposite-side pairs equal; both opposite-angle pairs equal; ya diagonals mutually bisecting: har test sufficient hai (pp 140-141).

One-pair test

Ek hi opposite-side pair equal AUR parallel ho to parallelogram (p 145). Ek equal pair alone ya parallel pair alone se conclusion nahin.

4. Diagonal traps

Equal, perpendicular aur bisecting alag facts hain.

Rectangle aur rhombus

Rectangle: equal + mutually bisecting diagonals. Rhombus: perpendicular + mutually bisecting. Square: dono sets (pp 142,151).

Converse caution

Bisection condition zaroor likho. Sirf equal diagonals rectangle prove nahin karte; sirf perpendicular diagonals rhombus prove nahin karte.

5. Mid-point theorem

Do midpoint givens, do conclusions.

Forward

Triangle ABC mein E,F respectively AB,AC ke midpoints: EF ∥ BC aur EF=BC/2 (p 148).

Converse

Ek side ke midpoint se doosri side ke parallel line third side ko bisect karti hai (pp 148-149). Sirf midpoint ya sirf parallel enough nahin.

6. Proof plan

Midpoint quadrilateral ko triangles mein dekho.

Two triangles

P,Q,R,S consecutive side midpoints. Diagonal AC se ΔABC aur ΔADC lo. PQ aur SR dono AC ke parallel aur half hain (Ex 8.2 Q 1 p 150).

Finish

PQ ∥ SR aur PQ=SR. Same opposite-side pair equal aur parallel hai, isliye PQRS parallelogram. Figure source mein dekho; yahan drawing nahin.
