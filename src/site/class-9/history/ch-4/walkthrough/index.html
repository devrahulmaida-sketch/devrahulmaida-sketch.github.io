Forest Society and Colonialism

वन्य समाज और उपनिवेशवाद
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation ya video nahin.

1. Demand ka chain

Forest sirf timber nahin.

Forest livelihoods

Food, fuel, fodder, medicines, leaves aur crafts: mixed forests multiple needs support karte the (pp 77, 85).

Colonial demand

Revenue/crops, ships, rail fuel/sleepers aur plantations ne clearing badhayi. Unproductive wilderness colonial label tha, neutral fact nahin (pp 78-82).

2. Forestry aur law

Timber management biodiversity ke barabar nahin.

Planned timber

Mixed forest replace karke single-species rows aur cutting/replanting plans aaye. Villagers aur officials ki needs different thi (pp 83-85).

Access restrictions

1878: reserved/protected/village forests. Reserved category mein local access most restricted; daily practices affected hue (pp 84-86).

3. Unequal livelihoods

New jobs automatically better life nahin.

Swidden aur hunting

Rotation/fallow wali cultivation ban hui; subsistence hunting punishable hua jabki elite big-game sport badha (pp 86-88).

Trade aur work

European firms ke exclusive rights aur plantation labour mein unequal gains/coercion. Colonial criminalising labels ko communities ka fact mat banao (pp 88-89).

4. Bastar 1910

Grievances, action aur partial concession.

Why rebellion?

1905 two-thirds reservation proposal, restrictions, free-work demands, rents aur famine background the. Kanger Dhurwas ne initiative liya (p 91).

Outcome

No single leader; Gunda Dhur important. Repression ke baad temporary suspension aur reserved area roughly half of plan, not complete end of control (pp 91-92).

5. Java

Dutch forest control aur varied resistance.

Kalangs aur labour

Skilled Kalangs resisted. Blandongdiensten: rent exemption for collective unpaid labour/buffaloes; later small wages, restricted cultivation (pp 93-94).

Samin challenge

State ne wind/water/earth/wood create nahin kiye, Samin ne ownership question ki. Followers ne surveys/taxes/fines/labour ka resistance kiya (p 94).

6. War aur conservation

Demand, destruction aur community care ko alag dekho.

War pressure

India war cutting; Java Dutch sawmills/logs destruction then Japanese forced extraction. Cultivation aur post-war conflicts bhi (p 95).

Community care

Sacred groves aur village patrols chapter ke conservation examples. Meri vyakhya: timber output alone forest wellbeing ka full measure nahin; present policy unverified (p 95).
