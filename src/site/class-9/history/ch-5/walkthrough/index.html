1. Movement ka logic

Season, forage aur negotiated access; aimless route nahin (pp 98-102).

Mountain example

Gujjar Bakarwal account: Siwalik winter → late April Pir Panjal/Kashmir summer → late September return. Multiple households ka travelling kafila. Textbook example, current itinerary nahin (p 98).

Plateau example

Dhangars: monsoon Maharashtra plateau → October after bajra harvest towards Konkan → rains aane par plateau return. Snow ke badle rainfall aur sheep needs important (p 100).

2. Farmers aur herders linked

Different activities ek livelihood support kar sakti hain (pp 100-102).

Stubble aur manure

Konkan ke harvested fields ka stubble flocks khati thi; manure next cultivation ko help karta. Peasants rice supplies dete the. Herding ko agriculture ka opposite mat samjho (p 100).

Different communities

Banjaras goods/cattle trade combine karte. Raikas cultivation-herding combine karte; Maru group camels, another group sheep-goats. Sab groups ka herd ya season same nahin (pp 101-102).

3. Four policy pressures

Reasons aur effects dono yaad rakho (pp 104-105).

Land aur forest

Waste Land Rules: revenue/crops ke liye cultivation → pasture loss. Reserved forests closed; Protected forests mein limited customary rights aur permits. Waste colonial label hai, unused-for-everyone ka fact nahin (p 104).

Classification aur tax

1871 Act ka criminal-by-birth label discriminatory colonial imposition tha, truth nahin. Settlement/surveillance ne movement roka. Grazing tax revenue ke liye; payment/pass records ne cost aur access control badhaya (p 105).

4. Effects aur adaptation

Loss ka effect sab par equal nahin tha (pp 106-107).

Pressure chain

Pasture shrink + movement limits → smaller area continuously grazed → vegetation recovery time kam → forage/animal health decline. Sirf pastoralists ko blame karna land-access changes ko ignore karta hai (p 106).

Different responses

Herd reduction, new routes, trade/cultivation/income mix possible the. Richer families land buy kar sakti thi; poorer families debt/lost stock/labour face karti thi. Pastoralism disappear nahin hua (p 107).

5. Maasai: land aur drought

Detailed Maasai case ko all African communities ka identical story mat banao (pp 109-113).

Grazing lands gone

1885 division, white settlement, cultivation aur game reserves ne land/access ghataya. About 60 per cent pre-colonial land loss textbook ka historical estimate hai, current survey nahin (pp 109-110).

Why drought worsened

Reserve boundaries/permits ne good alternative pasture tak jana roka. Land loss plus immobility ne drought crisis badhaya. Chapter 1933-34 Maasai Reserve mein over half cattle dying ka specific case deta hai (pp 112-113).

6. Inequality aur continuity

Authority, resources aur agency ko ek saath dekho (pp 113-115).

Unequal resources

British-appointed chiefs often had regular/mixed income and could replace animals. Poorer stock-dependent families bad times mein work dhundti thi. Elders-warriors distinction disturbed hua, entirely erased nahin (pp 113-115).

Still modern

Pastoralists route/herd/income change aur rights/support/resource-management voice demand karte hain. Many hilly-dry regions mein suitability ka argument hai, all grazing everywhere harmless ka claim nahin. Full activities/maps/source analysis yahan omitted (p 115).
