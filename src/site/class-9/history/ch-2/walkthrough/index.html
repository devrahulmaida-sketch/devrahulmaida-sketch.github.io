Socialism in Europe and the Russian Revolution

यूरोप में समाजवाद और रूसी क्रांति
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation ya video nahin.

1. बदलाव के विचार

Historical labels ko modern shortcuts mat banao.

Liberals

Rights, elected parliament, independent judiciary; mainly men of property vote, not universal franchise (pp 25-26).

Radicals

Majority-based government; many support women vote; against concentrated property, not all private property (p 26).

2. समाजवाद और रूस

Ideas aur social groups ke differences dekho.

Visions

Owen cooperative experiment; Blanc government support; Marx capitalist control overthrow/social property argument. Different socialist routes (p 28).

Groups

About 85% agricultural livelihoods; workers/peasants internally divided. Mir periodically land family needs se divide karta tha (pp 31-32).

3. संकट से फरवरी तक

Meri vyakhya: causes ko ek chain ki tarah jodo, single cause nahin.

Pressure

1905 Duma se autocracy end nahin. War defeats, disruption aur bread crisis se Tsar par trust kam hua (pp 33-35).

February

Women/worker strikes, soldiers joining, Petrograd Soviet; Tsar 2 March 1917 abdicates, Provisional Government follows. Julian chapter date (pp 35-36).

4. अप्रैल और अक्टूबर

February aur October same political outcome nahin.

April

Lenin: end war, land to peasants, banks nationalise. Yeh April Theses ke three demands (p 36).

October

Trotskii-led Military Revolutionary Committee organises seizure; 24 October uprising. Box 2: October Revolution Gregorian 7 November; calendar shift, second event nahin (p 38).

5. नीतियां और नियंत्रण

Meri vyakhya: property change ko political liberty ka proof mat banao.

Policy

Most industry/banks nationalised November 1917; land social property; large houses divided; titles banned (p 39).

Power

Assembly dismissed January 1918; later one-party rule/unions under party/censorship. Civil war aur USSR December 1922 ko same-day outcome mat banao (pp 39-41).

6. नियोजन और विरासत

Meri vyakhya: growth aur coercion dono padho.

Collectivisation

From 1929 forced kolkhoz cultivation; resistance, punishment, deportation/exile. Production immediately nahin badha; famine noted (pp 44-45).

Legacy

Global socialist/colonial debates influenced; not all socialists Bolshevik methods se agreed. Development ke saath freedoms denied/repression recorded (p 46).
