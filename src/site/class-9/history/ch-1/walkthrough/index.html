The French Revolution

फ्रांसीसी क्रांति
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation ya video nahin.

1. समाज और संकट

Social groups ko ek block mat banao.

Estates

Clergy aur nobility privileged; Third Estate wealthy professionals se labourers/peasants tak diverse tha (p 4).

Bread

Population/demand badhi; production aur wages peeche. Bad harvest se subsistence crisis deepen ho sakta tha (pp 5-6).

2. विचार और प्रतिनिधित्व

Economic aur political causes ko saath dekho.

Ideas

Locke: absolute/divine monarchy challenge; Rousseau: social contract; Montesquieu: power separation (pp 6-7).

1789

Voting dispute -> National Assembly ->20 June Tennis Court Oath.14 July Bastille aur countryside revolts alag linked developments hain (pp 8-9).

3. अधिकार और मताधिकार

Declaration ki language = sabko actual vote nahin.

1791

Active voters: men above 25 and tax >= 3 days labour wage; other men / all women passive. Assembly indirectly elected (p 10).

1792

All men 21+ regardless wealth; Convention declares republic 21 September 1792. Women included hone ka claim nahin (p 15).

4. आतंक से डिरेक्टरी

Political sequence ke stages alag rakho.

Terror

1793-94 severe controls/punishments; Robespierre fall July 1794. Yeh rights promise ka tension dikhata hai (p 16).

Directory

Five executives aur legislative conflict ne military leader Napoleon ke rise ko opening di. 1804 Emperor later milestone (pp 17,23).

5. समानता की सीमाएं

Participants aur beneficiaries hamesha same nahin.

Women

Clubs, vote/office demands, 1791 Olympe declaration; reforms ke bawajood political exclusion aur Terror restrictions (pp 18-20).

Slavery

1794 emancipation -> Napoleon rule mein reversal -> 1848 final French colonial abolition. Exact restoration year not taught (p 21).

6. रोजमर्रा और विरासत

Meri vyakhya: gains aur contradictions dono padho.

Communication

Censorship end ke baad newspapers/plays/songs political debate ke media bane (p 22).

Legacy

Liberty/democratic rights Europe aur anti-colonial movements mein reworked hue; Napoleon modern laws aur conquest dono laya (pp 23-24).
