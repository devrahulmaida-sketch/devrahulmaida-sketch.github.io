Food Security in India

भारत में खाद्य सुरक्षा
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation ya video nahin.

1. Teen dimensions

Supply aur family ke meal mein farq samjho.

Availability

P 42: country ka production, imports aur previous stored grain supply banate hain.

Access + affordability

P 42: food har person tak reachable ho aur sufficient safe/nutritious food kharidne ke liye enough money ho. Meri vyakhya: market stock alone enough nahin.

2. Calamity chain

Drought source ka example hai, universal forecast nahin.

Supply and prices

Pp 42-43: production down -> shortage -> prices up. Some families ki purchasing ability kam pad sakti hai.

Risk, not automatic outcome

Long/widespread crisis starvation risk badha sakta hai. Meri vyakhya: every shortage famine nahin; response, access aur income bhi matter karte hain.

3. Unequal exposure

Risk ko identity ka inherent trait nahin samjho.

Livelihoods

Pp 44-45 land-poor households, low-paid casual workers aur displacement discuss karte hain. Irregular income food access ko unstable kar sakti hai.

Nutrition and dignity

Source pregnant/nursing mothers aur young children ka concern batata hai. Meri vyakhya: structural barriers dekho; hunger human worth ka measure nahin. Old case wages/card rules current nahin.

4. Do hunger patterns

Time pattern aur diet quality dono dekho.

Chronic

Pp 45-46: diet persistently inadequate in quantity and/or quality, low income se linked ho sakti hai.

Seasonal

P 46: crop/work cycles se kuch periods mein employment na milna. Agriculture aur casual construction examples; seasonal ka matlab harmless nahin (meri vyakhya).

5. Reserve se delivery

Procurement/storage aur household access alag steps hain.

Buffer stock

P 47: government/FCI grain reserve; deficit areas aur poorer households ke liye distribution, calamity shortages ka response.

PDS

Pp 47-48: government-regulated ration/fair-price shop network. Chapter ke historical prices/quantities/cards current eligibility advice nahin.

6. Implementation aur scope

Food security ko sirf production total tak mat roko.

Delivery concerns

P 52 possible diversion, poor-quality grain aur irregular opening discuss karta hai. Har shop par current allegation nahin. Cooperatives/NGOs bhi source mein hain.

Scope guard

Meri vyakhya: supply + access + affordability saath assess karo. Full cases/graphs/scheme history/cooperative details omitted. No current benefit rules, latest data, full chapter or video.
