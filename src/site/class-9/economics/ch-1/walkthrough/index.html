The Story of Village Palampur

6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation ya video nahin.

1. Palampur ko case study samjho

Kalpanik kahani se production ke links samjho, current village survey nahin.

Story | p 1

Palampur hypothetical village hai. Farming main activity hai, saath mein dairy, manufacturing, shops aur transport hain.

Resources ka link | p 1

Roads aur electricity activities ko connect karte hain. Meri vyakhya: common facilities aur equal family ownership alag sawal hain.

2. Chaar requirements jodo

Land + labour + capital ke saath knowledge/enterprise bhi chahiye.

Factors | p 2

Land/natural resources, labour, physical capital aur human capital ko combine karke goods/services produce hote hain.

Capital do tarah | p 2

Fixed: tools, machines, buildings. Working: raw materials aur money in hand. Dono ko sirf cash kehna galat hoga.

3. Same land, alag mechanisms

Multiple cropping aur yield ka fark pakdo.

Multiple cropping | p 3

Ek piece of land par year mein more than one crop. Story mein electricity aur tubewells se irrigation expand hui.

Yield aur limits | pp 4-6

Modern inputs per-season yield badha sakte hain. Chapter soil fertility aur groundwater pressure bhi batata hai; yeh farming prescription nahin.

4. Land aur labour barabar nahin

Landless worker aur farm owner ki position alag hai.

Land | pp 6-7

Story: 150 landless, 240 small-plot aur 60 medium/large farmer families. Total 450 story families, national data nahin.

Wages | pp 7-9

Labourer wage ke liye kaam karta hai, crop ka owner nahin. Limited jobs ke liye competition low wages accept karne ka pressure banata hai; dated rates current law nahin.

5. Surplus se savings, ya loan

Production, consumption aur surplus ko alag rakho.

Surplus | p 10

Family consumption ke baad bacha output surplus hai. Market sale ki earnings ka hissa next season ke liye save ho sakta hai.

Unequal capital access | pp 9-10

Small farmer ko borrow karna pad sakta hai; Savita example mein high interest aur low-wage condition hai. Medium/large farmers savings use kar sakte hain. Koi loan recommendation nahin.

6. Non-farm ka network

Different activities, different inputs, buyers tak link.

Examples | pp 10-13

Milk sale, jaggery making, shops, computer centre aur transport. Fixed/working capital aur required skills activity ke hisaab se badalte hain.

Expansion | p 13

Chapter affordable finance aur markets/roads ke link par zor deta hai. Meri vyakhya: demand aur costs bhi test karni chahiye; income guarantee nahin.
