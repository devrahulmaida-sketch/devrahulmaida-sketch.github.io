1. Garibi ko pehchano

Bhookh aur unmet services ke saath dignity bhi dekho.

Basic needs

Pp 29-31: food, shelter, health, safe water, sanitation aur regular work ki kami poverty ke connected pehlu hain.

Case lens

Ram Saran/Lakha Singh source cases irregular work aur landlessness se needs ki difficulty dikhate hain. Meri vyakhya: people ko blame karne ke bajay constraints dekho; source earnings current wages nahin.

2. Exclusion vs risk

Dono concepts ek jaise nahin.

Exclusion

P 31: facilities, benefits aur opportunities se vanchit rehna; poverty ka cause aur result dono ho sakta hai.

Vulnerability

P 31: poor banne/rehne ka greater risk. Assets, health, education aur alternative jobs shocks handle karne ki capacity badalte hain.

3. Do measurement lenses

Money aur direct deprivation ko alag rakho.

Poverty line

P 32: basic needs ke minimum se income/consumption compare. Time, prices aur place matter karte hain. Current rupee threshold yahan nahin.

National MPI

Pp 31-33: health, education, standard of living. 12 indicator names notes mein. Meri vyakhya: one indicator alone se official poverty status decide nahin; weights/cutoffs not covered.

4. Ratio aur count

Measure, year aur denominator check karo.

Share vs number

Pp 32-33 ratio population ka share hai; count total people. Meri vyakhya: population change ki wajah se share aur count ka change alag ho sakta hai.

Do not splice

Table 3.1 consumption aur multidimensional rows alag rakhta hai. Global thresholds, historical graphs aur forecasts ko current facts nahin bataya gaya.

5. Causes aur action

Opportunity hona aur use kar pana alag hai.

Linked causes

Pp 38-39: insufficient jobs, unequal resources, colonial damage aur debt cycle. Group disadvantage kisi member ki inherent quality nahin.

Two approaches

P 39 growth aur targeted programmes. Education/health/access matter; scheme descriptions current applications, wages ya benefit eligibility ki guide nahin.

6. Human poverty

Minimum subsistence se aage dekho.

Reasonable life

P 40 education, shelter, healthcare, job security, self-confidence aur discrimination se freedom ki baat karta hai.

Scope guard

Meri vyakhya: choices aur dignity matter. Yeh selected concept walkthrough hai; full case/data/graph/scheme exercises, current statistics aur video nahin.
