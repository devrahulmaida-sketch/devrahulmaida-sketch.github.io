People as Resource

संसाधन के रूप में लोग
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation ya video nahin.

1. People se human capital

Economic capacity ka lens, human worth ki ranking nahin.

Investment

Education, training aur medical care se knowledge, skills aur working capacity develop ho sakti hai. Source p 16.

Unequal opportunities

Sakal ko schooling/training mili; Vilas ko poverty aur illness se barriers mile. Stories opportunity ko explain karti hain, blame ya guaranteed jobs nahin. Source pp 17-18.

2. Teen sectors

Activity ka nature dekho.

Goods

Primary: agriculture, forestry, fishing, mining. Secondary: materials ko goods mein transform karne wala manufacturing. Source p 19.

Services

Tertiary: trade, transport, banking, communication, education aur health. Service bhi economic activity ho sakti hai. Source p 19.

3. Market aur household work

Accounting aur social value alag sawal hain.

Purpose

Market activities remuneration ke liye. Non-market activities mein self-consumption ke liye production aata hai. Source pp 19-20.

Unpaid care

Cooking, cleaning aur care valuable work hai. Chapter national-income accounting mein unpaid domestic work ke exclusion ko discuss karta hai. Meri vyakhya: exclusion ka matlab useless nahin. Source p 20.

4. Education aur health

Skills ke saath access aur well-being.

Education

Knowledge aur skills productive capacity badha sakti hain; schooling ki quality aur retention matter karte hain. Historical tables ko current facts mat mano. Source pp 20-22.

Health

Nutrition aur medical services well-being aur working capacity support karte hain. Meri vyakhya: illness se dignity kam nahin hoti; yeh medical advice nahin. Source pp 23-24.

5. Do unemployment patterns

Time gap aur extra labour ko alag rakho.

Seasonal

Kuch mahino mein kaam nahin milta. Agriculture ke busy aur slack seasons chapter ka example hain. Source p 25.

Disguised

5 workers ka kaam 8 karte hain; 3 ko hataane par total output same. Extra workers additional output nahin badhate. Source p 25.

6. Skills aur opportunities

Village story ek illustration hai.

Mismatch

Educated log bhi job na milne ki problem face kar sakte hain. Chapter skills ki supply aur demand mein mismatch discuss karta hai. Source p 25.

New activities

Village story mein agricultural training, improved plough, school, teacher aur tailoring se activities judti hain. Meri vyakhya: investment ke saath work opportunities bhi chahiye. Source pp 26-27.

Original fact quiz grounded in the cited source. Har question mein ek sahi option. Current statistics ya scheme status par quiz nahin.
