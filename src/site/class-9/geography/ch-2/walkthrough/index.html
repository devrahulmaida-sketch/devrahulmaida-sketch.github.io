Physical Features of India

1. Six groups, teen relief contrasts

Landform ko political unit se alag rakho (p 7).

Six divisions

Himalayan Mountains; Northern Plains; Peninsular Plateau; Indian Desert; Coastal Plains; Islands. Yeh physical groups hain, state boundaries ka substitute nahin.

Relief contrast

Young fold Himalaya: peaks/deep valleys/fast rivers. Northern Plain: deposited alluvium. Old rocky Plateau: broad valleys/rounded hills. Meri vyakhya: geological age current disaster prediction nahin.

2. Himalaya ko do directions mein padho

North-south ranges aur west-east regional divisions same list nahin (pp 7-10).

North se south

Himadri/Great Himalaya → Himachal/Lesser Himalaya → Shiwaliks. Source Himadri ko snow-bound/glacier-bearing; Himachal ko rugged valleys wala; Shiwaliks ko unconsolidated sediments wala batata hai.

Duns aur eastern hills

Lesser Himalaya aur Shiwaliks ke beech longitudinal valleys=Duns. Dehra Dun, Kotli Dun, Patli Dun examples. Purvachal: Patkai, Naga, Manipur, Mizo hills. Yeh textbook regional labels hain, map/boundary adjudication nahin.

3. Plain flat hai, par bilkul uniform nahin

Deposition se geographic sections aur relief belts dono samjho (pp 11-12).

River systems

Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra systems aur tributaries alluvium deposit karte hain. Broad sections: Punjab, Ganga, Brahmaputra plains. Lower-course deposition riverine islands/distributaries se linked hai.

Four relief terms

Bhabar: foothill pebbles. Terai: south ka wet belt. Bhangar: older alluvium/terraces/kankar. Khadar: newer floodplain deposits, almost annually renewed in the account. Regional model, har stream/flood ka survey nahin.

4. Plateau aur Ghats ka relationship

Narmada reference aur west-east slope ko saath rakho (p 12).

Two divisions

Central Highlands Narmada ke north, Malwa ka major area; Deccan south mein triangular landmass, higher west aur gently eastward slope. Old rocks, broad valleys, rounded hills: perfectly flat slab nahin.

Edges of Deccan

Western Ghats: western edge, coast-parallel, comparatively higher/continuous. Eastern Ghats: eastern edge, irregular/discontinuous, rivers se cut. Western slopes par rising moist winds orographic rain explain karti hain, daily rain guarantee nahin.

5. Desert se coasts tak

Sandy dunes aur delta plains alag processes dikhate hain (p 13).

Desert account

Aravali ke western margins: undulating sandy plain, arid climate, low vegetation. Crescent-shaped dunes=barchans; Luni named large river. Source ka low-rainfall description har locality/year ki forecast nahin.

Coastal contrast

West: Western Ghats-Arabian Sea ke beech narrow strip, Konkan/Kannad/Malabar. East: Bay of Bengal side wide/level plains, Northern Circar/Coromandel; Mahanadi-Godavari-Krishna-Kaveri deltas. Scale map yahan nahin.

6. Islands aur complementary roles

Location aur source-specific examples ko current statistics se alag rakho (p 14).

Island groups

Lakshadweep: Malabar coast ke paas coral islands. Andaman-Nicobar: Bay of Bengal mein north-south chain, Andaman north/Nicobar south. Indian island group aur independent neighbouring country alag categories hain.

Different roles

Mountains: water/forest wealth. Plains: agriculture. Plateau: minerals. Coasts/islands: fishing/ports. Meri vyakhya: complementary roles, exclusive occupations nahin. No present area/admin/volcano-status verification; no source-map/puzzle solution.
