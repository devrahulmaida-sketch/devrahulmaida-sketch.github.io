Natural Vegetation and Wildlife

1. प्राकृतिक वनस्पति: kya count hoga?

Natural growth aur cultivated example ka distinction (p 39).

Natural community

Human aid ke bina grown aur long undisturbed plant community natural vegetation hai. Flora plants ko, fauna animals ko kehte hain. Dono terms ko interchange mat karo.

Cultivated contrast

Source crops, fruits aur orchards ko vegetation kehta hai, natural vegetation nahin. Meri vyakhya: planted orchard ko sirf green hone se natural forest nahin keh sakte.

2. सदाबहार aur पर्णपाती

Water availability aur leaf-shedding pattern ko compare karo (p 40).

Evergreen

Source >200 cm rain aur short dry season deta hai. Trees, shrubs, creepers ki layers; all trees ka fixed common leaf-shedding time nahin. Meri vyakhya: evergreen does not mean no leaf ever falls.

Deciduous

Source 70-200 cm rain aur dry summer mein six-eight weeks leaf shedding. Moist 100-200 cm; dry 70-100 cm source bands. Teak aur sal examples; bands every patch ki exact boundaries nahin.

3. कँटीले वन: paani bachana

Thorn aur scrub vegetation ka source account (pp 40,42).

Dry setting

Source less than 70 cm rainfall ko thorny trees/bushes se jodta hai. Northwest/semi-arid examples; acacias, euphorbias aur cacti plant examples hain.

Adaptations

Long roots deep moisture tak, succulent stems water store karne mein, thick-small leaves evaporation kam karne mein help karti hain. Meri vyakhya: har species mein saare features ek saath assume mat karo.

4. पर्वतीय पेटियाँ: altitude ka effect

Temperature change aur overlapping belts (p 42).

Lower to higher

Source 1,000-2,000 m wet temperate oak/chestnut aur 1,500-3,000 m conifers pine/deodar/spruce deta hai. Meri vyakhya: overlap dikhata hai ki ye rigid stair steps nahin.

Alpine aur tundra

Generally above 3,600 m alpine vegetation; snow-line ke paas trees stunted, phir shrubs aur alpine grassland; higher tundra mein mosses/lichens. Source mountain examples: yak aur snow leopard, not current sightings.

5. मैंग्रोव: tidal delta

Coastal setting ko rainfall-only explanation se alag rakho (pp 42-43).

Tides aur silt

Mangroves tide-influenced coasts par mud/silt accumulation wale areas mein. Source Ganga, Mahanadi, Krishna, Godavari, Kaveri deltas deta hai.

Sundari example

Ganga-Brahmaputra delta mein sundari trees mention hain. Meri vyakhya: tidal coastal setting important hai; kisi delta ke har patch ka current cover ya har animal ka present range yahan verify nahin.

6. संरक्षण: roles aur threats

Species roles ko threats se connect karo (pp 44,46).

Ecosystem roles

Source insects ke pollination aur biological-control roles batata hai. Crops aur domestic animals biodiversity se select hue. Har species ecosystem mein role rakhti hai; yahan medical treatment instructions nahin.

Threats aur response

Hunting, overuse, pollution, introduced alien species aur reckless clearing threats hain. Source reserves/parks/sanctuaries/projects discuss karta hai; current counts, membership ya legal updates verify nahin. Meri vyakhya: habitat protection ko answer mein connect karo.
