1. Area aur dividing high ground

Drainage basics: pp 17-18,20.

Basin aur system

River+tributaries=river system. Us ek system ka drained area=drainage basin. Chhoti streams main river se judti hain; river large water body tak water le jaati hai.

Water divide

Do basins ke beech elevated mountain/upland=water divide. Chapter example Ambala, Indus-Ganga systems ke beech. Basin area hai; divide separating high ground, same cheez nahin.

2. Flow ke patterns

Most/many qualifiers preserve karo (pp 17-18).

Himalayan account

Most Himalayan rivers perennial: rain+snowmelt. Long course; upper erosion, silt/sand transport; middle/lower course mein meanders, oxbow lakes, deltas. Perennial flood-free nahin.

Peninsular contrast

Many Peninsular rivers seasonal aur rain-dependent. Dry-season flow reduced; comparatively shorter/shallower courses. Har Peninsular river dry aur har Himalayan river identical kehna galat hoga.

3. Indus aur Ganga

Selected sources aur meeting point, full map nahin (p 18).

Indus route

Tibet mein Lake Mansarowar ke paas source; westward Ladakh, aage Pakistan aur Arabian Sea ka course described. Yeh current treaty/statistics lesson nahin.

Ganga headstreams

Bhagirathi Gangotri Glacier se fed; Alaknanda se Devaprayag mein meeting. Haridwar=mountains se plains. Devaprayag aur Haridwar ko interchangeable mat samjho.

4. Himalayan networks

Tributaries aur rainfall/silt relation (pp 20-21).

Ganga tributaries

Himalayan examples: Yamuna, Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi. Peninsular upland examples: Chambal, Betwa, Son. Farakka ke paas Bhagirathi-Hooghly distributary alag hoti hai; full delta map yahan nahin.

Brahmaputra contrast

Tibet cold/dry: less water and silt. Indian high-rainfall stretch: more water/silt. Assam mein braided channel, deposits, islands aur channel shifts described. Current flood forecast/ranking nahin.

5. Peninsular routes

Direction aur source examples (pp 21-22).

Eastward group

Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri: generally east Bay of Bengal, deltas. Western Ghats main water divide. Meri vyakhya: yeh selected named group hai, complete river inventory nahin.

Westward exceptions

Narmada: Amarkantak hills. Tapi: Satpura/Betul. Dono westward rift valleys aur estuaries. Chhoti west-flowing coastal streams bhi hain, isliye only two west-flowing rivers mat likho.

6. Lakes aur usable water

Origin, use, pollution ko jodo (pp 22-23).

Lake origins

Oxbow=meander cut-off; lagoons=coastal spits/bars. Sambhar salt-water, Wular tectonic, Guru Gobind Sagar dam-created examples. Full natural/artificial lake exercise pending.

Economy aur pollution

Irrigation, navigation, hydropower river uses. Lakes flow regulation, ecosystems, tourism support kar sakti hain. Untreated sewage/effluents quality ko affect karte hain; regulation har flood rokne ki guarantee nahin.
