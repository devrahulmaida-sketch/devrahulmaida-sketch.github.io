Population

जनसंख्या
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation ya video nahin.

1. Size, distribution, density

Teen questions ko alag rakho (pp 48-49).

Kitne aur kahan?

Size total number hai; distribution batata hai log areas mein kaise spread hain. Same concept mat samjho.

Per unit area

Density persons per unit area hai, jaise persons per square kilometre. Meri vyakhya: total aur density compare karte waqt area zaroor dekho.

2. Growth ka measure

Number aur pace alag hain (p 51).

Absolute increase

Later population minus earlier population = absolute increase. Period aur units same rakho.

Rate aur base

Growth rate base population ke comparison mein change hai. Meri vyakhya: bade base par small rate bhi many people add kar sakta hai; additions har decade badhna zaroori nahin.

3. Births aur deaths

Rates ke units padho (pp 52-53).

Per 1000 per year

Birth rate live births per 1000 persons in a year; death rate deaths per 1000 persons in a year. Ye total counts nahin hain.

Natural increase

Births-deaths natural increase se juda hai; rates ka difference natural-increase rate hai. Meri vyakhya: counts aur rates ko mix mat karo.

4. Migration aur scale

Country ke andar ya countries ke beech (p 53).

Internal

Country ke andar movement regional distribution badalta hai, national total ko khud nahin. Departure aur arrival dono ka scale dekho.

International

Countries ke beech movement international migration hai. Meri vyakhya: kis boundary ko cross kiya gaya, wahi internal/international distinction settle karta hai.

5. Push aur pull

Source examples, har vyakti ka reason nahin (p 53).

Push

Source rural poverty aur unemployment ko push examples deta hai. Ye present migration survey ya har migrant ka personal account nahin.

Pull

Source city employment opportunities aur better living conditions ko pull examples deta hai. Migration distribution aur composition ko bhi badal sakta hai.

6. Historical policy ko kaise padhein?

NPP 2000 aims aur achievement alag (p 53).

Source ke aims

Schooling up to 14, infant mortality below 30 per 1000 live births, universal child immunisation, delayed marriage aur people-centred family welfare: source ke historical policy aims.

Kya claim nahin hai

Target ka hona achievement prove nahin karta. Current law, outcomes, census date, medical/diet advice aur live population rankings yahan verify nahin kiye gaye.

7. Fact check - 5 questions

Original fact quiz grounded in the cited source. Har question mein ek sahi option. Current counts, rankings, health advice ya policy outcomes par quiz nahin.
