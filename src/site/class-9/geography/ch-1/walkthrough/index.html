1. Mainland ya poora Union?

Coordinates ko unke label ke saath padho (p 1).

Mainland extent

Latitude: 8°4'N se 37°6'N. Longitude: 68°7'E se 97°25'E. N aur E answer ka part hain. Text India ko Northern Hemisphere mein rakhta hai.

Scope check

Mainland ke alawa islands bhi hain. Meri vyakhya: mainland ka southern endpoint poore Indian Union ka endpoint nahin. Yeh text-box explanation hai, political ya scale map nahin.

2. Tropic aur island-sea pairs

Direction aur water body ko saath rakho (p 1).

Tropic

Textbook: Tropic of Cancer 23°30'N. Country ko almost two equal parts mein divide karti hai. Almost ka matlab exactly equal area nahin.

Island pairs

Andaman-Nicobar: mainland ke southeast, Bay of Bengal. Lakshadweep: southwest, Arabian Sea. Dono India ke island groups hain, independent neighbour countries nahin.

3. Size ka figure: date ka dhyan

Printed chapter aur dated official correction ko mix mat karo.

Printed account

Own Reprint 2025-26 pp 1-2: area 3.28 million square km, about 2.4 per cent world geographical area, land boundary about 15,200 km. Yeh cited figures hain, new survey nahin.

Coastline correction

Printed p 2: 7,516.6 km including islands. Ministry circular 29 April 2025: recalculated 11,098.81 km. Methodology changed; coast physically badhne ka proof nahin. Neeche official circular link hai.

4. Sunrise vs standard clock

Local difference ko shared clock se alag samjho (pp 2, 6 Q 3).

Local difference

Chapter Gujarat se Arunachal Pradesh tak about two-hour local time lag batata hai. Arunachal mein sunrise pehle ho sakta hai; common clock se sunrise same nahin hota (meri vyakhya).

Common standard

82°30'E through Mirzapur in Uttar Pradesh: time along this Standard Meridian whole country ke standard time ke liye liya jata hai. Yeh shared reference hai, simultaneous local sunrise ka claim nahin.

5. Sea location aur exchanges

Shape se contact ka relationship samjho (pp 2, 4).

Coastal contacts

Deccan Peninsula Indian Ocean mein project karta hai. West coast: West Asia, Africa, Europe. East coast: Southeast aur East Asia. Arabian Sea west aur Bay of Bengal east mein hai. Yeh chapter ka geographical account hai.

Ideas aur goods

Northern passes ne land travellers ko passages diye. Chapter ideas aur goods ke exchange ko routes se jodta hai: numerals/decimal system, texts, spices/muslin aur art-architecture ke influences examples hain, exhaustive origin proof nahin.

6. Neighbour country ya Indian island?

India ke island groups ko countries se distinguish karo (pp 4-5).

Land account

Chapter p 4: Pakistan, Afghanistan; China (Tibet), Nepal, Bhutan; Myanmar, Bangladesh. Yeh cited textbook convention hai, disputed boundaries ya present de-facto control ka determination nahin. Yahan map redraw nahin hai.

Across the sea

Sri Lanka aur Maldives southern island neighbours hain. Palk Strait aur Gulf of Mannar ka narrow sea channel Sri Lanka ko India se separate karta hai. Maldives Lakshadweep ke south mein hai.
