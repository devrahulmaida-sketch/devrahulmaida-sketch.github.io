Climate

जलवायु
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation ya video nahin.

1. Same elements, different timescale

Basics: p 26.

Weather

Kisi area ke atmosphere ka given time par haal: temperature, pressure, wind, humidity aur precipitation.

Climate

Bade area ka long-term weather conditions aur variation ka account. Source more than thirty years kehta hai; ek rainy day se climate decide nahin hoti (aakhri baat meri vyakhya).

2. Controls ko connect karo

Controls aur Indian factors: p 27.

Sea distance

Sea temperature ko moderate karta hai. Inland areas mein moderation kam aur seasonal contrasts stronger ho sakte hain: continentality.

Mountain relief

Mountains winds ko block kar sakte hain. Rain-bearing winds ke saamne slope par rain aur leeward par relatively dry conditions. Himalayan cold-wind barrier bhi source ka factor hai.

3. Wind reversal

Seasonal pressure account: pp 26,28.

Winter pattern

Himalaya ke north high pressure; southern oceans ki taraf lower pressure. Generally dry land-to-sea winds, lekin Tamil Nadu coast par sea-to-land exception.

Summer pattern

Interior Asia/northwestern India low pressure. Southern ocean winds equator cross karke right turn leti hain: southwest monsoon, warm ocean moisture ke saath.

4. Cold aur hot season

Selected seasonal features: pp 28,30.

Winter rain

Western disturbances Mediterranean/western Asia se plains mein winter rain aur mountains mein snow laate hain. Chhoti mahawat rainfall rabi crops ke liye important hai.

Summer winds

Loo hot, dry, strong gusty wind hai in north/northwest. West Bengal: Kaal Baisakhi; Kerala/Karnataka: pre-monsoon mango showers. Textbook account, safety advice nahin.

5. Rain continuous nahin

Advancing monsoon: pp 30,32.

Wet aur dry spells

Monsoon ke breaks rainy spells ke beech rainless intervals hain. Trough ka north-south movement rainfall distribution ko affect karta hai.

Regional difference

Windward Western Ghats aur northeast wet examples hain; Ganga valley mein generally east-to-west rainfall decrease hoti hai. Arrival, retreat aur spells uncertain; yeh live forecast nahin.

6. Retreat aur shared rhythm

Transition/unifying bond: pp 32,35.

October heat

Retreat ke waqt clear sky, high daytime temperature aur moist land ki humidity oppressive October heat de sakti hain. Eastern coast par depressions/cyclones rain la sakte hain.

Unifying bond

Monsoon water farming aur seasonal life ko link karta hai, including landscape, plants/animals aur festivals. Meri vyakhya: regional differences ke bawajood connection, equal rain ka claim nahin.
