Is Matter Around Us Pure?

क्या हमारे आस-पास के पदार्थ शुद्ध हैं?
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation, video ya lab procedure nahin.

1. Pure ka scientific matlab

Printed pp14-15

Pure substance

Same chemical nature ke particles. Element ya compound ho sakta hai. "Natural" ya "unadulterated" label alag baat hai.

Mixture

Ek se zyada pure substances. Homogeneous: har part uniform; heterogeneous: composition non-uniform.

2. Solution, suspension, colloid

Printed pp16-18

Solution

Homogeneous; solute settle nahin hota, ordinary filtration se separate nahin hota. True solution light beam ka path visible nahin banata.

Suspension

Heterogeneous; visible particles settle ho sakte aur filtration se alag ho sakte hain.

Colloid

Heterogeneous par uniform lag sakta hai. Individual particles naked eye se nahin dikhte; Tyndall effect, normally no settling.

3. Denominator sahi rakho

Printed pp16-17; original numerical example

Mass percentage

10 g salt + 90 g water = 100 g solution. 10 / 100 × 100 = 10%. Denominator solution hai, solvent nahin.

Saturation

Given temperature par limit tak solute dissolved ho to saturated. Concentrated aur saturated ek hi baat nahin.

4. Method property se choose karo

Printed pp19-23; concepts only

Filter ya funnel?

Insoluble solid-liquid suspension: filtration. Immiscible liquid layers: separating funnel. True dissolved salt ko ordinary filter nahin rokta.

Distillation ya crystals?

Miscible liquids ke suitable boiling-point difference: distillation; close points: fractional distillation. Solution se pure solid crystals: crystallisation.

Safety boundary

Heating, acids, flammable solvents aur gas tests ke steps nahin. Saaf dikhne wala filtered water automatically drinking-safe nahin: meri vyakhya.

5. Change ko composition se pehchano

Printed p24

Physical change

Ice melt hoti hai: chemical nature same. Wax melting physical hai.

Chemical change

Rusting aur wax vapour burning new substances banate hain. Candle mein physical aur chemical dono changes ho sakte hain.

6. Element, compound, mixture

Printed pp25-27

Element

Chemical reactions se simpler substances mein break nahin hota. Copper aur oxygen examples hain.

Compound

Different elements fixed proportion mein chemically combined. Compound ki properties constituent elements se alag.

Mixture

Variable composition; suitable physical separation possible. Element ya compound dono mixture ke components ho sakte hain.
