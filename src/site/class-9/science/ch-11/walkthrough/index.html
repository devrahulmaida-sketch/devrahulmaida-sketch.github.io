Work and Energy

कार्य और ऊर्जा
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation, video ya lab procedure nahin.

1. कार्य किस बल का?

Effort aur mechanical work ko separate karo.

बल और विस्थापन

Constant force along displacement: W = Fs. Opposite: −Fs. Perpendicular: 0. Particular force ka naam likho; displacement alone nonzero work prove nahin karta (meri vyakhya).

दीवार वाला अंतर

Wall move nahin hui to wall par mechanical work zero. Body energy use aur tiredness phir bhi ho sakti hai. Zero mechanical work ka matlab zero biological energy use nahin (meri vyakhya).

2. गतिज ऊर्जा

Speed squared ka effect dekho.

चाल बदलने पर

K = ½mv². Same mass par speed 2 v se K four times; 3 v se nine times. SI mein kg aur m/s use karo.

कुल कार्य

W_net = K_final − K_initial. 20 kg, 5 se 2 m/s: K 250 se 40 J, net work −210 J. Chosen force ka work tab same jab others ka net work zero ho (meri vyakhya).

3. ऊंचाई और स्थितिज ऊर्जा

Zero level aur vertical height declare karo.

ऊंचाई का अर्थ

Constant near-Earth g: U = mgh. h vertical height above chosen zero hai, curved path ki length nahin. U ka numerical value zero level se badalta hai.

धीरे उठाना

Slow lift/no losses: applied work ≈ +mgh aur gravity work −mgh, ΔK ≈ 0. Accelerated lift mein applied force necessarily mg nahin (meri vyakhya).

4. ऊर्जा कहां गई?

Mechanical aur total energy alag rakho.

आदर्श मुक्त पतन

Rest se release, no drag: lost U = gained K. 40 kg from 5 m, g = 9.8: total mechanical energy 1960 J. Halfway down U = 980 J aur K = 980 J (meri vyakhya).

प्रतिरोध के साथ

Friction/drag mechanical energy ko internal/thermal aur other forms mein transfer karte hain. Energy destroy nahin hoti. K + U constant claim ke liye model conditions zaroori hain (meri vyakhya).

5. शक्ति

Same work, alag time.

दर और मात्रा

Average power = total work / total time. 3200 J in 20 s is 160 W; in 50 s is 64 W. Work same hai, rate different.

इकाई पहचानो

1 W = 1 J/s; 1 kW = 1000 W. Watt power ki unit hai, joule energy/work ki. Changing rate mein average aur instantaneous power differ kar sakte hain (meri vyakhya).

6. बिजली की यूनिट

kWh ko power mat samjho.

kWh का रूपांतरण

Constant 1 kW for 1 hour gives 1 kWh = 1000×3600 J = 3.6×10⁶ J. Electricity ki 1 unit = 1 kWh. kW power, kWh energy.

चुना हुआ हिसाब

Four 500 W devices: total 2 kW. Constant operation for 10 h: E = 2×10 = 20 kWh. Heater 1.5 kW for 10 h: 15 kWh. Actual cycling/on-time matter karta hai (meri vyakhya).
