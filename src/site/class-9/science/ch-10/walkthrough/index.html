1. Inverse-square law

Masses fixed rakho; distance centres ka.

Formula

F = GMm/r² in the point-mass/spherical model (model qualification: meri vyakhya). Source pp 132-134.

Distance half

EndQ 1 p 143: r becomes r/2, so force becomes 4 F. Same masses, same model.

2. Free-fall acceleration

Sirf gravity ka effect; air drag neglect karo.

Mass cancels

a = F/m = GM/r², independent of falling body mass at the same location. Source pp 135-136.

Top of flight

Upward positive: a = −g even when v = 0 at highest point. Constant-g/no-drag model; top-point clarification meri vyakhya. Source p 136.

3. Mass is not weight

Location se g badalta hai, same object ki mass nahin.

Earth

EndQ 12 p 144: mass 10 kg, Earth g 9.8 gives weight 98 N.

Moon

Same 10 kg mass; source approximate Moon weight 98/6 ≈ 16.33 N. It is not a mass of 1.67 kg. Source pp 137-138.

4. Pressure and area

Same thrust par area ka effect dekho.

Small area

Source example 10.6 p 139:49 N / 0.02 m² = 2450 Pa.

Large area

Same 49 N / 0.08 m² = 612.5 Pa. Same force, greater area, smaller average pressure.

5. Buoyant force

Fluid displacement aur equilibrium alag checks.

Archimedes

Buoyant force = weight of displaced fluid. Fully or partly immersed body. Source p 142.

Floating at rest

Freely floating rest equilibrium: F_b = W. Rising body can have F_b > W; final balance has equality (meri vyakhya clarifying pp 140-142).

6. Density test

Units same rakho; packet ki average density compare karo.

Packet sinks

EndQ 22 p 145:500 g /350 cm³ ≈ 1.43 g/cm³ > water 1 g/cm³, so packet sinks in the ordinary sealed-rigid model.

Displaced water

Fully immersed volume 350 cm³ displaces 350 g water. Not 500 g. Negligible deformation assumed (meri vyakhya); selected worked answer to Q 22.
