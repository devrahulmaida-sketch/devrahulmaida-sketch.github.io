Improvement in Food Resources

खाद्य संसाधनों में सुधार
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation, video ya lab procedure nahin.

1. उपलब्धता और पहुँच

Sirf godown bharna hunger ka complete solution nahin.

Availability

Enough food produce/store hona availability hai. Sustainable efficiency resources ko degrade kiye bina output improve karti hai (p 203).

Access

Log food afford aur obtain kar saken, yeh access hai. Production alone access guarantee nahin karta (p 203).

2. किस्म और पोषण

Seed traits aur soil management ko mix mat karo.

Variety

Yield, quality, resistance, maturity aur adaptability selection goals hain. Hybridisation genetically different plants cross karti hai (pp 204-205).

Nutrients

Manure organic matter add karta hai; fertilisers concentrated nutrients dete hain. Dono ko unlimited dose instructions nahin samjho (pp 205-207).

3. साथ या क्रम से?

Row pattern aur timing se cropping ideas pehchano.

Together

Mixed cropping saath crops grow karti hai. Intercropping mein definite rows hoti hain (pp 207-208).

Succession

Crop rotation mein different crops planned succession mein aati hain. Ek saath aur baari-baari same nahin (p 208).

4. खेत से भंडार तक

Field protection aur storage conditions dono zaroori.

Field

Weeds competition, insects damage aur pathogens disease se losses hote hain. Preventive management aur resistant varieties help karte hain (p 208).

Storage

Insects/fungi biotic; unsuitable moisture/temperature abiotic factors hain. Cleaning, drying aur suitable storage help karte hain. Chemical procedure nahin (p 209).

5. दूध, अंडे और मांस

Animal purpose ke saath management needs badalti hain.

Cattle

Milk aur draught labour alag cattle purposes hain. Clean shelter, appropriate feed aur disease prevention core management hain (pp 209-210).

Poultry

Layers eggs, broilers meat ke liye. Housing, nutrition aur environment needs alag ho sakti hain. Veterinary advice nahin (pp 210-211).

6. जल और फूलों के संसाधन

Food sources ka efficient use concept hai, handling protocol nahin.

Fish

Capture natural sources se; culture farming hai. Composite culture mein species different foods/zones use karke competition reduce karti hain (pp 211-212).

Bees

Pasturage nectar/pollen ke liye available flowers hain; quantity aur kind production/taste influence karte hain. Bees sting kar sakti hain; handling guide nahin (p 213).
