1. Net force ka test

Balanced ka matlab stationary hi nahin.

Zero net force

Rest ya straight-line constant velocity dono possible. Inertia force nahin. Source pp 115-118.

Nonzero net force

Constant mass par acceleration net force ke direction mein. Velocity ka magnitude ya direction badal sakta hai. Source pp 115,119.

2. Inertia aur mass

Heavier object ka inertia zyada.

Bus brakes

Bus slow hoti hai, body forward motion continue karne ki tendency rakhti hai. Belt force se motion badalti hai. Source pp 116-118.

No phantom push

Inertia koi aage dhakka dene wala force nahin. Mass resistance-to-change ka measure hai. Source p 118; force clarification meri vyakhya.

3. Momentum aur signs

Pehle positive direction fix karo.

Definition

p = mv; kg m/s. Direction velocity ki. Source p 119.

Opposite directions

Meri vyakhya: right positive ho to 2 kg at +3 m/s ka p = +6 kg m/s; at −3 m/s ka p = −6 kg m/s. Equal magnitudes, opposite momenta.

4. Force ka calculation

Constant mass, uniform acceleration example.

Find acceleration

Source end Q 16 p 129: a = (8−5)/6 = 0.5 m/s².

Find net force

Same Q 16: 100×0.5 = 50 N; p changes 500 to 800 kg m/s. Calculations are selected worked answers, not new source numbers.

5. Third-law pair

Dono forces ek body par nahin.

Walking

Foot ground ko backward force deta hai; ground foot ko forward force deta hai. Source p 122.

Do not cancel

Different bodies ke interaction forces equal-opposite hain. Ek body ka net force us par lage saare forces se banta hai. Source p 122.

6. System momentum

External-force condition ko mat bhoolo.

Condition

Net external force zero ho to total vector momentum conserved. Individual objects ka momentum change ho sakta hai. Source pp 123-124.

Stick-together model

m₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = (m₁+m₂)V, 1 D with negligible external impulse. Sticking does not imply kinetic-energy conservation (meri vyakhya); source common-velocity examples pp 125-126.
