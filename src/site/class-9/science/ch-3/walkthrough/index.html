1. Count ratio ≠ mass ratio

Printed pp31-32,36

Water ke atoms

H₂O mein H ke 2 atoms aur O ka 1 atom: atom ratio H:O = 2:1.

Water ka mass

H = 1 u aur O = 16 u lene par 2×1 : 16 = 1:8. Fixed mass ratio ko 2:1 mat likho.

2. Dalton ko historical padho

Printed pp32-33

Model ka useful idea

Different elements ke atoms small whole-number ratios mein combine hote hain. Chemical reaction mein atoms rearrange hote hain.

Limit yaad rakho

Indivisible atom historical postulate tha. Chapter batata hai ki atom smaller particles se bana hai. Atomic structure ki full explanation yahan nahin.

3. Ion charge balance

Printed pp36-38

Magnesium chloride

Mg²⁺ ko balance karne ke liye two Cl⁻ chahiye: MgCl₂. +2 + 2(−1) = 0.

Simplify karo

Ca²⁺ aur O²⁻: ratio 1:1, formula CaO. Criss-cross ke baad Ca₂O₂ ko simplest ratio mein reduce karo.

4. Brackets poora group repeat karte hain

Printed p39

Calcium hydroxide

Ca(OH)₂ mein Ca 1, O 2 aur H 2. Subscript 2 poore bracket wale OH ko multiply karta hai.

Ammonium sulphate

(NH₄)₂SO₄ mein N 2, H 8, S 1 aur O 4. NH₄⁺ ke two groups ek SO₄²⁻ ko balance karte hain.

5. u aur g mol⁻¹ ko alag rakho

Printed pp39-41

Molecule / formula unit

H₂O molecular mass = 18 u. NaCl formula unit mass = 58.5 u. Ionic solid ko discrete molecules mat maan lo.

One mole

School-rounded water molar mass ≈18 g mol⁻¹. 1 mol H₂O molecules ka mass ≈18 g, ek molecule ka 18 g nahin.

6. Mole bridge: mass → amount → count

Printed pp40-42; BIPM SI definition

Relations

n = m/M aur N = nNₐ. 22 g CO₂ / 44 g mol⁻¹ = 0.5 mol CO₂ molecules. Entity ka naam likho.

Exact aur rounded

1 mol exactly 6.02214076×10²³ specified entities. Is page ke numericals Nₐ ≈6.022×10²³ mol⁻¹ use karte hain.

N versus N₂

0.5 mol N atoms: 7 g. 0.5 mol N₂ molecules: 14 g. Mole number same hone se particle identity same nahin ho jati.
