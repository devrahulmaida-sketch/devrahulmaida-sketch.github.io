1. Teen particles, do locations

Charge aur location alag-alag check karo. · pp 46,49,54

Charge card

Proton +1, neutron 0, electron−1. Proton/neutron about 1 u; electron bahut halka hai, zero-mass particle nahin.

Neutral vs ion

Neutral atom: p=e. Na has p 11,e 11. Na⁺ has p 11,e 10. Electron lose hua, proton nahin; element sodium hi hai.

2. Evidence se nucleus

Rutherford observations ko inference se alag rakho. · pp 47-48

Thomson

Positive sphere mein embedded electrons: equal +/− charges explain neutrality. Concentrated nucleus is model ka part nahin tha.

Gold foil

Most α straight → much empty space; few deflect → concentrated positive charge; rare rebound → tiny dense centre. Source about 1 in 12000 rebound batata hai.

Limit

Orbiting-electron Rutherford picture stability explain nahin kar saka. Classical energy-loss argument model ka problem tha, stable atoms ke vanish hone ka observation nahin.

3. Shell capacity vs occupancy

Historical Bohr model aur first 18 scheme. · pp 49-51

Allowed levels

Bohr postulate: allowed discrete orbit mein electron energy radiate nahin karta. K,L,M,N: n 1,2,3,4. Yeh historical model hai, exact modern path nahin.

Count check

2 n² capacity:2,8,18,32. First 18 examples: Na 2,8,1; Cl 2,8,7; Ar 2,8,8. Capacity 18 ka matlab M shell har example mein 18 filled nahin.

4. Valence electrons ≠ valency

Same word jaisa lagta hai, quantity alag hai. · pp 50-52

Oxygen vs chlorine

O 2,6: valence electrons 6, valency 2. Cl 2,8,7: valence electrons 7, valency 1. Filled-shell explanation introductory scheme hai.

Helium exception

He configuration 2: K shell full, valency 0. Har atom ko octet chahiye kehna sahi nahin; K shell maximum 2 hai.

5. Z, A aur charge

Nucleus ka count aur electrons ka count alag rakho. · p 52

Al-27

Z 13 ⇒13 protons. A 27 ⇒neutrons 27−13=14. Neutral e 13. Al³⁺ mein e 10, par Z 13 aur A 27 unchanged.

Unit check

A=p+n integer count hai, unitless. Atomic mass u mein hai. Average 35.5 u ko ek nucleus ke 35.5 nucleons na samjho.

6. Isotopes vs isobars

Pehle same kya hai, phir different kya hai? · pp 52-53

Same Z

¹H,²H,³H: Z 1 each; A 1,2,3; neutrons 0,1,2. Isliye zero-neutron hydrogen statement protium ke liye hai, all hydrogen isotopes ke liye nahin.

Same A

Ar-40 Z 18 aur Ca-40 Z 20: same A 40,different Z ⇒isobars. Chlorine example average 35×0.75+37×0.25=35.5 u; mixture average, not half a nucleon.
