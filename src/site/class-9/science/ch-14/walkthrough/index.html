1. जीव और संसाधन

Biotic aur abiotic ko connect karo.

जैविक

Organisms biotic components hain. Source p 189 life ko air, water, soil aur Sun energy se jodta hai.

अजैविक

Air, water aur soil abiotic hain. Biosphere inke interactions ka life-supporting zone hai (p 189). Isolated lists nahin.

2. पवन और बादल

Uneven heating se movement; cooling se condensation.

पवन

Simple coastal day model: land faster heat, air rise, sea se surface flow. Raat ko tendency reverse; rotation/terrain bhi matter (pp 190-191).

बादल

Moist rising air expand/cool; vapour droplets mein condense hota hai. Droplets grow karke rain de sakte hain (pp 191-192). Vapour aur visible cloud alag (meri vyakhya).

3. जल की गुणवत्ता

Pollution sirf ganda rang nahin.

जरूरत

Water reactions aur dissolved substances ka transport support karta hai (p 193). Usable water plus soil/temperature life influence karte hain.

प्रदूषण

Undesirable substances add, useful dissolved oxygen reduce, ya harmful temperature change ho sakta hai (p 194). Clear appearance safety test nahin (meri vyakhya).

4. मिट्टी बनना और हटना

Formation aur erosion alag processes hain.

बनना

Weathering: heating/cooling, water, wind aur organisms rock break karne mein help karte hain (p 195). Soil mein humus/life bhi hote hain.

हटना

Wind/flowing water topsoil carry away karte hain: erosion. Roots/vegetative cover loss kam karne mein help karte hain (p 196).

5. चक्र और रूपांतरण

Same element, different forms.

जल

Evaporation → condensation → rain → surface/underground routes. Saara rainwater turant sea nahin pahunchta; organisms bhi part hain (p 197).

नाइट्रोजन

Some bacteria fix nitrogen; plants usable compounds lete, animals food se paate. Recycling aur denitrification atmosphere return karte hain (pp 197-198). Most organisms gas directly use nahin karte.

6. कार्बन और ओजोन

Greenhouse effect ko ozone hole se alag rakho.

कार्बन

Photosynthesis carbon incorporate; respiration/combustion CO₂ return kar sakte hain. Source greenhouse gases ko heat retention se jodta hai (pp 198-199). Old doubling claim current data nahin.

ओजोन

O₂ oxygen gas; O₃ ozone. Upper-atmosphere ozone harmful solar radiation absorb karta hai (p 200), ground inhalation safe nahin. Ozone depletion ≠ greenhouse warming (meri vyakhya).
