1. Tissue: cells se common job tak

Team analogy sirf meri vyakhya hai; tissue ko office team literally mat samjho.

Common function

Tissue cells ka group hai: similar structure aur/or common function. Complex tissue mein different cell types bhi ek job ke liye coordinate karte hain. Source p 68.

Simple vs complex

Simple permanent: ek cell type, jaise parenchyma. Complex: multiple cell types, jaise xylem/phloem. Complex ka matlab sirf bada size nahin. Sources pp 70-72.

2. Growth aur simple support

Location, wall aur function ko alag clues samjho.

Meristems

Apical → root/shoot tips, length. Lateral → girth. Intercalary → kuch plants mein node ke paas. Dividing cells se differentiation ke baad permanent tissue ka basic model. Sources pp 69-70.

Flexible vs stiff

Collenchyma: living, corners par wall thickening, flexible support. Sclerenchyma: lignified thick walls, chapter model mein dead, stiffness/strength. Parenchyma thin-walled living tissue hai. Sources pp 70-71.

3. Cover aur conduct

Xylem/phloem ko ek hi cell type mat banao.

Protection

Epidermis covering; root hairs absorption area; stomata gas exchange/transpiration. Cork ke compact dead cells ki walls mein suberin passage ko rokta hai. Sources pp 71-72.

Xylem

Tracheids, vessels, xylem parenchyma, xylem fibres: four elements. Water/minerals conduct, storage/support bhi. Parenchyma living hai; all-xylem-dead shortcut nahin. Source p 72.

Phloem

Food conducting complex tissue. Chapter list: sieve cells, sieve tubes, companion cells, phloem fibres, phloem parenchyma. Fibres ke alawa cells living batayi hain. Source p 72.

4. Lining aur matrix

Epithelial cells ki close sheet aur connective matrix ko compare karo.

Epithelium

Simple squamous: thin lining. Stratified squamous: protective layers. Columnar: intestine. Ciliated columnar: mucus movement. Cuboidal: kidney tubules. Glandular: secretion. Sources pp 73-74.

Matrix roles

Blood ka fluid matrix plasma; bone ka hard matrix support; cartilage joint surface. Areolar packing/repair; adipose fat storage/insulation. Yeh sab connective examples hain. Sources pp 74-75.

Connection pair

Tendon muscle ko bone se jodta hai; ligament bone ko bone se. Dono strong connective tissues hain, lekin chapter ligament ko relatively elastic aur tendon ko limited flexible batata hai. Source p 75.

5. Muscle control vs bands

Striated ka matlab hamesha voluntary nahin.

Skeletal vs smooth

Skeletal: long cylindrical unbranched multinucleate/banded fibres, mostly bones se attached, voluntary. Smooth: spindle-shaped uninucleate/unbanded, involuntary; gut aur blood vessels examples. Sources pp 75-76.

Cardiac

Heart: rhythmic involuntary contraction/relaxation. Chapter model: branched cylindrical striated uninucleate cells. Isliye cardiac involuntary hote hue bhi striated hai. Source p 76.

6. Nervous tissue

Yeh text labels hain, full neuron diagram nahin.

Neuron parts

Cell body mein nucleus/cytoplasm; usually ek long axon aur many short branched dendrites. Nerve ending labelled part hai. Source p 77.

Signal aur nerve

Signal nerve impulse hai. Many nerve fibres connective tissue se bound hokar nerve banate hain. Nervous aur muscle tissue milkar response/movement coordinate kar sakte hain. Source p 77.
