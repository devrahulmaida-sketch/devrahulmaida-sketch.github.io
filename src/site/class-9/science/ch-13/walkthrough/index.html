Why Do We Fall Ill?

हम बीमार क्यों होते हैं?
6 Text-box Walkthrough scenes + 5 fact questions. Hinglish captions. Animation, video ya lab procedure nahin.

1. स्वास्थ्य का अर्थ

Disease-free aur healthy ko alag samjho.

रोग-मुक्त

Identified disease absent hona narrower condition hai. Source p 177 isko full health ke equal nahin rakhta.

स्वास्थ्य

Physical, mental aur social well-being saath mein. Safe surroundings aur public services individual health se linked hain (pp 176-178).

2. अवधि और गंभीरता

Do alag questions: kitni der, kitna effect?

अवधि

Acute relatively short duration; chronic longer duration. Source pp 178-179 duration ke basis par contrast karta hai.

सावधानी

Short duration = harmless nahin. Symptoms exact cause nahin batate. Yeh distinction meri vyakhya; diagnosis ke liye qualified assessment chahiye.

3. कारणों की परतें

Immediate cause aur background conditions mix mat karo.

तात्कालिक

Source diarrhoea example mein infectious agent immediate cause hai (p 179). Har diarrhoea case ka diagnosis yeh example nahin karta.

सहायक

Unsafe water, poor nourishment aur public-service gaps contribute karte hain. Poverty pathogen nahin; causal levels alag hain (p 179; last distinction meri vyakhya).

4. कारक और वाहक

Carrier ko pathogen mat banao.

कारक

Viruses, bacteria, fungi, protozoa aur worms source groups hain (pp 180-182). Malaria agent protozoan group mein hai.

वाहक

Mosquito malaria example ka vector hai; agent ko hosts ke beech carry karta hai. Route-specific prevention chahiye (pp 182-183).

5. राहत और कारण

Treatment ke aims alag hain.

लक्षणों की राहत

Discomfort kam karna cause remove hone ke equal nahin. Source p 184 symptom-directed aur cause-directed aims separate karta hai.

कारण पर उपचार

Antibiotics bacterial pathways target karte hain; viral cold ka viral component treat nahin karte (p 182). Yeh drug/dose advice nahin; self-medication ka basis nahin.

6. रोकथाम और स्मृति

General aur specific prevention ko saath dekho.

सामान्य

Safe water, sanitation, less overcrowding aur sufficient nourishment exposure/body-function side ko address karte hain (pp 185-187). Guaranteed immunity nahin.

विशिष्ट

Immune memory particular infection par faster response ka basis hai (p 186). Vaccination principle ko current schedule mat samjho; historical deliberate exposure imitate mat karo.
