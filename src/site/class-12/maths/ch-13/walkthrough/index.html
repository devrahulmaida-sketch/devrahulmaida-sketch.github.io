Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo! ⭐

Conditional ⭐⭐

P(A|B) = P(A∩B)/P(B), P(B)≠0 | B ke baad sample space = sirf B | P(A′|B) = 1 − P(A|B)

Multiplication & Independence ⭐⭐

P(A∩B) = P(A)·P(B|A) | Independent: P(A∩B) = P(A)P(B) | Mutually exclusive ≠ INDEPENDENT (trap!) | Without replacement → draws NOT independent

Total Probability ⭐⭐

E₁..Eₙ partition (disjoint, poora S) → P(A) = Σ P(Eᵢ)·P(A|Eᵢ) | Tree: branch pe multiply, across add!

Bayes ⭐⭐

P(Eᵢ|A) = P(Eᵢ)P(A|Eᵢ) / Σⱼ P(Eⱼ)P(A|Eⱼ) | Denominator = TOTAL PROBABILITY (half work!) | "A ho gaya - kis raaste se?" = posterior

Random Variable & Mean ⭐

X = outcome → number | Table: har pᵢ ≥ 0, Σpᵢ = 1 | E(X) = μ = Σxᵢ·pᵢ = long-run average | Variance/binomial DELETED

Exam Tips ⭐

Bayes 5-marker = denominator pehle! | "Without replacement" → independence check | ΣP=1 verify karna aa sakta hai | Tree diagram = full marks ka rasta
