2. Differentiability vs Continuity - Corner Machine ⭐⭐

DIFFERENTIABLE hai to CONTINUOUS ZAROOR - par ULTA TRUE NAHI! Point a pe LEFT slope aur RIGHT slope compare karo: barabar = differentiable (smooth), alag = corner/cusp (fail). [x] to pehle hi toota hua hai - na continuous, na differentiable. ∛x ka vertical tangent bhi fail!

3. Chain Rule - Onion Peeler ⭐⭐

d/dx[f(g(x))] = f′(g(x)) · g′(x) - bahar wale ka derivative (andar AS-IS rakho) × andar wale ka derivative. "Peel the onion" - sabse bahar wali layer pehle! Outer aur Inner chuno, x chalao - symbolic answer ka NUMERIC VERIFY (finite difference) bhi live: dono match hote hain!

4. Log & Implicit Lab - xˣ aur Circle ⭐⭐

(a) y = xˣ: power me bhi x! Dono taraf ln lo: ln y = x ln x → differentiate → dy/dx = xˣ(1 + ln x). (b) x² + y² = r²: y alag nahi hota - dono taraf d/dx, y ke saath dy/dx chipkao: 2x + 2y·dy/dx = 0 → dy/dx = −x/y. Tangent LIVE dekho dono me!

1.4

5. Rapid Quiz - Continuity-Differentiability Pakki? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: continuous ke liye LHL=RHL=f(a); corner pe continuous par differentiable nahi; chain rule = bahar × andar; power me x ho to ln lo; parametric me dy/dx = (dy/dt)/(dx/dt) - sirf t ke respect me mat kar dena!

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo! ⭐

Continuity Definition ⭐⭐

lim(x→a) f(x) = f(a) | TEEN conditions: limit EXISTS (LHL = RHL), f(a) DEFINED, dono EQUAL | Polynomials, sin x, cos x, eˣ HAR JAGAH continuous

Discontinuity Types ⭐

JUMP: LHL ≠ RHL ([x] har integer pe!) | HOLE: limit exists par f(a) undefined/alag | INFINITE: 1/x at 0 | Algebra: sum/diff/product/scalar continuous; quotient jahan denominator ≠ 0

Differentiable ⟹ Continuous ⭐⭐

Ulta FALSE! |x| at 0: continuous HAI, differentiable NAHI (left slope −1, right +1) | Corner, cusp, vertical tangent, break → differentiability FAIL | Ye counterexample exam me pakka!

Chain Rule ⭐⭐

d/dx[f(g(x))] = f′(g(x)) · g′(x) | "Peel the onion": bahar wali layer pehle, andar AS-IS | d/dx sin(x²) = cos(x²)·2x

Exp/Log + Implicit ⭐⭐

d/dx eˣ = eˣ | d/dx aˣ = aˣ ln a | d/dx ln x = 1/x | Power me x: ln lo dono taraf → y = xˣ gives dy/dx = xˣ(1 + ln x) | Implicit: x²+y²=r² → dy/dx = −x/y

Parametric + 2nd Order + DELETED ⭐

dy/dx = (dy/dt)/(dx/dt) | d²y/dx² = d/dt(dy/dx) ÷ dx/dt (90% galti: sirf t ke respect me!) | DELETED from NCERT: Rolle's Theorem & LMVT - ab nahi padhne!
