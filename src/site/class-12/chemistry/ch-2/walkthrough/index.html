1. Daniell Cell - Chemical Energy se Current ⭐

Galvanic cell me spontaneous redox reaction khud electricity banati hai. Zn rod ZnSO4 me ANODE (oxidation: Zn → Zn2+ + 2e-), Cu rod CuSO4 me CATHODE (reduction: Cu2+ + 2e- → Cu). Electrons external circuit se Zn → Cu flow karte hain. Salt bridge ions migrate karke charge balance rakhta hai - solutions mix nahi hote. Cell notation: Zn | Zn2+ || Cu2+ | Cu.

2. Electrochemical Series - Kaun Anode, Kaun Cathode? ⭐

Har electrode ka standard reduction potential (E°) SHE (standard hydrogen electrode, E° = 0 V assigned) ke against measure hota hai. Zyada NEGATIVE E° = strong reducing agent (electron deta hai, anode banega); zyada POSITIVE E° = strong oxidizing agent (electron leta hai, cathode banega). E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode. 3. Nernst Equation - Concentration Ka Asar ⭐⭐

Standard conditions (1 M) pe E° milta hai; concentration badalte hi EMF badalta hai. 298 K pe: E_cell = E°_cell − (0.059/n)·log Q. Reactant ([Cu2+]) zyada → Q chhota → E ZYADA. Battery discharge hoti hai → reactant ghatta → Q badha → EMF girti. Equilibrium pe E = 0 aur Q = K, isliye E° = (0.059/n)·log K. Slider se log Q badlo (Daniell cell, n = 2):

4. Conductance - Dilution Ka Ulta Khel ⭐

κ (conductivity) = solution abhi kitna current le ja raha (per cm); Λm (molar conductivity) = κ×1000/C = PER-MOLE kitna current. Dilution pe κ GHATTI hai (ions per cm3 kam) lekin Λm BADHTI hai (interference kam / dissociation zyada). Strong electrolyte: Λm dheere badhti, extrapolate se Λ°m milti. Weak electrolyte: Λm tez badhti (α badhta) - Λ°m Kohlrausch se nikaalte hain. Slider = concentration:

0.1

5. Electrolysis aur Faraday's Laws - Charge se Mass ⭐⭐

Electrolytic cell me current laga ke NON-spontaneous reaction karvate hain. Faraday's 1st law: deposited mass ∝ charge, m = Z·I·t. Trick: moles of e- = (I×t)/96487, phir stoichiometry (n-factor) se mass. Faraday's 2nd law: same charge pe masses equivalent weights ke proportional. Cells & Notation ⭐

Galvanic: chemical → electrical (spontaneous) | Electrolytic: electrical → chemical (current lagao) | Daniell: Zn anode (oxidation), Cu cathode (reduction), e- external circuit me Zn → Cu | Salt bridge: charge balance, mixing nahi | Zn | Zn2+ || Cu2+ | Cu

E° & Series ⭐

SHE reference = 0 V (Pt, H2 1 bar, H+ 1 M) | E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode | Positive EMF = spontaneous | Negative E° = strong reducing agent (anode) | Positive E° = strong oxidizing agent (cathode)

Nernst ⭐⭐

E = E° − (0.059/n)·log Q (298 K) | Reactant zyada → Q chhota → E zyada | Discharge → EMF girti | Equilibrium pe E = 0, Q = K: E° = (0.059/n)·log K | 0.059/n har numerical me!

ΔG-E-K Triangle ⭐

ΔG = −nF·E_cell | ΔG° = −nF·E° | log K = n·E°/0.059 (298 K) | E° positive → ΔG° negative → K bada → spontaneous | F = 96487 C/mol | Ek pata → teeno pata!

Conductance ⭐

κ = 1/ρ (S/cm) | Λm = κ×1000/C (S·cm2/mol) | Dilution: κ GHATTI, Λm BADHTI | Strong: dheere, extrapolate → Λ°m | Weak: tez, α = Λm/Λ°m, Ka = C·α²/(1−α) | Kohlrausch: Λ°m = ν+·λ°+ + ν−·λ°− (CH3COONa + HCl − NaCl trick)

Faraday + Batteries ⭐⭐

m = Z·I·t | mol e- = Q/96487 | 1 mol Mn+ ko n×96487 C | Same Q → masses ∝ equivalent weights | Lead battery rechargeable, fuel cell H2+O2 (~70%) | Corrosion: Fe + O2 + H2O → Fe2O3·xH2O | Galvanization = Zn sacrificial anode
