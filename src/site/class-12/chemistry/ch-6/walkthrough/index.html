1. Kaunsa Class? - 1° 2° 3°, Allylic ya Vinylic ⭐⭐

Haloalkane: alkane ka H halogen se replace (R-X). Haloarene: benzene ring pe halogen (Ar-X). Haloalkanes ka type: C-X wale carbon ke C-NEIGHBOURS se - 1° (1 neighbour), 2° (2), 3° (3). Allylic (X, C=C ke adjacent C pe) / benzylic (ring ke adjacent C pe) = REACTIVE. Vinylic (X double-bond C pe) / aryl (ring pe) = LAZY (resonance se C-X strong). Molecule dekho, class batao!

2. Banao Machine - Alcohol Se / Finkelstein / Swarts ⭐⭐

Alcohol se: R-OH + HX (HCl ke saath anhydrous ZnCl2 = LUCAS reagent), PCl5, ya SOCl2 (DARZENS - best, kyunki side products SO2 + HCl GAS banke nikal jate). FINKELSTEIN: R-Cl/Br + NaI (dry acetone) → R-I + NaCl/NaBr PPT (acetone me insoluble, reaction aage dhakka). SWARTS: R-Cl/Br + AgF/SbF3 → R-F (fluoride banana mushkil, isliye special method).

3. BP Lab - Bada Halogen, Bada BP ⭐⭐

C-X bond polar (halogen δ-, carbon δ+) phir bhi pani me INSOLUBLE (pani ke saath H-bond nahi bana paate). Same alkyl group pe BP: RI > RBr > RCl > RF (size/mass badhne pe van der Waals badhte). Isomers me branching badhne pe BP GHATTA (surface area kam). Symmetry zyada = MP zyada (para > ortho, crystal packing achhi).

4. SN1 vs SN2 Arena - Order ULTA Hai! ⭐⭐

Nucleophile (Nu-, electron-rich) halogen ko replace karta. SN2: EK step - backside attack, rate = k[R-X][Nu-] (2nd order), 1° FAST (kam steric), INVERSION of configuration (Walden). SN1: DO step - pehle slow ionization se CARBOCATION (RDS), phir Nu attack; rate = k[R-X] (1st order), 3° FAST (carbocation stable), RACEMIZATION. Solvent: SN2 polar APROTIC, SN1 polar PROTIC.

5. KOH Ka Double Role + Saytzeff + Metals ⭐⭐

AQUEOUS KOH → SUBSTITUTION (alcohol banta). ALCOHOLIC KOH → ELIMINATION (HX nikalta, alkene banta - beta-elimination). SAYTZEFF rule: major product woh alkene jisme double bond pe ZYADA alkyl groups (zyada substituted = zyada stable). Metals: WURTZ (2R-X + 2Na, dry ether → R-R, chain DOUBLE), GRIGNARD (R-X + Mg, dry ether → R-MgX, moisture NAHI!).

6. Haloarene Lazy Kyun? + Polyhalogens ⭐⭐

Chlorobenzene nucleophilic substitution me REACTIVE NAHI: (1) RESONANCE - halogen ka lone pair ring se conjugate, C-X ko partial double-bond character, (2) sp2 carbon zyada electronegative = C-X chhota/strong, (3) electron-rich ring nucleophile ko REPEL karta. Sirf harsh conditions: DOW (NaOH, 623 K / 300 atm) → phenol. Electrophilic substitution: halogen DEACTIVATING lekin o/p-DIRECTING. Polyhalogens: DDT aur Freons (CFCs) = environment villains!

7. Rapid Quiz - Haloalkanes Pakka? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: SN2 me 1° fast / SN1 me 3° fast, Finkelstein = NaI, alc KOH + Saytzeff = zyada substituted alkene, haloarene lazy (resonance + sp2), BP order RI > RBr > RCl > RF.

Classification ⭐⭐

Haloalkane R-X, haloarene Ar-X | mono (CH3Cl), di (CH2Cl2), tri (CHCl3), poly (CCl4) | 1°/2°/3° = C-X wale C ke C-NEIGHBOURS (1/2/3) | Allylic/benzylic = REACTIVE | Vinylic/aryl = LAZY (resonance se C-X strong) | IUPAC: halo prefix (2-chlorobutane)

Preparation ⭐⭐

Alcohol + HCl needs anhydr. ZnCl2 (LUCAS) | PCl5, PCl3 bhi | SOCl2 = DARZENS = BEST (SO2+HCl gas nikal jate) | FINKELSTEIN: R-Cl/Br + NaI (dry acetone) → R-I (NaCl ppt aage dhakka) | SWARTS: AgF/SbF3 → R-F | Free-radical halogenation = mixture (limited use)

Physical Properties ⭐⭐

BP: RI > RBr > RCl > RF (bada halogen = zyada van der Waals) | Branching badhe = BP GHATTA | Symmetry zyada = MP zyada (para > ortho) | Pani me INSOLUBLE (no H-bond) | Bromo/iodo-alkanes pani se HEAVY | C-X polar: halogen δ-, carbon δ+

SN1 vs SN2 ⭐⭐

SN2: 1 step BACKSIDE attack | rate = k[RX][Nu] (2nd order) | 1° FAST | INVERSION (Walden) | polar APROTIC solvent | SN1: 2 step, CARBOCATION pehle (RDS) | rate = k[RX] (1st order) | 3° FAST | RACEMIZATION | polar PROTIC | Order ULTA! | Leaving group: RI>RBr>RCl>RF | Ambident: KCN→R-CN, AgCN→R-NC

Elimination + Metals ⭐⭐

ALCOHOLIC KOH = ELIMINATION → alkene | AQUEOUS KOH = SUBSTITUTION → alcohol | SAYTZEFF: zyada substituted alkene = MAJOR | Bulky base / 3° halide = elimination favour | WURTZ: 2R-X + 2Na (dry ether) → R-R (chain double) | GRIGNARD: R-X + Mg (dry ether) → R-MgX (moisture NAHI)

Haloarenes + Polyhalogen ⭐⭐

Haloarene Nu-substitution me LAZY (resonance C-X partial double + sp2 strong + ring Nu ko repel) | DOW: +NaOH 623 K/300 atm → phenol | -NO2 o/p pe = substitution aasaan | Electrophilic: deactivating par o/p-DIRECTING | Wurtz-Fittig → alkylbenzene | CHCl3 liver toxic | CHI3 yellow antiseptic | DDT bio-magnification (banned) | Freons/CFC = ozone hole (Montreal)
