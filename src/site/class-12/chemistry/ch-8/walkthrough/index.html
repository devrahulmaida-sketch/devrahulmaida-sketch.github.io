1. Carbonyl Identifier - Aldehyde, Ketone ya Acid? ⭐

Carbonyl C=O polar hai: O par delta-, C par delta+ - isliye nucleophile CARBON pe attack karta (chapter ka main theme). Aldehyde = C=O end pe (ek H zaroori, R-CHO), ketone = beech me (do alkyl, R-CO-R'), carboxylic acid = -COOH. Carbonyl carbon sp2 = trigonal planar (~120 deg).

2. Preparation Map - Naam + Product Yaad Karo ⭐⭐

Rosenmund = acyl chloride se ALDEHYDE (Pd/BaSO4 catalyst ko poison karta, over-reduction rukti). Alkyne hydration (HgSO4/H2SO4) = Markovnikov - acetylene se acetaldehyde, baaki se ketones. Friedel-Crafts acylation = aryl ketone. Stephen = nitrile se aldehyde. Har route ka reactant - reagent - product dekho.

3. Nucleophilic Addition - Mechanism Step by Step ⭐⭐

Nu- carbonyl carbon (delta+) pe attack karta, pi electrons oxygen pe shift, phir H+ oxygen ko milta. Example: HCN addition = cyanohydrin (C-C bond banta, chain badhti). Reactivity: HCHO > RCHO > R2CO - aldehyde me kam steric + kam electron-donating alkyl (C zyada delta+). Step slider chalao.

4. Test Lab - Kaunsa Test Kya Deta? ⭐⭐

Tollens' (ammoniacal AgNO3) = SILVER MIRROR, Fehling's (alkaline Cu2+) = RED-BROWN Cu2O ppt - dono SIRF aldehydes dete. 2,4-DNP = orange-yellow ppt SABHI carbonyls dete. Iodoform (I2/NaOH) = yellow CHI3 ppt sirf CH3-CO- wale (methyl ketones + acetaldehyde eklauta aldehyde). Compound chuno, test dabao.

5. Acidity Comparator - Carboxylic Acid Kyun Strong? ⭐⭐

Carboxylate ion (RCOO-) RESONANCE-stabilized (dono equivalent structures, charge equally share) - isliye acid > phenol > water > alcohol. Electron-withdrawing group (-I) acidity BADHATA: FCH2COOH > ClCH2COOH > BrCH2COOH > CH3COOH. NaHCO3 test: sirf carboxylic acid CO2 bubbles deta (phenol nahi).

6. Rapid Quiz - Carbonyl Pakka? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: Tollens/Fehling sirf aldehyde, iodoform sirf CH3-CO wale, Rosenmund = aldehyde, reactivity HCHO > RCHO > R2CO, acidity me EWG = badhata, NaHCO3 test = CO2 bubbles sirf acid.

Carbonyl + Naming ⭐

C=O polar: O delta-, C delta+ (Nu- carbon pe attack) | Carbonyl C sp2, trigonal planar ~120 deg | Aldehyde = R-CHO (end, ek H) suffix -al | Ketone = R-CO-R' (beech) suffix -one + position | Acid = -COOH suffix -oic acid | Aldehyde ka C hamesha C-1

Preparation ⭐⭐

Rosenmund: RCOCl + H2 (Pd/BaSO4 poisoned) = ALDEHYDE | Stephen: RCN + SnCl2/HCl = aldehyde | Alkyne + H2O (HgSO4/H2SO4): acetylene = acetaldehyde, baaki = ketone | Etard: toluene + CrO2Cl2 = benzaldehyde | Friedel-Crafts acylation: benzene + RCOCl (AlCl3) = aryl ketone | 1 deg alcohol + PCC = aldehyde (strong oxidant = acid tak jata)

Physical Properties ⭐

Carbonyls POLAR par self H-bond NAHI (O-H nahi) to BP: alcohol > carbonyl > alkane | Chhote aldehydes/ketones pani me soluble (water se H-bond) | CARBOXYLIC ACIDS: H-bond DIMER banate (vapour me bhi) = HIGHEST BP | Chain badhe to solubility ghate

Nucleophilic Addition ⭐⭐

Nu- attack carbonyl C, phir H+ O pe | Reactivity: HCHO > RCHO > R2CO (steric + +I) | +HCN = cyanohydrin | +NaHSO3 = bisulphite product (purification) | +RMgX phir H2O = alcohol (HCHO=1 deg, RCHO=2 deg, ketone=3 deg) | +alcohol (dry HCl) = acetal (protection) | +2,4-DNP = ORANGE PPT (carbonyl test)

Tests + Named Reactions ⭐⭐

TOLLENS (ammoniacal AgNO3) = silver mirror, FEHLING (alkaline Cu2+) = red Cu2O - SIRF ALDEHYDE | Iodoform (I2/NaOH) = yellow CHI3 - sirf CH3-CO- wale (+CH3CHO) | ALDOL: alpha-H wale + dilute NaOH = beta-hydroxy, heat = enal | CANNIZZARO: alpha-H NAHI wale (HCHO, C6H5CHO) + conc KOH = acid salt + alcohol | Clemmensen (Zn-Hg/HCl) / Wolff-Kishner (NH2NH2/KOH): C=O = CH2

Carboxylic Acids ⭐⭐

SABSE acidic: carboxylate resonance (2 equivalent structures) | Order: acid > phenol > water > alcohol | EWG (-I) acidity UP: FCH2 > ClCH2 > BrCH2 > CH3COOH | NaHCO3 = CO2 bubbles (acid test, phenol nahi deta) | Prep: RMgX + CO2 = acid (chain +1), nitrile hydrolysis | HVZ: Br2/red P = alpha-bromo acid | Soda lime = decarboxylation (alkane)
