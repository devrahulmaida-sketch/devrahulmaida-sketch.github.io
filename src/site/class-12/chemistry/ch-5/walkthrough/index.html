1. Werner Ka Sphere Lab - Do Valencies ⭐

Werner (1893): metal ke paas DO valencies. Primary = ionizable (counter ions, sphere ke BAHAR - AgNO3 test dete hain). Secondary = non-ionizable (ligands sphere ke ANDAR, fixed direction = CN). Slider se Cl ko sphere ke andar/bahar karo aur dekho AgCl ppt kaise badalta hai.

2. Ligand Lab - Denticity, CN aur Oxidation Number ⭐⭐

Ligand = electron pair donor (Lewis base). Denticity = kitne donor atoms (NH3 = 1, en = 2, EDTA = 6). Bidentate+ jo RING banaye = chelate (extra stable). CN = donor atoms ki TOTAL ginti. Metal ka oxidation number = sphere ke charge ka hisab-kitab.

3. IUPAC Naam Builder - Step by Step ⭐⭐

Cation pehle, phir complex. Complex ke andar: ligands ALPHABETICAL (prefix ke saath), phir metal + (oxidation state Roman me). Anionic ligand = -o (chlorido, cyano); neutral exceptions: H2O = aqua, NH3 = ammine, CO = carbonyl. Anion complex ho to metal + -ate (ferrate). Step slider se naam ka hissa-hissa dekho.

3b. Isomerism Studio - Cis, Trans aur Mirror ⭐⭐

Same formula, alag arrangement. GEOMETRICAL (cis = same side / trans = opposite): square planar [MA2B2] aur octahedral [MA4B2] me. Cisplatin (cis-[Pt(NH3)2Cl2]) = cancer drug! OPTICAL: chiral mirror images, chelate [M(AA)3] jaise [Co(en)3]3+ (d aur l forms).

4. CFT d-Splitting Lab - Colour + Magnetism ⭐⭐

Octahedral field me d-orbitals TOOT jaate: t2g (3, neeche) + eg (2, upar), gap = Δo. Weak ligand (F-, H2O) = chhota gap, electrons pair NAHI (high spin). Strong (CN-, CO) = bada gap, t2g me PAIR (low spin). Colour = d-d transition se absorb hua complementary colour. d0/d10 = colourless.

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo! ⭐

Werner's Theory ⭐

Primary valency = IONIZABLE = oxidation state, counter ions sphere ke bahar satisfy karte (AgNO3 test dete) | Secondary = NON-IONIZABLE = coordination number, ligands andar, space me FIXED direction | [Co(NH3)6]Cl3 + AgNO3 = 3 mol AgCl (teeno Cl bahar) | [CoCl2(NH3)4]Cl = sirf 1 mol AgCl

Ligand + CN ⭐⭐

Ligand = e- pair donor (Lewis base), metal = acceptor (Lewis acid) | Denticity: unidentate (NH3, Cl-), bidentate (en, C2O4²-), hexadentate (EDTA) | Bidentate+ ring = CHELATE = zyada stable | CN = donor atoms ki ginti: [Co(en)3]3+ me 3x2 = 6 | CN 4 = SP/tetra, CN 6 = octahedral

Oxidation Number ⭐⭐

Hisab: complex charge = metal ox + (ligand charges ka sum) | [Fe(CN)6]4-: Fe + 6(-1) = -4 to Fe = +2 | [Cu(NH3)4]SO4: SO4²- hai to complex +2, NH3 neutral, Cu = +2 | [CoCl2(NH3)4]+: Co + 2(-1) = +1 to Co = +3

IUPAC Naming ⭐⭐

Cation PEHLE, anion baad me | Complex me: ligands ALPHABETICAL (di/tri ya bis/tris), phir metal + (Roman ox state) | Anionic ligand = -o: chlorido, cyano, hydroxo, sulphato | Exceptions: H2O = aqua, NH3 = ammine, CO = carbonyl | Anion complex = metal + -ate: K4[Fe(CN)6] = potassium hexacyanidoferrate(II)

Isomerism ⭐⭐

STRUCTURAL: ionization ([Co(NH3)5SO4]Br vs [Co(NH3)5Br]SO4), linkage (NO2 N-se vs ONO O-se), coordination, hydrate | STEREO: geometrical cis/trans - square planar [MA2B2] + octahedral [MA4B2]; optical - chiral [M(AA)3] jaise [Co(en)3]3+ (d/l) | Cisplatin = CIS-[Pt(NH3)2Cl2] = cancer drug

CFT ⭐⭐

Octahedral: d split = t2g (3 neeche) + eg (2 upar), gap Δo | Series: I- < Br- < Cl- < F- < H2O < NH3 < en < NO2- < CN- < CO | Weak = high spin (no pairing), Strong = low spin (pair) | Colour = d-d transition (complementary dikhta); d0/d10 colourless | Applications: chlorophyll (Mg), haemoglobin (Fe), B12 (Co), cisplatin (Pt)
