Werner theory, IUPAC naming, isomerism, Valence Bond aur Crystal Field Theory

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Werner's Theory — Coordination Ka Foundation

  • Double salts vs coordination compounds: double salts (jaise Mohr's salt, carnallite) pani me simple ions me toot jate (saare ions test dete). Coordination compounds (jaise [Cu(NH₃)₄]SO₄) pani me complex ion banate jo intact rehta — central metal apne ions ka test nahi deta.
  • Werner's theory ⭐ (1893): metal ke paas do type ki valency — primary (oxidation state) = ionizable, negative ions satisfy karte (sphere ke bahar); secondary (coordination number) = non-ionizable, ligands satisfy karte, space me fixed direction (sphere ke andar).
  • Example [Co(NH₃)₆]Cl₃: 3 Cl⁻ ionizable (AgNO₃ se 3 mol AgCl) = primary valency 3; 6 NH₃ non-ionizable (sphere me) = secondary valency 6. Modern form: [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺ 3Cl⁻.
  • Coordination sphere: square bracket [ ] ke andar central metal + ligands = coordination entity (non-ionizable). Bracket ke bahar = ionization sphere (counter ions, ionizable).

💡 Werner = "do darwaze" — andar (sphere) ka maal fixed hai (ligands, CN), bahar (counter ions) ka ionize hota. AgNO₃ test se pata chalta kitne Cl⁻ bahar hain!

2️⃣ Key Terms — Ligand, CN, Oxidation Number ⭐⭐

  • Central metal atom/ion: coordination entity ka center (jaise Co³⁺, Fe²⁺, Ni⁰) — electron pairs accept karta (Lewis acid).
  • Ligand ⭐: electron pair donate karne wala ion/molecule (Lewis base), central metal se coordinate bond banata. Denticity = kitne donor atoms: unidentate (1: NH₃, H₂O, Cl⁻), bidentate (2: en, oxalate C₂O₄²⁻), polydentate (6: EDTA). Bidentate+ jo ring banaye = chelate ligand (en, EDTA) — chelate complex zyada stable.
  • Coordination number (CN): central metal se jude donor atoms ki ginti. Common: 4 (square planar/tetrahedral) aur 6 (octahedral). Example: [Co(en)₃]³⁺ me CN = 6 (en bidentate, 3×2).
  • Oxidation number ⭐: sphere ka charge nikalo — [Cu(NH₃)₄]SO₄: SO₄²⁻ hai to complex +2; NH₃ neutral, to Cu = +2. [Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻: CN⁻ 6×(−1)=−6, complex −4, to Fe = −4+6 = +2.
  • Coordination polyhedron: donor atoms ka spatial arrangement — CN 4 = tetrahedral ya square planar; CN 6 = octahedral.

💡 Oxidation number nikalna = "hisab-kitab": counter ion ka charge → sphere ka charge → ligand charges ghatao → metal ka number. Chelate (en, EDTA) = "pakad wale" ligand, ring banake zyada stable!

3️⃣ IUPAC Nomenclature — Naam Kaise Likhte ⭐⭐

  • Basic rule: pehle cation, phir anion (jaise normal salts). Complex ion ke andar: pehle ligands (alphabetical), phir metal + oxidation state (Roman numeral).
  • Ligand naming ⭐: anionic ligands end in −o (Cl⁻=chlorido, CN⁻=cyano, OH⁻=hydroxo, SO₄²⁻=sulphato); neutral ligands waise hi — lekin exceptions: H₂O=aqua, NH₃=ammine, CO=carbonyl, NO=nitrosyl. Ginti ke liye: di/tri/tetra (simple) ya bis/tris/tetrakis (complex ligand naam pehle se di/tri rakhta, jaise en).
  • Metal naming: complex cation ya neutral ho to metal ka normal naam (cobalt, iron). Complex anion ho to metal ke naam me −ate (ferrate, cobaltate, zincate, argentate for Ag).
  • Examples ⭐: [Co(NH₃)₆]Cl₃ = hexaamminecobalt(III) chloride. K₄[Fe(CN)₆] = potassium hexacyanidoferrate(II). [CoCl₂(NH₃)₄]⁺ = tetraamminedichloridocobalt(III) ion. [Ni(CO)₄] = tetracarbonylnickel(0).
  • Order in formula: bracket me pehle metal symbol, phir ligands (anionic pehle ya alphabetical per convention) — lekin naam me ligands hamesha alphabetical.

💡 Formula: ligands alphabetical, metal last + (oxidation state). Anion complex = metal+ate. aqua/ammine/carbonyl exceptions rat lo — nomenclature 2-3 marks ka pakka question!

4️⃣ Isomerism — Same Formula, Alag Structure ⭐⭐

  • Isomerism: same molecular formula, different structure/arrangement → different compounds. Do bade types.
  • Structural isomerism ⭐: (a) Ionization: alag ions solution me dete — [Co(NH₃)₅SO₄]Br (Br⁻ ionizable) vs [Co(NH₃)₅Br]SO₄ (SO₄²⁻ ionizable). (b) Linkage (ambidentate ligand): ligand alag atom se jude — [Co(NH₃)₅NO₂]²⁺ (nitro, N se) vs [Co(NH₃)₅ONO]²⁺ (nitrito, O se). NO₂⁻, SCN⁻ ambidentate. (c) Coordination: dono ions me ligands interchange — [Co(NH₃)₆][Cr(CN)₆] vs [Cr(NH₃)₆][Co(CN)₆]. (d) Solvate/hydrate: water sphere ke andar/bahar — [Cr(H₂O)₆]Cl₃ vs [CrCl(H₂O)₅]Cl₂·H₂O.
  • Stereoisomerism ⭐: same bonds, different space arrangement. (a) Geometrical (cis/trans): square planar [MA₂B₂] (jaise [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂]: cis = same side, trans = opposite) aur octahedral [MA₄B₂] (jaise [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]⁺) me hota. Cisplatin (cis) = cancer drug! (b) Optical: non-superimposable mirror images (chiral) — octahedral [M(AA)₃] jaise [Co(en)₃]³⁺ me hota (d aur l forms, plane-polarized light rotate karte).

💡 Structural = "jodne ka tarika alag" (ionization/linkage/coordination/hydrate). Stereo = "space me alag" (cis/trans + optical). Cis/trans CN4 aur CN6 me, optical chelate [M(en)₃] me — yeh do yaad rakho!

5️⃣ Bonding — VBT aur Crystal Field Theory ⭐⭐

  • Valence Bond Theory (VBT): ligand ka lone pair metal ke hybridized empty orbitals me jata. CN 4: sp³ = tetrahedral, dsp² = square planar. CN 6: sp³d² (outer orbital, n d use) ya d²sp³ (inner orbital, (n−1)d use). Magnetic behaviour se pata chalta kaunsa — [FeF₆]³⁻ paramagnetic (sp³d², weak F⁻), [Fe(CN)₆]³⁻ less paramagnetic (d²sp³, strong CN⁻ pair karta).
  • Crystal Field Theory (CFT) ⭐⭐: ligands metal ke d-orbitals ko split karte. Octahedral me: d-orbitals do sets me — t₂g (lower, 3 orbitals) aur eg (higher, 2 orbitals), gap = Δo (crystal field splitting energy).
  • Splitting depends on ligand strength ⭐: spectrochemical series — I⁻ < Br⁻ < Cl⁻ < F⁻ < H₂O < NH₃ < en < NO₂⁻ < CN⁻ < CO. Weak field (F⁻, H₂O): chhota Δo, electrons pair NAHI hote (high spin, zyada unpaired). Strong field (CN⁻, CO): bada Δo, electrons pair ho jate t₂g me (low spin, kam unpaired).
  • Colour explain ⭐: electron t₂g se eg me d-d transition karke visible light absorb karta — jo colour absorb nahi hua woh dikhta. Isliye [Ti(H₂O)₆]³⁺ purple. d⁰/d¹⁰ me transition nahi → colourless.
  • Magnetic + stability: CFSE (crystal field stabilization energy) se stability aur unpaired electrons se magnetic moment decide hota — strong field low spin = kam unpaired (diamagnetic possible).

💡 CFT = "d-orbitals ka tootna" — octahedral me t₂g (neeche) + eg (upar), gap Δo. Strong ligand (CN⁻) = bada gap = electrons pair (low spin, colour change). Spectrochemical series CN⁻>NH₃>H₂O>F⁻ rat lo — colour aur magnetism dono isse solve!

6️⃣ Applications — Real Life Me Coordination

  • Qualitative analysis: complex formation se metal ions identify — jaise Ni²⁺ + DMG (dimethylglyoxime) = rosy-red precipitate; Cu²⁺ + NH₃ = deep blue [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺.
  • Metallurgy: Au/Ag extraction me cyanide complex — 4Au + 8CN⁻ + 2H₂O + O₂ → 4[Au(CN)₂]⁻ + 4OH⁻. Mond process (Ni purification): Ni + 4CO → Ni(CO)₄ (volatile) → heat → pure Ni.
  • Medicine ⭐: cisplatin [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂] = cancer chemotherapy. EDTA = lead poisoning treatment (chelate therapy, Ca-EDTA). Excess copper/iron remove karne me chelating agents.
  • Biological ⭐: chlorophyll (Mg complex, photosynthesis), haemoglobin (Fe²⁺ complex, O₂ transport), vitamin B12 (Co complex). Nature bhi coordination chemistry karti hai!
  • Electroplating/photography: Ag plating me [Ag(CN)₂]⁻; photography me hypo (Na₂S₂O₃) se unexposed AgBr dissolve as [Ag(S₂O₃)₂]³⁻.

💡 Coordination har jagah — chlorophyll (Mg), haemoglobin (Fe), B12 (Co), cisplatin (Pt cancer drug), Au extraction (CN⁻). Nature aur industry dono chelates use karte!

1️⃣ Werner सिद्धांत

  • प्राथमिक संयोजकता = आयनन योग्य (ऑक्सीकरण अवस्था)। द्वितीयक = अन-आयनन योग्य (उपसहसंयोजन संख्या)। उपसहसंयोजन गोले [ ] के अंदर लिगंड + धातु अखंड रहते हैं।

💡 गोले के बाहर आयनन योग्य।

2️⃣ मुख्य पद ⭐

  • लिगंड = e⁻ युग्म दाता। उपसहसंयोजन संख्या (CN) = दाता परमाणुओं की संख्या। कीलेट (en, EDTA) अधिक स्थायी। ऑक्सीकरण संख्या आवेश-हिसाब से।

💡 CN = दाता परमाणु।

3️⃣ IUPAC नामकरण ⭐

  • धनायन पहले, लिगंड वर्णानुक्रम में। ऋणायनी लिगंड −o (क्लोरिडो)। H₂O=aqua, NH₃=ammine। ऋणायनी संकुल में धातु −ate।

💡 aqua/ammine अपवाद याद रखें।

4️⃣ समावयवता ⭐

  • संरचनात्मक: आयनन, लिंकेज (NO₂⁻/ONO⁻), उपसहसंयोजन, हाइड्रेट। स्थिर: ज्यामितीय (cis/trans, CN4/CN6), प्रकाशिक ([M(AA)₃] चिरल)।

💡 cis/trans CN4 व CN6 में।

5️⃣ CFT ⭐⭐

  • अष्टफलकीय में d-कक्षक t₂g (निचले) + eg (ऊपरी) में विभक्त, अंतर Δo। प्रबल लिगंड (CN⁻) = युग्मन (low spin)। d-d संक्रमण = रंग। स्पेक्ट्रोरासायनिक श्रेणी: CN⁻>NH₃>H₂O>F⁻।

💡 प्रबल लिगंड = युग्मन + रंग।

6️⃣ अनुप्रयोग

  • सिस्प्लेटिन (कैंसर), क्लोरोफिल (Mg), हीमोग्लोबिन (Fe), B12 (Co), Au निष्कर्षण (CN⁻), EDTA (सीसा विषाक्तता)।

💡 प्रकृति भी संकुल बनाती है।

1️⃣ Werner's Theory

  • Primary valency = ionizable (oxidation state), satisfied by counter ions outside the sphere. Secondary valency = non-ionizable (coordination number), satisfied by ligands inside the coordination sphere [ ].

💡 The sphere stays intact; outer ions ionize.

2️⃣ Key Terms ⭐

  • Ligand = electron-pair donor (Lewis base). Coordination number (CN) = number of donor atoms. Chelate ligands (en, EDTA) form rings and are extra stable. Oxidation number comes from charge balance.

💡 CN counts donor atoms.

3️⃣ IUPAC Naming ⭐

  • Cation first, ligands alphabetical, metal last with (oxidation state). Anionic ligands end in −o (chlorido); exceptions H₂O=aqua, NH₃=ammine, CO=carbonyl. Anionic complex → metal + −ate (ferrate).

💡 Learn the aqua/ammine exceptions.

4️⃣ Isomerism ⭐

  • Structural: ionization, linkage (NO₂/ONO), coordination, hydrate. Stereo: geometrical (cis/trans in CN 4 & 6) and optical (chiral [M(AA)₃]). Cisplatin (cis) is a cancer drug.

💡 cis/trans in CN 4 and CN 6.

5️⃣ CFT ⭐⭐

  • In an octahedral field, d-orbitals split into t₂g (lower) + eg (upper), gap Δo. Strong-field ligands (CN⁻, CO) cause pairing (low spin); weak-field (F⁻, H₂O) give high spin. d-d transitions cause colour. Spectrochemical series: CN⁻>NH₃>H₂O>F⁻.

💡 Strong field = pairing + colour.

6️⃣ Applications

  • Cisplatin (cancer), chlorophyll (Mg), haemoglobin (Fe), vitamin B12 (Co), Au extraction (CN⁻), EDTA chelation therapy, Ni purification (Mond, Ni(CO)₄).

💡 Nature uses coordination compounds too.

JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Werner theory ki primary aur secondary valency batao.
Answer dekho

Primary = ionizable (oxidation state, counter ions se)। Secondary = non-ionizable (coordination number, ligands se, sphere ke andar).

Q2 Ligand kya hota hai? Bidentate ka example.
Answer dekho

Electron pair donate karne wala ion/molecule (Lewis base). Bidentate (2 donor atoms): en (ethylenediamine), oxalate.

Q3 [Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻ me Fe ka oxidation number nikalo.
Answer dekho

CN⁻ 6×(−1) = −6; complex −4; to Fe = −4 + 6 = +2.

Q4 [Co(NH₃)₆]Cl₃ ka IUPAC naam.
Answer dekho

Hexaamminecobalt(III) chloride.

Q5 Anionic complex me metal ka naam kaise badalta?
Answer dekho

Metal ke naam me −ate jodta — jaise ferrate, cobaltate, zincate. Example: K₄[Fe(CN)₆] = potassium hexacyanidoferrate(II).

Q6 Linkage isomerism kya hai? Example.
Answer dekho

Ambidentate ligand alag donor atom se jude. [Co(NH₃)₅NO₂]²⁺ (nitro, N se) vs [Co(NH₃)₅ONO]²⁺ (nitrito, O se). NO₂⁻/SCN⁻ ambidentate.

Q7 Geometrical isomerism kin me hoti hai?
Answer dekho

cis/trans — square planar [MA₂B₂] (jaise [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂]) aur octahedral [MA₄B₂] me. Cisplatin (cis) = cancer drug.

Q8 Octahedral field me d-orbital splitting kaise hoti?
Answer dekho

d-orbitals t₂g (3, lower) aur eg (2, higher) me split hote; energy gap = Δo. Strong ligand bada Δo deta.

Q9 Strong aur weak field ligand me farak (spin).
Answer dekho

Strong field (CN⁻, CO): bada Δo → electrons pair (low spin, kam unpaired). Weak field (F⁻, H₂O): chhota Δo → high spin (zyada unpaired).

Q10 Coordination compounds ka colour kyun hota hai?
Answer dekho

d-d transition — electron t₂g se eg me visible light absorb karke excite hota; complementary colour dikhta. d⁰/d¹⁰ colourless.
