1. Rate of Reaction - Average vs Instantaneous ⭐

Kinetics = reaction kitni TEZ chalti hai. Rate = concentration change ÷ time (mol L-1 s-1). Average rate = poore interval ka; instantaneous rate = ek instant pe graph ki TANGENT slope, r_inst = -d[R]/dt. Curve dekho: reactant ghat-ta hai, product badhta hai - aur time ke saath rate bhi ghat-ti hai. Slider se time point badlo, tangent dekho. Stoichiometry yaad rakho: aA + bB → cC + dD me rate = -(1/a)d[A]/dt = +(1/c)d[C]/dt - coefficient se divide karna mat bhoolo!

2. Rate Law aur Order - Experiment Ka Result ⭐⭐

Rate law EXPERIMENT se aata hai: Rate = k·[A]^x·[B]^y. Overall order = x + y (coefficients se NAHI aata - fractional ya 0 bhi ho sakta). k sirf temperature pe depend karta hai; k ki UNIT order se nikalti: order 0 → mol L-1 s-1, order 1 → s-1, order 2 → L mol-1 s-1. Molecularity alag cheez: elementary step me takrane wale molecules ki ginti (hamesha integer 1/2/3). Order chuno, [A] badlo, rate ka curve dekho:

3. Integrated Rate Laws - Zero vs First Order ⭐⭐

ZERO order: rate = k (concentration pe depend NAHI), [R] = [R]0 − kt → straight line graph, t1/2 = [R]0/2k (half-life [R]0 pe DEPEND karti). FIRST order: rate = k[R], k = (2.303/t)·log([R]0/[R]) → exponential decay, t1/2 = 0.693/k (CONSTANT - radioactive decay isliye first order!). Toggle karo aur half-life ka behavior dekho:

4. Arrhenius Equation - Temperature ka Power ⭐⭐

k = A·e^(−Ea/RT). T badhao → k EXPONENTIALLY badhta (har 10°C pe rate ~double, temperature coefficient ≈ 2-3). Ea = activation energy - reaction ka energy barrier. Catalyst Ea GHATA deta (alternate path) → k badh jata, lekin equilibrium shift NAHI karta. Do-temperature formula: log(k2/k1) = (Ea/2.303R)·[(1/T1) − (1/T2)] - Ea numericals ka favourite. 5. Collision Theory - Har Takraana Kaafi Nahi ⭐

Reaction tabhi jab molecules takrate hain - lekin EFFECTIVE collision ke liye DO shartein: (1) proper orientation, (2) energy ≥ Ea. Rate = Z (collision frequency) × e^(−Ea/RT) (E≥Ea wala fraction) × P (orientation factor). T badhane pe zyada molecules Ea paar karte (Maxwell-Boltzmann shift) → effective collisions badhti. 6. Rapid Quiz - Kinetics Pakki? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: stoichiometric rate me coefficient se divide, order experimental + fractional possible, first order t1/2 = 0.693/k constant, har 10°C pe rate ~double, catalyst Ea ghatata hai (equilibrium shift nahi).

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo! ⭐

Rate ⭐

Rate = −Δ[reactant]/Δt = +Δ[product]/Δt (mol L-1 s-1) | Average = interval ka | Instantaneous = tangent slope −d[R]/dt | aA + bB → cC + dD: rate = −(1/a)d[A]/dt = +(1/c)d[C]/dt | Coefficient se divide mat bhoolo!

Rate Law & Order ⭐⭐

Rate = k·[A]^x·[B]^y (EXPERIMENTAL) | Order = x+y (fractional/0 possible) | k sirf T pe depend | k units: order 0 → mol L-1 s-1, 1 → s-1, 2 → L mol-1 s-1 | Molecularity: elementary step ke molecules (integer 1/2/3 only) | Complex reaction = slowest step decide karta

Zero Order ⭐

Rate = k (conc independent) | [R] = [R]0 − kt (straight line, slope −k) | t1/2 = [R]0/2k ([R]0 pe DEPEND) | Examples: NH3 on Pt, photochemical

First Order ⭐⭐

Rate = k[R] | k = (2.303/t)·log([R]0/[R]) | log[R]0/[R] vs t → straight line | t1/2 = 0.693/k (CONSTANT!) | Radioactive decay, N2O5, H2O2 | Pseudo first order: ek reactant excess (ester hydrolysis)

Arrhenius ⭐⭐

k = A·e^(−Ea/RT) | log k = log A − Ea/2.303RT | log(k2/k1) = (Ea/2.303R)·[(1/T1) − (1/T2)] | 10°C rise → rate ~double (coeff 2-3) | Ea = energy barrier (activated complex peak pe)

Collision & Catalyst ⭐

Effective collision = proper orientation + E ≥ Ea | Rate = Z × e^(−Ea/RT) × P | T badhao → E≥Ea fraction badhta | Catalyst: lower Ea ka alternate path, khud consume nahi, EQUILIBRIUM SHIFT NAHI - sirf jaldi pahunchata
