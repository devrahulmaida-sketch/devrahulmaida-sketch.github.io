1. Configuration Inspector - Transition ya Fake? ⭐⭐

Transition elements: atom ya common ion me INCOMPLETELY filled d-orbital (d1-d9). General config (n-1)d1-10 ns0-2. Exceptions: Cr = 3d5 4s1 (half-filled stable), Cu = 3d10 4s1 (full d stable). Zn/Cd/Hg transition NAHI (d10 full). Ion bante waqt ns electrons PEHLE nikalte: Fe2+ = 3d6, Fe3+ = 3d5.

2. Oxidation States - Variable Wala Funda ⭐⭐

(n-1)d aur ns electrons ki energy almost BARABAR - isliye transition metals variable oxidation states dikhate (chapter ka sabse bada point). Maximum state middle of series pe: Mn +7 (KMnO4), Cr +6 (K2Cr2O7). d5 half-filled (Mn2+, Fe3+) aur d10 full (Zn2+) EXTRA stable. Higher state (MnO4-, Cr2O7 2-) = strong oxidizing agent.

3. Colour + Magnetism Lab - d-d Dance ⭐⭐

Coloured ions: partially filled d-orbital me electron visible light absorb karke d-d transition karta, baaki colour dikhta. d0 (Sc3+, Ti4+) ya d10 (Zn2+, Cu+) = NO d-d transition = COLOURLESS. Magnetic moment mu = sqrt(n(n+2)) BM (n = unpaired electrons). n zyada = zyada paramagnetic; sab paired = diamagnetic.

4. KMnO4 + K2Cr2O7 - Medium Wala Game ⭐⭐

KMnO4 (purple, +7) strong oxidizer: ACIDIC me Mn2+ (colourless, +2, 5e- gain - SELF-INDICATOR), neutral/alkaline me MnO2 (brown, +4), strong alkaline me manganate MnO4 2- (green, +6). K2Cr2O7 (orange, +6): acidic me Cr3+ (green, +3, 6e- gain); alkaline me CrO4 2- (YELLOW chromate) - pH se dichromate-chromate interconvert.

5. Lanthanoid Contraction - 4f Ka Kamzor Chhatri ⭐⭐

Lanthanoids (Ce-Lu, 4f): 4f electrons nuclear charge ko achhi tarah shield NAHI karte - isliye atomic size dheere-dheere SIKUDTA (lanthanoid contraction). Direct result: 4d aur 5d series ka size almost SAME (Zr approx Hf) - dono ko alag karna mushkil. Lanthanoids: +3 dominant, non-radioactive. Actinoids: zyada variable states, SAB radioactive, contraction aur zyada.
