1. Kaunsa Class? - 1° 2° 3°, Phenol ya Ether ⭐⭐

Alcohol: OH sp3 carbon pe (R-OH). Phenol: OH SEEDHA benzene ring pe. Ether: oxygen do alkyl/aryl groups ke beech (R-O-R'). Alcohols ka type: OH wale carbon ke C-neighbours se - 1° (1 neighbour), 2° (2), 3° (3). Reactivity aur oxidation products ISI se decide hote! Molecule dekho, class batao.

2. Banao Machine - Alkene / Grignard / Williamson ⭐⭐

Alkene se: acid hydration (H2O/H+) = MARKOVNIKOV (OH zyada substituted C pe); hydroboration-oxidation (BH3, phir H2O2/OH-) = ANTI-MARKOVNIKOV. Grignard RMgX: HCHO → 1°, doosra aldehyde → 2°, ketone → 3° alcohol. Williamson (ether): R-ONa + R'-X = SN2 - chhota/1° halide lo, 3° halide pe ELIMINATION ho jata.

3. H-Bonding - Boiling Point Ka Boss ⭐⭐

Alcohol ka BP comparable alkane/ether se BAHUT zyada - intermolecular HYDROGEN BONDING (molecules hath pakad ke baithe, alag karne zyada energy). Ethers: khud O-H donor nahi = BP kam. Solubility: chhote alcohols (C1-C3) pani me fully miscible (pani se H-bond); chain badhne pe solubility ghatti (hydrophobic part badhta). Branching badhne pe bhi BP ghatta.

4. Oxidation + Lucas Test - 1° 2° 3° Pehchano ⭐⭐

Oxidation: 1° → aldehyde → carboxylic ACID (PCC aldehyde pe rokta, KMnO4/H+ acid tak), 2° → KETONE, 3° → NAHI (alpha-H nahi, sirf drastic pe C-C tootta). Lucas test (ZnCl2 + HCl): 3° = INSTANT turbidity, 2° = ~5 min, 1° = room temp pe NAHI. Degree pehchanne ke 2 hatiyar!

5. Phenol Power - Acidity aur Named Reactions ⭐⭐

Phenol alcohol se ZYADA acidic - phenoxide ion RESONANCE-stabilized (charge ring me delocalize). Lekin carboxylic se KAM: NaHCO3 se CO2 NAHI (distinguish test!). Br2 water → 2,4,6-tribromophenol WHITE PPT (phenol ka test). -OH = activating, o/p-directing. Named: Reimer-Tiemann (CHCl3+NaOH) → salicylaldehyde; Kolbe (CO2) → salicylic acid (aspirin ka start).

6. Rapid Quiz - Alcohols Phenols Pakka? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: Grignard HCHO→1° aldehyde→2° ketone→3°, hydroboration = anti-Markovnikov, Lucas 3° instant, phenol NaHCO3 se CO2 NAHI, Br2 water = white ppt, Williamson me chhota halide.

Classification ⭐⭐

Alcohol: OH sp3 C pe | Phenol: OH seedha ring pe | Ether: R-O-R' | 1°/2°/3° = OH wale C ke C-neighbours (1/2/3) | Monohydric (ethanol), dihydric (glycol), trihydric (glycerol) | Naming: alkane+ol (propan-2-ol), ether = alkoxy+alkane (methoxyethane)

Preparation ⭐⭐

Hydration H2O/H+ = MARKOVNIKOV (propan-2-ol) | Hydroboration-oxidation (BH3, H2O2/OH-) = ANTI-MARK (propan-1-ol) | Grignard RMgX: HCHO→1°, aldehyde→2°, ketone→3° | Phenol: CUMENE process (phenol + acetone dono), Dow (chlorobenzene + NaOH, 623 K 300 atm)

Physical Properties ⭐⭐

Alcohol BP >> alkane/ether = H-BONDING | Branching badhe = BP ghatta | C1-C3 alcohols pani me FULLY miscible | Chain badhe = solubility ghatti | Ether BP kam (no O-H donor) par solubility ~comparable alcohol jitni (O acceptor) | Phenol BP zyada, partially soluble

Alcohol Reactions ⭐⭐

Esterification: alcohol + acid (conc H2SO4) → ester + H2O | Dehydration → alkene (SAYTZEFF, ease 3°>2°>1°); 443 K alkene, 413 K ether | Oxidation: 1°→aldehyde(PCC)→acid(KMnO4), 2°→ketone, 3°→NAHI | LUCAS (ZnCl2+HCl): 3° instant, 2° ~5 min, 1° RT nahi

Phenol ⭐⭐

Acidity: phenol > water > alcohol (phenoxide RESONANCE stable) | NaOH se react, NaHCO3 se CO2 NAHI (carboxylic se kamzor - distinguish) | Br2 water → 2,4,6-TRIBROMOPHENOL white ppt (TEST) | -OH o/p-directing | Reimer-Tiemann → salicylaldehyde (o-CHO) | Kolbe → salicylic acid (o-COOH)

Ethers ⭐⭐

WILLIAMSON: R-ONa + R'-X → R-O-R' (SN2 - chhota/1° halide lo; 3° halide = ELIMINATION) | HX cleavage: CHHOTE alkyl group ko halogen milta | Excess HX = dono alkyl halides | Anisole me -OCH3 activating, o/p-directing | Ethers inert = Grignard ka DRY ETHER solvent
