Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Classification aur Nomenclature

  • Amines: ammonia (NH₃) ke derivatives jisme H atoms alkyl/aryl groups se replace hote. General: R−NH₂, R₂NH, R₃N.
  • 1°/2°/3° amines ⭐: nitrogen pe kitne alkyl/aryl groups jude — 1° (primary): ek group (R−NH₂, jaise CH₃NH₂); 2° (secondary): do groups (R₂NH); 3° (tertiary): teen groups (R₃N). Yeh carbon wale 1°/2°/3° alcohol se ALAG hai — yahan N pe groups gino, carbon pe nahi!
  • Simple vs mixed: teeno groups same (trimethylamine) = simple; alag (ethylmethylamine) = mixed.
  • IUPAC naming: aliphatic = alkane + −amine (ethanamine, CH₃CH₂NH₂). 2°/3° me bade group ko parent, chhote ko N-substituent (N-methylethanamine). Aromatic: aniline (C₆H₅NH₂) / benzenamine.

💡 Amine classification = NITROGEN pe groups gino (alcohol wala carbon logic mat lagao). Aniline = C₆H₅NH₂. Naming: alkane+amine, N-substituent for 2°/3°. Common confusion point!

2️⃣ Preparation — Amines Kaise Banate

  • Reduction of nitro compounds ⭐: R−NO₂ + H₂ (Ni/Pt) ya Sn/HCl, Fe/HCl → R−NH₂. Nitrobenzene → aniline (industrial/lab dono me common).
  • Ammonolysis of alkyl halides: R−X + NH₃ (excess) → R−NH₂. Lekin mixture banta (1° + 2° + 3° + quaternary) kyunki product amine bhi nucleophile hai — excess NH₃ se 1° ko favour karo. Aryl halides se nahi banta aasani (C−X strong, resonance).
  • Reduction of nitriles/amides: R−C≡N + LiAlH₄/H₂(Ni) → R−CH₂−NH₂ (1° amine, chain ek C se badhti). R−CONH₂ (amide) + LiAlH₄ → R−CH₂−NH₂.
  • Hoffmann bromamide degradation ⭐: amide + Br₂ + KOH → 1° amine with ONE CARBON LESS (RCONH₂ → R−NH₂). Chain ek C se ghatti — migration reaction. Naam yaad rakho!
  • Gabriel phthalimide synthesis ⭐: phthalimide + KOH → potassium phthalimide, phir R−X → N-alkylphthalimide → hydrolysis → pure 1° amine (no 2°/3° mixture). Aromatic amines (aniline) nahi ban sakte isse (aryl halide SN2 nahi deta).

💡 Hoffmann bromamide = amide se amine, CHAIN EK C SE GHATTI (RCONH₂→RNH₂). Gabriel phthalimide = PURE 1° amine (mixture nahi), but aromatic nahi. Nitro reduction = nitro→amine. Ye teen named methods pakke!

3️⃣ Basic Character — Amines Base Kyun ⭐⭐

  • Amines basic hain: nitrogen ka lone pair proton (H⁺) ko donate karke accept karta — R−NH₂ + H⁺ → R−NH₃⁺. Basicity pKb se measure: kam pKb = zyada basic (zyada Kb).
  • Aliphatic vs aromatic ⭐: aliphatic amines (jaise CH₃NH₂) aromatic (aniline) se ZYADA basic. Kyun? Aniline me N ka lone pair benzene ring ke saath resonance me delocalize — H⁺ ke liye kam available. Alkyl groups (+I effect) aliphatic me electron density badhate → lone pair zyada available.
  • Gas phase order: 3° > 2° > 1° > NH₃ (pure +I effect). Aqueous solution me order alag ⭐: solvation (H-bonding with water) bhi matter karta. Result: (CH₃)₂NH > CH₃NH₂ > (CH₃)₃N > NH₃ (methylamines); (C₂H₅)₂NH > (C₂H₅)₃N > C₂H₅NH₂ > NH₃ (ethyl). Combined steric + inductive + solvation ka net effect.
  • Aromatic substituents: aniline pe electron-donating group basicity badhata, electron-withdrawing (−NO₂) ghatata (lone pair aur kam available).

💡 pKb KAM = basic ZYADA (ulta yaad rakho). Aliphatic > aromatic basic (aniline lone pair resonance me busy). Aqueous me order: 2° > 1° > 3° (methyl). Lone pair ki availability = basicity!

4️⃣ Chemical Reactions of Amines ⭐⭐

  • Alkylation: amine + R−X → 2° → 3° → quaternary ammonium salt (sequence chalti, isliye ammonolysis me mixture banta).
  • Acylation ⭐: amine + acyl chloride (CH₃COCl) ya acid anhydride → amide (H atom replace hoke acyl group aata). Pyridine/base use hote. Aniline ko protect karne me kaam aata (acetylation se −NH₂ ko kam reactive banao, phir substitution, phir hydrolysis).
  • Carbylamine reaction ⭐: sirf 1° amines + CHCl₃ + alcoholic KOH (heat) → isocyanide (carbylamine) — bahut gandi smell. 1° amine ka test (2°/3° nahi dete). Aliphatic aur aromatic dono 1° dete.
  • Reaction with HNO₂ ⭐: 1° aliphatic amine + HNO₂ (NaNO₂+HCl) → alcohol + N₂ gas (bubbles). 1° aromatic (aniline) + HNO₂ (273-278 K) → benzenediazonium chloride (diazotisation). 2° amine → N-nitrosamine (yellow oily). 3° amine → salt (no clear reaction). Isse 1°/2°/3° distinguish.
  • Aromatic substitution (aniline) ⭐: −NH₂ strongly activating, o/p-directing. Br₂ water → 2,4,6-tribromoaniline (white ppt). Controlled substitution ke liye pehle acetylation (→ acetanilide, kam activating), phir substitution, phir hydrolysis.

💡 Carbylamine = 1° amine ka TEST (gandi smell, CHCl₃+KOH). HNO₂ se 1°/2°/3° alag hote (aromatic 1° → diazonium salt). Aniline + Br₂ water = white ppt. Acylation = −NH₂ protect. Ye reactions + tests exam pakke!

5️⃣ Diazonium Salts — Synthesis Ka Jugaadu Tool ⭐⭐

  • Diazotisation ⭐: aniline + NaNO₂ + 2HCl (273-278 K, cold) → benzenediazonium chloride (C₆H₅N₂⁺Cl⁻). Temperature low rakhna zaroori (warm pe phenol ban jata).
  • Kyun important ⭐: diazonium group (−N₂⁺) bahut achha leaving group hai (N₂ gas nikal jata, stable) — uski jagah kuch bhi laga sakte! Isse aniline se bahut saare compounds bante jo directly nahi ban sakte.
  • Replacement reactions ⭐: + CuCl → chlorobenzene, + CuBr → bromobenzene (Sandmeyer), + CuCN → cyanobenzene, + KI → iodobenzene, + HBF₄ phir heat → fluorobenzene (Balz-Schiemann), + H₃PO₂ → benzene (−NH₂ hata diya), + H₂O (warm) → phenol.
  • Coupling reaction ⭐: diazonium salt + phenol ya aniline (dusra electron-rich ring) → azo compounds (Ar−N=N−Ar′) — coloured dyes. Example: + phenol (alkaline, cold) → p-hydroxyazobenzene (orange dye). Azo dyes industry me important.
  • Importance: Sandmeyer + coupling + replacement se aniline → almost kuch bhi (halides, phenol, nitrile, dyes). Synthesis conversions me diazonium salt = bridge.

💡 Diazonium salt = "magic middleman" — aniline se banao (cold), phir −N₂⁺ ki jagah Cl/Br/CN/I/F/OH/H kuch bhi (Sandmeyer). Coupling se azo dyes (coloured). Conversions me har jagah kaam aata!

1️⃣ वर्गीकरण और नामकरण

  • 1°/2°/3° ऐमीन = नाइट्रोजन पर जुड़े alkyl/aryl समूहों की संख्या (कार्बन पर नहीं)। ऐनिलीन = C₆H₅NH₂। नाम: alkane+amine, N-प्रतिस्थापी।

💡 N पर समूह गिनें।

2️⃣ विधियाँ

  • नाइट्रो अपचयन → ऐमीन। Hoffmann bromamide: amide + Br₂/KOH → 1° ऐमीन (एक C कम)। Gabriel phthalimide: शुद्ध 1° ऐमीन (मिश्रण नहीं, ऐरोमैटिक नहीं)।

💡 Hoffmann = एक C कम।

3️⃣ क्षारीय स्वभाव ⭐

  • N का एकाकी युग्म H⁺ ग्रहण करता → क्षारीय। pKb कम = क्षारीय अधिक। ऐलिफैटिक > ऐरोमैटिक (ऐनिलीन lone pair अनुनाद में)। जलीय: 2°>1°>3° (मेथिल)।

💡 pKb कम = क्षारीय अधिक।

4️⃣ रासायनिक अभिक्रियाएँ ⭐

  • कार्बिलऐमीन = 1° ऐमीन परीक्षण (दुर्गंध, CHCl₃+KOH)। HNO₂ से 1°/2°/3° पहचान (ऐरोमैटिक 1° → डायएज़ोनियम)। ऐनिलीन + Br₂ जल → श्वेत अवक्षेप। एसिलीकरण −NH₂ सुरक्षा।

💡 कार्बिलऐमीन = 1° परीक्षण।

5️⃣ डायएज़ोनियम लवण ⭐

  • ऐनिलीन + NaNO₂ + HCl (ठंडा) → बेंजीनडायएज़ोनियम क्लोराइड। Sandmeyer: CuCl/CuBr/CuCN। कपलिंग → azo रंजक। −N₂⁺ उत्तम leaving group — संश्लेषण सेतु।

💡 डायएज़ोनियम = संश्लेषण सेतु।

1️⃣ Classification & Naming

  • 1°/2°/3° amines = number of alkyl/aryl groups on nitrogen (not carbon). Aniline = C₆H₅NH₂. IUPAC: alkane+amine; N-substituent for 2°/3°.

💡 Count groups on nitrogen.

2️⃣ Preparation

  • Nitro reduction → amine. Hoffmann bromamide degradation: amide + Br₂/KOH → 1° amine with one C fewer. Gabriel phthalimide: pure 1° amine (no mixture; not for aryl amines). Ammonolysis gives a mixture.

💡 Hoffmann = one C fewer.

3️⃣ Basic Character ⭐

  • Nitrogen's lone pair accepts H⁺ → basic. Lower pKb = stronger base. Aliphatic > aromatic (aniline's lone pair is resonance-delocalized). In water: 2°>1°>3° for methylamines.

💡 Lower pKb = stronger base.

4️⃣ Chemical Reactions ⭐

  • Carbylamine reaction = 1° amine test (foul smell, CHCl₃+alc KOH). HNO₂ distinguishes 1°/2°/3° (aromatic 1° → diazonium salt). Aniline + Br₂ water → white ppt. Acylation protects −NH₂.

💡 Carbylamine = 1° test.

5️⃣ Diazonium Salts ⭐

  • Aniline + NaNO₂ + HCl (cold) → benzenediazonium chloride. Sandmeyer: CuCl/CuBr/CuCN replace −N₂⁺. Coupling with phenols → azo dyes. −N₂⁺ is an excellent leaving group — a synthetic bridge.

💡 Diazonium = synthetic bridge.

JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 1°, 2°, 3° amines kaise classify hote?
Answer dekho

Nitrogen pe jude alkyl/aryl groups ki ginti se — 1° = ek group (R−NH₂), 2° = do, 3° = teen. (Carbon pe nahi — yeh alcohol wale logic se alag hai!)

Q2 Hoffmann bromamide degradation kya deta hai?
Answer dekho

Amide (RCONH₂) + Br₂/KOH → 1° amine jisme EK CARBON KAM (R−NH₂). Chain ek C se ghat-ti.

Q3 Gabriel phthalimide synthesis kyun useful?
Answer dekho

Pure 1° amine deta (2°/3° mixture nahi). Lekin aromatic amines (aniline) nahi ban sakte (aryl halide SN2 nahi deta).

Q4 Aliphatic amines aromatic se zyada basic kyun?
Answer dekho

Aniline me N ka lone pair benzene ring ke saath resonance me delocalize — H⁺ ke liye kam available. Alkyl groups (+I) aliphatic me electron density badhate.

Q5 pKb aur basicity ka relation?
Answer dekho

pKb jitna KAM, base utna STRONG (Kb zyada). Aliphatic amines ka pKb aniline se kam (zyada basic).

Q6 Carbylamine reaction kis ka test hai?
Answer dekho

Sirf 1° amines ka — CHCl₃ + alcoholic KOH se isocyanide (bahut gandi smell) banta. 2°/3° nahi dete.

Q7 HNO₂ se 1°, 2°, 3° amines kaise distinguish?
Answer dekho

1° aromatic (aniline) → diazonium salt (cold); 1° aliphatic → N₂ gas bubbles + alcohol. 2° → N-nitrosamine (yellow oily). 3° → sirf salt (no clear reaction).

Q8 Aniline + Br₂ water ka result?
Answer dekho

2,4,6-tribromoaniline (white precipitate) — −NH₂ strongly activating, o/p-directing. Controlled substitution ke liye pehle acetylation karte.

Q9 Sandmeyer reaction kya hai?
Answer dekho

Benzenediazonium salt + CuCl → chlorobenzene, CuBr → bromobenzene, CuCN → cyanobenzene. Diazonium group ko halogen/CN se replace karna.

Q10 Diazonium salts synthesis me kyun important?
Answer dekho

−N₂⁺ bahut achha leaving group (N₂ gas) — uski jagah Cl/Br/CN/I/F/OH/H kuch bhi laga sakte. Coupling se azo dyes bhi. Aniline se bahut saare compounds bante.
