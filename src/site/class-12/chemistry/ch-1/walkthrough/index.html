1. Concentration Units - Kitna Solute, Kitna Solvent ⭐

Solution = homogeneous mixture (solute + solvent, particles <1 nm - Tyndall nahi). Concentration batane ke 4 tareeke: mass % (w/w), mole fraction (x), MOLARITY (M = moles/volume L - temperature se BADALTI, kyunki volume expand hota hai!), MOLALITY (m = moles/solvent kg - temperature-proof, isliye colligative me yahi). 2. Henry's Law - Pressure Badhao, Gas Ghulo ⭐

Constant temperature pe gas ki solubility uske partial pressure ke DIRECTLY proportional: p = K_H·x (x = solution me gas ka mole fraction). K_H jitna BADA, solubility utni KAM. Soda bottle high pressure pe seal hoti hai - kholte hi p girta, CO2 nikalta (fizz!). Scuba divers ko bends N2 bubbles se hota hai - isliye He mix (He ka K_H bahut bada = kam soluble). Law tab FAIL jab gas solvent se react kare (NH3, HCl paani me). Gas chuno, pressure slider ghumao:

3. Raoult's Law - Ideal vs Non-Ideal ⭐⭐

Volatile liquid-liquid solution me har component ka partial VP = pure VP × mole fraction: p1 = p1°·x1, p2 = p2°·x2, total = p1 + p2 (schematic: p1° = 100, p2° = 50 mmHg). IDEAL (benzene+toluene): A-B ≈ A-A ≈ B-B, ΔH=0, ΔV=0 - straight lines. POSITIVE deviation (ethanol+water): A-B kamzor, molecules bhaagte, VP Raoult se UPAR, ΔH>0 - minimum-boiling azeotrope. NEGATIVE deviation (chloroform+acetone, H-bonding): A-B strong, VP NEECHE, ΔH<0 - maximum-boiling azeotrope. Toggle karo, x1 slider se composition badlo:

4. Colligative BP/FP + van't Hoff Factor ⭐⭐

Colligative properties sirf particles ki GINTI pe depend karti (nature pe NAHI). Solute daalo: BP BADHTA (ΔT_b = i·K_b·m, K_b water = 0.52) aur FP GHATTA (ΔT_f = i·K_f·m, K_f water = 1.86 - isliye barf pe namak!). van't Hoff factor i = asli headcount multiplier: dissociation pe i>1 (NaCl → 2, CaCl2 → 3), association pe i<1 (acetic acid benzene me dimer, i≈0.5), non-electrolyte (glucose) i=1. Abnormal molar mass: tootta hai to M2 KAM aata, judta hai to ZYADA. Solute chuno, molality badlo, thermometer dekho:

5. Osmosis - Paani Ka Bheed-Balancing ⭐

Osmosis: solvent semipermeable membrane (SPM) se dilute → concentrated side behta hai. Osmotic pressure π = i·C·R·T (C = molarity, T Kelvin, R = 0.0821) - SABSE accurate colligative (room temp pe measure, biological molecules ke liye best). RO (reverse osmosis): π se ZYADA pressure lagao → solvent ulta, sea water desalination + ghar ke RO purifier! Blood cell dekho: 0.9% NaCl = ISOTONIC (π same, cell safe), hypertonic me SIKUDTA, hypotonic me PHAT-ta hai. Toggle karo, C slider badlo:

0.3

6. Rapid Quiz - Solutions Pakke? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: molarity temperature se badalti (molality nahi), Henry's law p = K_H·x (soda fizz, scuba bends), positive deviation = bhaago / negative = pakdo, ΔT_f = i·K_f·m, 0.9% NaCl = isotonic.

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo! ⭐

Concentration Units ⭐

Mass % = (w_solute/w_solution)×100 | Mole fraction x = n/(n_total), sum = 1 | Molarity M = n/V(L) - TEMPERATURE se badalti! | Molality m = n/solvent(kg) - temp-proof, colligative me yahi | Dilute water: m ≈ M×(1000/d)

Henry's Law ⭐

p = K_H · x | K_H BADA → solubility KAM | Soda bottle high p seal → kholte hi fizz | Scuba bends = N2 bubbles (He kam soluble isliye mix) | FAIL jab gas react kare (NH3, HCl) | Cold water me O2 zyada (fish!)

Raoult + Deviations ⭐⭐

p1 = p1°·x1, p2 = p2°·x2, p_total = p1+p2 | Ideal: A-B ≈ A-A, ΔH=ΔV=0 (benzene+toluene) | Positive dev: A-B kamzor, VP UPAR, ΔH>0 (ethanol+water) | Negative dev: A-B strong, VP NEECHE, ΔH<0 (chloroform+acetone H-bond)

Azeotropes ⭐

Constant BP pe ubale + vapour same composition → distillation se alag NAHI | Minimum-boiling = positive dev wale (ethanol-water 95.4% - pure ethanol nahi milta!) | Maximum-boiling = negative dev wale (HCl-water 20.2%, HNO3-water 68%)

Colligative Properties ⭐⭐

Sirf GINTI pe depend | (Δp/p°) = i·x2 (VP lowering) | ΔT_b = i·K_b·m (K_b = 0.52) | ΔT_f = i·K_f·m (K_f = 1.86) | π = i·C·R·T (sabse accurate) | M2 = i·K_f·w2·1000/(ΔT_f·w1) se molar mass

van't Hoff Factor ⭐⭐

i = observed/calculated colligative = normal M/abnormal M | Dissociation i>1: NaCl→2, CaCl2→3, K4[Fe(CN)6]→5 | Association i<1: acetic acid dimer →0.5 | Non-electrolyte (glucose) i=1 | α(diss) = (i−1)/(n−1), α(assoc) = (1−i)/(1−1/n)
