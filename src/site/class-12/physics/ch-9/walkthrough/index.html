1. Mirror Lab - Image Kahan, Kaisi? ⭐⭐

Spherical mirror ka poora khel 2 formulas ka: f = R/2 aur 1/f = 1/v + 1/u (Cartesian signs: object side NEGATIVE!). Magnification m = −v/u: negative m = inverted, |m| > 1 = magnified. 2. Refraction + TIR Lab - Snell Ka Rule ⭐⭐

Snell's law: n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r. Denser me jaao → normal ki TARAF bend; rarer me → DOOR. Par glass → air me i badhate jao... ek angle ke BAAD refraction possible hi nahi - TOTAL INTERNAL REFLECTION! sin C = n_rare/n_dense (glass→air: C = 41.8°). Direction chuno, i slider ghumao, TIR LIVE dekho - optical fibre ka principle!

3. Prism Lab - Deviation Ka Graph ⭐⭐

Prism: A = r₁ + r₂ aur δ = i + e − A. i slider ghumao - δ pehle GHATTA hai phir BADHTA hai. Beech ka sweet spot = MINIMUM DEVIATION: wahan i = e, r₁ = r₂ = A/2, andar ki ray base ke parallel! Formula: n = sin[(A+δmin)/2] / sin(A/2). A zyada karo toh second face pe TIR bhi ho sakta hai - dekho!

4. Thin Lens Lab - 1/f = 1/v − 1/u ⭐⭐

Lens mirror jaisa hi khel, par light THROUGH jaati hai: 1/f = 1/v − 1/u, m = v/u. Convex CONVERGES (real image possible), concave DIVERGES (hamesha virtual, erect, diminished). u < f pe convex lens = SIMPLE MICROSCOPE (virtual, erect, magnified!). Power P = 1/f(metres) dioptre me. Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo! ⭐

Mirrors ⭐⭐

f = R/2 | 1/f = 1/v + 1/u (Cartesian!) | m = −v/u | Concave: real image possible (5 cases ratto!) | Convex: HAMESHA virtual, erect, diminished (real object ke liye)

Refraction + TIR ⭐⭐

Snell: n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r | n = c/v | Boundary pe frequency SAME, wavelength badalti | TIR: denser→rarer + i > C | sin C = n_rare/n_dense | Optical fibre = repeated TIR

Prism ⭐⭐

A = r₁ + r₂ | δ = i + e − A | δ_min pe: i = e, r₁ = r₂ = A/2 | n = sin[(A+δmin)/2] / sin(A/2) | Dispersion: n colour pe depend - violet > red deviate

Lenses ⭐⭐

Spherical surface: n₂/v − n₁/u = (n₂−n₁)/R | Thin lens: 1/f = 1/v − 1/u, m = v/u | Lens maker: 1/f = (n_rel − 1)(1/R₁ − 1/R₂) | Power P = 1/f(m) dioptre | Convex converges, concave diverges

Microscopes ⭐

Simple: M = D/f (infinity) ya 1 + D/f (near point), D ≈ 25 cm | Compound: M ≈ (L/fₒ)(D/fₑ) - objective real intermediate image, eyepiece aage magnify

Telescopes ⭐

Astronomical (normal adjustment): |M| = fₒ/fₑ | Tube length = fₒ + fₑ | Reflecting telescope: curved MIRROR objective (no chromatic aberration!)
