1. Faraday Lab - Flux Badlo, EMF Banao ⭐⭐

Φ = BA cosθ (θ = B aur area NORMAL ke beech!). Faraday ka law: ε = −N dΦ/dt - CHANGE hi EMF banata hai, flux bada hona kaafi nahi! dB/dt slider se field badlao - bulb dekho jalta hua. Rate ZERO karo → EMF gayab. θ = 90° karo → flux hi zero!

2. Lenz Lab - Magnet Ko Roko! ⭐⭐

Induced current CHANGE ko oppose karta hai. Magnet paas lao → coil ka samne wala face N ban jaata hai (repel = oppose!). Door le jao → S ban jaata hai (attract = oppose!). Ruka magnet = koi current NAHI. Yehi energy conservation hai - oppose na ho toh free energy! Magnet slider ghumao ya ▶ Chalao dabao.

3. Motional EMF - Rails Pe Rod ⭐⭐

Rod B field me chalti hai → andar ke charges pe F = qv×B → + upar, − neeche → EMF! ε = Blv (teeno perpendicular hon). Current I = ε/R, aur rod pe drag force F = B²l²v/R - isliye rod ko khinchna padta hai, P = Fv = εI (energy conserved!). v aur B badhao, bulb dekho!

2.0 m/s
1.0 T

4. AC Generator - Ghumo aur Banao ⭐⭐

Coil B field me ghoomti hai → flux oscillate karta hai → ε = NBAω sin ωt. Peak ε₀ = NBAω. Dhyan do: coil VERTICAL (flux MAX) pe ε = ZERO, coil HORIZONTAL (flux zero) pe ε MAX - kyunki EMF rate of change pe depend karta hai! Har half cycle pe current REVERSE - isliye ALTERNATING current. India: 50 Hz, ω ≈ 314 rad/s.

5.0 rad/s

5. Rapid Quiz - EMI Pakki? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: Φ = BA cosθ (θ normal se!), ε = −N dΦ/dt, Lenz = oppose karo (paas N, door S), ε = Blv + F = B²l²v/R, inductor U = ½LI², generator ε₀ = NBAω, 50 Hz = India!

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo! ⭐

Flux + Faraday ⭐⭐

Φ = BA cosθ (weber), θ = B↔normal | ε = −N dΦ/dt | CHANGE hi EMF banata hai - flux constant toh EMF zero, chahe flux kitna bhi bada!

Lenz ⭐⭐

Induced current change ko OPPOSE | Paas aata N-pole → face N ban jao (repel) | Door jaata → S (attract) | Yehi energy conservation hai

Motional EMF ⭐⭐

ε = Blv (B, l, v mutually perpendicular) | I = ε/R | Drag force F = B²l²v/R | P = Fv = εI | Right-hand rule for current

Inductance ⭐

Self: ε = −L dI/dt | Energy U = ½LI² | Energy density u = B²/2μ₀ | Mutual: ε₂ = −M dI₁/dt

AC Generator ⭐

ε = NBAω sin ωt | Peak ε₀ = NBAω | Flux MAX pe ε = 0, flux ZERO pe ε MAX | Har half cycle pe current reverse | India: 50 Hz, ω ≈ 314 rad/s

Traps ⭐

θ plane se NAHI, normal se | "Oppose" matlab cancel nahi - resist | Blv sirf perpendicular case | Eddy currents: oppose motion (brakes) ya waste energy (laminated core isliye!)
