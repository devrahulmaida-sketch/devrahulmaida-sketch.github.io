1. Displacement Current - Maxwell Ka Fix ⭐

Charging capacitor ke plates ke beech charge flow nahi hota, phir bhi gap ke aas-paas magnetic field milta hai. Maxwell ka fix: changing electric field ek "displacement current" ki tarah act karta hai, I_d = ε₀ dΦ_E/dt. Capacitor me I_d lead ke conduction current ke barabar hota hai. Ampere-Maxwell law: ∮B·dl = μ₀(I_c + ε₀ dΦ_E/dt).

1.0

2. EM Wave Ka Safar - E aur B Saath Saath ⭐

Changing E magnetic field banata hai, changing B electric field - dono ek-doosre ko sustain karke aage badhte hain, medium ki zaroorat nahi. E aur B same phase me hote hain (saath peak, saath zero). Vacuum me speed c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀) ≈ 3×10⁸ m/s, aur E₀/B₀ = c. Frequency badhao to wavelength ghat-ti hai (c = fλ).

3. Transverse Nature - 3D View ⭐

E ⟂ B ⟂ propagation - teeno ek-doosre ke perpendicular. Propagation ki direction E×B se milti hai. Neeche E (z-axis) aur B (y-axis) ek x-direction me travel kar rahi wave ke liye snapshot hai. View ghuma ke dekho: har x pe E aur B hamesha perpendicular rehte hain. (Snapshot schematic; vectors same phase.)

4. EM Spectrum - Ek Hi Family ⭐⭐

Radio se gamma tak sab EM waves hain - vacuum me sabki speed c, bas frequency aur wavelength alag. Order: radio → microwave → infrared → visible → ultraviolet → X-ray → gamma; is direction me frequency aur photon energy badhti hain, wavelength ghat-ti hai. Visible sirf ~400-700 nm: red badi λ, violet chhoti λ. Band pe click karke details dekho.

5. Rapid Quiz - EM Waves Pakka? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: I_d = ε₀ dΦ_E/dt, c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀), E ⟂ B ⟂ propagation, E₀/B₀ = c, spectrum radio se gamma.

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo! ⭐

Displacement Current ⭐

Capacitor gap me charge flow nahi, phir bhi changing E | I_d = ε₀ dΦ_E/dt | Charging me I_d = lead ka conduction current | Literal charge flow nahi hai gap me

Ampere-Maxwell Law ⭐

∮B·dl = μ₀(I_c + ε₀ dΦ_E/dt) | Changing E field bhi magnetic field banata hai | Maxwell ne Ampere law ko complete kiya

EM Wave Ka Janam ⭐

Changing E → B, changing B → E, ek-doosre ko sustain | Accelerated charges radiate karte hain; stationary/static nahi | Medium zaroori nahi - vacuum me bhi travel

Speed aur Relations ⭐⭐

c = 1/√(μ₀ε₀) ≈ 3×10⁸ m/s | c = fλ (medium me v = fλ) | E₀/B₀ = c | Vacuum me SAB EM waves same speed

Transverse Nature ⭐

E ⟂ B ⟂ propagation | Direction of travel = E×B | E aur B same phase: saath peak, saath zero | Sound longitudinal hai, light transverse

Spectrum ⭐⭐

radio → microwave → infrared → visible (400-700 nm) → UV → X-ray → gamma | Is side f aur energy badhti, λ ghat-ti | Uses: radio/microwave = communication, radar, heating; IR = remote, thermal imaging; UV = sterilization; X-ray = medical imaging; gamma = radiotherapy
