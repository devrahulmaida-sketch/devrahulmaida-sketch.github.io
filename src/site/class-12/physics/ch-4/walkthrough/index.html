1. Lorentz Force Lab - F = qv×B ⭐⭐

Moving charge pe magnetic force F = |q|vB sinθ. Direction right-hand rule se - NEGATIVE charge ke liye ULTA! Sabse zaroori baat: force hamesha velocity ke PERPENDICULAR → work done ZERO, speed change nahi hoti, sirf direction! Aur static charge (v = 0) pe magnetic force bhi ZERO - magnetic field sirf moving charges ko pakadta hai.

2. Circle aur Helix - r = mv/qB ⭐⭐

v ⊥ B → magnetic force centripetal ban jaata hai → charge CIRCLE me ghoomta hai! r = mv/(qB), T = 2πm/(qB) - T velocity pe depend NAHI karta (tez particle bada circle, same time!). v ∥ B → force zero, seedhi line. Angle θ pe dono milke HELIX (spring jaisa), pitch = v∥ × T. v badhao - circle bada hota hai par T same! (demo units: m = 1, q = +1)

3. Field Sources - Wire, Loop, Solenoid ⭐⭐

Teen B-formulas 80% questions cover karte hain! Infinite wire: B = μ₀I/(2πd) - 1/d se girta hai, right-hand thumb rule. Loop ke CENTER: B = μ₀NI/(2R) - anticlockwise current → field tumhari TARAF. Solenoid ke ANDAR: B = μ₀nI uniform (n = turns per meter), bahar ≈ zero. Source chuno, sliders ghumao, field dekho!

4. Galvanometer Lab - Ammeter aur Voltmeter ⭐⭐

Coil pe torque τ = NIAB sinθ, spring ka restoring torque kφ - balance pe φ ∝ I (radial field se θ = 90° hamesha, linear scale!). AMMETER banana? Parallel me CHHOTA shunt S = I_g·G/(I − I_g) - zyada current bypass. VOLTMETER? Series me BADA resistance R = V/I_g − G. (Demo galvanometer: G = 50 Ω, full-scale I_g = 10 mA, N = 100, A = 2×10⁻⁴ m², B = 0.1 T.)

5. Rapid Quiz - Magnetism Pakka? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: F = qvB sinθ (work ZERO!), r = mv/qB aur T speed pe depend nahi, wire μ₀I/2πd + loop μ₀NI/2R + solenoid μ₀nI, parallel wires same direction ATTRACT, τ = NIAB sinθ, ammeter = parallel chhota shunt, voltmeter = series bada R!

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo! ⭐

Lorentz Force ⭐⭐

F = q(v × B), |F| = |q|vB sinθ | Negative charge: direction ULTA | F hamesha v ke ⊥ → work ZERO, speed constant | Static charge pe force = 0 | Total: F = q(E + v×B)

Circle + Helix ⭐⭐

v ⊥ B: circle, r = mv/(|q|B), T = 2πm/(|q|B) - T speed pe depend NAHI | f = |q|B/(2πm) cyclotron frequency | Angle θ pe HELIX, pitch = v cosθ × T | r = p/(|q|B)

Field Formulas ⭐⭐

Infinite wire: B = μ₀I/(2πd) | Loop center: B = μ₀NI/(2R) | Loop axis: B = μ₀IR²/(2(R²+x²)^3/2) | Arc: (θ/2π) × loop ka B | Solenoid: B = μ₀nI (andar uniform)

Force + Torque ⭐⭐

Conductor: F = BIL sinθ (Fleming left-hand) | Parallel wires: F/L = μ₀I₁I₂/(2πd) - SAME direction ATTRACT, opposite repel | Loop pe τ = NIAB sinθ = mB sinθ, m = NIA

Galvanometer ⭐⭐

nIAB = kφ → φ ∝ I (radial field = linear scale) | Ammeter: parallel CHHOTA shunt S = I_gG/(I−I_g), resistance LOW | Voltmeter: series BADA R = V/I_g − G, resistance HIGH

Traps ⭐

−q ki direction ulta mat bhoolo | T velocity pe depend nahi karta | Work = 0 → KE constant | Like currents ATTRACT (charges repel!) | Ammeter circuit me SERIES, voltmeter PARALLEL me lagta hai
