1. Nucleus Ki Rachna aur Size ⭐

Nuclide: Z protons + N neutrons, A = Z + N. Same Z, alag N = ISOTOPES. Size: R ≈ R₀·A^(1/3), R₀ ≈ 1.2 fm (1 fm = 10⁻¹⁵ m). A badhne pe density lagbhag CONSTANT. Nucleus ~10⁻¹⁵ m vs atom ~10⁻¹⁰ m - par mass ka almost sab nucleus me! A 8× → radius sirf 2×.

2. Nuclear Force - Short Range, Strong ⭐

Nuclear force: STRONG attraction, short-range (~kuch fm) - nearby nucleons ko bind karta, proton repulsion ke bawajood. Charge independence: p-p, n-n, p-n pairs pe lagbhag SAME. Saturation: har nucleon mainly nearby neighbours se interact. Ye Coulomb force nahi - strong interaction ka residual effect hai. (Graph SCHEMATIC hai.)

3. Mass Defect aur Binding Energy ⭐⭐

Mass defect: free nucleons ka mass − bound nucleus ka mass = Δm > 0. Binding energy B = Δm·c² (E = mc²), 1 u·c² ≈ 931.5 MeV. Atomic masses se: Δm = Z·m_H + N·m_n − M_atom (electron masses cancel). B/A (per nucleon) stability compare karta hai. Atomic aur nuclear mass mix mat karo!

4. Binding Energy Curve - Fission aur Fusion ⭐⭐

B/A vs A: light nuclei se tez rise, Fe/Ni ke paas PEAK ~8.7-8.8 MeV, heavy me slow decline. FISSION: heavy nucleus ko medium pieces me todo → B/A higher → energy (U-235: ~200 MeV per event, chain reaction). FUSION: light nuclei jodo → B/A higher → energy (stars; Coulomb barrier ke liye bahut high temperature). Dono me products zyada tightly bound!
