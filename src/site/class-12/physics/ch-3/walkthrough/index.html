1. Drift Velocity - Electron Ka Slow Walk ⭐⭐

Current I = Q/t, conventional direction POSITIVE charge ki taraf (electrons ULTA chalte hain!). Relation: I = neAv_d, jahan n = electron density, A = area, v_d = drift velocity. Shock: electrons sirf ~mm/s drift karte hain! Phir bhi switch on karte hi light kyun jal jaati hai? Kyunki ELECTRIC FIELD almost light ki speed se establish hota hai aur saare electrons EK SAATH dhakka lagate hain - pipe me paani jaisa. Kisi ek electron ko travel nahi karna!

2. Ohm's Law aur R = ρL/A ⭐⭐

Ohm's law: V = IR (constant temperature). Resistance R = ρL/A - LAMBA wire zyada R, MOTA wire kam. ρ sirf MATERIAL ki property (Ω·m); R shape pe bhi depend karta hai! Temperature: metals me T ↑ → R ↑ (R = R₀(1+αΔT)); semiconductors me T ↑ → R ↓ (zyada free carriers). Diode/transistor non-ohmic - V-I graph straight nahi. Superconductors: critical T ke neeche R = 0!

2.0 m
1.0 mm²
0°C

3. Cell, EMF aur Internal Resistance ⭐

EMF (ε) = cell ka total voltage jab current ZERO ho - ye FORCE nahi hai, naam misleading! Cell ka apna internal resistance r hota hai. Discharge me TERMINAL voltage V = ε − Ir (EMF se kam!); charging me V = ε + Ir. SHORT CIRCUIT (R=0): I = ε/r - max current, DANGER! Purani battery weak kyun? r badh jaata hai, voltage andar hi kho jaata hai. Max power transfer jab R = r; efficiency η = R/(R+r).

4. Wheatstone Bridge - Balance Dhundo ⭐⭐

4 resistors ka diamond + beech me galvanometer. BALANCED condition: P/Q = R/S - tab galvanometer me ZERO current, beech wali branch DEAD (hata do, koi farak nahi)! B aur D same potential pe. Bridge simple series-parallel ban jaata hai. Unbalanced ho to Kirchhoff se solve karna padega - shortcut nahi! Meter bridge (practical): S = R(100−l)/l. Exam tip: PEHLE balance check karo!

5. Rapid Quiz - Current Pakka? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: I = neAv_d (electrons SLOW, field FAST!), R = ρL/A, metals me T ↑ → R ↑, V = ε − Ir (discharge), short circuit I = ε/r, Wheatstone balance P/Q = R/S, series me zyada R wala bulb ZYADA glow (P = I²R)!

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo! ⭐

Current + Drift ⭐⭐

I = Q/t; conventional direction +ve ki taraf (electrons ulta) | I = neAv_d | j = I/A = nev_d | Mobility μ = v_d/E | Drift ~mm/s SLOW, par field light-speed se establish - isliye bulb turant jalta hai

Ohm + Resistance ⭐⭐

V = IR (constant T) | R = ρL/A | σ = 1/ρ, j = σE | Metals: T ↑ → R ↑, R = R₀(1+αΔT) | Semiconductors: T ↑ → R ↓ | Diode/transistor non-ohmic | Superconductor: R = 0 below critical T

Cells + EMF ⭐

EMF = voltage at zero current (force NAHI!) | Discharge: V = ε − Ir | Charging: V = ε + Ir | Short circuit: I = ε/r | Series: ε₁+ε₂, r₁+r₂ | Parallel identical: ε same, r/n | Purani battery: r badhta hai

Kirchhoff ⭐⭐

KCL: junction pe ΣI = 0 (charge conservation) | KVL: closed loop me ΣV = 0 (energy conservation) | Resistor current ke saath: −IR; battery − se +: +ε | Galat direction guess → negative answer, no problem

Wheatstone ⭐⭐

Balanced: P/Q = R/S → galvanometer ZERO | B, D same potential, middle branch dead | Balance pe circuit simple series-parallel | Meter bridge: S = R(100−l)/l | Exam me PEHLE balance check karo

Power + Traps ⭐

P = VI = I²R = V²/R | Heat H = I²Rt | 1 unit = 1 kWh = 3.6×10⁶ J | SERIES: zyada R wala bulb zyada glow (P=I²R) | PARALLEL: kam R wala zyada glow (P=V²/R) | Max power transfer: R = r | η = R/(R+r)
