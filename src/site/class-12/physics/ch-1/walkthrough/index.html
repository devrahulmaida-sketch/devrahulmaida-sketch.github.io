1. Coulomb's Law - Charges Ke Beech Ka Force ⭐

Do point charges ke beech force F = kq₁q₂/r², k = 9×10⁹ N·m²/C². LIKE charges REPEL (door dhakka), UNLIKE charges ATTRACT. INVERSE SQUARE: distance DOUBLE → force 1/4! Charge bhi quantized hai (q = ±ne) aur conserved - na banta hai, na destroy hota hai, sirf transfer. Medium me force F_air/K se ghat jaata hai (K = dielectric constant).

2. Electric Field - Charge Ka Influence Zone ⭐

Electric field E = F/q₀ - kisi point pe +1 C test charge pe lagne wala force. Point charge ka field E = kQ/r², +Q se radially OUTWARD, −Q me INWARD. Field lines ke RULES: (1) + se shuru, − pe khatam; (2) KABHI intersect nahi karti (ek point pe field ki do direction ho hi nahi sakti!); (3) jitni dense, utna strong field; (4) conductor surface pe perpendicular. Slider ghumao - Q aur distance dono ka effect dekho!

3. Electric Dipole - Do Charges Ki Jodi ⭐

Dipole = −q aur +q chhoti distance 2a pe; dipole moment p = q×2a (direction − se + ki taraf). FAR FIELD me: AXIAL line pe E = 2kp/r³, EQUATORIAL pe E = kp/r³ (direction p ke OPPOSITE) - axial EXACTLY DOUBLE, dono 1/r³ se girti hain (point charge 1/r² se zyada tez!). Uniform field E me dipole pe torque τ = pE sinθ: θ=0 pe ZERO (stable), θ=90° pe MAX. Potential energy U = −pE cosθ: θ=0 pe minimum (−pE), θ=180° pe max.

4. Gauss's Law - Symmetry Ka Jaadu ⭐⭐

Flux Φ = EA cosθ = kitni field lines surface se guzri. GAUSS'S LAW: kisi bhi CLOSED surface se total flux = q_andar/ε₀ - surface ki SHAPE matter nahi karti, sirf andar ka charge! Teen star results: INFINITE LINE charge → E = λ/(2πε₀r) (1/r se girta); INFINITE SHEET → E = σ/(2ε₀) - distance se INDEPENDENT!; SPHERICAL SHELL → bahar E = kQ/r² (saara charge center jaisa), ANDAR E = 0 (shell theorem!). Ye 4 results 90% Gauss questions solve kar dete hain.

1.0 m

5. Rapid Quiz - Charges Pakka? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: q = ±ne (fractional NAHI!), r double → force 1/4, field lines KABHI intersect nahi karti, dipole axial = 2 × equatorial (far field), sheet ka E constant σ/2ε₀, shell ke ANDAR E = 0!

Charge Properties ⭐

Like repel, unlike attract | Quantization: q = ±ne, e = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ C | Conservation: total charge fixed, sirf transfer | Additivity: algebraic sum | Conductors: free electrons; insulators: charge chipka rehta hai

Coulomb's Law ⭐

F = kq₁q₂/r², k = 1/(4πε₀) = 9×10⁹ | Line joining charges ke along; same sign repel | r double → F = 1/4 | Medium me F_medium = F_air/K | Sirf point charges ke liye exact

Superposition ⭐

Net force = har charge ke force ka VECTOR sum | Pair ka force baaki charges se affect NAHI hota | Components todke add karo | Symmetry me components cancel - pehle dekh lo! | Sirf magnitude add karna = classic GALTI

Field + Lines ⭐

E = F/q₀; point charge E = kQ/r² | +Q se outward, −Q me inward | Lines + se −, KABHI intersect nahi | Dense = strong | Conductor surface pe perpendicular | Uniform field: parallel equally spaced

Dipole ⭐

p = q×2a (− se +) | Axial: E = 2kp/r³ | Equatorial: E = kp/r³, p ke OPPOSITE - axial DOUBLE | τ = pE sinθ (θ=90 max) | U = −pE cosθ (θ=0 stable, −pE)

Gauss's Law ⭐⭐

Φ = EA cosθ; closed surface flux = q_andar/ε₀ | Line: E = λ/(2πε₀r) ∝ 1/r | Sheet: E = σ/(2ε₀) CONSTANT | Shell bahar: kQ/r²; ANDAR: ZERO | Shape matter nahi, sirf q_andar
