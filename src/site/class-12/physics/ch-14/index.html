Band gaps se p-n diode aur rectifier

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Energy bands ⭐

  • Valence/conduction bands: electrons available states; band gap E_g = unke beech forbidden region.
  • Conductor: overlap ya partially filled band; insulator: large gap; semiconductor: modest gap (Si≈1.1 eV at room temperature).
  • Temperature: semiconductor ko heat karne par more carriers bante, conductivity generally badhti (metal ke opposite).

💡 Band gap se material ka electrical behavior samajh aata hai.

2️⃣ Intrinsic aur doping ⭐

  • Intrinsic: pure Si/Ge me thermal electron-hole pairs; n_e=n_h. Hole positive carrier ki tarah behave karta hai.
  • n-type ⭐: pentavalent donor (P/As) se extra electrons; electrons majority, holes minority. Material net neutral rehta.
  • p-type ⭐: trivalent acceptor (B/Al) se holes majority, electrons minority; material net neutral.

🎯 n-type negative charged block nahi hota, overall neutral hai.

3️⃣ p-n junction aur depletion ⭐

  • Join p and n: electrons n→p, holes p→n diffuse and recombine near boundary.
  • Depletion region: mobile carriers kam; fixed ionized donors/acceptors se built-in field aur barrier potential.
  • Equilibrium: diffusion aur field-driven drift balance; external bias ke bina net current zero.

💡 Barrier ko cross karne ke liye forward bias madad karta hai.

4️⃣ Forward aur reverse bias I-V ⭐⭐

  • Forward bias ⭐: p to battery +, n to −; barrier/depletion shrink, current rapidly rises after knee (~0.7 V Si, ~0.3 V Ge as rough room-temp guides).
  • Reverse bias ⭐: p to −, n to +; depletion widens, small minority-carrier leakage until breakdown.
  • Diode: current mostly ek direction me; I-V non-linear. Breakdown-rated devices alag application hain, core CBSE simple diode tak hai.

🎯 Forward = p plus. Reverse = p minus.

5️⃣ Rectifier ⭐

  • Rectifier: diode AC ka ek direction pass karke pulsating DC banata; smooth DC ke liye filter alag chahiye.
  • Half-wave: single diode ek half-cycle conduct, dusra block; output frequency input jaisi.
  • Full-wave: suitable multiple-diode/centre-tap arrangement dono half-cycles same output polarity; ripple frequency 2f.

💡 Rectification ka matlab perfect flat DC nahi, pehle pulsating output.

1️⃣ ऊर्जा बैंड ⭐

  • संयोजकता/चालन बैंड: इलेक्ट्रॉन अवस्थाएँ; बीच का वर्जित क्षेत्र E_g।
  • चालक: बैंड अतिव्यापन/अधभरा; कुचालक: बड़ा अंतराल; अर्धचालक: मध्यम अंतराल (Si≈1.1 eV)।
  • ताप: गर्म अर्धचालक में अधिक वाहक, चालकता सामान्यतः बढ़ती है।

💡 बैंड अंतराल से पदार्थ का विद्युत व्यवहार समझ आता है।

2️⃣ निज और अपद्रव्य अर्धचालक ⭐

  • निज: शुद्ध Si/Ge में ताप से इलेक्ट्रॉन-होल युग्म; n_e=n_h। होल धन वाहक जैसा।
  • n-प्रकार ⭐: पंचसंयोजी दाता (P/As), इलेक्ट्रॉन बहुसंख्यक; पदार्थ कुल तटस्थ।
  • p-प्रकार ⭐: त्रिसंयोजी ग्राही (B/Al), होल बहुसंख्यक; पदार्थ कुल तटस्थ।

🎯 n-प्रकार पदार्थ कुल ऋण आवेशित नहीं होता।

3️⃣ p-n संधि और अवक्षय ⭐

  • p और n मिलाने पर: इलेक्ट्रॉन n→p, होल p→n विसरित होकर संधि के पास पुनर्संयोजित।
  • अवक्षय क्षेत्र: गतिमान वाहक कम; स्थिर आयनों से आंतरिक क्षेत्र व अवरोध विभव।
  • संतुलन: विसरण और क्षेत्र-जनित अनुगमन बराबर; बाहरी बायस के बिना कुल धारा शून्य।

💡 अग्र अभिनति अवरोध पार करने में मदद करती है।

4️⃣ अग्र व पश्च अभिनति I-V ⭐⭐

  • अग्र अभिनति ⭐: p बैटरी +, n −; अवरोध/अवक्षय घटे, knee के बाद धारा तीव्र (~0.7 V Si, ~0.3 V Ge, अनुमान)।
  • पश्च अभिनति ⭐: p −, n +; अवक्षय बढ़े, अल्पसंख्यक वाहक की छोटी धारा, फिर भंजन।
  • डायोड: अधिकतर एक दिशा में प्रवाह; I-V अरैखिक। पाठ्यक्रम में सरल डायोड का अध्ययन।

🎯 अग्र = p धन; पश्च = p ऋण।

5️⃣ दिष्टकारी ⭐

  • दिष्टकारी: डायोड AC को एक दिशा की स्पंदित DC में बदलता; समतल DC हेतु फिल्टर चाहिए।
  • अर्ध-तरंग: एक डायोड एक अर्धचक्र पास, दूसरा रोके; निर्गत स्पंदन आवृत्ति f।
  • पूर्ण-तरंग: उपयुक्त बहु-डायोड/मध्य-टैप व्यवस्था दोनों अर्धचक्रों को समान ध्रुवता दे; स्पंदन 2f।

💡 दिष्टकरण के बाद पहले स्पंदित DC मिलती है, पूरी समतल नहीं।

1️⃣ Energy bands ⭐

  • Valence and conduction bands: allowed electron states separated by forbidden gap E_g.
  • Conductor: overlapping or partly filled bands; insulator: wide gap; semiconductor: modest gap (Si≈1.1 eV at room temperature).
  • Temperature: heating a semiconductor creates more carriers, generally increasing conductivity.

💡 Band gap helps explain electrical behaviour.

2️⃣ Intrinsic semiconductors and doping ⭐

  • Intrinsic: pure Si/Ge makes thermal electron-hole pairs; n_e=n_h. A hole acts as a positive carrier.
  • n-type ⭐: pentavalent donor (P/As) supplies extra electrons; electrons majority, holes minority. Material remains neutral overall.
  • p-type ⭐: trivalent acceptor (B/Al) creates holes as majority carriers; overall material remains neutral.

🎯 An n-type block is not negatively charged overall.

3️⃣ p-n junction and depletion ⭐

  • Joining p and n: electrons diffuse n→p and holes p→n, recombining near the boundary.
  • Depletion region: few mobile carriers; fixed ions make an internal field and barrier potential.
  • Equilibrium: diffusion and field-driven drift balance, giving zero net current without external bias.

💡 Forward bias helps carriers overcome the barrier.

4️⃣ Forward and reverse bias I-V ⭐⭐

  • Forward bias ⭐: p to battery + and n to −; barrier and depletion shrink, current climbs rapidly beyond the knee (~0.7 V Si, ~0.3 V Ge, rough room-temperature guides).
  • Reverse bias ⭐: p to − and n to +; depletion widens, only small minority-carrier leakage until breakdown.
  • Diode: current flows mainly one way and I-V is nonlinear; current CBSE theory focuses on the simple junction diode.

🎯 Forward means p to plus; reverse means p to minus.

5️⃣ Rectifier ⭐

  • Rectifier: diode turns AC into one-polarity pulsating DC; a separate filter is needed for smooth DC.
  • Half-wave: one diode passes one half-cycle and blocks the other; ripple frequency f.
  • Full-wave: a suitable multi-diode or centre-tapped circuit uses both half-cycles with the same output polarity; ripple frequency 2f.

💡 Rectification alone gives pulsating, not perfectly smooth, DC.

JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 Si ka approximate band gap room temperature pe?
Answer dekho

लगभग 1.1 eV.

Q2 Intrinsic semiconductor me electrons aur holes?
Answer dekho

Equal numbers of thermal electron-hole pairs.

Q3 n-type dopant ka example?
Answer dekho

Phosphorus (pentavalent donor).

Q4 p-type majority carriers?
Answer dekho

Holes.

Q5 n-type material overall charged?
Answer dekho

Nahi, overall neutral.

Q6 Junction pe depletion ka reason?
Answer dekho

Diffusion/recombination se mobile carriers kam; fixed ions remain.

Q7 Forward bias me p side kis terminal?
Answer dekho

Battery ke positive terminal se.

Q8 Reverse bias me depletion width?
Answer dekho

Increases.

Q9 Si diode knee approx?
Answer dekho

Roughly 0.7 V at room temperature (not exact).

Q10 50 Hz AC ko full-wave rectify karne par ripple frequency?
Answer dekho

2f=100 Hz.
