1. Energy Bands - Conductor, Semiconductor, Insulator ⭐

Electrons ke available states bands me aate hain: valence band (bound electrons) aur conduction band (free ho sakte hain). Beech ka forbidden region = band gap E_g. Conductor: bands overlap ya partially filled. Insulator: bahut bada gap. Semiconductor: modest gap (Si ≈ 1.1 eV room temperature pe). Semiconductor ko garam karo to aur zyada electrons gap cross karke conduction band me aate hain - conductivity BADHTI hai (metal ke opposite!).

300

2. Intrinsic aur Doping - n-type, p-type ⭐

Pure Si/Ge (intrinsic) me thermal energy se electron-hole pairs bante hain: n_e = n_h. Hole positive carrier ki tarah behave karta hai. Pentavalent donor (P/As) milao → n-type: electrons majority, holes minority. Trivalent acceptor (B/Al) milao → p-type: holes majority, electrons minority. Yaad rakho: doped material NET NEUTRAL rehta hai - n-type ka matlab negatively charged block NAHI hai!

3. p-n Junction - Depletion Region aur Barrier ⭐

p aur n ko join karo: junction ke paas electrons n→p aur holes p→n diffuse hokar recombine ho jaate hain. Beech ka region mobile carriers se khaali ho jaata hai - DELETION REGION, jisme sirf fixed ionized donors (+, n-side) aur acceptors (−, p-side) rehte hain. Inse built-in electric field aur barrier potential (~0.7 V Si) banta hai. Equilibrium me diffusion aur field-driven drift balance - bina bias ke net current ZERO.

4. Diode I-V - Forward aur Reverse Bias ⭐⭐

Forward bias: p ko battery ke +, n ko − → barrier aur depletion shrink, knee (~0.7 V Si, ~0.3 V Ge) ke baad current tezi se badhta hai. Reverse bias: p ko −, n ko + → depletion widen, sirf tiny minority-carrier leakage, bahut bade reverse voltage pe breakdown. Diode current mostly EK direction me jaane deta hai - I-V bilkul non-linear. Yaad: forward = p plus, reverse = p minus.

0.8

5. Rectifier - AC se Pulsating DC ⭐

Diode AC ka sirf ek direction pass karta hai, isliye output pulsating DC banta hai (perfect flat DC nahi - uske liye filter alag chahiye). Half-wave: ek diode sirf ek half-cycle conduct karta hai, dusra block - output ripple frequency input jaisi (f). Full-wave: multiple-diode/centre-tap arrangement dono half-cycles ko same polarity deta hai - ripple frequency 2f, output zyada smooth.

6. Rapid Quiz - Semiconductors Pakke? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: Si gap 1.1 eV, n-type electrons majority (neutral!), depletion = fixed ions + barrier, knee 0.7 V Si, full-wave ripple 2f.
