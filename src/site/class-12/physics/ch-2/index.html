📖 Full Explanation (Long Notes)

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Electric Potential — Energy Ka Height Map

  • Potential (V): unit positive charge ko infinity se us point tak laane me kitna work kiya — V = W/q₀. Scalar hai (sirf magnitude, no direction!). Unit: volt (1 V = 1 J/C).
  • Potential difference: do points ke beech V ka difference hi matter karta hai — absolute zero infinity pe maana jaata hai.
  • Point charge ka potential ⭐: V = kQ/r (1/r se girta hai — field 1/r² se, potential 1/r se!).
  • System of charges: scalar hai to seedha ADD karo: V = kΣ(qᵢ/rᵢ). Vector addition ki jhanjhat nahi! ⭐
  • Dipole ka potential: V = kp cos θ / r² — axial pe kp/r², equatorial pe ZERO.
  • Relation E aur V ⭐: E = −∇V (ek direction me Eₓ=−dV/dx) — field potential ka negative gradient. Uniform field me ΔV=−E·Δr.

💡 Feel: potential = pahad ki height, field = slope. Jahan tez slope, wahan strong field!

2️⃣ Equipotential Surfaces aur Potential Energy ⭐

  • Equipotential surface ⭐: jahan har point pe potential same — uspe charge move karne me ZERO work lagta hai (W = qΔV = 0).
  • Properties: field lines equipotential ke hamesha perpendicular; do equipotential surfaces kabhi intersect nahi kar sakti (ek point pe do V? impossible!).
  • Point charge ke liye: concentric spheres; uniform field ke liye: parallel planes.
  • Potential energy of 2 charges ⭐: U = k q₁q₂ / r — like charges: positive U (door bhejna energy deta hai), unlike: negative (bandhe hue hain).
  • 3 charges ka system: har pair ka U add karo: U = k(q₁q₂/r₁₂ + q₂q₃/r₂₃ + q₁q₃/r₁₃).
  • Dipole in field: U = −pE cos θ — θ = 0 pe minimum (stable).
  • Electron volt (eV) ⭐: 1 eV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J — atomic physics ka standard energy unit.

🎯 Scalar (V, U) me sirf add karo; vector (E, F) me components. Isliye potential se calculate karna easy padta hai!

3️⃣ Conductors aur Dielectrics ⭐

  • Electrostatics me conductor ke rules ⭐: (1) andar E = 0 hamesha; (2) saara charge surface pe rehta hai; (3) surface equipotential hai; (4) surface pe field perpendicular hota hai, E = σ/ε₀.
  • Electrostatic shielding: conductor ke hollow cavity me E = 0 — andar cheezein bahar ke field se safe (car me bijli se suraksha!).
  • Dielectric (insulator): free charges nahi, par molecules polarize ho jaate hain — induced dipoles ban jaate hain.
  • Polar dielectrics: permanent dipole moment (H₂O); non-polar: field lagane pe hi dipole banta hai (CO₂, N₂).
  • Effect ⭐: dielectric andar ka field kam kar deta hai: E = E₀/K, jahan K = dielectric constant (≥ 1). Paani ka K ≈ 80!
  • Dielectric strength: max field jisse dielectric bina breakdown ke jhel sake (air: ~3 × 10⁶ V/m).

💡 Conductor field ko zero kar deta hai (free charges move karke); dielectric sirf K times kam karta hai (bound charges polarize hoke).

4️⃣ Capacitor aur Capacitance ⭐⭐

  • Capacitor: charge store karne ka device — do conductors, beech me insulator. Capacitance C = Q/V. Unit: farad (bahut bada! practical: μF, nF, pF).
  • Parallel plate capacitor ⭐: C = ε₀A/d — area badhao ya gap ghatao, capacitance badhti hai. Beech ka field: E = σ/ε₀ = Q/(ε₀A).
  • Dielectric ke saath ⭐: C = Kε₀A/d = KC₀ — dielectric daalne se capacitance K times badh jaati hai.
  • Battery connected rahe to: V constant, Q badhta hai (K times). Battery disconnect karke dielectric daalo to: Q constant, V ghat jaata hai (1/K). ⭐ Exam favourite confusion!
  • Isolated sphere ka C (extra): C = 4πε₀R.
  • Beyond current CBSE outline: spherical and cylindrical capacitor formulas are not needed for the listed theory syllabus.

🎯 Capacitor ka formula KA/d: Kam Area ya zyada Distance = kam C. Yaad rakhne ka trick: CAPACITANCE upar (A), doori neeche (d).

5️⃣ Capacitors Ka Combination aur Stored Energy ⭐

  • Series ⭐: 1/C = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ + ... — har capacitor pe SAME charge Q, voltage divide hota hai. Series capacitance sabse chhote se bhi CHHOTI hoti hai.
  • Parallel ⭐: C = C₁ + C₂ + ... — har capacitor pe SAME voltage, charge divide hota hai. (Resistors ke ULTE rules!)
  • Series kab? voltage rating badhani ho; parallel kab? zyada capacitance/storage chahiye.
  • Energy stored ⭐⭐: U = ½CV² = ½Q²/C = ½QV — teeno forms equivalent hain, situation ke hisaab se use karo.
  • Energy density ⭐: u = ½ε₀E² (field me stored energy per unit volume) — ye energy field ME hai, plates pe nahi!
  • Common plates (redistribution): do charged capacitors connect karo to charge conserve, energy ka kuch hissa heat me lost.

💡 Capacitors resistors ke opposite hain: series me reciprocal add, parallel me direct add. Ek line me yaad!

1️⃣ विद्युत विभव — ऊर्जा का मानचित्र

  • विभव (V): इकाई धन आवेश को अनंत से उस बिंदु तक लाने में किया गया कार्य — V = W/q₀। अदिश राशि। मात्रक: वोल्ट (1 V = 1 J/C)।
  • विभवांतर: दो बिंदुओं के बीच V का अंतर ही सार्थक है — शून्य अनंत पर माना जाता है।
  • बिंदु आवेश का विभव ⭐: V = kQ/r (1/r से घटता है — क्षेत्र 1/r² से, विभव 1/r से!)।
  • आवेशों का निकाय: अदिश है अतः सीधे योग: V = kΣ(qᵢ/rᵢ) ⭐
  • द्विध्रुव का विभव: V = kp cos θ / r² — निरक्षीय पर शून्य।
  • E और V का संबंध ⭐: E = −∇V (एक दिशा में Eₓ=−dV/dx) — क्षेत्र विभव की ऋणात्मक प्रवणता।

💡 विभव = पहाड़ की ऊंचाई, क्षेत्र = ढलान। जहाँ तेज ढलान, वहाँ प्रबल क्षेत्र!

2️⃣ समविभव पृष्ठ और स्थितिज ऊर्जा ⭐

  • समविभव पृष्ठ ⭐: जहाँ प्रत्येक बिंदु पर विभव समान — उस पर आवेश गतिमान करने में शून्य कार्य।
  • गुण: क्षेत्र रेखाएं समविभव पृष्ठ के सदैव लंबवत; दो समविभव पृष्ठ कभी प्रतिच्छेद नहीं करते।
  • बिंदु आवेश: संकेंद्री गोले; एकसमान क्षेत्र: समांतर तल।
  • दो आवेशों की स्थितिज ऊर्जा ⭐: U = k q₁q₂ / r — समान आवेश: धनात्मक U; असमान: ऋणात्मक।
  • तीन आवेशों का निकाय: प्रत्येक युग्म का U जोड़ो।
  • क्षेत्र में द्विध्रुव: U = −pE cos θ।
  • इलेक्ट्रॉन वोल्ट (eV) ⭐: 1 eV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J।

🎯 अदिश (V, U) में केवल योग; सदिश (E, F) में घटक। इसीलिए विभव से गणना आसान!

3️⃣ चालक और परावैद्युत ⭐

  • स्थिरवैद्युतिकी में चालक के नियम ⭐: (1) भीतर E = 0 सदैव; (2) समस्त आवेश पृष्ठ पर; (3) पृष्ठ समविभव; (4) पृष्ठ पर क्षेत्र लंबवत, E = σ/ε₀।
  • स्थिरवैद्युत परिरक्षण: चालक की कोटर में E = 0 — भीतर की वस्तुएं बाहरी क्षेत्र से सुरक्षित।
  • परावैद्युत (कुचालक): मुक्त आवेश नहीं, परंतु अणु ध्रुवित हो जाते हैं।
  • ध्रुवीय परावैद्युत: स्थायी द्विध्रुव आघूर्ण (H₂O); अध्रुवीय: क्षेत्र लगाने पर ही द्विध्रुव बनता है।
  • प्रभाव ⭐: परावैद्युत भीतर का क्षेत्र घटाता है: E = E₀/K। जल का K ≈ 80!
  • परावैद्युत सामर्थ्य: अधिकतम क्षेत्र जिसे परावैद्युत भंजन के बिना सह सके।

💡 चालक क्षेत्र को शून्य करता है; परावैद्युत केवल K गुना घटाता है।

4️⃣ संधारित्र और धारिता ⭐⭐

  • संधारित्र: आवेश संचय करने की युक्ति — दो चालक, बीच में कुचालक। धारिता C = Q/V। मात्रक: फैरड (व्यावहारिक: μF, nF, pF)।
  • समांतर पट्टिका संधारित्र ⭐: C = ε₀A/d — क्षेत्रफल बढ़ाओ या दूरी घटाओ, धारिता बढ़ती है। बीच का क्षेत्र: E = σ/ε₀।
  • परावैद्युत सहित ⭐: C = Kε₀A/d = KC₀ — धारिता K गुना बढ़ जाती है।
  • बैटरी जुड़ी रहे तो: V नियत, Q बढ़ता है (K गुना)। बैटरी हटाकर परावैद्युत डालो तो: Q नियत, V घटता है (1/K) ⭐
  • विलगित गोले की C: C = 4πε₀R।

🎯 सूत्र KA/d: कम क्षेत्रफल या अधिक दूरी = कम C।

5️⃣ संधारित्रों का संयोजन और संचित ऊर्जा ⭐

  • श्रेणी ⭐: 1/C = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ + ... — प्रत्येक पर समान आवेश Q, वोल्टता विभाजित।
  • समांतर ⭐: C = C₁ + C₂ + ... — प्रत्येक पर समान वोल्टता, आवेश विभाजित। (प्रतिरोधों के विपरीत नियम!)
  • संचित ऊर्जा ⭐⭐: U = ½CV² = ½Q²/C = ½QV — तीनों रूप तुल्य हैं।
  • ऊर्जा घनत्व ⭐: u = ½ε₀E² — ऊर्जा क्षेत्र में संचित है।
  • पुनर्वितरण: दो आवेशित संधारित्र जोड़ने पर आवेश संरक्षित, कुछ ऊर्जा ऊष्मा में ह्रास।

💡 संधारित्र प्रतिरोधों के विपरीत: श्रेणी में व्युत्क्रम योग, समांतर में प्रत्यक्ष योग।

1️⃣ Electric Potential — An Energy Map

  • Potential (V): the work done in bringing a unit positive charge from infinity to that point — V = W/q₀. It is a scalar (magnitude only, no direction!). Unit: volt (1 V = 1 J/C).
  • Potential difference: only the difference in V between two points matters — the zero is taken at infinity.
  • Potential of a point charge ⭐: V = kQ/r (falls as 1/r — field falls as 1/r², potential as 1/r!).
  • System of charges: being a scalar, just ADD directly: V = kΣ(qᵢ/rᵢ). No vector headaches! ⭐
  • Dipole potential: V = kp cos θ / r² — kp/r² on the axis, ZERO on the equatorial line.
  • Relation between E and V ⭐: E = −∇V (along x, Eₓ=−dV/dx) — field is the negative potential gradient. In a uniform field ΔV=−E·Δr.

💡 Feel: potential = the height of a hill, field = the slope. Steeper slope, stronger field!

2️⃣ Equipotential Surfaces and Potential Energy ⭐

  • Equipotential surface ⭐: every point on it has the same potential — moving a charge on it needs ZERO work (W = qΔV = 0).
  • Properties: field lines are always perpendicular to equipotentials; two equipotential surfaces can never intersect (one point, two potentials? impossible!).
  • For a point charge: concentric spheres; for a uniform field: parallel planes.
  • Potential energy of 2 charges ⭐: U = k q₁q₂ / r — like charges: positive U (letting them move apart releases energy); unlike: negative (bound together).
  • Three-charge system: add U over every pair: U = k(q₁q₂/r₁₂ + q₂q₃/r₂₃ + q₁q₃/r₁₃).
  • Dipole in a field: U = −pE cos θ — minimum at θ = 0 (stable).
  • Electron volt (eV) ⭐: 1 eV = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J — the standard energy unit of atomic physics.

🎯 Scalars (V, U) just add; vectors (E, F) need components. That is why calculating through potential is easier!

3️⃣ Conductors and Dielectrics ⭐

  • Conductor rules in electrostatics ⭐: (1) E = 0 inside, always; (2) all charge resides on the surface; (3) the surface is equipotential; (4) the field at the surface is perpendicular, E = σ/ε₀.
  • Electrostatic shielding: E = 0 inside a conductor's hollow cavity — things inside stay safe from outside fields (why a car is safe in lightning!).
  • Dielectric (insulator): no free charges, but molecules polarize — induced dipoles appear.
  • Polar dielectrics: permanent dipole moment (H₂O); non-polar: a dipole appears only when a field is applied (CO₂, N₂).
  • Effect ⭐: a dielectric reduces the internal field: E = E₀/K, where K = dielectric constant (≥ 1). Water has K ≈ 80!
  • Dielectric strength: the maximum field a dielectric can withstand without breakdown (air: ~3 × 10⁶ V/m).

💡 A conductor kills the field completely (free charges move); a dielectric only reduces it K times (bound charges polarize).

4️⃣ Capacitors and Capacitance ⭐⭐

  • Capacitor: a device to store charge — two conductors with an insulator between. Capacitance C = Q/V. Unit: farad (very large! practical units: μF, nF, pF).
  • Parallel plate capacitor ⭐: C = ε₀A/d — increase area or shrink the gap, capacitance grows. Field between plates: E = σ/ε₀ = Q/(ε₀A).
  • With a dielectric ⭐: C = Kε₀A/d = KC₀ — inserting a dielectric multiplies the capacitance by K.
  • Battery stays connected: V constant, Q grows (K times). Battery disconnected first: Q constant, V drops (by 1/K). ⭐ Favourite exam confusion!
  • Capacitance of an isolated sphere (extra): C = 4πε₀R.
  • Beyond current CBSE outline: spherical and cylindrical capacitor formulas are not needed for the listed theory syllabus.

🎯 Formula KA/d: less Area or more Distance = less C. Memory trick: capacitance on top (A), distance below (d).

5️⃣ Combining Capacitors and Stored Energy ⭐

  • Series ⭐: 1/C = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ + ... — SAME charge Q on each, voltage divides. Series capacitance is SMALLER than the smallest one.
  • Parallel ⭐: C = C₁ + C₂ + ... — SAME voltage across each, charge divides. (Exactly OPPOSITE to resistor rules!)
  • Series when? you need a higher voltage rating; parallel when? you need more capacitance/storage.
  • Stored energy ⭐⭐: U = ½CV² = ½Q²/C = ½QV — all three forms are equivalent; pick by situation.
  • Energy density ⭐: u = ½ε₀E² (energy per unit volume stored in the field) — the energy lives IN the field, not on the plates!
  • Charge redistribution: connecting two charged capacitors conserves charge but loses some energy as heat.

💡 Capacitors are the opposite of resistors: series adds reciprocals, parallel adds directly. One line to remember!

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Point charge +5 μC se 50 cm door potential kitna?
Answer dekho

V = kQ/r = 9×10⁹ × 5×10⁻⁶ / 0.5 = 9 × 10⁴ V (90,000 V).

Q2. Equipotential surface pe charge move karne me kitna work?
Answer dekho

Zero — kyunki ΔV = 0, W = qΔV = 0.

Q3. Do charges +3 μC aur −2 μC 10 cm pe hain. Potential energy?
Answer dekho

U = kq₁q₂/r = 9×10⁹ × (3×10⁻⁶)(−2×10⁻⁶)/0.1 = −0.54 J (negative — bound system).

Q4. E aur V ka relation batao.
Answer dekho

E = −∇V — field potential ka negative gradient hai. Uniform field me E = V/d.

Q5. Parallel plate capacitor ka C = 8 μF. Plates ke beech dielectric K = 4 daal diya (battery disconnected). Naya C aur V pe effect?
Answer dekho

C = KC₀ = 32 μF; Q constant hai to V = Q/C 1/4 ho jaayega.

Q6. 6 μF aur 3 μF capacitors series me. Equivalent C?
Answer dekho

1/C = 1/6 + 1/3 = 3/6 → C = 2 μF (sabse chhote se bhi chhota!).

Q7. Wahi dono parallel me?
Answer dekho

C = 6 + 3 = 9 μF.

Q8. 100 μF capacitor 50 V pe charged hai. Stored energy?
Answer dekho

U = ½CV² = ½ × 100×10⁻⁶ × 2500 = 0.125 J.

Q9. Conductor ke andar electric field zero kyun hota hai (electrostatics me)?
Answer dekho

Free charges surface pe rearrange ho jaate hain jab tak andar ka field exactly cancel na ho jaaye — nahi to current behta rahega, jo electrostatics nahi hai.

Q10. Energy density of electric field ka formula?
Answer dekho

u = ½ε₀E² — energy field me stored hoti hai, plates pe nahi.
