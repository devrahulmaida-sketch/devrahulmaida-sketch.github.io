1. Electric Potential - Energy Ka Height Map ⭐

Potential V = W/q₀ - unit positive charge ko infinity se us point tak laane me kitna work. SCALAR hai (no direction!), unit volt. Point charge ka V = kQ/r - field 1/r² se girta hai, potential sirf 1/r se! Aur scalar hone ka sabse bada fayda: system ka potential seedha ADD: V = kΣqᵢ/rᵢ - vector components ki jhanjhat nahi!

2. E aur V Ka Rishta - Field = Slope ⭐

E = −dV/dx: field, potential ka NEGATIVE gradient hai - potential = pahad ki height, field = slope! Uniform field me ΔV = −E·Δr: do plates ke beech E = V/d. Equipotential surfaces pe har point same V - unpe charge move karne me ZERO work (W = qΔV = 0); field lines equipotential ke hamesha PERPENDICULAR, aur do equipotentials kabhi intersect nahi karti. Electron volt: 1 eV = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ J - 1 electron ko 1 V se accelerate karne ki energy.

3. Potential Energy - Charges Ki Stored Taakat ⭐

Do charges ka system: U = kq₁q₂/r. LIKE charges: U POSITIVE - paas laane me work karna pada, door bhejne pe energy milegi. UNLIKE: U NEGATIVE - bound system, alag karne me work karna padega. 3 charges ka system: har PAIR ka U add karo: U = k(q₁q₂/r₁₂ + q₂q₃/r₂₃ + q₁q₃/r₁₃) - sirf pairs, kuch double count nahi! Scalar hai to seedha sum - isliye energy se sochna easy.

4. Capacitor Lab - Charge Ka Bank ⭐⭐

Capacitance C = Q/V (farad; practical µF/pF). Parallel plate: C = ε₀A/d - area Upar, doori Neeche! Dielectric daalo to C = KC₀. EXAM FAVOURITE: battery CONNECTED rahe to V constant, charge K times badhta hai; battery DISCONNECT karke dielectric daalo to Q constant, voltage 1/K! Energy U = ½CV² = ½Q²/C = ½QV - aur energy FIELD me stored hai: density u = ½ε₀E². Combination: SERIES me 1/C = Σ1/Cᵢ (same Q, sabse chhote se chhoti), PARALLEL me C = ΣCᵢ (same V) - resistors ke bilkul ULTE!

5. Rapid Quiz - Potential Pakka? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: V scalar (seedha add!), field 1/r² par potential 1/r, equipotential pe ZERO work, dielectric me connected vs disconnected ka farak, capacitors resistors ke ULTE combine hote hain!

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo! ⭐

Potential ⭐

V = W/q₀, SCALAR, volt | Point charge V = kQ/r (∝1/r; field 1/r²!) | System: V = kΣqᵢ/rᵢ seedha add | Dipole: V = kp cosθ/r², equatorial pe ZERO | E = −dV/dx; uniform me ΔV = −E·Δr

Equipotential ⭐

Har point same V | Move karne me W = qΔV = ZERO | Field lines hamesha PERPENDICULAR | Kabhi intersect nahi | Point charge: concentric spheres; uniform field: parallel planes | 1 eV = 1.6×10⁻¹⁹ J

Potential Energy ⭐

U = kq₁q₂/r | Like: U positive (door bhejna energy deta); unlike: negative (bound) | 3 charges: har PAIR add, double count nahi | Dipole: U = −pE cosθ (θ=0 stable)

Conductor + Dielectric ⭐

Conductor: andar E = 0, charge surface pe, surface equipotential, E = σ/ε₀ ⊥ | Shielding: cavity me E = 0 | Dielectric: polarize hoke E = E₀/K (water K≈80) | Dielectric strength air ≈ 3×10⁶ V/m

Capacitance ⭐⭐

C = Q/V (farad) | Parallel plate C = ε₀A/d | Dielectric se C = KC₀ | Battery CONNECTED: V const, Q → KQ | DISCONNECTED: Q const, V → V/K | Isolated sphere C = 4πε₀R

Combination + Energy ⭐⭐

Series: 1/C = Σ1/Cᵢ (same Q, V divide, chhote se CHHOTI) | Parallel: C = ΣCᵢ (same V) - resistors ke ULTE | U = ½CV² = ½Q²/C = ½QV | Density u = ½ε₀E² (energy FIELD me!)
