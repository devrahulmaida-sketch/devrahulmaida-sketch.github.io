AC Ka Visual Walkthrough

1. RMS Lab - 230 V Ka Asli Matlab ⭐⭐

AC har half-cycle pe direction badalta hai - pure cycle ka average ZERO! Toh ghar ki 230 V kya hai? RMS value: woh DC value jo same resistor me utni hi heating kare. V_rms = V₀/√2, I_rms = I₀/√2. 230 V RMS hai - peak V₀ = 230√2 ≈ 325 V! Half-cycle average = 2V₀/π. Bulb dekho - uski heating RMS se decide hoti hai!

2. Phase Lab - ELI the ICE Man ⭐⭐

Resistor: V aur I SAME phase me. Inductor: X_L = ωL, current voltage se 90° PEECHE (lag) - frequency badhao toh opposition badhta hai. Capacitor: X_C = 1/(ωC), current voltage se 90° AAGE (lead) - frequency badhao toh opposition ghatta. Yaad rakho: ELI the ICE man - E leads I in L; I leads E in C! Element chuno aur phasor + waves dekho.

50 Hz
100 mH

3. LCR Lab - Impedance aur Resonance ⭐⭐

Series LCR me voltages VECTOR add hote hain (L ka +90°, C ka −90°), arithmetic add NAHI! Z = √[R² + (X_L − X_C)²], tanφ = (X_L − X_C)/R. f badhao → X_L badhta, X_C ghatta. Jab X_L = X_C → RESONANCE: Z = R minimum, current MAXIMUM, φ = 0! (L = 0.2 H, C = 50 µF fixed - f aur R ghumao, curve ka peak dekho.)

50 Hz
20 Ω

4. Transformer Lab - Voltage Ka Jadu ⭐

Mutual induction se AC voltage badlo! Ideal transformer: V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p, I_s/I_p = N_p/N_s - power same rehti hai. Step-up (N_s > N_p): voltage badhe, current ghate. Isliye power lines high voltage pe - same power ke liye I kam → I²R loss bahut kam! Turns ghumao aur loss comparison dekho. Yaad rakho: transformer steady DC pe kaam NAHI karta.

5. Rapid Quiz - AC Pakka? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: V_rms = V₀/√2 (230 V RMS hai, peak 325 V!), ELI the ICE man (L me I lag, C me I lead), Z = √[R² + (X_L − X_C)²], resonance pe Z = R min aur I max, P = V_rms I_rms cosφ (pure L/C me zero!), V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p, transformer sirf AC pe!

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo! ⭐

AC + RMS ⭐⭐

i(t) = I₀ sin ωt, ω = 2πf | India: f = 50 Hz | I_rms = I₀/√2, V_rms = V₀/√2 | 230 V = RMS, peak = 230√2 ≈ 325 V | Full-cycle avg = 0, half-cycle avg = 2I₀/π

R / L / C Phase ⭐⭐

R: V, I same phase | L: X_L = ωL, I 90° LAG (f ↑ → X_L ↑) | C: X_C = 1/(ωC), I 90° LEAD (f ↑ → X_C ↓) | ELI the ICE man: E leads I in L, I leads E in C

Series LCR ⭐⭐

Z = √[R² + (X_L − X_C)²] | tanφ = (X_L − X_C)/R | I_rms = V_rms/Z | Phasors VECTOR add hote hain - arithmetic nahi!

Resonance ⭐⭐

X_L = X_C → ω₀ = 1/√(LC) | Z = R MINIMUM, current MAXIMUM, φ = 0 | L aur C ke effects cancel, par unke individual voltages bade ho sakte hain

Power Factor ⭐

P = V_rms I_rms cosφ = I_rms²R | cosφ = R/Z | Pure L/C: φ = 90°, P = 0 - WATTLESS current | Apparent power VI (VA) ≠ real power VI cosφ (W)

Transformer + Traps ⭐

V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p, I_s/I_p = N_p/N_s | Step-up: N_s > N_p | Sirf AC - steady DC pe NAHI | Traps: 230 V peak NAHI RMS hai | lead/lag ulta mat karo | Z arithmetic sum nahi | resonance pe Z minimum
