1. Light - Wave Bhi, Photon Bhi ⭐

Wave evidence: interference aur diffraction. Particle evidence: photoelectric effect. Photon ki energy E = hf = hc/λ aur momentum p = h/λ = E/c (h ≈ 6.626×10⁻³⁴ J s). Intensity badhao → photons ki SANKHYA badhti hai; frequency badhao → HAR photon ki energy badhti hai. 2× intensity se har photon double energetic NAHI hota!

2. Photoelectric Effect Lab ⭐⭐

Metal pe light daalo: threshold frequency f₀ se NEECHE (f < f₀) kitni bhi intensity - emission ZERO! f₀ ke upar emission lagbhag turant. Fixed f pe intensity badhao → electrons ki sankhya (current) badhti hai, par maximum kinetic energy NAHI. Ek photon - ek electron: K_max = hf − φ, jahan φ = hf₀ work function (material pe depend). Shortcut: E(eV) ≈ 1240/λ(nm).

3. Einstein Equation aur Graphs ⭐⭐

K_max = hf − φ = h(f − f₀) = eV_s (V_s = stopping potential ka magnitude). Graph K_max vs f: SEEDHI line, slope = h (sab metals ka SAME!), x-intercept = f₀. Photocurrent vs voltage: positive V pe saturation current (intensity se badhta), reverse V pe current stopping potential −V_s pe zero. Frequency badhao → |V_s| badhta hai; sirf intensity badhao → V_s NAHI.

4. de Broglie Matter Waves ⭐

Har moving particle ki wavelength: λ = h/p = h/(mv) (nonrelativistic). Electron V volt se accelerate: p = √(2meV), λ = h/√(2meV), handy: λ(Å) ≈ 12.27/√V. V badhao → speed badhi → λ GHATI. Macroscopic cheez ka momentum bada → λ bahut hi chhoti - isliye daily life me wave character nahi dikhta!

5. Rapid Quiz - Dual Nature Pakka? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: intensity = photon count, frequency = energy per photon; f < f₀ pe emission zero; K_max = hf − φ = eV_s; graph slope = h; λ(Å) ≈ 12.27/√V.

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo! ⭐

Photon ⭐

E = hf = hc/λ | p = h/λ = E/c | h ≈ 6.626×10⁻³⁴ J s | E(eV) ≈ 1240/λ(nm) | Intensity = photon RATE, frequency = energy per photon | 2× intensity ≠ 2× photon energy

Observations ⭐

Threshold f₀: f < f₀ → NO emission, kitni bhi intensity | f > f₀ → turant emission | f fixed: intensity↑ → current↑, K_max same | f₀ material pe depend

Einstein ⭐⭐

1 photon : 1 electron | K_max = hf − φ = h(f−f₀) = eV_s | φ = hf₀ | |V_s| frequency se badhta, intensity se nahi

Graphs ⭐

K_max vs f: seedhi line, slope = h, x-intercept = f₀ | I vs V: +V saturation (intensity se↑) | −V_s pe current zero | V_s frequency/material se

de Broglie ⭐

λ = h/p = h/(mv) | Electron V se: p = √(2meV), λ = h/√(2meV) | λ(Å) ≈ 12.27/√V | v↑ → λ↓ | Macroscopic: λ ~ 10⁻³⁴ m, imperceptible

Numericals ⭐⭐

λ nm me lo: E(eV) = 1240/λ | K_max(eV) = E − φ(eV) | K_max < 0 ka matlab emission NAHI | V_s = K_max/e (volt me K_max eV ke barabar)
