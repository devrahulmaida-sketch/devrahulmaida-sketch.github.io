1. Bar Magnet - Magnet Ka ABCD ⭐

Bar magnet ke do poles - N aur S. Monopole KABHI nahi milta: tod do to har tukda apna N-S wala chhota magnet ban jaata hai (atom tak - har electron khud magnet hai!). Properties: iron ko ATTRACT, free magnet N-S me ALIGN (directive - compass ka basis), like poles repel. Field lines: BAHAR N se S, ANDAR S se N - CLOSED loops! (Electric lines + se − pe khatam hoti hain, magnetic kabhi khatam nahi hoti.) Kabhi intersect nahi, dense = strong.

2. Magnetic Dipole Field - Axial vs Equatorial ⭐

Bar magnet = current solenoid (same field pattern!) - dono magnetic dipoles, moment m (S se N). AXIAL line pe B = (μ₀/4π)·2m/r³ (m ki direction me), EQUATORIAL pe B = (μ₀/4π)·m/r³ (m ke OPPOSITE) - axial EXACTLY DOUBLE, electric dipole wala hi factor 2! Dono 1/r³ se girte hain: doori DOUBLE → field 1/8! Current loop ka bhi dipole moment hota: m = NIA - coil, solenoid, magnet sab ek family.

3. Uniform Field Me Magnet - Torque aur Energy ⭐

Uniform field B me bar magnet pe torque τ = mB sinθ (vector: τ = m×B) - field magnet ko apne saath ALIGN karna chahta hai! θ=0: torque ZERO, U = −mB (MINIMUM - stable equilibrium, chhota dhakka wapas kheench le aata hai). θ=90°: torque MAX. θ=180°: torque phir zero par U = +mB (MAX - UNSTABLE, palat jaayega!). Ghumane ka work: W = mB(cosθ₁ − cosθ₂); 0 se 180° = 2mB. Compass needle isi torque se N-S align hoti hai (Earth ka field!).

4. Magnetic Materials - Dia, Para, Ferro ⭐⭐

DIAMAGNETIC: field ko weakly REPEL - lines andar se door dhakelta (Bi, Cu, Ag, water; χ chhota NEGATIVE ~−10⁻⁵). PARAMAGNETIC: weakly attract - atoms ke permanent dipoles align (Al, Na, Pt, oxygen; χ chhota POSITIVE ~10⁻⁵ se 10⁻³). FERROMAGNETIC: STRONGLY attract - DOMAINS (lakhs aligned atoms) field ke saath line me, lines andar KHEENCH leta (Fe, Co, Ni; χ ~10³!). Curie temperature ke upar ferro → para (iron: 1043 K). Permanent magnets = hard ferro (steel, Alnico); electromagnets = soft iron. Trick: DIA = डरावना, PARA = प्यार, FERRO = फ़ुल प्यार!

5. Rapid Quiz - Magnetism Pakka? ⭐⭐

5 sawaal. Yaad rakho: monopole nahi milta, field lines closed loops (bahar N→S, andar S→N), axial = 2× equatorial (dono 1/r³), θ=0 stable aur θ=180 unstable, DIA repel PARA weak FERRO strong!

Formula Recap - Screenshot Le Lo! ⭐

Bar Magnet ⭐

Do poles, monopole NAHI - todne pe har tukda N-S magnet | Attract iron, align N-S (directive) | Like repel, unlike attract | Lines: bahar N→S, andar S→N = CLOSED loops | Kabhi intersect nahi | m = q_m×2l (S se N)

Dipole Field ⭐

Magnet = solenoid (same pattern) | Axial: B = (μ₀/4π)·2m/r³ | Equatorial: B = (μ₀/4π)·m/r³, OPPOSITE m - axial DOUBLE | Dono 1/r³: r double → B = 1/8 | Loop: m = NIA

Torque + Energy ⭐

τ = mB sinθ (m×B) | θ=0: τ=0, U=−mB STABLE | θ=90°: τ MAX | θ=180°: U=+mB UNSTABLE | W = mB(cosθ₁−cosθ₂); 0→180 = 2mB | Compass = Earth field me alignment

Magnetization ⭐

M = dipole moment per volume (A/m) | M = χH, B = μ₀(H+M) | χ sign se material pehchano: dia −, para +, ferro +++ | Para response T badhane pe kam

Dia + Para ⭐

DIA: weakly REPEL, χ ~−10⁻⁵ (Bi, Cu, Ag, water) - lines door dhakelta | PARA: weakly attract, χ ~10⁻⁵-10⁻³ (Al, Na, Pt, O₂) - dipoles align | Trick: DIA=डरावना, PARA=प्यार

Ferro + Curie ⭐⭐

FERRO: STRONG attract, χ ~10³ (Fe, Co, Ni) | DOMAINS align → strong magnet, lines andar KHEENCHTA | Curie T ke upar ferro→para (Fe: 1043 K) | Hard (steel, Alnico) = permanent; soft iron = electromagnet
