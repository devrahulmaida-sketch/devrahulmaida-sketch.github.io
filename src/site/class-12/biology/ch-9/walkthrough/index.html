Class 12 Biology - Chapter 9 - Biotechnology: Principles and Processes

Biotechnology Ka Visual Walkthrough

8 interactive scenes - biotech ke 2 pillars, restriction enzyme cut, ligase join, vector toolkit, DNA delivery methods, PCR + gel electrophoresis, bioreactor, aur quiz.

1. Biotech Ke 2 Pillars - Gene Edit + Scale Up ⭐

Biotechnology = living cells/enzymes se useful product ya process. Iske DO core pillars hain: GENETIC ENGINEERING (DNA cut, join, host me transfer) aur BIOPROCESS ENGINEERING (contamination-free large-scale growth). Applications: insulin, vaccines, diagnostics, crops, industrial enzymes. Pillar chuno, detail dekho!

2. Restriction Enzymes - Molecular Scissors ✂️

Restriction endonucleases specific PALINDROMIC DNA sequence pe dono strands cut karte hain. EcoRI: 5'-GAATTC-3'. Staggered cut se STICKY ends (complementary overhangs); kuch enzymes BLUNT ends banate hain. Steps dekho!

3. DNA Ligase - Fragments Jodo 🔗

DNA ligase sugar-phosphate backbone me covalent PHOSPHODIESTER bonds banata hai - RECOMBINANT DNA ready! Sticky ends pehle hydrogen-bond se align hote hain, phir ligase seal karta hai. Bacteria apni DNA METHYLATE karke protect karte hain; foreign phage DNA cut hota hai.

4. Vector Toolkit - pBR322 Map 🧬

Plasmid vector self-replicating circular DNA hai jo gene ko host bacteria tak le jaata hai. Usse chahiye: ORI (origin of replication), SELECTABLE MARKER (antibiotic resistance), CLONING SITE (unique restriction site). Part chuno - blue-white screening bhi dekho!

5. DNA Ko Cell Me Kaise Daalein? 🚪

Recombinant DNA bana liya - ab host cell ke andar kaise? 5 tareeke: competent cells (CaCl2 + heat shock), electroporation (electric pulse se pores), microinjection (animal cells), gene gun (gold/tungsten particles, plants), Agrobacterium (Ti plasmid, plants). Yaad rakho: ENTRY alag, SELECTION alag, EXPRESSION alag!

6. PCR + Gel Electrophoresis - Amplify Karo, Check Karo 🧪

PCR target DNA ko amplify karta hai: DENATURATION (~95°C) → primer ANNEALING → EXTENSION (~72°C, Taq polymerase - Thermus aquaticus se heat-stable). Har cycle DNA DOUBLE! Phir gel pe check: negative DNA ANODE ki taraf, CHHOTE fragments door. Mode chuno!

7. Bioreactor + Downstream - Industry Scale 🏭

Bioreactor bada vessel hai jo pH, temperature, oxygen, nutrients aur mixing CONTROL rakhta hai. Stirred-tank me IMPELLER mix karta hai, SPARGER air/oxygen deta hai. Product banne ke baad DOWNSTREAM PROCESSING: separate, purify, formulate, quality test. Part chuno!

8. Quiz - Biotech Pakka? ⭐

5 sawaal - EcoRI, sticky ends, blue-white screening, PCR order, gel migration. Score 5/5 lao!

Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

2 pillarsGenetic engineering: DNA targeted cut, join, new host me transfer + express. Bioprocess engineering: sterile, contamination-free, controlled large-scale growth. Applications: insulin, vaccines, diagnostics, crops, industrial enzymes.
Restriction + ligaseEcoRI cuts 5'-GAATTC-3' (palindrome). Staggered cut → sticky ends (complementary overhangs); blunt = no overhang. Ligase = phosphodiester bonds → recombinant DNA. Bacteria apni DNA methylate karte (defence); phage DNA cut hota hai.
Vector toolkitpBR322: ori (copy number) + selectable marker (antibiotic resistance) + cloning site (unique restriction site). Insertional inactivation: insert marker disrupt kare to recombinant pehchan. Blue-white: WHITE colony = insert (lacZ disrupt), BLUE = empty vector. Ti plasmid (disarmed) → plants.
DNA deliveryCompetent cells: CaCl2 + brief heat shock. Electroporation: electric pulse → temporary pores. Microinjection: needle → animal cell nucleus. Gene gun: DNA-coated gold/tungsten → plant cells. Agrobacterium: Ti plasmid natural transfer. Entry + selection + expression = 3 alag checks!
PCR + gelPCR order: Denaturation (~95°C) → Annealing (primers) → Extension (~72°C). Taq polymerase = heat-stable, Thermus aquaticus se. Copies = 2^n cycles. Gel: DNA negative → ANODE move; CHHOTE fragments DOOR. Band elute karke DNA recover. Negative controls zaroori (contamination = false positive).
Bioreactor + downstreamStirred-tank bioreactor: pH, temp, oxygen, nutrients, mixing controlled. Impeller = mixing, sparger = air/O2. Downstream: separate → purify → formulate → quality/safety test. Scale-up tricky: oxygen transfer + contamination control. Injectable protein = purity critical.
