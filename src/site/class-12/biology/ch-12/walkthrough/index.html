Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

StructureEcosystem = biotic + abiotic. Producers, consumers, decomposers. Standing crop = living biomass; standing state = nutrients. Detritus = dead matter base of decomposer chain.
ProductivityGPP = total photosynthetic fixation. NPP = GPP - R. NPP feeds consumers + decomposers. Secondary productivity = consumers' new biomass. Light, temperature, moisture, nutrients affect it.
DecompositionFragmentation (detritivores), leaching, catabolism (enzymes), humification (humus), mineralisation (inorganic nutrients). Fast: warm, moist, oxygen. Slow: cold, anaerobic, lignin/chitin.
Energy flowEnergy one-way, nutrients recycle. Grazing chain: producer -> herbivore -> carnivore. Detritus chain: dead matter -> detritivore -> predator. 10% law about 10% passes on.
PyramidsNumbers: grassland upright, tree inverted. Biomass: terrestrial upright, aquatic often inverted (fast phytoplankton turnover). Energy: ALWAYS upright.
Food webOverlapping chains = food web. One organism can sit at several levels. Decomposers central but omitted in simple pyramids.
