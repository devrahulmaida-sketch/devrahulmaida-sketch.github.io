Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

MicrobesBacteria, fungi, protozoa, algae; viruses microscopic. Many useful. LAB: lactose to lactic acid. Fermenters, sterile conditions.
FoodsCurd: LAB. Bread: S. cerevisiae CO₂. Idli/dosa fermentation. Swiss cheese holes: Propionibacterium sharmanii CO₂. Roquefort: Penicillium roqueforti.
IndustrialAlcohol: yeast. Penicillin: P. notatum (Fleming, Florey, Chain). Citric: A. niger; acetic: Acetobacter; butyric: Clostridium. Streptokinase, cyclosporin A, statins.
SewagePrimary: screening + settling. Secondary: aerobic flocs. BOD high = polluted. Sludge to anaerobic digester (methanogens).
Biogas + biofertBiogas: dung, methanogens, Methanobacterium, no O₂. Rhizobium, Azotobacter, Azospirillum, Anabaena, Nostoc; mycorrhiza (Glomus) for P and water.
BiocontrolLadybird: aphids. Dragonfly: mosquitoes. Bt: caterpillars. Trichoderma: fungal pathogens. NPV baculovirus: narrow host range.
