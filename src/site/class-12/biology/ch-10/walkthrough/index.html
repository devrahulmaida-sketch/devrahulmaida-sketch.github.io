Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

Bt cottoncry genes from Bacillus thuringiensis. Protoxin inactive; alkaline gut activates; pores in midgut. cryIAc/cryIIAb: cotton bollworm; cryIAb: corn borer. Resistance management needed.
RNAidsRNA silences matching mRNA. Meloidogyne incognita nematode on tobacco. Agrobacterium vector: sense + antisense RNA -> dsRNA. Host-induced gene silencing.
InsulinProinsulin = A + B + C-peptide; mature = A + B. 1983 Eli Lilly: E. coli made A and B separately, then joined. Purify and verify.
Gene therapy + diagnosisADA deficiency (SCID): lymphocytes cultured, ADA gene added, reinfused; cells short-lived. PCR amplifies DNA (RT-PCR for RNA). ELISA = antigen-antibody.
Transgenic animalsForeign gene expressed. Mice disease models. Uses: gene function, disease research, drug/vaccine safety, products. Rosie cow: human alpha-lactalbumin in milk.
EthicsBiopiracy = use of resources/knowledge without permission or fair benefit sharing. Basmati, neem, turmeric. GEAC oversees GE organisms in India.
