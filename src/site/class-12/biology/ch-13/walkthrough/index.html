Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

LevelsGenetic (within species), species (variety + abundance), ecosystem (habitats). Species counts uncertain.
PatternsTropics richer. S = C A^Z; log S = log C + Z log A. Z ~0.1-0.2 regional, steeper continental. Correlation not causation.
Ecosystem roleTilman: diverse plots stabler. Rivet-popper (Ehrlich). Services: pollination, water, soil, cycling, carbon, food.
Evil quartetHabitat loss/fragmentation, overexploitation, alien invasion (Parthenium, Nile perch), co-extinction.
Why conserveNarrow utilitarian (goods), broad utilitarian (services), ethical (intrinsic value, custodians).
In/ex situIn situ: parks, sanctuaries, biosphere reserves, sacred groves. Ex situ: zoo, garden, seed bank, cryo, tissue culture. Hotspots = endemism + severe loss.
