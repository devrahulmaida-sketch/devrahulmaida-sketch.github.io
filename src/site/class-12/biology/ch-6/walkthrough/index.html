Class 12 Biology - Chapter 6 - Evolution

Evolution Ka Visual Walkthrough

8 interactive scenes - origin of life theories, Miller-Urey experiment, evolution ke 5 saboot, Darwin ke finches, industrial melanism, Hardy-Weinberg, human evolution, aur quiz.

1. Life Kaise Shuru Hui? - 4 Theories 🌍

Big Bang ~13.8 billion years ago, Earth ~4.5 billion years purani - shuru me life nahi thi. Life ki origin ke 4 theories: special creation, spontaneous generation (GALAT sabit!), panspermia, aur Oparin-Haldane ka chemosynthesis (sabse accepted). Theory chuno!

2. Miller-Urey Experiment (1953) - Lab Me Primitive Earth ⚡

Miller ne Urey ke saath primitive Earth ka MINI MODEL banaya: CH4, NH3, H2, water vapour (NO free oxygen - reducing atmosphere!) + electric discharge = lightning. 1 week baad flask me AMINO ACIDS mil gaye - chemical evolution ka proof! Steps dekho!

3. Evolution Ke 5 Saboot 🔍

Evolution ke strong evidences: PALEONTOLOGY (fossils rock layers me), HOMOLOGOUS organs (same structure, alag function = divergent), ANALOGOUS organs (alag structure, same function = convergent), VESTIGIAL organs (useless leftovers), EMBRYOLOGY (sab vertebrate embryos early similar). Saboot chuno!

4. Darwin Ke Finches - Adaptive Radiation 🐦

Galapagos islands pe EK common ancestor se alag-alag beak types ke finches bane - har beak us island ke DIET ke hisaab se! Yeh hai ADAPTIVE RADIATION. Australian marsupials (kangaroo, koala) bhi aise hi placental mammals ke parallel bane. Beak chuno!

5. Industrial Melanism - Selection Live! 🦋

England me pollution se pehle LIGHT moths common the (light bark pe camouflage). Pollution se bark DARK hua → light moths birds ko dikhne lage → DARK moths survive karke multiply hue! Pollution kam hua to light wapas. Bark mode chuno, RUN dabao - dekho kaun bachta hai!

6. Hardy-Weinberg Principle - Evolution Ka Null Hypothesis 🧮

Bina evolutionary forces ke population ke ALLELE FREQUENCIES constant rehte hain: p + q = 1 aur p² + 2pq + q² = 1. Frequencies BADLE = evolution ho raha hai! Evolution ke factors: mutation, gene flow, genetic drift (founder effect), natural selection, non-random mating. Example chuno - q² se poora solve!

7. Human Evolution - Ape Se Insaan 🚶

Sequence rat lo: Dryopithecus (~15 mya, ape-like) → Australopithecus (~4 mya, BIPEDAL, brain 500cc, Lucy) → Homo habilis (~2 mya, tools, 650-800cc) → Homo erectus (~1.5 mya, FIRE, 900cc) → Neanderthal (1400cc, dead bury!) → Homo sapiens (art + agriculture). Yaad rakho: yeh branching tree hai, seedhi ladder NAHI!

8. Quiz - Evolution Pakka? ⭐

5 sawaal - Miller-Urey, homologous organs, industrial melanism, Hardy-Weinberg numerical, human evolution sequence. Score 5/5 lao!

Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

Origin of lifeOparin-Haldane: life non-living chemicals se bani - REDUCING atmosphere (CH4, NH3, H2, water vapour; free oxygen NAHI thi). Miller-Urey (1953): ye gases + electric discharge → AMINO ACIDS. First self-replicating molecules (shayad RNA world) → first cells ~4 billion years ago.
EvidencesHomologous = same bone pattern, alag function (human hand, whale flipper, bat wing) → DIVERGENT = common ancestor. Analogous = alag structure, same function (butterfly vs bird wing) → CONVERGENT. Vestigial: appendix, wisdom teeth. Fossils: purane layers simple, naye complex (horse: Eohippus → Equus). Embryos: sab vertebrates early similar (gill slits).
Adaptive radiationEK common ancestor se alag habitats ke liye species - Darwin ke finches (Galapagos, beaks diet ke hisaab), Australian marsupials. Marsupial wolf vs placental wolf = convergent evolution ka best example - bina related similar!
Natural selectionDarwin: variation + overproduction + struggle + survival of the fittest → favorable traits pass on. Fitness = reproductive success. Industrial melanism: pollution → dark bark → dark moths survive. Antibiotic resistance = aaj bhi chalta selection. Lamarck (use/disuse + acquired traits) GALAT, Darwin SAHI.
Hardy-Weinbergp + q = 1, p² + 2pq + q² = 1. p² = homozygous dominant, 2pq = heterozygous, q² = homozygous recessive. Frequencies constant = NO evolution (null hypothesis). Change ke factors: mutation, gene flow/migration, genetic drift (founder effect - chhote group ka naya settlement), natural selection, non-random mating. Numerical: q² diya → q nikalo → 2pq!
Human evolutionDryopithecus (~15 mya) → Australopithecus (~4 mya, bipedal, 500cc, Lucy) → Homo habilis (~2 mya, tools, 650-800cc) → Homo erectus (~1.5 mya, fire, 900cc, Java/Peking man) → Neanderthal (~100-40k years, 1400cc, dead bury) → Homo sapiens (~3 lakh years, Africa origin, Bhimbetka cave art, agriculture ~10k years). Branching tree, ladder NAHI!
