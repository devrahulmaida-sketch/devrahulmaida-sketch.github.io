Formula / Fact Recap - Ek Nazar Mein

PopulationReproductive health = physical, emotional, social well-being (WHO). India first with family planning, 1951. ~1 billion by 2000. RCH programmes.
Natural + barrierIdeal contraceptive traits. Periodic abstinence (day 10-17), withdrawal, lactational amenorrhea. Condoms, diaphragms, caps, vaults. Condom also protects from STDs.
IUDs + hormonalLippes loop; CuT, Cu7, Multiload 375; Progestasert, LNG-20. Saheli = weekly non-steroidal. Emergency: pill ~72 h, Cu IUD ~5 days.
Surgical + MTPVasectomy (vas deferens), tubectomy (fallopian tube): permanent, hormones normal. MTP Act 1971. Sex-determination illegal (PCPNDT).
STDsBacterial + protozoan curable; viral (HIV, Hep-B, herpes) not. Complications: PID, infertility, cervical cancer. Prevention: condoms, screening, sterile needles.
ARTIVF (Louise Brown 1978). ZIFT up to 8 blastomeres to tube; IUT more than 8 to uterus; GIFT ovum to tube; ICSI sperm into egg; AI; surrogacy.
