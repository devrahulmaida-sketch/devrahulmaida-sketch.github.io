Molecular Basis of Inheritance Ka Visual Walkthrough

7 interactive scenes - base-pairing lab (Chargaff rule), Hershey-Chase experiment bench, DNA packaging zoom, replication fork (Okazaki fragments), transcription + splicing, translation codon-decode game, lac operon switch + HGP/fingerprinting facts, aur quiz.

1. Base-Pairing Lab - A=T, G≡C Ka Rule ⭐⭐

Watson-Crick double helix (1953): 2 strands ANTIPARALLEL (5'→3' aur 3'→5'), bases andar, sugar-phosphate backbone bahar. Rule: A=T (2 H-bonds), G≡C (3 H-bonds) - Chargaff ka ratio hamesha 1 (purine:pyrimidine bhi 1:1). Base chuno, sahi partner lagao!

2. Hershey-Chase Bench - DNA Hi Genetic Material ⭐⭐

Griffith (1928) ne "transforming principle" bola, Avery ne DNA ki taraf ishara kiya, aur Hershey-Chase ne radioactive S35 (protein tag) aur P32 (DNA tag) se PROVE kiya - phage ka DNA bacteria ke ANDAR gaya, protein BAHAR raha. Isotope label chuno, result dekho!

3. DNA Packaging Zoom - 2.2 Metre Nucleus Me! ⭐

Human diploid cell ka DNA ~2.2 m lamba - nucleus me fit KAISE? Positively charged histones (H1, H2A, H2B, H3, H4) pe DNA lipatta hai → nucleosome (8 histones + ~200 bp) → "beads on string" → 30 nm fiber → loops → chromosome. Zoom level chuno!

4. Replication Fork - Leading Vs Lagging ⭐⭐

Replication SEMICONSERVATIVE hai (Meselson-Stahl ne N15 + E. coli se prove kiya): har naye DNA me 1 purani + 1 nayi strand. Fork pe: helicase (unwind), topoisomerase (tension release), primase (RNA primer), DNA pol III (SIRF 5'→3' banata), ligase (Okazaki fragments jodta). Step by step dekho!

5. Transcription + RNA Processing ⭐⭐

Template strand (3'→5') se RNA polymerase complementary RNA banata hai (T ki jagah U). Bacteria: EK hi RNA pol sab banata, coding continuous. Eukaryotes: Pol I (rRNA), Pol II (hnRNA→mRNA), Pol III (tRNA) + hnRNA processing: splicing (introns hatao), 5' capping, 3' poly-A tail. View chuno!

6. Translation + Codon Decode Game ⭐⭐

Genetic code TRIPLET hai: 64 codons - 61 amino acids ke liye, 3 STOP (UAA, UAG, UGA). AUG = start codon + methionine. Code degenerate (ek aa ke kai codons), unambiguous, universal, commaless; wobble 3rd base pe. tRNA ka anticodon mRNA codon se pair karta; ribosome A/P/E sites. Codon chuno, amino acid batao!

7. Lac Operon Switch + HGP / Fingerprinting ⭐⭐

Jacob-Monod lac operon (E. coli): lacZ (β-galactosidase - lactose todta), lacY (permease - andar laata), lacA (transacetylase); lacI repressor banata, operator pe baithta = OFF. LACTOSE (inducer) aaya → repressor hata = ON (negative inducible control). HGP: 30,000 genes, 99.9% same; fingerprinting: Jeffreys (1985), VNTR basis. Toggle karo!

8. Quiz - Molecular Basis Pakka? ⭐

5 sawaal - base pairing, Hershey-Chase, replication enzymes, RNA processing, lac operon. Score 5/5 lao!

Replication forkSemiconservative (Meselson-Stahl, N15). Helicase unwind, topoisomerase tension release, primase RNA primer, DNA pol III SIRF 5'→3' banata, ligase Okazaki jodta. Leading continuous; lagging discontinuous (Okazaki).

Lac operon + HGPlacZ/Y/A structural; lacI repressor → operator = OFF; lactose (inducer) aaya = ON (negative inducible). HGP: 30,000 genes, 99.9% same, ~2% coding. Fingerprinting: Jeffreys (1985), VNTR, Southern blot.
