Notes language:

1️⃣ DNA Ka Structure — Double Helix Ka Khel ⭐

  • Building block: nucleotide = sugar (deoxyribose) + phosphate + nitrogen base (A, T, G, C). Nucleotides phosphodiester bonds se judte hain — polynucleotide chain.
  • Watson-Crick model (1953) ⭐: double helix — 2 strands antiparallel (5'→3' aur 3'→5'), bases andar, sugar-phosphate backbone bahar.
  • Base pairing rule ⭐⭐: A=T (2 H-bonds), G≡C (3 H-bonds) — Chargaff's rule: A=T aur G=C ratio hamesha 1. Isliye purine:pyrimidine bhi 1:1.
  • Dimensions: ek turn = 10 base pairs = 3.4 nm; har base pair 0.34 nm. Human DNA ~2.2 metres lamba (diploid cell me) — nucleus me fit kaise hota hai, dekho aage!
  • DNA vs RNA: DNA me deoxyribose + thymine; RNA me ribose + uracil. DNA double-stranded (zyada stable) — isliye genetic material DNA better.
  • Griffith (1928), Avery-Hershey-Chase ⭐: Hershey-Chase ne radioactive S³⁵ (protein) aur P³² (DNA) se prove kiya — DNA hi genetic material hai, protein nahi!

🎯 Hershey-Chase: P³² (DNA) bacteria ke andar gaya, S³⁵ (protein) bahar raha — DNA = genetic material. Direct question!

2️⃣ DNA Packaging aur Replication ⭐⭐

  • Packaging: DNA histone proteins (H1, H2A, H2B, H3, H4 — positively charged) ke around lipatta hai → nucleosome (8 histones + ~200 bp DNA) → "beads on string" → 30 nm fiber → loops → chromosome.
  • Replication = semiconservative ⭐⭐: har nayi DNA me ek purani strand + ek nayi strand. Meselson-Stahl ne prove kiya (N¹⁵ heavy nitrogen + E. coli) — famous experiment!
  • Replication fork pe ⭐: helicase (unwind), topoisomerase (tension release), primase (RNA primer), DNA polymerase III (main enzyme — 5'→3' direction me hi banata hai), ligase (joins Okazaki fragments).
  • Leading strand: continuous (5'→3' fork ki taraf). Lagging strand: discontinuous — Okazaki fragments me banta hai, ligase jodta hai.
  • Origin of replication: jahan se replication shuru hota hai — E. coli me ek (oriC), eukaryotes me kai.

💡 Trick: DNA polymerase sirf 5'→3' banata hai — isliye lagging strand tukdo me banti hai. Ye constraint hi Okazaki fragments ka reason!

3️⃣ Transcription — DNA Se RNA ⭐⭐

  • Transcription: DNA ki ek strand se RNA banana — RNA polymerase enzyme se. Template strand (3'→5') se complementary RNA banta hai (coding strand jaisa, bas T ki jagah U).
  • Steps: initiation (promoter pe bind) → elongation → termination (terminator sequence).
  • Bacteria me: ek hi RNA polymerase sab RNAs banata hai (mRNA, tRNA, rRNA). Coding region continuous hoti hai.
  • Eukaryotes me ⭐: teen polymerases — RNA Pol I (rRNA), Pol II (hnRNA → mRNA), Pol III (tRNA, 5S rRNA, snRNA).
  • hnRNA processing ⭐⭐: splicing (introns hatana, exons jodna — snRNPs se), 5' capping (methyl guanosine triphosphate), 3' poly-A tail (200-300 adenine) — tab mature mRNA banta hai!
  • Split genes: eukaryotic genes me exons (coding) + introns (non-coding) — bacteria me nahi.

🎯 Capping + tailing + splicing = hnRNA → mRNA ka makeover. Introns bacteria me nahi hote — ye difference exam favourite hai!

4️⃣ Genetic Code aur Translation ⭐⭐

  • Genetic code ⭐: triplet codons (3 bases = 1 amino acid), 64 codons — 61 amino acids ke liye, 3 stop codons (UAA, UAG, UGA). AUG = start codon (methionine bhi code karta hai).
  • Code ke features ⭐: degenerate (ek amino acid ke kai codons), unambiguous (ek codon sirf ek amino acid), universal (lagbhag sab organisms me same), commaless (continuous padha jaata hai).
  • tRNA ⭐: adapter molecule — anticodon loop (mRNA codon se pair) + amino acid acceptor end (3' CCA). Clover-leaf structure. Wobble hypothesis: 3rd base pe flexibility — isliye kam tRNAs se kaam chalta hai.
  • Translation steps ⭐: amino acid activation (aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase) → initiation (ribosome + mRNA + Met-tRNA) → elongation (codon by codon, peptide bonds) → termination (stop codon pe release factor).
  • Ribosome = protein factory: A site (aminoacyl), P site (peptidyl), E site (exit) — 23S rRNA hi peptidyl transferase hai (ribozyme!).

💡 AUG = start + methionine; UAA/UAG/UGA = stop (koi amino acid nahi). Ye do facts 80% questions cover karte hain!

5️⃣ Gene Regulation — Lac Operon ⭐⭐

  • Regulation kyun: har protein har waqt nahi chahiye — energy bachani hai. Prokaryotes me operon model se control.
  • Lac operon (Jacob-Monod) ⭐⭐: E. coli ka lactose digestion system — 3 structural genes: lacZ (β-galactosidase — lactose todta hai), lacY (permease — lactose andar laata hai), lacA (transacetylase).
  • Regulatory parts ⭐: promoter (RNA pol bind), operator (repressor bind), regulator gene lacI (repressor protein banata hai).
  • Lactose nahi: repressor operator pe baitha → transcription OFF. Lactose hai: lactose (inducer/allolactose) repressor se judta hai → repressor hat → transcription ON! ⭐
  • Negative regulation: lac operon ka repressor-based control negative hai — inducer aane pe ON hota hai (inducible operon).
  • Eukaryotes me regulation complex hai — transcription, processing, translation sab levels pe.

🎯 Simple yaad rakho: lactose = ON switch ka key. No lactose → repressor ON operator → genes OFF. Lactose aaya → repressor gayab → genes ON!

6️⃣ HGP aur DNA Fingerprinting ⭐

  • Human Genome Project (1990–2003) ⭐: 13 saal, $3 billion, ~3 × 10⁹ bp sequence. Goals: saare genes identify + map karna, sab chromosomes sequence karna.
  • Key findings ⭐: ~30,000 genes (pehle socha 80,000–140,000!), 99.9% DNA sab humans me same, sirf ~2% DNA protein-coding sequence hai (98% "junk" — ab pata regulatory roles hain), chromosome 1 me sabse zyada genes (2968), Y me sabse kam (231).
  • Methods: ESTs (expressed sequence tags) + sequence annotation. Repetitive DNA bhi sequence kiya.
  • DNA fingerprinting ⭐⭐: Alec Jeffreys ne develop kiya (1985). Basis: VNTRs (variable number tandem repeats) — har insaan me alag count!
  • Steps ⭐: DNA isolate → restriction enzyme se kaato → gel electrophoresis se separate → Southern blotting → probe hybridization → autoradiography → band pattern compare.
  • Uses: paternity disputes, crime investigation, identifying dead bodies. (Aaj kal PCR-based STR analysis use hota hai.)

🎯 VNTR = fingerprinting ka base; Jeffreys = father of DNA fingerprinting. HGP: 30,000 genes, 99.9% same — ye numbers likh lo!

1️⃣ DNA की संरचना — द्विकुंडली का खेल ⭐

  • बिल्डिंग ब्लॉक: न्यूक्लियोटाइड = शर्करा (डीऑक्सीराइबोज) + फॉस्फेट + नाइट्रोजन क्षार (A, T, G, C)। न्यूक्लियोटाइड फॉस्फोडाइएस्टर बंधों से जुड़ते हैं।
  • वॉटसन-क्रिक मॉडल (1953) ⭐: द्विकुंडली — 2 रज्जुक प्रतिसमांतर (5'→3' और 3'→5'), क्षार अंदर, शर्करा-फॉस्फेट बाहर।
  • क्षार युग्मन नियम ⭐⭐: A=T (2 H-बंध), G≡C (3 H-बंध) — चारगाफ नियम: A=T और G=C अनुपात सदैव 1।
  • आयाम: एक घुमाव = 10 क्षार युग्म = 3.4 nm। मानव DNA ~2.2 मीटर लंबा!
  • DNA बनाम RNA: DNA में डीऑक्सीराइबोज + थायमिन; RNA में राइबोज + यूरेसिल।
  • हर्शी-चेस ⭐: S³⁵ (प्रोटीन) और P³² (DNA) से सिद्ध किया — DNA ही आनुवंशिक पदार्थ है!

🎯 हर्शी-चेस: P³² जीवाणु के अंदर गया, S³⁵ बाहर रहा — DNA = आनुवंशिक पदार्थ!

2️⃣ DNA संकुलन और प्रतिकृति ⭐⭐

  • संकुलन: DNA हिस्टोन प्रोटीन (धनावेशित) के चारों ओर लिपटता है → न्यूक्लियोसोम (8 हिस्टोन + ~200 bp DNA) → "डोरी पर मोती" → गुणसूत्र।
  • प्रतिकृति = अर्धसंरक्षी ⭐⭐: प्रत्येक नए DNA में एक पुराना + एक नया रज्जुक। मेसेलसन-स्टाहल ने सिद्ध किया (N¹⁵ + E. coli)।
  • प्रतिकृति कांटे पर ⭐: हेलिकेज (खोलता), प्राइमेज (RNA प्राइमर), DNA पॉलीमरेज III (मुख्य एंजाइम — केवल 5'→3'), लाइगेज (ओकाजाकी खंड जोड़ता)।
  • अग्रणी रज्जुक: सतत। पश्चगामी रज्जुक: असतत — ओकाजाकी खंडों में।
  • प्रतिकृति मूल बिंदु: जहां से प्रतिकृति शुरू — E. coli में एक, यूकैरियोट में अनेक।

💡 DNA पॉलीमरेज केवल 5'→3' बनाता है — इसीलिए पश्चगामी रज्जुक टुकड़ों में बनता है!

3️⃣ प्रतिलेखन — DNA से RNA ⭐⭐

  • प्रतिलेखन: DNA के एक रज्जुक से RNA बनाना — RNA पॉलीमरेज से। टेम्पलेट रज्जुक (3'→5') से पूरक RNA बनता है (T की जगह U)।
  • चरण: प्रारंभ (प्रमोटर पर बंधन) → दीर्घीकरण → समाप्ति।
  • जीवाणु में: एक ही RNA पॉलीमरेज सभी RNA बनाता है।
  • यूकैरियोट में ⭐: तीन पॉलीमरेज — Pol I (rRNA), Pol II (hnRNA → mRNA), Pol III (tRNA)।
  • hnRNA प्रसंस्करण ⭐⭐: स्प्लाइसिंग (इंट्रॉन हटाना, एक्सॉन जोड़ना), 5' कैपिंग, 3' पॉली-A पूंछ — तब परिपक्व mRNA बनता है!
  • विखंडित जीन: यूकैरियोटिक जीन में एक्सॉन (कूटबद्ध) + इंट्रॉन (गैर-कूटबद्ध)।

🎯 कैपिंग + पूंछ + स्प्लाइसिंग = hnRNA → mRNA का रूपांतरण। इंट्रॉन जीवाणु में नहीं!

4️⃣ आनुवंशिक कूट और अनुवादन ⭐⭐

  • आनुवंशिक कूट ⭐: त्रिक कोडोन (3 क्षार = 1 अमीनो अम्ल), 64 कोडोन — 61 अमीनो अम्ल के लिए, 3 समाप्ति कोडोन (UAA, UAG, UGA)। AUG = प्रारंभ कोडोन।
  • कूट के गुण ⭐: अपभ्रष्ट (एक अमीनो अम्ल के कई कोडोन), अद्वितीय (एक कोडोन केवल एक अमीनो अम्ल), सार्वभौमिक, अविच्छिन्न।
  • tRNA ⭐: एडाप्टर — प्रतिकोडोन लूप + अमीनो अम्ल स्वीकार सिरा (3' CCA)। लौंगपत्र संरचना।
  • अनुवादन चरण ⭐: अमीनो अम्ल सक्रियण → प्रारंभ (राइबोसोम + mRNA + Met-tRNA) → दीर्घीकरण → समाप्ति।
  • राइबोसोम = प्रोटीन कारखाना: A स्थल, P स्थल, E स्थल — 23S rRNA ही पेप्टिडिल ट्रांसफरेज है (राइबोजाइम!)।

💡 AUG = प्रारंभ + मिथियोनीन; UAA/UAG/UGA = समाप्ति। ये दो तथ्य 80% प्रश्न कवर करते हैं!

5️⃣ जीन नियमन — लैक ऑपेरॉन ⭐⭐

  • नियमन क्यों: प्रत्येक प्रोटीन हर समय नहीं चाहिए — ऊर्जा बचानी है।
  • लैक ऑपेरॉन (जैकब-मोनो) ⭐⭐: E. coli का लैक्टोज पाचन तंत्र — 3 संरचनात्मक जीन: lacZ (β-गैलेक्टोसिडेज), lacY (पर्मिएज), lacA (ट्रांसएसिटिलेज)।
  • नियामक भाग ⭐: प्रमोटर (RNA पॉल बंधन), ऑपरेटर (दमनकारी बंधन), नियामक जीन lacI (दमनकारी प्रोटीन)।
  • लैक्टोज नहीं: दमनकारी ऑपरेटर पर → प्रतिलेखन बंद। लैक्टोज है: लैक्टोज (प्रेरक) दमनकारी से जुड़ता → दमनकारी हटता → प्रतिलेखन चालू! ⭐
  • ऋणात्मक नियमन: लैक ऑपेरॉन का दमनकारी-आधारित नियंत्रण ऋणात्मक है — प्रेरणीय ऑपेरॉन।
  • यूकैरियोट में नियमन जटिल — सभी स्तरों पर।

🎯 सरल याद रखें: लैक्टोज = चालू स्विच की कुंजी। लैक्टोज नहीं → दमनकारी ऑपरेटर पर → जीन बंद!

6️⃣ HGP और DNA फिंगरप्रिंटिंग ⭐

  • मानव जीनोम परियोजना (1990–2003) ⭐: 13 वर्ष, $3 अरब, ~3 × 10⁹ bp अनुक्रम। लक्ष्य: सभी जीन पहचानना + मानचित्रित करना।
  • प्रमुख निष्कर्ष ⭐: ~30,000 जीन, 99.9% DNA सभी मानवों में समान, केवल ~2% DNA प्रोटीन-कूटन अनुक्रम है, गुणसूत्र 1 में सर्वाधिक जीन (2968), Y में न्यूनतम (231)।
  • DNA फिंगरप्रिंटिंग ⭐⭐: एलेक जेफ्रीज ने विकसित (1985)। आधार: VNTR (परिवर्ती संख्या युग्म पुनरावृत्तियां) — प्रत्येक व्यक्ति में भिन्न!
  • चरण ⭐: DNA पृथक्करण → प्रतिबंध एंजाइम से कटाई → जेल इलेक्ट्रोफोरेसिस → साउदर्न ब्लॉटिंग → प्रोब संकरण → स्वतःविकिरणचित्रण।
  • उपयोग: पैतृत्व विवाद, अपराध जांच, मृत शरीरों की पहचान।

🎯 VNTR = फिंगरप्रिंटिंग का आधार; जेफ्रीज = जनक। HGP: 30,000 जीन, 99.9% समान!

1️⃣ DNA Structure — The Double Helix Game ⭐

  • Building block: a nucleotide = sugar (deoxyribose) + phosphate + nitrogen base (A, T, G, C). Nucleotides join by phosphodiester bonds into a polynucleotide chain.
  • Watson-Crick model (1953) ⭐: a double helix — 2 antiparallel strands (5'→3' and 3'→5'), bases inside, sugar-phosphate backbone outside.
  • Base pairing rule ⭐⭐: A=T (2 H-bonds), G≡C (3 H-bonds) — Chargaff's rule: the A=T and G=C ratios are always 1. Purine:pyrimidine is 1:1 too.
  • Dimensions: one turn = 10 base pairs = 3.4 nm; each base pair 0.34 nm. Human DNA is ~2.2 metres long in a diploid cell — read on to see how it fits inside the nucleus!
  • DNA vs RNA: DNA has deoxyribose + thymine; RNA has ribose + uracil. DNA is double-stranded and more stable — that is why it makes the better genetic material.
  • Griffith (1928), Avery, Hershey-Chase ⭐: Hershey-Chase used radioactive S³⁵ (protein) and P³² (DNA) to prove DNA is the genetic material, not protein!

🎯 Hershey-Chase: P³² (DNA) entered the bacteria, S³⁵ (protein) stayed out — DNA = genetic material. A direct question!

2️⃣ DNA Packaging and Replication ⭐⭐

  • Packaging: DNA wraps around histone proteins (H1, H2A, H2B, H3, H4 — positively charged) → the nucleosome (8 histones + ~200 bp DNA) → "beads on a string" → 30 nm fiber → loops → chromosome.
  • Replication is semiconservative ⭐⭐: each new DNA keeps one old strand + one new strand. Meselson and Stahl proved it (N¹⁵ heavy nitrogen + E. coli) — a famous experiment!
  • At the replication fork ⭐: helicase (unwinds), topoisomerase (releases tension), primase (lays RNA primer), DNA polymerase III (the main enzyme — builds only 5'→3'), ligase (joins Okazaki fragments).
  • Leading strand: continuous (5'→3' toward the fork). Lagging strand: discontinuous — built as Okazaki fragments, joined by ligase.
  • Origin of replication: where replication starts — one in E. coli (oriC), many in eukaryotes.

💡 Trick: DNA polymerase builds only 5'→3' — that constraint alone explains why the lagging strand is made in pieces!

3️⃣ Transcription — DNA to RNA ⭐⭐

  • Transcription: making RNA from one DNA strand — by RNA polymerase. The template strand (3'→5') is read to build a complementary RNA (like the coding strand, but U instead of T).
  • Steps: initiation (binding at the promoter) → elongation → termination (at the terminator sequence).
  • In bacteria: a single RNA polymerase makes all RNAs (mRNA, tRNA, rRNA). The coding region is continuous.
  • In eukaryotes ⭐: three polymerases — RNA Pol I (rRNA), Pol II (hnRNA → mRNA), Pol III (tRNA, 5S rRNA, snRNA).
  • hnRNA processing ⭐⭐: splicing (introns removed, exons joined — by snRNPs), 5' capping (methyl guanosine triphosphate), 3' poly-A tail (200-300 adenines) — only then is the mRNA mature!
  • Split genes: eukaryotic genes have exons (coding) + introns (non-coding) — bacteria do not.

🎯 Capping + tailing + splicing = the hnRNA → mRNA makeover. No introns in bacteria — an exam favourite difference!

4️⃣ The Genetic Code and Translation ⭐⭐

  • The genetic code ⭐: triplet codons (3 bases = 1 amino acid), 64 codons — 61 code for amino acids, 3 are stop codons (UAA, UAG, UGA). AUG = the start codon (also codes methionine).
  • Features of the code ⭐: degenerate (several codons per amino acid), unambiguous (one codon = one amino acid), universal (nearly the same in all organisms), commaless (read continuously).
  • tRNA ⭐: the adapter molecule — an anticodon loop (pairs with the mRNA codon) + an amino acid acceptor end (3' CCA). Clover-leaf structure. Wobble hypothesis: flexibility at the 3rd base — that is why fewer tRNAs suffice.
  • Translation steps ⭐: amino acid activation (aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase) → initiation (ribosome + mRNA + Met-tRNA) → elongation (codon by codon, peptide bonds) → termination (release factor at a stop codon).
  • The ribosome = the protein factory: A site (aminoacyl), P site (peptidyl), E site (exit) — the 23S rRNA itself acts as peptidyl transferase (a ribozyme!).

💡 AUG = start + methionine; UAA/UAG/UGA = stop (no amino acid). These two facts cover 80% of the questions!

5️⃣ Gene Regulation — The Lac Operon ⭐⭐

  • Why regulate: not every protein is needed all the time — energy must be saved. Prokaryotes control genes with the operon model.
  • The lac operon (Jacob-Monod) ⭐⭐: E. coli's lactose digestion system — 3 structural genes: lacZ (β-galactosidase — breaks lactose), lacY (permease — brings lactose in), lacA (transacetylase).
  • Regulatory parts ⭐: the promoter (RNA pol binds), the operator (repressor binds), the regulator gene lacI (makes the repressor protein).
  • No lactose: the repressor sits on the operator → transcription OFF. Lactose present: lactose (inducer/allolactose) binds the repressor → repressor leaves → transcription ON! ⭐
  • Negative regulation: the lac operon's repressor-based control is negative — it switches ON in the presence of an inducer (an inducible operon).
  • In eukaryotes, regulation is complex — at transcription, processing and translation levels.

🎯 Simply remember: lactose is the key to the ON switch. No lactose → repressor on operator → genes OFF. Lactose arrives → repressor gone → genes ON!

6️⃣ HGP and DNA Fingerprinting ⭐

  • Human Genome Project (1990–2003) ⭐: 13 years, $3 billion, ~3 × 10⁹ bp sequenced. Goals: identify and map all genes, sequence all chromosomes.
  • Key findings ⭐: ~30,000 genes (earlier estimates were 80,000–140,000!), 99.9% of DNA is the same in all humans, only ~2% of DNA is protein-coding sequence (the rest includes regulatory and repetitive sequences; not all is functionless), chromosome 1 has the most genes (2968), Y the fewest (231).
  • Methods: ESTs (expressed sequence tags) + sequence annotation. Repetitive DNA was sequenced too.
  • DNA fingerprinting ⭐⭐: developed by Alec Jeffreys (1985). Basis: VNTRs (variable number tandem repeats) — the count differs in every person!
  • Steps ⭐: isolate DNA → cut with restriction enzymes → separate by gel electrophoresis → Southern blotting → probe hybridization → autoradiography → compare band patterns.
  • Uses: paternity disputes, crime investigation, identifying dead bodies. (Today PCR-based STR analysis is used.)

NEET Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 DNA me A aur T ke beech kitne hydrogen bonds hote hain? G aur C me?
Answer dekho

A=T me 2, G≡C me 3 hydrogen bonds — isliye G-C rich DNA zyada stable.

Q2 Hershey-Chase experiment ne kya prove kiya?
Answer dekho

DNA hi genetic material hai — P³² (DNA) bacteria ke andar gaya, S³⁵ (protein) bahar raha.

Q3 Semiconservative replication ka matlab kya hai?
Answer dekho

Har nayi DNA me ek purani (parental) strand + ek nayi strand hoti hai — Meselson-Stahl ne N¹⁵ se prove kiya.

Q4 Okazaki fragments kahan aur kyun bante hain?
Answer dekho

Lagging strand pe — kyunki DNA polymerase sirf 5'→3' direction me kaam karta hai, isliye discontinuous synthesis hoti hai.

Q5 hnRNA ko mature mRNA banne ke liye kaun se changes chahiye?
Answer dekho

Splicing (introns hatana), 5' capping, aur 3' poly-A tailing.

Q6 Start aur stop codons batao.
Answer dekho

AUG = start (methionine); UAA, UAG, UGA = stop codons.

Q7 Lac operon kab ON hota hai?
Answer dekho

Jab lactose (inducer) present ho — ye repressor ko operator se hata deta hai, transcription shuru.

Q8 Lac operon ke 3 structural genes aur unke products batao.
Answer dekho

lacZ (β-galactosidase), lacY (permease), lacA (transacetylase).

Q9 Human Genome Project ke 2 main findings batao.
Answer dekho

~30,000 genes (expected se bahut kam) aur 99.9% DNA sab humans me same.

Q10 DNA fingerprinting ka basis kya hai?
Answer dekho

VNTRs (variable number tandem repeats) — har insaan me inki count alag hoti hai; Alec Jeffreys ne develop kiya.
