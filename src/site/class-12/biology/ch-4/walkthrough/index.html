Principles of Inheritance Ka Visual Walkthrough

1. Mendel Ke 7 Characters - Pea Plant Kyun? ⭐

Gregor Mendel (1856-1863) ne pea plant (Pisum sativum) chuna: short life cycle, clearly alag traits, self-pollination se true-breeding lines easy, manual cross-pollination possible. 7 characters, 14 true-breeding lines - HAR character ke 2 contrasting forms. Character chuno, dono forms dekho!

2. Monohybrid Cross + Test Cross - 3:1 Ka Raaz ⭐⭐

Ek character (height): TT (tall) x tt (dwarf) → F1 sab Tt tall → F1 self → F2: phenotypic 3:1, genotypic 1:2:1. Law of Segregation: gamete banne pe alleles ALAG - har gamete sirf ek allele. Test cross (F1 x tt) → 1:1 - hidden genotype pakadne ka tool! Step chalo!

3. Dihybrid Cross - 9:3:3:1 Grid ⭐⭐

Do characters saath: RRYY (round yellow) x rryy (wrinkled green) → F1 RrYy → F2: 9:3:3:1. Law of Independent Assortment: ek character ke alleles dusre se INDEPENDENTLY sort hote hain. Recombinants (round-green, wrinkled-yellow) = variation ka source! (3:1) x (3:1) = 9:3:3:1. Category chuno, boxes count karo!

4. Incomplete Dominance vs Codominance ⭐⭐

Incomplete dominance: snapdragon (dog flower) red RR x white rr → F1 PINK (Rr) - blend! F2: 1 red : 2 pink : 1 white (phenotypic = genotypic). Codominance: ABO blood - IA aur IB DONO full express (AB group) - blend nahi, dono saath! Dono Mendel ke laws ke exceptions nahi, extensions hain. Toggle karo!

5. ABO Blood Group - Multiple Alleles ⭐⭐

3 alleles: IA, IB, i (lekin ek insaan ke paas sirf 2). IA = IB codominant; dono i se dominant. IAIA/IAi = A, IBIB/IBi = B, IAIB = AB, ii = O. O-negative ko universal donor aur AB-positive ko universal recipient commonly bola jaata hai (crossmatch zaroori!). Dono alleles chuno, blood group banao!

6. Sex Determination - Kaun Decide Karta Hai? ⭐⭐

XX-XY (humans, Drosophila): male XY heterogametic - FATHER decide karta hai (X ya Y sperm; mother hamesha X). ZW-ZZ (birds): female ZW heterogametic - FEMALE decide karti hai! XO (grasshopper): male sirf ek X. Honeybee: fertilized → female (diploid), unfertilized → male (haploid) - haplodiploidy! System chuno!

7. Genetic Disorders - Pedigree Se Numbers Tak ⭐⭐

Mutation = DNA me sudden change (point ya frameshift). Pedigree analysis se pattern track karte hain. Autosomal recessive: sickle cell (6th codon GAG→GUG, Glu→Val!), PKU, cystic fibrosis, thalassemia. Dominant: Huntington's (50% chance). X-linked recessive: haemophilia, colour blindness (males zyada - sirf ek X!). Chromosomal: Down (trisomy 21 = 47), Klinefelter (XXY), Turner (XO = 45). Category chuno!
