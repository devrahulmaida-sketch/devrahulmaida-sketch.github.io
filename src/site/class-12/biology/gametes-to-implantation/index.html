Human reproduction: gametes, cycle, implantation

Start with one idea: both gametes come from meiosis, but the two processes run on different schedules. Then read the menstrual cycle as a chain of causes, and follow the zygote from fertilisation to implantation.

Learn in this order

Gametogenesis: same job, different schedule

Spermatogenesis and oogenesis both make haploid gametes from diploid germ cells through two meiotic divisions. The schedule and the division style differ completely.

Spermatogenesis starts at puberty and runs continuously: one primary spermatocyte gives four equal haploid spermatids, which then change shape into sperms.

Oogenesis starts before birth, pauses in prophase I, and each division is unequal: one large secondary oocyte keeps the cytoplasm and a tiny polar body takes the spare chromosomes.

Our worked example

How many chromosomes does a human spermatid carry?

Three steps:

  1. A primary spermatocyte is diploid, with 46 chromosomes.
  2. Meiosis I halves the set, so each secondary spermatocyte has 23.
  3. Meiosis II separates chromatids, not pairs. Answer: 23 chromosomes.

The menstrual cycle as linked events

The menstrual cycle repeats at an average interval of about 28 to 29 days. Read it as a chain of causes, not as fixed calendar dates.

In the follicular phase, rising FSH and LH grow a follicle, and the follicle's estrogens rebuild the endometrium. Near the middle of the cycle, about day 14, the LH surge triggers ovulation.

In the luteal phase the ruptured follicle becomes the corpus luteum, whose progesterone maintains the endometrium. Without fertilisation the corpus luteum degenerates, progesterone falls, and the lining breaks down: the next menstruation.

Our worked example

No fertilisation happened this cycle. Why does menstruation follow?

Three steps:

  1. Without fertilisation, the corpus luteum degenerates.
  2. Progesterone from the corpus luteum was maintaining the endometrium.
  3. When progesterone falls, the lining breaks down. Answer: menstruation starts.

Fertilisation to implantation

Fertilisation happens in the ampullary region of the fallopian tube. A sperm contacts the zona pellucida, which then blocks every other sperm, and fusion restores the diploid set in the zygote.

Cleavage begins as the zygote travels: mitotic divisions make 2, 4, 8, then 16 blastomeres without growth, and the 8 to 16 cell solid ball is the morula.

The morula becomes a blastocyst with an outer trophoblast and an inner cell mass. The trophoblast attaches to the endometrium and embeds: that attachment is implantation.

Our worked example

A zygote completes four cleavage divisions. How many cells?

Three steps:

  1. Cleavage is mitosis without growth, so each division doubles the cells.
  2. After four doublings: 1 to 2 to 4 to 8 to 16.
  3. Answer: 16 cells, a morula.

Our checks

Q1. Why does one primary oocyte yield only one ovum?

See answer

Three steps:

  1. Each meiotic division in oogenesis is unequal.
  2. The large cell keeps the cytoplasm; the small polar bodies take spare chromosomes.
  3. Answer: one large oocyte survives; the polar bodies do not become gametes.

Q2. Which hormone surge triggers ovulation?

See answer

Three steps:

  1. FSH and LH both rise through the follicular phase.
  2. Near day 14, LH rises fastest and highest.
  3. Answer: the LH surge triggers ovulation.

Q3. Where does fertilisation normally occur?

See answer

Three steps:

  1. Sperm and ovum must meet in the same place at the same time.
  2. NCERT places that meeting in the ampullary region of the fallopian tube.
  3. Answer: in the ampulla of the fallopian tube, not the uterus.

Q4. Cleavage makes many cells. Does the embryo grow bigger yet?

See answer

Three steps:

  1. Cleavage divisions are rapid mitoses with no growth phase.
  2. The 16-cell morula fits inside the original zona pellucida.
  3. Answer: no; cells multiply but total size stays about the same.

युग्मकजनन: काम एक, समय-सारणी अलग

शुक्रजनन और अंडजनन दोनों द्विगुणित जर्म कोशिकाओं से दो अर्धसूत्री विभाजनों द्वारा अगुणित युग्मक बनाते हैं। समय-सारणी और विभाजन की शैली बिल्कुल अलग है।

आर्तव चक्र: जुड़ी हुई घटनाएं

आर्तव चक्र औसतन लगभग 28 से 29 दिनों के अंतराल पर दोहराया जाता है। इसे तय तारीखों की तरह नहीं, कारणों की शृंखला की तरह पढ़ें।

पीत प्रावस्था में फटा हुआ पुटक पीत पिंड बनता है, जिसका प्रोजेस्टेरोन अंतःअस्तर बनाए रखता है। निषेचन न होने पर पीत पिंड क्षीण होता है, प्रोजेस्टेरोन घटता है, और अस्तर टूटता है: अगला आर्तव स्राव।

निषेचन से अंतःरोपण तक

निषेचन अंडवाहिनी के अम्पुला क्षेत्र में होता है। एक शुक्राणु जोना पेल्यूसिडा से टकराता है, जो फिर बाकी सभी शुक्राणुओं को रोक देती है, और संलयन से युग्मनज में द्विगुणित समूह लौटता है।

यात्रा के दौरान ही विदलन शुरू होता है: समसूत्री विभाजन बिना वृद्धि के 2, 4, 8, फिर 16 कोरकखंड बनाते हैं, और 8 से 16 कोशिकाओं की ठोस गेंद मोरूला है।

Is order mein padho

Gametogenesis: kaam ek, schedule alag

Spermatogenesis aur oogenesis dono diploid germ cells se do meiotic divisions se haploid gametes banate hain. Schedule aur division ka style bilkul alag hai.

Spermatogenesis puberty par shuru hokar continuously chalta hai: ek primary spermatocyte chaar equal haploid spermatids deti hai, jo shape badalkar sperms bante hain.

Oogenesis birth se pehle shuru hota hai, prophase I mein rukta hai, aur har division unequal hai: ek badi secondary oocyte cytoplasm rakhti hai aur ek chhota polar body extra chromosomes le jaata hai.

Hamara worked example

Human spermatid mein kitne chromosomes hote hain?

Teen steps:

  1. Primary spermatocyte diploid hai, matlab 46 chromosomes.
  2. Meiosis I set aadha karta hai, isliye har secondary spermatocyte mein 23.
  3. Meiosis II chromatids alag karta hai, pairs nahi. Answer: 23 chromosomes.

Menstrual cycle: judi hui events

Menstrual cycle average lagbhag 28 se 29 din ke interval par repeat hota hai. Ise fixed dates ki tarah nahi, causes ki chain ki tarah padho.

Follicular phase mein badhta FSH aur LH ek follicle badhata hai, aur follicle ke estrogens endometrium rebuild karte hain. Cycle ke beech mein, lagbhag day 14, LH surge ovulation karata hai.

Luteal phase mein ruptured follicle corpus luteum banta hai, jiska progesterone endometrium maintain karta hai. Fertilisation na hone par corpus luteum degenerate hota hai, progesterone girta hai, aur lining toot jaati hai: agla menstruation.

Hamara worked example

Is cycle mein fertilisation nahi hua. Menstruation kyun aata hai?

Teen steps:

  1. Fertilisation na hone par corpus luteum degenerate ho jaata hai.
  2. Corpus luteum ka progesterone hi endometrium maintain kar raha tha.
  3. Progesterone girte hi lining toot-ti hai. Answer: menstruation shuru.

Fertilisation se implantation tak

Fertilisation fallopian tube ke ampullary region mein hota hai. Ek sperm zona pellucida se takraata hai, jo phir baaki sab sperms ko rok deti hai, aur fusion se zygote mein diploid set wapas aata hai.

Journey ke dauran hi cleavage shuru hota hai: mitotic divisions bina growth ke 2, 4, 8, phir 16 blastomeres banate hain, aur 8 se 16 cells ki solid ball morula hai.

Morula blastocyst banta hai jisme outer trophoblast aur inner cell mass hota hai. Trophoblast endometrium se chipak kar dhas jaata hai: yahi implantation hai.

Ek zygote mein chaar cleavage divisions poore hue. Kitni cells?

  1. Cleavage bina growth ki mitosis hai, har division cells double karta hai.

  2. Lagbhag day 14 par LH sabse tez aur sabse uncha uthta hai.
  3. Answer: LH surge ovulation karata hai.

Q3. Fertilisation normally kahan hota hai?

Answer dekho

Teen steps:

  1. Sperm aur ovum ko ek hi jagah, ek hi time milna hota hai.
  2. NCERT is meeting ko fallopian tube ke ampullary region mein batati hai.
  3. Answer: fallopian tube ke ampulla mein, uterus mein nahi.

Q4. Cleavage se kai cells banti hain. Kya embryo abhi bada hota hai?

Answer dekho

Teen steps:

  1. Cleavage divisions bina growth phase ki tez mitoses hain.
  2. 16-cell morula original zona pellucida ke andar fit ho jaata hai.
  3. Answer: nahi; cells badhti hain par total size lagbhag wahi rehta hai.
