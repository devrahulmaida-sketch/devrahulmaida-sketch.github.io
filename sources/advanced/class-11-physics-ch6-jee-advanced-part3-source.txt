PART 3: Angular Momentum aur Collisions - JEE Advanced Problems

Q9. M mass aur R radius ki horizontal turntable (disc) vertical axis par omega angular speed se ghoom rahi hai. m mass ka aadmi rim par khada hai aur dheere dheere centre tak chalta hai. Naya angular speed aur kinetic energy ka change?
Solution: Bahari torque zero, isliye angular momentum conserve. Shuruat: (MR^2/2 + mR^2)omega. Ant: (MR^2/2) omega'. omega' = omega(1 + 2m/M). KE badhti hai: kyunki aadmi ne kaam kiya (andar ki taraf chalne ke liye apni body ki energy lagayi). Trap: KE conserve nahi hoti, L hoti hai.

Q10. M mass aur L lambai ki rod smooth horizontal table par rest mein hai. m = M mass ka kan v speed se rod ke ek chhor par perpendicular takraata hai aur chipak jaata hai. Final: COM ka velocity, angular velocity, aur KE ka loss?
Solution: Linear momentum conserve: Mv = 2M V, V = v/2. System ka COM rod ke centre se L/4 kan ki taraf hai. COM ke bare mein I = rod: ML^2/12 + M(L/4)^2 = 7ML^2/48, kan: M(L/4)^2 = 3ML^2/48. Total I = 10ML^2/48 = 5ML^2/24. Angular momentum COM ke bare mein: kan ka momentum Mv, perpendicular doori L/4: Mv L/4. omega = (MvL/4)/(5ML^2/24) = 6v/(5L). Final KE = (1/2)(2M)(v/2)^2 + (1/2)(5ML^2/24)(6v/5L)^2 = Mv^2/4 + 0.15 Mv^2 = 0.4 Mv^2. Initial 0.5 Mv^2, isliye 20 percent KE ka loss. Trap: rod ke centre ke bare mein L conserve karne se galat answer aata hai, isliye pehle COM nikalo.

Q11. Wahi rod (M, L) ab ek chhor par hinge se lagi hai, vertical hang hai. m mass ka kan v speed se niche wale chhor par horizontal takra kar chipak jaata hai. Takkar ke turant baad angular speed, aur hinge par impulse?
Solution: Hinge ke bare mein angular momentum conserve (hinge ka impulse torque nahi deta). mvL = (ML^2/3 + mL^2) omega, omega = 3mv/((M + 3m)L). Linear momentum conserve nahi hoti, kyunki hinge impulse deta hai. Hinge impulse = (M + m... ) rod ke COM ki velocity omega L/2 aur kan ki omega L: total momentum badla = M(omega L/2) + m(omega L) - mv, ye hinge ka impulse hai. Note: agar impulse rod ke centre of percussion (hinge se 2L/3) par diya jaye to hinge impulse zero hota hai.

Q12. m mass ka projectile u speed se horizontal se theta kon par origin se phenka gaya. Origin ke bare mein angular momentum: (a) maximum height par, (b) t samay par.
Solution: Maximum height par velocity u cos(theta) horizontal, H = u^2 sin^2(theta)/2g, L = m(u cos theta)(H) = m u^3 sin^2(theta) cos(theta)/(2g), direction ke saath (kagaz ke andar). (b) L = m (u cos theta)(u sin theta t - g t^2/2) - m(u sin theta - g t)(u cos theta t) = -(1/2) m g u cos(theta) t^2. Magnitude (1/2) m g u cos(theta) t^2. Yeh torque = r x mg ka integral hai. Dekho: L ka rate = mg ko x (horizontal doori) se multiply karna.
