🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Root — Neeche Ka Network ⭐

  • Root system ke types ⭐: Tap root — seed radicle se seedha main root + laterals (dicots — mustard); Fibrous root — radicle ki jagah thin roots ka cluster base se (monocots — wheat); Adventitious roots ⭐ — root ke alawa kisi bhi part se (grass, banyan, monstera).
  • Root ke regions ⭐: root cap (protection), region of meristematic activity (division), region of elongation, region of maturation (root hairs — absorption!).
  • Functions: absorption (water + minerals), anchorage, synthesis (plant growth regulators).
  • Root modifications ⭐⭐: Food storage — tap: carrot, turnip; adventitious: sweet potato; Support — prop roots (banyan ⭐), stilt roots (sugarcane/maize — lower nodes se); Respiration (pneumatophores) ⭐ — Rhizophora (mangrove) ki roots UPWARD grow karti hain oxygen ke liye (swampy areas); Nitrogen fixation — nodules (legume roots me Rhizobium).

💡 Feel: pneumatophores = mangrove trees ke 'snorkel' — doobe rehke bhi saans lete hain!

2️⃣ Stem — Plant Ka Backbone ⭐

  • Stem: ascending aerial part — nodes (leaves/branches attach) + internodes. Buds: terminal + axillary. Young stems green (photosynthesis bhi karte hain!).
  • Functions: branches/leaves/flowers ko spread karna, conduction (water + food), support, storage, vegetative propagation.
  • Stem modifications ⭐⭐: Underground — food storage: potato (tuber ⭐), ginger (rhizome), Colocasia (corm), onion/garlic (bulb); Subaerial — vegetative propagation: runner (grass, strawberry), stolon (jasmine), offset (Pistia, Eichhornia — water hyacinth ⭐), sucker (mint, Chrysanthemum); Aerial: tendrils (cucumber, grape vine — climbing ke liye ⭐), thorns (Citrus, Bougainvillea — defence), phylloclade (Opuntia — fleshy GREEN stem, photosynthesis, leaves spines! ⭐), cladode (Asparagus).
  • Confusion alert ⭐: potato = stem (eyes = buds!), ginger = stem (rhizome), par onion = bulb bhi stem modification hi hai!

🎯 Potato-ginger-onion = underground STEMS (buds/nodes hote hain!) vs carrot-turnip = ROOTS. Sabse common trap!

3️⃣ Leaf — Solar Panel ⭐

  • Leaf ke 3 parts ⭐: leaf base (stem se attach; stipules ho sakti hain — lateral leaf-like structures), petiole (leaf ko lamina se connect), lamina/blade (flat green part — veins + midrib).
  • Venation ⭐⭐: Reticulate (net-like — dicots) vs Parallel (veins parallel — monocots). Leaf arrangement = phyllotaxy: alternate (China rose), opposite (Calotropis, guava), whorled (Alstonia).
  • Simple leaf: single lamina (incised ho sakti hai par incision midrib tak nahi). Compound leaf ⭐: lamina leaflets me divided — pinnate (leaflets midrib/rachis pe — neem), palmate (leaflets ek point pe — silk cotton).
  • Leaf modifications ⭐⭐: tendrils (peas — climbing), spines (cactus — defence), fleshy leaves (onion, garlic — food storage), pitcher (Nepenthes — insect trap! ⭐), bladder (Utricularia — insect trap), reproductive leaves (Bryophyllum — buds at leaf margin ⭐).
  • Support structures ⭐: stipules protect young leaf; tendrils climbing.

🎯 Nepenthes (pitcher plant) + Utricularia (bladderwort) = insectivorous leaf modifications — har saal puche jaate hain!

4️⃣ Flower — Reproduction Ka Stage ⭐⭐

  • Flower ke 4 whorls ⭐⭐: Calyx (sepals — outermost, usually green, protection), Corolla (petals — coloured, attract pollinators), Androecium (stamens — male: filament + anther), Gynoecium (carpels/pistil — female: stigma + style + ovary). Sepals + petals = accessory organs; stamens + carpels = reproductive organs.
  • Position of ovary ⭐⭐: Hypogynous (ovary top pe, baaki neeche — superior ovary: mustard, brinjal, China rose), Perigynous (gynoecium centre me, baaki thalamus rim pe — half inferior: plum, rose, peach), Epigynous (ovary neeche — inferior ovary: guava, cucumber, sunflower ray florets).
  • Aestivation ⭐: sepals/petals ka arrangement in bud — valvate (touch only — Calotropis), twisted (overlap one side — China rose, cotton), imbricate (irregular overlap — gulmohar, cassia), vexillary/papilionaceous ⭐ (5 petals: standard 2 alae 2 keels — pea, bean).
  • Placentation ⭐⭐: ovules ki arrangement ovary me — marginal (pea), axile (China rose, tomato, lemon), parietal (mustard, Argemone), free central (Dianthus, Primrose), basal (sunflower, marigold).
  • Inflorescence: racemose (main axis grows on, flowers laterally — radish, mustard), cymose (main axis terminates in flower — jasmine, Bougainvillea).

💡 Flower = 4-ring circus: bahar se andar — sepals (guards), petals (advertisers), stamens (male workers), carpels (female HQ)!

5️⃣ Fruit, Seed aur Floral Families ⭐⭐

  • Fruit ⭐: fertilized ovary se banta hai. Parthenocarpic fruit ⭐: bina fertilization ke (seedless — banana!). Pericarp = fruit wall. True fruit = sirf ovary se; false fruit = thalamus bhi contribute (apple, strawberry).
  • Seed structure ⭐: Dicot seed (gram, bean): seed coat (testa + tegmen), hilum (scar), micropyle, 2 cotyledons (food), embryo axis (plumule + radicle). Monocot seed (maize ⭐): seed coat fruit wall se FUSED; single cotyledon = scutellum; endosperm = food storage (bulky); coleoptile (plumule cover) + coleorhiza (radicle cover) ⭐.
  • Endospermic vs non-endospermic ⭐: endosperm bacha (castor, coconut, maize) vs embryo ne kha liya (pea, bean, gram).
  • Fabaceae (legume family) ⭐⭐: papilionaceous corolla (vexillary aestivation), 10 stamens (diadelphous 9+1), superior ovary, marginal placentation, fruit = legume/pod. Examples: pea, gram, bean, soybean, groundnut, indigo.
  • Solanaceae ⭐⭐: 5 fused sepals + 5 fused petals, 5 stamens (epipetalous), superior oblique ovary, axile placentation, fruit = berry/capsule. Examples: tomato, brinjal, potato, chilli, belladonna, tobacco, Petunia.
  • Liliaceae ⭐⭐: 6 tepals (2 whorls of 3), 6 stamens, tricarpellary superior ovary, axile placentation. Examples: onion, garlic, Asparagus, lily, tulip, Aloe, Colchicine (colchicine drug!).

🎯 Floral formula teeno families ka yaad rakho — Fabaceae = butterfly flower; Solanaceae = 5+5+5; Liliaceae = 3 ke multiples!

1️⃣ मूल — भूमिगत नेटवर्क ⭐

  • मूल तंत्र के प्रकार ⭐: मूसला मूल — मूलांकुर से सीधी मुख्य मूल (द्विबीजपत्री — सरसों); रेशेदार मूल — पतली मूलों का समूह (एकबीजपत्री — गेहूँ); अपस्थानिक मूल ⭐ — मूल के अतिरिक्त किसी भाग से (घास, बरगद)।
  • मूल के क्षेत्र ⭐: मूल-टोपी (सुरक्षा), विभज्योतक क्षेत्र, दीर्घीकरण क्षेत्र, परिपक्वता क्षेत्र (मूल-रोम — अवशोषण!)।
  • कार्य: अवशोषण (जल + खनिज), स्थिरीकरण, वृद्धि-नियामकों का संश्लेषण।
  • मूल रूपांतरण ⭐⭐: भोजन-संचय — मूसला: गाजर, शलजम; अपस्थानिक: शकरकंद; आधार — स्तंभ मूल (बरगद ⭐), खंभे मूल (गन्ना); श्वसन (न्यूमैटोफ़ोर) ⭐ — राइज़ोफ़ोरा की मूलें ऊपर उगती हैं; नाइट्रोजन स्थिरीकरण — ग्रंथिकाएँ (फलियों में राइज़ोबियम)।

💡 न्यूमैटोफ़ोर = मैंग्रोव के 'स्नॉर्कल'!

2️⃣ तना — पादप की रीढ़ ⭐

  • तना: आरोही वायवीय भाग — पर्व (नोड) + अन्तःपर्व। कलिकाएँ: शीर्षस्थ + अक्षीय।
  • कार्य: शाखाओं/पत्तियों/पुष्पों का फैलाव, संचालन, आधार, संचय, कायिक प्रवर्धन।
  • तने के रूपांतरण ⭐⭐: भूमिगत — भोजन-संचय: आलू (कंद ⭐), अदरक (प्रकंद), अरबी (घनकंद), प्याज़/लहसुन (कंदल); अधःवायवीय: रनर (घास, स्ट्रॉबेरी), ऑफ़सेट (पिस्टिया, जलकुंभी ⭐), सकर (पुदीना); वायवीय: प्रतान (ककड़ी, अंगूर ⭐), काँटे (नींबू, बूगनविलिया), फाइलोक्लेड (ऑपंशिया — मांसल हरा तना, पत्तियाँ काँटे! ⭐), क्लैडोड (शतावरी)।
  • भ्रम चेतावनी ⭐: आलू = तना (आँखें = कलिकाएँ!), अदरक = तना; परंतु गाजर-शलजम = मूलें!

🎯 आलू-अदरक-प्याज़ = भूमिगत तने बनाम गाजर-शलजम = मूलें — सबसे बड़ा जाल!

3️⃣ पत्ती — सौर पैनल ⭐

  • पत्ती के 3 भाग ⭐: पर्णाधार (तने से जुड़ाव; उपपर्ण संभव), पर्णवृंत (डंठल), पर्णफलक (चपटा हरा भाग — शिराएँ + मध्यशिरा)।
  • शिरा-विन्यास ⭐⭐: जालिकाभ (द्विबीजपत्री) बनाम समांतर (एकबीजपत्री)। पर्ण-विन्यास: एकान्तर (गुड़हल), सम्मुख (आक, अमरूद), चक्रीय (छातिम)।
  • सरल पर्ण बनाम संयुक्त पर्ण ⭐: पंख-सदृश (पर्णिकाएँ मध्यशिरा पर — नीम), तले-सदृश (एक बिंदु पर — शिमुल)।
  • पर्ण रूपांतरण ⭐⭐: प्रतान (मटर), काँटे (कैक्टस), मांसल पत्तियाँ (प्याज़, लहसुन), घटी (नेपेन्थीज़ — कीट-भक्षी! ⭐), थैली (यूट्रिकुलेरिया), जनन पर्ण (ब्रायोफिलम — पत्ती-किनारे पर कलिकाएँ ⭐)।

🎯 नेपेन्थीज़ + यूट्रिकुलेरिया = कीटभक्षी पर्ण रूपांतरण — हर साल पूछे जाते हैं!

4️⃣ पुष्प — जनन का मंच ⭐⭐

  • पुष्प के 4 चक्र ⭐⭐: बाह्यदलपुंज (बाह्यदल — सबसे बाहर, हरे, सुरक्षा), दलपुंज (पंखुड़ियाँ — रंगीन, परागकर्षण), पुंकेसरपुंज (पुंकेसर — नर: तंतु + परागकोश), जायांगपुंज (स्त्रीकेसर — मादा: वर्तिकाग्र + वर्तिका + अंडाशय)।
  • अंडाशय की स्थिति ⭐⭐: जायांगाधोवर्ती (hypogynous) — अंडाशय ऊपर (उत्कृष्ट): सरसों, बैंगन, गुड़हल; जायांगपरिवर्ती (perigynous) — आधा-अधीन: आड़ू, गुलाब; जायांगाभिवर्ती (epigynous) — अंडाशय नीचे (अधीन): अमरूद, खीरा।
  • सुसज्जन (aestivation) ⭐: कली में दलों की व्यवस्था — संपुटी (आक), घूर्णित (गुड़हल, कपास), अधःस्तरी (गुलमोहर), वक्रपुटी/तितली-सदृश ⭐ (मटर, बीन्स)।
  • बीजांडविन्यास ⭐⭐: किनारी (मटर), अक्षीय (गुड़हल, टमाटर, नींबू), भित्तीय (सरसों), मुक्त-केन्द्रीय (डायन्थस), आधारीय (सूरजमुखी)।
  • पुष्पक्रम: असीमाक्षी (मुख्य अक्ष बढ़ता रहता है — सरसों), सीमाक्षी (मुख्य अक्ष पुष्प में समाप्त — चमेली)।

💡 पुष्प = 4-वलय का समूह: बाहर से अंदर — बाह्यदल (रक्षक), पंखुड़ियाँ (विज्ञापन), पुंकेसर (नर), स्त्रीकेसर (मादा)।

5️⃣ फल, बीज और पुष्पीय कुल ⭐⭐

  • फल ⭐: निषेचित अंडाशय से बनता है। अनिषेचितफल (parthenocarpic) ⭐: बिना निषेचन के (बीजहीन — केला!)। फल-भित्ति = फलचोल।
  • बीज संरचना ⭐: द्विबीजपत्री (चना): बीजावरण, नाभि, सूक्ष्मछिद्र, 2 बीजपत्र (भोजन), भ्रूणाक्ष। एकबीजपत्री (मक्का ⭐): बीजावरण फल-भित्ति से जुड़ा; एक बीजपत्र = स्कूटेलम; भ्रूणपोष = भोजन; कोलियोप्टाइल + कोलियोराइज़ा ⭐।
  • भ्रूणपोषी बनाम अभ्रूणपोषी ⭐: भ्रूणपोष शेष (अरंडी, नारियल, मक्का) बनाम भ्रूण ने उपभोग (मटर, चना)।
  • फ़ैबेसी (फली-कुल) ⭐⭐: तितली-सदृश दलपुंज, 10 पुंकेसर (द्विसंघी 9+1), उत्कृष्ट अंडाशय, किनारी बीजांडविन्यास, फल = फली। उदा: मटर, चना, सोयाबीन, मूंगफली।
  • सोलेनेसी ⭐⭐: 5 संयुक्त बाह्यदल + 5 संयुक्त पंखुड़ियाँ, 5 पुंकेसर (पंखुड़ी-युक्त), उत्कृष्ट तिर्यक अंडाशय, अक्षीय विन्यास, फल = बेरी। उदा: टमाटर, बैंगन, आलू, मिर्च, तम्बाकू।
  • लिलिएसी ⭐⭐: 6 दल (3 के 2 चक्र), 6 पुंकेसर, त्रि-अंगपूटी उत्कृष्ट अंडाशय, अक्षीय विन्यास। उदा: प्याज़, लहसुन, शतावरी, कुमारी, एलो।

🎯 तीनों कुलों के पुष्प-सूत्र याद रखें — फ़ैबेसी = तितली-पुष्प; सोलेनेसी = 5+5+5; लिलिएसी = 3 के गुणक!

1️⃣ Root — The Underground Network ⭐

  • Root system types ⭐: Tap root — main root straight from the radicle (dicots — mustard); Fibrous root — cluster of thin roots from the base (monocots — wheat); Adventitious roots ⭐ — from any part other than the radicle (grass, banyan).
  • Regions of root ⭐: root cap (protection), meristematic zone (division), elongation zone, maturation zone (root hairs — absorption!).
  • Functions: absorption of water and minerals, anchorage, synthesis of growth regulators.
  • Root modifications ⭐⭐: Food storage — tap: carrot, turnip; adventitious: sweet potato; Support — prop roots (banyan ⭐), stilt roots (sugarcane/maize); Respiration (pneumatophores) ⭐ — Rhizophora roots grow UPWARD for oxygen; Nitrogen fixation — root nodules (Rhizobium in legumes).

💡 Feel: pneumatophores are the mangrove's snorkels — breathing while submerged!

2️⃣ Stem — The Plant's Backbone ⭐

  • Stem: the ascending aerial part — nodes (leaves/branches attach) + internodes. Buds: terminal + axillary.
  • Functions: spreading branches/leaves/flowers, conduction, support, storage, vegetative propagation.
  • Stem modifications ⭐⭐: Underground — food storage: potato (tuber ⭐), ginger (rhizome), Colocasia (corm), onion (bulb); Subaerial — propagation: runner (grass, strawberry), stolon (jasmine), offset (Pistia, water hyacinth ⭐), sucker (mint); Aerial: tendrils (cucumber, grape vine ⭐), thorns (Citrus, Bougainvillea), phylloclade (Opuntia — fleshy GREEN stem doing photosynthesis, leaves as spines! ⭐), cladode (Asparagus).
  • Confusion alert ⭐: potato = stem (eyes are buds!), ginger = stem — but carrot/turnip = ROOTS!

🎯 Potato-ginger-onion = underground STEMS (they have buds/nodes!) vs carrot-turnip = roots. The most common trap!

3️⃣ Leaf — The Solar Panel ⭐

  • 3 parts of a leaf ⭐: leaf base (attaches to stem, may bear stipules), petiole (stalk), lamina/blade (flat green part — veins + midrib).
  • Venation ⭐⭐: Reticulate (net-like — dicots) vs Parallel (monocots). Arrangement = phyllotaxy: alternate (China rose), opposite (Calotropis, guava), whorled (Alstonia).
  • Simple leaf (single lamina) vs compound leaf ⭐: pinnate (leaflets on the rachis — neem), palmate (leaflets at one point — silk cotton).
  • Leaf modifications ⭐⭐: tendrils (peas), spines (cactus), fleshy leaves (onion, garlic), pitcher (Nepenthes — insect trap! ⭐), bladder (Utricularia), reproductive leaves (Bryophyllum — buds on the leaf margin ⭐).

🎯 Nepenthes + Utricularia = insectivorous leaf modifications — asked every year!

4️⃣ Flower — The Reproduction Stage ⭐⭐

  • 4 whorls of a flower ⭐⭐: Calyx (sepals — outermost, green, protection), Corolla (petals — coloured, attract pollinators), Androecium (stamens — male: filament + anther), Gynoecium (carpels — female: stigma + style + ovary).
  • Ovary position ⭐⭐: Hypogynous (superior ovary — mustard, brinjal, China rose), Perigynous (half-inferior — plum, rose, peach), Epigynous (inferior ovary — guava, cucumber, sunflower).
  • Aestivation ⭐: arrangement of sepals/petals in bud — valvate (Calotropis), twisted (China rose, cotton), imbricate (gulmohar), vexillary/papilionaceous ⭐ (standard + 2 wings + 2 keels — pea, bean).
  • Placentation ⭐⭐: ovule arrangement in the ovary — marginal (pea), axile (China rose, tomato, lemon), parietal (mustard), free central (Dianthus, Primrose), basal (sunflower, marigold).
  • Inflorescence: racemose (main axis keeps growing — radish, mustard), cymose (main axis ends in a flower — jasmine, Bougainvillea).

💡 A flower is a 4-ring circus: outside-in — sepals (guards), petals (advertisers), stamens (male), carpels (female HQ)!

5️⃣ Fruit, Seed and Floral Families ⭐⭐

  • Fruit ⭐: forms from the fertilized ovary. Parthenocarpic fruit ⭐: without fertilization (seedless — banana!). Pericarp = fruit wall.
  • Seed structure ⭐: Dicot seed (gram): seed coat, hilum, micropyle, 2 food-storing cotyledons, embryo axis. Monocot seed (maize ⭐): seed coat fused with fruit wall; single cotyledon = scutellum; endosperm stores food; coleoptile + coleorhiza ⭐ cover plumule and radicle.
  • Endospermic vs non-endospermic ⭐: endosperm persists (castor, coconut, maize) vs consumed by embryo (pea, gram).
  • Fabaceae (legume family) ⭐⭐: papilionaceous corolla (vexillary aestivation), 10 diadelphous stamens (9+1), superior ovary, marginal placentation, fruit = legume. E.g. pea, gram, soybean, groundnut, indigo.
  • Solanaceae ⭐⭐: 5 united sepals + 5 united petals, 5 epipetalous stamens, superior oblique ovary, axile placentation, fruit = berry/capsule. E.g. tomato, brinjal, potato, chilli, tobacco.
  • Liliaceae ⭐⭐: 6 tepals (2 whorls of 3), 6 stamens, tricarpellary superior ovary, axile placentation. E.g. onion, garlic, Asparagus, lily, Aloe.

🎯 Memorize the three floral formulas — Fabaceae = butterfly flower; Solanaceae = 5+5+5; Liliaceae = multiples of 3!

🏆 NEET Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Tap root aur fibrous root system me kya difference hai?
Answer dekho

Tap root me radicle se seedhi main root + laterals (dicots); fibrous me radicle ki jagah thin roots ka cluster base se (monocots).

Q2. Pneumatophores kya hote hain? Example do.
Answer dekho

Kuch mangrove plants (Rhizophora) ki roots upward grow karti hain swamp me se oxygen lene ke liye — inhe pneumatophores kehte hain.

Q3. Potato ko stem modification kyun maana jaata hai, root nahi?
Answer dekho

Kyunki potato pe 'eyes' naam ki buds hoti hain aur ye underground stem (tuber) hai jo food store karta hai. Roots pe buds/nodes nahi hote.

Q4. Nepenthes aur Utricularia me leaf ka kya modification hai?
Answer dekho

Nepenthes me leaf pitcher (ghada) aur Utricularia me bladder (thaili) ban jaati hai — dono insects trap karne ke liye (insectivorous plants!).

Q5. Vexillary aestivation kya hai?
Answer dekho

Pea/bean ke flower me 5 petals ka arrangement: 1 standard (largest) + 2 wings (alae) + 2 keels — papilionaceous corolla bhi kehte hain (Fabaceae family).

Q6. Hypogynous, perigynous aur epigynous flowers me farak batao.
Answer dekho

Hypogynous: ovary superior, baaki parts neeche (mustard); Perigynous: ovary half-inferior, parts thalamus rim pe (rose); Epigynous: ovary inferior (guava, cucumber).

Q7. Maize seed me scutellum kya hai?
Answer dekho

Maize (monocot) ka single cotyledon jise scutellum kehte hain — ye endosperm se food embryo tak pahunchata hai. (Dicots me 2 cotyledons.)

Q8. Axile placentation kya hai? Examples do.
Answer dekho

Ovules central axis (placenta) pe multi-chambered ovary me lagte hain — China rose, tomato, lemon.

Q9. Fabaceae family ke 3 identifying characters batao.
Answer dekho

Vexillary (papilionaceous) corolla, 9+1 diadelphous stamens, marginal placentation — fruit = legume/pod.

Q10. Solanaceae aur Liliaceae me stamens ka arrangement kaise different hai?
Answer dekho

Solanaceae: 5 stamens, petals se fused (epipetalous); Liliaceae: 6 stamens, tepals se fused (epitepalous).
