Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Photosynthesis Basics aur Experiments ⭐

  • Photosynthesis: green plants sunlight ki energy se CO2 + H2O ko glucose me convert karte hain — O2 by-product! Equation: 6CO2 + 12H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2 + 6H2O. Saari life ka ultimate food source yahi hai!
  • Early experiments ⭐: Joseph Priestley (1770) ⭐ — candle + mint plant bell jar me: candle jal-ti rahi! Conclusion: plants air ko 'restore' karte hain (O2 dete hain). Jan Ingenhousz ⭐ — same experiment dark me FAIL! Conclusion: sunlight zaroori hai O2 release ke liye (aur sirf green parts me hota hai).
  • Julius von Sachs (1854) ⭐: dikhaya ki photosynthesis me glucose banta hai jo starch ke roop me store hota hai — aur ye green parts (chlorophyll) me hota hai.
  • T.W. Engelmann ⭐⭐: prism se light ko alga (Cladophora) pe split kiya — bacteria blue aur red regions me jama hue! Conclusion: photosynthesis ke liye blue + red light MOST effective (first action spectrum!).
  • Cornelius van Niel ⭐⭐: purple/green sulphur bacteria pe kaam karke propose kiya — photosynthesis ka O2 water se aata hai, CO2 se NAHI! (Radioactive tracer experiments ne baad me prove kiya.)

💡 4 naam yaad karo: Priestley = plant air restore karta hai; Ingenhousz = light zaroori; Sachs = starch; Engelmann = blue-red light!

2️⃣ Photosynthetic Machinery — Pigments aur Site ⭐⭐

  • Site = chloroplast ⭐: double membrane; andar thylakoids (flattened sacs — inme pigments) → stacks = grana; fluid matrix = stroma (enzymes + own DNA/ribosomes).
  • Light reactions = THYLAKOID membranes pe (grana me) ⭐⭐; Dark reactions (biosynthetic phase) = STROMA me ⭐⭐.
  • Pigments ⭐⭐: Chlorophyll a (bright/blue green — MAIN pigment, reaction centre!), chlorophyll b (yellow green), xanthophylls (yellow), carotenoids (yellow-orange). Chl a + b blue aur red light absorb karte hain, green REFLECT (isliye plants green dikhte hain!).
  • Accessory pigments (chl b, xanthophylls, carotenoids) ⭐: light absorb karke energy chl a ko dete hain (antennae ka kaam!) + chlorophyll a ko photo-oxidation se protect karte hain.
  • Chromatography se pigments separate ⭐: paper chromatography pe — chl a (blue green), chl b (yellow green), xanthophylls (yellow), carotenoids (yellow-orange) alag-alag bands!

🎯 Light reaction = thylakoid/grana; Dark reaction = stroma. Chlorophyll a = hero, baaki = supporting cast (antennae)!

3️⃣ Light Reactions — Electron Transport aur Photophosphorylation ⭐⭐

  • Photosystems ⭐⭐: PS II — reaction centre P680 (680nm best absorb); PS I — reaction centre P700. Dono grana membranes me (PS I stroma lamellae me bhi!). Light energy se P680/P700 ke electrons EXCITE hote hain.
  • Non-cyclic photophosphorylation (Z-scheme) ⭐⭐: PS II excite → electrons primary acceptor ko → electron transport chain (plastoquinone → cytochrome complex → plastocyanin) → PS I → NADP+ reductase → NADPH + H+ banta hai. PS II ke khali electrons ki jagah WATER SPLIT hoti hai (photolysis of water ⭐⭐ — thylakoid LUMEN me, Mn+Cl+Ca se): 2H2O → 4H+ + O2 + 4e− — O2 yahi se nikalta hai! ETC se proton gradient banta hai → ATP (chemiosmosis!).
  • Cyclic photophosphorylation ⭐: SIRF PS I use hota hai (light zyada ho ya NADPH ki zaroorat na ho) — electrons PS I se cycle karke wapas aa jaate hain → sirf ATP banta hai, NADPH nahi, O2 nahi (water split nahi hoti!).
  • Products of light reaction ⭐⭐: ATP + NADPH (+ O2 release) — ye dono dark reaction ke fuel hain.
  • Chemiosmosis ⭐: proton gradient lumen (high H+) → stroma (low H+) — ATP synthase se guzarke ATP banta hai (mitochondria jaise, par energy source = light!).

💡 Z-scheme = water tootke O2 + electrons deta hai, electrons rollercoaster pe PS II → PS I → NADPH. Cyclic = sirf ATP wala shortcut!

4️⃣ Calvin Cycle — Carbon Fixation ⭐⭐

  • Dark reaction / biosynthetic phase ⭐: stroma me — CO2 se sugar banta hai. ATP + NADPH (light reaction se) use hote hain. Directly light pe depend NAHI (par products chahiye!).
  • C3 cycle = Calvin cycle ⭐⭐ (Melvin Calvin — Nobel!): 3 stages — Carboxylation ⭐⭐: CO2 + RuBP (ribulose bisphosphate, 5C) → 2 molecules 3-PGA (3-phosphoglycerate, 3C). Enzyme = RuBisCO ⭐⭐ (sabse abundant protein in biosphere!). Pehla stable product = 3-PGA (3 carbon — isliye 'C3 cycle'!). Reduction: 3-PGA → glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate (sugar!) — ATP + NADPH use. Regeneration ⭐: RuBP wapas banta hai (ATP use) — cycle chalta rahe!
  • Numbers yaad karo ⭐: 1 glucose ke liye 6 CO2 fix karne padte hain → 18 ATP + 12 NADPH use hote hain.
  • RuBisCO = carboxylase + oxygenase ⭐: CO2 bhi bind, O2 bhi — O2 zyada ho to photorespiration (wasteful — next block!).

🎯 RuBP (5C) + CO2 → 2 × 3-PGA (3C). RuBisCO = world's most abundant enzyme. 6 turns = 1 glucose = 18 ATP + 12 NADPH!

5️⃣ C4 Pathway, Photorespiration aur Factors ⭐⭐

  • C4 pathway (Hatch-Slack pathway) ⭐⭐: tropical grasses (maize, sugarcane, sorghum) ki jugaad! Pehla product = OAA (oxaloacetic acid, 4C) — isliye 'C4'. CO2 pehle mesophyll cells me fix hoti hai (enzyme PEP carboxylase/PEPcase ⭐ — O2 bind NAHI karta!), phir C4 acid bundle sheath cells me jaake CO2 release karta hai → wahan Calvin cycle (C3) chalti hai. Kranz anatomy ⭐⭐ = mesophyll ring around bundle sheath (special leaf structure!).
  • C4 advantage ⭐⭐: photorespiration NAHI (CO2 concentrated around RuBisCO!), high temperature + drought me bhi efficient, productivity zyada. Cost: extra ATP (30 per glucose vs 18 in C3) — par efficiency ke aage sasta deal!
  • Photorespiration ⭐: C3 plants me — RuBisCO O2 bind karle to RuBP + O2 → phosphoglycolate — NO sugar, NO ATP — energy waste! High temp + high O2/low CO2 me badhti hai. C4 me absent!
  • Factors affecting photosynthesis ⭐⭐ (Blackman's Law of Limiting Factors ⭐): sabse kam factor hi rate decide karta hai! Light (quality + intensity + duration — high intensity pe photooxidation bhi!), CO2 (major limiting — 0.03-0.04% atmosphere me; C3 plants CO2 enrichment se zyada productive ⭐), Temperature (dark reactions enzymatic — optimum ~25-35°C), Water (indirect — stomata band → CO2 kam).

🎯 C4 = maize/sugarcane ki AC car — Kranz anatomy + PEPcase + no photorespiration. C3 = normal car — CO2 kam mile to photorespiration ka nuksaan!

1️⃣ प्रकाश-संश्लेषण की मूल बातें और प्रयोग ⭐

  • प्रकाश-संश्लेषण: हरे पादप सूर्य-प्रकाश से CO2 + H2O को ग्लूकोज़ में बदलते हैं — O2 उप-उत्पाद! समीकरण: 6CO2 + 12H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2 + 6H2O।
  • प्रारम्भिक प्रयोग ⭐: जोसेफ प्रीस्टली (1770) ⭐ — मोमबत्ती + पुदीना: मोमबत्ती जलती रही! पादप वायु 'पुनर्स्थापित' करते हैं। इन्गेनहौज़ ⭐ — अंधेरे में असफल! प्रकाश आवश्यक।
  • जूलियस वॉन सैक्स (1854) ⭐: ग्लूकोज़ बनता है जो स्टार्च में संचित होता है — हरे भागों (हरितलवक) में।
  • टी.डब्ल्यू. ऐंगेलमैन ⭐⭐: प्रिज़्म-प्रकाश पर जीवाणु नीले और लाल क्षेत्रों में जमा — नीला + लाल प्रकाश सबसे प्रभावी!
  • वान नील ⭐⭐: प्रकाश-संश्लेषण का O2 जल से आता है, CO2 से नहीं!

💡 4 नाम: प्रीस्टली = वायु पुनर्स्थापना; इन्गेनहौज़ = प्रकाश; सैक्स = स्टार्च; ऐंगेलमैन = नीला-लाल!

2️⃣ प्रकाश-संश्लेषण मशीनरी — वर्णक और स्थल ⭐⭐

  • स्थल = हरितलवक ⭐: द्वि-झिल्ली; थाइलैकॉइड (वर्णक यहाँ!) → ढेर = ग्रैना; तरल मैट्रिक्स = स्ट्रोमा।
  • प्रकाश-अभिक्रियाएँ = थाइलैकॉइड झिल्ली (ग्रैना) ⭐⭐; अंध-अभिक्रियाएँ = स्ट्रोमा ⭐⭐।
  • वर्णक ⭐⭐: हरितलवक a (नीला-हरा — मुख्य!), हरितलवक b (पीला-हरा), ज़ैन्थोफ़िल (पीला), कैरोटिनॉइड (पीला-नारंगी)। नीला + लाल अवशोषण, हरा परावर्तन!
  • सहायक वर्णक ⭐: ऊर्जा हरितलवक a को देते हैं (ऐंटेना!) + प्रकाश-ऑक्सीकरण से सुरक्षा।

🎯 प्रकाश-अभिक्रिया = ग्रैना; अंध-अभिक्रिया = स्ट्रोमा। हरितलवक a = नायक!

3️⃣ प्रकाश-अभिक्रियाएँ — इलेक्ट्रॉन परिवहन ⭐⭐

  • प्रकाश-तंत्र ⭐⭐: PS II — अभिक्रिया-केंद्र P680; PS I — P700।
  • अचक्रीय प्रकाश-फ़ॉस्फोरीकरण (Z-स्कीम) ⭐⭐: PS II → इलेक्ट्रॉन-शृंखला → PS I → NADPH। PS II के इलेक्ट्रॉन की भरपाई जल-विघटन (photolysis) ⭐⭐ से — थाइलैकॉइड ल्यूमेन में: 2H2O → 4H+ + O2 + 4e− — O2 यहीं से! प्रोटॉन-प्रवणता → ATP (रासायनिक-परासरण!)।
  • चक्रीय प्रकाश-फ़ॉस्फोरीकरण ⭐: केवल PS I — केवल ATP, NADPH नहीं, O2 नहीं!
  • प्रकाश-अभिक्रिया के उत्पाद ⭐⭐: ATP + NADPH (+ O2)।

💡 Z-स्कीम = जल टूटकर O2 + इलेक्ट्रॉन देता है; चक्रीय = केवल-ATP शॉर्टकट!

4️⃣ कैल्विन चक्र — कार्बन स्थिरीकरण ⭐⭐

  • अंध-अभिक्रिया ⭐: स्ट्रोमा में — CO2 से शर्करा; ATP + NADPH उपयोग।
  • C3 चक्र = कैल्विन चक्र ⭐⭐: 3 चरण — कार्बोक्सिलीकरण ⭐⭐: CO2 + RuBP (5C) → 2 × 3-PGA (3C)। एंज़ाइम = RuBisCO ⭐⭐ (जैव-मंडल का सर्वाधिक प्रोटीन!)। प्रथम स्थायी उत्पाद 3-PGA (3 कार्बन — 'C3'!)। अपचयन: 3-PGA → शर्करा (ATP + NADPH)। पुनर्जनन: RuBP पुनः (ATP)।
  • संख्याएँ ⭐: 1 ग्लूकोज़ = 6 CO2 → 18 ATP + 12 NADPH।
  • RuBisCO ⭐: CO2 भी बांधता, O2 भी — O2 अधिक हो तो प्रकाश-श्वसन!

🎯 RuBP (5C) + CO2 → 2 × 3-PGA (3C)। 6 चक्र = 1 ग्लूकोज़ = 18 ATP + 12 NADPH!

5️⃣ C4 मार्ग, प्रकाश-श्वसन और कारक ⭐⭐

  • C4 मार्ग (हैच-स्लैक) ⭐⭐: उष्णकटिबंधीय घास (मक्का, गन्ना)। प्रथम उत्पाद = OAA (4C) — 'C4'! CO2 पहले मीज़ोफ़िल कोशिकाओं में (PEP कार्बोक्सिलेज़ ⭐ — O2 नहीं बांधता!), फिर C4 अम्ल बंडल-शीथ कोशिकाओं में CO2 छोड़ता है → कैल्विन चक्र। क्रैंज़ शारीरिकी ⭐⭐ = बंडल-शीथ के चारों ओर मीज़ोफ़िल वलय!
  • C4 लाभ ⭐⭐: प्रकाश-श्वसन नहीं, उच्च ताप + सूखे में भी कुशल, उत्पादकता अधिक। लागत: अतिरिक्त ATP (30 बनाम 18)।
  • प्रकाश-श्वसन ⭐: C3 में — RuBisCO O2 बांध ले तो शर्करा नहीं, ATP नहीं — ऊर्जा-बर्बादी! C4 में अनुपस्थित!
  • कारक ⭐⭐ (ब्लैकमैन का सीमित-कारक नियम ⭐): सबसे कम कारक ही दर तय करता है! प्रकाश, CO2 (प्रमुख सीमित — 0.03-0.04%!), तापमान, जल (अप्रत्यक्ष — रंध्र बंद)।

🎯 C4 = एसी गाड़ी — क्रैंज़ + PEPcase + प्रकाश-श्वसन-रहित; C3 = साधारण गाड़ी!

1️⃣ Photosynthesis Basics and Experiments ⭐

  • Photosynthesis: green plants convert CO2 + water into glucose using sunlight — O2 is the by-product! Equation: 6CO2 + 12H2O → C6H12O6 + 6O2 + 6H2O.
  • Early experiments ⭐: Joseph Priestley (1770) ⭐ — a candle kept burning with a mint plant in a bell jar: plants 'restore' the air! Jan Ingenhousz ⭐ — the same experiment FAILED in the dark: sunlight is essential!
  • Julius von Sachs (1854) ⭐: showed glucose is made and stored as starch in green parts (chlorophyll).
  • T.W. Engelmann ⭐⭐: split light with a prism onto an alga — bacteria clustered in blue and red regions: blue + red light are most effective!
  • Cornelius van Niel ⭐⭐: proposed that the O2 comes from WATER, not CO2 — later proven with radioactive tracers.

💡 Four names: Priestley = plants restore air; Ingenhousz = light needed; Sachs = starch; Engelmann = blue-red light!

2️⃣ Photosynthetic Machinery — Pigments and Site ⭐⭐

  • Site = chloroplast ⭐: double membrane; thylakoids (pigment-bearing sacs) stack into grana; the fluid matrix is the stroma.
  • Light reactions = on THYLAKOID membranes (grana) ⭐⭐; Dark reactions = in the STROMA ⭐⭐.
  • Pigments ⭐⭐: Chlorophyll a (blue-green — the MAIN pigment!), chlorophyll b (yellow-green), xanthophylls (yellow), carotenoids (yellow-orange). They absorb blue + red light and reflect green!
  • Accessory pigments ⭐: hand absorbed energy to chlorophyll a (the antennae!) + protect it from photo-oxidation.

🎯 Light reaction = grana; dark reaction = stroma. Chlorophyll a = the hero, the rest = antennae!

3️⃣ Light Reactions — Electron Transport ⭐⭐

  • Photosystems ⭐⭐: PS II — reaction centre P680; PS I — reaction centre P700. Light excites their electrons.
  • Non-cyclic photophosphorylation (Z-scheme) ⭐⭐: PS II → electron transport chain → PS I → NADPH. PS II's lost electrons are replaced by SPLITTING WATER (photolysis) ⭐⭐ in the thylakoid lumen: 2H2O → 4H+ + O2 + 4e− — this is where O2 comes from! The proton gradient drives ATP synthesis (chemiosmosis!).
  • Cyclic photophosphorylation ⭐: PS I only — only ATP; no NADPH, no O2!
  • Light reaction products ⭐⭐: ATP + NADPH (+ O2) — the fuel for the dark reaction.

💡 Z-scheme = water splits to give O2 + electrons; cyclic = the ATP-only shortcut!

4️⃣ Calvin Cycle — Carbon Fixation ⭐⭐

  • Dark reaction ⭐: in the stroma — CO2 becomes sugar using ATP + NADPH from the light reaction.
  • C3 cycle = Calvin cycle ⭐⭐: 3 stages — Carboxylation ⭐⭐: CO2 + RuBP (5C) → 2 × 3-PGA (3C). Enzyme = RuBisCO ⭐⭐ (the most abundant protein in the biosphere!). First stable product = 3-PGA (3 carbons — hence 'C3'!). Reduction: 3-PGA → sugar (uses ATP + NADPH). Regeneration: RuBP is rebuilt (uses ATP).
  • Numbers ⭐: 1 glucose needs 6 CO2 → 18 ATP + 12 NADPH.
  • RuBisCO ⭐: binds CO2 AND O2 — too much O2 triggers photorespiration!

🎯 RuBP (5C) + CO2 → 2 × 3-PGA (3C). 6 turns = 1 glucose = 18 ATP + 12 NADPH!

5️⃣ C4 Pathway, Photorespiration and Factors ⭐⭐

  • C4 pathway (Hatch-Slack) ⭐⭐: tropical grasses (maize, sugarcane, sorghum). First product = OAA (4C) — hence 'C4'! CO2 is first fixed in mesophyll cells by PEP carboxylase ⭐ (never binds O2!), then the C4 acid delivers CO2 to bundle sheath cells for the Calvin cycle. Kranz anatomy ⭐⭐ = mesophyll ring around the bundle sheath!
  • C4 advantage ⭐⭐: NO photorespiration (CO2 is concentrated around RuBisCO!), efficient in heat + drought, higher productivity. Cost: extra ATP (30 vs 18 in C3).
  • Photorespiration ⭐: in C3 plants — when RuBisCO binds O2: no sugar, no ATP — pure waste! Absent in C4!
  • Factors ⭐⭐ (Blackman's Law of Limiting Factors ⭐): the scarcest factor sets the rate! Light (quality + intensity), CO2 (the major limiting factor — only 0.03-0.04% of air!), temperature, water (indirect — closed stomata cut CO2).

🎯 C4 = the AC car — Kranz anatomy + PEPcase + no photorespiration; C3 = the regular car!

🏆 NEET Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Photosynthesis me nikalne wala O2 kahan se aata hai?
Answer dekho

WATER ke photolysis (splitting) se — thylakoid lumen me: 2H2O → 4H+ + O2 + 4e−. van Niel ne propose kiya tha, radioactive tracers ne prove kiya.

Q2. Light aur dark reactions kahan hoti hain?
Answer dekho

Light reactions = thylakoid membranes (grana); Dark reactions (Calvin cycle) = stroma.

Q3. Light reaction ke products kya hain?
Answer dekho

ATP + NADPH (dark reaction ke fuel) + O2 (water splitting se — atmosphere me release).

Q4. PS I aur PS II ke reaction centres ke naam batao.
Answer dekho

PS II = P680, PS I = P700 (apni best absorption wavelength pe naam).

Q5. Calvin cycle me pehla stable product kya hai?
Answer dekho

3-PGA (3-phosphoglycerate, 3 carbon) — isliye isse 'C3 cycle' kehte hain. RuBP (5C) + CO2 → 2 × 3-PGA, enzyme = RuBisCO.

Q6. Ek glucose banane me kitne ATP aur NADPH lagte hain?
Answer dekho

18 ATP + 12 NADPH (6 CO2 fix karne ke liye — Calvin cycle ke 6 turns).

Q7. C4 plants ka pehla product aur special enzyme kya hai?
Answer dekho

Pehla product = OAA (oxaloacetic acid, 4C); enzyme = PEP carboxylase (PEPcase) — ye O2 bind nahi karta, isliye photorespiration nahi hoti!

Q8. Kranz anatomy kya hai?
Answer dekho

C4 plants ki leaf ka special structure — bundle sheath cells ke around mesophyll ki ring. CO2 mesophyll me fix hoti hai, Calvin cycle bundle sheath me chalti hai.

Q9. Photorespiration kya hai aur ye C4 plants me kyun nahi hoti?
Answer dekho

C3 me jab RuBisCO O2 bind kar leta hai to phosphoglycolate banta hai — na sugar, na ATP = waste. C4 me CO2 RuBisCO ke around concentrated hai (PEPcase pehle pakad leta hai) — O2 ko mauka nahi milta!

Q10. Blackman ka Law of Limiting Factors kya kehta hai?
Answer dekho

Photosynthesis ki rate sabse kam available factor se decide hoti hai — light, CO2, temperature, water me jo sabse kam ho, wahi bottleneck!
