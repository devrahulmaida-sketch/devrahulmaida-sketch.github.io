1️⃣ Classification Ka Basis — Levels, Symmetry, Germ Layers ⭐

  • Levels of organisation ⭐: Cellular (Porifera — cells loose, tissue nahi), Tissue (Coelenterata/Cnidaria + Ctenophora), Organ (Platyhelminthes), Organ-system (Annelida se upar sab).
  • Symmetry ⭐⭐: Asymmetrical (Sponges/Porifera — koi plane nahi!), Radial (Coelenterata, Ctenophora, adult Echinodermata — koi bhi plane centre se kaato to 2 equal halves), Bilateral (Platyhelminthes se Chordata tak — SIRF ek plane se 2 halves, left/right mirror).
  • Germ layers ⭐⭐: Diploblastic — 2 layers (ectoderm + endoderm), beech me non-cellular mesoglea (Coelenterata, Ctenophora); Triploblastic — 3 layers (+ mesoderm) (Platyhelminthes se Chordata tak).
  • Coelom (body cavity) ⭐⭐: mesoderm-lined cavity. Acoelomates — cavity nahi (Platyhelminthes); Pseudocoelomates — mesoderm pouches ka jhootha coelom (Aschelminthes/Roundworms); Coelomates/Eucoelomates — true coelom (Annelida, Mollusca, Arthropoda, Echinodermata, Hemichordata, Chordata).
  • Segmentation ⭐: true segmentation (metameric) sirf Annelida, Arthropoda, Chordata me — body similar segments ki series.
  • Notochord ⭐: mesodermally derived rod-like structure — chordates me (embryonic); non-chordates me absent.

💡 Feel: ye 6 characters = animal kingdom ki ATM PIN — inhe yaad kiya to poora chapter khol jaayega!

2️⃣ Porifera Se Platyhelminthes — Lower Invertebrates ⭐

  • Porifera (Sponges) ⭐: cellular level, asymmetrical, aquatic (mostly marine), sessile (attached). Body me pores (ostia) + water canal system (canal system ⭐); choanocytes (collar cells) se water current. Skeleton: spicules (CaCO3/SiO2) ya spongin fibres. Hermaphrodite; asexual = fragmentation/gemmules, sexual bhi. Larva = motile! Examples: Sycon (Scypha), Spongilla (freshwater), Euspongia (bath sponge).
  • Coelenterata/Cnidaria ⭐⭐: tissue level, radial symmetry, diploblastic, aquatic (mostly marine). Cnidoblasts/stinging cells (tentacles pe — defence + prey capture, naam yahi se!). Polymorphism ⭐: 2 body forms — polyp (sessile: Hydra, Adamsia) aur medusa (free-swimming: Aurelia/jellyfish). Digestion extracellular + intracellular dono. Examples: Hydra, Obelia, Physalia (Portuguese man-of-war), Adamsia (sea anemone), Aurelia, Corals (Meandrina — brain coral!).
  • Ctenophora (Comb jellies) ⭐: marine, radial, diploblastic. 8 comb plates (ciliary plates) ⭐ locomotion ke liye — isliye 'sea walnuts/comb jellies'. Bioluminescence ⭐ (light emit karte hain!) — unique property. Cnidoblasts NAHI (Cnida se difference!). Examples: Pleurobrachia, Ctenoplana.
  • Platyhelminthes (Flatworms) ⭐⭐: bilateral symmetry, triploblastic, acoelomate, organ level. Dorsoventrally FLATTENED body (naam = platy=flat, helminth=worm). Mostly parasitic (endoparasites); hooks/suckers present. Flame cells ⭐ = excretion + osmoregulation. Hermaphrodite. Examples: Taenia (tapeworm), Fasciola (liver fluke), Planaria.

🎯 Quick traps: Ctenophora me bioluminescence + comb plates par cnidoblasts nahi; Platy me flame cells; Porifera me canal system!

3️⃣ Aschelminthes, Annelida, Arthropoda ⭐

  • Aschelminthes (Roundworms) ⭐: bilateral, triploblastic, pseudocoelomate, organ-system level. Body cylindrical/round cross-section (naam roundworm); complete digestive tract (mouth + anus). Parasitic mostly; dioecious (sexes separate), females longer than males. Examples: Ascaris, Wuchereria (filaria — elephantiasis!), Ancylostoma (hookworm), Enterobius (pinworm).
  • Annelida (Segmented worms) ⭐⭐: bilateral, triploblastic, coelomate (eucoelomate), organ-system. Metameric segmentation ⭐ — body true segments me (first phylum with this!). Aquatic + terrestrial. Setae/parapodia ⭐ locomotion ke liye. Closed circulatory system. Nephridia excretion. Examples: Nereis (aquatic, parapodia), Pheretima (earthworm), Hirudinaria (leech).
  • Arthropoda ⭐⭐: LARGEST phylum (2/3 of all species — insects included!). Bilateral, triploblastic, coelomate. Jointed appendages ⭐ (naam = arthro+pod), chitinous exoskeleton ⭐. Body: head, thorax, abdomen. Open circulatory system. Compound eyes + antennae. Respiration: gills/trachea/book lungs. Examples: Palaemon (prawn), cockroach, mosquitoes, housefly, spider, scorpion, crab, butterfly, Periplaneta.

💡 Annelida = segmented + setae; Arthropoda = jointed legs + chitin armour. Dono coelomate!

4️⃣ Mollusca, Echinodermata, Hemichordata ⭐

  • Mollusca ⭐: second largest phylum. Bilateral, triploblastic, coelomate. Soft body (naam = mollis=soft), usually calcareous shell; body = head + muscular foot + visceral hump; visceral hump ko mantle cover karta hai jo shell banata hai. Radula ⭐ = rasping feeding organ (rasping tongue). Respiration: gills. Examples: Pila (apple snail), Octopus, Sepia, Loligo, Pinctada (pearl oyster ⭐), Unio.
  • Echinodermata ⭐⭐: sirf marine! Adults radial symmetry (pentamerous — 5-part), larvae BILATERAL. Triploblastic, coelomate. Water vascular system ⭐⭐ — unique! Locomotion + food capture + respiration isse hota hai. Spiny skin (naam = echino=spiny, derma=skin). Calcareous ossicles endoskeleton. Regeneration power! Examples: Asterias (starfish), Echinus (sea urchin), Cucumaria (sea cucumber), Ophiura (brittle star), Antedon (sea lily).
  • Hemichordata ⭐: marine, worm-like, bilateral, triploblastic, coelomate. Body = proboscis + collar + trunk. Open circulatory system, respiration by gills. Excretion: proboscis gland. Sexes separate. Development indirect. Examples: Balanoglossus, Saccoglossus. (Ye 'connecting link' hai non-chordates aur chordates ke beech!)

🎯 NEET specials: Echinodermata = water vascular system + spiny skin; Mollusca = radula + mantle; Hemichordata = proboscis-collar-trunk!

5️⃣ Chordata — Vertebrates Ka Complete Map ⭐⭐

  • Chordata ke 3 fundamental characters ⭐⭐: Notochord (flexible rod), dorsal hollow nerve cord, paired pharyngeal gill slits — kisi na kisi life stage me zaroor! Plus: bilateral, triploblastic, coelomate, post-anal tail, closed circulatory, ventral heart.
  • 3 sub-phyla ⭐: Urochordata (notochord sirf larval tail me — Ascidia), Cephalochordata (notochord head se tail tak, life-long — Amphioxus/Branchiostoma), Vertebrata (notochord replace hoke vertebral column banta hai).
  • Vertebrata ke classes ⭐⭐: Cyclostomata (jawless fishes — sucking circular mouth, scales nahi, 6-15 gill slits; Petromyzon/lamprey, Myxine/hagfish — ectoparasites on fishes!). Chondrichthyes (cartilaginous fishes — cartilage skeleton, placoid scales, ventral mouth, 5-7 gill slits, operculum NAHI, air bladder nahi so constantly swim; Scoliodon/dogfish, sting ray, electric ray/Torpedo). Osteichthyes (bony fishes — bone skeleton, cycloid/ctenoid scales, terminal mouth, 4 gill pairs + operculum, air bladder present; marine = Hippocampus/sea horse, flying fish; freshwater = Labeo/rohu, Catla; aquarium = Betta, Pterophyllum/angelfish).
  • Amphibia ⭐: land + water dono (naam!), moist skin (scales nahi), 2 pairs limbs, 3-chambered heart (2 auricle + 1 ventricle), cold-blooded, gills (larva) → lungs (adult), external fertilization, indirect development. Examples: Rana (frog), Bufo (toad), Hyla (tree frog), Salamandra, Ichthyophis (limbless!).
  • Reptilia ⭐: creeping/crawling, dry cornified skin with scales/scutes, 3-chambered heart (Crocodilus me 4-chambered!), cold-blooded (poikilotherms), internal fertilization, terrestrial eggs (calcareous shell). Examples: Chelone (turtle), Chameleon, Calotes (garden lizard), Crocodilus, Hemidactylus (wall lizard), Naja (cobra), Bangarus (krait), Vipera.
  • Aves ⭐: feathers!, forelimbs = wings, hollow (pneumatic) bones, 4-chambered heart, WARM-blooded (first class with mammals!), beak (teeth nahi), air sacs (double respiration), oviparous. Examples: Corvus (crow), Columba (pigeon), Pavo (peacock), Struthio (ostrich), Psittacula (parrot), Neophron (vulture).
  • Mammalia ⭐: mammary glands (milk!), hair, external ears (pinnae), 4-chambered heart, warm-blooded, 2 pairs limbs (adapted), viviparous mostly (EXCEPTIONS: Ornithorhynchus/platypus + Echidna = egg-laying mammals ⭐). Examples: humans, kangaroo, Rattus, Felis (cat), Canis (dog), Panthera (lion/tiger), Pteropus (flying fox/bat), Balaenoptera (blue whale), Macropus (kangaroo), Equus (horse), Delphinus (dolphin).

🎯 Heart chambers: fish 2 → amphibian 3 → reptile 3 (crocodile 4!) → aves/mammal 4. Aur platypus = egg-laying mammal — ye 2 exam ke darling hain!

1️⃣ वर्गीकरण का आधार — स्तर, सममिति, जर्म परतें ⭐

  • संगठन स्तर ⭐: कोशिकीय (पोरिफेरा), ऊतकीय (सीलेंट्रेटा + टीनोफोरा), अंगीय (प्लेटीहेल्मिंथीज़), अंग-तंत्रीय (ऐनेलिडा से ऊपर)।
  • सममिति ⭐⭐: असममित (स्पंज), अरीय (सीलेंट्रेटा, टीनोफोरा, वयस्क इकाइनोडर्मेटा), द्विपार्श्वीय (प्लेटीहेल्मिंथीज़ से कॉर्डेटा तक)।
  • जर्म परतें ⭐⭐: द्विकोरकीय — 2 परतें + मध्य में मीज़ोग्लिया (सीलेंट्रेटा, टीनोफोरा); त्रिकोरकीय — 3 परतें (प्लेटीहेल्मिंथीज़ से कॉर्डेटा तक)।
  • गुहा (coelom) ⭐⭐: अगुहीय — प्लेटीहेल्मिंथीज़; कूटगुहीय — ऐस्केल्मिंथीज़; सगुहीय — ऐनेलिडा से कॉर्डेटा।
  • खंडीभवन ⭐: सच्चा खंडीभवन केवल ऐनेलिडा, आर्थ्रोपोडा, कॉर्डेटा में।
  • पृष्ठरज्जु (notochord) ⭐: मीज़ोडर्म से बनी छड़ — कॉर्डेटा में, अकॉर्डेटा में अनुपस्थित।

💡 ये 6 लक्षण = सम्पूर्ण अध्याय की कुंजी!

2️⃣ पोरिफेरा से प्लेटीहेल्मिंथीज़ ⭐

  • पोरिफेरा (स्पंज) ⭐: कोशिकीय स्तर, असममित, जलीय (अधिकतर समुद्री), स्थावर। छिद्र (ओस्टिया) + प्रणाल तंत्र (canal system) ⭐; कॉलर कोशिकाएँ (कोएनोसाइट्स)। कंकाल: स्पिक्यूल/स्पंजिन। उभयलिंगी; अलैंगिक = खंडन/जेम्यूल। उदा: साइकॉन, स्पंजिला, यूस्पंजिया।
  • सीलेंट्रेटा/निडेरिया ⭐⭐: ऊतकीय स्तर, अरीय सममिति, द्विकोरकीय, जलीय। निडोब्लास्ट (डंक कोशिकाएँ) ⭐ — शिकार + रक्षा। बहुरूपता ⭐: पॉलिप (स्थावर — हाइड्रा) + मेड्यूसा (मुक्त-तैराक — जेलीफ़िश)। उदा: हाइड्रा, ओबेलिया, फ़िसेलिया, ऑरेलिया, प्रवाल (मिएंड्रिना)।
  • टीनोफोरा (कंघी-जेली) ⭐: समुद्री, अरीय, द्विकोरकीय। 8 कंघी प्लेटें (पक्ष्माभ पट्टिकाएँ) ⭐ गमन हेतु; जीव-दीप्ति (bioluminescence) ⭐! निडोब्लास्ट नहीं। उदा: प्लूरोब्रेकिया, टीनोप्लाना।
  • प्लेटीहेल्मिंथीज़ (चपटे कृमि) ⭐⭐: द्विपार्श्वीय, त्रिकोरकीय, अगुहीय, अंग स्तर। पृष्ठ-अधर समतल शरीर। अधिकतर परजीवी; पंख/चूषक उपस्थित। ज्वाला कोशिकाएँ ⭐ = उत्सर्जन + परासरण-नियमन। उभयलिंगी। उदा: टीनिया (फ़ीता कृमि), फ़ैसिओला (यकृत कृमि), प्लेनेरिया।

🎯 टीनोफोरा में जीव-दीप्ति + कंघी प्लेटें, पर निडोब्लास्ट नहीं — क्लासिक जाल!

3️⃣ ऐस्केल्मिंथीज़, ऐनेलिडा, आर्थ्रोपोडा ⭐

  • ऐस्केल्मिंथीज़ (गोल कृमि) ⭐: द्विपार्श्वीय, त्रिकोरकीय, कूटगुहीय, अंग-तंत्र स्तर। बेलनाकार शरीर; पूर्ण आहारनाल (मुख + गुदा)। अधिकतर परजीवी; लिंग पृथक, मादा नर से लंबी। उदा: ऐस्कारिस, वुचेरेरिया (फ़ाइलेरिया — हाथीपाँव!), ऐन्काइलोस्टोमा, एंटरोबियस।
  • ऐनेलिडा (खंडित कृमि) ⭐⭐: द्विपार्श्वीय, त्रिकोरकीय, सगुहीय, अंग-तंत्र। खंडीभवन ⭐ — शरीर सच्चे खंडों में (प्रथम संघ!)। जलीय + स्थलीय। शूलबाल/पैरापोडिया ⭐ गमन हेतु। बंद परिसंचरण तंत्र। उदा: नीरिस, केंचुआ (फ़ेरेटिमा), जोंक।
  • आर्थ्रोपोडा ⭐⭐: सबसे बड़ा संघ (कुल जातियों का 2/3!)। द्विपार्श्वीय, त्रिकोरकीय, सगुहीय। संधिग्राही उपांग ⭐, काइटिनी बहिःकंकाल ⭐। शरीर: शीर्ष, वक्ष, उदर। खुला परिसंचरण। संयुक्त नेत्र + शृंगिकाएँ। उदा: झींगा, तिलचट्टा, मच्छर, मक्खी, मकड़ी, बिच्छू, केकड़ा, तितली।

💡 ऐनेलिडा = खंड + शूलबाल; आर्थ्रोपोडा = संधिग्राही पैर + काइटिन कवच।

4️⃣ मोलस्का, इकाइनोडर्मेटा, हेमिकॉर्डेटा ⭐

  • मोलस्का ⭐: द्वितीय बृहत्तम संघ। द्विपार्श्वीय, त्रिकोरकीय, सगुहीय। कोमल शरीर, प्रायः चूना-खोल; शरीर = शीर्ष + पेशीय पद + आन्त्रकपुंज; मैंटल खोल बनाता है। रैड्युला ⭐ = रगड़ने वाला आहार अंग। उदा: पिला, ऑक्टोपस, सीपिया, लोलिगो, पिंक्टाडा (मोती सीप ⭐)।
  • इकाइनोडर्मेटा ⭐⭐: केवल समुद्री! वयस्क अरीय सममिति (पंच-अरीय), डिंभक द्विपार्श्वीय। त्रिकोरकीय, सगुहीय। जल-वाहिका तंत्र ⭐⭐ — अद्वितीय! गमन + आहार + श्वसन इसी से। कँटीली त्वचा; चूना-अस्थिकण अन्तःकंकाल; पुनरुद्भवन क्षमता! उदा: ऐस्टेरियस (तारा-मीन), इकाइनस (समुद्री काँटेदार), समुद्री ककड़ी, ऑफ़िओरा, समुद्री लिली।
  • हेमिकॉर्डेटा ⭐: समुद्री, कृमि-सदृश, द्विपार्श्वीय, त्रिकोरकीय, सगुहीय। शरीर = प्रोबोसिस + कॉलर + त्रंक। खुला परिसंचरण, गिल्स से श्वसन। उदा: बैलेनोग्लॉसस। (अकॉर्डेटा-कॉर्डेटा की 'सेतु कड़ी'!)

🎯 इकाइनोडर्मेटा = जल-वाहिका तंत्र + कँटीली त्वचा; मोलस्का = रैड्युला + मैंटल!

5️⃣ कॉर्डेटा — कशेरुकियों का मानचित्र ⭐⭐

  • कॉर्डेटा के 3 मूल लक्षण ⭐⭐: पृष्ठरज्जु (notochord), पृष्ठीय खोखला तंत्रिका-रज्जु, युग्मित ग्रसनी गिल-छिद्र — किसी न किसी अवस्था में अवश्य! साथ में: द्विपार्श्वीय, त्रिकोरकीय, सगुहीय, गुदा-पश्च पूच्छ, बंद परिसंचरण, अधर हृदय।
  • 3 उप-संघ ⭐: यूरोकॉर्डेटा (पृष्ठरज्जु केवल डिंभक-पूच्छ में — ऐसीडिया), सिफैलोकॉर्डेटा (जीवनपर्यंत, शीर्ष से पूच्छ तक — ऐम्फ़िऑक्सस), वर्टीब्रेटा (पृष्ठरज्जु → कशेरुकदंड)।
  • वर्ग ⭐⭐: चक्रमुखी (Cyclostomata) — बेजबड़ी मछलियाँ, चूषण-मुख, स्केल नहीं; पेट्रोमाइज़ॉन, मिक्सीन। कॉन्ड्रिक्थीज़ — उपास्थि मछलियाँ, प्लैकॉइड स्केल, वेंट्रल मुख, ऑपरकुलम नहीं, वायु-थैली नहीं (निरंतर तैरना!); कुत्ता-मछली (स्कोलिओडॉन), स्टिंग रे। ऑस्टिक्थीज़ — अस्थि मछलियाँ, चक्रीय/कांटेदार स्केल, ऑपरकुलम उपस्थित, वायु-थैली; हिप्पोकैम्पस (समुद्री घोड़ा), लेबियो (रोहू), कतला, ऐंजलफ़िश।
  • उभयचर ⭐: स्थल + जल, नम त्वचा (स्केल नहीं), 2 जोड़ी पैर, 3-कक्षीय हृदय, शीतरक्ती, बाह्य निषेचन। उदा: मेंढ़क (राना), ब्यूफ़ो, सैलामैंडर, इच्थियोफ़िस।
  • सरीसृप ⭐: रेंगने वाले, शुष्क स्केलयुक्त त्वचा, 3-कक्षीय हृदय (मगरमच्छ में 4!), शीतरक्ती, आंतरिक निषेचन, कठोर खोल वाले अंडे। उदा: कछुआ, गिरगिट, मगरमच्छ, कोबरा, करैत, वाइपर।
  • पक्षी ⭐: पंख!, अग्रपद = विंग्स, खोखली (वायुपूर्ण) हड्डियाँ, 4-कक्षीय हृदय, उष्णरक्ती, चोंच (दाँत नहीं), वायुकोश, अंडज। उदा: कौवा, कबूतर, मोर, शुतुरमुर्ग, तोता, गिद्ध।
  • स्तनधारी ⭐: स्तन ग्रंथियाँ, बाल, बाह्य कर्ण (पिन्ना), 4-कक्षीय हृदय, उष्णरक्ती, जरायुज (अपवाद: प्लैटिपस + इकिडना = अंडे देने वाले स्तनधारी ⭐)। उदा: मनुष्य, कंगारू, चूहा, बिल्ली, कुत्ता, शेर, चमगादड़, नील व्हेल, घोड़ा, डॉल्फ़िन।

🎯 हृदय-कक्ष: मछली 2 → उभयचर 3 → सरीसृप 3 (मगरमच्छ 4!) → पक्षी/स्तनधारी 4। प्लैटिपस = अंडज स्तनधारी!

1️⃣ Basis of Classification — Levels, Symmetry, Germ Layers ⭐

  • Levels of organisation ⭐: Cellular (Porifera), Tissue (Coelenterata + Ctenophora), Organ (Platyhelminthes), Organ-system (Annelida upwards).
  • Symmetry ⭐⭐: Asymmetrical (sponges), Radial (Coelenterata, Ctenophora, adult echinoderms — any plane through the centre gives 2 halves), Bilateral (Platyhelminthes → Chordata — only ONE plane divides into mirror halves).
  • Germ layers ⭐⭐: Diploblastic — 2 layers with non-cellular mesoglea between (Coelenterata, Ctenophora); Triploblastic — 3 layers (Platyhelminthes → Chordata).
  • Coelom ⭐⭐: mesoderm-lined body cavity. Acoelomates — none (Platyhelminthes); Pseudocoelomates — false coelom from mesoderm pouches (Aschelminthes); Coelomates — true coelom (Annelida → Chordata).
  • Segmentation ⭐: true metameric segmentation only in Annelida, Arthropoda, Chordata.
  • Notochord ⭐: mesodermally derived rod — present in chordates, absent in non-chordates.

💡 These 6 characters are the master key — learn them and the whole chapter unlocks!

2️⃣ Porifera to Platyhelminthes — Lower Invertebrates ⭐

  • Porifera (Sponges) ⭐: cellular level, asymmetrical, aquatic (mostly marine), sessile. Body has pores (ostia) with a water canal system ⭐; collar cells (choanocytes) drive water current. Skeleton: spicules or spongin. Hermaphrodite; asexual by fragmentation/gemmules. E.g. Sycon, Spongilla, Euspongia (bath sponge).
  • Coelenterata/Cnidaria ⭐⭐: tissue level, radial, diploblastic, aquatic. Cnidoblasts/stinging cells ⭐ on tentacles for defence and prey capture. Polymorphism ⭐: polyp (sessile — Hydra) and medusa (free-swimming — Aurelia). E.g. Hydra, Obelia, Physalia, Adamsia (sea anemone), Aurelia, corals (Meandrina).
  • Ctenophora (Comb jellies) ⭐: marine, radial, diploblastic. 8 comb plates (ciliary plates) ⭐ for locomotion; bioluminescence ⭐ — they emit light! No cnidoblasts (key difference from Cnidaria). E.g. Pleurobrachia, Ctenoplana.
  • Platyhelminthes (Flatworms) ⭐⭐: bilateral, triploblastic, acoelomate, organ level. Dorsoventrally flattened. Mostly parasitic with hooks/suckers. Flame cells ⭐ for excretion and osmoregulation. Hermaphrodite. E.g. Taenia (tapeworm), Fasciola (liver fluke), Planaria.

🎯 Quick traps: Ctenophora = comb plates + bioluminescence but NO cnidoblasts; Platy = flame cells; Porifera = canal system!

3️⃣ Aschelminthes, Annelida, Arthropoda ⭐

  • Aschelminthes (Roundworms) ⭐: bilateral, triploblastic, pseudocoelomate, organ-system level. Cylindrical body; complete digestive tract. Mostly parasitic; sexes separate (females longer than males). E.g. Ascaris, Wuchereria (filaria worm — elephantiasis!), Ancylostoma (hookworm), Enterobius (pinworm).
  • Annelida (Segmented worms) ⭐⭐: bilateral, triploblastic, coelomate, organ-system. Metameric segmentation ⭐ — first phylum with true segments! Setae/parapodia ⭐ for locomotion. Closed circulatory system. E.g. Nereis, Pheretima (earthworm), Hirudinaria (leech).
  • Arthropoda ⭐⭐: LARGEST phylum (2/3 of all species). Bilateral, triploblastic, coelomate. Jointed appendages ⭐, chitinous exoskeleton ⭐. Body: head, thorax, abdomen. Open circulation; compound eyes + antennae. E.g. prawn, cockroach, mosquito, housefly, spider, scorpion, crab, butterfly.

💡 Annelida = segments + setae; Arthropoda = jointed legs + chitin armour. Both coelomates!

4️⃣ Mollusca, Echinodermata, Hemichordata ⭐

  • Mollusca ⭐: second largest phylum. Bilateral, triploblastic, coelomate. Soft body, usually a calcareous shell; head + muscular foot + visceral hump covered by the mantle (makes the shell). Radula ⭐ = rasping feeding organ. E.g. Pila, Octopus, Sepia, Loligo, Pinctada (pearl oyster ⭐), Unio.
  • Echinodermata ⭐⭐: exclusively marine! Adults radially symmetrical (pentamerous), larvae bilateral. Triploblastic, coelomate. Water vascular system ⭐⭐ — unique! Drives locomotion, food capture, respiration. Spiny skin; calcareous ossicle skeleton; can regenerate! E.g. Asterias (starfish), Echinus (sea urchin), sea cucumber, brittle star, sea lily.
  • Hemichordata ⭐: marine, worm-like, bilateral, triploblastic, coelomate. Body = proboscis + collar + trunk. Open circulation, gill respiration. E.g. Balanoglossus, Saccoglossus. (The 'connecting link' between non-chordates and chordates!)

🎯 NEET specials: Echinodermata = water vascular system; Mollusca = radula + mantle; Hemichordata = proboscis-collar-trunk!

5️⃣ Chordata — The Complete Vertebrate Map ⭐⭐

  • 3 fundamental chordate characters ⭐⭐: Notochord, dorsal hollow nerve cord, paired pharyngeal gill slits — present at SOME life stage! Plus: bilateral, triploblastic, coelomate, post-anal tail, closed circulation, ventral heart.
  • 3 sub-phyla ⭐: Urochordata (notochord only in larval tail — Ascidia), Cephalochordata (notochord lifelong, head to tail — Amphioxus), Vertebrata (notochord replaced by vertebral column).
  • Vertebrate classes ⭐⭐: Cyclostomata (jawless fishes — sucking circular mouth, no scales; Petromyzon/lamprey, Myxine/hagfish). Chondrichthyes (cartilaginous fishes — cartilage skeleton, placoid scales, ventral mouth, no operculum, no air bladder so they swim constantly; dogfish, sting ray, Torpedo). Osteichthyes (bony fishes — bone skeleton, cycloid/ctenoid scales, operculum present, air bladder; sea horse, rohu, Catla, angelfish, flying fish).
  • Amphibia ⭐: land + water, moist scale-less skin, 2 limb pairs, 3-chambered heart, cold-blooded, external fertilization, indirect development. E.g. Rana (frog), Bufo (toad), Hyla, Salamandra, Ichthyophis (limbless!).
  • Reptilia ⭐: crawling, dry cornified skin with scales, 3-chambered heart (4-chambered in crocodiles!), cold-blooded, internal fertilization, shelled terrestrial eggs. E.g. turtle, Chameleon, Calotes, crocodile, wall lizard, cobra, krait, viper.
  • Aves ⭐: feathers!, forelimbs as wings, hollow pneumatic bones, 4-chambered heart, warm-blooded, beak (no teeth), air sacs, oviparous. E.g. crow, pigeon, peacock, ostrich, parrot, vulture.
  • Mammalia ⭐: mammary glands, hair, external ear pinnae, 4-chambered heart, warm-blooded, mostly viviparous (EXCEPTIONS: platypus + Echidna lay eggs ⭐). E.g. humans, kangaroo, rat, cat, dog, lion, bat, blue whale, horse, dolphin.

🎯 Heart chambers: fish 2 → amphibian 3 → reptile 3 (crocodile 4!) → birds/mammals 4. Platypus = egg-laying mammal — both exam darlings!

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Diploblastic aur triploblastic animals me kya farak hai? Examples do.
Answer dekho

Diploblastic me 2 germ layers (ectoderm + endoderm, beech me mesoglea) — Coelenterata, Ctenophora. Triploblastic me 3 layers (mesoderm bhi) — Platyhelminthes se Chordata tak.

Q2. Ctenophora ko Cnidaria se alag karne wale 2 features batao.
Answer dekho

8 comb plates (ciliary plates) locomotion ke liye + bioluminescence (light emission). Aur Ctenophora me cnidoblasts nahi hote!

Q3. Acoelomate, pseudocoelomate aur coelomate — ek-ek example do.
Answer dekho

Acoelomate: Platyhelminthes (Taenia); Pseudocoelomate: Aschelminthes (Ascaris); Coelomate: Annelida (earthworm) aur upar ke phyla.

Q4. Arthropoda animal kingdom ka sabse bada phylum kyun hai? Iske 2 defining features batao.
Answer dekho

Kyunki isme 2/3 se zyada species hain (insects sahit). Features: jointed appendages + chitinous exoskeleton.

Q5. Echinodermata ka sabse unique feature kya hai aur ye kya karta hai?
Answer dekho

Water vascular system — ye locomotion, food capture aur respiration me madad karta hai. Ye sirf echinoderms me hota hai.

Q6. Adult echinoderms aur unke larvae ki symmetry me kya difference hai?
Answer dekho

Adults radially symmetrical (pentamerous), larvae bilaterally symmetrical.

Q7. Chordata ke 3 fundamental characters batao.
Answer dekho

Notochord, dorsal hollow nerve cord, paired pharyngeal gill slits — kisi na kisi life stage me present hote hain.

Q8. Crocodile ka heart baaki reptiles se alag kyun hai?
Answer dekho

Baaki reptiles me 3-chambered heart, par crocodile me 4-chambered — reptiles ka exception!

Q9. Chondrichthyes aur Osteichthyes me 3 differences batao.
Answer dekho

Skeleton: cartilage vs bone; Operculum: absent vs present; Air bladder: absent vs present (isliye cartilaginous fishes constantly swim karti hain).

Q10. Kaunse mammals eggs lay karte hain?
Answer dekho

Ornithorhynchus (platypus) aur Echidna — egg-laying mammals (monotremes). Baaki mammals viviparous hain.
