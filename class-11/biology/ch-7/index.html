🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Epithelial Tissue — Covering aur Lining ⭐⭐

  • Epithelium: body surfaces ko cover karne aur cavities/tubes ko line karne wala tissue. Cells tightly packed, very little intercellular material, basement membrane pe rest karti hain. Origin: teeno germ layers se.
  • Simple epithelium ⭐⭐ (single layer): Squamous — flat tiles jaisi cells; diffusion walls (alveoli, blood vessel walls/endothelium ⭐). Cuboidal — cube cells; secretion + absorption (kidney tubules, ducts of glands). Columnar — lambe pillar cells; absorption + secretion (stomach-intestine lining). Ciliated columnar/cuboidal — cilia se particles move (respiratory tract, oviducts!).
  • Compound/stratified epithelium ⭐: multi-layered — protection against wear and tear (skin, mouth, oesophagus lining — limited role in secretion/absorption).
  • Glandular epithelium ⭐: cuboidal/columnar cells glands banati hain — exocrine (ducts se secretion: mucus, saliva, earwax, milk, digestive enzymes) aur endocrine (ductless — hormones seedha blood me!).
  • Cell junctions ⭐: tight junctions (leakage rokte hain), adhering junctions (cells ko jodte hain — cementing), gap junctions (cells connect — communication/exchange of ions+signals!).

🎯 Squamous = flat (diffusion ke liye patli deewar — alveoli!); ciliated = jhaadu wali (respiratory tract). Endocrine = ductless!

2️⃣ Connective Tissue — Body Ka Filler aur Framework ⭐⭐

  • Connective tissue: sabse abundant + widely distributed. Cells DOOR-DOOR (unlike epithelium!), beech me matrix (ground substance) + fibres. Functions: linking, supporting, transport, defence.
  • Loose connective tissue ⭐: cells + fibres loose network me — areolar tissue (skin ke neeche, epithelium ko support; fibroblasts + macrophages + mast cells) aur adipose tissue ⭐ (fat storage — adipocytes; kidneys, heart ke around, blubber of whales — insulation!).
  • Dense connective tissue ⭐: fibres dense — regular (collagen parallel — tendons (muscle→bone ⭐) aur ligaments (bone→bone ⭐)!) aur irregular (bundles alag directions — skin dermis).
  • Specialised connective tissue ⭐⭐: Cartilage — solid + pliable, matrix me chondrocytes lacunae me; flexible (ear tip, nose tip, trachea rings, intervertebral discs ⭐). Bone — HARD matrix (calcium salts), osteocytes lacunae me; strong, non-pliable; marrow inside (blood cells banate hain!). Blood ⭐ — FLUID connective tissue! Plasma (matrix) me RBC (oxygen), WBC (immunity), platelets (clotting).
  • Yaad rakho ⭐: tendon = muscle to bone; ligament = bone to bone — DONO dense regular connective tissue!

💡 Blood bhi connective tissue hai! Matrix = plasma (liquid), cells = RBC/WBC/platelets. Exam twist favourite.

3️⃣ Muscle aur Neural Tissue ⭐⭐

  • Muscle tissue: contractile proteins (actin + myosin) — movement ka source. 3 types ⭐⭐:
  • Skeletal/striated muscle ⭐: voluntary (ichha se control), striations (alternate light-dark bands ⭐), long cylindrical multinucleate fibres, bones se attached (tendons via) — locomotion + posture.
  • Smooth/visceral muscle ⭐: involuntary, NON-striated, spindle-shaped (fusiform), uninucleate — blood vessels, stomach, intestine walls me; food transport, blood flow control.
  • Cardiac muscle ⭐⭐: SIRF heart me! Involuntary par STRIATED (combo!), branched fibres, intercalated discs ⭐ (communication junctions — cells ko sync me dhadkate hain!), uninucleate. Kabhi thakte nahi!
  • Neural tissue ⭐: neurons (functional unit) + neuroglia (support/protection — brain ke aadhe se zyada volume!). Neuron = cell body (cyton) — nucleus + cytoplasm; dendrites — chhote branches, signals RECEIVE; axon — lamba process, signals TRANSMIT away. Axons bundles = nerves.
  • Neuroglia ⭐: neurons ko protect + support + nourish karte hain; neural tube ke lining cells bhi (ependyma).

🎯 Cardiac muscle = striated + involuntary + intercalated discs — NEET ka all-time favourite combo question!

4️⃣ Cockroach — Morphology ⭐

  • Cockroach (Periplaneta americana) ⭐: brown/black, nocturnal, omnivorous; ~34-53 mm. Body covered by hard chitinous exoskeleton (sclerites se bana); body = head + thorax + abdomen.
  • Head ⭐: triangular, flexible neck se mobile; antennae (sensory), compound eyes, mouthparts (biting & chewing type — mandibles, maxillae, labium, labrum).
  • Thorax ⭐: 3 segments (pro/meso/metathorax); har segment pe 1 pair walking legs; 2 pairs wings — forewings (tegmina — opaque, leathery, protection) + hindwings (transparent, membranous — flying).
  • Abdomen ⭐: 10 segments; 10th segment pe 1 pair anal cerci (jointed filaments — sensory). Males me anal styli bhi (females me nahi — sex differentiate!).
  • Sexes: dioecious (alag); male thoda lamba; female me 7th-9th sterna milke genital pouch (brood pouch) — ootheca wahan rakhti hai!

💡 Anal cerci = dono me, anal styli = SIRF male me — sexing ka asaan trick!

5️⃣ Cockroach — Anatomy aur Life Cycle ⭐

  • Digestive system ⭐: foregut (mouth → pharynx → oesophagus → crop (storage) → gizzard/proventriculus (grinding — chitinous teeth!)), midgut (hepatic caeca se digestive enzymes, absorption), hindgut (ileum → colon → rectum). Malpighian tubules midgut-hindgut junction pe — excretion!
  • Circulatory system ⭐: OPEN type — blood vessels poori tarah se enclosed nahi; haemolymph colourless (respiratory pigment NAHI!), haemocoel me fill; heart = elongated muscular tube, 13-chambered funnel-shaped, dorsal.
  • Respiratory system ⭐: tracheal system — 10 pairs spiracles (body sides pe openings) → tracheae → tracheoles → har cell tak oxygen directly! Blood gas transport nahi karta.
  • Nervous + excretory: ganglia ka series (3 thoracic + 6 abdominal); mushroom bodies brain me (memory!); compound eyes = mosaic vision. Malpighian tubules ⭐ = excretory organ (uric acid — uricotelic!).
  • Reproduction ⭐: female me ovaries (2 ovaries × 8 ovarian tubules = 16 eggs per ootheca); male me testes + mushroom-shaped accessory gland. Fertilization INTERNAL. Female 9-10 oothecae ⭐ (egg cases — 14-16 eggs each!) produce karti hai; development paurometabolous (nymph stages — adults jaisi dikhti hain, sirf chhoti + wingless, moult karke badhti hain).
  • Vectors ⭐: cockroaches diseases ke carriers — contamination se food spoil, kuch pathogens spread (asthma triggers bhi!).

🎯 Ootheca = 14-16 eggs ka capsule; malpighian tubules = excretion; spiracles = breathing openings. Ye 3 naam pakka yaad rakho!

1️⃣ उपकला ऊतक — आवरण और अस्तर ⭐⭐

  • उपकला: शरीर की सतहें ढकने व गुहाओं/नलियों का अस्तर बनाने वाला ऊतक। कोशिकाएँ सँकर बँधी, आधार-झिल्ली पर स्थित।
  • सरल उपकला ⭐⭐ (एक परत): शल्की — चपटी टाइल-सी; विसरण-भित्तियाँ (वायुकोशिकाएँ, रक्तवाहिका-अस्तर/endothelium ⭐)। घनाभ — घन-सी; स्राव + अवशोषण (वृक्क-नलिकाएँ)। स्तंभाकार — लंबे स्तंभ; आहारनाल अस्तर। पक्ष्माभी — पक्ष्माभों से कण गति (श्वसन-मार्ग, अंडवाहिनी)।
  • संयुक्त/स्तरित उपकला ⭐: बहु-परतीय — घर्षण से सुरक्षा (त्वचा, मुख, ग्रासनली)।
  • ग्रंथीय उपकला ⭐: बहिःस्रावी (वाहिनियों द्वारा: लार, दूध, पाचक रस) + अन्तःस्रावी (वाहिनी-रहित — हॉर्मोन सीधे रक्त में!)।
  • कोशिका-संधियाँ ⭐: दृढ़ संधि (रिसाव रोक), संलग्न संधि (जोड़न), अंतराल संधि (संचार/आयन-विनिमय)।

🎯 शल्की = चपटी (विसरण हेतु पतली भित्ति); अन्तःस्रावी = वाहिनी-रहित!

2️⃣ संयोजी ऊतक — भराव और ढाँचा ⭐⭐

  • संयोजी ऊतक: सबसे प्रचुर + व्यापक वितरण। कोशिकाएँ दूर-दूर, बीच में मैट्रिक्स + रेशे। कार्य: जोड़न, आधार, परिवहन, रक्षा।
  • शिथिल संयोजी ⭐: अरिओलर ऊतक (त्वचा के नीचे, उपकला को आधार; फ़ाइब्रोब्लास्ट + मैक्रोफ़ेज + मास्ट कोशिकाएँ) + वसा ऊतक ⭐ (वसा-संचय — वृक्क/हृदय के चारों ओर, व्हेल-वसा = ऊष्मा-रोधन!)।
  • सघन संयोजी ⭐: नियमित (समांतर कॉलेजन — कंडरा/tendon (पेशी→अस्थि ⭐) + स्नायु/ligament (अस्थि→अस्थि ⭐)) + अनियमित (त्वचा-अधोचर्म)।
  • विशिष्ट संयोजी ⭐⭐: उपास्थि — ठोस + लचीली; कॉन्ड्रोसाइट्स लैक्यूना में (कान-नाक की टिप, ट्रेकिया वलय, अन्तःकशेरुकी डिस्क)। अस्थि — कठोर मैट्रिक्स (Ca-लवण), ऑस्टियोसाइट्स; भीतर मज्जा (रक्त-कोशिकाएँ बनाती है!)। रक्त ⭐ — तरल संयोजी ऊतक! प्लाज़्मा में RBC (ऑक्सीजन), WBC (प्रतिरक्षा), प्लेटलेट्स (थक्का)।

💡 रक्त भी संयोजी ऊतक! मैट्रिक्स = प्लाज़्मा, कोशिकाएँ = RBC/WBC/प्लेटलेट्स।

3️⃣ पेशी और तंत्रिका ऊतक ⭐⭐

  • पेशी ऊतक: संकुचनशील प्रोटीन (ऐक्टिन + मायोसिन) — गति का स्रोत। 3 प्रकार ⭐⭐:
  • कंकालीय/रेखित पेशी ⭐: ऐच्छिक, रेखाएँ (हल्की-गहरी पट्टियाँ ⭐), लंबी बेलनाकार बहुकेंद्रकी रेशे, अस्थियों से जुड़े।
  • चिकनी/आन्त्रिक पेशी ⭐: अनैच्छिक, अरेखित, वर्काकार, एक-केंद्रकी — रक्तवाहिकाएँ, आमाशय, आँतें।
  • हृदय पेशी ⭐⭐: केवल हृदय में! अनैच्छिक पर रेखित, शाखित रेशे, संधिस्थ चक्कियाँ (intercalated discs) ⭐, एक-केंद्रकी। कभी नहीं थकती!
  • तंत्रिका ऊतक ⭐: न्यूरॉन (कार्यात्मक इकाई) + न्यूरोग्लिया (सहायता/संरक्षण)। न्यूरॉन = काय (केंद्रक) + द्रुमिकाएँ (संकेत ग्रहण) + अक्षतंतु (संकेत संचारण दूर)।

🎯 हृदय पेशी = रेखित + अनैच्छिक + संधिस्थ चक्कियाँ — पक्का प्रश्न!

4️⃣ तिलचट्टा — बाह्य आकारिकी ⭐

  • तिलचट्टा (Periplaneta americana) ⭐: भूरा/काला, रात्रिचर, सर्वाहारी। कठोर काइटिनी बहिःकंकाल; शरीर = शीर्ष + वक्ष + उदर।
  • शीर्ष ⭐: त्रिभुजाकार, लचीली ग्रीवा; शृंगिकाएँ (संवेदी), संयुक्त नेत्र, मुखांग (चबाने वाले)।
  • वक्ष ⭐: 3 खंड; प्रत्येक पर 1 जोड़ी चलन-पाद; 2 जोड़ी पंख — अग्रपंख (टेग्मिना — अपारदर्शी, चमड़े-से, सुरक्षा) + पश्चपंख (पारदर्शी, झिल्लीदार — उड़ान)।
  • उदर ⭐: 10 खंड; 10वें खंड पर 1 जोड़ी गुद-शिरक (anal cerci)। नरों में गुद-शूलिका (anal styli) भी (मादा में नहीं!)।
  • लिंग: पृथक; मादा में 7-9वें स्टर्ना से जनन-थैली (ऊथेका रखने हेतु)।

💡 गुद-शिरक = दोनों में, गुद-शूलिका = केवल नर में!

5️⃣ तिलचट्टा — आंतरिक संरचना और जीवनचक्र ⭐

  • पाचन तंत्र ⭐: अग्रांत्र (मुख → ग्रसनी → ग्रासनली → क्रॉप (भंडारण) → भोजनालय/gizzard (पिसाई — काइटिनी दाँत!)), मध्यांत्र (यकृत-अंगुलिकाओं से रस, अवशोषण), पश्चांत्र। मैल्पीघीय नलिकाएँ = उत्सर्जन!
  • परिसंचरण ⭐: खुला प्रकार — रक्त वाहिकाओं में पूर्णतः सीमित नहीं; हीमोलिम्फ रंगहीन (श्वसन-वर्णक नहीं!); हृदय = 13-कक्षीय नलिका, पृष्ठीय।
  • श्वसन ⭐: श्वासनल-तंत्र — 10 जोड़ी श्वासरंध्र (spiracles) → श्वासनलियाँ → हर कोशिका तक सीधे ऑक्सीजन!
  • तंत्रिका + उत्सर्जन: पर्विकाओं (गैंग्लिया) की शृंखला; मैल्पीघीय नलिकाएँ ⭐ = उत्सर्जन अंग (यूरिक अम्ल — यूरिकोटेलिक!)।
  • जनन ⭐: मादा में अंडाशय (16 अंडे प्रति ऊथेका); निषेचन आंतरिक। मादा 9-10 ऊथेका ⭐ (अंड-संपुट — 14-16 अंडे!) बनाती है; विकास पौरोमेटाबोलस (निम्फ़ — वयस्क-सदृश, पंखहीन, मुकुलन द्वारा वृद्धि)।

🎯 ऊथेका = 14-16 अंडों का संपुट; मैल्पीघीय नलिकाएँ = उत्सर्जन; श्वासरंध्र = श्वसन-द्वार।

1️⃣ Epithelial Tissue — Covering and Lining ⭐⭐

  • Epithelium: covers body surfaces and lines cavities and tubes. Tightly packed cells on a basement membrane; very little intercellular material.
  • Simple epithelium ⭐⭐ (single layer): Squamous — flat tile-like cells; diffusion surfaces (lung alveoli, blood vessel endothelium ⭐). Cuboidal — cube cells; secretion and absorption (kidney tubules, gland ducts). Columnar — tall pillars (stomach-intestine lining). Ciliated — cilia move particles (respiratory tract, oviducts!).
  • Compound/stratified epithelium ⭐: multi-layered — protection against wear and tear (skin, mouth, oesophagus).
  • Glandular epithelium ⭐: forms glands — exocrine (secrete through ducts: mucus, saliva, milk, enzymes) and endocrine (ductless — hormones straight into blood!).
  • Cell junctions ⭐: tight junctions (stop leaking), adhering junctions (cement cells together), gap junctions (connect cells — ion and signal exchange!).

🎯 Squamous = flat (thin walls for diffusion — alveoli!); endocrine = ductless!

2️⃣ Connective Tissue — The Body's Filler and Framework ⭐⭐

  • Connective tissue: most abundant and widely distributed. Cells far apart with a matrix + fibres between them. Functions: linking, support, transport, defence.
  • Loose connective tissue ⭐: areolar tissue (beneath skin, supports epithelium; fibroblasts + macrophages + mast cells) and adipose tissue ⭐ (fat storage — around kidneys/heart, whale blubber for insulation!).
  • Dense connective tissue ⭐: regular (parallel collagen — tendons: muscle→bone ⭐ and ligaments: bone→bone ⭐) and irregular (skin dermis).
  • Specialised connective tissue ⭐⭐: Cartilage — solid but pliable, chondrocytes in lacunae (ear tip, nose tip, trachea rings, intervertebral discs). Bone — hard calcium matrix, osteocytes; marrow makes blood cells! Blood ⭐ — a FLUID connective tissue! Plasma matrix carrying RBC (oxygen), WBC (immunity), platelets (clotting).

💡 Blood is connective tissue too! Matrix = plasma, cells = RBC/WBC/platelets. An exam-favourite twist.

3️⃣ Muscle and Neural Tissue ⭐⭐

  • Muscle tissue: contractile proteins (actin + myosin) drive movement. Three types ⭐⭐:
  • Skeletal/striated muscle ⭐: voluntary, striated (light-dark bands ⭐), long cylindrical multinucleate fibres, attached to bones.
  • Smooth/visceral muscle ⭐: involuntary, non-striated, spindle-shaped, uninucleate — walls of blood vessels, stomach, intestine.
  • Cardiac muscle ⭐⭐: heart ONLY! Involuntary but STRIATED, branched fibres, intercalated discs ⭐ (sync the heartbeat!), uninucleate. Never tires!
  • Neural tissue ⭐: neurons (functional units) + neuroglia (support and protection — over half the brain's volume!). Neuron = cell body + dendrites (receive signals) + axon (transmits signals away).

🎯 Cardiac muscle = striated + involuntary + intercalated discs — the all-time favourite combo question!

4️⃣ Cockroach — Morphology ⭐

  • Cockroach (Periplaneta americana) ⭐: brown/black, nocturnal, omnivorous. Hard chitinous exoskeleton; body = head + thorax + abdomen.
  • Head ⭐: triangular, mobile neck; antennae (sensory), compound eyes, biting-and-chewing mouthparts.
  • Thorax ⭐: 3 segments, each with a pair of walking legs; 2 wing pairs — forewings (tegmina — opaque, leathery, protective) + hindwings (transparent, membranous — for flying).
  • Abdomen ⭐: 10 segments; the 10th bears a pair of anal cerci (sensory filaments). Males also have anal styli (females don't — easy sexing!).
  • Sexes: separate (dioecious); females have a genital pouch (from 7th-9th sterna) for carrying the ootheca.

💡 Anal cerci = both sexes, anal styli = males only!

5️⃣ Cockroach — Anatomy and Life Cycle ⭐

  • Digestive system ⭐: foregut (mouth → pharynx → oesophagus → crop (storage) → gizzard (grinding with chitinous teeth!)), midgut (hepatic caeca add enzymes; absorption), hindgut (ileum → colon → rectum).
  • Circulatory system ⭐: OPEN type — colourless haemolymph (no respiratory pigment!) fills the haemocoel; the heart is a 13-chambered dorsal tube.
  • Respiratory system ⭐: tracheal system — 10 pairs of spiracles → tracheae → tracheoles deliver oxygen DIRECTLY to every cell!
  • Nervous + excretory: chain of ganglia; Malpighian tubules ⭐ = excretory organs (uric acid — cockroaches are uricotelic!).
  • Reproduction ⭐: internal fertilization; females produce 9-10 oothecae ⭐ (egg cases of 14-16 eggs each!); development is paurometabolous — nymphs look like small wingless adults and moult as they grow.

🎯 Ootheca = capsule of 14-16 eggs; Malpighian tubules = excretion; spiracles = breathing pores!

  • गुद-शिरक दोनों, शूलिका केवल नर

तिलचट्टा तंत्र ⭐

  • खुला परिसंचरण, रंगहीन हीमोलिम्फ
  • श्वासनल + 10 श्वासरंध्र
  • ऊथेका: 14-16 अंडे

Epithelium ⭐

  • Squamous: flat, alveoli
  • Columnar: stomach/intestine
  • Endocrine glands: ductless

Connective ⭐⭐

  • Tendon: muscle→bone; ligament: bone→bone
  • Cartilage: flexible; bone: hard (Ca)
  • Blood = fluid connective tissue!

Muscle/Neural ⭐⭐

  • Skeletal: voluntary+striated
  • Smooth: involuntary, non-striated
  • Cardiac: striated+involuntary, intercalated discs

Cockroach body ⭐

  • Head+thorax+abdomen; tegmina forewings
  • Anal cerci both, styli only male
  • 10 abdominal segments

Cockroach systems ⭐

  • Open circulation, colourless haemolymph
  • Trachea + 10 spiracles
  • Ootheca: 14-16 eggs

🏆 NEET Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Tendon aur ligament me kya farak hai?
Answer dekho

Tendon = muscle ko bone se jodta hai; ligament = bone ko bone se. Dono dense regular connective tissue hain (collagen fibres parallel).

Q2. Blood ko connective tissue kyun maana jaata hai?
Answer dekho

Kyunki isme bhi cells + matrix ka combo hai — matrix = plasma (fluid), cells = RBC/WBC/platelets. Linking + transport ka kaam karta hai.

Q3. Cardiac muscle ki 3 khas pehchaan batao.
Answer dekho

Striated + involuntary + branched fibres with intercalated discs. Sirf heart me hoti hai aur kabhi fatigue nahi hoti!

Q4. Squamous epithelium kahan milti hai aur kyun?
Answer dekho

Lung alveoli aur blood vessels ki lining me — flat hone ki wajah se ye diffusion ke liye patli deewar banati hai.

Q5. Exocrine aur endocrine glands me difference kya hai?
Answer dekho

Exocrine glands ducts ke through secretion deti hain (saliva, enzymes); endocrine ductless hain — hormones seedha blood me (thyroid, pituitary).

Q6. Cockroach ke forewings ko kya kehte hain aur unka kaam kya hai?
Answer dekho

Tegmina — opaque, dark, leathery wings jo hindwings ko protect karti hain (flying me hindwings use hoti hain).

Q7. Male aur female cockroach ko externally kaise differentiate karte hain?
Answer dekho

Male me anal styli hote hain (10th segment pe), female me nahi. Anal cerci dono me hote hain.

Q8. Cockroach ka respiratory system kaise kaam karta hai?
Answer dekho

10 pairs spiracles → tracheae → tracheoles se oxygen seedha har cell tak — blood gas transport me involved nahi (isliye haemolymph colourless!).

Q9. Ootheca kya hai?
Answer dekho

Cockroach ka egg case/capsule jisme 14-16 eggs hote hain. Female ise genital pouch me carry karti hai aur 9-10 oothecae deti hai.

Q10. Gap junctions ka kya kaam hai?
Answer dekho

Ye adjacent cells ko connect karke ions, small molecules aur signals ka exchange karwate hain — rapid communication (jaise cardiac cells me sync).
