🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Growth Basics — Measurement aur Phases ⭐

  • Growth: irreversible permanent increase in size — plants me cell division (meristems) + cell elongation + cell maturation se hoti hai. Plants ki growth open/indeterminate hai — zindagi bhar hoti rehti hai (meristems ki wajah se)!
  • Growth ke phases ⭐⭐: Meristematic — root/shoot apex ki cells constantly divide (rich in protoplasm, large nuclei, thin walls!); Elongation — cells lambe hote hain (vacuoles bade, walls stretch!); Maturation — cells final size/shape + specialized (thick walls — jaise tracheids!).
  • Growth rate ⭐: Arithmetic growth — ek daughter cell divide karti hai, dusri differentiate (linear — root elongation; curve = straight line!); Geometric growth ⭐ — dono cells divide karti rehti hain (exponential — embryo/young plant; sigmoid curve jab slow-fast-slow ho!).
  • Sigmoid growth curve ⭐⭐: 3 phases — lag phase (slow start), log/exponential phase (rapid!), stationary phase (plateau — resources limited). Most organisms/organs me yehi curve!
  • Growth conditions: water (turgidity + enzymes!), oxygen (respiration/energy), nutrients (raw materials), temperature, light, gravity.

💡 Sigmoid curve = S-shape: slow → rocket → plateau. Lag me engine warm, log me full speed, stationary me brake!

2️⃣ Differentiation, Dedifferentiation, Redifferentiation ⭐⭐

  • Differentiation ⭐: meristematic cells mature hoke specialized functions lene lagti hain — jaise tracheary elements (vessels/tracheids): cells elongate, walls lignified thicken, protoplasm lost (DEAD cells jo water conduct karein!). Structural + functional changes = differentiation.
  • Dedifferentiation ⭐⭐: mature/differentiated cells wapas divide karne ki capacity PA lein — jaise cork cambium aur interfascicular cambium ka banna (secondary growth ke time permanent cells wapas meristematic!) + wound healing!
  • Redifferentiation ⭐: dedifferentiated cells phir se mature hoke new specialized cells bana dein (cork cambium se cork cells!).
  • Sequence yaad karo ⭐: Meristem → differentiate → (dedifferentiate → divide) → redifferentiate. Plasticity = plants ki superpower!
  • Development: growth + differentiation ka sum — seed se plant tak ka poora journey (cell division → elongation → maturation → flowering → senescence).

🎯 Dedifferentiation = differentiated cell ka U-turn (cork cambium!) — NEET definition question pakka!

3️⃣ Plant Growth Regulators — Auxins aur Gibberellins ⭐⭐

  • Plant Growth Regulators (PGRs)/phytohormones: chhote molecules jo growth control karte hain. 2 types: growth promoters (auxins, gibberellins, cytokinins) aur growth inhibitors (abscisic acid, ethylene — ethylene dono roles!).
  • Auxins ⭐⭐: (IAA = indole-3-acetic acid — natural; NAA, 2,4-D = synthetic) — growing apices (shoot/root tips) me bante hain. Functions: apical dominance ⭐ (shoot tip grow, side buds suppressed — isliye tea gardens/hedges me tip nikaal dete hain — bushier!), phototropism (light ki taraf bending!), rooting (cuttings me roots — NAA!), parthenocarpy ⭐ (seedless fruits!), xylem differentiation, abscission rokna (fruit drop prevent).
  • Weedicide use ⭐⭐: 2,4-D = dicot weeds killer — cereal/monocot fields (wheat) me dicot weeds maar deta hai, crop bach jaati hai!
  • Gibberellins (GAs) ⭐⭐: (100+ types — GA1, GA2, GA3 = gibberellic acid; fungus Gibberella se discover!). Functions: stem elongation ⭐⭐ (bolting — rosette plants jaise cabbage/beet achanak lambe!), dwarf varieties me height badhana, seed germination (aleurone layer se amylase — sugar release!), grapes me size/shape improve, sugarcane me stem length (sugar yield!), parthenocarpy (grapes me seedless!), senescence delay.

🎯 Auxin = apical dominance + phototropism + 2,4-D weedicide; Gibberellin = bolting + seed germination + grape size. Dono promoters!

4️⃣ Cytokinins, Ethylene aur ABA ⭐⭐

  • Cytokinins ⭐⭐: (zeatin — corn kernals/coconut milk se!; kinetin = synthetic) — root apices + developing fruits/seeds me bante hain. Functions: cell division promote ⭐⭐ (naam hi 'cyto-kinesis'!), lateral buds grow (apical dominance ko counter!), senescence DELAY ⭐ (leaves ko fresh rakhte hain — Richmond-Lang effect!), adventitious shoot formation (tissue culture!), nutrient mobilization.
  • Ethylene ⭐⭐: sirf GASEOUS PGR! Ripening fruits + senescent tissues me banta hai. Functions: fruit ripening ⭐⭐ (commercial use — Ethephon spray se mangoes/tomatoes/citrus ripe!), senescence + abscission promote (leaves/flowers/fruits girna), apical hook formation (dicot seedlings — soil se nikalte waqt shoot protect!), feminization (cucumber me female flowers badhana!), horizontal growth/swelling of axis, respiration rate badhana (respiratory climactic!).
  • Abscisic acid (ABA) ⭐⭐: growth INHIBITOR — 'stress hormone' ⭐⭐. Functions: stomata CLOSE ⭐⭐ (water stress me — transpiration rokna!), seed/bud dormancy (seeds ko dormant rakhta hai — desert plants me rain aane pe ABA wash out → germination!), abscission, senescence promote, stress tolerance (salt/drought me protein synthesis).
  • Summary table ⭐: Auxin = dominance+rooting; GA = bolting+germination; Cytokinin = division+anti-ageing; Ethylene = ripening+fall; ABA = close stomata+dormancy.

💡 PGR team: 3 cheerleaders (auxin, GA, cytokinin — grow!) + 2 referees (ABA, ethylene — stop/shed/ripen). ABA = plant ka 'panic button'!

5️⃣ Photoperiodism aur Vernalisation ⭐⭐

  • Photoperiodism ⭐⭐: flowering pe day/night ki length ka effect! (Garner & Allard, 1920 — Maryland Mammoth tobacco pe discover.) 3 categories: Long-day plants (LDP) — critical duration se ZYADA light chahiye (wheat, oats, spinach!); Short-day plants (SDP) — critical se KAM light/zyada darkness (rice, tobacco, chrysanthemum!); Day-neutral plants — day length se farak nahi (tomato, cotton, sunflower!).
  • Critical duration ⭐: wo day-length jiske upar/neeche flowering trigger — har plant ki apni. SDP actually 'long-NIGHT' plants hain (dark period matter karta hai!).
  • Site of perception ⭐: light/dark stimulus LEAVES me detect hota hai — phir signal (florigen — hormonal!) flower buds tak jaata hai.
  • Vernalisation ⭐⭐: flowering ko promote karne ke liye COLD/LOW temperature treatment! (Winter varieties of wheat/rye/barley — seeds ko cold treat karo to spring me flower+fruit jaldi!) Examples: winter wheat, biennials (cabbage, carrot, sugarbeet — pehle saal grow, cold ke baad hi flower!).
  • Vernalisation mechanism: cold stimulus shoot apex/embryo pe — specific cold period ke baad reproductive phase ON. Agriculture me fayda: growing season short karna, flowering sync karna.

🎯 Photoperiodism = light ka timer (LDP/SDP/day-neutral); Vernalisation = thandi ka kickstart (winter wheat, biennials). Dono flowering ke switches!

1️⃣ वृद्धि की मूल बातें — मापन और चरण ⭐

  • वृद्धि: आकार में अपरिवर्तनीय स्थायी वृद्धि — कोशिका-विभाजन + विस्तार + परिपक्वता से। पादपों की वृद्धि अनिश्चित/खुली — जीवनभर!
  • वृद्धि के चरण ⭐⭐: विभज्योतक (सतत विभाजन!), दीर्घीकरण (कोशिकाएँ लंबी — रिक्तिकाएँ बड़ी!), परिपक्वता (अंतिम आकार + विशेषीकरण)।
  • वृद्धि-दर ⭐: अंकगणितीय — एक कोशिका विभाजित, दूसरी विशेषीकृत (रेखीय!); ज्यामितीय ⭐ — दोनों विभाजित (घातांकीय!)।
  • सिग्मॉइड वक्र ⭐⭐: 3 चरण — मंडन (lag) (धीमा), लघुगणकीय (log) (तीव्र!), स्थिर (पठार)।
  • दशाएँ: जल, ऑक्सीजन, पोषक, तापमान, प्रकाश, गुरुत्व।

💡 सिग्मॉइड = S-आकार: धीमा → रॉकेट → पठार!

2️⃣ विशेषीकरण, अविशेषीकरण, पुनर्विशेषीकरण ⭐⭐

  • विशेषीकरण ⭐: विभज्योतक कोशिकाएँ परिपक्व होकर विशेष कार्य — जैसे वाह्य अवयव (मृत, लिग्निन-भित्ति!)।
  • अविशेषीकरण ⭐⭐: परिपक्व कोशिकाएँ पुनः विभाजन-क्षमता पाएँ — कॉर्क-कैम्बियम + अन्तःपूलीय कैम्बियम का बनना + क्षत-पूर्ति!
  • पुनर्विशेषीकरण ⭐: अविशेषीकृत कोशिकाएँ फिर नई विशेष कोशिकाएँ बनाएँ।
  • क्रम ⭐: विभज्योतक → विशेषीकरण → (अविशेषीकरण) → पुनर्विशेषीकरण। लचीलापन = पादप-शक्ति!
  • विकास: वृद्धि + विशेषीकरण का योग — बीज से पुष्पन तक।

🎯 अविशेषीकरण = परिपक्व कोशिका का U-टर्न (कॉर्क-कैम्बियम!) — पक्का प्रश्न!

3️⃣ पादप-वृद्धि नियामक — ऑक्सिन और जिब्बरेलिन ⭐⭐

  • PGR/फ़ाइटोहॉर्मोन: वृद्धि-नियंत्रक छोटे अणु। प्रवर्धक (ऑक्सिन, जिब्बरेलिन, साइटोकाइनिन) और संदमक (ABA, एथिलीन)।
  • ऑक्सिन ⭐⭐: (IAA — प्राकृतिक; NAA, 2,4-D — कृत्रिम) — वर्धमान शीर्षों में। कार्य: शीर्ष-प्रभुत्व ⭐ (पार्श्व-कलिकाएँ दबीं — चाय-बागों में टिप काटते हैं — घने पौधे!), प्रकाशानुक्रमण (प्रकाश की ओर झुकाव!), जड़न, अनिषेचितफल ⭐, फल-पतन रोक।
  • खरपतवारनाशी ⭐⭐: 2,4-D = द्विबीजपत्री खरपतवार-घातक — गेहूँ के खेत सुरक्षित!
  • जिब्बरेलिन ⭐⭐: (GA3; कवक जिब्बरेला से खोज!)। कार्य: तना-दीर्घीकरण ⭐⭐ (बोल्टिंग — गोभी/चुकंदर अचानक लंबे!), बीज-अंकुरण (एमाइलेज़ स्राव!), अंगूर में आकार, गन्ने में लंबाई, देर-जीर्णता।

🎯 ऑक्सिन = प्रभुत्व + 2,4-D; जिब्बरेलिन = बोल्टिंग + अंकुरण + अंगूर!

4️⃣ साइटोकाइनिन, एथिलीन और ABA ⭐⭐

  • साइटोकाइनिन ⭐⭐: (ज़ीएटिन — मक्का/नारियल-दूध; काइनेटिन — कृत्रिम) — मूल-शीर्ष + फलों में। कार्य: कोशिका-विभाजन ⭐⭐, पार्श्व-कलिका वृद्धि (प्रभुत्व-विरोधी!), जीर्णता-विलंब ⭐, कलिका-निर्माण।
  • एथिलीन ⭐⭐: एकमात्र गैसीय PGR! कार्य: फल-पकना ⭐⭐ (ईथेफ़ॉन — आम/टमाटर!), पतन (abscission) प्रवर्धन, शीर्ष-हुक (द्विबीजपत्री-अंकुर सुरक्षा!), खीरे में मादा-पुष्प वृद्धि।
  • एब्सिसिक अम्ल (ABA) ⭐⭐: वृद्धि-संदमक — 'तनाव-हॉर्मोन' ⭐⭐। कार्य: रंध्र-बंदन ⭐⭐ (जल-तनाव!), बीज/कलिका सुप्तावस्था (मरू पादप — वर्षा पर ABA धुलता → अंकुरण!), पतन, तनाव-सहिष्णुता।
  • सारांश ⭐: ऑक्सिन = प्रभुत्व+जड़न; GA = बोल्टिंग+अंकुरण; साइटोकाइनिन = विभाजन+युवावस्था; एथिलीन = पकना+पतन; ABA = रंध्र-बंदन+सुप्तता।

💡 3 प्रवर्धक (ऑक्सिन, GA, साइटोकाइनिन) + 2 नियंत्रक (ABA, एथिलीन)। ABA = पादप का 'पैनिक बटन'!

5️⃣ प्रकाश-अवधिवाद और वसंतीकरण ⭐⭐

  • प्रकाश-अवधिवाद ⭐⭐: पुष्पन पर दिन/रात की लंबाई का प्रभाव! (गार्नर और ऑलार्ड, 1920 — तम्बाकू पर!) 3 श्रेणियाँ: दीर्घ-दिवसीय (LDP) — क्रांतिक से अधिक प्रकाश (गेहूँ, जई, पालक!); अल्प-दिवसीय (SDP) — क्रांतिक से कम प्रकाश/अधिक अंधकार (चावल, तम्बाकू, गेंदा!); दिवस-तटस्थ — कोई फ़र्क़ नहीं (टमाटर, कपास, सूरजमुखी!)।
  • क्रांतिक-अवधि ⭐: वह दिन-लंबाई जिसके ऊपर/नीचे पुष्पन — SDP वास्तव में 'दीर्घ-रात्रि' पादप!
  • अनुभूति-स्थल ⭐: प्रकाश-संकेत पत्तियों में — फिर संकेत (फ़्लोरिजन!) कलिकाओं तक।
  • वसंतीकरण ⭐⭐: पुष्पन-प्रवर्धन हेतु शीत/न्यून-ताप उपचार! (शीतकालीन गेहूँ/जई — बीजों को शीत दो तो वसंत में शीघ्र पुष्पन!) उदा: शीतकालीन गेहूँ, द्विवार्षिक (गोभी, गाजर, चुकंदर)।

🎯 प्रकाश-अवधिवाद = प्रकाश का टाइमर; वसंतीकरण = ठंडे का किकस्टार्ट (शीतकालीन गेहूँ)!

1️⃣ Growth Basics — Measurement and Phases ⭐

  • Growth: an irreversible permanent increase in size — through cell division + elongation + maturation. Plant growth is open/indeterminate — it never stops!
  • Phases of growth ⭐⭐: Meristematic (constant division!), Elongation (cells lengthen — big vacuoles!), Maturation (final size + specialisation).
  • Growth rate ⭐: Arithmetic — one daughter cell divides, the other differentiates (linear); Geometric ⭐ — both keep dividing (exponential!).
  • Sigmoid growth curve ⭐⭐: 3 phases — lag (slow), log/exponential (rapid!), stationary (plateau).
  • Conditions: water, oxygen, nutrients, temperature, light, gravity.

💡 Sigmoid = S-shape: slow → rocket → plateau!

2️⃣ Differentiation, Dedifferentiation, Redifferentiation ⭐⭐

  • Differentiation ⭐: meristematic cells mature into specialised roles — like tracheary elements (dead, lignified cells conducting water!).
  • Dedifferentiation ⭐⭐: mature cells regain the power to divide — the formation of cork cambium and interfascicular cambium + wound healing!
  • Redifferentiation ⭐: dedifferentiated cells mature AGAIN into new specialised cells.
  • Sequence ⭐: meristem → differentiate → (dedifferentiate) → redifferentiate. Plasticity = the plant superpower!
  • Development: growth + differentiation — the whole journey from seed to flowering to ageing.

🎯 Dedifferentiation = a mature cell's U-turn (cork cambium!) — a guaranteed definition question!

3️⃣ Plant Growth Regulators — Auxins and Gibberellins ⭐⭐

  • PGRs/phytohormones: small molecules controlling growth. Promoters (auxins, gibberellins, cytokinins) and inhibitors (ABA, ethylene).
  • Auxins ⭐⭐: (IAA — natural; NAA, 2,4-D — synthetic) made in growing tips. Jobs: apical dominance ⭐ (side buds suppressed — so tea hedges are de-tipped for bushy growth!), phototropism (bending towards light!), rooting in cuttings, parthenocarpy ⭐ (seedless fruits!), preventing fruit drop.
  • Weedicide ⭐⭐: 2,4-D kills dicot weeds while cereal (monocot) crops stay safe!
  • Gibberellins (GAs) ⭐⭐: (GA3 — discovered from the fungus Gibberella!). Jobs: stem elongation ⭐⭐ (bolting — cabbage/beet shoot up suddenly!), seed germination (amylase release!), bigger grapes, longer sugarcane stems, delayed senescence.

🎯 Auxin = dominance + phototropism + 2,4-D; GA = bolting + germination + grapes!

4️⃣ Cytokinins, Ethylene and ABA ⭐⭐

  • Cytokinins ⭐⭐: (zeatin — from corn kernels and coconut milk; kinetin — synthetic) made in root tips and fruits. Jobs: cell division ⭐⭐, lateral bud growth (countering apical dominance!), delaying senescence ⭐, shoot formation in tissue culture.
  • Ethylene ⭐⭐: the only GASEOUS PGR! Jobs: fruit ripening ⭐⭐ (Ethephon sprays ripen mangoes and tomatoes commercially!), abscission (leaf/fruit fall), apical hook in dicot seedlings, more female flowers in cucumber.
  • Abscisic acid (ABA) ⭐⭐: the growth INHIBITOR — 'stress hormone' ⭐⭐. Jobs: closing stomata ⭐⭐ (in water stress!), seed/bud dormancy (desert seeds sprout when rain washes ABA out!), abscission, stress tolerance.
  • Summary ⭐: auxin = dominance + rooting; GA = bolting + germination; cytokinin = division + anti-ageing; ethylene = ripening + falling; ABA = closed stomata + dormancy.

💡 The PGR team: 3 cheerleaders (auxin, GA, cytokinin) + 2 referees (ABA, ethylene). ABA = the plant's panic button!

5️⃣ Photoperiodism and Vernalisation ⭐⭐

  • Photoperiodism ⭐⭐: the effect of day/night length on flowering! (Garner & Allard, 1920 — on tobacco.) Three groups: Long-day plants (LDP) — need MORE than critical light (wheat, oats, spinach!); Short-day plants (SDP) — need LESS light / longer darkness (rice, tobacco, chrysanthemum!); Day-neutral — unaffected (tomato, cotton, sunflower!).
  • Critical duration ⭐: the day-length threshold triggering flowering — SDPs are really 'long-NIGHT' plants!
  • Perception site ⭐: the light signal is detected by the LEAVES — then a signal (florigen) travels to the buds.
  • Vernalisation ⭐⭐: promoting flowering through COLD treatment! (Winter wheat/rye — cold-treated seeds flower early in spring!) Examples: winter wheat, biennials (cabbage, carrot, sugarbeet).

🎯 Photoperiodism = the light timer; vernalisation = the cold kickstart (winter wheat, biennials)!

  • पत्तियाँ प्रकाश-अवधि पहचानती हैं
  • वसंतीकरण: शीत → पुष्पन

Growth ⭐

  • 3 phases: meristem-elongation-maturation
  • Sigmoid: lag-log-stationary
  • Arithmetic vs geometric

Differentiation ⭐⭐

  • Dedifferentiation: mature → divide (cork cambium!)
  • Redifferentiation: again specialised
  • Plasticity = plant superpower

Auxin/GA ⭐⭐

  • Auxin: apical dominance, phototropism
  • 2,4-D = weedicide (dicot killer)
  • GA: bolting, germination, grape size

CK/ETH/ABA ⭐⭐

  • Cytokinin: cell division, anti-ageing
  • Ethylene: only GAS — ripening + abscission
  • ABA: stress hormone, closes stomata, dormancy

Photo/Vernal ⭐⭐

  • LDP: wheat; SDP: rice; neutral: tomato
  • Leaves detect photoperiod
  • Vernalisation: cold → flowering (winter wheat)

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Plant growth 'open/indeterminate' kyun kehlati hai?
Answer dekho

Kyunki plants me meristems zindagi bhar active rehte hain — division + elongation chalti rehti hai, koi fixed final size nahi hota!

Q2. Sigmoid growth curve ke 3 phases batao.
Answer dekho

Lag phase (slow start), log/exponential phase (rapid growth!), stationary phase (plateau — limited resources).

Q3. Dedifferentiation kya hai? Example do.
Answer dekho

Mature/differentiated cells ka wapas division power paana — jaise cork cambium aur interfascicular cambium ka banna (secondary growth me) + wound healing!

Q4. Auxin ke 3 agricultural uses batao.
Answer dekho

2,4-D weedicide (dicot weeds maarta, cereals safe!), rooting in cuttings (NAA), parthenocarpy (seedless fruits) + apical dominance/fruit drop control.

Q5. Gibberellin 'bolting' me kaise help karta hai?
Answer dekho

Rosette plants (cabbage, beet) me internodes achanak elongate karwata hai — plant flower ke liye lamba shoot nikalta hai. GA dwarf plants ki height bhi badhata hai!

Q6. Ethylene ke 2 commercial uses batao.
Answer dekho

Fruit ripening (Ethephon spray — mangoes, tomatoes, citrus artificially ripe!) aur cucumber me female flowers badhana (zyada fruits!).

Q7. ABA ko 'stress hormone' kyun kehte hain?
Answer dekho

Kyunki water stress me ye stomata CLOSE karwata hai (transpiration kam!) + seeds ko dormant rakhta hai + drought/salt tolerance badhata hai — har stress situation me ye activate!

Q8. Long-day aur short-day plants me farak batao (examples ke saath).
Answer dekho

LDP: critical duration se zyada light chahiye (wheat, oats, spinach); SDP: kam light/zyada darkness (rice, tobacco, chrysanthemum). Day-neutral: tomato, cotton.

Q9. Photoperiod ka stimulus plant me kahan detect hota hai?
Answer dekho

LEAVES me — phir flowering signal (florigen) buds tak travel karta hai.

Q10. Vernalisation kya hai? Example do.
Answer dekho

Low temperature/cold treatment se flowering promote karna — jaise winter wheat ke seeds ko cold treat karo to spring me jaldi flower+grain! Biennials (cabbage, carrot) bhi.
