1️⃣ Chemical Composition — Cell Me Kya Kya Hai ⭐

  • Do types ke analysis ⭐: living tissue ko grind karo (trichloroacetic acid me) — acid-soluble pool (chhote molecules: amino acids, sugars, nucleotides, lipids — 1000-5000 molecular weight, ~1000 types) aur acid-insoluble fraction (bade molecules: proteins, nucleic acids, polysaccharides — biomacromolecules!).
  • Average composition ⭐: water 70-90% (sabse zyada!), proteins 10-15%, nucleic acids 5-7%, carbohydrates 3%, lipids 2%, ions 1%.
  • Elements ⭐: C, H, O, N sabse zyada (~96%); phir P, S, Ca, K, Mg, Na; trace elements (Fe, Zn, Cu, I...) kam par zaroori!
  • Micromolecules vs macromolecules ⭐: micro = 18-800 Da (amino acids, sugars, lipids, nucleotides); macro = 10000+ Da (proteins, nucleic acids, polysaccharides — polymers!). Exception ⭐⭐: lipids macromolecule fraction me aate hain par polymer NAHI hain (800 Da ke neeche bhi ho sakte hain — cell membrane ke vesicles acid-insoluble ban jaate hain!).

💡 Cell = chemical soup: 70% paani, phir proteins. Lipid = macromolecule club ka non-polymer member — exam trap!

2️⃣ Carbohydrates aur Amino Acids/Proteins ⭐⭐

  • Carbohydrates ⭐: C, H, O (ratio 1:2:1 — hydrated carbon!). Monosaccharides: glucose, fructose, ribose (C5). Disaccharides: sucrose (glucose+fructose), maltose, lactose. Polysaccharides: monosaccharides ki long chains — starch (plant storage — iodine se BLUE colour! ⭐), glycogen (animal storage), cellulose (plant cell wall — glucose polymer par digest nahi hota humse!), chitin (fungi wall + arthropod exoskeleton — complex polysaccharide!).
  • Reducing vs non-reducing ⭐: reducing sugars (glucose, maltose, lactose — free aldehyde/ketone) vs non-reducing (sucrose!).
  • Amino acids ⭐⭐: 20 types protein me — har ek me amino group (NH2) + carboxyl group (COOH) + H + variable R group ek hi alpha-carbon pe. R group decide karta hai nature: acidic (glutamic acid), basic (lysine), neutral (valine), aromatic (tyrosine, phenylalanine, tryptophan!). Zwitterion form — ek hi molecule me + aur − dono charges!
  • Proteins ⭐⭐: amino acid POLYMERS — peptide bonds se jude (COOH ek ka + NH2 dusre ka — water nikalti hai, dehydration!). Proteins kaam: structure (collagen — sabse abundant protein in animals! ⭐), transport (haemoglobin), defence (antibodies), movement (actin/myosin), enzymes (almost sab!), hormones (insulin), receptors. RU-BisCO ⭐ = sabse abundant protein in the whole biosphere (photosynthesis enzyme — plants me)!
  • Protein structure levels ⭐: primary (amino acid sequence), secondary (helix/pleated sheet — hydrogen bonds), tertiary (3D folding — functional shape!), quaternary (multiple subunits — haemoglobin: 4 subunits!).

🎯 Collagen = animal kingdom ka #1 protein; RuBisCO = biosphere ka #1. Peptide bond = COOH+NH2 − H2O!

3️⃣ Lipids aur Nucleic Acids ⭐⭐

  • Lipids ⭐: water insoluble, fatty acids + glycerol se bane. Simple lipids (triglycerides): glycerol + 3 fatty acids (ester bonds). Fatty acids: saturated (no double bond — butter/ghee, solid) vs unsaturated (double bonds — oils, liquid; essential fatty acids body nahi bana sakti — diet me chahiye!).
  • Phospholipids ⭐: glycerol + 2 fatty acids + 1 phosphate group — cell membranes ka main material (lecithin example)! Head hydrophilic, tails hydrophobic — bilayer ban jaati hai paani me!
  • Nucleic acids ⭐⭐: nucleotide polymers — har nucleotide = nitrogenous base + pentose sugar + phosphate. Bases: purines (adenine, guanine — 2 rings) + pyrimidines (cytosine, thymine/uracil — 1 ring).
  • DNA ⭐⭐: double helix (Watson-Crick model!) — 2 antiparallel strands, sugar-phosphate backbone bahar, bases andar; base pairing: A=T (2 hydrogen bonds), G≡C (3 hydrogen bonds); deoxyribose sugar; thymine present (uracil nahi!).
  • RNA ⭐: usually single-stranded; ribose sugar; uracil (thymine ki jagah!); 3 main types: mRNA (template for protein), tRNA (adapter — amino acid laata hai, anticodon!), rRNA (ribosome structure + catalytic!).

🎯 A=T (2 bonds), G≡C (3 bonds) — GC zyada = zyada stable DNA! Phospholipid = membrane ka building block!

4️⃣ Enzymes — Biological Catalysts ⭐⭐

  • Enzymes ⭐⭐: almost sab proteins hain (exception: ribozymes — RNA enzymes! ⭐) jo reactions ki speed 1000s guna badhate hain BINa khud change hue. Har enzyme specific substrate pe kaam karta hai!
  • Kaise kaam karte hain ⭐: activation energy (Ea) GHATA dete hain — reaction ka 'hill' chhota! Enzyme + substrate → ES complex → enzyme + product. Enzyme change nahi hota, phir se use ho sakta hai!
  • Naming ⭐: substrate ke peechhe '-ase' — maltase (maltose), lipase (lipids), protease (proteins). International commission: 6 classes — oxidoreductases (oxidation-reduction), transferases (group transfer), hydrolases (water se todna), lyases, isomerases, ligases (jodna — ATP se!).
  • Cofactors ⭐⭐: protein part = apoenzyme; non-protein helper = cofactor; complete = holoenzyme. 3 types: prosthetic groups (tightly bound — haem in peroxidase/catalase), coenzymes (loosely attached, organic — NAD, NADP (vitamins se derived!)), metal ions (Zn2+ carboxypeptidase ke liye!).
  • Temperature + pH effect ⭐⭐: har enzyme ka optimum temperature (human ~37°C) aur optimum pH hota hai — deviation se activity girti hai, extreme pe denaturation (structure kharab — activity GONE!). Low temperature pe preserved rehte hain (isliye enzymes fridge me!).
  • Inhibition ⭐: competitive inhibitor — substrate jaisa dikhta hai, active site pe baith jaata hai (classic: malonate inhibits succinic dehydrogenase ⭐).

💡 Enzyme = smart jugaadu: pahad (activation energy) ko chhota karke reaction ko boost karta hai, khud thakta nahi!

5️⃣ Metabolism, Bonds aur Biomacromolecules Recap ⭐

  • Metabolites ⭐: primary metabolites — normal metabolism me directly involved (amino acids, sugars, lipids, nucleic acids — sab cells me same roles!); secondary metabolites ⭐ — specific plants/microbes me, ecological roles (alkaloids — morphine, codeine; flavonoids; rubber; essential oils — lemongrass; antibiotics; pigments; scents; toxins — abricin, ricin!).
  • Chemical bonds ⭐⭐: peptide bond (amino acids — proteins me), glycosidic bond (monosaccharides — polysaccharides me), phosphodiester bond ⭐ (nucleotides — nucleic acids me!), ester bond (fatty acid + glycerol — lipids me), hydrogen bond (DNA base pairs, protein structure — weak par bahut maayne rakhta hai!).
  • Metabolism: saari chemical reactions = anabolism (banana — energy use) + catabolism (todna — energy release). Metabolic pathways: series of enzyme reactions (glycolysis, Krebs...). Living state = steady state (non-equilibrium) — energy constantly invest hoti hai!
  • Catalysed reactions ⭐: enzyme-mediated reactions metabolic pathways me flow karti hain — jaise traffic flow, multi-step, har step pe apna enzyme!
  • Yaad rakhne ka master table ⭐: proteins = amino acids + peptide; carbs = sugars + glycosidic; nucleic acids = nucleotides + phosphodiester; lipids = fatty acids + glycerol + ester.

🎯 4 bonds yaad karo: peptide (protein), glycosidic (carb), phosphodiester (nucleic acid), ester (lipid). Har saal ek question pakka!

1️⃣ रासायनिक संगठन — कोशिका में क्या-क्या ⭐

  • दो प्रकार के विश्लेषण ⭐: ऊतक को पीसने पर — अम्ल-विलेय अंश (छोटे अणु: एमिनो अम्ल, शर्करा, न्यूक्लियोटाइड) और अम्ल-अविलेय अंश (बड़े अणु: प्रोटीन, न्यूक्लिक अम्ल, बहुशर्करा — जैव-विशालाणु!)।
  • औसत संगठन ⭐: जल 70-90% (सर्वाधिक!), प्रोटीन 10-15%, न्यूक्लिक अम्ल 5-7%, कार्बोहाइड्रेट 3%, लिपिड 2%, आयन 1%।
  • तत्त्व ⭐: C, H, O, N ~96%; फिर P, S, Ca, K; सूक्ष्म-तत्त्व (Fe, Zn, Cu, I) कम पर आवश्यक!
  • सूक्ष्माणु बनाम विशालाणु ⭐: सूक्ष्माणु = 18-800 Da; विशालाणु = 10000+ Da (बहुलक!)। अपवाद ⭐⭐: लिपिड विशालाणु-अंश में आते हैं परंतु बहुलक नहीं!

💡 कोशिका = रासायनिक सूप: 70% जल, फिर प्रोटीन। लिपिड = विशालाणु-क्लब का गैर-बहुलक सदस्य!

2️⃣ कार्बोहाइड्रेट और एमिनो अम्ल/प्रोटीन ⭐⭐

  • कार्बोहाइड्रेट ⭐: C, H, O (1:2:1)। मोनोसैकेराइड: ग्लूकोज़, फ्रक्टोज़, राइबोज़। डाइसैकेराइड: सूक्रोज़, माल्टोज़, लैक्टोज़। पॉलीसैकेराइड ⭐: स्टार्च (पादप-संचय — आयोडीन से नीला! ⭐), ग्लाइकोजन (प्राणी-संचय), सेलुलोज़ (भित्ति — हम पचा नहीं सकते!), काइटिन (कवक-भित्ति + आर्थ्रोपोड-कवच)।
  • अपचयी बनाम अनपचयी ⭐: अपचयी शर्करा (ग्लूकोज़, माल्टोज़) बनाम अनपचयी (सूक्रोज़!)।
  • एमिनो अम्ल ⭐⭐: 20 प्रकार — प्रत्येक में एमिनो (NH2) + कार्बोक्सिल (COOH) + H + R समूह एक ही α-कार्बन पर। R समूह प्रकृति तय करता है: अम्लीय (ग्लूटैमिक), क्षारीय (लाइसीन), उदासीन, ऐरोमैटिक।
  • प्रोटीन ⭐⭐: एमिनो-अम्ल बहुलक — पेप्टाइड बंध (COOH + NH2 − जल!)। कार्य: संरचना (कोलेजन — प्राणियों में सर्वाधिक प्रोटीन! ⭐), परिवहन (हीमोग्लोबिन), रक्षा (प्रतिरक्षी), एंज़ाइम, हॉर्मोन। RuBisCO ⭐ = सम्पूर्ण जैव-मंडल में सर्वाधिक प्रोटीन!
  • प्रोटीन संरचना ⭐: प्राथमिक (अनुक्रम), द्वितीयक (कुंडलिनी/चादर), तृतीयक (3D मोड़ — कार्यात्मक आकार!), चतुर्थीय (बहु-इकाइयाँ — हीमोग्लोबिन: 4!)।

🎯 कोलेजन = प्राणी-जगत #1; RuBisCO = जैव-मंडल #1; पेप्टाइड बंध = COOH+NH2 − जल!

3️⃣ लिपिड और न्यूक्लिक अम्ल ⭐⭐

  • लिपिड ⭐: जल-अविलेय। सरल लिपिड (ट्राइग्लिसराइड): ग्लिसरॉल + 3 वसा-अम्ल (एस्टर बंध)। वसा-अम्ल: संतृप्त (घी/मक्खन — ठोस) बनाम असंतृप्त (तेल — द्रव; आवश्यक वसा-अम्ल शरीर नहीं बनाता!)।
  • फॉस्फोलिपिड ⭐: ग्लिसरॉल + 2 वसा-अम्ल + 1 फॉस्फेट — कोशिका-झिल्ली की मूल सामग्री! जलरागी शीर्ष + जलविरागी पुच्छ = द्विपरत।
  • न्यूक्लिक अम्ल ⭐⭐: न्यूक्लियोटाइड-बहुलक — प्रत्येक = नाइट्रोजनी क्षार + पेंटोज़ शर्करा + फॉस्फेट। क्षार: प्यूरीन (एडिनीन, ग्वानीन — 2 वलय) + पिरिमिडीन (साइटोसिन, थाइमीन/यूरेसिल — 1 वलय)।
  • DNA ⭐⭐: द्वि-कुंडलिनी (वॉटसन-क्रिक!) — 2 प्रतिसमांतर रज्जुक, शर्करा-फॉस्फेट बाहर, क्षार अंदर; A=T (2 हाइड्रोजन बंध), G≡C (3 बंध); डीऑक्सीराइबोज़; थाइमीन (यूरेसिल नहीं!)।
  • RNA ⭐: सामान्यतः एकल-रज्जुक; राइबोज़ शर्करा; यूरेसिल; 3 प्रकार: mRNA (प्रोटीन-खाका), tRNA (एडाप्टर — प्रतिकोडन!), rRNA (राइबोसोम)।

🎯 A=T (2 बंध), G≡C (3 बंध) — GC अधिक = अधिक स्थायी DNA!

4️⃣ एंज़ाइम — जैविक उत्प्रेरक ⭐⭐

  • एंज़ाइम ⭐⭐: लगभग सभी प्रोटीन (अपवाद: राइबोज़ाइम — RNA एंज़ाइम! ⭐) — अभिक्रिया-गति हज़ारों गुना बढ़ाते हैं, स्वयं अपरिवर्तित।
  • कैसे ⭐: सक्रियण-ऊर्जा (Ea) घटाते हैं! एंज़ाइम + क्रियाधार → ES संकुल → एंज़ाइम + उत्पाद।
  • नामकरण ⭐: क्रियाधार + '-एज़' — माल्टेज़, लाइपेज़, प्रोटिएज़। 6 वर्ग — ऑक्सीडोरिडक्टेज़, ट्रांस्फ़रेज़, हाइड्रोलेज़, लायेज़, आइसोमरेज़, लाइगेज़।
  • सहकारक (cofactors) ⭐⭐: प्रोटीन भाग = एपोएंज़ाइम; गैर-प्रोटीन सहायक = सहकारक; पूर्ण = होलोएंज़ाइम। 3 प्रकार: प्रोस्थेटिक समूह (दृढ़-बंधे), सह-एंज़ाइम (शिथिल — NAD, NADP), धातु-आयन (Zn2+)।
  • ताप + pH प्रभाव ⭐⭐: प्रत्येक एंज़ाइम का इष्टतम ताप (~37°C) व इष्टतम pH; चरम पर विकृतीकरण — क्रियाशीलता समाप्त!
  • संदमन ⭐: प्रतिस्पर्धी संदमक — क्रियाधार-सदृश, सक्रिय-स्थल पर बैठ जाता है (मैलोनेट × सक्सीनिक डीहाइड्रोजिनेज़ ⭐)।

💡 एंज़ाइम = चतुर जुगाड़ू: सक्रियण-पहाड़ को छोटा कर अभिक्रिया को बूस्ट करता है!

5️⃣ उपापचय, बंध और पुनरावृत्ति ⭐

  • उपापचयी ⭐: प्राथमिक — सामान्य उपापचय में (एमिनो अम्ल, शर्करा — सभी कोशिकाओं में समान!); द्वितीयक ⭐ — विशिष्ट पादपों/सूक्ष्मजीवों में, पारिस्थितिक भूमिका (ऐल्केलॉइड — मॉर्फ़ीन; रबर; सुगंधित तेल; प्रतिजैविक; विष — रिसिन!)।
  • रासायनिक बंध ⭐⭐: पेप्टाइड बंध (प्रोटीन), ग्लाइकोसिडिक बंध (बहुशर्करा), फॉस्फोडाइएस्टर बंध ⭐ (न्यूक्लिक अम्ल!), एस्टर बंध (लिपिड), हाइड्रोजन बंध (DNA युग्म, प्रोटीन-संरचना)।
  • उपापचय: संश्लेषण (बनाना — ऊर्जा-व्यय) + अपघटन (तोड़ना — ऊर्जा-मुक्ति)। जीवित अवस्था = स्थायी-अवस्था (असंतुलन) — ऊर्जा निरंतर लगती है!
  • मास्टर तालिका ⭐: प्रोटीन = एमिनो अम्ल + पेप्टाइड; शर्करा = शर्करा-अणु + ग्लाइकोसिडिक; न्यूक्लिक अम्ल = न्यूक्लियोटाइड + फॉस्फोडाइएस्टर; लिपिड = वसा-अम्ल + एस्टर।

🎯 4 बंध याद रखें: पेप्टाइड (प्रोटीन), ग्लाइकोसिडिक (शर्करा), फॉस्फोडाइएस्टर (न्यूक्लिक), एस्टर (लिपिड) — हर साल प्रश्न!

1️⃣ Chemical Composition — What's Inside a Cell ⭐

  • Two analyses ⭐: grind living tissue in acid — acid-soluble pool (small molecules: amino acids, sugars, nucleotides) and acid-insoluble fraction (big molecules: proteins, nucleic acids, polysaccharides — biomacromolecules!).
  • Average composition ⭐: water 70-90% (the most!), proteins 10-15%, nucleic acids 5-7%, carbohydrates 3%, lipids 2%, ions 1%.
  • Elements ⭐: C, H, O, N make ~96%; then P, S, Ca, K; trace elements (Fe, Zn, Cu, I) are few but essential!
  • Micro vs macromolecules ⭐: micro = 18-800 Da; macro = 10000+ Da (polymers!). Exception ⭐⭐: lipids sit in the macromolecule fraction but are NOT polymers!

💡 A cell is chemical soup: 70% water, then proteins. Lipid = the non-polymer member of the macromolecule club — exam trap!

2️⃣ Carbohydrates and Amino Acids/Proteins ⭐⭐

  • Carbohydrates ⭐: C, H, O (1:2:1). Monosaccharides: glucose, fructose, ribose. Disaccharides: sucrose, maltose, lactose. Polysaccharides ⭐: starch (plant storage — turns BLUE with iodine! ⭐), glycogen (animal storage), cellulose (plant walls — we can't digest it!), chitin (fungal walls + arthropod armour).
  • Reducing vs non-reducing ⭐: reducing sugars (glucose, maltose, lactose) vs non-reducing (sucrose!).
  • Amino acids ⭐⭐: 20 types — each has an amino group (NH2) + carboxyl group (COOH) + H + variable R group on one alpha-carbon. R decides the nature: acidic (glutamic acid), basic (lysine), neutral, aromatic.
  • Proteins ⭐⭐: amino acid polymers joined by peptide bonds (COOH + NH2 − water!). Roles: structure (collagen — most abundant animal protein! ⭐), transport (haemoglobin), defence (antibodies), enzymes, hormones. RuBisCO ⭐ = the most abundant protein in the entire biosphere!
  • Protein structure ⭐: primary (sequence), secondary (helix/sheet), tertiary (3D fold — functional shape!), quaternary (multiple subunits — haemoglobin has 4!).

🎯 Collagen = animal kingdom #1; RuBisCO = biosphere #1; peptide bond = COOH + NH2 − H2O!

3️⃣ Lipids and Nucleic Acids ⭐⭐

  • Lipids ⭐: water-insoluble. Simple lipids (triglycerides): glycerol + 3 fatty acids (ester bonds). Fatty acids: saturated (ghee/butter — solid) vs unsaturated (oils — liquid; essential ones must come from diet!).
  • Phospholipids ⭐: glycerol + 2 fatty acids + 1 phosphate — the main material of cell membranes! Hydrophilic head + hydrophobic tails = bilayer in water.
  • Nucleic acids ⭐⭐: nucleotide polymers — each nucleotide = nitrogen base + pentose sugar + phosphate. Bases: purines (adenine, guanine — 2 rings) + pyrimidines (cytosine, thymine/uracil — 1 ring).
  • DNA ⭐⭐: double helix (Watson-Crick!) — 2 antiparallel strands, sugar-phosphate backbone outside, bases inside; A=T (2 hydrogen bonds), G≡C (3 hydrogen bonds); deoxyribose sugar; thymine (no uracil!).
  • RNA ⭐: usually single-stranded; ribose sugar; uracil instead of thymine; 3 types: mRNA (protein blueprint), tRNA (adapter with anticodon!), rRNA (ribosome).

🎯 A=T (2 bonds), G≡C (3 bonds) — more GC = more stable DNA!

4️⃣ Enzymes — Biological Catalysts ⭐⭐

  • Enzymes ⭐⭐: almost all are proteins (exception: ribozymes — RNA enzymes! ⭐) that speed reactions thousands of times without being changed themselves.
  • How ⭐: they LOWER the activation energy (Ea)! Enzyme + substrate → ES complex → enzyme + product. The enzyme stays reusable!
  • Naming ⭐: substrate + '-ase' — maltase, lipase, protease. 6 classes — oxidoreductases, transferases, hydrolases, lyases, isomerases, ligases.
  • Cofactors ⭐⭐: protein part = apoenzyme; non-protein helper = cofactor; together = holoenzyme. Three kinds: prosthetic groups (tightly bound), coenzymes (loosely attached — NAD, NADP), metal ions (Zn2+).
  • Temperature + pH ⭐⭐: every enzyme has an optimum temperature (~37°C in humans) and optimum pH; extremes cause denaturation — activity destroyed!
  • Inhibition ⭐: a competitive inhibitor resembles the substrate and blocks the active site (classic: malonate vs succinic dehydrogenase ⭐).

💡 An enzyme is a smart shortcut: it shrinks the activation-energy hill and boosts the reaction without wearing out!

5️⃣ Metabolism, Bonds and Recap ⭐

  • Metabolites ⭐: primary metabolites — part of normal metabolism (amino acids, sugars — same roles in all cells!); secondary metabolites ⭐ — specific plants/microbes, ecological roles (alkaloids — morphine; rubber; essential oils; antibiotics; pigments; toxins — ricin!).
  • Chemical bonds ⭐⭐: peptide bond (proteins), glycosidic bond (polysaccharides), phosphodiester bond ⭐ (nucleic acids!), ester bond (lipids), hydrogen bond (DNA pairs, protein folding).
  • Metabolism: anabolism (building — costs energy) + catabolism (breaking — releases energy). The living state is a non-equilibrium steady state — energy is constantly invested!
  • Master table ⭐: proteins = amino acids + peptide bonds; carbs = sugars + glycosidic; nucleic acids = nucleotides + phosphodiester; lipids = fatty acids + glycerol + ester.

🎯 Four bonds to remember: peptide (protein), glycosidic (carb), phosphodiester (nucleic acid), ester (lipid) — one question every year!

🏆 NEET Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Acid-soluble pool aur acid-insoluble fraction me kya farak hai?
Answer dekho

Acid-soluble = chhote molecules (amino acids, sugars, nucleotides — ~1000-5000 Da); acid-insoluble = bade molecules (proteins, nucleic acids, polysaccharides — biomacromolecules).

Q2. Lipids macromolecule fraction me aate hain par polymer kyun nahi hain?
Answer dekho

Kyunki lipids repeating units se nahi bante (800 Da se bhi chhote ho sakte hain) — ye sirf acid-insoluble vesicles banane ki wajah se macro fraction me aa jaate hain!

Q3. Sabse abundant protein animal kingdom me aur biosphere me kaunsa hai?
Answer dekho

Animals: collagen; Poori biosphere: RuBisCO (photosynthesis enzyme).

Q4. Peptide bond kaise banta hai?
Answer dekho

Ek amino acid ka COOH group + dusre ka NH2 group — water release hoke bond banta hai (dehydration/condensation).

Q5. DNA ki base pairing rules batao.
Answer dekho

Adenine = Thymine (2 hydrogen bonds), Guanine ≡ Cytosine (3 hydrogen bonds). DNA me uracil nahi, RNA me thymine nahi hota!

Q6. Phospholipids cell membrane ke liye perfect kyun hain?
Answer dekho

Unka hydrophilic head + hydrophobic tails hota hai — paani me khud-ba-khud bilayer ban jaati hai (tails andar, heads bahar)!

Q7. Enzymes reactions ko kaise speed up karte hain?
Answer dekho

Activation energy (Ea) ko LOWER karke — enzyme + substrate → ES complex → product + enzyme (enzyme change nahi hota, reuse hota hai!).

Q8. Cofactor kya hai? Iske 3 types batao.
Answer dekho

Enzyme ka non-protein helper. Types: prosthetic group (tightly bound), coenzyme (loose, organic — NAD/NADP), metal ions (Zn2+).

Q9. Ribozyme kya hai?
Answer dekho

RNA ka bana enzyme — exception to 'all enzymes are proteins'! (RNA bhi catalytic ho sakti hai.)

Q10. Nucleic acids me nucleotides kaunse bond se jude hote hain?
Answer dekho

Phosphodiester bond — ek nucleotide ke phosphate se dusre ke sugar tak (sugar-phosphate backbone banata hai).
