1️⃣ Endocrine System Basics ⭐

  • Endocrine glands ⭐⭐: DUCTLESS glands — apne secretions (hormones!) directly BLOOD me release karte hain (duct se nahi — exocrine glands jaise salivary/sweat ducts use karte hain!). Hormones = non-nutrient chemicals jo target organs pe intercellular messengers ki tarah act karte hain.
  • Human endocrine system ⭐⭐: pituitary, pineal, thyroid, parathyroid, thymus, adrenal, pancreas + gonads (testes/ovaries) + hypothalamus (neuroendocrine!) + heart/kidney/GI tract bhi hormones banate hain.
  • Hormones ke nature ⭐: peptides/proteins (insulin, GH — membrane receptors!); steroids (cortisol, testosterone — intracellular/nuclear receptors!); amino acid derivatives (thyroxine, adrenaline!); iodothyronines (thyroid!).
  • Feedback control ⭐: hormone release target ke effect se regulate hota hai — negative feedback (zyada hormone → release RUK jaata — common!) vs positive feedback (rare — oxytocin in childbirth!).
  • Neural vs chemical coordination ⭐: neural = fast, short-lived, point-to-point (nerve impulse!); chemical = slow, long-lasting, widespread (blood ke through!). Dono milke 'integration' karte hain — hypothalamus dono ka link!

💡 Endocrine = ductless (blood me!); exocrine = ducts (saliva/sweat!). Neural = fast/short; hormonal = slow/long!

2️⃣ Hypothalamus aur Pituitary ⭐⭐

  • Hypothalamus ⭐⭐: brain me diencephalon ka hissa — neurosecretory cells jo releasing + inhibiting hormones banate hain — ye pituitary ko control karte hain! Pituitary ka 'boss' (GnRH → gonadotropins; TRH → TSH; CRH → ACTH; GHRH → GH; somatostatin → GH inhibit!).
  • Pituitary gland ⭐⭐: hypothalamus ke neeche (sella turcica bony cavity me!) — 'master gland' — 2 parts: Adenohypophysis (anterior) + Neurohypophysis (posterior).
  • Anterior pituitary ⭐⭐: GH (growth hormone ⭐⭐) — growth + protein synthesis; over-secretion childhood me = gigantism; adulthood me = acromegaly; under-secretion childhood me = dwarfism ⭐⭐!; TSH — thyroid stimulate; ACTH — adrenal cortex stimulate; LH + FSH (gonadotropins) — testes/ovaries stimulate; prolactin (PRL) — milk production; MSH — skin pigmentation.
  • Posterior pituitary ⭐⭐: store + release (banata hypothalamus hai!) — Oxytocin ⭐⭐ — uterus contraction (childbirth!) + milk ejection; Vasopressin/ADH ⭐⭐ — kidney se water reabsorption (diabetes insipidus = ADH ki kami — bahut urine!).
  • Pineal gland ⭐: brain me — melatonin ⭐ release — sleep-wake cycle (circadian rhythm!), body temperature, menstrual cycle regulate + antioxidant.

🎯 GH: zyada bachpan me = gigantism, kam = dwarfism, adults me zyada = acromegaly! Oxytocin = childbirth/milk; ADH = water; melatonin = sleep!

3️⃣ Thyroid, Parathyroid aur Thymus ⭐⭐

  • Thyroid gland ⭐⭐: neck me, trachea ke dono taraf (largest endocrine!) — 2 lobes + isthmus. Hormones: T4 (thyroxine) + T3 — iodine chahiye ⭐! Functions: BMR (basal metabolic rate!) regulate, RBC formation, protein/carb/fat metabolism, growth + development, nervous tissue development.
  • Thyroid disorders ⭐⭐: Hypothyroidism ⭐ — kam secretion — goitre (iodine deficiency — thyroid enlarge!), cretinism (babies me — stunted growth + mental retardation!), myxoedema (adults me); Hyperthyroidism ⭐ — zyada secretion — Graves' disease/exophthalmic goitre (eyes bulge, weight loss, fast heartbeat!).
  • Thyrocalcitonin (TCT) ⭐: thyroid se — blood calcium GHATAATA hai (bones me calcium deposit!).
  • Parathyroid glands ⭐⭐: thyroid ke back pe 4 chhoti glands — PTH (parathyroid hormone) — blood calcium BADHAATA hai (bones se release + kidney/gut se absorption — hypercalcemic hormone ⭐!). TCT vs PTH = OPPOSITE actions (calcium balance!).
  • Thymus ⭐: lungs ke beech, heart ke peeche (bache me active, adults me shrink!) — thymosins ⭐ — T-lymphocytes differentiate (immunity!) + antibody production promote. Ageing me immunity kam = thymus shrink!

🎯 Thyroid = T3/T4 (BMR!) — kam = goitre/cretinism, zyada = Graves'. TCT = Ca↓, PTH = Ca↑ (opposite!). Thymus = T-cells (immunity!).

4️⃣ Adrenal aur Pancreas ⭐⭐

  • Adrenal glands ⭐⭐: kidneys ke upar (1 each!) — 2 parts: outer adrenal CORTEX + inner adrenal MEDULLA.
  • Adrenal cortex ⭐⭐: 3 layers se hormones: glucocorticoids (cortisol ⭐) — glucose metabolism, anti-inflammatory, stress response; mineralocorticoids (aldosterone ⭐⭐) — Na+ aur water reabsorption kidney me (BP + electrolyte balance!); androgenic steroids — sex characteristics (small amount!).
  • Adrenal medulla ⭐⭐: adrenaline (epinephrine) + noradrenaline (norepinephrine) ⭐⭐ — 'emergency hormones'/'fight or flight hormones'! — alertness, pupil dilation, piloerection (goosebumps!), sweating, heart rate ↑, BP ↑, glycogen breakdown (glucose ↑!). Noradrenaline bhi BP ↑.
  • Pancreas ⭐⭐: 1-2 million Islets of Langerhans (endocrine part!) — alpha cells = GLUCAGON ⭐⭐ — glycogen → glucose (liver me — blood sugar BADHAATA — hyperglycemic!); beta cells = INSULIN ⭐⭐ — glucose uptake + glycogenesis (blood sugar GHATAATA — hypoglycemic!). Glucagon vs insulin = OPPOSITE.
  • Diabetes mellitus ⭐⭐: insulin ki kami ya resistance — blood sugar HIGH (hyperglycemia!) — glucose urine me (glycosuria!), thirst, hunger, weight loss. Type 1 (insulin-dependent!) vs Type 2 (resistance!).

🎯 Aldosterone = Na+ bachao (cortex!); adrenaline = fight-or-flight (medulla!); insulin = sugar↓ (beta!), glucagon = sugar↑ (alpha!). Diabetes = insulin problem!

5️⃣ Gonads aur Hormone Mechanism ⭐⭐

  • Testes ⭐⭐: male gonads — Leydig cells (interstitial cells!) se androgens (mainly TESTOSTERONE ⭐⭐) — spermatogenesis, male secondary sexual characters (beard, deep voice, muscles!), male sex behaviour, protein anabolism. Sertoli cells = sperm nourish.
  • Ovaries ⭐⭐: female gonads — estrogen ⭐ (growing follicles se!) — female secondary sexual characters (breast development, high voice!), uterus/vagina growth, mammary duct development + female behaviour; progesterone ⭐⭐ (corpus luteum se!) — pregnancy SUPPORT (uterus maintain!), mammary alveoli, milk secretion me role. Relaxin = ovary se late pregnancy me (pelvic ligaments relax!).
  • Menstrual cycle hormones ⭐: FSH (follicle grow!) + LH (ovulation trigger + corpus luteum!) — pituitary se; estrogen + progesterone ovary se.
  • Hormone action mechanism ⭐⭐: Membrane-bound receptors ⭐ — protein/peptide hormones (insulin!) — cell SURFACE pe receptor bind → SECOND MESSENGER (cAMP, Ca2+, IP3!) generate → cellular response; Intracellular (nuclear) receptors ⭐ — steroid hormones (cortisol!) — cytoplasm/nucleus me bind → hormone-receptor complex DNA pe act → mRNA → new proteins (genome action!).
  • Heart + kidney + GI tract hormones ⭐: ANF (heart — BP ↓!), erythropoietin (kidney — RBC formation!), gastrin/secretin/CCK/GIP (GI tract — digestion!).

🎯 Testosterone = Leydig cells (male!); estrogen = follicles; progesterone = corpus luteum (pregnancy!). Peptides = membrane receptor + 2nd messenger; steroids = nuclear receptor + DNA!

1️⃣ अंतःस्रावी-तंत्र मूल बातें ⭐

  • अंतःस्रावी-ग्रंथियाँ ⭐⭐: वाहिनी-रहित — हॉर्मोन सीधे रक्त में स्रावित (बहिःस्रावी — वाहिनी से, जैसे लार/पसीना!)। हॉर्मोन = अंतःकोशिकीय संदेशवाहक।
  • मानव-तंत्र ⭐⭐: पीयूषिका, पिनियल, थायरॉइड, पैराथायरॉइड, थायमस, अधिवृक्क, अग्न्याशय + जननग्रंथि + हाइपोथैलेमस + हृदय/वृक्क/GI।
  • हॉर्मोन-प्रकृति ⭐: पेप्टाइड/प्रोटीन (इंसुलिन — झिल्ली-ग्राही!); स्टेरॉइड (कॉर्टिसोल — केंद्रकीय-ग्राही!); अमीनो-व्युत्पन्न (थायरॉक्सिन!)।
  • पुनर्भरण-नियंत्रण ⭐: ऋणात्मक पुनर्भरण (सामान्य — अधिक हॉर्मोन → स्राव रुके!)।
  • तंत्रिका बनाम रासायनिक ⭐: तंत्रिका = तीव्र, अल्पकालिक; रासायनिक = धीमा, दीर्घकालिक, व्यापक।

💡 अंतःस्रावी = वाहिनी-रहित (रक्त में!); बहिःस्रावी = वाहिनी (लार/पसीना)!

2️⃣ हाइपोथैलेमस और पीयूषिका ⭐⭐

  • हाइपोथैलेमस ⭐⭐: मस्तिष्क का भाग — तंत्रिका-स्रावी कोशिकाएँ जो मोचक + संदमक हॉर्मोन बनातीं — पीयूषिका को नियंत्रित! पीयूषिका का 'मालिक'।
  • पीयूषिका ⭐⭐: 'मास्टर-ग्रंथि' — 2 भाग: अग्र (adenohypophysis) + पश्च (neurohypophysis)।
  • अग्र-पीयूषिका ⭐⭐: GH ⭐⭐ — वृद्धि; बचपन में अधिक = दीर्घकायता (gigantism); वयस्क में अधिक = अंगुलिवृद्धि (acromegaly); बचपन में कम = बौनापन ⭐⭐! TSH — थायरॉइड; ACTH — अधिवृक्क-कॉर्टेक्स; LH + FSH — जननग्रंथि; प्रोलैक्टिन — दुग्ध-उत्पादन; MSH — वर्णकता।
  • पश्च-पीयूषिका ⭐⭐: भंडार + स्राव — ऑक्सीटोसिन ⭐⭐ — गर्भाशय-संकुचन (प्रसव!) + दुग्ध-स्राव; वैसोप्रेसिन/ADH ⭐⭐ — वृक्क से जल-पुनःअवशोषण (मधुमेह-अस्फुटता = ADH की कमी!)।
  • पिनियल ⭐: मेलाटोनिन ⭐ — निद्रा-जागरण चक्र + दैनिक-लय!

🎯 GH: बचपन में अधिक = दीर्घकायता, कम = बौनापन! ऑक्सीटोसिन = प्रसव/दुग्ध; ADH = जल!

3️⃣ थायरॉइड, पैराथायरॉइड और थायमस ⭐⭐

  • थायरॉइड ⭐⭐: गले में (सबसे बड़ी!) — T4 + T3 — आयोडीन चाहिए ⭐! कार्य: BMR नियमन, RBC-निर्माण, उपापचय, वृद्धि + तंत्रिका-विकास।
  • विकार ⭐⭐: हाइपोथायरॉइडिज़्म ⭐ — कम — गलगंड (goitre) (आयोडीन-कमी!), वामनता (cretinism) (शिशुओं में!), मिक्सीडीमा; हाइपरथायरॉइडिज़्म ⭐ — अधिक — ग्रेव्स-रोग (आँखें उभरीं, वजन-घटना!)।
  • थायरोकैल्सिटोनिन (TCT) ⭐: रक्त-कैल्शियम घटाता (अस्थियों में जमा!)।
  • पैराथायरॉइड ⭐⭐: 4 छोटी — PTH — रक्त-कैल्शियम बढ़ाता (अस्थियों से मुक्त + अवशोषण — हाइपरकैल्सीमिक ⭐!)। TCT बनाम PTH = विपरीत!
  • थायमस ⭐: फेफड़ों के बीच — थायमोसिन ⭐ — T-लसीकाणु विशेषीकरण (प्रतिरक्षा!)।

🎯 थायरॉइड = T3/T4 (BMR!) — कम = गलगंड, अधिक = ग्रेव्स। TCT = Ca↓, PTH = Ca↑!

4️⃣ अधिवृक्क और अग्न्याशय ⭐⭐

  • अधिवृक्क ⭐⭐: वृक्कों के ऊपर — बाहरी कॉर्टेक्स + आंतरिक मेडुला।
  • कॉर्टेक्स ⭐⭐: ग्लूकोकॉर्टिकॉइड (कॉर्टिसोल ⭐) — ग्लूकोज़-उपापचय, तनाव; मिनरलोकॉर्टिकॉइड (एल्डोस्टेरॉन ⭐⭐) — Na+ + जल पुनःअवशोषण (BP + लवण-संतुलन!); एंड्रोजन।
  • मेडुला ⭐⭐: एड्रेनलिन + नॉरएड्रेनलिन ⭐⭐ — 'आपातकालीन/लड़ो-या-भागो हॉर्मोन' — सतर्कता, पुतली-फैलना, हृदय-दर ↑, BP ↑, ग्लाइकोजन-अपघटन (शर्करा ↑!)।
  • अग्न्याशय ⭐⭐: लैंगरहैन्स-द्वीपिकाएँ — अल्फ़ा = ग्लूकागॉन ⭐⭐ — ग्लाइकोजन → ग्लूकोज़ (शर्करा बढ़ाता!); बीटा = इंसुलिन ⭐⭐ — ग्लूकोज़-ग्रहण + ग्लाइकोजन-निर्माण (शर्करा घटाता!)। विपरीत!
  • मधुमेह ⭐⭐: इंसुलिन-कमी/प्रतिरोध — रक्त-शर्करा उच्च — मूत्र में ग्लूकोज़, प्यास, भूख। Type 1 (इंसुलिन-आश्रित!) बनाम Type 2!

🎯 एल्डोस्टेरॉन = Na+ बचाओ; एड्रेनलिन = लड़ो-भागो; इंसुलिन = शर्करा↓, ग्लूकागॉन = शर्करा↑!

5️⃣ जननग्रंथि और हॉर्मोन-क्रियाविधि ⭐⭐

  • वृषण ⭐⭐: लेडिग-कोशिकाओं से एंड्रोजन (टेस्टोस्टेरॉन ⭐⭐) — शुक्राणुजनन, पुरुष-द्वितीयक-लक्षण (दाढ़ी, गंभीर-स्वर!), प्रोटीन-संश्लेषण। सर्टोली = शुक्राणु-पोषण।
  • अंडाशय ⭐⭐: ईस्ट्रोजन ⭐ (पुटक से!) — स्त्री-द्वितीयक-लक्षण (स्तन-विकास!), गर्भाशय-वृद्धि; प्रोजेस्टेरॉन ⭐⭐ (पीत-पिंड (corpus luteum) से!) — गर्भावस्था-समर्थन! रिलैक्सिन = स्नायु-शिथिलन।
  • आर्तव-चक्र ⭐: FSH (पुटक-वृद्धि!) + LH (अंडोत्सर्ग + पीत-पिंड!) — पीयूषिका से।
  • क्रियाविधि ⭐⭐: झिल्ली-ग्राही ⭐ — पेप्टाइड (इंसुलिन!) — सतह पर बंधे → द्वितीय-संदेशवाहक (cAMP, Ca2+, IP3!) → अनुक्रिया; केंद्रकीय-ग्राही ⭐ — स्टेरॉइड (कॉर्टिसोल!) — DNA पर → mRNA → नई प्रोटीन!
  • अन्य ⭐: ANF (हृदय — BP ↓!), एरिथ्रोपोइटिन (वृक्क — RBC!), गैस्ट्रिन/सीक्रेटिन (GI!)।

🎯 टेस्टोस्टेरॉन = लेडिग; ईस्ट्रोजन = पुटक; प्रोजेस्टेरॉन = पीत-पिंड! पेप्टाइड = झिल्ली-ग्राही; स्टेरॉइड = केंद्रकीय!

1️⃣ Endocrine System Basics ⭐

  • Endocrine glands ⭐⭐: DUCTLESS glands — they release hormones straight into the BLOOD (unlike exocrine glands like salivary glands that use ducts!).
  • Human system ⭐⭐: the pituitary, pineal, thyroid, parathyroid, thymus, adrenal, pancreas + gonads + hypothalamus + heart/kidney/GI tract.
  • Hormone nature ⭐: peptides/proteins (insulin — membrane receptors!); steroids (cortisol — nuclear receptors!); amino acid derivatives (thyroxine!).
  • Feedback ⭐: mostly negative feedback — too much hormone → release stops!
  • Neural vs chemical ⭐: neural = fast, short-lived; chemical = slow, long-lasting, widespread.

💡 Endocrine = ductless (into blood!); exocrine = ducts (saliva/sweat)!

2️⃣ Hypothalamus and Pituitary ⭐⭐

  • Hypothalamus ⭐⭐: in the brain — neurosecretory cells making releasing + inhibiting hormones that control the pituitary. The pituitary's 'boss'!
  • Pituitary gland ⭐⭐: the 'master gland' — two parts: anterior (adenohypophysis) + posterior (neurohypophysis).
  • Anterior pituitary ⭐⭐: GH (growth hormone ⭐⭐) — growth; over-secretion in childhood = gigantism; in adulthood = acromegaly; under-secretion in childhood = dwarfism ⭐⭐! TSH — stimulates the thyroid; ACTH — the adrenal cortex; LH + FSH — the gonads; prolactin — milk; MSH — pigmentation.
  • Posterior pituitary ⭐⭐: stores + releases — Oxytocin ⭐⭐ — uterus contraction (childbirth!) + milk ejection; Vasopressin/ADH ⭐⭐ — kidney water reabsorption (diabetes insipidus = ADH deficiency!).
  • Pineal ⭐: melatonin ⭐ — the sleep-wake cycle + circadian rhythm!

🎯 GH: too much in childhood = gigantism, too little = dwarfism! Oxytocin = childbirth/milk; ADH = water!

3️⃣ Thyroid, Parathyroid and Thymus ⭐⭐

  • Thyroid ⭐⭐: in the neck (the largest endocrine!) — T4 + T3 — needs iodine ⭐! Jobs: regulate BMR, RBC formation, metabolism, growth + nervous development.
  • Disorders ⭐⭐: Hypothyroidism ⭐ — too little — goitre (iodine deficiency!), cretinism (in babies!), myxoedema; Hyperthyroidism ⭐ — too much — Graves' disease (bulging eyes, weight loss!).
  • Thyrocalcitonin (TCT) ⭐: LOWERS blood calcium (deposits it in bones!).
  • Parathyroid ⭐⭐: four small glands — PTH — RAISES blood calcium (releases from bone + absorption — hypercalcemic ⭐!). TCT vs PTH = OPPOSITE!
  • Thymus ⭐: between the lungs — thymosins ⭐ — T-lymphocyte differentiation (immunity!).

🎯 Thyroid = T3/T4 (BMR!) — low = goitre, high = Graves'. TCT = Ca down, PTH = Ca up!

4️⃣ Adrenal and Pancreas ⭐⭐

  • Adrenal glands ⭐⭐: on top of the kidneys — outer CORTEX + inner MEDULLA.
  • Adrenal cortex ⭐⭐: glucocorticoids (cortisol ⭐) — glucose metabolism, stress; mineralocorticoids (aldosterone ⭐⭐) — Na+ + water reabsorption (BP + electrolyte balance!); androgens.
  • Adrenal medulla ⭐⭐: adrenaline + noradrenaline ⭐⭐ — the 'emergency/fight-or-flight hormones' — alertness, pupil dilation, heart rate up, BP up, glycogen breakdown (glucose up!).
  • Pancreas ⭐⭐: the Islets of Langerhans — alpha cells = GLUCAGON ⭐⭐ — glycogen → glucose (RAISES blood sugar!); beta cells = INSULIN ⭐⭐ — glucose uptake + glycogenesis (LOWERS blood sugar!). OPPOSITE actions!
  • Diabetes mellitus ⭐⭐: insulin deficiency or resistance — high blood sugar — glucose in urine, thirst, hunger. Type 1 vs Type 2!

🎯 Aldosterone = save Na+; adrenaline = fight-or-flight; insulin = sugar down, glucagon = sugar up!

5️⃣ Gonads and Hormone Mechanism ⭐⭐

  • Testes ⭐⭐: the Leydig cells make androgens (mainly TESTOSTERONE ⭐⭐) — spermatogenesis, male secondary characters (beard, deep voice!), protein anabolism. Sertoli cells = nourish sperm.
  • Ovaries ⭐⭐: estrogen ⭐ (from growing follicles!) — female secondary characters (breast development!), uterus growth; progesterone ⭐⭐ (from the corpus luteum!) — SUPPORTS pregnancy! Relaxin = relaxes pelvic ligaments.
  • Menstrual cycle ⭐: FSH (follicle growth!) + LH (ovulation + corpus luteum!) — from the pituitary.
  • Mechanism ⭐⭐: Membrane-bound receptors ⭐ — peptide hormones (insulin!) — bind on the SURFACE → a SECOND MESSENGER (cAMP, Ca2+, IP3!) → response; Intracellular (nuclear) receptors ⭐ — steroid hormones (cortisol!) — act on DNA → mRNA → new proteins!
  • Others ⭐: ANF (heart — BP down!), erythropoietin (kidney — RBC!), gastrin/secretin (GI!).

🎯 Testosterone = Leydig cells; estrogen = follicles; progesterone = corpus luteum! Peptides = membrane receptor; steroids = nuclear!

🏆 NEET Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Endocrine aur exocrine glands me farak batao.
Answer dekho

Endocrine = DUCTLESS — hormones directly BLOOD me release; exocrine = ducts se secretions (salivary glands, sweat glands!).

Q2. GH ki over aur under-secretion se kya hota hai?
Answer dekho

Over-secretion bachpan me = gigantism (bahut lamba!); adults me = acromegaly (hands/feet/face bones bade!); under-secretion bachpan me = dwarfism (chhota kad!).

Q3. ADH ki kami se kaunsi bimari hoti hai?
Answer dekho

Diabetes insipidus — kidney se water reabsorption nahi ho paata → bahut zyada dilute urine (polyuria!). ADH = vasopressin (posterior pituitary se release!).

Q4. Thyroid ke hormones aur unka mineral kya hai?
Answer dekho

T4 (thyroxine) aur T3 — IODINE chahiye banane ke liye! Functions: BMR regulate, growth, RBC formation, nervous tissue development.

Q5. Hypothyroidism ke 2 effects batao.
Answer dekho

Goitre (iodine deficiency se thyroid ENLARGE hoti hai!) aur cretinism (babies me — stunted growth + mental retardation!). Zyada secretion = Graves' disease (eyes bulge!).

Q6. TCT aur PTH me kya farak hai?
Answer dekho

TCT (thyroid se) = blood calcium GHATAATA hai (bones me deposit!); PTH (parathyroid se) = blood calcium BADHAATA hai (bones se release!) — dono OPPOSITE (calcium balance!).

Q7. Adrenaline kyun 'emergency hormone' hai?
Answer dekho

Stress/danger me adrenal medulla se release — fight-or-flight response — alertness, pupil dilation, heart rate ↑, BP ↑, glucose ↑ (glycogen breakdown!) — body ko action ke liye ready!

Q8. Insulin aur glucagon kahan se aate hain aur kya karte hain?
Answer dekho

Insulin = beta cells of Islets of Langerhans — blood sugar GHATAATA hai (glucose uptake + glycogenesis!); glucagon = alpha cells — blood sugar BADHAATA hai (glycogenolysis!). Diabetes = insulin problem!

Q9. Testosterone aur progesterone kahan se bante hain?
Answer dekho

Testosterone = Leydig cells of testes (spermatogenesis + male characters!); progesterone = corpus luteum of ovary (pregnancy support — uterus maintain!).

Q10. Steroid aur peptide hormones ka mechanism me farak batao.
Answer dekho

Peptides = membrane-bound receptors (cell SURFACE!) → second messenger (cAMP!) → response; steroids = intracellular/nuclear receptors → DNA pe act → mRNA → new proteins!
