1️⃣ Basics Recap — Class 9/10 revision

  • Atom — chemistry ki sabse chhoti unit (H, O, C).
  • Molecule — atoms ka group: H₂, O₂, CO₂.
  • Element — sirf ek type ke atoms (Fe, Au, O).
  • Compound — alag elements, fixed ratio me: H₂O = 2 H + 1 O.
  • Valency — combine karne ki capacity: H = 1, O = 2 → isliye H₂O, H₂O₂ nahi.

💡 Shortcut: Element = ek type ka, Compound = fixed ratio ka. Compound ko todo → elements milenge (chemical method).

2️⃣ Matter aur uske states

Matter = jiske paas mass ho + space ghere. Light/sound matter nahi — energy hain.

StateShapeVolumeExample

LiquidNahiFixedWater
GasNahiNahiAir

Mixture — variable composition, physical methods se alag hoti hai: salt water (homogeneous = har jagah same), sand + water (heterogeneous = alag dikhta hai).

Pure substance — element ya compound.

💡 Trick: Mixture = salad (alag kar sakte ho). Compound = smoothie (alag nahi kar sakte).

3️⃣ Properties & Measurement (SI Units)

  • Physical — bina composition badle observe hoti hai (color, melting point, density).
  • Chemical — composition badalne par dikhti hai (burning, rusting).
  • Intensive — amount par depend nahi: temperature, density, boiling point.
  • Extensive — amount par depend: mass, volume, energy.

Prefixes: pico 10⁻¹² • nano 10⁻⁹ • micro 10⁻⁶ • milli 10⁻³ • centi 10⁻² • deci 10⁻¹ • kilo 10³ • mega 10⁶ • giga 10⁹.

Scientific notation: 3000 = 3 × 10³  |  0.00045 = 4.5 × 10⁻⁴

4️⃣ Significant Figures (JEE ka favourite)

  • Non-zero digits hamesha significant: 123 → 3.
  • Leading zeros nahi: 0.0025 → 2.
  • Beech ke zeros haan: 1003 → 4.
  • Decimal ke baad ke zeros haan: 2.500 → 4.
  • Addition/Subtraction → least decimal places: 12.53 + 3.1 + 0.025 = 15.7.
  • Multiply/Divide → least sig figs: 1.5 × 2 = 3 (calculator 3.0 batayega, exam me 3!).

⚠️ Feel: ruler se 3.5 cm mila — 3.500 nahi likh sakte, ruler utna precise nahi. Sig figs = precision ka pata.

5️⃣ Laws of Chemical Combination (5 laws)

  1. Conservation of Mass (Lavoisier): mass na banta na mitta — lakdi jalao, total mass wahi (raakh + dhuan + gas).
  2. Definite Proportions (Proust): H₂O me H:O = 1:8 by mass, hamesha.
  3. Multiple Proportions (Dalton): CO (12:16) vs CO₂ (12:32) → O ka ratio 1:2.
  4. Gay-Lussac (Gases): simple whole-number volume ratios: H₂:Cl₂:HCl = 1:1:2.
  5. Avogadro: same T,P par equal volumes → equal molecules → isi se 22.4 L aaya.

Dalton's Atomic Theory: matter atoms se bana hai; reaction me atoms rearrange hote hain. Limitations: atom divisible hai (e⁻, p⁺, n⁰ mile!), isotopes hain, mass↔energy (E = mc²).

6️⃣ Atomic Mass, amu, Molecular Mass

1 amu = 1/12 × mass of one C-12 atom = 1.66 × 10⁻²⁴ g
  • Atomic mass: H = 1 u, O = 16 u.
  • Average atomic mass (isotopes ka weighted average): Cl = (35×75 + 37×25)/100 = 35.5 u.
  • Molecular mass: H₂SO₄ = 2×1 + 32 + 4×16 = 98 u.
  • Formula mass (ionic ke liye): NaCl = 23 + 35.5 = 58.5 u.

7️⃣ THE MOLE CONCEPT ⭐ (is chapter ka dil)

Problem: atoms dikhte nahi, count karna impossible. Solution: shopkeeper dozann (12) / gross (144) me count karta hai — chemist mole me.

1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ particles = NA (Avogadro Number) = molar mass (g) = 22.4 L gas (STP)

🤯 Feel: 18 g paani (~1.5 chammach) me 6.022 × 10²³ molecules. 8 billion log × 1/sec count karein → 2.4 million saal!

n = m / M   |   n = N / NA   |   n = V / 22.4 (gas, STP)

Example: 46 g Na (M = 23) → n = 46/23 = 2 mol → atoms = 2 × 6.022×10²³ = 1.204 × 10²⁴.

⚠️ Atoms vs Molecules (sabse bada trap)

  • 1 mol O₂ → 2 mol O atoms (2 × NA)
  • 1 mol CO₂ → 3 mol total atoms (1 C + 2 O)
  • 1 mol H₂O → 3 mol total atoms

Rule: molecule ke andar jitne atoms → moles ko utne se multiply karo.

8️⃣ Percentage Composition + Empirical/Molecular Formula

% element = (mass of element in 1 mol compound / molar mass) × 100

Empirical = simplest whole-number ratio • Molecular = actual = n × Empirical

n = Molecular mass / Empirical formula mass

Steps: ① % ko gram maano (100 g) ② moles nikalo (mass/atomic mass) ③ chhote se divide karo ④ ratio → empirical.

Example: C 40%, H 6.67%, O 53.33%, M = 180 → C: 3.33, H: 6.67, O: 3.33 → divide by 3.33 → CH₂O (30 u) → n = 180/30 = 6 → C₆H₁₂O₆ = Glucose! 🍬

9️⃣ Stoichiometry + Limiting Reagent

Equation = recipe. N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ matlab 1 mol N₂ + 3 mol H₂ dega 2 mol NH₃.

🍔 Burger: 1 burger = 2 buns + 1 patty. 10 buns + 3 patties → sirf 3 burger (patty = limiting reagent), 4 buns excess.

Example: 56 g N₂ (2 mol) + 6 g H₂ (3 mol) → 2 mol N₂ ko chahiye 6 mol H₂, paas me 3 → H₂ = LR → NH₃ = 2 mol = 34 g; N₂ ka 1 mol excess.

% Yield = (actual mass / theoretical mass) × 100

⚡ Fast trick: moles ÷ coefficient compare karo — jo chhota, wo Limiting Reagent.

1️⃣ Basics दोहराएँ — Class 9/10 revision

  • परमाणु (Atom) — रसायन विज्ञान की सबसे छोटी इकाई (H, O, C)।
  • अणु (Molecule) — परमाणुओं का समूह: H₂, O₂, CO₂।
  • तत्व (Element) — केवल एक ही प्रकार के परमाणु (Fe, Au, O)।
  • यौगिक (Compound) — अलग-अलग तत्व, निश्चित अनुपात में: H₂O = 2 H + 1 O।
  • संयोजकता (Valency) — जुड़ने की क्षमता: H = 1, O = 2 → इसीलिए H₂O बनता है, H₂O₂ नहीं।

💡 Shortcut: तत्व = एक प्रकार का, यौगिक = निश्चित अनुपात का। यौगिक को तोड़ो → तत्व मिलेंगे (chemical method से)।

2️⃣ पदार्थ (Matter) और उसकी अवस्थाएँ

पदार्थ = जिसका द्रव्यमान (mass) हो + जगह घेरता हो। प्रकाश/ध्वनि पदार्थ नहीं — ऊर्जा है।

गैसनिश्चित नहींनिश्चित नहींहवा

मिश्रण (Mixture) — composition बदल सकता है, physical methods से अलग होता है: नमक-पानी (समांगी = हर जगह एक समान), रेत + पानी (विषमांगी = अलग दिखता है)।

शुद्ध पदार्थ — तत्व या यौगिक।

💡 Trick: मिश्रण = सलाद (अलग कर सकते हो)। यौगिक = स्मूदी (अलग नहीं कर सकते)।

3️⃣ गुण और मापन (SI इकाइयाँ)

  • भौतिक गुण (Physical) — बिना composition बदले दिखते हैं (रंग, गलनांक, घनत्व)।
  • रासायनिक गुण (Chemical) — composition बदलने पर दिखते हैं (जलना, जंग लगना)।
  • Intensive — मात्रा पर depend नहीं: तापमान, घनत्व, क्वथनांक।
  • Extensive — मात्रा पर depend: द्रव्यमान, आयतन, ऊर्जा।

4️⃣ सार्थक अंक (Significant Figures)

  • Non-zero अंक हमेशा सार्थक: 123 → 3।
  • शुरुआती zeros नहीं: 0.0025 → 2।
  • बीच के zeros हाँ: 1003 → 4।
  • Decimal के बाद के zeros हाँ: 2.500 → 4।
  • जोड़/घटाव → सबसे कम decimal places: 12.53 + 3.1 + 0.025 = 15.7।

5️⃣ रासायनिक संयोजन के नियम (5 नियम)

  1. द्रव्यमान संरक्षण (Lavoisier): mass न बनता है न मिटता है — लकड़ी जलाओ, कुल mass वही (राख + धुआँ + गैस)।
  2. निश्चित अनुपात (Proust): H₂O में H:O = 1:8 by mass, हमेशा।
  3. गुणित अनुपात (Dalton): CO (12:16) vs CO₂ (12:32) → O का ratio 1:2।
  4. Gay-Lussac (गैसें): सरल whole-number आयतन अनुपात: H₂:Cl₂:HCl = 1:1:2।
  5. Avogadro: same T,P पर बराबर आयतन → बराबर अणु → इसी से 22.4 L आया।

Dalton का परमाणु सिद्धांत: पदार्थ परमाणुओं से बना है; अभिक्रिया में परमाणु rearrange होते हैं। सीमाएँ: परमाणु divisible है (e⁻, p⁺, n⁰ मिले!), isotopes हैं, mass↔energy (E = mc²)।

1️⃣ Basics Recap — Class 9/10 revision

  • Atom — the smallest unit of chemistry (H, O, C).
  • Molecule — a group of atoms: H₂, O₂, CO₂.
  • Element — only one type of atom (Fe, Au, O).
  • Compound — different elements in a fixed ratio: H₂O = 2 H + 1 O.
  • Valency — combining capacity: H = 1, O = 2 → that's why water is H₂O, not H₂O₂.

💡 Shortcut: Element = one type, Compound = fixed ratio. Break a compound → you get elements (by chemical methods).

2️⃣ Matter and its states

Matter = anything that has mass and occupies space. Light and sound are not matter — they are energy.

StateShapeVolumeExample
SolidFixedFixedIce, iron
LiquidNot fixedFixedWater
GasNot fixedNot fixedAir

Mixture — variable composition, separable by physical methods: salt water (homogeneous = uniform throughout), sand + water (heterogeneous = visibly different parts).

Pure substance — an element or a compound.

💡 Trick: Mixture = salad (you can separate it). Compound = smoothie (you cannot).

3️⃣ Properties & Measurement (SI Units)

  • Physical property — observed without changing composition (colour, melting point, density).
  • Chemical property — seen when composition changes (burning, rusting).
  • Intensive — independent of amount: temperature, density, boiling point.
  • Extensive — depends on amount: mass, volume, energy.

4️⃣ Significant Figures (a JEE favourite)

  • Non-zero digits are always significant: 123 → 3.
  • Leading zeros are not: 0.0025 → 2.
  • Zeros between digits are: 1003 → 4.
  • Trailing zeros after a decimal are: 2.500 → 4.
  • Addition/Subtraction → fewest decimal places: 12.53 + 3.1 + 0.025 = 15.7.

5️⃣ Laws of Chemical Combination (5 laws)

  1. Conservation of Mass (Lavoisier): mass is neither created nor destroyed — burn wood and the total mass stays the same (ash + smoke + gases).
  2. Definite Proportions (Proust): in H₂O, H:O = 1:8 by mass, always.
  3. Multiple Proportions (Dalton): CO (12:16) vs CO₂ (12:32) → the O ratio is 1:2.
  4. Gay-Lussac (gases): simple whole-number volume ratios: H₂:Cl₂:HCl = 1:1:2.
  5. Avogadro: at the same T and P, equal volumes contain equal numbers of molecules — this is where 22.4 L comes from.

Dalton's Atomic Theory: matter is made of atoms; reactions only rearrange atoms. Limitations: atoms are divisible (e⁻, p⁺, n⁰ were discovered!), isotopes exist, and mass↔energy convert (E = mc²).

6️⃣ Atomic Mass, amu, Molecular Mass

1 amu = 1/12 × mass of one C-12 atom = 1.66 × 10⁻²⁴ g
  • Atomic mass: H = 1 u, O = 16 u.
  • Average atomic mass (weighted average of isotopes): Cl = (35×75 + 37×25)/100 = 35.5 u.
  • Molecular mass: H₂SO₄ = 2×1 + 32 + 4×16 = 98 u.
  • Formula mass (for ionic compounds): NaCl = 23 + 35.5 = 58.5 u.

7️⃣ THE MOLE CONCEPT ⭐ (the heart of this chapter)

Problem: atoms are invisible and impossible to count one by one. Solution: a shopkeeper counts in dozens (12) or gross (144) — a chemist counts in moles.

1 mole = 6.022 × 10²³ particles = NA (Avogadro's number) = molar mass in grams = 22.4 L of gas at STP

🤯 Feel it: 18 g of water (~1.5 spoons) holds 6.022 × 10²³ molecules. 8 billion people counting 1 per second would need 2.4 million years!

n = m / M   |   n = N / NA   |   n = V / 22.4 (gas, STP)

Example: 46 g Na (M = 23) → n = 46/23 = 2 mol → atoms = 2 × 6.022×10²³ = 1.204 × 10²⁴.

⚠️ Atoms vs Molecules (the biggest trap)

  • 1 mol O₂ → 2 mol O atoms (2 × NA)
  • 1 mol CO₂ → 3 mol total atoms (1 C + 2 O)
  • 1 mol H₂O → 3 mol total atoms

Rule: however many atoms sit inside the molecule — multiply the moles by that number.

8️⃣ Percentage Composition + Empirical/Molecular Formula

% element = (mass of element in 1 mol of compound / molar mass) × 100

Empirical formula = simplest whole-number ratio • Molecular formula = actual = n × Empirical

n = Molecular mass / Empirical formula mass

Steps: ① take % as grams (100 g sample) ② convert to moles (mass/atomic mass) ③ divide by the smallest ④ the ratio gives the empirical formula.

Example: C 40%, H 6.67%, O 53.33%, M = 180 → C: 3.33, H: 6.67, O: 3.33 → divide by 3.33 → CH₂O (30 u) → n = 180/30 = 6 → C₆H₁₂O₆ = Glucose! 🍬

9️⃣ Stoichiometry + Limiting Reagent

An equation is a recipe. N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ means 1 mol N₂ + 3 mol H₂ gives 2 mol NH₃.

🍔 Burger logic: 1 burger = 2 buns + 1 patty. With 10 buns + 3 patties you can make only 3 burgers (patty is the limiting reagent); 4 buns are left over.

Example: 56 g N₂ (2 mol) + 6 g H₂ (3 mol) → 2 mol N₂ needs 6 mol H₂, but only 3 is available → H₂ is the LR → NH₃ = 2 mol = 34 g; 1 mol N₂ is in excess.

% Yield = (actual mass / theoretical mass) × 100

⚡ Fast trick: compare moles ÷ coefficient — the smallest value is the Limiting Reagent.

Q1 4.6 g sodium me kitne atoms hain?
Answer dekho

n = 4.6/23 = 0.2 mol → 0.2 × 6.022×10²³ = 1.204 × 10²³ atoms

Answer dekho

n = 4.4/44 = 0.1 mol → 6.022 × 10²² molecules

Q3 18 g H₂O me total kitne atoms hain?
Answer dekho

1 mol H₂O → 3 mol atoms → 1.807 × 10²⁴ atoms

Q4 100 g CaCO₃ (M = 100) ko garam karne par STP par kitna CO₂ (L) milega? (CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂)
Answer dekho

n = 1 mol → CO₂ = 1 mol → 22.4 L

Q5 Compound me Na = 43.4%, C = 11.3%, O = 45.3% — empirical formula?
Answer dekho

Na: 43.4/23 = 1.89, C: 11.3/12 = 0.94, O: 45.3/16 = 2.83 → divide by 0.94 → Na₂CO₃

Q6 28 g N₂ + 6 g H₂ → N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃. LR kya hai? NH₃ kitni banegi?
Answer dekho

n(N₂) = 1 mol, n(H₂) = 3 mol → exact ratio, koi excess nahi → NH₃ = 2 mol = 34 g

Q7 12.53 + 3.1 + 0.025 = ? (sig figs ke saath)

15.655 → least decimals (3.1 ke 1 decimal) → 15.7

Q8 Boron: ¹⁰B (20%) + ¹¹B (80%) → average atomic mass?

0.2×10 + 0.8×11 = 10.8 u

Q9 4.4 g CO₂ + 3.2 g CH₄ mixture me total mol atoms?

CO₂: 0.1×3 = 0.3 | CH₄: 0.2×5 = 1.0 → total 1.3 mol atoms

0.5 × 7 mol atoms = 3.5 N_A = 2.108 × 10²⁴ atoms
