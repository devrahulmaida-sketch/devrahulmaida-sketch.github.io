1️⃣ Sub-atomic Particles — atom ke andar kya hai

Class 9 tak humne suna: atom sabse chhota hai. Jhooth nahi, adhoora sach hai — atom ke andar 3 chhote particles hain:

ParticleChargeMassKhoja kisne
Electron (e⁻)−1 (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)9.1 × 10⁻³¹ kgJ.J. Thomson (1897)
Proton (p⁺)+11.672 × 10⁻²⁷ kgGoldstein (1886, canal rays)
Neutron (n⁰)01.675 × 10⁻²⁷ kgChadwick (1932)
  • Thomson ka discharge tube: cathode rays negative plate ki taraf kheenchi → matlab rays negative hain → electron mila. e/m ratio = 1.76 × 10⁸ C/g.
  • Millikan ka oil-drop experiment: electron ka exact charge nikala = −1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C.
  • Neutron sabse late mila kyunki charge hi nahi hai — kisi taraf bhaagta nahi.

💡 Trick: Electron sabse halka, proton+neutron lagbhag barabar (~1836 × electron).

2️⃣ Atom ke Models — Thomson, phir Rutherford

  • Thomson model (1898): atom = positive pudding jisme electrons kishmish ki tarah gade hain. Simple, par galat nikla.
  • Rutherford ka gold-foil experiment (1911): α-particles (helium nuclei, positive) ko sonay ki patli foil par maara.

Observations → Conclusions:

  1. Zyada tar α-particles seedha nikal gaye → atom mostly khaali hai.
  2. Kuch thoda mud gaye → beech me positive charge hai (like charges repel).
  3. 20,000 me se 1 wapas aa gaya → poora positive mass ek chhote, dense nucleus me hai (~10⁻¹⁵ m; atom ~10⁻¹⁰ m).

Rutherford model: nucleus beech me, electrons uske around ghoomte hain (planets ki tarah).

⚠️ Drawbacks (exam favourite): ① Ghoomta electron accelerate hota hai → energy radiate karke nucleus me gir jaana chahiye (par girte nahi!). ② Spectrum ke baare me kuch nahi batata.

3️⃣ Atomic Number, Mass Number, Isotopes, Isobars

Atomic number (Z) = protons ki sankhya  |  Mass number (A) = protons + neutrons
  • Isotopes: same Z, alag A — jaise ¹H, ²H (deuterium), ³H (tritium). Chemical properties same (electrons same).
  • Isobars: same A, alag Z — jaise ¹⁴C aur ¹⁴N.
  • Isotones: same neutrons — jaise ³⁹K (20 n) aur ⁴⁰Ca (20 n).

💡 Trick: Isotopes = same Protons, Isobars = same mass number (A), Isotones = same Neutrons.

4️⃣ Electromagnetic Radiation + Planck ka Quantum

Light ek wave hai: wavelength (λ) = do crests ke beech ka distance, frequency (ν) = per second kitni waves.

c = ν × λ = 3 × 10⁸ m/s (speed of light)

Matlab λ aur ν ulte chalte hain — λ bada toh ν chhota. EM spectrum (chhoti λ → badi): gamma → X-ray → UV → visible → IR → microwave → radio.

Planck (1900): energy continuous nahi — quanta ke packets me milti hai:

E = hν  ,  h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s (Planck constant)

Photoelectric effect (Einstein, 1905): light metal par pade toh electrons nikalte hain — sirf tab jab ν ≥ ν₀ (threshold frequency). Badi light ho ya chhoti, frequency hi matter karti hai → light particle (photon) jaisi bhi hai!

KE of emitted electron = hν − hν₀

5️⃣ Hydrogen Spectrum + Rydberg Formula

Hydrogen ke electron ko energy do → uuncha orbit → wapas gira → light nikli. Prism se dekho toh lines dikhti hain (continuous rainbow nahi!).

1/λ = R(1/n₁² − 1/n₂²)  ,  R = 109,677 cm⁻¹ (Rydberg constant)
Seriesn₁ → n₂Region
Lyman1 ← 2,3,4…UV
Balmer2 ← 3,4,5…Visible
Paschen3 ← 4,5…IR
Brackett4 ← 5,6…IR
Pfund5 ← 6,7…IR

💡 Trick: Lyman = 1 (donon me L ek), Balmer = 2, aur Baad me P, B, P = 3, 4, 5. Sirf Balmer visible hai — isliye H ka red-green-blue glow dikhta hai.

6️⃣ Bohr ka Model ⭐ (is chapter ka hero)

Postulates:

  1. Electrons sirf fixed orbits (energy levels) me ghoom sakte hain — wahan energy radiate NAHI hoti.
  2. Angular momentum quantized hai: mvr = nh/2π (n = 1, 2, 3…).
  3. Energy tabhi absorb/emit hoti hai jab electron orbit badalta hai: ΔE = E₂ − E₁ = hν.
Eₙ = −13.6 × Z²/n² eV   |   rₙ = 0.529 × n²/Z Å
  • n badhao → energy badhti hai (−13.6 → −3.4 → −1.5 → 0). n = ∞ par electron free (ionization!).
  • Hydrogen ka spectrum perfectly explain — Lyman, Balmer sab nikal aaye.

⚠️ Limitations (ratta nahi, samajh): ① Sirf 1-electron species ke liye (H, He⁺, Li²⁺) — multi-electron par fail. ② Zeeman (magnetic field) / Stark (electric field) effect explain nahi. ③ Fine lines nahi bata paaya. ④ Dual nature + uncertainty ko ignore kiya.

7️⃣ Dual Nature + Heisenberg Uncertainty

de Broglie (1924): light wave+particle hai, toh electron (matter) bhi wave ho sakta hai!

λ = h / mv (de Broglie wavelength)

Isliye electron microscope banti hai — fast electrons ki λ chhoti → zyada zoom.

Heisenberg Uncertainty: chhote particle ki position aur momentum ek saath exact nahi naap sakte:

Δx × Δp ≥ h/4π  (ya Δx × Δv ≥ h/4πm)

💡 Feel: electron ko dekhne ke liye light maarni padegi, aur light ne usko dhaka de diya — measurement hi cheez badal deti hai. Isliye "orbit" (fixed path) ka concept khatam → ab sirf probability ki baat hoti hai.

8️⃣ Quantum Numbers + Orbitals + Configuration ⭐

Electron ka "address" 4 quantum numbers se milta hai:

QNSymbolValuesBatata hai
Principaln1, 2, 3…Shell, size + energy
Azimuthall0 se n−1Subshell (0=s, 1=p, 2=d, 3=f) + shape
Magneticm−l se +lOrbital ki orientation
Spins+½ ya −½Electron ka spin
  • Shapes: s = spherical (ball), p = dumbbell (px, py, pz), d = double-dumbbell (5 orbitals).
  • Aufbau rule: pehle low-energy orbital bharo — (n+l) chhota wala pehle; barabar ho toh chhota n pehle. Order: 1s, 2s, 2p, 3s, 3p, 4s, 3d, 4p, 5s…
  • Pauli exclusion: ek orbital me max 2 electrons, opposite spins. (Hostel room me 2 students, ulta muh karke sote hain 😄)
  • Hund's rule: same energy ke orbitals me pehle sab me 1-1 electron (parallel spin), phir pairing. (Bus ki khaali seats pe log pehle alag-alag baithte hain!)
Max electrons in shell n = 2n²  |  subshell me: s=2, p=6, d=10, f=14

⚠️ Famous exceptions (ratta maaro): Cr = [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹ (nahi 3d⁴ 4s²), Cu = [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s¹ (nahi 3d⁹ 4s²) — half-filled aur fully-filled d extra stable hota hai.

🎯 Example: Fe (Z=26) → 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s² 3p⁶ 4s² 3d⁶. Likhte waqt 4s pehle, par electrons nikaalte waqt 4s se pehle nikalta hai (Fe²⁺ = 3d⁶)!

1️⃣ Sub-atomic कण — परमाणु के अंदर क्या है

Class 9 तक सुना: परमाणु सबसे छोटा है। अधूरा सच है — परमाणु के अंदर 3 छोटे कण हैं:

कणChargeMassखोजा किसने
इलेक्ट्रॉन (e⁻)−1 (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)9.1 × 10⁻³¹ kgJ.J. Thomson (1897)
प्रोटॉन (p⁺)+11.672 × 10⁻²⁷ kgGoldstein (1886, canal rays)
न्यूट्रॉन (n⁰)01.675 × 10⁻²⁷ kgChadwick (1932)
  • Thomson का discharge tube: cathode rays negative plate की तरफ खिंची → मतलब rays negative हैं → इलेक्ट्रॉन मिला। e/m ratio = 1.76 × 10⁸ C/g।
  • Millikan का oil-drop experiment: इलेक्ट्रॉन का exact charge निकला = −1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C।
  • न्यूट्रॉन सबसे late मिला क्योंकि उस पर charge ही नहीं — किसी तरफ भागता नहीं।

💡 Trick: इलेक्ट्रॉन सबसे हल्का, प्रोटॉन+न्यूट्रॉन लगभग बराबर (~1836 × इलेक्ट्रॉन)।

2️⃣ परमाणु के Models — Thomson, फिर Rutherford

  • Thomson model (1898): परमाणु = positive pudding जिसमें इलेक्ट्रॉन किशमिश की तरह गड़े हैं। Simple, पर गलत निकला।
  • Rutherford का gold-foil experiment (1911): α-कणों (helium nuclei, positive) को सोने की पतली foil पर मारा।

Observations → Conclusions:

  1. ज़्यादा तर α-कण सीधा निकल गए → परमाणु ज़्यादा तर खाली है।
  2. कुछ थोड़ा मुड़ गए → बीच में positive charge है (like charges repel)।
  3. 20,000 में से 1 वापस आ गया → पूरा positive mass एक छोटे, dense nucleus में है (~10⁻¹⁵ m; परमाणु ~10⁻¹⁰ m)।

Rutherford model: nucleus बीच में, इलेक्ट्रॉन उसके चारों ओर घूमते हैं (planets की तरह)।

⚠️ Drawbacks (exam favourite): ① घूमता इलेक्ट्रॉन accelerate होता है → energy radiate करके nucleus में गिर जाना चाहिए (पर गिरते नहीं!)। ② Spectrum के बारे में कुछ नहीं बताता।

3️⃣ परमाणु संख्या, द्रव्यमान संख्या, Isotopes, Isobars

परमाणु संख्या (Z) = प्रोटॉन की संख्या  |  द्रव्यमान संख्या (A) = प्रोटॉन + न्यूट्रॉन
  • समस्थानिक (Isotopes): same Z, अलग A — जैसे ¹H, ²H (deuterium), ³H (tritium)। Chemical properties same (इलेक्ट्रॉन same)।
  • समभारिक (Isobars): same A, अलग Z — जैसे ¹⁴C और ¹⁴N।
  • Isotones: same न्यूट्रॉन — जैसे ³⁹K (20 n) और ⁴⁰Ca (20 n)।

💡 Trick: Isotopes = same Protons, Isobars = same mass number (A), Isotones = same Neutrons।

4️⃣ विद्युतचुंबकीय विकिरण + Planck का Quantum

प्रकाश एक wave है: wavelength (λ) = दो crests के बीच की दूरी, frequency (ν) = per second कितनी waves।

c = ν × λ = 3 × 10⁸ m/s (प्रकाश की चाल)

मतलब λ और ν उल्टे चलते हैं — λ बड़ा तो ν छोटा। EM spectrum (छोटी λ → बड़ी): gamma → X-ray → UV → visible → IR → microwave → radio।

Planck (1900): ऊर्जा continuous नहीं — quanta के packets में मिलती है:

E = hν  ,  h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s (Planck नियतांक)

Photoelectric effect (Einstein, 1905): प्रकाश metal पर पड़े तो इलेक्ट्रॉन निकलते हैं — सिर्फ तब जब ν ≥ ν₀ (threshold frequency)। तेज़ light हो या धीमी, frequency ही matter करती है → प्रकाश particle (photon) जैसा भी है!

निकले इलेक्ट्रॉन की KE = hν − hν₀

5️⃣ Hydrogen Spectrum + Rydberg सूत्र

Hydrogen के इलेक्ट्रॉन को energy दो → ऊँचा orbit → वापस गिरा → प्रकाश निकला। Prism से देखो तो lines दिखती हैं (continuous rainbow नहीं!)।

1/λ = R(1/n₁² − 1/n₂²)  ,  R = 109,677 cm⁻¹ (Rydberg नियतांक)
Seriesn₁ → n₂Region
Lyman1 ← 2,3,4…UV
Balmer2 ← 3,4,5…Visible
Paschen3 ← 4,5…IR
Brackett4 ← 5,6…IR
Pfund5 ← 6,7…IR

💡 Trick: Lyman = 1, Balmer = 2, और बाद में P, B, P = 3, 4, 5। सिर्फ Balmer visible है — इसीलिए H का red-green-blue glow दिखता है।

6️⃣ Bohr का Model ⭐ (इस chapter का hero)

Postulates:

  1. इलेक्ट्रॉन सिर्फ निश्चित orbits (energy levels) में घूम सकते हैं — वहाँ energy radiate नहीं होती।
  2. Angular momentum quantized है: mvr = nh/2π (n = 1, 2, 3…)।
  3. Energy तभी absorb/emit होती है जब इलेक्ट्रॉन orbit बदलता है: ΔE = E₂ − E₁ = hν।
Eₙ = −13.6 × Z²/n² eV   |   rₙ = 0.529 × n²/Z Å
  • n बढ़ाओ → energy बढ़ती है (−13.6 → −3.4 → −1.5 → 0)। n = ∞ पर इलेक्ट्रॉन free (ionization!)।
  • Hydrogen का spectrum perfectly explain — Lyman, Balmer सब निकल आए।

⚠️ Limitations: ① सिर्फ 1-इलेक्ट्रॉन species के लिए (H, He⁺, Li²⁺) — multi-electron पर fail। ② Zeeman / Stark effect explain नहीं। ③ Fine lines नहीं बता पाया। ④ Dual nature + uncertainty को ignore किया।

7️⃣ Dual Nature + Heisenberg Uncertainty

de Broglie (1924): प्रकाश wave+particle है, तो इलेक्ट्रॉन (पदार्थ) भी wave हो सकता है!

λ = h / mv (de Broglie wavelength)

इसीलिए electron microscope बनती है — तेज़ इलेक्ट्रॉन की λ छोटी → ज़्यादा zoom।

Heisenberg Uncertainty: छोटे कण की position और momentum एक साथ exact नहीं नाप सकते:

Δx × Δp ≥ h/4π  (या Δx × Δv ≥ h/4πm)

💡 Feel: इलेक्ट्रॉन को देखने के लिए प्रकाश मारना पड़ेगा, और प्रकाश ने उसको धक्का दे दिया — measurement ही चीज़ बदल देती है। इसलिए "orbit" (fixed path) का concept खत्म → अब सिर्फ probability की बात होती है।

8️⃣ Quantum Numbers + Orbitals + Configuration ⭐

इलेक्ट्रॉन का "पता" 4 quantum numbers से मिलता है:

QNSymbolValuesबताता है
Principal (मुख्य)n1, 2, 3…Shell, size + energy
Azimuthal (द्वितीयक)l0 से n−1Subshell (0=s, 1=p, 2=d, 3=f) + shape
Magnetic (चुंबकीय)m−l से +lOrbital की orientation
Spin (चक्रण)s+½ या −½इलेक्ट्रॉन का spin
  • Shapes: s = spherical (गोला), p = dumbbell (px, py, pz), d = double-dumbbell (5 orbitals)।
  • Aufbau rule: पहले low-energy orbital भरो — (n+l) छोटा वाला पहले; बराबर हो तो छोटा n पहले। Order: 1s, 2s, 2p, 3s, 3p, 4s, 3d, 4p, 5s…
  • Pauli exclusion: एक orbital में max 2 इलेक्ट्रॉन, opposite spins। (Hostel room में 2 students, उल्टा मुह करके सोते हैं 😄)

Shell n में max इलेक्ट्रॉन = 2n²  |  subshell में: s=2, p=6, d=10, f=14

🎯 उदाहरण: Fe (Z=26) → 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s² 3p⁶ 4s² 3d⁶। लिखते वक़्त 4s पहले, पर इलेक्ट्रॉन निकालते वक़्त 4s से पहले निकलता है (Fe²⁺ = 3d⁶)!

1️⃣ Sub-atomic Particles — what is inside the atom

Up to Class 9 we heard: the atom is the smallest unit. Not false, but incomplete — the atom contains 3 smaller particles:

ParticleChargeMassDiscovered by
Electron (e⁻)−1 (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)9.1 × 10⁻³¹ kgJ.J. Thomson (1897)
Proton (p⁺)+11.672 × 10⁻²⁷ kgGoldstein (1886, canal rays)
Neutron (n⁰)01.675 × 10⁻²⁷ kgChadwick (1932)
  • Thomson's discharge tube: cathode rays bent towards the positive plate → the rays are negative → the electron was found. e/m ratio = 1.76 × 10⁸ C/g.
  • Millikan's oil-drop experiment: gave the exact charge of the electron = −1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C.
  • The neutron was found last because it carries no charge — it drifts past every detector.

💡 Trick: the electron is the lightest; proton and neutron are nearly equal (~1836 × electron).

2️⃣ Atomic Models — Thomson, then Rutherford

  • Thomson model (1898): the atom is a positive pudding with electrons studded in it like raisins. Simple — but wrong.
  • Rutherford's gold-foil experiment (1911): fired α-particles (positive helium nuclei) at a thin gold foil.

    • Observations → Conclusions:

    1. Most α-particles passed straight through → the atom is mostly empty space.
    2. Some deflected slightly → there is a positive charge in the middle (like charges repel).
    3. 1 in 20,000 bounced back → all the positive mass sits in a tiny, dense nucleus (~10⁻¹⁵ m; the atom is ~10⁻¹⁰ m).

    Rutherford's model: nucleus at the centre, electrons revolve around it (like planets).

    ⚠️ Drawbacks (exam favourite): ① A revolving electron is accelerating → it should radiate energy and spiral into the nucleus (but it doesn't!). ② It says nothing about spectra.

3️⃣ Atomic Number, Mass Number, Isotopes, Isobars

Atomic number (Z) = number of protons  |  Mass number (A) = protons + neutrons
  • Isotopes: same Z, different A — e.g. ¹H, ²H (deuterium), ³H (tritium). Chemical properties are the same (same electrons).
  • Isobars: same A, different Z — e.g. ¹⁴C and ¹⁴N.
  • Isotones: same number of neutrons — e.g. ³⁹K (20 n) and ⁴⁰Ca (20 n).

💡 Trick: Isotopes = same Protons, Isobars = same mass number (A), Isotones = same Neutrons.

4️⃣ Electromagnetic Radiation + Planck's Quantum

Light is a wave: wavelength (λ) = distance between two crests, frequency (ν) = waves per second.

c = ν × λ = 3 × 10⁸ m/s (speed of light)

So λ and ν move opposite — bigger λ means smaller ν. EM spectrum (short λ → long): gamma → X-ray → UV → visible → IR → microwave → radio.

Planck (1900): energy is not continuous — it comes in packets called quanta:

E = hν  ,  h = 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s (Planck's constant)

Photoelectric effect (Einstein, 1905): light knocks electrons out of a metal — only when ν ≥ ν₀ (threshold frequency). Bright dim light does nothing; frequency is what matters → light also behaves like a particle (the photon)!

KE of the emitted electron = hν − hν₀

5️⃣ Hydrogen Spectrum + Rydberg Formula

Give energy to hydrogen's electron → it jumps to a higher orbit → falls back → emits light. Through a prism you see distinct lines (not a continuous rainbow!).

1/λ = R(1/n₁² − 1/n₂²)  ,  R = 109,677 cm⁻¹ (Rydberg constant)
Seriesn₁ → n₂Region
Lyman1 ← 2,3,4…UV
Balmer2 ← 3,4,5…Visible
Paschen3 ← 4,5…IR
Brackett4 ← 5,6…IR
Pfund5 ← 6,7…IR

💡 Trick: Lyman = 1, Balmer = 2, then P, B, P = 3, 4, 5. Only Balmer is visible — that's why hydrogen glows red-green-blue.

6️⃣ Bohr's Model ⭐ (the hero of this chapter)

Postulates:

  1. Electrons can revolve only in certain fixed orbits (energy levels) — and there they do NOT radiate energy.
  2. Angular momentum is quantized: mvr = nh/2π (n = 1, 2, 3…).
  3. Energy is absorbed or emitted only when the electron changes orbits: ΔE = E₂ − E₁ = hν.
Eₙ = −13.6 × Z²/n² eV   |   rₙ = 0.529 × n²/Z Å
  • Increase n → energy rises (−13.6 → −3.4 → −1.5 → 0). At n = ∞ the electron is free (ionization!).
  • It explains hydrogen's spectrum perfectly — Lyman, Balmer, all of them fall out.

⚠️ Limitations (understand, don't memorise): ① Works only for 1-electron species (H, He⁺, Li²⁺) — fails for multi-electron atoms. ② Can't explain Zeeman (magnetic) or Stark (electric) effects. ③ Misses the fine spectral lines. ④ Ignores dual nature and uncertainty.

7️⃣ Dual Nature + Heisenberg Uncertainty

de Broglie (1924): if light is both wave and particle, then an electron (matter) can behave like a wave too!

λ = h / mv (de Broglie wavelength)

This is why electron microscopes exist — fast electrons have tiny λ → huge zoom.

Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle: you cannot know a small particle's position and momentum exactly at the same time:

Δx × Δp ≥ h/4π  (or Δx × Δv ≥ h/4πm)

💡 Feel it: to see an electron you must shine light on it — and that light kicks it. Measurement itself changes the thing. So the fixed "orbit" idea dies → we now talk in probabilities.

Spins+½ or −½Electron's spin
  • Shapes: s = spherical (ball), p = dumbbell (px, py, pz), d = double-dumbbell (5 orbitals).
  • Aufbau rule: fill the lowest energy first — smaller (n+l) first; if equal, smaller n first. Order: 1s, 2s, 2p, 3s, 3p, 4s, 3d, 4p, 5s…
  • Pauli exclusion: max 2 electrons per orbital, with opposite spins. (A hostel room holds 2 students, sleeping head-to-toe 😄)
  • Hund's rule: in equal-energy orbitals, place one electron in each first (parallel spins), then pair up. (People take empty bus seats separately first!)
Max electrons in shell n = 2n²  |  per subshell: s=2, p=6, d=10, f=14

⚠️ Famous exceptions (memorise these): Cr = [Ar] 3d⁵ 4s¹ (not 3d⁴ 4s²), Cu = [Ar] 3d¹⁰ 4s¹ (not 3d⁹ 4s²) — half-filled and fully-filled d subshells are extra stable.

🎯 Example: Fe (Z=26) → 1s² 2s² 2p⁶ 3s² 3p⁶ 4s² 3d⁶. You write 4s first, but electrons leave 4s first (Fe²⁺ = 3d⁶)!

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Bohr model me H-atom ki n=2 orbit ki radius kitni hai? (a₀ = 0.529 Å)
Answer dekho

rn = 0.529 × n²/Z Å → n=2, Z=1: 0.529 × 4 = 2.116 Å

Q2. Ek metal ka work function 2.0 eV hai. 400 nm light (E ≈ 3.1 eV) se nikle electron ki max kinetic energy?
Answer dekho

KE = hν − φ₀ = 3.1 − 2.0 = 1.1 eV. (400 nm se lambe wavelength pe emission nahi hoga)

Q3. 1 g mass, 1000 m/s speed wale particle ka de Broglie wavelength? (h = 6.6×10⁻³⁴)
Answer dekho

λ = h/mv = 6.6×10⁻³⁴/(10⁻³ × 1000) = 6.6 × 10⁻³⁴ m — macroscopic cheezon ka wavelength negligible!

Q4. Heisenberg: electron ki speed ±300 m/s ki uncertainty se measured hai (m = 9.1×10⁻³¹ kg). Position ki min uncertainty?
Answer dekho

Δx ≥ h/(4π·m·Δv) = 6.6×10⁻³⁴/(4π × 9.1×10⁻³¹ × 300) ≈ 1.9 × 10⁻⁴ m

Q5. H-spectrum me Balmer series ki pehli line (n=3→2) ki wavenumber? (R = 1.097×10⁷ m⁻¹)
Answer dekho

ν̄ = R(1/2² − 1/3²) = 1.097×10⁷ × (1/4 − 1/9) = 1.52 × 10⁶ m⁻¹ (red line, ~656 nm)

Q6. Kaunsa quantum number set VALID hai? (a) n=3,l=3,m=0 (b) n=2,l=1,m=−1 (c) n=1,l=1,m=0
Answer dekho

(b) — l hamesha n−1 tak ho sakta hai (0 se n−1). (a) me l=3 allowed nahi n=3 ke liye, (c) me l=1 invalid n=1 ke liye.

Q7. 3p aur 4d orbitals me radial aur angular nodes kitne?
Answer dekho

Angular nodes = l, radial = n−l−1. 3p: l=1 → 1 angular, 1 radial. 4d: l=2 → 2 angular, 1 radial.

Q8. Cr (Z=24) aur Cu (Z=29) ki electronic configuration kya hai — exception kyun?
Answer dekho

Cr: [Ar] 3d⁵4s¹, Cu: [Ar] 3d¹⁰4s¹ — half-filled (d⁵) aur fully-filled (d¹⁰) d-subshell extra stable hote hain, isliye 4s ka ek electron 3d me shift.

Q9. Ek shell me maximum electrons ka formula aur n=3 ke liye value?
Answer dekho

2n² → n=3: 18 electrons (3s² 3p⁶ 3d¹⁰)

Q10. H-atom ka electron n=4 se n=1 pe aaya. Emitted photon ki energy? (E₁ = −13.6 eV)
Answer dekho

ΔE = 13.6(1/1² − 1/4²) = 13.6 × 15/16 = 12.75 eV (Lyman series, UV)
