Energy ka hisaab — first law, enthalpy, entropy, aur Gibbs ka final faisla

📖 Full Explanation (Long Notes)

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Basic Terms — system, surroundings, types

Thermodynamics = energy ka hisaab — reaction me heat kitni nikli/kitni lagi, kaam kitna hua, aur reaction khud hogi ya nahi.

  • System: jisko dekh rahe hain (beaker ka mixture). Surroundings: baaki sab. Dono milke = universe.
  • Open system: matter + energy dono exchange (khula bartan).
  • Closed system: sirf energy exchange (dhakkan wala bartan).
  • Isolated system: kuch nahi exchange (thermos flask).
  • State functions: sirf initial-final par depend — U, H, S, G, P, V, T. Path functions: raasta matter karta hai — q (heat), w (work).
  • Extensive: amount par depend (mass, V, U, H) • Intensive: nahi (T, P, density).

💡 Feel: State function = aapki height (seedha measure), path function = kitne kadam chalke aaye (raaste par depend).

2️⃣ First Law — energy na banti na mitta

ΔU = q + w   (ΔU = internal energy change, q = heat, w = work ON the system)
  • Sign convention (ratta): system par kaam/heat aayi → positive. System ne kaam kiya/heat nikali → negative.
  • Expansion work: w = −PextΔV (gas expand kare toh w negative — system ne kaam kiya).
  • Reversible process: frictionless, infinitesimally slow — maximum work deta hai. Irreversible = real life, kam work.
  • Isothermal (ΔT=0): ΔU = 0 → q = −w • Adiabatic (q=0): ΔU = w • Isochoric (ΔV=0): w = 0 → ΔU = qv.

🎯 JEE trap: qv = ΔU (constant volume) aur qp = ΔH (constant pressure) — dono state functions ban jaate hain constant conditions par!

3️⃣ Enthalpy (ΔH) — constant pressure ka heat

H = U + PV   →   ΔH = ΔU + ΔngRT (sirf gases ke moles count!)
  • ΔH negative → exothermic (heat nikli, jalna, condensation) • ΔH positive → endothermic (heat li, pighalna, photosynthesis).
  • Standard enthalpy of reaction = Σ ΔH°f(products) − Σ ΔH°f(reactants). Elements ki standard state me ΔH°f = 0 (O₂ gas, C graphite, Br₂ liquid!).
  • Bond enthalpy method: ΔH = Σ BE(reactants ke bonds todo) − Σ BE(products ke bonds banao).

💡 Feel: bond todna = paisa kharch (energy input), bond banana = paisa vasooli (energy output). Net = profit ya loss.

4️⃣ Hess's Law — raasta koi bhi, hisaab same

Reaction ek step me ho ya 10 steps me — total ΔH same rahega (kyunki H state function hai!).

  • Equations ko jodo/ghatao/guna karo jaise algebra — ΔH bhi waise hi jodta-ghat'ta hai. Equation reverse karo → ΔH ka sign flip.
  • Famous use: C + ½O₂ → CO ka ΔH direct nahi naap sakte (CO₂ bhi ban jaata hai) — par CO₂ tak ke do raaste jodke nikal lete hain.
  • Isi se born-haber cycle (lattice enthalpy) aur ΔHsolution nikalte hain.
Target equation jo chahiye → di gayi equations ko aise jodo ki beech ke species cancel ho jayein

5️⃣ Entropy (ΔS) — randomness ka meter ⭐

S = disorder/randomness ka measure   |   ΔS = qrev/T
  • Gas > Liquid > Solid (entropy ka order). Gas ke moles badhein reaction me → ΔS positive.
  • Pighalna/boil hona → ΔS positive. Jamna → negative.
  • Second Law: spontaneous process me universe ki entropy badhti hai (ΔStotal = ΔSsys + ΔSsurr > 0).
  • System ki entropy kam bhi ho sakti hai — par surroundings ki zyada badhni chahiye (jaise paani jamna: system ordered, par heat nikli → surroundings ka randomness badha).

💡 Feel: entropy = aapka room — khud bigadta hai, theek karne me mehnat (energy) lagti hai. Universe ka room bhi bigad raha hai. 😄

6️⃣ Gibbs Free Energy — final referee ⭐⭐

ΔG = ΔH − TΔS   (constant T, P par)
ΔGMatlab
NegativeSpontaneous — reaction khud hogi ✅
PositiveNon-spontaneous — energy daalni padegi ❌
ZeroEquilibrium — dono taraf barabar ⚖️
  • ΔH −ve, ΔS +ve: har temperature par spontaneous.
  • ΔH +ve, ΔS −ve: kabhi spontaneous nahi.
  • ΔH +ve, ΔS +ve: HIGH temperature par spontaneous (TΔS jeet-ta hai) — jaise pighalna.
  • ΔH −ve, ΔS −ve: LOW temperature par spontaneous — jaise jamna.
  • Equilibrium se link: ΔG° = −RT ln K = −2.303 RT log K.
K > 1 → ΔG° negative → products favoured | K < 1 → reactants favoured

🎯 Ek line me: ΔG negative = green signal. Baaki sab (ΔH, ΔS) sirf advisors hain, final faisla ΔG ka.

1️⃣ Basic Terms — निकाय (system), परिसर (surroundings), प्रकार

Thermodynamics (ऊष्मागतिकी) = energy का हिसाब — अभिक्रिया में heat कितनी निकली/कितनी लगी, काम कितना हुआ, और अभिक्रिया खुद होगी या नहीं।

  • निकाय (System): जिसको देख रहे हैं (beaker का mixture)। परिसर (Surroundings): बाकी सब। दोनों मिलकर = universe।
  • खुला निकाय (Open): matter + energy दोनों exchange (खुला बर्तन)।
  • बंद निकाय (Closed): सिर्फ energy exchange (ढक्कन वाला बर्तन)।
  • विविक्त निकाय (Isolated): कुछ नहीं exchange (thermos flask)।
  • State functions: सिर्फ initial-final पर depend — U, H, S, G, P, V, T। Path functions: रास्ता matter करता है — q (heat), w (work)।
  • Extensive: मात्रा पर depend (mass, V, U, H) • Intensive: नहीं (T, P, density)।

💡 Feel: State function = आपकी height (सीधा measure), path function = कितने कदम चलकर आए (रास्ते पर depend)।

2️⃣ प्रथम नियम — energy न बनती न मिटती

ΔU = q + w   (ΔU = आंतरिक ऊर्जा परिवर्तन, q = heat, w = निकाय पर किया गया कार्य)
  • Sign convention (रट्टा): निकाय पर काम/heat आई → positive। निकाय ने काम किया/heat निकाली → negative।
  • प्रसार कार्य (Expansion work): w = −PextΔV (gas expand करे तो w negative — निकाय ने काम किया)।
  • उत्क्रमणीय (Reversible) process: frictionless, बहुत धीमा — maximum work देता है। Irreversible = असली ज़िंदगी, कम work।
  • समतापीय (Isothermal, ΔT=0): ΔU = 0 → q = −w • रुद्धोष्म (Adiabatic, q=0): ΔU = w • समआयतनिक (Isochoric, ΔV=0): w = 0 → ΔU = qv।

🎯 JEE trap: qv = ΔU (constant volume) और qp = ΔH (constant pressure) — दोनों state functions बन जाते हैं constant conditions पर!

3️⃣ एन्थैल्पी (ΔH) — constant pressure का heat

H = U + PV   →   ΔH = ΔU + ΔngRT (सिर्फ gases के moles count!)
  • ΔH negative → exothermic (उष्माक्षेपी) (heat निकली, जलना) • ΔH positive → endothermic (उष्माशोषी) (heat ली, पिघलना)।
  • अभिक्रिया की मानक एन्थैल्पी = Σ ΔH°f(products) − Σ ΔH°f(reactants)। तत्वों की standard state में ΔH°f = 0 (O₂ gas, C graphite, Br₂ liquid!)।
  • बंध एन्थैल्पी method: ΔH = Σ BE(reactants के bonds तोड़ो) − Σ BE(products के bonds बनाओ)।

💡 Feel: bond तोड़ना = पैसा खर्च (energy input), bond बनाना = पैसा वसूली (energy output)। Net = profit या loss।

4️⃣ Hess का नियम — रास्ता कोई भी, हिसाब same

अभिक्रिया एक step में हो या 10 steps में — कुल ΔH same रहेगा (क्योंकि H state function है!)।

  • Equations को जोड़ो/घटाओ/गुणा करो जैसे algebra — ΔH भी वैसे ही जुड़ता-घटता है। Equation reverse करो → ΔH का sign flip।
  • Famous use: C + ½O₂ → CO का ΔH direct नहीं नाप सकते (CO₂ भी बन जाता है) — पर CO₂ तक के दो रास्ते जोड़कर निकाल लेते हैं।
  • इसी से born-haber cycle (lattice enthalpy) और ΔHsolution निकलते हैं।
Target equation जो चाहिए → दी गई equations को ऐसे जोड़ो कि बीच की species cancel हो जाएँ

5️⃣ एन्ट्रॉपी (ΔS) — randomness का meter ⭐

S = अव्यवस्था (disorder) का माप   |   ΔS = qrev/T
  • Gas > Liquid > Solid (entropy का order)। Gas के moles बढ़ें अभिक्रिया में → ΔS positive।
  • पिघलना/boil होना → ΔS positive। जमना → negative।
  • द्वितीय नियम (Second Law): spontaneous process में universe की entropy बढ़ती है (ΔStotal = ΔSsys + ΔSsurr > 0)।
  • निकाय की entropy कम भी हो सकती है — पर surroundings की ज़्यादा बढ़नी चाहिए (जैसे पानी जमना: निकाय ordered, पर heat निकली → surroundings का randomness बढ़ा)।

💡 Feel: entropy = आपका कमरा — खुद बिगड़ता है, ठीक करने में मेहनत (energy) लगती है। Universe का कमरा भी बिगड़ रहा है। 😄

6️⃣ Gibbs मुक्त ऊर्जा (Gibbs Free Energy) — final referee ⭐⭐

ΔG = ΔH − TΔS   (constant T, P पर)
ΔGमतलब
NegativeSpontaneous — अभिक्रिया खुद होगी ✅
PositiveNon-spontaneous — energy डालनी पड़ेगी ❌
ZeroEquilibrium — दोनों तरफ बराबर ⚖️
  • ΔH −ve, ΔS +ve: हर temperature पर spontaneous।
  • ΔH +ve, ΔS −ve: कभी spontaneous नहीं।
  • ΔH +ve, ΔS +ve: HIGH temperature पर spontaneous (TΔS जीतता है) — जैसे पिघलना।
  • ΔH −ve, ΔS −ve: LOW temperature पर spontaneous — जैसे जमना।
  • Equilibrium से link: ΔG° = −RT ln K = −2.303 RT log K।
K > 1 → ΔG° negative → products favoured | K < 1 → reactants favoured

🎯 एक line में: ΔG negative = green signal. बाकी सब (ΔH, ΔS) सिर्फ advisors हैं, final फैसला ΔG का।

1️⃣ Basic Terms — system, surroundings, types

Thermodynamics = the accounting of energy — how much heat a reaction gives out or absorbs, how much work happens, and whether the reaction will occur on its own at all.

  • System: what we're watching (the mixture in the beaker). Surroundings: everything else. Together = the universe.
  • Open system: exchanges matter + energy (an open pot).
  • Closed system: exchanges only energy (a lidded pot).
  • Isolated system: exchanges nothing (a thermos flask).
  • State functions: depend only on start and finish — U, H, S, G, P, V, T. Path functions: the route matters — q (heat), w (work).
  • Extensive: depends on amount (mass, V, U, H) • Intensive: doesn't (T, P, density).

💡 Feel: a state function is your height (measured directly); a path function is how many steps you walked (depends on the route).

2️⃣ First Law — energy is never created or destroyed

ΔU = q + w   (ΔU = change in internal energy, q = heat, w = work done ON the system)
  • Sign convention (memorise): work/heat going INTO the system → positive. Work done BY the system / heat released → negative.
  • Expansion work: w = −PextΔV (when a gas expands, w is negative — the system did the work).
  • Reversible process: frictionless and infinitely slow — delivers maximum work. Irreversible = real life, less work.
  • Isothermal (ΔT=0): ΔU = 0 → q = −w • Adiabatic (q=0): ΔU = w • Isochoric (ΔV=0): w = 0 → ΔU = qv.

🎯 JEE trap: qv = ΔU (constant volume) and qp = ΔH (constant pressure) — heat becomes a state function under fixed conditions!

3️⃣ Enthalpy (ΔH) — heat at constant pressure

H = U + PV   →   ΔH = ΔU + ΔngRT (count only the gas moles!)
  • ΔH negative → exothermic (heat released — burning, condensation) • ΔH positive → endothermic (heat absorbed — melting, photosynthesis).
  • Standard enthalpy of reaction = Σ ΔH°f(products) − Σ ΔH°f(reactants). For elements in their standard state, ΔH°f = 0 (O₂ gas, C graphite, Br₂ liquid!).
  • Bond enthalpy method: ΔH = Σ BE(bonds broken in reactants) − Σ BE(bonds formed in products).

💡 Feel: breaking a bond = spending money (energy in); forming a bond = getting paid (energy out). The net is your profit or loss.

4️⃣ Hess's Law — any route, same total

Whether a reaction happens in one step or ten — the total ΔH is the same (because H is a state function!).

  • Add, subtract and multiply equations like algebra — ΔH follows along. Reverse an equation → flip the sign of ΔH.
  • Famous use: you can't measure ΔH for C + ½O₂ → CO directly (CO₂ also forms) — but you can add two routes to CO₂ and extract it.
  • The Born-Haber cycle (lattice enthalpy) and ΔHsolution come from the same idea.
Want a target equation → combine the given equations so the middle species cancel out

5️⃣ Entropy (ΔS) — the randomness meter ⭐

S = a measure of disorder   |   ΔS = qrev/T
  • Gas > Liquid > Solid (the entropy order). If gas moles increase in a reaction → ΔS is positive.
  • Melting/boiling → ΔS positive. Freezing → negative.
  • Second Law: in a spontaneous process, the entropy of the universe increases (ΔStotal = ΔSsys + ΔSsurr > 0).
  • The system's entropy may fall — but the surroundings must gain more (like water freezing: the system orders up, but the released heat randomises the surroundings).

💡 Feel: entropy is your room — it messes up by itself; tidying it takes effort (energy). The universe's room is also getting messier. 😄

6️⃣ Gibbs Free Energy — the final referee ⭐⭐

ΔG = ΔH − TΔS   (at constant T, P)
ΔGMeaning
NegativeSpontaneous — the reaction goes by itself ✅
PositiveNon-spontaneous — needs energy input ❌
ZeroEquilibrium — balanced both ways ⚖️
  • ΔH −ve, ΔS +ve: spontaneous at every temperature.
  • ΔH +ve, ΔS −ve: never spontaneous.
  • ΔH +ve, ΔS +ve: spontaneous at HIGH temperature (TΔS wins) — like melting.
  • ΔH −ve, ΔS −ve: spontaneous at LOW temperature — like freezing.
  • The equilibrium link: ΔG° = −RT ln K = −2.303 RT log K.
K > 1 → ΔG° negative → products favoured | K < 1 → reactants favoured

🎯 In one line: ΔG negative = green signal. ΔH and ΔS are just advisors — the final call belongs to ΔG.

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Ek reaction ka ΔH = −41.2 kJ at 100°C (373 K), Δn(gas) = −2. ΔU kitna? (R = 8.314 J/mol·K)
Answer dekho

ΔH = ΔU + Δn·RT → ΔU = ΔH − Δn·RT = −41.2 − (−2)(8.314 × 373/1000) = −41.2 + 6.2 = −35.0 kJ

Q2. C + O₂ → CO₂ (ΔH = −393.5), H₂ + ½O₂ → H₂O (ΔH = −285.8), CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O (ΔH = −890.3 kJ). CH₄ ki formation enthalpy nikalo (Hess).
Answer dekho

Target: C + 2H₂ → CH₄. Reverse the combustion: ΔHf = [−393.5 + 2(−285.8)] − (−890.3) = −965.1 + 890.3 = −74.8 kJ/mol

Q3. CH₄ + Cl₂ → CH₃Cl + HCl ka ΔH bond enthalpies se: BE(C—H)=414, Cl—Cl=243, C—Cl=326, H—Cl=431 kJ/mol.
Answer dekho

ΔH = ΣBE(bonds broken) − ΣBE(bonds formed) = (414 + 243) − (326 + 431) = 657 − 757 = −100 kJ (exothermic)

Q4. Ek reaction ka ΔH = +30 kJ, ΔS = +100 J/K. Kis temperature pe spontaneous hogi?
Answer dekho

Spontaneous jab ΔG < 0 → T > ΔH/ΔS = 30000/100 = 300 K se upar (27°C ke upar spontaneous)

Q5. 2 mol ideal gas ka isothermal reversible expansion 10 L → 20 L at 300 K. Work nikalo.
Answer dekho

w = −2.303 nRT log(V₂/V₁) = −2.303 × 2 × 8.314 × 300 × log 2 ≈ −3.46 kJ (gas ne work KIYA, isliye negative)

Q6. Inme entropy badh rahi ya ghatt rahi: (a) H₂O(l) → H₂O(g) (b) N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃
Answer dekho

(a) badh rahi (liquid → gas = zyada randomness) • (b) ghatt rahi (4 gas molecules → 2 gas molecules)

Q7. Coffee-cup calorimeter me 0.5 mol neutralization se 50 mL solution ka temp 6.9°C badha. ΔH per mol? (c = 4.18 J/g·°C)
Answer dekho

q = mcΔT = 50 × 4.18 × 6.9 ≈ 1442 J (0.5 mol se) → ΔH = −1442/0.5 ≈ −2.88 kJ/mol (negative kyunki heat RELEASE hui)

Q8. Ek reaction ka K = 2×10³ at 298 K. ΔG° kitna?
Answer dekho

ΔG° = −2.303 RT log K = −2.303 × 8.314 × 298 × log(2×10³) = −2.303 × 8.314 × 298 × 3.301 ≈ −18.8 kJ/mol

Q9. Inme state function kaunse hain: q, w, U, H, S, G?
Answer dekho

U, H, S, G — sirf state pe depend karte hain. q (heat) aur w (work) PATH functions hain — rasta matter karta hai!

Q10. Adiabatic free expansion (vacuum me) me q, w, ΔU kitne?
Answer dekho

Free expansion: external pressure = 0 → w = 0. Adiabatic: q = 0. To ΔU = q + w = 0 — temperature same rehta hai (ideal gas).
