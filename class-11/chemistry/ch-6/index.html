📖 Full Explanation (Long Notes)

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Dynamic Equilibrium — ruka hua nahi, chalta hua balance

Reversible reaction me ek waqt aata hai jab forward speed = backward speed ho jaati hai → concentrations constant dikhti hain, par reaction ruki nahi — dono taraf chalti rehti hai. Isko kehte hain dynamic equilibrium.

  • Physical equilibrium: solid ⇌ liquid ⇌ gas, sugar ⇌ solution.
  • Chemical equilibrium: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ (Haber process — khana wahi se aata hai! 🌾).
  • Equilibrium closed system me hi aata hai — khule me products bhaag jaate hain, reaction complete ho jaati hai.

💡 Feel: mall ki escalator — upar jaane wale = neeche aane wale → har floor par log same dikhte hain, par sab chal rahe hain.

2️⃣ Equilibrium Constant (K) — kitna aage gayi reaction ⭐

aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD   →   Kc = [C]ᶜ[D]ᵈ / [A]ᵃ[B]ᵇ
  • K > 10³: products zyada (reaction aage tak gayi) • K < 10⁻³: reactants zyada (reaction peeche hi rahi) • beech me: dono mix.
  • Kp (gases ke liye, partial pressures se): Kp = Kc(RT)Δn, Δn = gas moles (products − reactants).
  • Rules: equation reverse → K becomes 1/K • ×2 karo → K² • do equations jodo → K₁ × K₂.
  • Pure solids/liquids K me nahi aate (unke "concentration" constant).

🎯 K sirf temperature par depend karta hai — concentration, pressure, catalyst se K nahi badalta!

3️⃣ Reaction Quotient (Q) + Direction ka pata

Q ka formula K jaisa hi — par kisi bhi moment par nikalo
  • Q < K: products kam hain → reaction aage jayegi (→ products).
  • Q > K: products zyada hain → reaction peeche jayegi (← reactants).
  • Q = K: equilibrium par hain — ruko mat, enjoy karo. ⚖️

💡 Feel: Q = abhi kahan ho, K = manzil. Q chhota → aage badho, Q bada → wapas aao.

4️⃣ Le Chatelier's Principle — system ka ego ⭐

Equilibrium par pressure badhao (concentration, pressure, temperature change) → system usko utralne ki koshish karta hai:

ChangeSystem kya karta hai
Reactant add karoForward shift — use karke khatam karega
Product nikaloForward shift — aur banayega
Pressure badhao (gas)Kam gas-moles wali side shift
Temperature badhaoEndothermic side shift (heat kha jayega)
  • Catalyst: equilibrium jaldi pahunchata hai, par K ya position nahi badalta — dono taraf ki speed barabar badhata hai.
  • Inert gas: constant V par add karo → koi effect nahi; constant P par → volume badhta hai (kam moles wali side ka ulta).

🌾 Haber process: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ + heat → isliye high pressure (kam moles: 4→2) + moderate temp + catalyst.

5️⃣ Acids & Bases — 3 definitions

  1. Arrhenius: acid paani me H⁺ deta hai (HCl), base OH⁻ deta hai (NaOH). Sirf paani me, limited.
  2. Bronsted-Lowry: acid = proton (H⁺) donor, base = proton acceptor. Isliye NH₃ bhi base (H⁺ accept karti hai), paani dono (amphiprotic!).
  3. Lewis: acid = electron-pair acceptor (BF₃, AlCl₃, H⁺), base = electron-pair donor (NH₃, OH⁻). Sabse broad.
Conjugate pairs: acid − H⁺ = uski conjugate base (HA → A⁻) | base + H⁺ = conjugate acid
  • Strong acid → weak conjugate base (HCl strong → Cl⁻ bekar base). Aur ulta.
  • Strong acids (ratta): HCl, HBr, HI, HNO₃, H₂SO₄, HClO₄. Strong bases: NaOH, KOH, Ba(OH)₂.

6️⃣ pH Scale — acidity ka number ⭐

pH = −log[H⁺]   |   pOH = −log[OH⁻]   |   pH + pOH = 14 (25°C par)
  • pH < 7 acidic • = 7 neutral • > 7 basic. 25°C par Kw = [H⁺][OH⁻] = 10⁻¹⁴.
  • pH ka 1 point = 10x ka difference! pH 3 vs pH 5 → 100 guna zyada acidic.
  • Weak acid/base poora dissociate nahi hote → unke liye Ka/Kb use hoti hai: chhoti Ka = kamzor acid. pKa = −log Ka (chhota pKa = strong acid).
  • % dissociation weak acid ka chhota hota hai, dilution par badhta hai (Ostwald dilution law).

🎯 Trap: strong ≠ concentrated! "Strong" = 100% dissociation, "concentrated" = zyada amount. HCl strong hai chahe patla ho.

7️⃣ Buffers + Salt Hydrolysis

  • Buffer: weak acid + uska conjugate base salt (CH₃COOH + CH₃COONa) ya weak base + salt (NH₄OH + NH₄Cl) → pH ko resist karta hai. Blood ka buffer (H₂CO₃/HCO₃⁻) humein zinda rakhta hai! 🩸
  • Henderson equation: pH = pKa + log([salt]/[acid]) — buffer ka pH nikaalne ka shortcut.
  • Salt hydrolysis: salt paani me acid/base bana sakta hai: strong acid + strong base → neutral (NaCl); strong + weak → usi strong wale ki taraf (CH₃COONa = basic, NH₄Cl = acidic).

8️⃣ Solubility Product (Ksp) — precipitate kab

AxBy ⇌ xA + yB   →   Ksp = [A]ˣ[B]ʸ
  • Ionic product > Ksp → precipitate girega • = Ksp → saturated • < Ksp → aur gholega.
  • AgCl (1:1): Ksp = s² • CaF₂ (1:2): Ksp = 4s³ (s = solubility).
  • Common ion effect: common ion wala salt daalo → solubility ghat-ti hai (Le Chatelier wapas!). Jaise AgCl, NaCl wale paani me kam gholega.

💡 Feel: Ksp = paani ki "seating capacity" — usse zyada log (ions) aaye toh neeche baith jayenge (precipitate).

1️⃣ गतिक साम्य (Dynamic Equilibrium) — रुका हुआ नहीं, चलता हुआ balance

Reversible अभिक्रिया में एक वक़्त आता है जब forward speed = backward speed हो जाती है → concentrations constant दिखती हैं, पर अभिक्रिया रुकी नहीं — दोनों तरफ चलती रहती है। इसे dynamic equilibrium कहते हैं।

  • भौतिक साम्य: solid ⇌ liquid ⇌ gas, चीनी ⇌ solution।
  • रासायनिक साम्य: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ (Haber process — खाना वहीं से आता है! 🌾)।
  • Equilibrium बंद निकाय में ही आता है — खुले में products भाग जाते हैं, अभिक्रिया complete हो जाती है।

💡 Feel: mall की escalator — ऊपर जाने वाले = नीचे आने वाले → हर floor पर लोग same दिखते हैं, पर सब चल रहे हैं।

2️⃣ साम्य स्थिरांक (Equilibrium Constant, K) — अभिक्रिया कितना आगे गई ⭐

aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD   →   Kc = [C]ᶜ[D]ᵈ / [A]ᵃ[B]ᵇ
  • K > 10³: products ज़्यादा (अभिक्रिया आगे तक गई) • K < 10⁻³: reactants ज़्यादा • बीच में: दोनों mix।
  • Kp (gases के लिए, partial pressures से): Kp = Kc(RT)Δn, Δn = gas moles (products − reactants)।
  • Rules: equation reverse → K becomes 1/K • ×2 करो → K² • दो equations जोड़ो → K₁ × K₂।
  • Pure solids/liquids K में नहीं आते (उनकी "concentration" constant)।

🎯 K सिर्फ temperature पर depend करता है — concentration, pressure, catalyst से K नहीं बदलता!

3️⃣ अभिक्रिया गुणनफल (Q) + दिशा का पता

Q का formula K जैसा ही — पर किसी भी moment पर निकालो
  • Q < K: products कम हैं → अभिक्रिया आगे जाएगी (→ products)।
  • Q > K: products ज़्यादा हैं → अभिक्रिया पीछे जाएगी (← reactants)।
  • Q = K: equilibrium पर हैं। ⚖️

💡 Feel: Q = अभी कहाँ हो, K = मंज़िल। Q छोटा → आगे बढ़ो, Q बड़ा → वापस आओ।

4️⃣ Le Chatelier का सिद्धांत — निकाय का ego ⭐

Equilibrium पर दबाव डालो (concentration, pressure, temperature change) → निकाय उसको उलटने की कोशिश करता है:

Changeनिकाय क्या करता है
Reactant add करोForward shift — use करके खत्म करेगा
Product निकालोForward shift — और बनाएगा
Pressure बढ़ाओ (gas)कम gas-moles वाली side shift
Temperature बढ़ाओEndothermic side shift (heat खा जाएगा)
  • उत्प्रेरक (Catalyst): equilibrium जल्दी पहुँचाता है, पर K या position नहीं बदलता — दोनों तरफ की speed बराबर बढ़ाता है।
  • Inert gas: constant V पर add करो → कोई effect नहीं; constant P पर → volume बढ़ता है।

🌾 Haber process: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ + heat → इसलिए high pressure (कम moles: 4→2) + moderate temp + catalyst।

5️⃣ अम्ल और क्षार (Acids & Bases) — 3 definitions

  1. Arrhenius: acid पानी में H⁺ देता है (HCl), base OH⁻ देता है (NaOH)। सिर्फ पानी में, limited।
  2. Bronsted-Lowry: acid = proton (H⁺) donor, base = proton acceptor। इसलिए NH₃ भी base (H⁺ accept करती है), पानी दोनों (amphiprotic!)।
  3. Lewis: acid = इलेक्ट्रॉन-pair acceptor (BF₃, AlCl₃, H⁺), base = इलेक्ट्रॉन-pair donor (NH₃, OH⁻)। सबसे broad।
Conjugate pairs: acid − H⁺ = उसकी conjugate base (HA → A⁻) | base + H⁺ = conjugate acid
  • Strong acid → weak conjugate base (HCl strong → Cl⁻ बेकार base)। और उल्टा।
  • Strong acids (रट्टा): HCl, HBr, HI, HNO₃, H₂SO₄, HClO₄। Strong bases: NaOH, KOH, Ba(OH)₂।

6️⃣ pH स्केल — acidity का number ⭐

pH = −log[H⁺]   |   pOH = −log[OH⁻]   |   pH + pOH = 14 (25°C पर)
  • pH < 7 acidic • = 7 neutral • > 7 basic। 25°C पर Kw = [H⁺][OH⁻] = 10⁻¹⁴।
  • pH का 1 point = 10x का difference! pH 3 vs pH 5 → 100 गुना ज़्यादा acidic।
  • दुर्बल अम्ल/क्षार (Weak acid/base) पूरा dissociate नहीं होते → उनके लिए Ka/Kb use होती है: छोटी Ka = कमज़ोर acid। pKa = −log Ka (छोटा pKa = strong acid)।
  • % dissociation weak acid का छोटा होता है, dilution पर बढ़ता है (Ostwald dilution law)।

🎯 Trap: strong ≠ concentrated! "Strong" = 100% dissociation, "concentrated" = ज़्यादा amount। HCl strong है चाहे पतला हो।

7️⃣ बफर (Buffers) + लवण जल-अपघटन (Salt Hydrolysis)

  • Buffer: weak acid + उसका conjugate base salt (CH₃COOH + CH₃COONa) या weak base + salt (NH₄OH + NH₄Cl) → pH को resist करता है। खून का buffer (H₂CO₃/HCO₃⁻) हमें ज़िंदा रखता है! 🩸
  • Henderson समीकरण: pH = pKa + log([salt]/[acid]) — buffer का pH निकालने का shortcut।
  • Salt hydrolysis: लवण पानी में acid/base बना सकता है: strong acid + strong base → neutral (NaCl); strong + weak → उसी strong वाले की तरफ (CH₃COONa = basic, NH₄Cl = acidic)।

8️⃣ विलेयता गुणनफल (Ksp) — अवक्षेप (precipitate) कब

AxBy ⇌ xA + yB   →   Ksp = [A]ˣ[B]ʸ
  • Ionic product > Ksp → अवक्षेप गिरेगा • = Ksp → saturated • < Ksp → और घुलेगा।
  • AgCl (1:1): Ksp = s² • CaF₂ (1:2): Ksp = 4s³ (s = solubility)।
  • समान आयन प्रभाव (Common ion effect): common ion वाला लवण डालो → solubility घटती है (Le Chatelier वापस!)। जैसे AgCl, NaCl वाले पानी में कम घुलेगा।

💡 Feel: Ksp = पानी की "seating capacity" — उससे ज़्यादा लोग (ions) आएँ तो नीचे बैठ जाएँगे (precipitate)।

1️⃣ Dynamic Equilibrium — a running balance, not a stop

In a reversible reaction a moment comes when the forward rate equals the backward rate → concentrations look constant, but the reaction has not stopped — it keeps running both ways. That's dynamic equilibrium.

  • Physical equilibrium: solid ⇌ liquid ⇌ gas, sugar ⇌ solution.
  • Chemical equilibrium: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ (the Haber process — our food literally comes from it! 🌾).
  • Equilibrium needs a closed system — in an open one the products escape and the reaction runs to completion.

💡 Feel: a mall escalator — people going up = people coming down → every floor looks equally full, yet everyone is moving.

2️⃣ Equilibrium Constant (K) — how far the reaction went ⭐

aA + bB ⇌ cC + dD   →   Kc = [C]ᶜ[D]ᵈ / [A]ᵃ[B]ᵇ
  • K > 10³: products dominate (reaction went far) • K < 10⁻³: reactants dominate (barely moved) • in between: a real mixture.
  • Kp (for gases, using partial pressures): Kp = Kc(RT)Δn, where Δn = gas moles (products − reactants).
  • Rules: reverse the equation → K becomes 1/K • double it → K² • add two equations → K₁ × K₂.
  • Pure solids and liquids do not appear in K (their "concentration" is constant).

🎯 K depends only on temperature — concentration, pressure and catalysts do not change K!

3️⃣ Reaction Quotient (Q) — which way will it go?

Q uses the same formula as K — but at any moment, not just at equilibrium
  • Q < K: too few products → reaction moves forward (→ products).
  • Q > K: too many products → reaction moves backward (← reactants).
  • Q = K: already at equilibrium. ⚖️

💡 Feel: Q is where you are, K is the destination. Q small → go forward; Q big → come back.

4️⃣ Le Chatelier's Principle — the system pushes back ⭐

Disturb an equilibrium (change concentration, pressure or temperature) → the system tries to undo your change:

ChangeWhat the system does
Add a reactantShifts forward — uses it up
Remove a productShifts forward — makes more
Increase pressure (gases)Shifts to the side with fewer gas moles
Increase temperatureShifts to the endothermic side (eats the heat)
  • Catalyst: reaches equilibrium faster but changes neither K nor the position — it speeds both directions equally.
  • Inert gas: added at constant V → no effect; at constant P → volume rises.

🌾 Haber process: N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ + heat → hence high pressure (fewer moles: 4→2) + moderate temperature + catalyst.

5️⃣ Acids & Bases — 3 definitions

  1. Arrhenius: an acid gives H⁺ in water (HCl), a base gives OH⁻ (NaOH). Limited to water.
  2. Bronsted-Lowry: acid = proton (H⁺) donor, base = proton acceptor. That's why NH₃ is a base (it accepts H⁺) and water is both (amphiprotic!).
  3. Lewis: acid = electron-pair acceptor (BF₃, AlCl₃, H⁺), base = electron-pair donor (NH₃, OH⁻). The broadest view.
Conjugate pairs: acid − H⁺ = its conjugate base (HA → A⁻) | base + H⁺ = conjugate acid
  • Strong acid → weak conjugate base (HCl is strong → Cl⁻ is a useless base). And vice versa.
  • Strong acids (memorise): HCl, HBr, HI, HNO₃, H₂SO₄, HClO₄. Strong bases: NaOH, KOH, Ba(OH)₂.

6️⃣ The pH Scale — acidity as a number ⭐

pH = −log[H⁺]   |   pOH = −log[OH⁻]   |   pH + pOH = 14 (at 25°C)
  • pH < 7 acidic • = 7 neutral • > 7 basic. At 25°C, Kw = [H⁺][OH⁻] = 10⁻¹⁴.
  • 1 pH point = a 10x difference! pH 3 vs pH 5 → 100 times more acidic.
  • Weak acids/bases don't fully dissociate → use Ka/Kb: smaller Ka = weaker acid. pKa = −log Ka (small pKa = strong acid).
  • % dissociation of a weak acid is small and grows on dilution (Ostwald's dilution law).

🎯 Trap: strong ≠ concentrated! "Strong" means 100% dissociation; "concentrated" means a lot of it. HCl is strong even when dilute.

7️⃣ Buffers + Salt Hydrolysis

  • Buffer: a weak acid + its conjugate-base salt (CH₃COOH + CH₃COONa), or a weak base + its salt (NH₄OH + NH₄Cl) → it resists pH change. The blood buffer (H₂CO₃/HCO₃⁻) keeps us alive! 🩸
  • Henderson equation: pH = pKa + log([salt]/[acid]) — the shortcut for a buffer's pH.
  • Salt hydrolysis: a salt can make water acidic or basic: strong acid + strong base → neutral (NaCl); strong + weak → follows the stronger parent (CH₃COONa = basic, NH₄Cl = acidic).

8️⃣ Solubility Product (Ksp) — when does it precipitate?

AxBy ⇌ xA + yB   →   Ksp = [A]ˣ[B]ʸ
  • Ionic product > Ksp → precipitate forms • = Ksp → saturated • < Ksp → more can dissolve.
  • AgCl (1:1): Ksp = s² • CaF₂ (1:2): Ksp = 4s³ (s = solubility).
  • Common ion effect: add a salt sharing an ion → solubility drops (Le Chatelier again!). E.g. AgCl dissolves less in NaCl solution.

💡 Feel: Ksp is water's "seating capacity" — bring more guests (ions) than that and they sit down as a precipitate.

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ ke liye Kc likho. Equilibrium pe [N₂]=0.1, [H₂]=0.3, [NH₃]=0.2 M ho to Kc?
Answer dekho

Kc = [NH₃]²/([N₂][H₂]³) = (0.2)²/(0.1 × 0.027) = 0.04/0.0027 ≈ 14.8

Q2. Ek reaction ka Kc = 2 at 400 K, Δn = +1. Kp kitna? (R = 0.0821)
Answer dekho

Kp = Kc(RT)Δn = 2 × (0.0821 × 400)¹ = 65.7

Q3. Ek reaction ka K = 50. Kisi moment pe Q = 200 hai. Reaction kis direction me jayegi?
Answer dekho

Q > K → products zyada hain → reaction BACKWARD (reactants ki taraf) jayegi jab tak Q = K na ho.

Q4. N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃ + heat me (a) pressure badhane (b) temperature badhane se kya hoga?
Answer dekho

(a) Pressure ↑ → forward (kam moles: 4→2) • (b) Temperature ↑ → reaction exothermic hai, to backward (system heat kam karna chahta hai). NH₃ ke liye: high P, moderate T!

Q5. 0.001 M HCl ka pH? Aur 0.01 M NaOH ka?
Answer dekho

HCl strong: [H⁺] = 10⁻³ → pH = 3. NaOH: [OH⁻] = 10⁻² → pOH = 2 → pH = 14 − 2 = 12

Q6. Ek solution ka pH = 4.5 hai. [H⁺] aur pOH batao.
Answer dekho

[H⁺] = 10⁻⁴·⁵ = 3.16 × 10⁻⁵ M; pOH = 14 − 4.5 = 9.5

Q7. 0.1 M weak acid HA ka pH = 3 hai. Ka nikalo.
Answer dekho

[H⁺] = 10⁻³ = C·α → α = 0.01. Ka = Cα² = 0.1 × (0.01)² = 10⁻⁵ (pKa = 5)

Q8. CH₃COOH (0.1 M) + CH₃COONa (0.1 M) buffer ka pH? (pKa = 4.74)
Answer dekho

Henderson: pH = pKa + log([salt]/[acid]) = 4.74 + log(1) = 4.74

Q9. AgCl ka Ksp = 1.8×10⁻¹⁰. Pure water me solubility (mol/L)?
Answer dekho

AgCl (1:1): s = √Ksp = √(1.8×10⁻¹⁰) ≈ 1.34 × 10⁻⁵ M

Q10. CaF₂ ka Ksp = 4×10⁻¹¹. Solubility? Aur common ion (NaF add) ka effect?
Answer dekho

CaF₂: Ksp = 4s³ → s = ∛(10⁻¹¹) ≈ 2.15 × 10⁻⁴ M. NaF (F⁻ common ion) add karne se solubility GHATTI hai (Le Chatelier — equilibrium peeche).
