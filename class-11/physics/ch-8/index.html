🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Stress aur Strain — Basic Jodi

Solids bhi deform hote hain — bas thoda sa! Deformation ko measure karne ke do terms:

  • Stress ⭐: force per unit area = F/A (unit: Pa = N/m²). Teen types: tensile/compressive (perpendicular), shearing (parallel), hydraulic (sab taraf se).
  • Strain: fractional change = ΔL/L (length), ΔV/V (volume), ya shear angle θ. Unitless hai!
  • Elasticity: force hatate hi shape wapas (spring, rubber approx). Plasticity: permanent deformation (clay, dough).
  • Elastic limit: is point tak body wapas aati hai; uske aage permanent set.

💡 Feel: stress woh pressure hai jo body FEEL karti hai (force/area), strain woh "kitna khich gayi" ka measure (percentage jaisa).

2️⃣ Hooke's Law aur Stress-Strain Curve ⭐

Hooke's law: elastic limit ke ANDAR, stress ∝ strain — stress/strain = constant = modulus of elasticity.

Stress-strain curve (exam favourite, har point yaad karo):

  • O → A (proportional limit): straight line, Hooke's law valid.
  • A → B (elastic limit / yield point): ab curve, lekin wapas aa sakti hai.
  • B → C (plastic region): strain tezi se badhta hai, permanent set shuru. C = ultimate tensile strength (max stress).
  • D (fracture point): toot gayi! C ke baad neck banti hai (wire patli), phir break.
  • Steel vs rubber: steel ka curve steep (bada modulus), rubber ka flat. Steel zyada elastic hai rubber se — HAAN, definition me! ⭐ (kam strain same stress pe = zyada elastic)

🎯 Yaad rakho: elasticity ka matlab "stretch hona" NAHI, "wapas aana + kam deform hona" hai — isliye steel > rubber in elasticity!

3️⃣ Teen Moduli — Young, Bulk, Shear ⭐

ModulusFormulaKya resist karta hai
Young's (Y)(F/A)/(ΔL/L)length change (stretch/compress)
Bulk (B)−p/(ΔV/V) = −pV/ΔVvolume change (pressure)
Shear / Rigidity (G ya η)(F/A)/θshape change (twist)
  • Compressibility: k = 1/B — kitna aasani se compress hota hai.
  • Poisson's ratio: σ = lateral strain / longitudinal strain — khichne pe patla hona! (~0.3 metals, < 0.5 hamesha)
  • Relations ⭐: Y = 2G(1 + σ) = 3B(1 − 2σ) = 9BG/(3B + G).
  • Elongation of wire under own weight: ΔL = ρgL²/2Y (half the load-effective, kyunki weight distributed hai!).

💡 Feel: Y length ka boss, B volume ka, G shape ka. Steel ka Y ≈ 2×10¹¹ Pa — isliye buildings me steel rods!

4️⃣ Elastic Potential Energy — Stretch Ki Energy

Wire stretch karne me jo kaam karte ho, woh elastic PE banke store hota hai:

  • Energy stored: U = ½ × stress × strain × volume = ½FΔL (stretching force ke against work).
  • Energy density (per volume) ⭐: u = ½ × stress × strain = (stress)²/2Y = ½Y(strain)².
  • Units: J/m³ — JEE me direct formula aata hai!
  • Applications: pole vault ka pole, bow and arrow, shock absorbers, bungee jumping — sab elastic energy storage!

🎯 ½ stress × strain — spring ki ½kx² ki yaad dilao. Har elastic material ek chhupa spring hai!

5️⃣ Applications aur Dimensional Tricks

  • Wire under load: ΔL = FL/AY — load double ya length double → extension double; radius double → extension 1/4 (A = πr²!). ⭐
  • Bridge design: girders ka cross-section I-shape kyun? — bending kam karne ke liye material center se door (neutral axis se door zyada strength).
  • Pillars chhote aur motey kyun: lambi patli pillars buckle ho jaati hain — isliye bridges ke pillars motey.
  • Maximum height of a mountain on Earth ~10 km: isse zyada height pe base ka stress rock ke elastic limit se zyada — neeche ka rock beh jaayega!
  • Submarine aur deep-sea pressure: bulk modulus se volume compression calculate — deep sea me pressure = ρgh + atmospheric.

💡 ΔL = FL/AY har numerical ka heart hai — F, L, A, Y me se koi bhi 3 diye ho, chautha nikal aata hai!

1️⃣ प्रतिबल और विकृति — मूल जोड़ी

ठोस भी विकृत होते हैं — थोड़ा सा! विकृति मापने के दो पद:

  • प्रतिबल (stress) ⭐: बल प्रति क्षेत्रफल = F/A (मात्रक: Pa = N/m²)। प्रकार: तनन/संपीड़न (लंबवत), अपरूपण (समांतर), द्रवस्थैतिक (सब ओर से)।
  • विकृति (strain): भinnात्मक परिवर्तन = ΔL/L (लंबाई), ΔV/V (आयतन), या अपरूपण कोण θ। मात्रक-रहित!
  • प्रत्यास्थता: बल हटते ही आकार वापस। सुघट्यता (plasticity): स्थायी विकृति (मिट्टी)।
  • प्रत्यास्थ सीमा: इस बिंदु तक पिंड वापस आता है; आगे स्थायी विकृति।

💡 प्रतिबल वह दबाव है जो पिंड अनुभव करता है, विकृति "कितना खिंचा" का माप।

2️⃣ हुक का नियम और प्रतिबल-विकृति वक्र ⭐

हुक का नियम: प्रत्यास्थ सीमा के अंदर, प्रतिबल ∝ विकृति — प्रतिबल/विकृति = नियतांक = प्रत्यास्थता गुणांक।

प्रतिबल-विकृति वक्र (परीक्षा प्रिय):

  • O → A (आनुपातिक सीमा): सरल रेखा, हुक नियम मान्य।
  • A → B (प्रत्यास्थ सीमा / पराभव बिंदु): वक्र, पर वापसी संभव।
  • B → C (सुघट्य क्षेत्र): स्थायी विकृति शुरू। C = परम तनन सामर्थ्य।
  • D (भंगुरता बिंदु): टूट गया! C के बाद ग्रीवा (neck) बनती है, फिर भंग।
  • स्टील बनाम रबर: स्टील का वक्र खड़ा (बड़ा गुणांक)। स्टील रबर से अधिक प्रत्यास्थ — परिभाषा में! ⭐

🎯 प्रत्यास्थता का अर्थ "खिंचना" नहीं, "वापस आना + कम विकृत होना" है — इसलिए स्टील > रबर!

3️⃣ तीन गुणांक — यंग, आयतन, अपरूपण ⭐

गुणांकसूत्रकिसका प्रतिरोध
यंग (Y)(F/A)/(ΔL/L)लंबाई परिवर्तन
आयतन (B)−p/(ΔV/V) = −pV/ΔVआयतन परिवर्तन
अपरूपण / दृढ़ता (G)(F/A)/θआकार परिवर्तन
  • संपीड्यता: k = 1/B।
  • पॉइसॉन अनुपात: σ = पार्श्व विकृति / अनुदैर्घ्य विकृति — खिंचने पर पतला होना! (< 0.5 सदैव)
  • संबंध ⭐: Y = 2G(1 + σ) = 3B(1 − 2σ) = 9BG/(3B + G)।
  • स्व-भार से तार की दीर्घन: ΔL = ρgL²/2Y (भार वितरित है, इसलिए आधा प्रभावी)।

💡 Y लंबाई का, B आयतन का, G आकार का। स्टील का Y ≈ 2×10¹¹ Pa — इसलिए भवनों में स्टील!

4️⃣ प्रत्यास्थ स्थितिज ऊर्जा

तार खींचने में किया कार्य प्रत्यास्थ PE बनकर संचित होता है:

  • संचित ऊर्जा: U = ½ × प्रतिबल × विकृति × आयतन = ½FΔL।
  • ऊर्जा घनत्व ⭐: u = ½ × प्रतिबल × विकृति = (प्रतिबल)²/2Y = ½Y(विकृति)² — J/m³।
  • अनुप्रयोग: पोल वॉल्ट का डंडा, धनुष-बाण, शॉक एब्ज़ॉर्बर, बंजी जंपिंग!

🎯 ½ प्रतिबल × विकृति — स्प्रिंग की ½kx² याद करें। हर प्रत्यास्थ पदार्थ एक छिपी स्प्रिंग है!

5️⃣ अनुप्रयोग

  • भारित तार: ΔL = FL/AY — भार या लंबाई दुगुनी → दीर्घन दुगुना; त्रिज्या दुगुनी → दीर्घन 1/4! ⭐
  • सेतु डिज़ाइन: गर्डर I-आकार क्यों? — मोड़ कम करने के लिए पदार्थ केंद्र से दूर।
  • स्तंभ छोटे और मोटे क्यों: लंबे पतले स्तंभ बकल (buckle) हो जाते हैं।
  • पर्वत की अधिकतम ऊँचाई ~10 km: आधार का प्रतिबल शैल की प्रत्यास्थ सीमा से अधिक हो जाएगा!
  • पनडुब्बी: गहरे समुद्र में दाब = ρgh + वायुमंडलीय — आयतन गुणांक से संपीडन।

💡 ΔL = FL/AY हर numerical का हृदय है!

1️⃣ Stress and Strain — The Basic Pair

Solids deform too — just a little! Two terms measure deformation:

  • Stress ⭐: force per unit area = F/A (unit: Pa = N/m²). Three types: tensile/compressive (perpendicular), shearing (parallel), hydraulic (from all sides).
  • Strain: fractional change = ΔL/L (length), ΔV/V (volume), or shear angle θ. Unitless!
  • Elasticity: shape returns when the force is removed. Plasticity: permanent deformation (clay, dough).
  • Elastic limit: up to this point the body recovers; beyond it, a permanent set.

💡 Feel: stress is the pressure the body FEELS (force/area); strain is the "how much did it stretch" measure (like a percentage).

2️⃣ Hooke's Law and the Stress-Strain Curve ⭐

Hooke's law: WITHIN the elastic limit, stress ∝ strain — stress/strain = constant = modulus of elasticity.

The stress-strain curve (exam favourite — know every point):

  • O → A (proportional limit): straight line, Hooke's law holds.
  • A → B (elastic limit / yield point): now curved, but recovery still possible.
  • B → C (plastic region): strain grows fast, permanent set begins. C = ultimate tensile strength (maximum stress).
  • D (fracture point): it breaks! After C a neck forms (wire thins), then it snaps.
  • Steel vs rubber: steel's curve is steep (large modulus), rubber's flat. Steel is MORE elastic than rubber — by definition! ⭐ (less strain for the same stress = more elastic)

🎯 Remember: elasticity does NOT mean "stretchy" — it means "returns + deforms little" — that is why steel > rubber in elasticity!

3️⃣ Three Moduli — Young, Bulk, Shear ⭐

ModulusFormulaResists
Young's (Y)(F/A)/(ΔL/L)length change (stretch/compress)
Bulk (B)−p/(ΔV/V) = −pV/ΔVvolume change (pressure)
Shear / Rigidity (G)(F/A)/θshape change (twist)
  • Compressibility: k = 1/B — how easily it compresses.
  • Poisson's ratio: σ = lateral strain / longitudinal strain — thinning when stretched! (~0.3 metals, always < 0.5)
  • Relations ⭐: Y = 2G(1 + σ) = 3B(1 − 2σ) = 9BG/(3B + G).
  • Elongation under own weight: ΔL = ρgL²/2Y (half the effective load, since weight is distributed!).

💡 Feel: Y bosses length, B volume, G shape. Steel's Y ≈ 2×10¹¹ Pa — that is why buildings use steel rods!

4️⃣ Elastic Potential Energy — The Energy of Stretching

The work you do stretching a wire is stored as elastic PE:

  • Energy stored: U = ½ × stress × strain × volume = ½FΔL (work against the stretching force).
  • Energy density (per volume) ⭐: u = ½ × stress × strain = (stress)²/2Y = ½Y(strain)².
  • Units: J/m³ — JEE asks the formula directly!
  • Applications: pole-vault pole, bow and arrow, shock absorbers, bungee jumping — all elastic energy storage!

🎯 ½ stress × strain — recall the spring's ½kx². Every elastic material is a hidden spring!

5️⃣ Applications and Quick Tricks

  • Wire under load: ΔL = FL/AY — double the load or length → double the extension; double the radius → quarter the extension (A = πr²!). ⭐
  • Bridge design: why I-shaped girders? — material far from the center resists bending better.
  • Why pillars are short and thick: long thin pillars buckle.
  • Maximum mountain height ~10 km: taller than this and the base stress exceeds the rock's elastic limit — the bottom rock would flow!
  • Submarines and deep-sea pressure: pressure = ρgh + atmospheric — bulk modulus gives the volume compression.

💡 ΔL = FL/AY is the heart of every numerical — given any three of F, L, A, Y, the fourth falls out!

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Wire pe 100 N force, cross-section 1 mm². Stress?
Answer dekho

Stress = F/A = 100/10⁻⁶ = 10⁸ Pa.

Q2. 2 m wire (Y = 2×10¹¹ Pa, A = 1 mm²) pe 200 N load. Extension?
Answer dekho

ΔL = FL/AY = 200×2/(10⁻⁶×2×10¹¹) = 2 mm.

Q3. Same material ki do wires: B double radius, double length, same load. Extension ratio (B/A)?
Answer dekho

ΔL ∝ L/A = L/r² → (2L)/(4r²) = 1/2 — B ka extension A ka aadha!

Q4. Steel aur rubber me zyada elastic kaun?
Answer dekho

Steel — same stress pe bahut kam strain (modulus zyada). Elasticity = kam deform hona, zyada stretch hona nahi!

Q5. Stress-strain curve pe 'ultimate tensile strength' kaunsa point hai?
Answer dekho

Curve ka maximum point (C) — iske baad neck banti hai aur D pe fracture.

Q6. Compressibility k = 5×10⁻¹⁰ /Pa. Bulk modulus?
Answer dekho

B = 1/k = 2×10⁹ Pa.

Q7. Poisson's ratio 0.3, Young's modulus Y. Shear modulus G?
Answer dekho

G = Y/2(1+σ) = Y/2.6 ≈ 0.385Y.

Q8. Wire ko 0.1% strain tak stretch kiya (Y = 2×10¹¹ Pa). Energy density?
Answer dekho

u = ½Y(strain)² = ½×2×10¹¹×(10⁻³)² = 10⁵ J/m³.

Q9. 10 m lambi wire (density ρ) apne weight se kitni stretch hoti hai?
Answer dekho

ΔL = ρgL²/2Y = ρ×9.8×100/2Y (distributed weight ka effective half).

Q10. Hooke's law kab tak valid hai?
Answer dekho

Proportional limit (point A) tak — stress ∝ strain. Uske aage curve, phir elastic limit B tak recovery possible.
