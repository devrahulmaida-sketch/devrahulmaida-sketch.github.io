Work, Energy and Power

कार्य, ऊर्जा और शक्ति

Kaam, capacity aur speed — W = Fs cosθ, KE+PE conservation, collisions

JEE Weightage: HIGH Video: 1 min 7 sec Long + Short notes Hindi • English • Hinglish
Watch on YouTube Short Notes JEE Practice

Long Notes — Full Explanation

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

Notes language:

1️⃣ Work — Force × Displacement (Direction Ke Saath!)

Work: W = F·s·cosθ = F.s (dot product) — scalar hai, lekin + / − / 0 ho sakta hai. Unit: joule (J) = N·m.

  • Positive work: force aur displacement same side (θ < 90°) — box ko dhakelna.
  • Negative work: opposite (θ > 90°) — friction hamesha negative work karta hai, braking force bhi.
  • Zero work ⭐: force ⊥ displacement (θ = 90°) — circular motion me centripetal force KAAM NAHI karta! Ya displacement = 0 (wall ko dhakka, wall nahi hili).
  • Variable force: W = ∫F·dx — F-x graph ke neeche ka area. Spring: W = ½kx² (stretch karne me).

💡 Feel: bag uthake khade raho — physics ke hisaab se work ZERO (displacement nahi), chahe thakan ho! Physics ka "work" daily life wala nahi hai.

2️⃣ Energy — Kaam Karne Ki Capacity

Kinetic Energy (KE): K = ½mv² — motion ki energy. Hamesha positive, speed ke square pe depend (speed double → KE 4x! ⭐ braking distance bhi 4x).

  • Work-Energy theorem ⭐: Wnet = ΔK — jitna net work, utna hi KE change. Sab forces ka total work (friction included) = final KE − initial KE.
  • Potential Energy (PE): position/configuration ki stored energy — gravitational: U = mgh (reference se height) • spring: U = ½kx².
  • PE sirf conservative forces ke liye define hoti hai (gravity, spring) — friction ke liye nahi (path dependent!).
  • Relation: F = −dU/dx — force PE ki ghataane wali direction me lagta hai (ball neeche girti hai, U kam hoti hai).

🎯 Work-energy theorem sabse powerful shortcut hai — acceleration, time sab skip karke seedha speed nikal deta hai!

3️⃣ Conservation of Mechanical Energy ⭐

Agar sirf conservative forces kaam karein (friction absent), toh KE + PE = constant:

  • Free fall / projectile: upar jaate PE badhti hai, KE ghatti — total same. Neeche aate reverse. Har point pe ½mv² + mgh = constant.
  • Pendulum: extreme points pe sab PE, bottom pe sab KE. Swing me energy idhar-udhar hoti rehti hai.
  • Roller coaster / loop: height h se start → bottom pe ½mv² = mgh → v = √(2gh) — mass pe depend NAHI karta! ⭐
  • Friction ho toh: mechanical energy conserve NAHI — kuch heat me jaati hai. Work-energy theorem tab bhi valid hai (friction ka negative work include karo).

💡 Feel: mechanical energy bank account hai — PE aur KE ke beech transfer hota hai, total balance same (jab tak friction "charges" na kaate).

4️⃣ Collisions — Takkar Ka Physics ⭐

Collision me momentum HAMESHA conserve (external force negligible), lekin KE ka hisaab type pe depend:

TypeMomentumKinetic EnergyExample
ElasticConservedConservedBilliard balls (approx), atoms
InelasticConservedLost (heat/deformation)Car crash, ball + floor
Perfectly inelasticConservedMax loss — saath chipak jaate hainBullet block me fasna
  • 1D elastic ⭐: v₁ = ((m₁−m₂)/(m₁+m₂))u₁ + (2m₂/(m₁+m₂))u₂. Equal masses → velocities EXCHANGE! Heavy ball hits light at rest → light 2x speed se udti hai. Light hits heavy → light wapas aati hai.
  • Perfectly inelastic (1D): dono chipak ke v = (m₁u₁ + m₂u₂)/(m₁ + m₂).
  • Coefficient of restitution: e = separation speed / approach speed (elastic e = 1, perfectly inelastic e = 0).

🎯 Newton ka cradle: equal mass elastic collision = velocity exchange — isliye sirf end wala ball udta hai!

5️⃣ Power — Kaam Ki Speed

Power: P = W/t = F·v (constant force, velocity ke along) — kaam kitni JALDI hua. Unit: watt (W) = J/s.

  • 1 horsepower = 746 W ≈ 750 W — exam conversion ⭐.
  • Electricity bill connection: 1 unit = 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J — energy ki commercial unit (power nahi!).
  • P = F·v se: constant power pe speed badhne se force ghatta hai (car gear down on uphill — force chahiye!).
  • Efficiency: η = useful output / total input — koi machine 100% efficient nahi (friction/heat losses).

💡 Feel: same kaam jaldi karna = zyada power. Stair race — dono same height chadhe (same work), lekin jo pehle pahuncha woh zyada powerful!

1️⃣ कार्य — बल × विस्थापन (दिशा सहित!)

कार्य (Work): W = F·s·cosθ = F.s (अदिश गुणन) — अदिश है, पर धनात्मक / ऋणात्मक / शून्य हो सकता है। मात्रक: जूल (J) = N·m।

  • धनात्मक कार्य: बल और विस्थापन समान दिशा में (θ < 90°)।
  • ऋणात्मक कार्य: विपरीत दिशा (θ > 90°) — घर्षण सदैव ऋणात्मक कार्य करता है।
  • शून्य कार्य ⭐: बल ⊥ विस्थापन (θ = 90°) — वृत्तीय गति में अभिकेंद्रीय बल कार्य नहीं करता! या विस्थापन = 0।
  • परिवर्ती बल: W = ∫F·dx — F-x ग्राफ का क्षेत्रफल। स्प्रिंग: W = ½kx²।

💡 बैग उठाए खड़े रहना — भौतिकी में कार्य शून्य (विस्थापन नहीं), चाहे थकान हो!

2️⃣ ऊर्जा — कार्य करने की क्षमता

गतिज ऊर्जा (KE): K = ½mv² — गति की ऊर्जा। सदैव धनात्मक, चाल के वर्ग पर निर्भर (चाल दुगुनी → KE चार गुना! ⭐)

  • कार्य-ऊर्जा प्रमेय ⭐: Wnet = ΔK — कुल कार्य = गतिज ऊर्जा में परिवर्तन।
  • स्थितिज ऊर्जा (PE): स्थिति की संचित ऊर्जा — गुरुत्वीय: U = mgh • स्प्रिंग: U = ½kx²।
  • PE केवल संरक्षी बलों (गुरुत्व, स्प्रिंग) के लिए परिभाषित — घर्षण के लिए नहीं।
  • संबंध: F = −dU/dx — बल PE घटाने की दिशा में लगता है।

🎯 कार्य-ऊर्जा प्रमेय सबसे बड़ा शॉर्टकट है — त्वरण और समय छोड़कर सीधे चाल मिलती है!

3️⃣ यांत्रिक ऊर्जा संरक्षण ⭐

यदि केवल संरक्षी बल कार्य करें (घर्षण न हो), तो KE + PE = नियत:

  • मुक्त पतन / प्रक्षेप्य: ऊपर जाते PE बढ़ती, KE घटती — योग समान। प्रत्येक बिंदु पर ½mv² + mgh = नियत।
  • दोलक (pendulum): सीरों पर सब PE, निम्नतम पर सब KE।
  • ऊँचाई h से: ½mv² = mgh → v = √(2gh) — द्रव्यमान पर निर्भर नहीं! ⭐
  • घर्षण हो तो: यांत्रिक ऊर्जा संरक्षित नहीं — कुछ ऊष्मा में जाती है। कार्य-ऊर्जा प्रमेय तब भी मान्य है।

💡 यांत्रिक ऊर्जा बैंक खाते जैसी — PE और KE में स्थानांतरण, कुल शेष समान (जब तक घर्षण कटौती न करे)।

4️⃣ संघट्ट (Collisions) ⭐

संघट्ट में संवेग सदैव संरक्षित, परंतु KE प्रकार पर निर्भर:

प्रकारसंवेगगतिज ऊर्जाउदाहरण
प्रत्यास्थ (elastic)संरक्षितसंरक्षितबिलियर्ड गेंदें (लगभग), परमाणु
अप्रत्यास्थ (inelastic)संरक्षितहानि (ऊष्मा/विरूपण)कार दुर्घटना
पूर्ण अप्रत्यास्थसंरक्षितअधिकतम हानि — चिपक जाते हैंगोली का ब्लॉक में फँसना
  • 1D प्रत्यास्थ ⭐: v₁ = ((m₁−m₂)/(m₁+m₂))u₁ + (2m₂/(m₁+m₂))u₂। समान द्रव्यमान → वेग की अदला-बदली! भारी गेंद हल्की को → हल्की 2x चाल से।
  • पूर्ण अप्रत्यास्थ (1D): v = (m₁u₁ + m₂u₂)/(m₁ + m₂)।
  • प्रत्यावस्थान गुणांक: e = पृथक्करण चाल / उपगमन चाल (प्रत्यास्थ e = 1, पूर्ण अप्रत्यास्थ e = 0)।

🎯 न्यूटन क्रैडल: समान द्रव्यमान प्रत्यास्थ संघट्ट = वेग विनिमय — इसलिए सिर्फ अंतिम गेंद उठती है!

5️⃣ शक्ति — कार्य की दर

शक्ति (Power): P = W/t = F·v — कार्य कितनी शीघ्रता से हुआ। मात्रक: वॉट (W) = J/s।

  • 1 अश्वशक्ति (HP) = 746 W — परीक्षा रूपांतरण ⭐।
  • बिजली बिल: 1 यूनिट = 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J — ऊर्जा की व्यावसायिक इकाई (शक्ति नहीं!)।
  • P = F·v से: नियत शक्ति पर चाल बढ़ने से बल घटता है (चढ़ाई पर गियर डाउन!)।
  • दक्षता: η = उपयोगी निर्गत / कुल निवेश — कोई यंत्र 100% दक्ष नहीं।

💡 समान कार्य जल्दी करना = अधिक शक्ति। सीढ़ियों की दौड़ — समान कार्य, पर पहले पहुँचने वाला अधिक शक्तिशाली!

1️⃣ Work — Force × Displacement (Direction Matters!)

Work: W = F·s·cosθ = F.s (dot product) — a scalar, but it can be positive, negative or zero. Unit: joule (J) = N·m.

  • Positive work: force and displacement in the same direction (θ < 90°) — pushing a box.
  • Negative work: opposite directions (θ > 90°) — friction always does negative work, so does braking.
  • Zero work ⭐: force ⊥ displacement (θ = 90°) — centripetal force does NO work in circular motion! Or displacement = 0 (push a wall all day — no work done).
  • Variable force: W = ∫F·dx — area under the F-x graph. Spring: W = ½kx² to stretch.

💡 Feel: hold a bag standing still — in physics you did ZERO work (no displacement), however tired you feel!

2️⃣ Energy — The Capacity to Do Work

Kinetic Energy (KE): K = ½mv² — energy of motion. Always positive, depends on speed squared (double speed → 4x KE! ⭐ and 4x braking distance).

  • Work-Energy theorem ⭐: Wnet = ΔK — total work by ALL forces (friction included) equals the change in KE.
  • Potential Energy (PE): stored energy of position/configuration — gravitational: U = mgh (above a reference) • spring: U = ½kx².
  • PE is defined only for conservative forces (gravity, spring) — never for friction (path dependent!).
  • Relation: F = −dU/dx — force acts in the direction of decreasing PE (a ball falls down, lowering U).

🎯 The work-energy theorem is the ultimate shortcut — skip acceleration and time, jump straight to speed!

3️⃣ Conservation of Mechanical Energy ⭐

If only conservative forces do work (no friction), then KE + PE = constant:

  • Free fall / projectile: going up, PE rises and KE falls — the total stays fixed. Everywhere: ½mv² + mgh = constant.
  • Pendulum: all PE at the extremes, all KE at the bottom — energy sloshes back and forth.
  • Roller coaster / loop: from height h → ½mv² = mgh → v = √(2gh) — independent of mass! ⭐
  • With friction: mechanical energy is NOT conserved — some becomes heat. The work-energy theorem still holds (include friction's negative work).

💡 Think: mechanical energy is a bank account — transfers between PE and KE, same balance (until friction charges its fee).

4️⃣ Collisions — The Physics of Impact ⭐

In every collision momentum is ALWAYS conserved (external force negligible), but KE depends on the type:

TypeMomentumKinetic EnergyExample
ElasticConservedConservedBilliard balls (approx), atoms
InelasticConservedLost (heat/deformation)Car crash, ball + floor
Perfectly inelasticConservedMax loss — they stick togetherBullet lodged in a block
  • 1D elastic ⭐: v₁ = ((m₁−m₂)/(m₁+m₂))u₁ + (2m₂/(m₁+m₂))u₂. Equal masses → velocities EXCHANGE! Heavy ball hits a light one at rest → the light one flies off at 2x. Light hits heavy → it bounces back.
  • Perfectly inelastic (1D): they stick and move at v = (m₁u₁ + m₂u₂)/(m₁ + m₂).
  • Coefficient of restitution: e = separation speed / approach speed (elastic e = 1, perfectly inelastic e = 0).

🎯 Newton's cradle: equal-mass elastic collision = velocity exchange — that's why only the end ball flies out!

5️⃣ Power — The Speed of Doing Work

Power: P = W/t = F·v (constant force along the motion) — how FAST work is done. Unit: watt (W) = J/s.

  • 1 horsepower = 746 W — exam conversion ⭐.
  • Electricity bill connection: 1 unit = 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J — the commercial unit of ENERGY (not power!).
  • From P = F·v: at constant power, more speed means less force (cars gear down uphill — they need force!).
  • Efficiency: η = useful output / total input — no machine is 100% efficient (friction/heat losses).

💡 Think: same work done faster = more power. A stair race — same work for both, but whoever reaches first is more powerful!

JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1 10 N ka force box ko 5 m tak 60° angle pe kheenchta hai. Work?
Answer dekho

W = Fs cosθ = 10×5×cos60° = 25 J.

Q2 2 kg ki ball 10 m/s se chal rahi hai. Uski KE? Speed 20 m/s ho toh?
Answer dekho

KE = ½×2×100 = 100 J. 20 m/s pe: ½×2×400 = 400 J — speed double, KE 4x!

Q3 Ek ball 20 m height se drop hoti hai (g = 10, no air). Zameen pe speed?
Answer dekho

v = √(2gh) = √(2×10×20) = 20 m/s (energy conservation se, mass ki zaroorat nahi!).

Q4 Equal mass ki do billiard balls: A 6 m/s se chalke rest wali B ko elastically hit karti hai. Baad me dono ki velocities?
Answer dekho

Equal mass elastic → exchange: A ruk jaati hai (0), B 6 m/s se chalti hai.

Q5 1 kg ka block 2 m/s se 4 kg ke rest block se takrakar CHIPAK jaata hai. Combined velocity?
Answer dekho

v = (1×2 + 4×0)/(1+4) = 0.4 m/s (perfectly inelastic, momentum conserved).

Q6 Frictionless track pe cart 5 m height se chhoda. Bottom pe uski KE (mass 2 kg, g = 10)?
Answer dekho

KE = mgh = 2×10×5 = 100 J (sab PE convert ho gayi).

Q7 Ek machine 500 J ka kaam 5 s me karti hai. Power? HP me?
Answer dekho

P = 500/5 = 100 W ≈ 0.134 HP (746 se divide karke).

Q8 Bullet (20 g) 400 m/s se wooden block me fas gayi. Block (2 kg) kitni speed se move karega?
Answer dekho

v = (0.02×400)/(2.02) = 3.96 m/s ≈ 4 m/s.

Q9 Spring (k = 200 N/m) ko 0.1 m stretch karne me kitna work?
Answer dekho

W = ½kx² = ½×200×0.01 = 1 J.

Q10 Car 1000 kg, 20 m/s se 30 m/s tak accelerate hoti hai. Net work done?
Answer dekho

W = ΔK = ½m(v²−u²) = ½×1000×(900−400) = 250,000 J = 250 kJ.
