📖 Full Explanation (Long Notes)

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Temperature aur Heat — Do Alag Cheezein ⭐

Ye confusion sabse pehle clear karo:

  • Temperature: hotness ka measure — average kinetic energy ka indicator. Jitna zyada T, utna tez molecules.
  • Heat: energy ka TRANSFER temperature difference ki wajah se — hot se cold tak behti hai (jab tak equal na ho — thermal equilibrium).
  • Thermometers measure: kisi property ka change — mercury ka expansion, gas ka pressure, resistance.
  • Scales ⭐: °C, °F (T_F = 9T_C/5 + 32), Kelvin (T_K = T_C + 273.15). Absolute zero = 0 K = −273.15 °C — isse neeche kuch nahi!
  • Kelvin scale absolute kyun: 0 K pe molecular motion minimum; negative Kelvin possible nahi.

💡 Feel: temperature = pani ka "level", heat = behta hua pani. Dono buckets same temperature, lekin bade bucket me zyada heat energy!

2️⃣ Thermal Expansion — Garmi Me Phailna ⭐

  • Linear expansion: ΔL = αLΔT — α = coefficient of linear expansion (material property, ~10⁻⁵/°C metals).
  • Area expansion: ΔA = βAΔT, β ≈ 2α.
  • Volume expansion: ΔV = γVΔT, γ ≈ 3α. ⭐ Relation yaad rakho!
  • Applications: railway tracks me gaps, bridges me rollers, bimetallic strip (do metals alag α → mud jaati hai — thermostat ka heart!), pendulum clocks garmi me slow.
  • Anomalous expansion of water ⭐: 0-4 °C me paani CONTRACT hota hai garm karne pe! 4 °C pe density max — isliye barf upar tairti hai aur ponds neeche se freeze nahi hote (machliyon ke liye blessing!).

🎯 Water ka anomaly exam favourite hai: 4 °C pe maximum density, minimum volume.

3️⃣ Specific Heat aur Calorimetry — Heat Ka Hisaab

  • Specific heat capacity ⭐: s = Q/(mΔT) — 1 kg ko 1 °C badhane ki heat. Paani ka s = 4186 J/kg·°C (bahut zyada — isliye samundar climate regulate karta hai!).
  • Heat capacity: C = ms — poori body ke liye. Molar heat: per mole.
  • Calorimetry principle ⭐: isolated system me heat lost = heat gained (energy conservation). m₁s₁(T₁−T) = m₂s₂(T−T₂).
  • Latent heat ⭐: state change ke time temperature CONSTANT rehta hai, heat sirf state todne me jaati hai. L = Q/m — L_f (fusion: ice↔water, 3.33×10⁵ J/kg), L_v (vaporization: water↔steam, 22.6×10⁵ J/kg).
  • Steam burns khatarnak kyun: 100 °C steam pehle L_v (2260 kJ/kg) release karti hai, PHIR 100 °C paani ki tarah cool — double damage!

💡 Latent heat = "chhupi hui heat" — thermometer ko dikhti nahi (T same), par energy andar ja rahi hai state change ke liye!

4️⃣ Heat Transfer — Conduction aur Convection

Conduction (touch se transfer):

  • Rate ⭐: H = kA(T₁−T₂)/d — k = thermal conductivity (metals high, wood/air low).
  • Thermal resistance: R = d/(kA) — series me resistances add hote hain (walls in layers!).
  • Metals me free electrons heat bhi carry karte hain — isliye achhe electrical conductors achhe thermal bhi.
  • Applications: cooking utensils ka metal base, insulating walls (double-layer), cooler me foam.

Convection (fluid ke movement se):

  • Garam fluid halka → upar, thanda heavy → neeche — cycle banta hai convection current.
  • Examples: sea breeze/land breeze ⭐ (din me zameen jaldi garam → hawa upar → samundar se hawa), room heater, boiling paani.
  • Forced convection: fans, blood circulation (body temperature regulate karta hai).

💡 Feel: conduction = relay race (molecules pass karte hain), convection = courier service (garam fluid khud travel karta hai)!

5️⃣ Radiation aur Black Body — Bina Medium Ki Heat ⭐

  • Radiation: electromagnetic waves se heat transfer — MEDIUM ki zaroorat nahi! Suraj ki dhoop isi se aati hai.
  • Stefan-Boltzmann law ⭐: power radiated P = eσAT⁴ — T Kelvin me, σ = 5.67×10⁻⁸ W/m²K⁴, e = emissivity (black body e=1).
  • Black body: perfect absorber AUR perfect emitter. Good absorbers are good emitters! ⭐ (dark clothes garmi me garam, thandi jagah jaldi thande bhi).
  • Newton's law of cooling ⭐: rate of cooling ∝ (T_body − T_surroundings) — approximation jab difference chhota ho. Chai jaldi thandi hoti hai jab bahut garam ho, phir slow!
  • Greenhouse effect: Earth sunlight absorb karti hai (short wavelength), infrared emit karti hai (long) — CO₂ infrared trap kar leta hai → warming.
  • Prevost's theory: har body har waqt radiate karti hai; net exchange pe depend karta hai thanda/garam lagna.

🎯 T⁴ yaad rakho — temperature double, radiation 16 guna! Isliye bulb ka filament (2500 K) itna chamakta hai.

1️⃣ तापमान और ऊष्मा — दो अलग बातें ⭐

  • तापमान: गर्माहट का माप — औसत गतिज ऊर्जा का सूचक।
  • ऊष्मा: तापमान अंतर के कारण ऊर्जा का स्थानांतरण — गर्म से ठंडे की ओर बहती है (तापीय साम्य तक)।
  • तापमापी: किसी गुण के परिवर्तन से मापता है — पारे का प्रसार, गैस का दाब, प्रतिरोध।
  • पैमाने ⭐: °C, °F (T_F = 9T_C/5 + 32), केल्विन (T_K = T_C + 273.15)। परम शून्य = 0 K = −273.15 °C।

💡 तापमान = पानी का "स्तर", ऊष्मा = बहता पानी।

2️⃣ तापीय प्रसार — गर्मी में फैलना ⭐

  • रेखीय प्रसार: ΔL = αLΔT — α रेखीय प्रसार गुणांक (~10⁻⁵/°C धातुओं)।
  • क्षेत्रीय प्रसार: ΔA = βAΔT, β ≈ 2α। आयतन प्रसार: ΔV = γVΔT, γ ≈ 3α। ⭐
  • उपयोग: रेल पटरियों में खाली स्थान, पुलों में रोलर, द्विधातु पट्टी (थर्मोस्टेट का दिल), पेंडुलम घड़ी गर्मी में धीमी।
  • जल का असामान्य प्रसार ⭐: 0-4 °C में गर्म करने पर पानी सिकुड़ता है! 4 °C पर घनत्व अधिकतम — इसलिए बर्फ ऊपर तैरती है और तालाब नीचे से जमते नहीं।

🎯 जल की विसंगति परीक्षा प्रिय: 4 °C पर अधिकतम घनत्व, न्यूनतम आयतन।

3️⃣ विशिष्ट ऊष्मा और ऊष्मामिति — ऊष्मा का हिसाब

  • विशिष्ट ऊष्मा धारिता ⭐: s = Q/(mΔT) — 1 kg को 1 °C बढ़ाने की ऊष्मा। पानी का s = 4186 J/kg·°C (बहुत अधिक — इसलिए समुद्र जलवायु संतुलित करता है!)।
  • ऊष्मा धारिता: C = ms।
  • ऊष्मामिति सिद्धांत ⭐: विलगित निकाय में खोई ऊष्मा = प्राप्त ऊष्मा।
  • गुप्त ऊष्मा ⭐: अवस्था परिवर्तन में तापमान स्थिर रहता है। L = Q/m — L_f (गलन: 3.33×10⁵ J/kg), L_v (वाष्पीकरण: 22.6×10⁵ J/kg)।
  • भाप के जलन खतरनाक क्यों: 100 °C भाप पहले L_v छोड़ती है, फिर ठंडी होती है — दोगुना नुकसान!

💡 गुप्त ऊष्मा = "छिपी ऊष्मा" — थर्मामीटर को नहीं दिखती, पर ऊर्जा अवस्था बदलने में जा रही है!

4️⃣ ऊष्मा स्थानांतरण — चालन और संवहन

चालन (स्पर्श से):

  • दर ⭐: H = kA(T₁−T₂)/d — k तापीय चालकता (धातु उच्च, लकड़ी/हवा निम्न)।
  • तापीय प्रतिरोध: R = d/(kA) — श्रेणी में जुड़ते हैं।
  • धातुओं में मुक्त इलेक्ट्रॉन ऊष्मा भी ले जाते हैं — अच्छे विद्युत चालक अच्छे तापीय चालक भी।

संवहन (तरल की गति से):

  • गर्म तरल हल्का → ऊपर, ठंडा भारी → नीचे — संवहन धारा बनती है।
  • उदाहरण: समुद्री/स्थल समीर ⭐, कमरे का हीटर, उबलता पानी।

💡 चालन = रिले रेस, संवहन = कूरियर सेवा (गर्म तरल खुद यात्रा करता है)!

5️⃣ विकिरण और कृष्णिका — बिना माध्यम की ऊष्मा ⭐

  • विकिरण: विद्युतचुंबकीय तरंगों से ऊष्मा स्थानांतरण — माध्यम की जरूरत नहीं! सूरज की रोशनी ऐसे ही आती है।
  • स्टीफन-बोल्ट्ज़मान नियम ⭐: P = eσAT⁴ — σ = 5.67×10⁻⁸ W/m²K⁴, e = उत्सर्जकता (कृष्णिका e=1)।
  • कृष्णिका (black body): पूर्ण अवशोषक और पूर्ण उत्सर्जक। अच्छे अवशोषक अच्छे उत्सर्जक होते हैं! ⭐
  • न्यूटन का शीतलन नियम ⭐: शीतलन दर ∝ (T_वस्तु − T_परिवेश) — अंतर छोटा हो तो। गर्म चाय पहले तेज़ी से ठंडी होती है!
  • ग्रीनहाउस प्रभाव: पृथ्वी सूर्यप्रकाश अवशोषित करती है, अवरक्त उत्सर्जित — CO₂ उसे रोक लेता है → तापमान वृद्धि।

🎯 T⁴ याद रखो — तापमान दोगुना, विकिरण 16 गुना!

1️⃣ Temperature and Heat — Two Different Things ⭐

  • Temperature: the measure of hotness — an indicator of average kinetic energy. Higher T means faster molecules.
  • Heat: the TRANSFER of energy due to a temperature difference — flows from hot to cold until thermal equilibrium.
  • Thermometers use a changing property — mercury expansion, gas pressure, resistance.
  • Scales ⭐: °C, °F (T_F = 9T_C/5 + 32), Kelvin (T_K = T_C + 273.15). Absolute zero = 0 K = −273.15 °C.

💡 Feel: temperature = the water level, heat = the flowing water. Two buckets can share a temperature but hold different heat energy!

2️⃣ Thermal Expansion — Growing in the Heat ⭐

  • Linear expansion: ΔL = αLΔT — α is the coefficient of linear expansion (~10⁻⁵/°C for metals).
  • Area expansion: ΔA = βAΔT, β ≈ 2α. Volume expansion: ΔV = γVΔT, γ ≈ 3α. ⭐
  • Applications: gaps in railway tracks, rollers under bridges, the bimetallic strip (two metals with different α bend — the heart of a thermostat!), pendulum clocks run slow in summer.
  • Anomalous expansion of water ⭐: from 0-4 °C water CONTRACTS on heating! Density is maximum at 4 °C — that is why ice floats and ponds freeze from the top only (a blessing for fish!).

🎯 Water's anomaly is an exam favourite: maximum density, minimum volume at 4 °C.

3️⃣ Specific Heat and Calorimetry — The Accounting of Heat

  • Specific heat capacity ⭐: s = Q/(mΔT) — heat needed to raise 1 kg by 1 °C. Water's s = 4186 J/kg·°C (very high — that is why the sea regulates climate!).
  • Heat capacity: C = ms for the whole body.
  • Calorimetry principle ⭐: in an isolated system, heat lost = heat gained. m₁s₁(T₁−T) = m₂s₂(T−T₂).
  • Latent heat ⭐: during a state change the temperature stays CONSTANT — heat goes only into breaking the state. L = Q/m — L_f (fusion: 3.33×10⁵ J/kg), L_v (vaporization: 22.6×10⁵ J/kg).
  • Why steam burns badly: 100 °C steam first releases L_v (2260 kJ/kg), THEN cools like water — double damage!

💡 Latent heat = hidden heat — invisible to the thermometer (T constant) but busy changing the state!

4️⃣ Heat Transfer — Conduction and Convection

Conduction (transfer by contact):

  • Rate ⭐: H = kA(T₁−T₂)/d — k is thermal conductivity (high in metals, low in wood/air).
  • Thermal resistance: R = d/(kA) — resistances add in series (layered walls!).
  • Free electrons in metals carry heat too — good electrical conductors are good thermal conductors.

Convection (transfer by fluid motion):

  • Warm fluid is lighter and rises; cool fluid sinks — a convection current forms.
  • Examples: sea breeze/land breeze ⭐ (land heats faster by day → air rises → wind from the sea), room heaters, boiling water.

💡 Feel: conduction = a relay race (molecules pass it on), convection = a courier service (the warm fluid travels itself)!

5️⃣ Radiation and Black Bodies — Heat Without a Medium ⭐

  • Radiation: heat transfer by electromagnetic waves — NO medium needed! Sunshine arrives this way.
  • Stefan-Boltzmann law ⭐: power radiated P = eσAT⁴ — T in kelvin, σ = 5.67×10⁻⁸ W/m²K⁴, e = emissivity (black body e=1).
  • Black body: perfect absorber AND perfect emitter. Good absorbers are good emitters! ⭐ (dark clothes heat up fast — and cool down fast too).
  • Newton's law of cooling ⭐: rate of cooling ∝ (T_body − T_surroundings) — an approximation for small differences. Very hot tea cools fastest at first!
  • Greenhouse effect: Earth absorbs sunlight (short wavelength) and emits infrared (long) — CO₂ traps the infrared → warming.

🎯 Remember T⁴ — double the temperature, 16 times the radiation! That is why a bulb filament (~2500 K) shines so bright.

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. 50 °C ko Fahrenheit me convert karo.
Answer dekho

T_F = 9×50/5 + 32 = 122 °F.

Q2. 2 m iron rod (α = 1.2×10⁻⁵/°C) ko 20 °C se 120 °C tak garam kiya. Expansion?
Answer dekho

ΔL = αLΔT = 1.2×10⁻⁵×2×100 = 2.4 mm.

Q3. Linear aur volume expansion coefficients me kya relation hai?
Answer dekho

γ ≈ 3α (aur β ≈ 2α) — teeno dimensions me expand hota hai.

Q4. Paani 4 °C pe kya special dikhata hai?
Answer dekho

Maximum density (minimum volume) — 0-4 °C me garm karne pe CONTRACT hota hai. Isliye barf tairti hai.

Q5. 2 kg paani ko 20 °C se 70 °C tak garam karne ki heat? (s = 4186 J/kg°C)
Answer dekho

Q = msΔT = 2×4186×50 = 4.186×10⁵ J.

Q6. 10 g ice (0 °C) ko paani (0 °C) banane ki heat? (L_f = 3.33×10⁵ J/kg)
Answer dekho

Q = mL = 0.01×3.33×10⁵ = 3.33×10³ J — temperature same rehta hai!

Q7. State change ke time temperature constant kyun rehta hai?
Answer dekho

Saari heat latent heat me jaati hai — bonds todne me, kinetic energy badhane me nahi.

Q8. Carpet pe pair rakhne se zyada thanda tile kyun lagta hai (same temperature)?
Answer dekho

Tile ki thermal conductivity zyada — pair se heat jaldi kheench leta hai. Carpet insulator hai.

Q9. Stefan's law: temperature double (T → 2T). Radiated power?
Answer dekho

P ∝ T⁴ → 2⁴ = 16 guna!

Q10. Garam chai pehle jaldi, phir dheere thandi kyun hoti hai?
Answer dekho

Newton's law of cooling — rate ∝ (T_chai − T_room). Difference ghatta hai to cooling slow.
