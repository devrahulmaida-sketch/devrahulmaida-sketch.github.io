📖 Full Explanation (Long Notes)

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Newton Ka First Law — Inertia Ka Niyam

First law: jab tak koi external force na lage, object apni state (rest ya uniform velocity) nahi badalta. Isi tendency ko inertia kehte hain.

  • Inertia of rest: bus achanak chale toh passengers peeche jhukte hain — body rest me rehna chahti hai.
  • Inertia of motion: bus achanak ruke toh passengers aage girte hain.
  • Inertia of direction: turn pe body seedha jaana chahti hai (bahar ki taraf feel).
  • Mass = inertia ka measure: zyada mass = zyada inertia = mushkil se state change hoti hai.
  • First law inertial frame define karta hai — jahan law valid ho. Accelerating frame (bus, lift) me pseudo effects aate hain.

💡 Feel: inertia aalas hai — har object bas wahi karna chahta hai jo woh kar raha hai!

2️⃣ Momentum aur Newton Ka Second Law ⭐

Momentum: p = mv — motion ki "quantity". Zyada mass ya zyada speed = zyada momentum = zyada mushkil rokna.

  • Second law (asli form): F = dp/dt — force = momentum change ki rate. Constant mass ke liye: F = ma.
  • F = ma vector equation hai — direction me force, wahi direction me acceleration.
  • Impulse ⭐: J = F × Δt = Δp (momentum change). Catching ball: haath peeche kheenchna Δt badhata hai → F kam → haath bachta hai! Airbags, cricket catch, gym mats — sab impulse game.
  • Variable mass: rocket — F = ma directly nahi lagta, dp/dt lagta hai (fuel mass change hoti hai).
  • Units: 1 N = 1 kg·m/s². Weight W = mg — ek force hai (mass nahi!).

🎯 Exam trap: "object chal raha hai toh force lag rahi hogi" — GALAT! Uniform velocity pe net force zero. Force velocity nahi, velocity ka CHANGE banata hai.

3️⃣ Newton Ka Third Law + Free Body Diagrams

Third law: har action ka equal-opposite reaction — lekin DO ALAG OBJECTS pe! (Isliye cancel nahi hote ⭐)

  • Walking: pair zameen ko peeche dhakelta hai, zameen pair ko aage. Rocket: gas neeche, rocket upar. Gun recoil: bullet aage, bandook peeche.
  • Action-reaction SAME nature ke hote hain (dono contact, ya dono gravitational) aur same magnitude.
  • Free Body Diagram (FBD) ⭐: object ko akela draw karo, sirf US pe lage forces dikhao (uske dwara lagaye gaye nahi!). Har numerical ka pehla step.
  • Connected bodies: elevator (normal force: upar accelerate → N = m(g+a), neeche → N = m(g−a), free fall → N = 0!), Atwood machine, string-pulley systems — FBD + F=ma har object pe alag lagao.

💡 FBD rule: "kis PE lag raha hai" socho, "kis NE lagaya" nahi. Normal force hamesha surface ke perpendicular!

4️⃣ Friction — Zaroori Evil ⭐

Friction surface ke parallel, relative motion (ya motion ki tendency) ko oppose karta hai.

TypeFormulaBaat
Static (limiting)fs ≤ μsNself-adjusting — utna hi jitna zaroorat, max μsN tak
Kineticfk = μkNconstant; hamesha μk < μs ⭐
Rollingfr = μrNsabse kam — isliye wheels!
  • Angle of friction: tanθ = μs • Angle of repose (incline pe slip shuru): tanα = μs.
  • Incline pe: component mg sinθ neeche kheenchta hai, normal N = mg cosθ. Slip condition: tanθ > μs.
  • Friction walking, writing, braking ke liye ZAROORI hai — bina friction ke koi bhi nahi chal sakta.
  • Reduce karne ke tareeke: lubricants, ball bearings, polishing, streamlining (air friction ke liye).

💡 Feel: static friction ek loyal dost hai — jitna zaroorat utna help karta hai, lekin uski limit (μsN) ke aage chhod deta hai!

5️⃣ Circular Turn — Banking aur Pseudo Force

Circular turn pe centripetal force KISI na kisi real force se aana chahiye:

  • Level road (friction se): vmax = √(μrg) — isse tez mudoge toh skid!
  • Banked road (friction ke bina ideal) ⭐: tanθ = v²/rg — banking angle speed pe design hota hai. Friction ke SAATH: vmax = √(rg(μ + tanθ)/(1 − μtanθ)).
  • Non-inertial frame me: ghoomte frame me baithne wale ko bahar ki taraf centrifugal (pseudo) force feel hota hai — real nahi, frame ki acceleration ka effect. Inertial frame me sirf centripetal (andar) force real hai ⭐.
  • Velodrome, railway tracks, aircraft banking — sab tanθ = v²/rg ka application.

🎯 Yaad rakho: centrifugal force kaam ki cheez FEEL hoti hai, lekin inertial analysis me usko mat lagao — wahan sirf centripetal real force hai.

1️⃣ न्यूटन का प्रथम नियम — जड़त्व का नियम

प्रथम नियम: जब तक कोई बाह्य बल न लगे, वस्तु अपनी अवस्था (विराम या समान वेग) नहीं बदलती। इसी प्रवृत्ति को जड़त्व (inertia) कहते हैं।

  • विराम का जड़त्व: बस अचानक चले तो यात्री पीछे झुकते हैं।
  • गति का जड़त्व: बस अचानक रुके तो यात्री आगे गिरते हैं।
  • दिशा का जड़त्व: मोड़ पर शरीर सीधा जाना चाहता है।
  • द्रव्यमान = जड़त्व का माप: अधिक द्रव्यमान = अधिक जड़त्व।
  • प्रथम नियम जड़त्वीय निर्देश तंत्र परिभाषित करता है — जहाँ नियम मान्य हो।

💡 जड़त्व एक प्रकार का आलस्य है — वस्तु वही करना चाहती है जो वह कर रही है!

2️⃣ संवेग और न्यूटन का द्वितीय नियम ⭐

संवेग (momentum): p = mv — गति की "मात्रा"।

  • द्वितीय नियम (वास्तविक रूप): F = dp/dt — बल = संवेग परिवर्तन की दर। नियत द्रव्यमान पर: F = ma।
  • F = ma सदिश समीकरण है — बल की दिशा में ही त्वरण।
  • आवेग (impulse) ⭐: J = F × Δt = Δp। कैच लेते समय हाथ पीछे खींचना Δt बढ़ाता है → F घटता है! एयरबैग, जिम मैट — सब आवेग के उदाहरण।
  • परिवर्ती द्रव्यमान: रॉकेट में dp/dt लगाते हैं (ईंधन से द्रव्यमान बदलता है)।
  • मात्रक: 1 N = 1 kg·m/s²। भार W = mg — एक बल है (द्रव्यमान नहीं!)।

🎯 परीक्षा त्रुटि: "वस्तु चल रही है तो बल लग रहा होगा" — गलत! समान वेग पर कुल बल शून्य।

3️⃣ न्यूटन का तृतीय नियम + मुक्त पिण्ड आरेख

तृतीय नियम: प्रत्येक क्रिया की समान-विपरीत प्रतिक्रिया — परंतु दो अलग वस्तुओं पर! (इसलिए निरस्त नहीं होते ⭐)

  • चलना: पैर भूमि को पीछे धकेलता है, भूमि पैर को आगे। रॉकेट: गैस नीचे, रॉकेट ऊपर। बंदूक: गोली आगे, बंदूक पीछे।
  • मुक्त पिण्ड आरेख (FBD) ⭐: वस्तु को अलग बनाकर केवल उस पर लगे बल दिखाएँ। प्रत्येक numerical का पहला चरण।
  • संयुक्त पिंड: लिफ्ट (ऊपर त्वरण: N = m(g+a), नीचे: N = m(g−a), मुक्त पतन: N = 0!), एटवुड मशीन, डोरी-घिरनी निकाय।

💡 FBD नियम: "किस पर लग रहा है" सोचें, "किसने लगाया" नहीं। अभिलंब बल सदैव सतह के लंबवत!

4️⃣ घर्षण — आवश्यक बुराई ⭐

घर्षण सतह के समांतर, सापेक्ष गति (या गति की प्रवृत्ति) का विरोध करता है।

प्रकारसूत्रविशेषता
स्थैतिक (सीमांत)fs ≤ μsNस्व-समायोजक — आवश्यकता अनुसार, अधिकतम μsN
गतिकfk = μkNनियत; सदैव μk < μs ⭐
लोटनीfr = μrNसबसे कम — इसीलिए पहिए!
  • घर्षण कोण: tanθ = μs • स्वसुख कोण (repose): tanα = μs।
  • नत तल पर: mg sinθ नीचे खींचता है, N = mg cosθ। सर्पण शर्त: tanθ > μs।
  • घर्षण चलने, लिखने, ब्रेक के लिए आवश्यक है। घटाने के उपाय: स्नेहक, बॉल बेयरिंग, पॉलिश, धारारेख आकृति।

💡 स्थैतिक घर्षण आवश्यकतानुसार सहायता करता है, पर सीमा (μsN) से आगे नहीं!

5️⃣ वृत्तीय मोड़ — बैंकिंग और छद्म बल

वृत्तीय मोड़ पर अभिकेंद्रीय बल किसी वास्तविक बल से आना चाहिए:

  • समतल सड़क (घर्षण से): vmax = √(μrg) — अधिक चाल पर फिसलन!
  • झुकी सड़क (banked) ⭐: tanθ = v²/rg। घर्षण सहित: vmax = √(rg(μ + tanθ)/(1 − μtanθ))।
  • अजड़त्वीय तंत्र में: घूमते तंत्र में अपकेंद्रीय (centrifugal) छद्म बल अनुभव होता है — वास्तविक नहीं। जड़त्वीय तंत्र में केवल अभिकेंद्रीय बल वास्तविक है ⭐।
  • वेलोड्रोम, रेल पटरी, वायुयान बैंकिंग — सब tanθ = v²/rg के अनुप्रयोग।

🎯 जड़त्वीय विश्लेषण में अपकेंद्रीय बल न लगाएँ — वहाँ केवल अभिकेंद्रीय वास्तविक बल है।

1️⃣ Newton's First Law — The Law of Inertia

First law: unless an external force acts, an object does not change its state (rest or uniform velocity). This tendency is called inertia.

  • Inertia of rest: when a bus starts suddenly, passengers lean back — the body wants to stay at rest.
  • Inertia of motion: when the bus stops suddenly, passengers fall forward.
  • Inertia of direction: on a turn the body wants to keep going straight.
  • Mass = measure of inertia: more mass = more inertia = harder to change the state.
  • The first law defines an inertial frame — one where the law holds. Accelerating frames (bus, lift) show pseudo effects.

💡 Think: inertia is laziness — every object just wants to keep doing whatever it is doing!

2️⃣ Momentum and Newton's Second Law ⭐

Momentum: p = mv — the "quantity of motion". More mass or more speed means more momentum and a harder stop.

  • Second law (true form): F = dp/dt — force equals the rate of change of momentum. For constant mass: F = ma.
  • F = ma is a vector equation — acceleration is along the net force.
  • Impulse ⭐: J = F × Δt = Δp. Pulling hands back while catching increases Δt → smaller F → safe hands! Airbags, cricket catches, gym mats — all impulse games.
  • Variable mass: rockets need dp/dt, not plain F = ma (fuel changes the mass).
  • Units: 1 N = 1 kg·m/s². Weight W = mg — a force (not mass!).

🎯 Exam trap: "it is moving, so a force must be acting" — WRONG! Uniform velocity means zero net force. Force produces CHANGE of velocity, not velocity itself.

3️⃣ Newton's Third Law + Free Body Diagrams

Third law: every action has an equal and opposite reaction — but on TWO DIFFERENT OBJECTS! (That is why they never cancel ⭐)

  • Walking: the foot pushes the ground back, the ground pushes the foot forward. Rocket: gas down, rocket up. Gun recoil: bullet forward, gun backward.
  • Action-reaction pairs share the same nature (both contact or both gravitational) and the same magnitude.
  • Free Body Diagram (FBD) ⭐: draw the object alone, show only forces acting ON it (never forces it applies). First step of every numerical.
  • Connected bodies: elevators (accelerating up: N = m(g+a), down: N = m(g−a), free fall: N = 0!), Atwood machines, string-pulley systems — apply FBD + F=ma to each body separately.

💡 FBD rule: think "what acts ON it", not "what does it act on". Normal force is always perpendicular to the surface!

4️⃣ Friction — The Necessary Evil ⭐

Friction acts parallel to the surface, opposing relative motion (or its tendency).

TypeFormulaNote
Static (limiting)fs ≤ μsNself-adjusting — as much as needed, up to μsN
Kineticfk = μkNconstant; always μk < μs ⭐
Rollingfr = μrNsmallest — hence wheels!
  • Angle of friction: tanθ = μs • angle of repose (incline starts to slip): tanα = μs.
  • On an incline: component mg sinθ pulls down, normal N = mg cosθ. Slip condition: tanθ > μs.
  • Friction is NECESSARY for walking, writing, braking — without it nothing moves controllably.
  • Ways to reduce it: lubricants, ball bearings, polishing, streamlining (for air friction).

💡 Think: static friction is a loyal friend — it helps exactly as much as needed, but beyond its limit (μsN) it gives up!

5️⃣ Circular Turns — Banking and Pseudo Force

On a circular turn, the centripetal force must come from some real force:

  • Level road (via friction): vmax = √(μrg) — faster than this and you skid!
  • Banked road (ideal, no friction) ⭐: tanθ = v²/rg — the banking angle is designed for a speed. With friction: vmax = √(rg(μ + tanθ)/(1 − μtanθ)).
  • In a non-inertial frame: someone in the rotating frame feels an outward centrifugal (pseudo) force — not real, just the frame's acceleration. In an inertial frame only the centripetal force is real ⭐.
  • Velodromes, railway tracks, aircraft banking — all applications of tanθ = v²/rg.

🎯 Remember: centrifugal force feels real but must not be drawn in inertial analysis — only the centripetal real force belongs there.

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. 2 kg ka object rest me hai. 10 N ka force 4 s tak lage. Final velocity?
Answer dekho

a = F/m = 5 m/s². v = 0 + 5×4 = 20 m/s.

Q2. Cricket ball (0.15 kg) 20 m/s se aakar 0.1 s me ruk gayi. Average force?
Answer dekho

F = Δp/Δt = (0.15×20)/0.1 = 30 N. (Haath peeche kheencho toh Δt badhega, F ghat jayega!)

Q3. Lift 2 m/s² se upar accelerate ho rahi hai. 60 kg aadmi pe normal force (g = 10)?
Answer dekho

N = m(g+a) = 60×12 = 720 N. (Neeche jati toh 480 N, free fall me 0!)

Q4. Gun (5 kg) se 20 g ki bullet 500 m/s se chhooti. Gun ka recoil velocity?
Answer dekho

Momentum conservation: v = (0.02×500)/5 = 2 m/s peeche.

Q5. Block 5 kg incline pe hai, θ = 30°, μs = 0.6. Slip hoga? (g = 10)
Answer dekho

tan30° = 0.577 < 0.6 → slip NAHI hoga. Friction = mg sinθ = 25 N (self-adjusted).

Q6. Flat road pe car circular turn leti hai: r = 50 m, μ = 0.4, g = 10. Max safe speed?
Answer dekho

v = √(μrg) = √(0.4×50×10) = √200 = 14.1 m/s ≈ 51 km/h.

Q7. Banked curve: r = 100 m, designed for 20 m/s (g = 10), frictionless. Banking angle?
Answer dekho

tanθ = v²/rg = 400/1000 = 0.4 → θ = tan⁻¹(0.4) ≈ 21.8°.

Q8. Newton ke third law ke hisaab se action-reaction pairs kabhi cancel kyun nahi hote?
Answer dekho

Kyunki woh alag-alag objects pe lagte hain. Cancellation sirf SAME object pe lage forces me ho sakta hai.

Q9. 10 kg block pe 50 N horizontal force, surface μk = 0.3 (g = 10). Acceleration?
Answer dekho

fk = 0.3×100 = 30 N. Net = 50 − 30 = 20 N. a = 20/10 = 2 m/s².

Q10. Rocket upar ja raha hai. Newton ka kaunsa law directly explain karta hai?
Answer dekho

Third law — rocket exhaust gas ko neeche dhakelta hai (action), gas rocket ko upar (reaction). Momentum conservation bhi same baat hai.
