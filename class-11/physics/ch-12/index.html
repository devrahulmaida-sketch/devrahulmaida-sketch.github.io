🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Molecular Nature of Matter — Gas Ke Andar Kya Hai

  • Matter ke assumptions: sab kuch molecules se bana, wo lagatar random motion me — isliye gases poora container bhar leti hain.
  • Avogadro's number ⭐: N_A = 6.022×10²³ molecules per mole. 1 mole gas STP (0 °C, 1 atm) pe 22.4 L gherती hai.
  • Boyle's law: constant T pe PV = constant. Charles' law: constant P pe V ∝ T (Kelvin!).
  • Dalton's law of partial pressures: mixture ka total pressure = har gas ke partial pressures ka sum.
  • Ideal gas: jispe ye saare laws perfectly apply hon — low pressure, high temperature pe real gases ideal jaisi behave karti hain.

💡 Feel: gas = crores of tiny balls, har waqt bhaagti, takraati, walls pe dhakka maarti — wahi dhakka pressure hai!

2️⃣ Kinetic Theory of Gases — Pressure Ka Asli Raaz ⭐

Postulates (assumptions):

  • Molecules point-size hain (volume negligible), elastic collisions, beech me koi force nahi (ideal gas).
  • Random motion, sab directions equally likely; collisions instant.

Pressure derivation ka result ⭐: P = (1/3)ρv²_rms = (1/3)(mn/V)v²_rms — molecules ke bombardment se pressure!

  • Kinetic interpretation of temperature ⭐: average KE per molecule = (3/2)k_BT — TEMPERATURE AUR KUCH NAHI, average kinetic energy hai! (k_B = Boltzmann constant = 1.38×10⁻²³ J/K)
  • Total KE per mole = (3/2)RT; per molecule = (3/2)k_BT (R = N_A k_B).
  • Absolute zero ka matlab: T = 0 pe KE → 0 — molecules ruk jaayenge (isliye 0 K possible nahi!).
  • Ideal gas ki internal energy SIRF kinetic — T pe hi depend karti hai, P ya V pe nahi! ⭐

🎯 Concept gold: temperature measure karta hai molecules ki average speed ki energy — garam = tez molecules, thanda = slow!

3️⃣ RMS, Average aur Most Probable Speed ⭐

SpeedFormulaValue (relative)
Most probable (v_p)√(2k_BT/m) = √(2RT/M)1 (base)
Average (v_avg)√(8k_BT/πm) = √(8RT/πM)1.128 v_p
RMS (v_rms) ⭐√(3k_BT/m) = √(3RT/M)1.224 v_p
  • Order yaad rakho: v_p < v_avg < v_rms ⭐ (exam me direct puchte hain!)
  • Temperature badhao → saari speeds badhti hain (√T se). Mass zyada → speed kam (H₂ fastest!).
  • Maxwell distribution: speed ka curve — zyadaatar molecules middle me, kuch bahut slow, kuch bahut fast (istribution symmetric NAHI hai).
  • Escape velocity connection: H₂/He ki rms speed zyada → atmosphere se bhaag jaati hain — isliye Earth pe H₂ kam, Moon pe atmosphere nahi!
  • v_rms = √(3P/ρ) bhi likh sakte ho (P aur density se).

💡 RMS = root-mean-square — squares ka average, phir root. Yehi formula KE se jodta hai: KE = ½mv²_rms.

4️⃣ Degrees of Freedom aur Equipartition — Energy Ka Bantwara ⭐

  • Degrees of freedom (f): kitne independent ways me molecule energy store kar sakta hai — translation, rotation, vibration.
  • Monatomic (He, Ne): sirf 3 translational → f = 3. Diatomic (O₂, N₂): 3 translational + 2 rotational → f = 5. ⭐
  • Equipartition theorem ⭐: har degree of freedom ko average (1/2)k_BT energy milti hai — energy ka fair distribution!
  • Internal energy per mole: U = (f/2)RT — monatomic (3/2)RT, diatomic (5/2)RT.
  • Specific heats ⭐: C_v = (f/2)R, C_p = (f/2 + 1)R → γ = C_p/C_v = 1 + 2/f. Monatomic γ = 5/3, diatomic γ = 7/5 = 1.4!
  • Law of equipartition in mixtures: mixture ka C_v = weighted average (moles ke hisaab se).
  • High temperature pe diatomic me vibration bhi activate hota hai → f = 7 (but room temp pe 5 hi count karo).

💡 Feel: har molecule ke paas energy rakhne ke "alag alag pockets" (degrees of freedom) — har pocket me equal paisa (½k_BT)! Isliye diatomic ka C_v zyada — zyada pockets!

5️⃣ Mean Free Path aur Real Gases

  • Mean free path (λ): do collisions ke beech average distance — λ = 1/(√2 πd²n) = k_BT/(√2 πd²P). d = molecule diameter, n = number density. ⭐
  • Pressure badhao → λ ghatta (zyada bheed, jaldi takraav). Air me λ ~ 10⁻⁷ m — bahut chhota!
  • Collision frequency: kitni baar takraata hai per second = v_avg/λ.
  • Real gas vs ideal: real gases me molecules ka apna volume + intermolecular forces hote hain — high P, low T pe ideal law fail!
  • Van der Waals equation ⭐: (P + a/V²)(V − b) = RT (per mole) — 'a' attraction correction, 'b' volume correction.
  • Critical temperature: jiske upar gas liquefy nahi hoti chahe kitna bhi pressure lagao (CO₂ ka 31 °C — isliye summers me cylinder risky!).

🎯 Ideal gas = simplification; Van der Waals = reality check. Exam me a, b ke meanings aur λ ka formula pakka aata hai!

1️⃣ द्रव्य की आणविक प्रकृति — गैस के अंदर क्या है

  • सभी पदार्थ अणुओं से बने हैं, वे लगातार यादृच्छिक गति में रहते हैं — इसलिए गैसें पूरा पात्र भर लेती हैं।
  • आवोगाद्रो संख्या ⭐: N_A = 6.022×10²³ अणु प्रति मोल। 1 मोल गैस STP पर 22.4 L घेरती है।
  • बॉयल नियम: नियत T पर PV = नियतांक। चार्ल्स नियम: नियत P पर V ∝ T (केल्विन!)।
  • डाल्टन का आंशिक दाब नियम: मिश्रण का कुल दाब = आंशिक दाबों का योग।

💡 गैस = करोड़ों छोटी गेंदें, हर वक्त दौड़ती, टकराती — वही टकराहट दाब है!

2️⃣ गैसों का अणुगति सिद्धांत — दाब का असली रहस्य ⭐

परिणाम ⭐: P = (1/3)ρv²_rms — अणुओं के बौछार से दाब!

  • तापमान की अणुगति व्याख्या ⭐: प्रति अणु औसत गतिज ऊर्जा = (3/2)k_BT — तापमान और कुछ नहीं, औसत गतिज ऊर्जा है!
  • प्रति मोल कुल KE = (3/2)RT (R = N_A k_B)।
  • परम शून्य: T = 0 पर KE → 0 — अणु रुक जाएंगे (इसलिए 0 K असंभव!)।
  • आदर्श गैस की आंतरिक ऊर्जा केवल गतिज — केवल T पर निर्भर! ⭐

🎯 तापमान मापता है अणुओं की गति की ऊर्जा — गर्म = तेज़ अणु, ठंडा = धीमे!

3️⃣ RMS, औसत और प्रायिक चाल ⭐

चालसूत्रमान

  • क्रम याद रखो: v_p < v_avg < v_rms ⭐

  • पलायन वेग संबंध: H₂/He तेज़ → वायुमंडल से बच निकलती हैं — इसलिए पृथ्वी पर H₂ कम!
  • v_rms = √(3P/ρ) भी।

💡 RMS = वर्गों का औसत, फिर वर्गमूल। KE = ½mv²_rms से जुड़ता है।

4️⃣ स्वातंत्र्य कोटि और समविभाजन — ऊर्जा की बांट ⭐

  • स्वातंत्र्य कोटि (f): अणु कितने स्वतंत्र तरीकों से ऊर्जा संचित कर सकता है।
  • एकपरमाणुक: f = 3। द्विपरमाणुक: 3 स्थानांतरण + 2 घूर्णन → f = 5। ⭐
  • समविभाजन प्रमेय ⭐: प्रत्येक स्वातंत्र्य कोटि को औसत (1/2)k_BT ऊर्जा मिलती है!
  • आंतरिक ऊर्जा प्रति मोल: U = (f/2)RT — एकपरमाणुक (3/2)RT, द्विपरमाणुक (5/2)RT।
  • विशिष्ट ऊष्माएं ⭐: C_v = (f/2)R, C_p = (f/2+1)R → γ = 1 + 2/f।

💡 हर अणु के ऊर्जा रखने की अलग-अलग "जेबें" (DOF) — हर जेब में बराबर ऊर्जा (½k_BT)!

5️⃣ माध्य मुक्त पथ और वास्तविक गैसें

  • माध्य मुक्त पथ (λ): दो टक्करों के बीच औसत दूरी — λ = 1/(√2 πd²n)। ⭐
  • दाब बढ़ाओ → λ घटता। हवा में λ ~ 10⁻⁷ m।
  • वास्तविक बनाम आदर्श: वास्तविक गैसों में अणुओं का आयतन + अंतराआण्विक बल — उच्च P, निम्न T पर आदर्श नियम विफल!
  • वान डर वाल्स समीकरण ⭐: (P + a/V²)(V − b) = RT — 'a' आकर्षण संशोधन, 'b' आयतन संशोधन।
  • क्रांतिक तापमान: इसके ऊपर गैस द्रवित नहीं होती चाहे जितना दाब लगाओ।

🎯 a, b के अर्थ और λ का सूत्र परीक्षा में पक्का!

1️⃣ The Molecular Nature of Matter — What Is Inside a Gas

  • All matter is made of molecules in constant random motion — that is why gases fill the whole container.
  • Avogadro's number ⭐: N_A = 6.022×10²³ molecules per mole. 1 mole of gas occupies 22.4 L at STP (0 °C, 1 atm).
  • Boyle's law: at constant T, PV = constant. Charles' law: at constant P, V ∝ T (kelvin!).
  • Dalton's law of partial pressures: total pressure of a mixture = sum of partial pressures.
  • Ideal gas: one that follows these laws perfectly — real gases behave ideally at low pressure, high temperature.

💡 Feel: a gas is crores of tiny balls, always racing and colliding — that bombardment on the walls is pressure!

2️⃣ Kinetic Theory of Gases — The Real Secret of Pressure ⭐

Key result ⭐: P = (1/3)ρv²_rms = (1/3)(mn/V)v²_rms — pressure comes from molecular bombardment!

  • Kinetic interpretation of temperature ⭐: average KE per molecule = (3/2)k_BT — TEMPERATURE IS the average kinetic energy! (k_B = 1.38×10⁻²³ J/K)
  • Total KE per mole = (3/2)RT (since R = N_A k_B).
  • Absolute zero: at T = 0, KE → 0 — molecules would stop (that is why 0 K is unreachable!).
  • An ideal gas's internal energy is purely kinetic — it depends on T alone, not P or V! ⭐

🎯 Golden concept: temperature measures the energy of molecular motion — hot = fast molecules, cold = slow!

3️⃣ RMS, Average and Most Probable Speed ⭐

SpeedFormulaRelative value
Most probable (v_p)√(2RT/M)1 (base)
Average (v_avg)√(8RT/πM)1.128 v_p
RMS (v_rms) ⭐√(3RT/M)1.224 v_p
  • Remember the order: v_p < v_avg < v_rms ⭐ (asked directly in exams!)
  • Raise the temperature → all speeds grow (as √T). Heavier gas → slower (H₂ is the fastest!).
  • Maxwell distribution: the speed curve — most molecules in the middle, a few very slow or very fast (NOT symmetric).
  • Escape-velocity connection: H₂/He are fast enough to escape the atmosphere — that is why Earth holds little H₂ and the Moon has no atmosphere!
  • Also v_rms = √(3P/ρ) (from pressure and density).

💡 RMS = root of the mean of the squares — it links straight into KE = ½mv²_rms.

4️⃣ Degrees of Freedom and Equipartition — Sharing the Energy ⭐

  • Degrees of freedom (f): the independent ways a molecule can store energy — translation, rotation, vibration.
  • Monatomic (He, Ne): only 3 translational → f = 3. Diatomic (O₂, N₂): 3 translational + 2 rotational → f = 5. ⭐
  • Equipartition theorem ⭐: every degree of freedom gets an average (1/2)k_BT of energy — a fair share for each!
  • Internal energy per mole: U = (f/2)RT — monatomic (3/2)RT, diatomic (5/2)RT.
  • Specific heats ⭐: C_v = (f/2)R, C_p = (f/2 + 1)R → γ = C_p/C_v = 1 + 2/f. Monatomic γ = 5/3, diatomic γ = 7/5 = 1.4!
  • At high temperatures diatomic vibration also activates → f = 7 (but at room temperature count 5).

💡 Feel: every molecule has separate "pockets" for energy (degrees of freedom) — each pocket gets the same amount (½k_BT)! That is why diatomic C_v is bigger — more pockets!

5️⃣ Mean Free Path and Real Gases

  • Mean free path (λ): the average distance between two collisions — λ = 1/(√2 πd²n) = k_BT/(√2 πd²P). d = molecular diameter, n = number density. ⭐
  • Raise the pressure → λ shrinks (more crowd, sooner collision). In air λ ~ 10⁻⁷ m — very small!
  • Collision frequency: collisions per second = v_avg/λ.
  • Real gas vs ideal: real molecules have their own volume and intermolecular forces — the ideal law fails at high P, low T!
  • Van der Waals equation ⭐: (P + a/V²)(V − b) = RT (per mole) — 'a' corrects for attraction, 'b' for volume.
  • Critical temperature: above it no pressure can liquefy the gas (CO₂'s is 31 °C!).

🎯 Ideal gas = simplification; Van der Waals = reality check. The meanings of a and b and the λ formula are guaranteed exam questions!

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Average KE per molecule 27 °C (300 K) pe? (k_B = 1.38×10⁻²³ J/K)
Answer dekho

KE = (3/2)k_BT = 1.5×1.38×10⁻²³×300 = 6.21×10⁻²¹ J.

Q2. Temperature ka kinetic interpretation kya hai?
Answer dekho

Temperature = molecules ki average kinetic energy ka measure — (3/2)k_BT per molecule.

Q3. O₂ aur H₂ same temperature pe. Kiske molecules tez?
Answer dekho

H₂ — v_rms = √(3RT/M), mass kam to speed zyada (√32/√2 = 4 guna!).

Q4. Monatomic gas ka γ?
Answer dekho

γ = 1 + 2/f = 1 + 2/3 = 5/3 ≈ 1.67 (diatomic 7/5 = 1.4).

Q5. Diatomic gas ka C_v per mole?
Answer dekho

C_v = (f/2)R = (5/2)R ≈ 20.8 J/mol·K.

Q6. Equipartition theorem kya kehta hai?
Answer dekho

Har degree of freedom ko average (1/2)k_BT energy milti hai — chahe translation ho ya rotation.

Q7. v_p, v_avg, v_rms ka order?
Answer dekho

v_p < v_avg < v_rms — √2 : √(8/π) : √3 (ya 1 : 1.128 : 1.224).

Q8. 1 mole ideal gas ki total internal energy (diatomic, 300 K)?
Answer dekho

U = (5/2)RT = 2.5×8.314×300 ≈ 6.24 kJ.

Q9. Pressure double karne pe mean free path pe kya asar?
Answer dekho

λ = k_BT/(√2πd²P) → P double → λ half (zyada bheed, jaldi takraav).

Q10. Van der Waals equation me 'a' aur 'b' kya correct karte hain?
Answer dekho

a = intermolecular attraction (pressure kam hota hai), b = molecules ka apna volume (available volume kam).
