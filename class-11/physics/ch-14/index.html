📖 Full Explanation (Long Notes)

Concept pakka karne ke liye — aaram se padho

🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Waves Kya Hain — Energy Ka Safar, Matter Ka Nahi

  • Wave: ek disturbance jo medium me travel karti hai — energy transfer hota hai, matter ka nahi! (Paani ki leher me paani aage nahi jaata, sirf upar-neeche hota hai.)
  • Transverse ⭐: particles medium ke PERPENDICULAR oscillate karte hain — rope ki wave, light. Crest aur trough bante hain. Sirf solids aur liquid SURFACE pe chalti hain (gases me nahi!).
  • Longitudinal ⭐: particles medium ke PARALLEL oscillate karte hain — sound. Compressions (bheed) aur rarefactions (khalai) banti hain. Solids, liquids, gases — teeno me chalti hai!
  • Wave terms: wavelength λ (ek cycle ki length), frequency ν, period T = 1/ν, amplitude A.
  • Wave speed ⭐: v = νλ = λ/T — medium decide karta hai, frequency source.

💡 Feel: stadium me Mexican wave — log apni seat se uthte-baithte hain (oscillate), lekin wave poora stadium ghoom jaati hai. Log = medium, wave = energy!

2️⃣ Wave Equation aur Speed ⭐

  • Travelling wave equation ⭐: y(x,t) = A sin(kx − ωt + φ) — +x direction me jaati wave. (kx + ωt = −x direction.)
  • Wave number: k = 2π/λ; angular frequency: ω = 2πν. Speed: v = ω/k = νλ. ⭐
  • String pe speed ⭐: v = √(T/μ) — T = tension, μ = linear mass density (kg/m). Tight string ya patli string → tez wave!
  • Sound ki speed: v = √(B/ρ) fluids me; solids me √(Y/ρ). Air me ~340 m/s (20 °C pe 343).
  • Temperature effect ⭐: sound v ∝ √T (Kelvin) — garmi me sound tez! (v_t = v_0√(1 + t/273))
  • Lightning pehle dikhti hai, thunder baad me — light (3×10⁸ m/s) vs sound (340 m/s)! 3 sec ka gap = ~1 km doori.

🎯 Formula pack: v = νλ hamesha; string me √(T/μ); sound me √(elasticity/density). Exam me 2 me se 1 pakka aata hai!

3️⃣ Superposition aur Standing Waves ⭐

  • Principle of superposition: do waves ek saath aayein to net displacement = individual displacements ka SUM. (Waves ek dusre se GUZAR jaati hain bina bigde!)
  • Interference: constructive (crest+crest = double) ya destructive (crest+trough = cancel).
  • Reflection: fixed end se → wave PALT ke aati hai (crest wapas trough banke — phase change π); free end se → bina palte (no phase change). ⭐
  • Standing (stationary) wave ⭐: do identical waves OPPOSITE directions me travel karein → pattern freeze ho jaata hai! y = 2A sin(kx)cos(ωt).
  • Nodes (N): hamesha zero displacement (khamosh points). Antinodes (A): maximum oscillation (sabse zyada hilne wale). Distance N-to-N = A-to-A = λ/2; N-to-A = λ/4. ⭐
  • Standing wave me energy travel NAHI hoti — sab kuch stored, isliye "stationary"!

💡 Feel: rope ka ek end pakad ke hilao — wave aage jaake reflect hoti hai, aur perfect speed pe tum dono waves ko "standing" pattern me lock kar dete ho. Sitar ki string wahi karti hai!

4️⃣ Vibrating Strings aur Air Columns — Music Ka Physics ⭐

String fixed at both ends (guitar, sitar):

  • Sirf woh wavelengths possible jisme ends pe nodes: L = nλ/2 → allowed: λₙ = 2L/n.
  • Fundamental (1st harmonic): ν₁ = v/2L. Harmonics ⭐: νₙ = nν₁ — SAARE multiples allowed (2ν₁, 3ν₁, ...)!
  • Short finger position (chhota L) → zyada frequency → tez sur!

Air columns (flute, organ):

  • Open pipe (dono ends khule) ⭐: L = nλ/2 → νₙ = nv/2L — saare harmonics (string jaisa!).
  • Closed pipe (ek end band) ⭐: L = (2n−1)λ/4 → sirf ODD harmonics: ν₁ = v/4L, 3ν₁, 5ν₁... (2ν₁ allowed NAHI!)
  • Closed end = node, open end = antinode (yaad rakhne ka rule!).
  • End correction: real pipe me effective length thodi zyada (e ≈ 0.3r per open end).

🎯 Exam trick: closed pipe fundamental = v/4L, open = v/2L — same length ka open pipe DOUBLE frequency deta hai!

5️⃣ Beats aur Doppler Effect — Sunne Wale Tricks ⭐

  • Beats ⭐: do waves thodi si alag frequencies (ν₁, ν₂) ki saath bajen → loud-soft-loud pattern! Beat frequency = |ν₁ − ν₂|.
  • Tuning: sitar player tuning peg ghumata hai — beats slow → zero → perfect tune! (Beat frequency sunke fine-tuning.)
  • Doppler effect ⭐: source ya observer move kare → apparent frequency change! Ambulance aati hai → pitch high; jaati hai → pitch low.
  • Formulas: source approaching stationary observer: ν' = ν×v/(v − v_s); receding: ν' = ν×v/(v + v_s). Observer approaching: ν' = ν(v + v_o)/v. ⭐ (v = sound speed)
  • General yaad rakho: approach → frequency UP, recede → DOWN — numerator/denominator me sign isi hisaab se.
  • Applications: radar speed guns, medical ultrasound (blood flow), astronomy redshift (universe expand kar raha hai!).

💡 Feel: Doppler = waves ka "crowd hona" — source aage badhti hai to aage wali waves ikatthi (chhoti λ → zyada ν), peeche faili hui (badi λ → kam ν).

1️⃣ तरंगें क्या हैं — ऊर्जा का सफर, पदार्थ का नहीं

  • तरंग: एक विक्षोभ जो माध्यम में चलता है — ऊर्जा स्थानांतरित होती है, पदार्थ नहीं!
  • अनुप्रस्थ ⭐: कण माध्यम के लंबवत दोलन करते हैं — रस्सी की तरंग, प्रकाश। शृंग (crest) और गर्त (trough)। केवल ठोसों और द्रव सतह पर!
  • अनुदैर्ध्य ⭐: कण माध्यम के समांतर दोलन करते हैं — ध्वनि। संपीडन और विरलन। तीनों अवस्थाओं में!
  • तरंग पद: तरंगदैर्ध्य λ, आवृत्ति ν, आवर्तकाल T = 1/ν, आयाम A।
  • तरंग चाल ⭐: v = νλ = λ/T — माध्यम तय करता है, आवृत्ति स्रोत।

💡 स्टेडियम की Mexican wave — लोग अपनी जगह उठते-बैठते हैं, तरंग पूरा स्टेडियम घूम जाती है!

2️⃣ तरंग समीकरण और चाल ⭐

  • प्रगामी तरंग समीकरण ⭐: y(x,t) = A sin(kx − ωt + φ) — +x दिशा में।
  • तरंग संख्या: k = 2π/λ; चाल: v = ω/k = νλ। ⭐
  • डोरी पर चाल ⭐: v = √(T/μ) — T = तनाव, μ = रैखिक द्रव्यमान घनत्व। कसी डोरी → तेज़ तरंग!
  • ध्वनि की चाल: v = √(B/ρ) द्रवों में; ठोसों में √(Y/ρ)। हवा में ~340 m/s।
  • तापमान प्रभाव ⭐: ध्वनि v ∝ √T — गर्मी में ध्वनि तेज़!
  • बिजली पहले दिखती है, गड़गड़ाहट बाद में — 3 sec का अंतर = ~1 km दूरी।

🎯 v = νλ सदा; डोरी में √(T/μ); ध्वनि में √(प्रत्यास्थता/घनत्व)।

3️⃣ अध्यारोपण और अप्रगामी तरंगें ⭐

  • अध्यारोपण सिद्धांत: दो तरंगें साथ आएं तो कुल विस्थापन = अलग-अलग विस्थापनों का योग।
  • व्यतिकरण: संपोषी (शृंग+शृंग = दोगुना) या विनाशी (शृंग+गर्त = निरसन)।
  • परावर्तन: बंधे सिरे से → तरंग पलटकर (कला परिवर्तन π); मुक्त सिरे से → बिना पलटे। ⭐
  • अप्रगामी तरंग ⭐: दो समान तरंगें विपरीत दिशाओं में → पैटर्न स्थिर! y = 2A sin(kx)cos(ωt)।
  • निस्पंद (node): सदा शून्य विस्थापन। प्रस्पंद (antinode): अधिकतम दोलन। निस्पंद-से-निस्पंद = λ/2; निस्पंद-से-प्रस्पंद = λ/4। ⭐
  • अप्रगामी तरंग में ऊर्जा स्थानांतरित नहीं होती — सब संचित!

💡 रस्सी हिलाओ — तरंग परावर्तित होकर लौटती है, सही गति पर "स्थिर" पैटर्न बनता है। सितार की तार यही करती है!

4️⃣ कंपित डोरियां और वायु स्तंभ — संगीत का भौतिकी ⭐

दोनों सिरों पर बंधी डोरी (गिटार, सितार):

  • केवल वे तरंगदैर्ध्य संभव जिनमें सिरों पर निस्पंद: L = nλ/2 → λₙ = 2L/n।
  • मूल आवृत्ति: ν₁ = v/2L। संनादी (harmonics) ⭐: νₙ = nν₁ — सभी गुणक संभव!

वायु स्तंभ (बांसुरी, ऑर्गन):

  • खुली नली ⭐: νₙ = nv/2L — सभी संनादी।
  • बंद नली ⭐: केवल विषम संनादी: ν₁ = v/4L, 3ν₁, 5ν₁... (2ν₁ संभव नहीं!)
  • बंद सिरा = निस्पंद, खुला सिरा = प्रस्पंद।

🎯 बंद नली मूल = v/4L, खुली = v/2L — समान लंबाई की खुली नली दोगुनी आवृत्ति!

5️⃣ विस्पंद और डॉप्लर प्रभाव — सुनने वाली तरकीबें ⭐

  • विस्पंद (beats) ⭐: थोड़ी अलग आवृत्तियों (ν₁, ν₂) की दो तरंगें साथ → तेज़-धीमा-तेज़ पैटर्न! विस्पंद आवृत्ति = |ν₁ − ν₂|।
  • ट्यूनिंग: विस्पंद धीमे → शून्य → सुर मिल गया!
  • डॉप्लर प्रभाव ⭐: स्रोत या श्रोता गतिमान → आभासी आवृत्ति बदलती है! एम्बुलेंस आती है → तीखी; जाती है → धीमी।
  • सूत्र: स्रोत आता हुआ: ν' = ν×v/(v − v_s); जाता हुआ: ν' = ν×v/(v + v_s)। ⭐
  • याद रखो: पास आए → आवृत्ति बढ़े, दूर जाए → घटे।
  • उपयोग: राडार स्पीड गन, अल्ट्रासाउंड (रक्त प्रवाह), खगोलशास्त्र में रेडशिफ्ट!

💡 डॉप्लर = तरंगों की "भीड़" — स्रोत आगे बढ़े तो आगे तरंगें सिकुड़तीं (छोटी λ → अधिक ν), पीछे फैलतीं।

1️⃣ What Waves Are — Energy Travels, Matter Does Not

  • Wave: a disturbance travelling through a medium — energy is transferred, matter is not! (In a water wave the water does not move forward, only up and down.)
  • Transverse ⭐: particles oscillate PERPENDICULAR to the medium — a rope wave, light. Crests and troughs form. Only in solids and liquid SURFACES (not gases!).
  • Longitudinal ⭐: particles oscillate PARALLEL to the medium — sound. Compressions and rarefactions form. Travels through solids, liquids AND gases!
  • Wave terms: wavelength λ (length of one cycle), frequency ν, period T = 1/ν, amplitude A.
  • Wave speed ⭐: v = νλ = λ/T — the medium decides the speed, the source decides the frequency.

💡 Feel: a stadium Mexican wave — people stay in their seats (oscillate), yet the wave travels the whole stadium. People = medium, wave = energy!

2️⃣ The Wave Equation and Speed ⭐

  • Travelling wave equation ⭐: y(x,t) = A sin(kx − ωt + φ) — a wave going in the +x direction. (kx + ωt travels in −x.)
  • Wave number: k = 2π/λ; angular frequency: ω = 2πν. Speed: v = ω/k = νλ. ⭐
  • Speed on a string ⭐: v = √(T/μ) — T = tension, μ = linear mass density. Tighter or thinner string → faster wave!
  • Speed of sound: v = √(B/ρ) in fluids; √(Y/ρ) in solids. ~340 m/s in air (343 at 20 °C).
  • Temperature effect ⭐: sound v ∝ √T (kelvin) — sound is faster in warm air!
  • Lightning appears before thunder — light (3×10⁸ m/s) vs sound (340 m/s)! A 3-second gap ≈ 1 km away.

🎯 Formula pack: v = νλ always; √(T/μ) on a string; √(elasticity/density) for sound. One of these appears in every exam!

3️⃣ Superposition and Standing Waves ⭐

  • Principle of superposition: when two waves meet, the net displacement is the SUM of the individual ones. (Waves pass through each other undisturbed!)
  • Interference: constructive (crest + crest = double) or destructive (crest + trough = cancel).
  • Reflection: from a fixed end the wave returns INVERTED (crest comes back as a trough — phase change π); from a free end, uninverted. ⭐
  • Standing (stationary) wave ⭐: two identical waves travelling in OPPOSITE directions freeze into a pattern! y = 2A sin(kx)cos(ωt).
  • Nodes (N): always zero displacement. Antinodes (A): maximum oscillation. N-to-N = A-to-A = λ/2; N-to-A = λ/4. ⭐
  • A standing wave transports NO energy — everything is stored, which is why it is called stationary!

💡 Feel: shake a rope at just the right rate and the reflected wave locks into a frozen pattern. That is exactly what a sitar string does!

4️⃣ Vibrating Strings and Air Columns — The Physics of Music ⭐

String fixed at both ends (guitar, sitar):

  • Only wavelengths with nodes at the ends survive: L = nλ/2 → λₙ = 2L/n.
  • Fundamental (1st harmonic): ν₁ = v/2L. Harmonics ⭐: νₙ = nν₁ — ALL multiples allowed (2ν₁, 3ν₁, ...)!
  • A shorter finger position (smaller L) → higher frequency → a sharper note!

Air columns (flute, organ):

  • Open pipe (both ends open) ⭐: L = nλ/2 → νₙ = nv/2L — all harmonics (like a string!).
  • Closed pipe (one end closed) ⭐: L = (2n−1)λ/4 → only ODD harmonics: ν₁ = v/4L, 3ν₁, 5ν₁... (2ν₁ is NOT allowed!)
  • Closed end = node, open end = antinode (the rule to remember!).
  • End correction: a real pipe's effective length is slightly longer (e ≈ 0.3r per open end).

🎯 Exam trick: closed pipe fundamental = v/4L, open = v/2L — the same-length open pipe sounds DOUBLE the frequency!

5️⃣ Beats and the Doppler Effect — Tricks You Can Hear ⭐

  • Beats ⭐: two waves of slightly different frequencies (ν₁, ν₂) playing together → a loud-soft-loud pattern! Beat frequency = |ν₁ − ν₂|.
  • Tuning: a sitar player turns the peg until the beats slow to zero — perfectly tuned!
  • Doppler effect ⭐: if the source or the observer moves, the apparent frequency changes! An approaching ambulance sounds high-pitched; a receding one, low.
  • Formulas: source approaching a stationary observer: ν' = ν×v/(v − v_s); receding: ν' = ν×v/(v + v_s). Observer approaching: ν' = ν(v + v_o)/v. ⭐
  • General rule: approach → frequency UP, recede → DOWN — the signs follow from this.
  • Applications: radar speed guns, medical ultrasound (blood flow), astronomical redshift (the universe is expanding!).

💡 Feel: Doppler is a crowding of waves — a source moving forward squeezes the waves ahead (shorter λ → higher ν) and stretches those behind.

🏆 JEE Practice — 10 Questions

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer dekho.

Q1. Wave ki frequency 50 Hz, wavelength 4 m. Speed?
Answer dekho

v = νλ = 50×4 = 200 m/s.

Q2. String ki tension 4 guna kar do. Wave speed?
Answer dekho

v = √(T/μ) → v ∝ √T → √4 = double.

Q3. Sound air me 340 m/s, frequency 170 Hz. Wavelength?
Answer dekho

λ = v/ν = 340/170 = 2 m.

Q4. Transverse aur longitudinal wave me farak?
Answer dekho

Transverse: particles perpendicular oscillate (rope, light); longitudinal: parallel (sound — compressions/rarefactions).

Q5. Rope ke fixed end se reflect hone pe wave ko kya hota hai?
Answer dekho

Phase change π — crest wapas trough banke aata hai (free end se no change).

Q6. Standing wave me do consecutive nodes ke beech distance?
Answer dekho

λ/2 (node se antinode λ/4).

Q7. 1 m lambi open pipe ka fundamental frequency? (v = 340 m/s)
Answer dekho

ν₁ = v/2L = 340/2 = 170 Hz.

Q8. Same length ki closed pipe ka fundamental?
Answer dekho

ν₁ = v/4L = 340/4 = 85 Hz — open pipe ka aadha! (Sirf odd harmonics: 85, 255, 425...)

Q9. Do tuning forks 256 Hz aur 260 Hz saath bajen. Beats per second?
Answer dekho

Beat frequency = |260 − 256| = 4 Hz.

Q10. Ambulance (source 500 Hz) tumhari taraf aa rahi hai. Pitch kaise lagegi?
Answer dekho

High (zyada frequency) — Doppler: approach me waves crowd hoti hain, ν' = νv/(v − v_s) > ν.
