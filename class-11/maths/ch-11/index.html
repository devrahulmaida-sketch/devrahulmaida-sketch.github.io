🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ 3D Geometry — Flat Se Full Space Tak

Ab tak hum 2D (x, y) me khel rahe the — paper ki sheet. Lekin asli duniya 3D hai: upar-neeche, aage-peeche, dayan-bayan. 3D geometry me har point ko TEEN coordinates chahiye: (x, y, z). Room ka kona socho — floor pe x aur y, deewar ke saath upar z. Chapter chhota hai lekin 12th ke Vectors aur 3D Geometry ka base hai.

  • Teen axes: x-axis, y-axis, z-axis — sab ek doosre ke perpendicular, origin (0,0,0) pe milte hain.
  • Coordinate planes: XY-plane (z = 0), YZ-plane (x = 0), ZX-plane (y = 0).
  • Point likhna: (3, 2, 5) = x me 3, y me 2, z me 5 — teen measurements, ek point.
  • Right-handed system: thumb x, first finger y, middle finger z — right hand rule!

💡 Feel: 2D = Google Maps (flat), 3D = drone delivery (height bhi chahiye)!

2️⃣ Octants — Space ke 8 Sections ⭐

Teen coordinate planes space ko 8 octants me divide karte hain (2D me quadrants 4 the, 3D me double!).

  • Octant I: (+, +, +) — sab positive.
  • Baaki octants: signs ka combination — (−,+,+), (−,−,+), (+,−,+), (+,+,−), (−,+,−), (−,−,−), (+,−,−).
  • Exam question ⭐: "(−2, 4, 7) kis octant me?" — signs dekho: (−,+,+).
  • Points ON planes/axes: x-axis pe (a, 0, 0); XY-plane pe (a, b, 0) — ek coordinate zero!

🎯 Trick: octant sirf signs ka game hai — coordinates ki VALUES matter nahi karti, sirf +/−!

3️⃣ Distance Formula — 3D Version ⭐

  • Distance between (x₁,y₁,z₁) aur (x₂,y₂,z₂) ⭐: √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)² + (z₂−z₁)²] — 2D formula me bas ek z-term aur!
  • Origin se distance: √(x² + y² + z²).
  • Pythagoras ka extension: room ki diagonal — floor ki diagonal pe deewar ka triangle!
  • Collinearity check: teen points ke liye AB + BC = AC (distance se).
  • Shape identification ⭐: equilateral triangle (3 sides same), isosceles (2 same), right triangle (Pythagoras), square/rhombus/rectangle (sides + diagonals check).

💡 Formula wahi Pythagoras hai — sirf ek dimension aur add. (x²+y²) pe z² aur jodo, root lo!

4️⃣ Section Formula — 3D Me Bhi Same ⭐

  • Internal division ⭐: point joining (x₁,y₁,z₁) aur (x₂,y₂,z₂) ko m:n me divide karta hai → ((mx₂+nx₁)/(m+n), (my₂+ny₁)/(m+n), (mz₂+nz₁)/(m+n)).
  • External division: m − n sign ke saath — ((mx₂−nx₁)/(m−n), ...).
  • Midpoint ⭐: ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2, (z₁+z₂)/2).
  • Triangle ka centroid: ((x₁+x₂+x₃)/3, (y₁+y₂+y₃)/3, (z₁+z₂+z₃)/3).
  • k nikalna: "point kis ratio me divide karta hai?" — k:1 assume karo, coordinates match karo.

💡 2D section formula yaad hai? 3D me kuch naya NAHI — sirf har coordinate ke saath z bhi same treatment!

5️⃣ Exam Patterns — 3D Ke Standard Questions

  • Octant batao: signs dekhke — easiest 1 mark.
  • Distance nikalo: direct formula, kabhi-kabhi unknown point ke liye equation.
  • Shape prove karo ⭐: "ye teen points equilateral triangle banate hain" — teeno distances same dikhao.
  • Section formula: ratio find karna ya point find karna.
  • Collinearity: distance method ya section method (koi point baaki do ke segment pe?).
  • Locus-lite: "given 2 points se equidistant points ka set" — perpendicular bisector plane.

💡 Chapter sirf 2 formulas ka: distance + section. Dono 2D ke twins hain — ek hi effort me dono pakke!

1️⃣ त्रिविम ज्यामिति — सपाट से पूर्ण आकाश तक

अब तक हम 2D (x, y) में खेल रहे थे। पर असली दुनिया 3D है। त्रिविम ज्यामिति में हर बिंदु को तीन निर्देशांक चाहिए: (x, y, z)। कमरे का कोना सोचो — फर्श पर x और y, दीवार के साथ ऊपर z। यह अध्याय 12वीं के सदिश और 3D ज्यामिति की नींव है।

  • तीन अक्ष: x-अक्ष, y-अक्ष, z-अक्ष — परस्पर लंबवत, मूलबिंदु (0,0,0) पर।
  • निर्देशांक तल: XY-तल (z = 0), YZ-तल (x = 0), ZX-तल (y = 0)।
  • बिंदु: (3, 2, 5) = x में 3, y में 2, z में 5।
  • दक्षिणावर्त नियम: अंगूठा x, तर्जनी y, मध्यमा z — दाएँ हाथ का नियम!

💡 2D = समतल नक्शा, 3D = ड्रोन डिलीवरी (ऊँचाई भी)!

2️⃣ अष्टांश — आकाश के 8 भाग ⭐

तीन निर्देशांक तल आकाश को 8 अष्टांशों में बाँटते हैं।

  • अष्टांश I: (+, +, +) — सभी धनात्मक।
  • अन्य अष्टांश: चिह्नों के संयोजन — (−,+,+), (−,−,+), (+,−,+), (+,+,−), (−,+,−), (−,−,−), (+,−,−)।
  • परीक्षा प्रश्न ⭐: "(−2, 4, 7) किस अष्टांश में?" — चिह्न देखो: (−,+,+)।
  • तल/अक्ष पर बिंदु: x-अक्ष पर (a, 0, 0); XY-तल पर (a, b, 0)।

🎯 अष्टांश केवल चिह्नों का खेल — मान मायने नहीं रखते, केवल +/−!

3️⃣ दूरी सूत्र — 3D संस्करण ⭐

  • (x₁,y₁,z₁) और (x₂,y₂,z₂) के बीच दूरी ⭐: √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)² + (z₂−z₁)²] — 2D सूत्र में बस एक z-पद और!
  • मूलबिंदु से दूरी: √(x² + y² + z²)।
  • पाइथागोरस का विस्तार: कमरे का विकर्ण।
  • संरेखता: AB + BC = AC।
  • आकृति पहचान ⭐: समबाहु (3 भुजाएँ समान), समद्विबाहु (2 समान), समकोण (पाइथागोरस), वर्ग/समचतुर्भुज/आयत।

💡 सूत्र वही पाइथागोरस — बस एक विमा और जोड़ो!

4️⃣ विभाजन सूत्र — 3D में भी समान ⭐

  • आंतरिक विभाजन ⭐: ((mx₂+nx₁)/(m+n), (my₂+ny₁)/(m+n), (mz₂+nz₁)/(m+n))।
  • बाह्य विभाजन: m − n चिह्न के साथ।
  • मध्यबिंदु ⭐: ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2, (z₁+z₂)/2)।
  • त्रिभुज का केंद्रक: ((x₁+x₂+x₃)/3, (y₁+y₂+y₃)/3, (z₁+z₂+z₃)/3)।
  • k निकालना: k:1 मानो, निर्देशांक मिलाओ।

💡 2D विभाजन सूत्र याद है? 3D में कुछ नया नहीं — z भी वही व्यवहार!

5️⃣ परीक्षा के पैटर्न

  • अष्टांश बताओ: चिह्न देखकर — सबसे आसान।
  • दूरी निकालो: सीधा सूत्र।
  • आकृति सिद्ध करो ⭐: "समबाहु त्रिभुज" — तीनों दूरियाँ समान दिखाओ।
  • विभाजन सूत्र: अनुपात या बिंदु ज्ञात करना।
  • संरेखता: दूरी विधि या विभाजन विधि।

💡 अध्याय केवल 2 सूत्रों का: दूरी + विभाजन — दोनों 2D के जुड़वाँ!

1️⃣ 3D Geometry — From Flatland to Full Space

Until now we played in 2D (x, y) — a sheet of paper. But the real world is 3D: up-down, forward-back, left-right. In 3D geometry every point needs THREE coordinates: (x, y, z). Think of a room's corner — x and y along the floor, z up the wall. The chapter is small but it's the base for Class 12 Vectors and 3D Geometry.

  • Three axes: x-axis, y-axis, z-axis — all mutually perpendicular, meeting at the origin (0,0,0).
  • Coordinate planes: XY-plane (z = 0), YZ-plane (x = 0), ZX-plane (y = 0).
  • Reading a point: (3, 2, 5) = 3 in x, 2 in y, 5 in z — three measurements, one point.
  • Right-handed system: thumb = x, first finger = y, middle finger = z — the right-hand rule!

💡 Feel it: 2D is a flat map; 3D is drone delivery (height matters too)!

2️⃣ Octants — the 8 Sections of Space ⭐

The three coordinate planes divide space into 8 octants (2D had 4 quadrants; 3D has double!).

  • Octant I: (+, +, +) — all positive.
  • Other octants: sign combinations — (−,+,+), (−,−,+), (+,−,+), (+,+,−), (−,+,−), (−,−,−), (+,−,−).
  • Exam question ⭐: "Which octant holds (−2, 4, 7)?" — read the signs: (−,+,+).
  • Points ON planes/axes: on the x-axis (a, 0, 0); on the XY-plane (a, b, 0) — one coordinate is zero!

🎯 Trick: octants are only about signs — the VALUES don't matter, just +/−!

3️⃣ Distance Formula — the 3D Version ⭐

  • Distance between (x₁,y₁,z₁) and (x₂,y₂,z₂) ⭐: √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)² + (z₂−z₁)²] — the 2D formula with one extra z-term!
  • Distance from the origin: √(x² + y² + z²).
  • Pythagoras extended: a room's diagonal — a triangle on the floor's diagonal plus the wall!
  • Collinearity check: three points satisfy AB + BC = AC.
  • Shape identification ⭐: equilateral triangle (3 equal sides), isosceles (2 equal), right triangle (Pythagoras), square/rhombus/rectangle (check sides + diagonals).

💡 It's the same Pythagoras — just add one more dimension. Add z² to (x²+y²) and root it!

4️⃣ Section Formula — Same in 3D ⭐

  • Internal division ⭐: the point dividing the join of (x₁,y₁,z₁) and (x₂,y₂,z₂) in ratio m:n is ((mx₂+nx₁)/(m+n), (my₂+ny₁)/(m+n), (mz₂+nz₁)/(m+n)).
  • External division: with the m − n sign — ((mx₂−nx₁)/(m−n), ...).
  • Midpoint ⭐: ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2, (z₁+z₂)/2).
  • Centroid of a triangle: ((x₁+x₂+x₃)/3, (y₁+y₂+y₃)/3, (z₁+z₂+z₃)/3).
  • Finding k: "in what ratio does a point divide?" — assume k:1 and match coordinates.

💡 Remember the 2D section formula? Nothing new in 3D — the z-coordinate just gets the same treatment!

5️⃣ Exam Patterns — Standard 3D Questions

  • Name the octant: read the signs — the easiest mark.
  • Find the distance: direct formula, sometimes forming equations for an unknown point.
  • Prove the shape ⭐: "these three points form an equilateral triangle" — show all three distances equal.
  • Section formula: find the ratio or find the point.
  • Collinearity: distance method or section method (does one point lie on the segment of the other two?).
  • Locus-lite: "set of points equidistant from 2 given points" — the perpendicular bisector plane.

💡 The chapter is just 2 formulas: distance + section. Both are twins of their 2D versions — one effort, two wins!
