🌐 Notes language:

1️⃣ Coordinate Geometry — Geometry + Algebra ka Combo

Geometry ki problems ko algebra se solve karna hi coordinate geometry hai — Descartes ka genius idea. Ek line ko equation me likh do, phir angle, distance, slope sab calculation ban jaata hai. Straight Lines chapter pura coordinate geometry ka foundation hai — Circles, Conic Sections sab isi pe bante hain.

  • Revision basics: point (x, y), distance formula √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²], section formula.
  • Slope (gradient) m ⭐: line kitni steep hai — m = tan θ, jahan θ line ka x-axis ke saath angle (anti-clockwise).
  • Do points se slope: m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁) — rise over run!
  • Horizontal line: m = 0. Vertical line: slope undefined (tan 90°).

💡 Feel: slope = pahadi raste ka gradient board — "1 in 10" matlab 10 chalne pe 1 upar!

2️⃣ Parallel aur Perpendicular Lines ⭐

  • Parallel lines: slopes EQUAL — m₁ = m₂. (Same steepness, kabhi nahi milengi.)
  • Perpendicular lines ⭐: slopes ka product = −1 — m₁ × m₂ = −1. Ek doosre ka negative reciprocal!
  • Angle between two lines: tan θ = |(m₁ − m₂)/(1 + m₁m₂)|.
  • Collinearity ⭐: teen points A, B, C ek line pe hain ⟺ slope(AB) = slope(BC). (Ya area of triangle = 0.)
  • Vertical lines (x = a): perpendicular check me dhyan rakho — inka slope undefined hota hai, formula seedha nahi lagta.

🎯 m₁m₂ = −1 exam me sabse zyada puchha jaata hai — perpendicular bisector, altitude of triangle, sab isi pe!

3️⃣ Line ki Equations — 5 Forms ⭐

Situation ke hisaab se sahi form choose karo:

  • Point-slope form ⭐: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁) — ek point + slope pata ho.
  • Two-point form: y − y₁ = [(y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁)](x − x₁) — do points pata hon.
  • Slope-intercept form ⭐: y = mx + c — c = y-intercept (line y-axis ko kahan kaat ti hai).
  • Intercept form: x/a + y/b = 1 — line x-axis ko (a, 0) aur y-axis ko (0, b) pe kaate.
  • Normal form: x cos α + y sin α = p — p = origin se line ka perpendicular distance, α = us perpendicular ka angle.
  • General form: Ax + By + C = 0 — slope = −A/B, intercepts −C/A aur −C/B.

💡 General form se slope nikalna sabse tez: Bx ko right side bhejo, A ka sign palat do — m = −A/B!

4️⃣ Distance Formulas ⭐

  • Point se line ka distance ⭐: point (x₁, y₁) se line Ax + By + C = 0 ka perpendicular distance = |Ax₁ + By₁ + C| / √(A² + B²).
  • Do parallel lines ke beech distance: Ax + By + C₁ = 0 aur Ax + By + C₂ = 0 → |C₁ − C₂| / √(A² + B²).
  • Origin se distance: |C| / √(A² + B²) — point formula me (0,0) rakho.
  • Concurrency: teen lines ek point se guzrein — kisi do ka intersection teesri pe check karo.

💡 Distance formula ka use: triangle ka area (base × height/2), angle bisectors, locus problems — sab jagah!

5️⃣ Exam Patterns — Kya Kya Aata Hai

  • Slope/collinearity: teen points collinear prove karo ya k nikalo.
  • Line find karo: given conditions (point + slope, two points, parallel/perpendicular to a given line).
  • Family of lines: point (x₁, y₁) se guzarne wali sab lines: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁).
  • Perpendicular distance ⭐: point se line, ya parallel lines ke beech.
  • Triangle problems: altitudes, medians, perpendicular bisectors ki equations.
  • Image/foot of perpendicular: point ka mirror image kisi line me — JEE favourite.
  • Shifting of origin: axes translate karo — x = X + h, y = Y + k.

💡 Straight Lines easy marks hain — formulas kam (5 forms + 2 distance), patterns fixed. Practice se pakka!

1️⃣ निर्देशांक ज्यामिति — ज्यामिति + बीजगणित का मेल

ज्यामिति की समस्याओं को बीजगणित से हल करना ही निर्देशांक ज्यामिति है। रेखा को समीकरण में लिख दो, फिर कोण, दूरी, ढलान सब गणना बन जाता है। Straight Lines पूरे निर्देशांक ज्यामिति की नींव है।

  • आधार: बिंदु (x, y), दूरी सूत्र √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²], विभाजन सूत्र।
  • ढलान (slope) m ⭐: रेखा कितनी ढलुआँ है — m = tan θ, जहाँ θ रेखा का x-अक्ष से कोण (वामावर्त)।
  • दो बिंदुओं से ढलान: m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁)।
  • क्षैतिज रेखा: m = 0। ऊर्ध्व रेखा: ढलान अपरिभाषित (tan 90°)।

💡 ढलान = पहाड़ी रास्ते का ग्रेडिएंट बोर्ड — "1 in 10" अर्थात 10 चलने पर 1 ऊपर!

2️⃣ समांतर और लंबवत रेखाएँ ⭐

  • समांतर रेखाएँ: ढलान समान — m₁ = m₂।
  • लंबवत रेखाएँ ⭐: ढलानों का गुणनफल = −1 — m₁ × m₂ = −1।
  • दो रेखाओं के बीच कोण: tan θ = |(m₁ − m₂)/(1 + m₁m₂)|।
  • संरेखता ⭐: तीन बिंदु A, B, C एक रेखा पर ⟺ slope(AB) = slope(BC)।
  • ऊर्ध्व रेखाएँ (x = a): इनकी ढलान अपरिभाषित — सूत्र सीधे नहीं लगता।

🎯 m₁m₂ = −1 परीक्षा में सबसे अधिक पूछा जाता है!

3️⃣ रेखा के समीकरण — 5 रूप ⭐

  • बिंदु-ढलान रूप ⭐: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)।
  • द्वि-बिंदु रूप: y − y₁ = [(y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁)](x − x₁)।
  • ढलान-अंतःखंड रूप ⭐: y = mx + c — c = y-अंतःखंड।
  • अंतःखंड रूप: x/a + y/b = 1।
  • अभिलंब रूप: x cos α + y sin α = p।
  • व्यापक रूप: Ax + By + C = 0 — ढलान = −A/B।

💡 व्यापक रूप से ढलान सबसे तेज़: m = −A/B!

4️⃣ दूरी सूत्र ⭐

  • बिंदु से रेखा की दूरी ⭐: (x₁, y₁) से Ax + By + C = 0 की लंब दूरी = |Ax₁ + By₁ + C| / √(A² + B²)।
  • समांतर रेखाओं के बीच दूरी: |C₁ − C₂| / √(A² + B²)।
  • मूलबिंदु से दूरी: |C| / √(A² + B²)।
  • संगामिता: तीन रेखाएँ एक बिंदु से — किन्हीं दो का प्रतिच्छेद तीसरी पर जाँचो।

💡 दूरी सूत्र का प्रयोग: त्रिभुज का क्षेत्रफल, कोण समद्विभाजक, बिंदुपथ — सब जगह!

5️⃣ परीक्षा के पैटर्न

  • ढलान/संरेखता: तीन बिंदु संरेख सिद्ध करो या k निकालो।
  • रेखा ज्ञात करो: दी गई शर्तों से (बिंदु + ढलान, समांतर/लंबवत)।
  • रेखा-परिवार: बिंदु (x₁, y₁) से जाने वाली सब रेखाएँ: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁)।
  • लंब दूरी ⭐: बिंदु से रेखा, या समांतर रेखाओं के बीच।
  • त्रिभुज प्रश्न: शीर्षलंब, माध्यिकाएँ, लंब समद्विभाजक।
  • प्रतिबिंब/लंब पाद: बिंदु का दर्पण प्रतिबिंब — JEE का पसंदीदा।

💡 Straight Lines आसान अंक हैं — सूत्र कम, पैटर्न स्थिर!

1️⃣ Coordinate Geometry — Where Geometry Meets Algebra

Solving geometry problems with algebra is coordinate geometry — Descartes' genius idea. Write a line as an equation, and angles, distances, and slopes all become calculations. Straight Lines is the foundation of all coordinate geometry — Circles and Conic Sections build on it.

  • Basics revision: point (x, y), distance formula √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²], section formula.
  • Slope (gradient) m ⭐: how steep a line is — m = tan θ, where θ is the line's angle with the x-axis (anti-clockwise).
  • Slope from two points: m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁) — rise over run!
  • Horizontal line: m = 0. Vertical line: slope undefined (tan 90°).

💡 Feel it: slope is the gradient board on a mountain road — "1 in 10" means 1 up for every 10 forward!

2️⃣ Parallel and Perpendicular Lines ⭐

  • Parallel lines: slopes are EQUAL — m₁ = m₂. (Same steepness, they never meet.)
  • Perpendicular lines ⭐: product of slopes = −1 — m₁ × m₂ = −1. Each is the negative reciprocal of the other!
  • Angle between two lines: tan θ = |(m₁ − m₂)/(1 + m₁m₂)|.
  • Collinearity ⭐: three points A, B, C lie on one line ⟺ slope(AB) = slope(BC). (Or: area of triangle = 0.)
  • Vertical lines (x = a): watch out in perpendicular checks — their slope is undefined, so the formula doesn't apply directly.

🎯 m₁m₂ = −1 is the most-asked fact — perpendicular bisectors, altitudes of triangles, everything uses it!

3️⃣ Equations of a Line — 5 Forms ⭐

Pick the right form for the situation:

  • Point-slope form ⭐: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁) — when you know one point and the slope.
  • Two-point form: y − y₁ = [(y₂−y₁)/(x₂−x₁)](x − x₁) — when you know two points.
  • Slope-intercept form ⭐: y = mx + c — c is the y-intercept (where the line cuts the y-axis).
  • Intercept form: x/a + y/b = 1 — the line cuts the x-axis at (a, 0) and y-axis at (0, b).
  • Normal form: x cos α + y sin α = p — p is the perpendicular distance from the origin, α its angle.
  • General form: Ax + By + C = 0 — slope = −A/B, intercepts −C/A and −C/B.

💡 Fastest slope from general form: shift Bx across, flip A's sign — m = −A/B!

4️⃣ Distance Formulas ⭐

  • Point-to-line distance ⭐: the perpendicular distance from (x₁, y₁) to Ax + By + C = 0 is |Ax₁ + By₁ + C| / √(A² + B²).
  • Distance between parallel lines: Ax + By + C₁ = 0 and Ax + By + C₂ = 0 → |C₁ − C₂| / √(A² + B²).
  • Distance from the origin: |C| / √(A² + B²) — plug (0,0) into the point formula.
  • Concurrency: three lines pass through one point — find the intersection of any two and check it on the third.

💡 The distance formula powers everything: triangle areas, angle bisectors, locus problems!

5️⃣ Exam Patterns — What Shows Up

  • Slope/collinearity: prove three points collinear or find k.
  • Find the line: from given conditions (point + slope, two points, parallel/perpendicular to a given line).
  • Family of lines: all lines through (x₁, y₁): y − y₁ = m(x − x₁).
  • Perpendicular distance ⭐: point to line, or between parallel lines.
  • Triangle problems: equations of altitudes, medians, perpendicular bisectors.
  • Image/foot of perpendicular: mirror image of a point in a line — a JEE favourite.
  • Shifting of origin: translate axes — x = X + h, y = Y + k.

💡 Straight Lines is easy marks — few formulas (5 forms + 2 distances), fixed patterns. Practice locks it in!
